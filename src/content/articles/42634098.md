### 重點摘要
本研究旨在量化體重減輕對預測十年重大不良心血管事件 (MACE) 和第二型糖尿病 (T2D) 風險的影響，並探討不同運動模式的作用。研究招募了221名超重久坐女性，隨機分配至飲食結合有氧運動、飲食結合阻力運動或單純飲食組，直至BMI降至25以下。結果顯示，平均BMI從28.3降至23.9後，MACE和T2D風險顯著降低，但隨著體重回升，這些益處在一年後部分反彈。研究結論指出，在體重減輕干預中，阻力運動可能特別有助於預防T2D的進展。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了體重減輕後十年心血管事件和第二型糖尿病風險變化軌跡的知識空白，特別是益處可能隨體重回升而反彈的現象，並指出阻力運動在預防糖尿病進展中的潛在益處。未來的研究可進一步探討如何長期維持體重減輕的益處，並擴展到不同人群。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Weight Loss Improves Predicted 10-Year MACE and Type 2 Diabetes Risk, but Benefits Partially Rebound at 1 Year.
**Abstract:** This study aimed to quantify changes in predicted 10-year risk for major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) and type 2 diabetes (T2D) across weight loss, post-weight loss (Post-WL), and 1-year follow-up and to determine whether trajectories varied by exercise modality. Sedentary women with overweight (21-49 years) were randomized to Diet+Aerobic, Diet+Resistance, or Diet-only and completed an 800 kcal/day diet until BMI < 25 kg m Among 221 women (115 African American), mean BMI decreased from 28.3 to 23.9 kg m Weight loss reduced MACE and T2D risks, but both rebounded with weight regain. Resistance exercise may be beneficial in weight loss interventions to prevent progression to T2D. ClinicalTrials.gov identifier: NCT00067873.
