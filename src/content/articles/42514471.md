### 重點摘要
鑑於肥胖及相關代謝疾病日益增加，非營養性人工甜味劑（NASs）作為代糖的應用日益廣泛，但其對腸道菌群、宿主代謝及健康結果的影響仍存在爭議。本回顧綜合分析了八種主要NASs在體外、動物及人體研究中的發現，並進行了化合物特定分析。研究結果顯示，目前證據無法得出NASs「一概有害或安全」的普適性結論，其影響因甜味劑類型、暴露條件及研究模型背景而異。本研究整合了現有知識，闡明了不一致結果的來源，並為未來更具機制性的研究及精確的公共衛生指導提供了框架。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本回顧填補了非營養性人工甜味劑對腸道菌群和宿主代謝影響的零散且具爭議的證據空白，透過綜合性分析來釐清不一致的研究結果。它指出了未來研究應專注於更具機制性、標準化的報告，並需提供特定甜味劑、暴露條件和人群的精確飲食及公共衛生指南。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effects of Non-Nutritive Artificial Sweeteners on Gut Microbiota and Host Metabolism and Health-Related Outcomes: A Review.
**Abstract:** As obesity, diabetes and other diet-related metabolic disorders continue to rise, non-nutritive artificial sweeteners (NASs) are widely used as sugar substitutes in free-sugar reduction and weight-management strategies, but their effects on the gut microbiota, host metabolism and related health outcomes remain debated. To clarify this evidence landscape, this review synthesizes current knowledge on the effects of major NAS on the gut microbiota and host metabolic and health-related outcome. A bibliometric analysis was conducted to characterize the development, knowledge structure and thematic evolution of this research area. For the eight NAS exposure categories included in this review (saccharin, cyclamate, aspartame, acesulfame potassium, sucralose, neotame, neohesperidin dihydrochalcone and mixed NAS), we performed a compound-specific analysis integrating physicochemical characteristics, regulatory status, and research findings from in vitro, ex vivo, animal and human studies on gut microbiota alterations, host metabolic and health-related outcomes. These findings were further compared through four interpretive dimensions: exposure conditions, research model background, outcome assessment and interpretation of discordant results. Current research evidence does not support a class-wide conclusion that NAS are uniformly harmful or safe. To make research findings searchable, we developed the NAS-MAP knowledge graph, an interactive non-nutritive artificial sweetener-microbiota-associated phenotype knowledge graph. Overall, this review consolidates current knowledge, clarifies the sources of inconsistent findings, and provides a framework for more mechanistically informative studies, standardized evidence reporting, and more precise sweetener-, exposure- and population-specific dietary and public health guidance.
