### 重點摘要
這項多中心回溯性研究旨在評估烏司奴單抗 (ustekinumab) 和維多珠單抗 (vedolizumab) 在96名極早發性炎症性腸病 (VEO-IBD) 患者中的療效和安全性，這些患者在六歲前確診並開始治療，且多數曾接受其他生物製劑。研究發現，在第52週時，烏司奴單抗和維多珠單抗的無類固醇臨床緩解率分別為35.3%和37.3%，且感染性不良事件發生率低且無嚴重案例。結論指出這兩種藥物在VEO-IBD患者中展現了高的治療持久性，儘管一年期的無類固醇臨床緩解率仍屬中等。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究為極早發性炎症性腸病患者（一個數據相對缺乏的特定族群）提供了烏司奴單抗和維多珠單抗的療效與安全性數據，填補了該領域的研究空白。然而，研究結果顯示一年期的無類固醇臨床緩解率仍屬中等，這表明儘管有高治療持久性，這些先進療法在實現最佳疾病控制方面仍面臨挑戰，未來可能需要進一步研究以優化治療策略。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Efficacy and safety of ustekinumab and vedolizumab in very early-onset inflammatory bowel disease: A multicenter study from the pediatric inflammatory bowel disease Porto Group of ESPGHAN.
**Abstract:** We aimed to assess vedolizumab and ustekinumab efficacy and safety in patients with very-early onset inflammatory bowel disease (VEO-IBD). A retrospective study involving 29 European and North American sites affiliated with the IBD Porto Group of European Society for Paediatric Gastroenterology Hepatology and Nutrition. Clinical and laboratory data were collected from patients with VEO-IBD who commenced vedolizumab or ustekinumab before 6 years of age, between 2014 and 2024. Ninety-six patients (60.4% females) were included (58 ulcerative colitis, 29 Crohn's disease, and 9 IBD-Unclassified). Median (Q1-Q3) age at diagnosis was 2.3 (0.4-4.2) years, 37 (38.5%) patients were diagnosed at age <2 years old. Thirty-seven (38.5%) patients received ustekinumab, 67 (69.7%) vedolizumab, and 11 (11.5%) both drugs. Median (Q1-Q3) follow-up time was 11 (6-26) and 14 (7-39) months for ustekinumab and vedolizumab, respectively. Eighty (84.2%) patients were previously treated with infliximab, 23 (24.2%) with adalimumab, and 20 (20.8%) with both. The median (Q1-Q3) time from diagnosis to vedolizumab and ustekinumab initiation was 15 (9-26) and 20 (11-36) months, respectively. Ustekinumab-treated patients achieved clinical remission in 24.3%, 51.4%, and 40.5% at end of induction, week 26, and week 52, with steroid-free remission in 18.9%, 45.9%, and 35.3%, respectively. Vedolizumab-treated patients achieved clinical remission in 29%, 47.5%, and 40.3%, with steroid-free clinical remission (SFCR) rates of 10.4%, 43.3%, and 37.3% at the same time points. Infectious adverse events were reported in 3 (8.3%) of ustekinumab-treated and 5 (7.6%) vedolizumab-treated patients, none severe. Ustekinumab and vedolizumab demonstrated high therapeutic durability in patients with VEO-IBD; 1-year rates of SFCR were modest.
