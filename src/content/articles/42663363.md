### 重點摘要
本研究旨在透過質性紮根理論設計，探討中國中南部湖南省PCOS女性健康行為改變的原因與方式，並建立其理論框架。研究共訪談了20名患者、13名醫生和4名護士，並運用紮根理論的編碼方法分析數據。結果揭示了一個包含意圖、採納、維持三個進程階段及一個脫離動態狀態的健康行為改變框架，此框架由驅動因素與限制因素的交互作用所塑造。這些發現為PCOS患者的健康行為提供了新見解，並有助於醫療人員制定客製化的干預策略。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究透過建立一個具體且全面的紮根理論框架，填補了PCOS女性健康行為改變過程及其影響因素的理解空白。它詳細闡述了行為改變的階段與驅動及限制因素的複雜互動，為針對PCOS患者的行為干預提供了實證基礎與理論指導。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Revealing the Complexity of Health Behavior Change Among Women with Polycystic Ovary Syndrome: A Qualitative Grounded Theory Study.
**Abstract:** To explore why and how health behavior change occurs in women with polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) and aims to generate a grounded theoretical framework that illustrates the stages, along with the influencing factors involved in the stage shifts. Qualitative grounded theory design. Tertiary Grade A comprehensive hospitals in Hunan Province, located in Central South China. The study included 20 patients, 13 doctors, and 4 nurses. Not applicable. This study focused on how behavior change occurred and what influenced the change. We followed the processes of open coding, axial coding, and selective coding for analyzing the interview data. This theoretical framework outlines the health behavior change using 3 progressive stages (intention, adoption, maintenance) and a dynamic state of disengagement, shaped by the interplay between driving factors (needs generation, cognitive change, intrapsychic resources, external resources, self-regulation, ego identity, and positive feedback) and restraining factors (personal limitations, environmental constraints, disease-related barriers, and behavioral drawbacks). Our findings provide new insights on health behavior among patients with PCOS and help health care personnel develop tailored intervention strategies based on an individual's stage or status of behavior change.
