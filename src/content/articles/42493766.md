### 重點摘要
肥胖照護中，有八個重要的病人自評結果領域未被現有通用工具充分涵蓋。本研究旨在開發BODY-Q-7 Dimensions (BODY-Q-7D) 工具來測量這些領域，為偏好型工具提供評估框架。研究團隊利用線上國際樣本，對123名參與者進行內容效度測試，並對770名參與者進行現場測試及3個月追蹤。經Rasch測量理論分析，BODY-Q-7D最終縮減為7個項目，並展現出高信度、良好的建構效度及反應性等強健的心理測量學表現。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
現有減重研究中常用的通用偏好型測量工具，未能充分涵蓋肥胖照護中自尊、身心社健康、飲食、汙名、身體意象及過度皮膚等關鍵病人自評結果領域。BODY-Q-7D填補了此空白，提供了一個能評估這些多面向且具共識結果的專業工具。未來的研究限制或下一步是需要進一步針對BODY-Q-7D定義的各健康狀態，進行偏好權重建模以賦予其效用值。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** BODY-Q-7D Measures Consensus-Based Patient-Reported Outcomes for the Management of Obesity Treatment.
**Abstract:** A global multidisciplinary consensus initiative identified eight patient-reported outcome domains important in obesity care: self-esteem; physical, mental, and social health; eating; stigma; body image; and excess skin. Several of these domains are not represented in generic preference-based measures typically used in weight loss research. We developed the BODY-Q-7 Dimensions (BODY-Q-7D) to provide a valuation-ready framework for a preference-based instrument. Concepts from the BODY-Q were used to inform the development of items that covered the eight consensus initiative domains. Psychometric performance of the BODY-Q-7D was examined using an online international sample (i.e., Prolific). Content validity was established with 123 participants. The field test included 770 participants, of whom 269 completed a test-retest, and 599 completed a 3-month follow-up. Rasch Measurement Theory analysis reduced the BODY-Q-7D to 7 items. Items fit the Rasch model with ordered thresholds and structural independence. Reliability was > 0.80 across three reliability coefficients. Hypothesis-based tests of construct validity and responsiveness were supported with 22/28 (79%) and 12/16 (75%) tests accepted. The BODY-Q-7D demonstrated strong psychometric performance in a weight loss sample. The next phase of development is to elicit and model preference weights to assign utility values to each defined health state.
