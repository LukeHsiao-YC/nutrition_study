### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討不同加工程度的全穀物對第二型糖尿病患者糖化血色素（HbA1c）的影響。這是一項為期12週的隨機平行試驗，招募160名第二型糖尿病成人，提供不同加工程度的全穀物，並建議參與者以其取代日常穀物攝取。結果顯示，攝取較少加工的全穀物與第二型糖尿病患者的糖化血色素改善有關。這項研究指出穀物加工程度會影響代謝結果，並支持在飲食指南、食品行銷和標籤中區分精製穀物、磨碎全穀物和完整全穀物的重要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
儘管全穀物被廣泛推廣，但以往關於其加工程度對血糖影響的研究多為小型試驗。本研究透過一項為期12週的隨機對照試驗，首次明確證實不同加工程度的全穀物對第二型糖尿病患者的糖化血色素具有臨床上的顯著影響，填補了該領域在實際臨床應用與飲食建議上的空白。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Impact of minimally processed versus milled whole grains on glycaemic control in type 2 diabetes: A 12-week randomised trial.
**Abstract:** Whole grains are widely promoted within a healthy diet, with dietary advice encouraging the replacement of refined grains with whole grains. However, data from small trials indicates that milling or processing whole grains may influence the glycaemic response. To identify if clinically relevant HbA1c differs with processed (milled) or less processed (unmilled) whole grain intakes in type 2 diabetes management. We conducted a 12-week randomised parallel trial in adults with type 2 diabetes. Participants received free and delivered whole grain foods that differed in degree of processing. Each intervention was of four foods from whole grain wheat, rice, and oats, in sufficient amounts (4.5-9kg per fortnight) to accommodate family size. Participants were advised to replace the grain foods they normally consumed with the provided products. The primary outcome was glycated haemoglobin (HbA1c). All analyses were performed using an intention-to-treat approach, with outcome variables investigated using a generalised linear model. 160 adults with type 2 diabetes (mean age 58.5 ± 11.5 years; BMI 33.7 ± 7.3 kg/m Less processed whole grain foods were associated with improved glycated haemoglobin compared with more processed whole grain foods in adults with type 2 diabetes. These findings indicate that the degree of grain processing influences metabolic outcomes, support food reformulation, and distinguishing between refined grains, milled whole grains (wholemeal), and intact whole grains in dietary guidance, food marketing and labelling.
