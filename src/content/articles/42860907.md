### 重點摘要
這項丹麥的世代研究旨在調查居家腸道外營養患者發生導管相關血流感染、感染性心內膜炎和脊椎骨髓炎的發生率。研究分析了2019年至2023年間323名第三型腸道衰竭患者的數據，共計293,650個導管日。結果顯示，導管相關血流感染的發生率為每千導管日0.93次，其中隧道式有套中央靜脈導管的發生率顯著高於周邊置入中心靜脈導管。本研究強調了早期偵測與管理導管相關血流感染的重要性，以預防感染性心內膜炎或脊椎骨髓炎等嚴重併發症。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究提供了丹麥單一照護中心，針對居家腸道外營養患者導管相關血流感染、感染性心內膜炎和脊椎骨髓炎的具體發生率數據，特別是比較了不同中央靜脈導管類型（隧道式有套導管與周邊置入導管）的風險差異，有助於填補該特定族群此類併發症流行病學資料的空白。然而，作為描述性世代研究，其主要限制在於未能深入探討這些併發症的潛在風險因子或預防策略，且研究為單一中心數據，可能影響結果的普遍性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Catheter-related bloodstream infections, infective endocarditis, and vertebral osteomyelitis in adults receiving home parenteral nutrition: A descriptive cohort study.
**Abstract:** Intestinal failure patients receiving home parenteral nutrition are at risk of catheter-related bloodstream infection. Metastatic infections include infective endocarditis and vertebral osteomyelitis. We aimed to investigate the incidences of catheter-related bloodstream infection, infective endocarditis, and vertebral osteomyelitis in type 3 intestinal failure patients. This was a cohort study conducted in a Danish referral intestinal failure center. All data on catheter-related bloodstream infections, infective endocarditis, and vertebral osteomyelitis were retrieved from medical records on patients receiving home parenteral nutrition through tunneled cuffed central venous access devices or peripherally inserted central catheters from 01 January 2019 to 31 December 2023. A total of 323 patients (209 females) were included, totaling 913 catheters and 293,650 catheter days. The catheter-related bloodstream infection incidence was 0.93 per 1,000 catheter days (95% CI: 0.82-1.03) over the 5-year period, with a statistically significantly higher incidence for tunneled cuffed central venous access devices than for peripherally inserted central catheters (1.10 [95% CI: 0.95-1.26] vs. 0.66 [95% CI: 0.52-0.81] per 1,000 catheter days, respectively). Thirteen cases of infective endocarditis (0.04 per 1,000 catheter days [95% CI: 0.02-0.07]) and ten cases of vertebral osteomyelitis (0.03 per 1000 [95% CI: 0.01-0.06]) were identified. The catheter-related bloodstream infection incidence is within the acceptable range recommended by current guidelines. The incidence of infective endocarditis is higher, and that of vertebral osteomyelitis is similar to those in other studies. Catheter-related bloodstream infection is a serious complication that may lead to infective endocarditis or vertebral osteomyelitis, underscoring the need for timely detection and management.
