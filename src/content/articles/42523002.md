### 重點摘要
創意音樂治療(CMT)對早產兒及其父母有益並能促進親子連結，但其可近性受限。本研究旨在探索早產兒父母和醫療專業人員對基於CMT的行動健康(mHealth)介入措施的需求與偏好，透過多方利害關係人線上焦點團體與心智圖的質性研究方法進行。共有20位參與者貢獻意見，歸納出五個主題，涵蓋了介入措施的目標、功能、內容、可接受性及NICU外的可用性。本研究提供了設計此類mHealth介入措施的關鍵考量，這些深入的使用者洞察將指導未來的開發與評估。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了在開發基於創意音樂治療的行動健康介入措施前，對目標使用者（早產兒父母與醫療專業人員）具體需求與偏好了解不足的空白。它為未來迭代開發此類介入措施提供了重要的使用者導向基礎。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Developing a Music-Based Mobile Health Intervention for Parents and Staff in Neonatal Care and Beyond: Online Focus Group Study.
**Abstract:** Creative music therapy (CMT) in preterm infants has been associated with improved health outcomes in both parents and infants while promoting parent-infant bonding. However, access to CMT is limited. This study aimed to explore the needs and preferences of parents of preterm infants and health care professionals as a first step in developing a CMT-based mobile health (mHealth) intervention. An iterative qualitative study combining multistakeholder online focus groups with mind mapping. Map contributions were analyzed using reflexive thematic analysis. A total of 20 participants (7 parents, 1 grandparent, and 12 health care professionals) contributed to creating the mind map, offering insights into the identified five themes: (1) aims of the mHealth intervention, (2) desired functions and design, (3) content, (4) important factors for acceptability, and (5) usability beyond the neonatal intensive care unit. The study highlights critical considerations for designing a CMT-based mHealth intervention. The in-depth user insights gathered will inform iterative development and refinement of the intervention, which will be evaluated in future usability, feasibility, and effectiveness studies.
