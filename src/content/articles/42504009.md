### 重點摘要
這項在日本進行的9年追蹤研究旨在闡明人口統計學和生活方式因素如何影響身體質量指數（BMI）與肌少症之間的關聯。研究分析了6,632名40-74歲社區居民的數據，發現較低的BMI與較高的肌少症發生機率顯著相關。此外，此關聯受年齡和身體活動水平的顯著調節，其中體重過輕對肌少症的不利影響在中年人及身體活動水平高的人群中更為顯著。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了先前對於身體質量指數（BMI）與肌少症關聯中，哪些人口統計學和生活方式因素會扮演調節角色不明確的空白。它明確指出年齡和身體活動水平是重要的調節因子。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Impact of demographic and lifestyle factors on the association between body mass index and the presence of sarcopenia at follow-up in community-dwelling people aged 40-74 years: a 9-year follow-up study.
**Abstract:** Although body mass index (BMI) is associated with sarcopenia, factors that modify this association are unclear. This study aimed to clarify how demographic and lifestyle factors affect the association between BMI and sarcopenia in older adults. A 9-year follow-up study. Community-based cohort in Japan. A total of 6,632 individuals aged 40-74 years participated in this study. BMI, demographic and lifestyle factors, and disease history were assessed in a baseline survey conducted between 2011 and 2014. Sarcopenia was not assessed at baseline. Sarcopenia status was assessed based on the diagnostic criteria of the Asian Working Group for Sarcopenia 2019 during follow-up examinations conducted in 2021 and 2022. The odds ratio (OR) for the presence of sarcopenia at follow-up was calculated using multivariable logistic regression analysis. A lower BMI was associated with a higher odds of sarcopenia in both males and females (P for trend <0.0001). Significant interactions were observed between BMI and age (P for interaction=0.0003), and between BMI and physical activity level (P for interaction=0.0264), in sarcopenia. Specifically, the odds of sarcopenia among underweight individuals (<18.5 kg/m Low BMI is strongly associated with sarcopenia, and this relationship is modified by age and physical activity level. Specifically, the adverse impact of being underweight on sarcopenia was greater among middle-aged individuals and those with high physical activity levels.
