### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討苯酮尿症 (PKU) 患者使用的酪蛋白醣巨肽-胺基酸補充劑 (CGMP-AA) 在單獨食用或與不同低蛋白餐點共同攝取時的急性餐後代謝反應。一項針對15名健康成人進行的隨機交叉試驗顯示，CGMP-AA與低蛋白食物共同攝取會產生與單獨攝取時不同的餐後反應，尤其影響酪胺酸的濃度。研究結論指出，CGMP-AA的代謝效果會因餐點內容（例如搭配含纖維或脂肪的碳水化合物）而異，並非與單獨攝取時等效，因此在制定飲食建議前，需在PKU患者中進一步驗證這些發現。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了關於CGMP-AA與其他食物共同攝取時，其急性代謝效果缺乏了解的研究空白，特別區分了不同低蛋白餐點情境（如含纖維或脂肪的碳水化合物）的影響。然而，研究結果是在健康成人中觀察到的，其限制在於這些發現需要在苯酮尿症患者中得到確認，才能轉化為針對該病患群體的飲食建議。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Postprandial metabolic responses to a glycomacropeptide-based protein substitute in different meal contexts - Implications for phenylketonuria (PKU).
**Abstract:** Casein glycomacropeptide supplemented with amino acids (CGMP-AA) is used in the treatment of phenylketonuria (PKU). However, its acute metabolic effects when consumed with other foods remain poorly understood. This study investigated the impact CGMP-AA consumed alone or with two low-protein meals providing carbohydrates with either fibre or fat. In this randomised crossover trial, fifteen healthy adults (27.0 ± 7.9 y; 7 females) consumed: A) CGMP-AA alone; B) CGMP-AA with low-protein bread and unpeeled apple; or C) CGMP-AA with low-protein bread and olive oil. Blood samples were collected over 120 min to assess glucose, insulin, C-peptide, amino acids, and appetite-related hormones (glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP), glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1), and peptide YY (PYY)). Co-ingestion with low-protein foods had limited effects on total essential and large neutral amino acid concentrations over 120 min, but increased Tyr incremental area under the curve (iAUC In healthy adults, CGMP-AA elicited distinct acute postprandial responses depending on whether it was consumed alone or with low-protein foods. CGMP-AA consumed with food is not metabolically equivalent to CGMP-AA consumed alone, and carbohydrate-with-fibre and carbohydrate-with-fat meal contexts affect distinct aspects of the postprandial response. These findings should be confirmed in individuals with PKU before being translated into dietary recommendations.
