### 重點摘要
食品行銷中，透明與半透明包裝並用，但其對享樂性食品消費意圖的差異影響尚不明確。本研究透過四項實驗，發現半透明（相較於透明）包裝能增加消費者對享樂性食品的消費意圖與選擇。此效應經由鼓勵精緻意象處理並增加想像愉悅感所介導，且在品牌高度熟悉時會減弱。研究結論指出，半透明包裝為消費者留下想像空間，能使其食品吸引力高於完全透明包裝。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了透明與半透明食品包裝如何差異影響消費者對享樂性食品反應的知識空白。然而，包裝效應的統計支持度在不同研究中有所差異，且對於「較不享樂性食品」的衰減效應僅為暗示性，未達統計顯著，這些都是未來研究可進一步探討的限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** How Translucent Packaging Increases the Appeal of Hedonic Foods.
**Abstract:** Food marketers commonly use both transparent and translucent packaging to showcase food products. Transparent packaging allows consumers to see the product clearly, whereas translucent, frosted packaging keeps the product visible but hazy, making its details harder to see. Despite the widespread use of both package types, little is known about how they differentially influence consumers' responses to hedonic foods. Across four experiments, including two in which participants made real food choices, we find that translucent (vs. transparent) packaging can increase consumption intentions and choice of hedonic foods, although statistical support for the packaging effect varied across studies. Mediation results are consistent with an account in which translucent packaging encourages elaborated imagery processing, which in turn increases the pleasure derived from imagery. Consistent with the proposed role of elaborated imagery, the positive effect of translucent packaging was attenuated when the brand was highly familiar. A similar attenuation for foods perceived as less hedonic was suggestive but not statistically confirmed. These findings suggest that translucent packaging, by leaving room for imagination, can make a food more appealing than transparent packaging that fully reveals it.
