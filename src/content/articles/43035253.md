### 重點摘要
本研究旨在利用大規模跨族裔遺傳數據，透過全蛋白質組孟德爾隨機化，識別代謝功能相關脂肪肝疾病（MASLD）的因果血漿蛋白。研究發現ENTPD5和APOE在東亞和歐洲人群中均對MASLD具有一致的因果效應，且其影響部分透過肥胖介導。縱向分析進一步支持ENTPD5、BMI降低與MASLD緩解間的時序關係。研究結論指出ENTPD5和APOE是MASLD的潛在因果生物標誌物，其中ENTPD5更是肥胖相關MASLD的候選治療靶點。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究透過大規模跨族裔遺傳數據和全蛋白質組孟德爾隨機化，首次確認ENTPD5和APOE為MASLD的因果血漿蛋白，並闡明了肥胖在其中扮演的介導角色，填補了MASLD因果生物標誌物識別的空白。然而，ENTPD5廣泛的多效性譜需進一步深入評估其作為治療靶點的安全性及藥物重塑潛力。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Proteome-Wide Genetic Study Identified ENTPD5 as a Candidate Target for Obesity-Related MASLD.
**Abstract:** This study aimed to discover causal plasma proteins for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) using trans-ancestry genetic data. We performed proteome-wide Mendelian randomization (MR) for 1256 proteins and MASLD using genome-wide association data from East Asian (n = 85,124) and European (n = 500,348) populations. Colocalization was conducted to validate putative causal proteins for MASLD, followed by two-step MR assessing adiposity mediation. Longitudinal cross-lagged analysis in a bariatric surgery cohort (n = 53) was used to assess temporal associations. ENTPD5 and APOE showed consistent causal effects in both East Asians and Europeans, both supported by colocalization. Both proteins influenced MASLD risk through adiposity, with BMI mediating 15.7% of the ENTPD5 effect. Longitudinal analysis in the bariatric surgery cohort supported the temporal pathway "ENTPD5→BMI reduction→MASLD remission (assessed by the fatty liver index)," with 29.2% mediation by BMI. Phenome-wide MR revealed a broad pleiotropic profile of ENTPD5, warranting further evaluation of target safety and repurposing potential. These findings suggest that ENTPD5 and APOE are potential causal biomarkers for MASLD and may contribute to its pathogenesis. ENTPD5 represents a candidate target for obesity-related diseases, although its pleiotropic profile warrants further investigation.
