### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討在不同飲食策略（限時飲食與高蛋白攝取）和阻力訓練下，過重女性瘦體素水平的變化如何與身體組成及血脂狀況相關聯。共32名18至30歲的過重女性被隨機分為四組，為期八週，所有組別均進行阻力訓練並有25%的熱量赤字。初步結果顯示，在限時飲食合併高蛋白組中，瘦體素的變化與脂肪量之間存在顯著的正相關。本研究為理解特定飲食運動介入如何影響瘦體素與體脂肪的關係提供了初步證據。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了瘦體素與身體脂肪區塊及血脂標記在不同飲食策略（如限時飲食和高蛋白攝取）下，結合阻力訓練後關係不清的空白。然而，此研究的樣本僅限於18至30歲的過重女性，其結果推論至其他族群或性別時可能存在限制。

**Title:** Leptin associations with body composition and lipid profile changes in women with overweight following dietary and exercise interventions.
**Abstract:** Leptin plays a key role in appetite regulation and fat metabolism, and its relationship with body fat compartments and lipid markers under different diet strategies remains unclear. This study aimed to evaluate how changes in leptin levels are associated with body composition and lipid profiles in overweight individuals engaged in resistance training (RT), across time-restricted eating (TRE) and high-protein consumption intervention groups. A total of thirty-two overweight women, aged 18 to 30 years, were randomly divided into four intervention groups: time-restricted eating with high-protein intake (THP), time-restricted eating with regular-protein intake (TRP), high protein without time restriction (HP) and regular protein without time restriction (RP). All participants engaged in RT and followed a diet with a 25 % caloric deficit for 8 weeks.
