### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討青少年對膳食行動健康應用程式 (mHealth apps) 的看法，以解釋使用者參與度低的問題，並透過範圍界定回顧，綜合來自14項質性研究的資料。研究識別出六個關鍵主題，顯示青少年普遍偏好易於導航、能促進社交連結、可個人化及遊戲化的應用程式。尤其，能提高個人飲食習慣和福祉意識的應用程式被認為更有效。這些發現提供寶貴的質性洞察，建議應用程式開發者應與青少年合作，將這些受歡迎的元素（如客製化、遊戲化、社交互動及有效追蹤功能）整合至未來的應用程式設計中，以提升使用者的參與度。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了先前量化研究未能充分捕捉青少年對膳食行動健康應用程式細微看法的空白，透過綜合質性研究資料，為理解青少年應用程式參與度及其未來設計提供了更全面的視角。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Perceptions of Dietary mHealth Apps Among Adolescents: Scoping Review.
**Abstract:** Adolescents' high engagement with smartphones creates opportunities for mobile health (mHealth) apps targeting dietary behaviors. However, despite the availability of diverse dietary mHealth apps, user engagement remains low. Existing quantitative literature has identified barriers and facilitators to app effectiveness in youth (aged 10-19 years) but has not fully captured the nuanced perceptions of adolescents (aged 12-19 years), who represent a unique life stage. Synthesizing similarities and differences in adolescents' perceptions of what they value in dietary mHealth apps from qualitative studies may provide a more comprehensive understanding of app engagement and future app design. We aimed to synthesize adolescents' perceptions of using diverse dietary mHealth apps. A scoping review was conducted according to the 5-stage framework by Arksey and O'Malley. Eligible studies were published in English and reported on adolescents (aged 12-19 years) with perceptions of dietary mHealth apps using qualitative methods. Apps that were not specific to diet, relied only on SMS text messaging, were delivered as part of a school-based intervention or telemedicine service, or were housed on a website or social media platform were excluded. Five evidence sources (PsycINFO, Embase, Medline, Web of Science, and CINAHL) were searched. Extracted data were charted by 1 researcher following a pilot test. Similarities and differences in adolescents' perceptions were synthesized across studies using thematic analysis. Of the 1195 search results, 1.2% (n=14) met the eligibility criteria. We identified six themes that represented adolescents' perceptions of using dietary mHealth apps: (1) functionality and design, (2) content and depth, (3) social connection and support, (4) entertainment and gamification, (5) goal setting and motivation, and (6) perceived effectiveness. Overall, adolescents favorably perceived apps that were easy to navigate, fostered social connections, were personalizable, and were gamified. Apps that were felt to increase awareness of personal eating habits, often through tracking calories and foods consumed, and foster awareness of personal well-being were especially discussed in relation to perceived app effectiveness. The findings of this review provide an innovative synthesis of adolescents' perceptions of diverse dietary mHealth apps, using qualitative data. Notably, dietary mHealth apps that were perceived as easy to navigate, tailored (eg, in terms of content), and gamified and those that included social engagement were perceived favorably, regardless of their dietary target and features. Apps that were perceived to improve adolescents' current dietary awareness were particularly valued. Inclusion of these elements in dietary mHealth apps may promote greater engagement. App developers should work with adolescents to establish how specific apps can incorporate these elements into their design, including how best to track dietary habits and integrate AI into app features.
