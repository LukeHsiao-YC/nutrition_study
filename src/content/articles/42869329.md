### 重點摘要
情緒性進食和對食物的渴望在超重或肥胖的成人中普遍存在，影響體重管理。本研究採用準前瞻性設計，對3834名參與社區體重管理計畫的成年人進行線上調查，旨在探討情緒性進食和食物渴望與體重管理成功之間的關係。研究測量了負面與正面情緒性進食及食物渴望，並根據參與者過去12週的體重減輕百分比將其分為高、中、低三組。此外，研究也使用開放性問題探索了管理情緒性進食的策略。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究透過大規模的社區體重管理計畫調查，旨在深入了解情緒性進食、食物渴望與體重管理成功之間的複雜關係，並匯報實際有效的管理策略，這有助於填補在真實世界情境下關於這些因素的實證數據空白。然而，此摘要並未提供具體的研究結果或結論，未來的研究限制可能包括其準前瞻性設計以及參與者性別（92%為女性）和年齡結構可能限制研究結果的廣泛適用性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Negative and positive emotional eating and food cravings related to weight loss during weight management.
**Abstract:** Emotional eating and food cravings are common among adults living with overweight or obesity. This study investigated these in relation to weight-management success and reports on strategies for managing emotional eating. A quasi-prospective study design was used including an online survey completed by 3834 adults on a community weight management programme. Measures included negative and positive emotional eating (BEES) and food craving (CoEQ). Additionally, objectively measured body weight over the previous 12 weeks was obtained and participants were grouped into Higher (>10%, N = 962), Moderate (5-10%, N = 2156), and Lower (<5%, N = 716) weight loss groups. Open questions were used to explore strategies for managing emotional eating. Between-group differences were tested using univariate ANOVAs, with Tukey post hoc comparisons where significant effects emerged. Open ended responses were analysed using inductive thematic analysis and mapped onto the Behaviour Change Taxonomy. Respondents were mostly female (92.0%), mean age = 57.2 years, and BMI = 32.1 kg/m
