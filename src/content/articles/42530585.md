### 重點摘要
傳統透過全國性調查估計「無活動限制的健康餘命」面臨更新頻率低和樣本量有限的挑戰，難以即時評估地方健康狀況。本研究開發了一種新穎的方法，利用日本京都府的醫療索賠數據，結合機器學習模型和生命表，估計市町村級的健康餘命。研究發現各地區健康餘命存在顯著差異，男性平均72.1歲（範圍67.3-75.2歲），女性平均75.8歲（範圍71.3-77.6歲），且與活動限制相關疾病的盛行率存在地區差異。這項研究提供了首個利用真實世界行政數據，對市町村級無活動限制的健康餘命進行穩健且可擴展的估計方法，有助於制定有針對性的健康促進政策並縮小健康差距。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了過去因全國性調查頻率不足和樣本量限制，導致無法即時且詳細評估地方層級健康餘命的研究空白。它提供了一個可擴展且穩健的方法，利用現有的醫療索賠數據進行地方層級的健康餘命估計，突破了傳統方法的局限性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Estimating healthy life years without activity limitations using medical claims data in Japan.
**Abstract:** Healthy life years are estimated from national population surveys that assess limitations in daily activities. However, the infrequent timing and limited sample sizes hinder the timely and detailed assessment of regional health conditions. We aimed to develop a novel method to estimate municipal-level healthy life years without activity limitations by using medical claims data. We analysed medical claims data in Kyoto Prefecture, Japan. Outpatient data from May to July in 2016 and 2019 (n = 1 489 920) were used for development and data from the same period in 2022 (n = 739 236) were used for evaluation. A total of 5743 diagnostic codes were aggregated into 40 disease categories. A machine-learning model was employed to produce the probability of activity limitation, which was calibrated by using data from the Comprehensive Survey of Living Conditions and applied to derive age-specific prevalence rates. Healthy life years at the municipal level as of June 2022 were then estimated by incorporating a life table. We observed variation in healthy life years among municipalities: 72.1 years across all regions for males, ranging from 67.3 to 75.2 years, and 75.8 years for females, ranging from 71.3 to 77.6 years. Regional disparities were also noted in the prevalence of diseases associated with activity limitations. This study provides the first robust, scalable method to estimate healthy life years without activity limitations at the municipal level by using real-world administrative data. Timely monitoring of regional healthy life years will support targeted health-promotion policies and contribute to reducing health disparities.
