### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估遠端食物攝影法 (RFPM) 在青少年中評估能量攝取 (EI) 的有效性，因為此方法已證明在成人中準確，但青少年自我報告飲食攝取常不準確。研究透過兩個階段，在實驗室、自助餐廳及自由生活情境下，比較RFPM與標準測量方法的結果，同時也與筆紙飲食記錄進行對比。結果顯示，RFPM在受控制情境下（如自助餐廳餐點）能準確估計青少年的餐次能量攝取，但在自由生活情境中，兩項研究均出現顯著的能量攝取低估。結論指出，儘管RFPM在受控制環境下對青少年是有效的，但在真實世界中其對青少年能量攝取的評估存在顯著低估，且BMI較高的青少年有更大的低估。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了遠端食物攝影法 (RFPM) 在青少年群體中評估能量攝取有效性的研究空白，證明其在受控制情境下的可行性。然而，研究同時揭示RFPM在青少年自由生活情境中仍存在顯著低估能量攝取的問題，突顯了當前技術在真實世界中準確評估青少年飲食攝取的局限性，這也成為未來研究需要克服的重要挑戰。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** The Validity of Measuring Energy and Nutrient Intake with the Remote Food Photography Method (RFPM) in Adolescents.
**Abstract:** The Remote Food Photography Method (RFPM) accurately estimates energy intake in adults, but its validity in adolescents has yet to be tested. Given the low accuracy of self-reported dietary intake in adolescents, evaluating the validity of the RFPM in both controlled and free-living settings is crucial. To examine the validity of the RFPM in estimating adolescents' energy intake (EI) in controlled and free-living settings. In Study 1, adolescents (n=45) used the RFPM/SmartIntake app during: 1) a laboratory test meal, 2) a cafeteria meal, and 3) 7-days of free-living intake, with EI estimations validated against weighed food intake, Digital Photography of Foods Method, and doubly labeled water (DLW), respectively. EI was measured under the same three settings with pen-and-paper food records. After refining the app and training, Study 2 (n=22) repeated the protocol with a 3-day free-living period. For both studies, equivalence was evaluated using two one-sided t-tests (±10% bounds), and linear mixed models assessed RFPM error by participant characteristics. In Study 1, RFPM estimates of EI were equivalent to the criterion measure during cafeteria meals (mean [SD] error: 4.1 [120.8] kcal; p=0.005), but were not equivalent for the lab test meal (mean [SD] error: 61.1 [112.7] kcal; p=0.675) and 7-days of free-living intake (-887.9 [554.8] kcal/day; p>0.99). In Study 2, RFPM EI estimates were equivalent to the criterion measure in the controlled setting (-2.2 [86.5] kcal; p=0.007), but not in free-living settings (-782.8 [947.0] kcal/day; p=0.99).
