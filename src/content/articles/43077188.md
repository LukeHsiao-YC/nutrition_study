### 重點摘要
這項追蹤研究旨在探討人資經理（HRM）在職場哺乳支持（WLS）決策中的影響因素，並評估實施氣候理論（IC）的作用。研究透過對11位美國人資經理進行半結構式深度訪談，並利用內容分析法進行資料分析。結果顯示，人資經理在福利決策時面臨內外部壓力，WLS不僅包括法定哺乳空間與時間，也包含彈性工時等其他支持，且帶薪育兒假被視為重要的招聘工具。研究建議，應考量人資經理的需求，尤其提供所有員工的教育，以改善WLS支持，並視人資經理及其專業組織為推廣WLS的重要夥伴。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究透過深入訪談人資經理，填補了對於職場哺乳支持決策中人資經理視角的理解空白，並將實施氣候理論納入考量。同時，它也明確了人資經理在實施WLS時所需的實證資訊與員工教育等資源，強調了人資經理及其專業組織在推廣WLS中的關鍵夥伴角色。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Exploring Human Resources Managers' Views of Workplace Lactation Support: A Follow-Up Study.
**Abstract:** To follow up on a previous exploration of factors that shape the decisions made by human resources managers (HRMs) concerning workplace lactation support (WLS). We also sought to determine the role of implementation climate (IC) theory in the benefits decision process and to obtain input on resources HRM would find helpful to guide their WLS implementation. Eleven semi-structured, in-depth interviews were conducted with HRMs from companies located in the United States and analyzed using content analysis. A priori codes were used, based on an interview guide informed by concepts from both organizational behavior and IC theories. New codes were developed for emergent concepts, with the two researchers coding to consensus. HRMs face pressures from both outside and inside their companies when making benefit decisions. In addition to space and time to pump as required by law, other supports such as flexible work hours were considered as WLS. Paid parental leave was viewed as an important benefit and useful recruiting across genders. Evidence based information was desired for all employees with a key source of information HR organizations, both national and local groups. Overall, interviews surfaced IC constructs involved in WLS implementation. WLS support may be improved by considering the needs of HRMs when providing guidance., especially education for all employees. HRMs and their professional organizations are important partners for researchers and practitioners to promote WLS.
