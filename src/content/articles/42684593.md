### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估阿富汗24-35個月大兒童國家全面免疫接種計畫的完成度及其相關因素，因該國兒童疫苗接種率偏低且缺乏相關證據。透過分析2022-23年全國多指標類集調查的6342名兒童數據，結果發現僅16.2%的兒童完成了完整接種，且IPV2和MCV-2覆蓋率尤低，跨劑次脫落率高，並存在顯著的地理差異。多變量分析顯示，父母的教育程度和母親擁有手機與更高的完成度呈正相關。研究結論指出，阿富汗的全面免疫接種計畫完成度仍非常低，需加強疫苗接種的連續性、後續追蹤以及以家庭為中心的溝通，以提升完成率。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了阿富汗兒童一歲後國家全面免疫接種計畫完成度及其影響因素的數據空白，因過去相關證據有限，有助於了解更廣泛的疫苗接種依從性問題，而非僅限於初次接種覆蓋率。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Completion of the National Childhood Immunization Schedule and Associated Factors Among Children Aged 24-35 Months in Afghanistan: A Nationwide Analysis of MICS 2022-23.
**Abstract:** Childhood immunization coverage remains low in Afghanistan, but evidence is limited regarding completion of the broader national immunization schedule beyond the first year of life. This study estimated full-schedule immunization coverage and examined factors associated with its completion among Afghan children aged 24-35 months. We analysed nationally representative data from the Afghanistan Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey (MICS) 2022-23, including 6342 children aged 24-35 months. Full immunization was defined according to the MICS TC.11b indicator for completion of all vaccinations included in the national childhood immunization schedule. Survey-weighted analyses estimated vaccine-specific coverage, within-series dropout and provincial variation. A prespecified survey-weighted multivariable logistic regression model was used to identify factors associated with full-schedule completion. Only 16.2% (95% CI 14.7-17.7) of children had completed the full national immunization schedule. Coverage was particularly low for Inactivated Poliovirus Vaccine (IPV2) (24.6%) and measles-containing vaccine (MCV)- 2 (36.8%), and 49.2% of children who had received IPV1 had not received IPV2. Full-schedule completion varied markedly across provinces, from 3.1 to 37.7%. In the adjusted analysis, maternal primary education (AOR 1.55, 95% CI 1.11-2.19) and secondary or higher education (AOR 1.72, 95% CI 1.15-2.57), paternal primary education (AOR 1.68, 95% CI 1.13-2.50) and secondary or higher education (AOR 1.94, 95% CI 1.48-2.55), and maternal mobile-phone ownership (AOR 1.29, 95% CI 1.03-1.61) were associated with greater odds of full-schedule completion. Completion of the national childhood immunization schedule remains very low in Afghanistan, with substantial attrition across multi-dose vaccine series and marked geographical variation. Strengthening continuity of vaccination, follow-up for later doses, and accessible family-focused vaccination communication may help improve schedule completion.
