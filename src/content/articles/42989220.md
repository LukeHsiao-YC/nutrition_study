### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討成年人長期行動電話使用與高血壓發生率之間的關聯。這項前瞻性世代研究納入7,490名基線無高血壓的成人，並根據行動電話使用年限進行分組追蹤。結果顯示，相較於使用行動電話少於5年者，使用5-10年及≥10年的參與者，其罹患高血壓的風險顯著增加。研究結論指出，長期行動電話使用與高血壓發生風險獨立相關，並強調需將數位科技相關行為納入心血管疾病預防策略。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了長期行動電話使用與高血壓發生風險關聯的證據空白，並提供了將數位科技相關行為納入心血管疾病預防策略的實證基礎。然而，研究主要關注使用年限，未深入探討不同使用模式（例如每日使用時間、通話量）的影響，這將是未來研究需進一步探索的限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Long-term mobile use and incident hypertension: findings from the Shahrekord PERSIAN cohort study.
**Abstract:** The widespread and increasing mobile use has raised concerns about hypertension risk; therefore, this study investigates the association between long-term use (> 10 years) and hypertension in adults aged 35-70 years. This prospective cohort study included 7,490 adults aged 35-70 years from the Shahrekord PERSIAN Cohort Study who were free of hypertension at baseline. Mobile use duration was categorized as < 5, 5-10, and ≥ 10 years. Incident hypertension was identified during follow-up, and hazard ratios (HRs) with 95% confidence intervals (CIs) were estimated using multivariable Cox proportional hazards regression after adjustment for potential confounders. The mean age of participants was 47.55 ± 8.33 years. During 61,895.9 person-years of follow-up, 236 participants developed incident hypertension, yielding an incidence rate of 3.81 per 1,000 person-years. In the multivariable Cox proportional hazards model, compared with participants with < 5 years of mobile use, those with 5-10 years (aHR = 1.60, 95% CI: 1.05, 2.43) and ≥ 10 years (aHR = 1.42, 95% CI: 1.03, 1.96) had significantly higher hazards of incident hypertension. Older age, obesity (BMI ≥ 30 kg/m²), depression, hypertriglyceridemia, and alcohol non-use were also independently associated with incident hypertension. Long-term mobile use was independently associated with an increased risk of incident hypertension in this prospective cohort. These findings highlight the need to incorporate digital technology-related behaviors into cardiovascular prevention strategies.
