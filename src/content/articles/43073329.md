### 重點摘要
代謝風險評估主要依賴空腹樣本，然而成人大部分時間處於餐後狀態，故本研究旨在探討空腹衍生的多基因和表型分數是否能預測餐後反應。研究對96名墨西哥成年人進行標準化高碳水化合物及高脂肪餐後挑戰，並評估全球多基因分數(gPRS)、墨西哥特定多基因分數(mxPRS)和表型分數對代謝指標的預測能力。結果顯示，表型分數能有效預測餐後血糖和胰島素軌跡，而mxPRS能預測三酸甘油酯的空腹濃度和增量曲線下面積(iAUC)，但全球多基因分數表現不佳。本研究為假設生成性，指出易於獲取的表型測量在預測餐後血糖和胰島素方面優於多種族基因組分數，且族群相關的基因變異在預測混合族群餐後脂肪血症方面具潛力。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了在混合族群中，利用基因與表型分數預測餐後代謝反應的探索性空白，特別強調了族群特定基因變異和易獲取表型測量的重要性，指出傳統空腹評估的局限性。然而，大部分變異仍未被解釋，且研究結果為假設生成性。未來的研究應聚焦於族群相關的基因變異在預測餐後脂肪血症上的應用，並進一步探討表型測量在餐後血糖和胰島素預測中的潛力。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Postprandial response prediction after high-carbohydrate and high-fat standardised meals using global, local, and phenotypic scores.
**Abstract:** Metabolic risk is assessed almost entirely from fasting samples, although adults spend most waking hours postprandially. We tested whether fasting-derived polygenic and phenotypic scores predict the postprandial excursion itself. In a hypothesis-generating physiological study, 96 Mexican adults underwent standardised high-carbohydrate and high-fat meal challenges in random order. We tested a multi-ancestry-derived global polygenic score (gPRS), a four-variant Mexico-specific score (mxPRS) and phenotypic scores against the fasting concentration, the postprandial trajectory and the incremental area under the curve (iAUC). The phenotypic score predicted glucose and insulin trajectories (β = 8.1 mg/dL and 17.5 µU/mL per SD, both p < 0.001) but not glucose iAUC (p = 0.358). The mxPRS predicted triglycerides in both domains (fasting p = 0.027; iAUC β = 2808 mg/dL·min per SD, p = 0.026) and neither glucose nor insulin iAUC. The gPRS predicted no fasting concentration and no iAUC except insulin after carbohydrate, which was unstable. Most variance was unexplained. These exploratory findings are hypothesis-generating. Ancestry-relevant variants merit further study as predictors of postprandial lipaemia in admixed populations, and accessible phenotypic measurements carry more information about postprandial glucose and insulin than a multi-ancestry genome-wide score.
