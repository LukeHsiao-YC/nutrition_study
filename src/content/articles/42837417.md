### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討高收入國家中與生活方式相關的癌症死亡率在不同出生世代間的演變模式，以了解全球生活方式轉變如何影響各國。研究分析了八個高收入國家（如美國、加拿大、日本等）2000年至2020年間30-84歲的肺與喉、大腸直腸、胃和肝癌死亡數據，使用Lexis曲面等方法評估死亡率改善率。雖然整體年齡標準化癌症死亡率呈下降趨勢，但不同癌症類型和出生世代的趨勢各異，例如大腸直腸癌和肝癌在年輕世代中趨勢惡化。研究結論指出，與生活方式相關的癌症死亡率的出生世代模式因國家而異，年輕世代中大腸直腸癌和肝癌趨勢惡化，亟需加強預防和篩檢策略。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻透過對高收入國家中與生活方式相關的癌症死亡率進行跨國、出生世代特異性分析，填補了全球生活方式轉變如何導致各國不同癌症趨勢的研究空白。它特別揭示了年輕世代大腸直腸癌和肝癌死亡率惡化的趨勢，為未來的預防和篩檢策略提供了重要的實證依據。未來的研究可進一步探討具體生活方式因素與這些癌症趨勢變化的因果關係，並評估不同介入措施的有效性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Evolution of birth-cohort patterns in lifestyle-associated cancer mortality across high-income countries.
**Abstract:** Cancer mortality levels have declined across high-income countries, but progress has been uneven by cancer type. Examining birth-cohort patterns of lifestyle-related cancer mortality in an international context can shed light on how shared and differential global lifestyle transitions manifest differently across national contexts. We used harmonized cause-of-death data from the Human Cause of Death series of the Human Mortality Database for Canada, Czechia, England & Wales, France, Germany, Japan, Spain, and the USA from 2000 to 2020. We examined mortality from lung and larynx, colorectal, stomach, and liver cancer for ages 30-84 years by using rates of mortality improvement displayed on Lexis surfaces. Single-age death rates were estimated from grouped data by using penalized composite link models. Two-factor Poisson models and sex-stratified surfaces served as sensitivity analyses. All eight countries showed declining age-standardized cancer mortality, but with diverging trends by cause and birth cohorts. Lung and larynx cancer declined broadly, with mortality patterns reflecting cohort cigarette-smoking histories. Colorectal cancer mortality increased among cohorts born after 1960 in the USA, Canada, and England & Wales, with a similar but delayed pattern in France and Germany. Liver cancer mortality was higher for cohorts born in 1945-55 than for earlier and later cohorts in several countries, with a re-emerging adverse pattern among cohorts born in around 1970-80. Birth-cohort patterns in lifestyle-associated cancer mortality differ across national contexts. Worsening colorectal and liver cancer trends among younger cohorts call for increased prevention and screening strategies.
