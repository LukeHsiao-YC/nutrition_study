### 重點摘要
吸菸已被證實為多種癌症（包括胃癌）的危險因子，但關於二手菸（ETS）與胃癌關聯的研究仍有限。本研究旨在探討中國人群中ETS與胃癌的關聯，納入2216例胃癌病例和8019例對照組，透過面對面訪談和血液樣本測量幽門螺旋桿菌抗體，並使用非條件邏輯回歸和半貝葉斯方法分析。結果顯示，ETS暴露與胃癌呈正相關，總體SB調整後的勝算比為1.29，女性中關聯性更強（勝算比1.36），且觀察到劑量反應關係。本研究強調ETS在胃癌發病中的關鍵作用，建議實施有效的預防措施和政策以降低胃癌發生率。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了過去關於二手菸（ETS）與胃癌之間關聯性研究有限的空白，尤其是在中國人群中確立了其正向關聯。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Environmental tobacco smoke and gastric cancer in a Chinese population.
**Abstract:** Tobacco smoking is an established risk factor for various cancers, including gastric cancer (GC). However, limited studies have explored the association of passive smoking, also known as environmental tobacco smoke (ETS), with GC. We investigated the association between ETS and GC in a Chinese population. A total of 2216 GC cases and 8019 population controls were included. Epidemiological data were obtained by face-to-face interviews and blood samples were collected for Helicobacter pylori antibody measurements. Unconditional logistic regression modeling and the semi-Bayes (SB) method were used to calculate odds ratios (ORs) and 95% confidence intervals (CIs), adjusting for potential confounding factors. P values for trend were calculated to assess dose-response relationships. A likelihood ratio test was employed, and adjusted interaction ORs and 95% CIs were calculated to assess potential interactions on both multiplicative and additive scales. The SB-adjusted OR for ETS (ever vs never) was 1.29 (95% CI: 1.16-1.43) among all participants, indicating a positive association between ETS exposure and GC. A stronger association was found among women with an SB-adjusted OR of 1.36 (95% CI: 1.11-1.66). A dose-response relationship between ETS intensity score and GC was observed in the overall population and both sexes. Our findings underscore the critical role of ETS in contributing to GC, suggesting that effective preventive measures and policies on ETS should be implemented to reduce GC incidence.
