### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討幼兒母親如何排列兒童健康飲食的優先順序，並評估此優先順序與其特定餵食行為的關聯。這項針對356位主要為高資源、高學歷母親的橫斷面研究發現，「我的孩子健康飲食」在育兒優先順序中排名第五。將兒童健康飲食列為較高優先順序的母親，更傾向於提供健康飲食指導、溝通、積極鼓勵及以身作則示範健康飲食。研究結論指出，母親對兒童健康飲食的優先順序越高，越可能採用支持性的餵養方式並塑造有利於健康的家庭飲食環境，提示公共衛生應強化父母的既有優先事項。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了對母親在眾多育兒優先事項中如何看待兒童健康飲食的理解空白，並揭示了此優先順序與實際餵養行為之間的關聯。然而，該研究主要針對資源豐富、受過高等教育的母親，其結果可能不完全適用於不同社經背景的群體，這是未來研究可拓展的方向或限制。

**Title:** High Priority? Examining How Mothers of Young Children Prioritize Children's Healthy Diet and Its Associations with Child Feeding.
**Abstract:** This cross-sectional study examined how mothers of young children ranked their children's consumption of a healthy diet relative to other social, health, and academic priorities and assessed the associations between these rankings and mothers' engagement in specific food parenting practices. Predominantly highly resourced, highly educated mothers ( In this sample, mothers ranked "My child eats a healthy diet" as the fifth most important parenting priority. Older mothers and mothers without a co-parent ranked children's healthy diets as a higher parenting priority. Adjusting for potential confounders, mothers who rated children's healthy diets as a higher priority were more likely to provide their child with guidance for healthy eating [B (95% confidence interval) = -0.03 (-0.06, -0.01),  Mothers who prioritize children's healthy diets are more likely to use supportive food parenting practices and shape the home food environment. Public health efforts should move beyond deficit-based narratives and empower parents by reinforcing their priorities.
