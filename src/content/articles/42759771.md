### 重點摘要
最近研究顯示高循環糖醇與心血管疾病風險增加相關。本研究透過觀察性分析，探討肥胖或超重成年人參與為期兩年的體重管理飲食干預後，血漿赤藻糖醇及其他多元醇的變化是否與動脈粥狀硬化性心血管疾病 (ASCVD) 風險及致動脈粥狀硬化脂質譜的改善相關。結果顯示，較高的基線赤藻糖醇水平與較高的ASCVD風險相關，而血漿赤藻糖醇的顯著下降（而非其他多元醇）與ASCVD風險估計值在6個月時的降低及2年後的長期改善有關，並伴隨含apoC-III的VLDL+LDL膽固醇減少。這些發現突顯了體重管理飲食干預導致的血漿赤藻糖醇水平下降，可能對超重或肥胖成年人的心臟代謝風險改善具有潛在作用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了體重管理飲食干預如何透過血漿赤藻糖醇水平的變化來影響心血管疾病風險和致動脈粥狀硬化脂質譜的空白，突顯了赤藻糖醇作為心臟代謝健康改善的潛在生物標誌。然而，本研究屬於觀察性分析，雖基於隨機飲食干預試驗的數據，但並未直接探討赤藻糖醇作為干預目標的因果關係，未來的研究可能需要更深入的機制探索或直接干預試驗。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Declines in Plasma Erythritol and Improvements in Cardiovascular Risk and Atherogenic Lipid Profiles: A 2-Year Randomized Weight-Loss Dietary Intervention Trial.
**Abstract:** High circulating levels of sugar alcohols (polyols) have recently been linked to increased risks of cardiovascular events. We investigated whether temporal changes in plasma levels of erythritol and other isomeric polyols (arabitol/xylitol and mannitol/sorbitol) in response to weight-loss dietary interventions were associated with improvements in atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) risk estimates and atherogenic lipid profiles among adults with overweight or obesity. We performed an observational analysis using data from adults with overweight or obesity who participated in the 2-year POUNDS Lost trial. Plasma erythritol, arabitol/xylitol, and mannitol/sorbitol were measured at baseline (n = 802, 805, and 803, respectively) and at 6 months. Temporal changes were calculated among participants with measurements at both time points (n = 664, 669, and 668, respectively). The primary outcome was the 10-year ASCVD risk estimated using the validated pooled cohort equations. Two-year changes in cholesterol in lipoprotein subfractions with or without apolipoprotein C-III (apoC-III) were analyzed as secondary outcomes. Higher baseline levels of erythritol (β per 1 SD 1.1% [SE 0.2%]), arabitol/xylitol (β 1.3% [0.2%]), and mannitol/sorbitol (β 1.1% [0.2%]) were associated with higher ASCVD risk estimates (p <0.001 for all). Greater decreases in erythritol, but not the other polyols, were significantly associated with reductions of ASCVD risk estimates at 6 months (β -0.16% [0.07%] per 1 SD reduction; p = 0.023). Initial (6-month) declines in plasma erythritol were associated with long-term (2-year) improvements in ASCVD risk (β -0.26% [0.11%]; p= 0.016) and reductions in cholesterol in VLDL+LDL containing apo-CIII. Declines in plasma erythritol levels in response to weight-loss dietary interventions were associated with improvements in ASCVD risk estimates and atherogenic lipid subtypes containing apoC-III. These findings highlight the potential role of changes in plasma erythritol levels in relation to improvements in cardiometabolic risk among adults with overweight or obesity.
