### 重點摘要
這項隨機對照試驗旨在探討在模擬外送應用程式中，10%的價格干預（對不健康食品徵稅、對健康食品補貼或兩者結合）對食物選擇健康度的影響。研究招募了3107名英國成年參與者，並依社會經濟地位分層。結果顯示，這些價格干預措施對選擇健康或不健康主餐，以及總卡路里攝取量均無統計學上的顯著影響，且效果未因社會經濟地位而異。因此，研究結論指出，在模擬情境下，10%的價格變動未能有效影響外送食品選擇的健康度。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究發現10%的價格變動在模擬外送應用程式中並未顯著影響食物選擇的健康度，這限制了目前對此類干預措施潛在影響的理解。未來的研究應考慮檢視更大比例的價格變動，並在真實世界的購買條件下進行探討，以更全面地評估其有效性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Socioeconomic position and the effect of price interventions on healthier and less healthy food on food selection in a simulated food delivery app: a randomised controlled trial.
**Abstract:** We examined the effects of price interventions on the healthiness of a hypothetical food order in a simulated online out-of-home food delivery app. The design was a randomised controlled trial, with four conditions (10% taxation of less healthy menu items; 10% subsidy of healthier menu items; a combination of taxation and subsidy; standard pricing) and recruitment was stratified by socioeconomic position (SEP). Primary outcomes were healthier vs less healthy main meal selected and total kilocalories (kcals) selected. Participants selected a hypothetical evening meal from out of home food outlets on a simulated online food delivery app. N=3107 adult participants from the UK were randomised into taxation (n=750), subsidies (n=808), a combination of taxation and subsidies (n=769), and standard pricing (n=779). 57-58% of participants were higher SEP. There were no statistically significant effects of the pricing interventions on whether a healthy vs less healthy main meal was selected (e.g. taxation vs control OR 0.91 (95% CI 0.74, 1.12); subsidies vs control OR 1.08 (95% CI 0.89, 1.31)) or on total kcals selected (e.g. taxation vs control regression coefficient -44.31 (95% CI -95.90, 7.28); subsidies vs control regression coefficient -7.94 (95% CI -60.23, 44.35)). There was no evidence that effects differed by SEP. We found no convincing evidence that 10% changes in price of healthy and less healthy food in a simulated out of home food delivery app impacted on healthiness of food selected. Further research may benefit from examining effects of larger price changes and in real-world purchasing conditions.
