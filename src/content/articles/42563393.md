### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討小兒體重管理計畫中，影響黑人青年留存率和體重減輕的家庭與計畫因素。研究對象為49對黑人親子（6-15歲），透過追蹤體重、計畫參與度、家庭功能和家庭共餐頻率等數據，並運用迴歸分析評估其關聯性。結果顯示，早期體重減輕、較多的提供者接觸次數以及每週家庭共餐的青年，其計畫留存率更高；而家庭功能健康的青年在六個月時有較低的身體質量指數（BMI）。這項研究強調了家庭與計畫因素在提升黑人青年體重管理成效與計畫留存方面的重要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了小兒體重管理計畫中，針對黑人家庭的青年，在留存率和體重減輕方面，對於家庭和計畫因素知識的空白。未來的研究可能需要更大的樣本量或多中心研究來驗證這些發現，並探討這些因素在不同文化背景下的普遍性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Program and Family Predictors of Retention and Weight Loss Among Black Youth in a Pediatric Weight Management Program.
**Abstract:** Gaps in knowledge about youth in pediatric weight management programs (PWMPs) include family and program factors related to retention and youth weight loss, specifically for Black families. The objective of this study was to identify family and program factors associated with retention and weight loss among Black youth in a PWMP. A total of 49 parent-child (ages 6-15) dyads who identified as Black at a PWMP had youth weight/height recorded through 12 months, program factors (total encounters and early weight loss), retention (at 6 months and total days retained), and completed assessments of family factors (family functioning, weekly family dinners) at their initial visit. Logistic regression, Wilcoxon rank-sum, and repeated-measure ANOVA were conducted to assess associations between family and program factors with retention at 6-months and weight loss at 6 and 12-months. A total of 42.9% of dyads were retained; each additional unique provider encounter resulted in 1.78 times greater odds of being retained. Youth who lost weight within 3 months were retained in the PWMP for more days compared to children who did not ( Black youth who experienced earlier weight loss, more provider encounters, and family dinners most of the week were more likely to be retained. Youth in families with healthy family functioning were more likely to have lower BMI at 6 months.
