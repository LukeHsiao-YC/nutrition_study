### 重點摘要
食道逆流後的吞嚥引發蠕動波 (PSPW) 是評估食道化學清除能力的指標，但其在兒童族群的數據有限。本研究回溯性分析了195名因疑似胃食道逆流而接受阻抗-pH監測的兒童，並依年齡分為三組（0-≤2歲、>2-≤10歲、>10-≤18歲）進行PSPW評估。結果顯示，0-≤2歲的嬰幼兒組其逆流事件顯著較多，以非酸性逆流為主且逆流範圍較廣，同時其PSPW指數顯著低於其他兩組。這項研究指出，年齡較小的兒童食道化學清除能力較差，因此建議在評估兒童胃食道逆流及治療時，應將年齡差異納入PSPW的解釋。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了兒童食道逆流後吞嚥引發蠕動波 (PSPW) 相關數據的空白，特別是提供了年齡分層的具體數據。過去對於此參數在兒童族群的表現缺乏深入了解，本研究揭示了不同年齡段兒童食道化學清除能力的差異，有助於改進對兒童胃食道逆流的評估與治療。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Are there age-related differences in the frequency of post-reflux peristaltic waves during impedance-pH monitoring?
**Abstract:** The post-reflux swallow-induced peristaltic wave (PSPW) reflects esophageal chemical clearance. Pediatric data on this parameter remain limited. The aim of this study was to evaluate the behavior of the PSPW in children of different ages. We conducted a retrospective analysis of pediatric multichannel intraluminal pH/impedance, studies were performed for suspected gastroesophageal reflux (GER). We included those with a normal reflux index, symptom index <50%, symptom association probability <95% and a number of reflux episodes within normal parameters for age. Patients were divided into three groups according to their age: Group I (0-≤2 years), Group II (>2-≤10 years), and Group III (>10-≤18 years). The PSPW index was calculated manually. Two hundred and sixty-one studies were analyzed, 195 were included (median age 6.3 years, 54% female). Group I showed significantly more reflux events (38 vs. 26 and 32 in Groups II-III, p = 0.004) with predominance of non-acid reflux (p < 0.001) and greater proximal extent (p = 0.005). PSPW index was significantly lower in Group I (42.1%) compared to Groups II (52.6%) and III (50.0%) (p = 0.009). In this pediatric cohort, a higher number of gastroesophageal reflux episodes, a predominance of non-acid reflux and greater proximal extent were observed in infants ≤2 years, as well as reduced chemical clearance, which is reflected in a lower PSPW index compared to the other groups analyzed. Age-specific interpretation of PSPW could improve the evaluation of pediatric GER and therapeutic management.
