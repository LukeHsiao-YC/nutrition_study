### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在評估2021年美國市售酒精飲料標籤上自願性揭露酒精含量、熱量、營養及成分資訊的普及率和格式。研究人員分析了來自美國前150個啤酒、烈酒和葡萄酒品牌的925個產品標籤，主要觀察項目包括「平均分析聲明」(SAA)、「份量說明」以及「成分列表」的出現頻率。結果顯示，啤酒品牌揭露SAA (63%)、份量說明 (28%) 和成分列表 (17%) 的比例顯著高於烈酒 (SAA 26%, 份量說明 12%, 無成分列表) 和葡萄酒品牌 (SAA 2%, 無份量說明和成分列表)。由於頂級酒精飲料品牌自願揭露營養和成分資訊的比例偏低，研究建議未來應推動強制性的標準化標籤。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻明確指出2021年美國市售酒精飲料品牌在自願揭露營養與成分資訊方面的現況與低普及率，尤其揭示了各類酒精飲品（啤酒、烈酒、葡萄酒）之間揭露程度的巨大差異，從而填補了相關資訊揭露透明度的研究空白。研究限制在於僅評估了自願性揭露，並未探討強制性標籤政策的潛在影響或消費者對此類資訊的需求程度。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Availability of alcohol content, calorie, nutrition, and ingredient information on labels of alcoholic beverages sold in the United States in 2021.
**Abstract:** To assess the prevalence and formatting of voluntary alcohol, nutrition, and ingredient information on the labels of alcoholic beverages sold in the U.S. in 2021. We assessed labels from the top 150 beer, spirits, and wine brands from 2021. Primary outcomes were presence of: 1) Statement of Average Analysis (SAA; includes calories, carbohydrates, fat, protein); 2) Serving Facts (SAA plus serving size, servings per container, alcohol content); and 3) ingredients lists. We also assessed prevalence of nutrition- and ingredient-related claims. Attributes were considered present for a brand if they appeared on the label of at least one product from that brand. We used Fisher's exact tests to assess associations between primary outcomes and beverage characteristics. Labels were from a government-run label approval database. Unit of analysis was brands. The analysis included 925 labels from 65 beer, 93 spirits, 101 wine brands. Forty-one beer brands (63%), 24 spirits brands (26%), and 2 wine brands (2%) had SAAs. Eighteen beer brands (28%), 11 spirits brands (12%), and no wine brands had Serving Facts statements. Eleven beer brands (17%), but no spirits or wine brands, had ingredients lists. Presence of SAA was positively associated with having calorie or carbohydrate claims (beer and spirits), larger market share (beer only), and being a member of the beer industry's Brewers' Voluntary Disclosure Initiative (beer only) (p<0.05). Low uptake of voluntary nutrition and ingredient information by top beer, spirits, and wine brands suggests the need for mandatory standardized labels.
