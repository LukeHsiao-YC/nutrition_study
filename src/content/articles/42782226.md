### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在透過與關鍵利害關係人進行質性訪談，找出印度兒童營養與健康教育（NHE）實施中，影響其有效性以降低兒童營養不良的障礙與促進因素。研究發現，NHE 的實施面臨基礎設施不足、資金延遲、人力短缺、社區信任度低及數碼工具加重報告負擔等挑戰。為提升 NHE 效益，研究建議採取由下而上的方法，加強技能培訓、監督、數據應用與行為改變評估，並明確界定非政府組織的角色。此研究為政策制定者提供了跨健康系統的障礙與促進因素概念框架。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究透過質性訪談，為政策制定者和公共衛生專業人員提供了一個理解營養與健康教育在健康系統中實施障礙與促進因素的實用概念框架。然而，未來的研究應考慮進行更廣泛的質性調查，以納入更多非政府組織和政府參與者，進一步支持基於證據的改革並強化 NHE 的提供。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Barriers and Facilitators to Effectiveness of Nutrition and Health Education in Reducing Child Malnutrition in India.
**Abstract:** To identify perceived barriers to and facilitators of Nutrition and Health Education (NHE) implementation to address malnutrition through qualitative interviews with key stakeholders. Eight key stakeholders were purposively sampled for virtual, semi-structured interviews, each averaging 50 minutes, in May-June 2023. Thematic data analysis was applied. NHE implementation is weakened by inadequate infrastructure, delayed funds, workforce shortages, and low community trust. Anganwadi workers remain overburdened and rely on limited participatory approaches. Digital tools add to reporting burdens without improving data use. Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) play a supportive but fragmented role. A bottom-up approach that strengthens skills, supervision, data use, and behavior change evaluation alongside structured roles for NGOs could improve NHE implementation. This study provides policymakers and public health professionals with a conceptual framework of barriers and facilitators across the health system. Broader qualitative inquiries that engage NGOs and government actors could support evidence-based reforms and strengthen NHE delivery.
