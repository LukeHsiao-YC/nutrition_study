### 重點摘要
個人化預後溝通在臨床決策支援中日益普及，但其對患者理解、記憶和決策的影響尚不明確。本研究透過對24位接受左心室輔助裝置 (LVAD) 治療教育並獲得個人化生存預測的晚期心臟衰竭患者進行訪談，探討他們如何理解和運用這些資訊。結果顯示，雖不到三分之一的患者能準確回憶數值預測，但79%的患者對其主要含義有高度理解，個人化預測影響了58%患者的決策方向並提升了50%的決策清晰度，但21%的患者經歷了痛苦。儘管數值記憶有限，大多數患者仍能掌握個人化預測的核心含義，本研究強調將其融入全面患者教育框架的重要性，並需同時處理認知與情感反應。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究初步填補了個人化預後溝通對患者理解、記憶和決策影響的知識空白，特別是在左心室輔助裝置 (LVAD) 治療背景下，揭示了患者雖難準確記憶數值，但能掌握其核心意義並影響決策。未來的研究應著重於發展最佳化風險溝通策略，以支持知情且符合患者價值觀的決策，並應將此溝通整合至全面性的患者教育框架中，以同時處理認知和情感反應。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Impact of personalized risk communication on patient understanding and decision-making in LVAD therapy.
**Abstract:** Personalized prognostic communication - delivering individualized survival estimates to patients - is increasingly integrated into clinical decision support, yet its effects on patient understanding, recall, and decision-making remains poorly understood. This cross-sectional observational study examines how patients receiving personalized survival estimates (PSEs) during education for Left Ventricular Assist Device (LVAD) therapy understand and act on this information. As part of a 5-year, multi-site study, we conducted structured interviews with 24 patients with advanced heart failure who received PSEs via the HeartMate 3 Risk Score (HM3RS) during their LVAD education session. Interviews assessed recall accuracy, gist understanding, perceived utility, decisional impact, and emotional responses. Fewer than one-third of patients accurately recalled their numerical survival estimates, yet 79% demonstrated high gist understanding - retaining key survival benefit messages. PSEs influenced decision direction in 14 of 24 participants (58%) and increased decisional clarity in 12 of 24 (50%). However, 21% experienced distress, particularly when estimates were lower than expected. Despite limited numerical recall, most patients grasped the essential implications of their PSEs. Findings underscore the importance of embedding personalized prognostic communication within comprehensive patient education frameworks that address both cognitive and emotional responses. Future research should develop strategies to optimize risk communication and support informed, values-aligned decision-making in preference-sensitive interventions.
