### 重點摘要
鐮刀型細胞貧血症 (SCA) 患者面臨慢性溶血及紅血球脂質組成改變。本研究是一項為期六個月的單盲臨床試驗，旨在評估魚油與維生素D補充劑對170名SCA成人溶血標誌物和紅血球脂肪酸的影響。結果顯示，單獨或合併維生素D補充魚油，能顯著降低乳酸脫氫酶 (LDH) 和血紅素 (heme) 水平，並改變紅血球脂肪酸組成。這些發現指出魚油補充劑可能在SCA成人中具有潛在的抗溶血作用，但維生素D單獨使用則無顯著益處。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究探討了魚油和維生素D在鐮刀型細胞貧血症成人溶血和紅血球脂肪酸方面的潛在益處，為此領域提供了初步的臨床證據。然而，研究本身也指出由於參與者分配偏差和大量流失，其結果需要謹慎解釋，這意味著未來需要設計更嚴謹的試驗以確認這些發現並克服方法學上的限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Anti-hemolytic effects of fish oil and vitamin D supplementation in adults with sickle cell anemia: A randomized placebo-controlled clinical trial.
**Abstract:** Sickle cell anemia (SCA) is characterized by chronic hemolysis and altered erythrocyte lipid composition. This study evaluated the effects of fish oil and vitamin D supplementation on hemolysis markers and erythrocyte fatty acids in adults with SCA. In this six-month, single-blind, placebo-controlled trial, 183 adults were allocated to four groups. Following the exclusion of 13 participants assigned outside the predefined random sequence, the primary analysis population comprised 170 participants receiving fish oil plus vitamin D (OD), fish oil alone (O), vitamin D alone (D), or both placebos (P), with corresponding placebos in the single-supplement groups. Daily supplementation provided 900 mg eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA) and 600 mg docosahexaenoic acid (DHA), and/or 4285 IU vitamin D. Linear mixed-effects models incorporated available baseline, three-month, and six-month measurements, adjusting for hydroxyurea use and blood transfusions. Pairwise comparisons were Bonferroni-adjusted. Significant group × time interactions were observed for lactate dehydrogenase (LDH; p < 0.001) and heme (p = 0.016), with model-estimated means decreasing from baseline to six months in OD and O. Interactions were not significant for total, direct, or indirect bilirubin. Significant interactions were also observed for erythrocyte lauric, myristic, palmitic, and stearic acids (all p < 0.001), linoleic acid (p = 0.001), arachidonic acid (p = 0.002), docosapentaenoic acid (p = 0.002), and DHA (p = 0.001), with estimated means decreasing in OD and O and increasing in D and P. EPA means increased in OD, O, and D, although its interaction was not significant (p = 0.586). The omega-6:omega-3 ratio interaction was also not significant (p = 0.602). In supplementary analyses of six-month completers, LDH decreased by 26.86% in OD and 39.81% in O; total and indirect bilirubin decreased by approximately 20% in both groups. Fish oil supplementation, alone or combined with vitamin D, was associated with changes in LDH and heme trajectories and erythrocyte fatty acid composition in adults with SCA. No significant benefit of vitamin D alone on hemolysis was detected. These findings suggest a potential role for fish oil supplementation but require cautious interpretation given allocation deviations and substantial attrition.
