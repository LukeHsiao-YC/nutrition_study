### 重點摘要
肉類消費對環境影響巨大，減少攝取是應對氣候變化的關鍵措施。本研究旨在探討地區性地方認同與減少肉類攝取意圖之間的關聯，並特別關注感知到的傳統素食餐點供應程度以及對地區烹飪傳統的依戀在其中扮演的媒介角色。此模型透過路徑分析，對4066名義大利個體進行了測試，旨在深入理解社會文化因素如何影響減少肉類消費的意願。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了過去對影響肉類消費的社會文化因素（如地方認同及對地區烹飪傳統的依戀）探索不足的研究空白。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Regional place attachment and meat reduction: The role of perceived availability of traditional vegetarian meals and attachment to culinary traditions.
**Abstract:** Meat consumption is among the most environmentally impactful human activities. Therefore, reducing meat intake represents a crucial step in addressing climate change. While researchers increasingly recognize that meat consumption is influenced by individual, environmental, and socio-cultural factors, the latter remain underexplored. The present study examined the association between regional place attachment and intentions to reduce meat consumption, focusing on the mediating roles of perceived availability of vegetarian dishes at the regional level and attachment to regional culinary traditions.
