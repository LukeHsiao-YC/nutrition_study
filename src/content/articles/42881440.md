### 重點摘要
隨著全球人口老化，糖尿病與貧血在老年人中日益共存。本研究利用美國CDC WONDER數據庫，分析1999至2023年間，美國老年人口（≥65歲）因糖尿病與貧血共存所導致的死亡模式及相關人口學差異。結果顯示，總計181,430例死亡，死亡率趨勢呈波動性變化，並存在顯著的人口學差異，包括男性高於女性、非裔黑人死亡率最高以及農村地區高於城市地區。本研究描述了美國老年人糖尿病與貧血共存的長期死亡模式及持續存在的人口學差異。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究描述了美國老年人糖尿病與貧血共存的長期死亡模式及人口學差異，但並未闡明這些觀察到的模式背後的潛在機制，這需要未來的研究進一步探討。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Mortality trends and disparities among older adults with coexisting diabetes and anemia from 1999 to 2023: a CDC WONDER database analysis.
**Abstract:** As the global population ages, diabetes and anemia increasingly coexist among older adults. This study examined mortality patterns involving both diabetes and anemia and associated demographic disparities among older adults in the United States from 1999 to 2023. This study utilized national mortality data from 1999 to 2023 obtained from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) Wide-ranging Online Data for Epidemiologic Research (WONDER) database, focusing on deaths involving both diabetes and anemia among adults aged ≥ 65 years. Crude mortality rates (CMR) and age-adjusted mortality rates (AAMR) per 100,000 population and annual percent change (APC) were calculated by year, sex, race/ethnicity, and urban-rural status. A total of 181,430 deaths involving both diabetes and anemia among the elderly were recorded during the study period. Trend analysis revealed a significant increase from 1999 to 2006 (APC = 3.52%), followed by a significant decline from 2006 to 2018 (APC = -1.25%). Subsequently, a sharp surge occurred from 2018 to 2021 (APC = 14.17%), after which a decrease was observed from 2021 to 2023 (APC = -4.13%). Marked disparities were observed: males consistently exhibited higher AAMRs than females; the non-Hispanic (NH) Black population had the highest AAMR among all the racial/ethnic groups; and AAMRs were higher in rural areas compared to urban areas. This study describes long-term mortality patterns involving both diabetes and anemia among older adults in the United States and highlights persistent demographic disparities. Further studies are warranted to clarify the mechanisms underlying these observed patterns.
