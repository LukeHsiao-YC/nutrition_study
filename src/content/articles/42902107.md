### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討脂肪過多（包括BMI、腰圍和體脂百分比）與主動脈剝離發生率及死亡風險的關聯。透過分析英國生物銀行和日本特定健康檢查研究的數據，並進行隊列研究的統合分析，共納入超過百萬名參與者。結果顯示，在兩項大型隊列研究中，高BMI和高腰圍與主動脈剝離風險呈正相關。統合分析進一步證實，脂肪過多（以高BMI、高腰圍和高體脂百分比衡量）顯著增加主動脈剝離的發生率與死亡風險，證明脂肪過多是其重要的危險因子。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
過去鮮少有研究探討脂肪過多與主動脈剝離風險的關聯，且大多數數據僅限於身體質量指數（BMI）一項測量。本研究透過納入腰圍和體脂百分比等多種脂肪測量指標，並結合大型隊列研究與統合分析，彌補了此一研究空白，提供了更全面和具說服力的證據。

**Abstract:** Few studies have investigated the association between adiposity and risk of aortic dissection and data is lacking on adiposity measures other than body mass index (BMI). We analyzed the association between adiposity and risk of aortic dissection in the UK Biobank, and the Japan Specific Health Checkups (J-SHC) Study, and conducted a meta-analysis of cohort studies to clarify the associations between adiposity and risk of aortic dissection. The analytical cohorts included 496,255 participants in the UK Biobank and 630,841 participants in the J-SHC Study. Multivariable Cox proportional hazards regression models were used to analyze associations between adiposity and risk of aortic dissection. PubMed and Embase were searched for relevant studies up to February 27, 2026. Random effects models were used to calculate summary relative risks (RRs) and 95% confidence intervals (CIs) for the association between adiposity and risk of aortic dissection. In the UK Biobank and J-SHC, there were 371 and 105 aortic dissections during 12.3 and 4 years follow-up, respectively.
