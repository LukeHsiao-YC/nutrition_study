### 重點摘要
本研究旨在開發並初步驗證PSE-NEST工具，以評估幼兒教育環境中以營養為重點的政策、系統與環境策略。透過項目生成、工具建構及28位營養與PSE專家的焦點團體內容與表面效度測試，PSE-NEST被開發為涵蓋策略描述、行為影響、實施過程和永續性四個構面的多維度工具。參與者對其清晰度和實用性給予正面回饋。初步結果顯示PSE-NEST作為評估幼兒教育營養策略的標準化工具具有潛力，並填補了現有方法的不足。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻透過整合多個評估維度於單一工具中，填補了現有方法在評估幼兒教育環境中營養相關政策、系統和環境策略方面的關鍵空白。然而，未來的研究仍需進一步評估PSE-NEST在不同環境下的信度、可用性及普遍適用性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Development and Initial Validation of the PSE-NEST: A Tool to Assess Nutrition-Focused Policy, Systems, and Environmental Strategies in Early Childhood Education Settings.
**Abstract:** To develop the policy, systems, and environmental nutrition evaluation in settings tool (PSE-NEST) and establish its initial content and face validity for assessing nutrition-focused PSE strategies in early childhood education (ECE) settings. Two-phase study including-(1) item generation and instrument construction informed by a scoping review and qualitative study, and (2) content and face validity testing conducted through focus groups (n = 5) using a modified nominal group technique (mNGT) to ensure item relevance, clarity, and comprehensiveness. Nutrition education professionals and PSE specialists (n = 28) with experience implementing and evaluating PSE strategies in ECE settings. Item rankings were analyzed using reverse-coded scores combined with participant importance ratings to prioritize items across domains. Agreement between individual and group rankings was assessed to evaluate consistency. Qualitative data from focus group discussions were analyzed using an iterative inductive coding approach to identify themes and inform item refinement. The PSE-NEST was developed as a multi-domain, practitioner-informed tool encompassing the following 4 constructs: (1) PSE strategies description, (2) perceived behavioral impact, (3) implementation process, and (4) perceived sustainability. Participants provided positive feedback on clarity and usability and recommended refinements to improve feasibility and alignment with real-world implementation. Final items reflected consensus-based prioritization and integration of both implementation processes and outcome-related domains. The PSE-NEST demonstrates preliminary promise as a standardized tool for assessing nutrition-focused PSE strategies in ECE settings. By integrating multiple dimensions within a single instrument, it addresses key gaps in existing approaches. Further research is needed to evaluate reliability, usability, and applicability across diverse settings.
