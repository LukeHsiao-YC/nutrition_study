### 重點摘要
該研究旨在探討食物紀錄片如何反映社會認知理論(SCT)與飲食行為的關係，並評估其營養資訊品質。研究人員對七部符合特定條件的食物紀錄片進行內容分析，編碼SCT構成要素並評估資訊的可靠性、清晰度與證據基礎。結果顯示所有紀錄片皆包含SCT構成要素，其中自我效能最為普遍，且資訊品質評分介於中等到高品質之間。因此，這些紀錄片傳遞了符合SCT的飲食行為訊息及中高質量的營養資訊，具備促進健康的潛力。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究首次系統性地分析食物紀錄片如何運用社會認知理論來傳達飲食行為訊息，並評估其資訊品質，揭示了此類媒體在健康促進方面的潛力。然而，研究樣本僅限於七部紀錄片，未來需要擴大分析範圍以增強結果的普遍性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Nutrition Messaging in Food Documentaries: Assessment of Social Cognitive Theory Constructs and Information Quality.
**Abstract:** To examine how food documentaries reflect Social Cognitive Theory (SCT) in relation to dietary behavior and assess the quality of dietary information they present. A content analysis of food documentaries on video streaming platforms, released between January 2020 and December 2024. Eligible documentaries exceeded 30 minutes and included content consistent with dietary patterns recommended by the Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2020-2025. Transcripts from 7 documentaries were obtained through subtitles and independently coded by 2 researchers using qualitative data analysis software. Content reflecting SCT constructs, including self-efficacy, observational learning, reciprocal determinism, outcome expectations, and reinforcement, was coded and analyzed. Information quality was assessed through scoring of reliability, clarity, and evidence base of health-related information. All documentaries included SCT constructs. Across 195 SCT-coded instances, self-efficacy was most prevalent and included practical tips to build confidence (13.3% of the codes) and encouragement for positive change (8.7%). Observational learning codes included expert demonstrations (9.2%) and success stories as models (11.8%). Information quality assessment scores ranged from 58 to 69, indicating moderate to high information quality. Food documentaries in this sample provided SCT-aligned dietary behavior messaging and moderate to high-quality nutrition information, highlighting their potential for health promotion.
