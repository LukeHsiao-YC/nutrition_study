### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討印度孕婦非缺鐵性貧血的病因，分析了687名孕晚期婦女的貧血與缺鐵狀態，並比較各組的紅血球生成活性標誌物、微量營養素及炎症指標。結果發現，貧血無缺鐵組儘管血紅素較低，但其紅血球生成素(EPO)水平與非貧血組相似，且EPO與血紅素無顯著負相關，這與缺鐵性貧血組的反應形成鮮明對比。研究指出，紅血球生成信號傳導受損可能是非缺鐵性貧血的潛在原因，且這與炎症或微量營養素缺乏無關。因此，準確診斷缺鐵對於有效的鐵劑治療至關重要。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了印度孕婦中非缺鐵性貧血病因不明的研究空白，提出紅血球生成信號傳導受損可能是其潛在機制。然而，本研究為橫斷面設計，無法建立明確的因果關係，且未深入探討紅血球生成信號傳導受損的具體分子機制，這將是未來研究的限制與方向。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Reduced Erythropoietic Signalling may be responsible for anemia without iron deficiency among Indian Pregnant Women: findings from a cross-sectional study.
**Abstract:** A substantial proportion of anemia is not associated with iron deficiency (ID), particularly in India. However, the aetiology of anemia without ID remains elusive. To compare erythropoietic activity markers (erythropoietin, erythroferrone), micronutrient profiles, and inflammatory markers across four mutually exclusive groups of anemia and iron deficiency (ID) status: anemia with ID, anemia without ID, no-anemia with ID, and no-anemia and no-ID, to identify the factors associated with anemia without ID. In this cross-sectional study, pregnant women in their third trimester (n=687) were screened for complete blood count including hemoglobin, plasma ferritin (PF), soluble transferrin-receptor (sTfR), high-sensitive C-reactive protein (hsCRP) and Alpha-1-Acid Glycoprotein (AGP), vitamins B12, A, D and red blood cell-folate and in a sub-sample hepcidin, erythropoietin (EPO) and erythroferrone (EFRE) levels were measured. Anemia was defined when Hemoglobin <11g/dL and ID was defined when inflammation adjusted PF levels <20μg/L. The prevalence of anemia with ID, anemia without ID, no-anemia with ID and no-anemia and no ID were 26%, 21%, 11%, 42%, respectively. The anemia with ID group exhibited significantly lower mean Hb (9.32g/dL; 95% CI: 9.1, 9.5 Vs 10.08g/dL 95%CI: 9.9, 10.2), EFRE (0.55ng/mL, 95%CI: 0.5, 0.7 Vs 0.19ng/mL 95%CI: 0.1, 0.3) and higher median EPO (28.18mIU/mL; IQRs: 22.7, 41.0 Vs 22.00mIU/mL; IQRs: 20.8, 24.5) compared to anemia without ID group. Furthermore, despite lower hemoglobin, median EPO in anemia without ID was comparable to non-anemic groups. Micronutrient or Inflammatory markers were either similar or did not show consistent trends across the four groups. Further, hemoglobin levels showed significant negative correlation with EPO (r=-0.55, p<0.001) in anemia with ID group, but not in anemia without ID group (r=0.11, p=0.438). Our study findings suggest that reduced erythropoietic signalling may underly anemia without ID, which is not explained by inflammation or micronutrient deficiencies. Thus, diagnosing ID is vital for effective iron treatment.
