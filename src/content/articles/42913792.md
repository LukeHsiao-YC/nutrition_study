### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討孟加拉國城市貧民窟中育齡母親的糧食不安全、工作時間負擔和飲食行為與飲食品質的關聯。此項橫斷面研究對400名母親進行調查，使用孟加拉健康飲食指數（BD-HEI）評估飲食品質，並評估糧食不安全與情緒性飲食。結果顯示，糧食不安全程度越高，飲食品質越差，特別是在水果和奶製品方面表現最差；年齡與飲食品質呈正相關，而情緒性飲食則呈負相關。研究未發現糧食不安全與情緒性飲食之間對飲食品質有顯著的交互作用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究填補了孟加拉國城市貧民窟中，糧食不安全與心理社會飲食行為對育齡母親飲食品質綜合影響的證據空白。然而，本研究為橫斷面設計，無法建立因果關係，且飲食品質僅基於單次24小時飲食回憶，可能無法完全代表長期飲食習慣，這些是未來研究的限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Structural constraints and psychosocial eating behaviours associated with dietary quality among reproductive-aged mothers in urban slums of Bangladesh.
**Abstract:** Dietary quality among women in urban slums may reflect structural constraints and eating behaviours, yet evidence examining both is limited. This cross-sectional study examined associations of food insecurity, work-time burden, and eating behaviours with dietary quality among 400 mothers aged 15-49 years in 20 urban slums of Dhaka, Bangladesh (two-stage stratified cluster sampling), and explored whether food insecurity moderated the emotional eating-dietary quality association. Dietary quality was assessed using the Bangladesh Healthy Eating Index (BD-HEI; 0-90) from a single 24-h recall, and food insecurity using the Food Insecurity Experience Scale (FIES; 0-8). Multilevel models with slum random intercepts included linear and quadratic food insecurity terms (quadratic term improved fit, p < .001). Mean BD-HEI was 61.5 (SD 15.7), lowest for fruits and milk products. Predicted BD-HEI was 17.3 (95% CI: -24.2 to -10.5) and 18.6 points (-26.7 to -10.4) lower at FIES = 4 and 8, respectively, than at FIES = 0, mostly at low scores, with no statistically significant difference between FIES 6 and 8.
