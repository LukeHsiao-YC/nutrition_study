### 重點摘要
這篇研究指出，美國目前減少槍枝暴力的努力主要集中於嚇阻高風險個人。然而，研究認為應更廣泛地考慮透過普適策略來降低對槍枝，特別是手槍的需求。作者主張，娛樂媒體中槍枝的廣泛呈現與手槍銷量增加及其用於攻擊行為（而非自殺），尤其是在年輕族群中的情況密切相關。文章建議可借鏡公共衛生領域降低香菸需求的成功模式，以減少手槍的吸引力，作為現有控制槍枝策略的補充。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了目前槍枝暴力防治策略中，缺乏針對「普適性需求降低策略」的研究空白，特別是指出了娛樂媒體中槍枝呈現與手槍需求增加的潛在關聯。它強調了目前過於側重於高風險個體的嚇阻，而忽略了從社會層面改變槍枝吸引力的可能性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Universal Strategies to End Appeal of Firearms in America.
**Abstract:** Efforts to reduce firearm violence and injury in the US focus mainly on policies that deter persons at risk of unjustified firearm use. These programs attempt to prevent those who might use firearms from harming others or themselves. What has been overlooked are more universal strategies to reduce the demand for firearms, especially handguns, which are the most frequently used weapon for violent purposes. It is also the weapon that is cited by the Supreme Court as sufficiently popular to justify its protection under the Second Amendment. Here it is argued that the widespread portrayal of firearms in entertainment is closely related to the increase in the sales of handguns and their use specifically for assaults rather than for suicide, especially in young people. The public health approach to the reduction of demand for cigarettes provides a useful model for reducing the appeal of handguns. Such a universal strategy can add to the selective and indicated strategies that are currently highlighted in efforts to control firearm use.
