### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討團體產前照護中的同儕支持動態，並引入「圍產期同儕支持指數」這一情境特定工具來評估。透過對馬拉威和坦尚尼亞孕婦的隨機對照試驗次級分析，比較團體與個別產前照護的差異。結果顯示，參與團體產前照護的孕婦報告最高同儕支持指數分數的可能性是個別照護者的16倍。本研究證明了該指數能有效捕捉團體成員間的互動，並建議未來應利用此工具探討同儕支持對母嬰健康結果的影響。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了過去研究中，團體產前照護裡對「獨特同儕連結」測量不足的空白，提供了一個專門評估此情境的工具。然而，本研究並未深入探討不同程度的同儕支持如何具體影響母嬰健康結果，這是未來研究需探索的限制與方向。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Preliminary Assessment of Perinatal Peer Support in Group Antenatal Care Among Pregnant Women in Malawi and Tanzania.
**Abstract:** Community building is a core component of group antenatal care, yet it is poorly understood and measured. Most prior research has relied on broad social support measures, which may miss the unique peer connections that develop within group antenatal care (ANC). This study introduces the Perinatal Peer Support Index, a context-specific tool designed to assess peer support occurring within group ANC. This secondary analysis used survey data from a multi-site pilot randomized controlled trial in Malawi and Tanzania. Women were randomly assigned to Group or Individual ANC and completed the Perinatal Peer Support Index during late pregnancy. Using bivariate and logistic regression, we examined relationships between ANC type and peer support, controlling for potentially related factors. Pregnant participants were from Malawi (n = 112) and Tanzania (n = 106). Women in Group ANC were significantly more likely to report the highest score on the perinatal peer support index than those in individual ANC (79.8% vs. 21.6%; p < 0.001). Logistic regression showed that Group ANC participants were 16 times more likely to endorse all 14 items, regardless of country or age. The Perinatal Peer Support Index offers a unique way to capture the dynamics among group members during health assessments and interactive learning. Future group care implementations should incorporate this or a similar index to explore how varying levels of peer support from other pregnant women influence maternal and infant outcomes.
