### 重點摘要
該研究旨在評估飲食減重結合三種不同身體活動介入（有氧運動、全日活動或兩者組合）對肥胖老年人感知身體疲勞度的影響。183名肥胖老年人同時進行約400大卡/天的飲食減重計畫，並被隨機分配至上述三組。研究結果顯示，多種身體活動策略與減重計畫結合，能隨著時間有效改善肥胖老年人的感知身體疲勞度。因此，減重與身體活動介入對降低肥胖老年人的身體疲勞感具有積極作用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了將減重與不同身體活動策略結合，以改善肥胖老年人感知身體疲勞度的研究空白，證明了這些綜合性生活方式介入的有效性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Weight Loss and Physical Activity Reduces Perceived Physical Fatigability Among Older Adults With Obesity.
**Abstract:** This study aimed to evaluate the impact of three lifestyle interventions on perceived physical fatigability. Concurrent with a dietary weight loss program (caloric deficit ~400 kcal/day, weekly dietitian support), older adults with obesity (N = 183) were randomized to three groups: aerobic exercise (150-200 min/week of moderate-intensity treadmill walking; n = 60), daylong movement (improving daily activity distribution, progressively increase steps/day; n = 63), or combination (aerobic and daylong movement interventions; n = 60). Perceived physical fatigability (Pittsburgh Fatigability Scale [PFS] Physical score, range 0-50, and Rating of Perceived Exertion [RPE] after a 5-min 0% grade treadmill walk, range 6-20; higher = greater fatigability) was measured at baseline, 6 months (post-intensive intervention), and 18 months (end of maintenance period). Participants (69.6 ± 4.3 years, 77% female, 74% White, 35.6 ± 3.8 kg/m Multiple physical activity strategies in conjunction with weight loss improved perceived physical fatigability over time among older adults with obesity. ClinicalTrials.gov identifier: NCT02923674.
