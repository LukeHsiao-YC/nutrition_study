### 重點摘要
針對嚴重肥胖青少年，代謝與減重手術(MBS)雖有效，但術後缺乏標準化的生活方式介入以維持行為改變。本前瞻性試驗性研究旨在評估一款名為TeenLyft的線上生活支持計畫對MBS術後青少年的可行性、可接受性及初步臨床與安全結果。研究招募了29名13至18歲青少年，結果顯示TeenLyft作為青少年MBS術後照護的輔助方案，展現出高度的可行性、可接受性與安全性。這些發現支持進一步進行一項全面性的隨機對照試驗，以評估其長期有效性和永續性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究填補了青少年代謝與減重手術後，缺乏標準化生活方式介入支持的空白。然而，作為一項試驗性研究，其主要限制在於並非旨在評估長期有效性和永續性，且未設計為隨機對照試驗，因此尚不能提供確切的臨床療效證據。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Online Lifestyle Support for Adolescents Undergoing Metabolic and Bariatric Surgery: Pilot Prospective Cohort Study.
**Abstract:** Metabolic and bariatric surgery (MBS) is a safe and effective treatment for adolescents with severe obesity, yet no standardized lifestyle interventions exist to support sustained behavior change after MBS. This pilot study assessed the feasibility and acceptability of TeenLyft, an online lifestyle support program for adolescents undergoing MBS, and explored preliminary clinical and safety outcomes to inform the design of a future randomized trial. Adolescents aged 13 to 18 years were recruited from a tertiary care MBS center and enrolled in TeenLyft. The study was not powered or designed as a randomized controlled trial but rather as a pilot to guide future randomized controlled trial development. Feasibility domains included recruitment, retention, data completeness, intervention fidelity, and engagement, with predefined progression criteria of ≥80% enrollment, ≥70% retention, ≥70% data completeness, and ≥90% fidelity. Exploratory clinical and safety measures (weight, BMI, and cardiometabolic markers) were collected descriptively to confirm that participation was not associated with harm. Twenty-nine adolescents (mean age 15.9 years, SD 1.1; n=22, 75.9% female; n=13, 44.8% Hispanic; mean BMI 47.8, SD 7.3 kg/m TeenLyft demonstrated high feasibility, acceptability, and safety as an adjunct to adolescent MBS care. Findings support progression to a fully powered randomized trial to evaluate long-term effectiveness and sustainability.
