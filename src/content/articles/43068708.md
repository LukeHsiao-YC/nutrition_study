### 重點摘要
這份臨床報告強調，對於患有幼年型特發性關節炎（JIA）兒童，因其葡萄膜炎風險，以及接受羥氯奎（HCQ）治療兒童，因其視網膜毒性風險，定期眼科篩檢至關重要。報告針對JIA、JIA相關葡萄膜炎患者及HCQ治療兒童的眼科檢查頻率與關鍵測試提供建議。在獲得更具體的兒童數據前，臨床醫師應參考成人HCQ劑量建議（<5毫克/公斤實際體重）。遵循這些篩檢指南對於降低疾病併發症或藥物毒性引起的視力障礙風險，並確保治療的相對安全性至關重要。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本報告填補了兒童JIA患者及接受HCQ治療兒童眼科篩檢指南的空白，提供了初步的建議。然而，未來的研究仍需提供更具體的兒童HCQ劑量數據，以及針對兒童眼科篩檢的精確頻率和關鍵測試的詳細指南。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Ocular Screening in Children With Juvenile Idiopathic Arthritis or Treated With Hydroxychloroquine: Clinical Report.
**Abstract:** Ophthalmic screening examinations are critical in children at risk for inflammatory ocular diseases or toxicity from medication use. Children with juvenile idiopathic arthritis (JIA) who are at risk for or have had a diagnosis of uveitis require scheduled ophthalmic examinations. Hydroxychloroquine (HCQ) is a treatment for several pediatric diseases and has a risk for retinal toxicity. Guidance is needed on the frequency of ophthalmic screening and the critical tests to be performed in both settings. Clinicians are highly encouraged to follow adult HCQ dosing recommendations (<5 mg/kg actual weight) until there are more specific data for children. This clinical report addresses recommendations for ophthalmic examinations in children with JIA, children with JIA-associated uveitis, and children receiving HCQ treatment. Families should be counseled about the critical importance of regular screening examinations for reducing the risk of visual impairment from disease-associated complications or medication toxicity. They can be reassured about the relative safety of medical therapy for these conditions if screening guidelines are followed.
