### 重點摘要
先前研究指出懷孕期間母體焦慮和憂鬱可能與兒童神經發育障礙及腸道菌群失調相關，但其是否增加兒童罹患伴隨腸胃道症狀的自閉症譜系障礙（ASD）風險仍不明確。本研究採病例對照設計，納入470名ASD兒童與486名典型發展兒童，透過照護者問卷收集母體心理健康及兒童腸胃道症狀資訊。結果顯示，ASD組中母親在懷孕期間經歷焦慮或憂鬱的比例顯著較高，且懷孕期間的母體焦慮或憂鬱是兒童罹患伴隨腸胃道症狀之ASD的獨立風險因子。研究結論指出，懷孕期間的母體焦慮或憂鬱可能增加其子女罹患伴隨腸胃道症狀之ASD的風險。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了先前對於懷孕期間母體焦慮和憂鬱是否會增加兒童罹患伴隨腸胃道症狀的自閉症譜系障礙風險的知識空白。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** The Association Between Prenatal Anxiety and Depression and Autism Spectrum Disorder With Gastrointestinal Symptoms in Children: Case-Control Study.
**Abstract:** Studies have indicated that maternal anxiety and depression during pregnancy can contribute to neurodevelopmental disorders and gut flora dysbiosis in children. However, it is unclear whether these factors increase the risk of autism spectrum disorder (ASD) with gastrointestinal (GI) symptoms in children. This study aimed to analyze the relationship between maternal anxiety and depression during pregnancy and ASD with GI symptoms. A total of 470 children with ASD and 486 children with typical development were enrolled in the study. The caregivers of all enrolled children completed questionnaires, including information on maternal mental health and GI symptoms. The percentage of maternal prenatal anxiety or depression was significantly higher in the ASD group than in the typical development group ( Mothers who experience anxiety or depression during pregnancy may have a higher likelihood of their children developing ASD with GI symptoms.
