### 重點摘要
這項研究探討了學齡前兒童的進食時間（時間營養學）與飲食品質之間的關聯，並將生理時型（chronotype）視為潛在的調節因子。研究分析了656名芬蘭3-6歲兒童的飲食記錄、睡眠資料和飲食頻率問卷。結果顯示，較長的早上進食潛伏期、較晚的進食中點和最後一餐時間與較低的飲食品質相關。這些關聯性受生理時型影響，例如，較長的晚餐後進食潛伏期僅在早型兒童中與較差飲食品質相關，而較晚的進食中點和最後一餐時間則在中型和晚型兒童中呈現負相關。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究填補了在學齡前兒童群體中，進食時間、飲食品質與生理時型之間複雜交互作用的知識空白，特別是確認了生理時型作為調節因子在這些關聯中的關鍵角色。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Chrononutrition and Diet Quality in Preschool Children: An Association Shaped by Chronotype.
**Abstract:** Later timing of eating may be associated with lower diet quality, and chronotype may act as an effect modifier. We examined the association between chrononutrition and diet quality and chronotype as a potential effect modifier in 656 Finnish 3-6-year-olds from the DAGIS Survey 2015-2016. Eight chrononutrition variables were calculated using a 3-day food record and sleep data obtained from 7-day actigraphy. Diet quality was assessed using food frequency questionnaires to derive the Healthy Food Intake Index score. Chronotype was estimated as the midpoint of sleep corrected for sleep debt, categorizing earliest 10% as early, middle 80% as intermediate, and latest 10% as late chronotypes. Parental education level, age, and sex were accounted for as potential confounders. In the whole sample, linear regression models indicated that a longer morning latency (β = -0.34, [95% CI -0.65, -0.02]), later eating midpoint (-0.70, [-1.18, -0.22]), and last eating occasion (-0.45, [-0.80, -0.01]), were associated with lower diet quality. Chronotype stratified analyses revealed that longer evening latency was inversely associated with diet quality only in early chronotypes (-1.22, [-2.36, -0.08]). Conversely, later eating midpoint and last eating occasion were inversely associated with diet quality in intermediate (-0.70, [-1.20, -0.20]);-0.46, [-0.82, -0.09]) and late (-2.47, [-3.88, -1.06]; -1.29, [-2.43, -0.16]), but not in early chronotypes. The findings indicate that a longer morning latency, and later timings for eating midpoint and last eating occasion, are associated with lower diet quality, and chronotype acts as an effect modifier in some associations.
