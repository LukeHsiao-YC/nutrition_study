### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討急性壓力如何影響胃部內感受（飽足感與豐滿感閾值）與零食攝取量之間的關係。研究對61名空腹成人進行壓力與控制任務，並測量其胃部內感受敏感度與零食攝取量。結果顯示，在低壓力下，飽足感和豐滿感閾值均與零食攝取量顯著相關，但在急性壓力下，僅豐滿感閾值仍與零食攝取量相關，且某些飲食風格（如情緒化或限制性飲食）會削弱此關聯。這表明急性壓力和特定飲食風格會干擾胃部信號與進食行為的連結，紊亂飲食可能反映了內感受輸入整合受損，尤其在壓力下。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了急性壓力如何影響胃部內感受與食物攝取量之間關聯的空白。它揭示了在急性壓力下，飽足感對終止進食的引導作用會被削弱，而豐滿感仍維持部分關聯，同時特定飲食風格也會影響這種連結。未來的研究應著重於強化飽足感信號在壓力情境下整合至進食決策的能力，而非僅提高內感受敏感度。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Acute stress attenuates the coupling between gastric interoception and snack consumption.
**Abstract:** A comprehensive understanding of the mechanisms underlying eating behavior is crucial for preventing and treating obesity and eating-related disorders. Gastric interoceptive sensitivity, defined as the perceptual threshold of satiation and fullness, is a key physiological marker of meal termination. Although stress is known to influence eating behavior, its impact on the relationship between gastric interoception and food intake remains insufficiently understood. This study behaviorally examined the association between gastric interoceptive sensitivity and snack consumption under high- and low-stress conditions, while accounting for individual eating traits. In 61 fasted adults, gastric interoceptive sensitivity and self-determined snack intake were assessed following both a stress and a control task across four laboratory sessions. Gastric interoceptive sensitivity was measured using a novel paradigm, the Magic Table (MT). Multilevel analyses showed that both satiation and fullness perception thresholds were significantly associated with snack consumption. Under stress, however, only the fullness threshold remained significantly related to snack intake. Higher emotional and restrained eating scores were linked to a weaker association between gastric interoceptive sensitivity and snack intake, whereas higher uncontrolled eating scores showed the opposite pattern. These findings suggest that satiation and fullness signals guide snack termination under low stress, whereas acute stress and certain eating styles weaken the coupling between gastric signals and behavioral regulation. Dysregulated eating may therefore reflect impaired integration of interoceptive input into decision-making, particularly under stress. Beyond enhancing interoceptive sensitivity, studies on interoceptive-focused interventions may benefit from strengthening the integration of satiation signals into eating decisions, especially in stressful contexts.
