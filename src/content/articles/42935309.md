### 重點摘要
本研究旨在分析離家求學的乳糜瀉（CD）大學生所面臨的社會與經濟挑戰，填補了此脆弱群體質性研究的不足。研究透過訪談土耳其九名離家獨立生活的乳糜瀉大學生，揭示他們面臨社會孤立、社會認知不足（混淆麩質與葡萄糖）及麩質飲食產品高昂的經濟負擔，導致學生將乳糜瀉視為「富人病」。結論指出乳糜瀉不僅是臨床疾病，更是影響學生教育與社會生活各層面的複雜生活挑戰。因此，從整體視角提供針對性心理諮詢及機構支持至關重要。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了針對離家獨立生活的乳糜瀉大學生，在社會經濟經歷與生活挑戰方面缺乏質性研究分析的空白。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** A 'luxury' health struggle: a qualitative analysis of the socio-economic experiences of university students with celiac disease.
**Abstract:** University students with celiac disease (CD) are considered a vulnerable group. The number of qualitative studies examining the challenges faced by this group is limited. The aim of the study was to analyse the social and economic impacts of the CD among university students diagnosed with CD who live away from their families. A qualitative phenomenological design was utilised. Semi-structured, face-to-face interviews were conducted. Data were analysed using Colaizzi's seven-stage strategy. Türkiye. Nine university students (mean age: 22 ± 1·6 years) diagnosed with CD. The students were living independently at home or dormitory in Konya. The qualitative analysis yielded six main themes: 'encountering the disease', 'adapting to the disease', 'living with the disease', 'impact on relationships', 'economic burden' and 'patient expectations'. Participants reported significant challenges regarding social isolation, lack of societal awareness (confusion between gluten and glucose) and food insecurity within university cafeterias and dormitories. The high cost of gluten-free products was identified as a major economic barrier, with the student described CD as a 'disease of the wealthy'. This study highlights that CD is not merely a clinical condition but a complex lifestyle challenge affecting all aspects of a student's educational and social life. Recognising CD from this holistic perspective is crucial for developing effective interventions, including targeted psychological counselling and institutional support mechanisms.
