### 重點摘要
本研究旨在比較重症成人患者中，利用「營養與飲食學會/ASPEN營養不良指標 (AAIM)」與「主觀整體評估 (SGA)」診斷營養不良的同步與預測效度。研究人員對450名重症患者進行評估，並測試了AAIM在疾病病因與液體堆積方面的多種變體，同時評估其與SGA的一致性及對住院時間和死亡率的預測能力。結果顯示，AAIM過度估計了營養不良的盛行率（70.9%-75.5%），且與SGA一致性差，對預後無預測價值。相較之下，SGA診斷的營養不良能有效預測患者延長的住院時間和加護病房死亡率，表明SGA在重症患者中具有更好的預後價值。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇研究直接比較了兩種在重症照護中常用的營養不良診斷工具（AAIM與SGA）的效度，特別是AAIM多個變體的應用，提供了關於AAIM在重症患者中應用局限性的實證依據，填補了AAIM在重症患者中效度評估的空白。未來的研究應專注於改進AAIM的診斷閾值或組成指標，使其更適用於重症族群，以提高其特異性和預測價值。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Assessment of tools for the diagnosis of malnutrition and ICU outcomes in critically ill adults: A comparison study.
**Abstract:** To evaluate the concurrent and predictive validity of the Academy of Nutrition & Dietetics/ASPEN Indicators for Malnutrition (AAIM) in critically ill patients compared with the SGA, considering disease etiology and fluid accumulation. This was a comparison study. Malnutrition was assessed within 24 h of ICU admission using the Subjective Global Assessment (SGA) and the AAIM consensus. The AAIM approach was applied assuming all patients had acute disease (AAIM I) or differentiating acute versus chronic disease-related malnutrition (AAIM II). Two variations were tested based on fluid accumulation: including (AAIM a) or excluding (AAIM b) this component. Concurrent validity was evaluated against SGA using diagnostic accuracy metrics, and predictive validity was assessed using hospital and ICU length of stay and mortality. Among 450 critically ill patients [median age 64 (54-71) years; 53.1% male; APACHE II score 18 (11-23)], 46.3% were malnourished by SGA, whereas AAIM prevalence ranged from 70.9% to 75.5%. AAIM showed moderate sensitivity (75.1%-78.9%) but low specificity (28.6%-32.4%) and poor agreement with SGA (κ = 0.066-0.073). AAIM-defined malnutrition was not associated with outcomes, while SGA-defined malnutrition predicted prolonged hospital stay (OR 1.48; 95% CI 1.00-2.20) and ICU mortality (HR 2.26; 95% CI 1.42-3.49). In critically ill patients, AAIM overestimated malnutrition prevalence and lacked concurrent and predictive validity, whereas SGA showed prognostic value.
