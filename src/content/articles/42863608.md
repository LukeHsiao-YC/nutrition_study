### 重點摘要
本研究旨在比較草飼與穀飼牛肉脂肪對飲食誘導肥胖小鼠代謝結果的影響，並與豬油進行對照。結果顯示，相較於豬油，兩種牛肉脂肪均顯著改善葡萄糖耐受性並減少脂肪量。然而，草飼牛肉脂肪額外降低了循環中的發炎標誌物，這與其獨特的脂肪酸組成（如較高的Omega-3脂肪酸和較低的Omega-6/Omega-3比率）相關。因此，雖然兩種牛肉脂肪都優於豬油，草飼牛肉脂肪可能提供額外的抗炎益處。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了關於草飼與穀飼牛肉脂肪對健康結果是否存在差異的證據空白，顯示雖然兩者在改善葡萄糖耐受性和減少脂肪量方面表現相似，但草飼牛肉脂肪可能因其獨特的脂肪酸組成而具有額外的抗炎效果。然而，此研究是在小鼠模型上進行，未來需要進一步的人體研究來驗證這些發現。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Grass-Fed and Grain-Fed Beef Fats Similarly Improve Metabolic Outcomes Compared with Pork Fat in Diet-Induced Obese Mice.
**Abstract:** Although grass-fed and grain-fed production systems significantly affect the fatty acid composition of beef, evidence for distinct health outcomes remains limited despite widespread perceptions that grass-fed beef has health benefits. We investigated whether chronic consumption of grass-fed versus grain-fed beef fat differentially affects metabolic outcomes compared with pork fat in a diet-induced obese mice. Sixty male C57BL/6J mice were fed a low-fat diet (LFD, 10% kcal fat) or a high-fat diet (HFD, 45% kcal fat) containing rendered pork fat (HFD-P), grain-fed beef fat (HFD-GN), or grass-fed beef fat (HFD-GS) for 16 weeks. Metabolic assessments included glucose and insulin tolerance tests, circulating biochemical and inflammatory markers, tissue fatty acid composition, and insulin-stimulated Akt phosphorylation. Statistical analyses were performed using GraphPad Prism, with significance set at P < 0.05. Both beef fat groups similarly (P > 0.05) affected body weight, adiposity, glucose homeostasis, and insulin tolerance. Compared with HFD-P, both HFD-GS and HFD-GN reduced inguinal adiposity by ∼30% (P < 0.01) and improved glucose tolerance GTT AUC by ∼17% (P < 0.05), without differences in systemic insulin sensitivity as assessed by ITT, HOMA-IR, and insulin-stimulated Akt phosphorylation in adipose tissue (P > 0.05). Circulating inflammatory markers were lower with HFD-GS, with resistin concentrations ∼30% lower than HFD-GN and HFD-P and IL-6 concentrations ∼70% lower than HFD-P (P < 0.01). These responses coincided with distinct tissue fatty acid remodeling in HFD-GS mice, characterized by higher omega-3 polyunsaturated fatty acids, trans vaccenic acid, and rumenic acid, and a lower omega-6/omega-3 ratio in blood and adipose compared with HFD-GN and HFD-P. Both beef fat sources similarly improved glucose tolerance and reduced adiposity compared with pork fat in HFD fed C57BL/6J mice. However, grass-fed beef fat may confer additional anti-inflammatory benefits associated with its distinct fatty acid composition.
