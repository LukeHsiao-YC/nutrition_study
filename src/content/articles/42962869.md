### 重點摘要
鴉片類藥物危機在美國仍是重大的公共衛生議題，其中多重物質使用在鴉片類藥物使用障礙 (OUD) 患者中普遍存在且具高死亡風險。本研究透過連結全國尿液藥物篩檢 (UDT) 資料與醫療及藥局理賠數據，分析了逾14萬名18-64歲、患有OUD的成人之多重物質使用情況。結果顯示，OUD患者即使沒有其他非鴉片類物質使用障礙診斷，其多重物質使用率仍高，其中大麻、甲基安非他命和酒精最為常見，且這些患者常伴隨特定人口統計學特徵和共病。這些發現強調了將UDT數據與臨床理賠數據結合做為公共衛生監測工具的實用性，能提供及時且具臨床背景的資訊，以補充傳統監測方法。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究透過結合大規模尿液藥物篩檢數據與醫療理賠資料，填補了傳統公共衛生監測在提供鴉片類藥物使用障礙患者多重物質使用模式方面，即時且具臨床背景資訊的不足。然而，作為一項觀察性研究，其主要限制在於無法建立因果關係或直接評估特定介入措施的有效性，這將需要未來進行更深入的實驗性或介入性研究。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Polysubstance use among patients with opioid use disorder.
**Abstract:** The opioid crisis remains a major U.S. public health priority. Overdose deaths involve 2.4 substances on average, underscoring the pervasiveness and mortality risks of polysubstance use in opioid use disorder (OUD). National laboratory urine drug test (UDT) data linked with medical and pharmacy claims were used to examine polysubstance use among patients with OUD. Data were collected from June 2017 through May 2025 and analyzed from November 2025 through February 2026. Data included 143,108 adults aged 18-64 years with at least one opioid UDT between December 2017 and May 2024, an OUD diagnosis within 180 days prior to the UDT, and continuous medical and pharmacy enrollment for 180 days before and 365 days after the UDT. Findings show high rates of polysubstance use among patients with OUD even without non-opioid SUD diagnoses. Marijuana, methamphetamine, and alcohol were the most prevalent substances in UDTs. Patients with positive non-opioid UDTs were more likely to be younger, male, White, non-Hispanic, and enrolled in managed Medicaid. These patients also had high rates of co-occurring mental and physical health conditions and MOUD and psychotropic medication use. These findings highlight the utility of UDTs linked with medical and pharmacy claims data as a public health surveillance tool. Large-scale UDT data linked with clinical claims may complement traditional surveillance approaches by providing timely, clinically contextualized information on polysubstance use patterns among patients with OUD.
