### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討科威特地區腦性麻痺兒童家庭在照護上的感知容易度及其對日常活動滿意度的影響。這項橫斷性研究招募了53名5至16歲腦性麻痺兒童的照護者，並透過相關量表進行評估。結果顯示，照護容易度分數與日常活動滿意度分數呈顯著正相關。研究建議應定期關懷照護者並建立支持團體，以提供及時協助。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究量化地呈現了照護腦性麻痺兒童的容易度與照護者日常活動滿意度之間的關聯。然而，文獻指出仍需要進行質性評估，以更深入地探索該地區照護者所面臨的具體挑戰與其複雜性，這也是未來研究的重要方向。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Caring for a Child With Cerebral Palsy: Cross-Sectional Study.
**Abstract:** Caregivers of children with cerebral palsy (CP) encounter unique challenges that are not common in typical families. The distress of these families starts in the diagnosis period, which is marked by a "wait and see" approach, and continues because of the lack of a clear prognosis. This study aimed to identify the level of perceived ease of care faced by families of children with CP in Kuwait and its effect on their satisfaction with daily activities. This cross-sectional study recruited caregivers of children with CP aged 5 to 16 years. The Ease of Caregiving for Children (ECC) scale, the self-care domain of the Child Engagement in Daily Life Measure (CEDLM), and the Satisfaction with Daily Occupations (SDO) questionnaire were used. Fifty-three caregivers participated (mean age 39.77, SD 7.56 years; children's mean age 8.04, SD 2.57 years; Gross Motor Function Classification System: level I, n=18; level II, n=10; level III, n=13; level IV, n=5; and level V, n=7). Regular check-ins with caregivers and the establishment of support groups for caregivers would help with providing help sooner. Qualitative assessment is needed to explore these challenges in this region.
