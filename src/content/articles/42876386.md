### 重點摘要
本研究旨在開發並驗證一份專為土耳其成年人（19-64歲）設計的半定量食物頻率問卷（TDG-SFFQ），該問卷基於土耳其膳食指南。研究透過面對面訪談，以24小時飲食回顧法評估其效度，並以4週後的再測法評估其信度。結果顯示，TDG-SFFQ與24小時飲食回顧法在能量與巨量營養素方面呈現中等或更高的統計學相關性，微量營養素也表現良好。信度評估則顯示能量與營養素之間有很強的線性相關。總體而言，TDG-SFFQ被證實是評估土耳其成年人飲食攝取量的一個有效且可靠的工具。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了針對土耳其成人，依據其國家膳食指南開發並驗證特定飲食評估工具（FFQ）的空白，提供了一個文化相關且科學驗證的膳食評估方法。未來的研究可能需要擴大採樣範圍，以進一步確認其在不同地區或亞群中的普適性。

**Title:** Development and validation of semi-quantitative FFQ according to Türkiye Dietary Guideline.
**Abstract:** This study aims to develop and validate a semi-quantitative FFQ specifically designed to assess dietary intake among Turkish adults (19-64 years), based on the Türkiye Dietary Guideline (TDG-SFFQ). The primary goal is to evaluate the validity and reproducibility of the TDG-SFFQ to ensure it accurately captures habitual dietary patterns in the target population. The TDG-SFFQ was administered through face-to-face interviews. Validity was assessed by comparing it with a 24-h dietary recall, while reliability was evaluated using a test-retest method after 4 weeks with fifty-six participants in Ankara, Türkiye. Daily intake means for energy, macronutrients and micronutrients obtained from both the TDG-SFFQ and 24-h dietary recall were calculated. Concordance between the two methods was analysed using the Pearson correlation coefficient and Bland-Altman plots. Participants with adequate communication skills were recruited through digital announcements on social media and professional networks, followed by a snowball sampling method to expand the study reach.
