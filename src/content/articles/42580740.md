### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估不同緊急醫療服務（EMS）機構在處理兒童院外心臟驟停（OHCA）時，判定施救無效（futility）及終止心肺復甦術（TOR）的差異。研究分析了2018-2023年間45,045例兒童非創傷性OHCA病例，發現6.6%的復甦被判定無效，其餘病例中有25.4%終止了復甦。結果顯示，在施救無效和終止復甦的決定上，存在顯著的機構層面差異（施救無效的中位數勝算比為2.33，終止復甦為3.50），甚至超越了許多病患層面的因素。這項研究強調了當地EMS實踐模式可能深刻影響復甦決策，對病患照護、資源利用和臨床預後具有重要意義。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了過去對兒童院外心臟驟停 (OHCA) 中，不同緊急醫療服務 (EMS) 機構在判定施救無效 (futility) 及終止心肺復甦術 (TOR) 決策上，其「機構層面差異」量化評估的空白。它揭示了當地 EMS 操作模式對復甦決策的深遠影響，超越了許多已知的病患因素。未來的研究可以進一步探討造成這些機構層面差異的具體原因（如：政策、訓練、資源），以及這些差異對長期病患預後的影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Futility and Termination of Resuscitation Variation for Pediatric Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest.
**Abstract:** To evaluate agency-level differences in determinations of futility (defined as withholding resuscitative efforts by emergency medical services [EMS]) and termination of resuscitation (TOR) during prehospital encounters for pediatric out-of-hospital cardiac arrest (OHCA). We conducted a retrospective cross-sectional study of EMS activations for children (aged <18 years) with nontraumatic OHCA from the 2018-2023 National EMS Information System. Our outcomes of interest were futility and TOR. We constructed mixed-effects models including patient- and agency-level covariables. We quantified the magnitude of agency-level variation using the median odds ratio. We included 45 045 encounters treated by 6550 EMS agencies. Resuscitation was considered futile in 6.6%; among the remainder, TOR occurred in 25.4%. Futility determinations were less likely in witnessed arrests, events with bystander cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR), public locations, and respiratory/asphyxia or drowning etiologies and more likely in rural/wilderness settings and adolescents. TOR was less likely among witnessed arrests and noncardiac etiologies. The median odds ratio for a futility determination was 2.33 (95% CI 2.16-2.47), which exceeded in magnitude the odds ratio for all fixed-effect variables except for arrest witnessed status, etiology of drowning/submersion, bystander CPR status, tribal-type EMS agency, and wilderness events. The median odds ratio for TOR was 3.50 (95% CI 3.32-3.72), which exceeded all fixed-effect variables. We found substantial agency-level variation in determinations of futility and TOR for pediatric OHCA, underscoring that local EMS practice patterns may shape resuscitation decisions. These findings carry implications for patient care, resource utilization, and clinical outcomes.
