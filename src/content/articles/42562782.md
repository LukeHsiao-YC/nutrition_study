### 重點摘要
本系統性回顧與統合分析旨在評估飲食介入對透析中慢性腎臟病患者相位角(PhA)的影響。研究透過廣泛文獻搜尋，納入14項試驗(其中9項為隨機對照試驗)，共969名參與者。結果顯示，無論是單純飲食介入、合併運動，或是使用胺基酸/蛋白質的單一模式介入，相較於常規照護，均未顯著影響患者的相位角。因此，本統合分析未能證明飲食介入對透析中的慢性腎臟病患者相位角有影響。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了在慢性腎臟病透析患者中，飲食介入對相位角影響數據有限的研究空白。然而，由於本研究納入的隨機對照試驗數量偏低且存在顯著的偏倚風險，未來仍需要更多高品質的隨機對照試驗來驗證本研究的發現。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effect of Dietary Interventions on Phase Angle in Patients with Chronic Kidney Disease Undergoing Dialysis: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
**Abstract:** Phase angle (PhA), derived from bioelectrical impedance analysis, is inversely associated with malnutrition, a common condition in chronic kidney disease (CKD) managed through dietary therapy. Although evidence from oncology and sports medicine suggests nutritional interventions may improve PhA, supporting its potential use as a marker of therapeutic efficacy, data from CKD remain limited. The impact of nutritional-related interventions on PhA values in patients with CKD and undergoing dialysis was evaluated in this systematic review and meta-analysis. A systematic literature search for clinical trial studies was conducted in the PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, CINAHL, and Embase databases up to September 2025. Eligible clinical trials included adults with CKD and undergoing dialysis, applying any dietetic intervention as compared with a control, and reporting PhA variations. Meta-analysis was conducted only on data from randomized clinical trials (RCTs) and analyzed by stratification according to intervention details. A total of 14 studies (n = 9 RCTs) comprising 969 participants were included in the review. Overall, there was no influence of dietetic intervention accompanied or not by an exercise-related component, compared with standard of care (ie, no intervention), on PhA values (mean difference [MD] = 0.22; 95% CI, -0.20 to 0.63; P < .001; I2 = 78%), nor did the isolated effect of the dietary component (MD = 0.26; 95% CI, -0.22 to 0.73; P < .001; I2 = 80%) or use of amino acids or a protein-based unimodal intervention (MD = 0.49; 95% CI, -0.19 to 1.17; P < .001; I2 = 73%) have an impact. Our meta-analysis did not show influences of dietetic intervention on PhA among patients with CKD who are undergoing dialysis. However, given low number of RCTs and important risk of bias, high-quality RCTs are needed to verify our findings. PROSPERO no. CRD42024601056.
