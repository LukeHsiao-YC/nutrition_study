### 重點摘要
這項前瞻性世代研究旨在評估女性在懷孕和產後期間的生活品質分數，並比較不同分娩方式的差異。研究對象為澳洲昆士蘭2866名孕婦，其生活品質分數於懷孕36週及產後6、12週收集。結果顯示，女性的生活品質效用分數從懷孕晚期到產後有所提升。經調整後，計畫性剖腹產女性在懷孕36週的生活品質顯著較低，而未選擇分娩方式的女性在懷孕36週的生活品質也顯著較低。然而，研究並未在產後6週和12週觀察到不同分娩方式在生活品質上的顯著差異。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇研究雖然探討了不同分娩方式的女性在懷孕和產後的生活品質，但最終未能在產後6週和12週發現分娩方式對生活品質的顯著差異，未能完全闡明分娩方式與產後生活品質之間的長期關聯。未來的研究應著重於為懷孕晚期生活品質較低的女性開發更具針對性的介入措施，以改善其生活品質。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Women's Quality-of-Life Trajectories During Pregnancy and Postpartum Periods by Mode of Birth: A Prospective Cohort Study.
**Abstract:** To determine the quality-of-life scores of women during pregnancy and postpartum and compare the differences by mode of birth. A secondary analysis of a prospective, observational cohort study comprising 2866 pregnant women in Queensland, Australia. Women's quality-of-life scores were collected at 36 weeks of gestation and 6- and 12-week postpartum and converted into utility values. On average, quality-of-life utility scores increased from 36 weeks of gestation to 12 weeks postpartum, regardless of birth type. After adjusting for socioeconomic characteristics, women who went on to have a planned caesarean section had a significantly lower utility score at 36 weeks of gestation compared to women who went on to have a spontaneous vaginal birth (adjusted mean difference =  - 0.07; 95% CI - 0.11, - 0.02); women who did not have select birth type had a statistically significantly lower utility score at 36 weeks of gestation compared to women who had a select birth type (adjusted mean difference =  - 0.05; 95% CI - 0.08, - 0.02). No significant differences were observed for other birth types at 6- and 12-week postpartum. Women's quality of life improves from late pregnancy to postpartum. Further research is needed to develop targeted interventions to improve quality of life among women at higher risk of low quality of life and adverse pregnancy and birth outcomes, however we did not identify any differences by mode of birth.
