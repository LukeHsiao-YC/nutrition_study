### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討後疫情時代的死亡模式及COVID-19負擔，透過比較2024年與疫情前（2017-2019年）奧地利的國家總體死亡率，並採用加權分析死亡證明上的多重死因，以減少死因歸因錯誤的影響。研究結果顯示，儘管2024年有COVID-19死亡病例，但60歲以上族群的總體死亡率與2019年持平或更低，而青少年及年輕成人族群的總體死亡率則有所上升。研究結論指出，後疫情時代COVID-19死亡對奧地利的死亡模式沒有明顯影響，且可能存在高估，並對年輕族群死亡率的增加表達關切。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究透過創新地使用死亡證明上多重死因的加權分析方法，有效填補了過去因單一死因歸因可能導致特定疾病負擔被高估或低估的研究空白。它為重新評估COVID-19在後疫情時代對整體死亡率的實際影響提供了更精確的視角，這對於制定如疫苗接種等持續的公共衛生政策具有重要參考價值。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Post-pandemic mortality patterns and COVID-19 burden considering multiple death causes.
**Abstract:** Post-pandemic mortality rates can explore the residual COVID-19 burden and changes in other causes of death. Considering weighted multiple causes of death from death certificates (underlying and others) may help compare post- versus pre-pandemic mortality patterns, while potentially reducing the impact of cause misattribution. Estimates of post-pandemic impact are critical also for proper continuing public health policies (e.g. vaccinations). We retrospectively analyse national all-cause mortality rate ratios between 2024 and pre-pandemic years (2017-2019) for sex-stratified 10-year age groups in Austria. In weighted analyses, the underlying death cause was weighted 50% and other causes shared the remaining 50%. Sensitivity analyses explored different weightings. Cause-specific weightings were also compared between 2024 and 2019. Despite 1212 reported COVID-19 deaths in 2024, all-cause mortality rates were equal or lower in 2024 compared to 2019 in all strata at risk from COVID-19 (i.e., aged 60 years and over). All-cause mortality rates in 2024 were higher than in 2019 in adolescent and young adult strata. The ratio of weighted over unweighted COVID-19 death rates was 0.51-0.58 for age strata 60 years and older and even lower in sensitivity analyses, indicating that COVID-19 deaths were possibly overestimated. Post-pandemic COVID-19 deaths had no visible impact on mortality patterns in Austria and were possibly overcounted. Increased post-pandemic mortality patterns in the young are particularly worrisome.
