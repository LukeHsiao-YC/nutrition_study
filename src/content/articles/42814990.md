### 重點摘要
為提升照顧者對嬰幼兒早期語言發展的知識，研究者開發了一項基於社群媒體的短影音介入措施，名為「BabyTok Project」。這項計畫針對三個月以下嬰兒的照顧者，採用多重方法、前後準實驗設計，評估其可行性、可接受性及初步成效。結果顯示，該計畫在參與度指標與問卷回饋中展現良好可行性與可接受性，且照顧者在知識測量上得分顯著提高。該研究證實BabyTok Project具有潛力，能以可擴展的方式提升照顧者對嬰幼兒早期溝通的認知，促進兒童最佳的語言發展。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究透過開發並測試基於社群媒體的短影音介入，填補了如何以具成本效益且可擴展的方式，提升照顧者對嬰幼兒早期溝通知識的研究空白，並展示了利用熱門社群平台作為教育工具的潛力。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** A Social Media Intervention to Increase Caregiver Knowledge of Early Infant Communication: Multiple Methods Intervention Development and Pilot Study.
**Abstract:** Greater caregiver knowledge of early language development may support more reciprocal interactions with infants and improved child language outcomes. Short-form video interventions on social media platforms like Instagram and TikTok could be a scalable, cost-effective way to increase caregiver knowledge. The objective of this investigation is to describe the development and testing of a version of the BabyTok Project, a social media-based intervention for caregivers of very young infants under 3 months of age. We used a multiple-method, pre-post quasi-experimental design to examine the feasibility, acceptability, and initial evidence of effectiveness of the BabyTok Project through a knowledge measure called the SPEAK-R (Survey of Parent/Provider Expectations and Knowledge-Research). Engagement metrics, participants' postintervention surveys, and feedback support the feasibility and acceptability of the intervention. Statistically significant scores on the SPEAK-R offer initial indications of change in caregiver knowledge. The BabyTok Project has multiple demonstrations of its feasibility and acceptability and shows promise as a means to increase caregiver knowledge of early infant communication development and strategies to enhance children's learning. Interventions like the BabyTok Project could be a scalable way to promote optimal child language outcomes.
