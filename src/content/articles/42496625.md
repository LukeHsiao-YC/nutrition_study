### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討拉丁裔家長對於8-12歲兒童體重管理的觀點與偏好。研究對16位其子女體重超標的拉丁裔主要照護者進行半結構式虛擬訪談，發現家長在處理兒童體重時會考量情境、視體重管理為個人責任但受環境挑戰，並傾向遵循可靠資訊。研究結果顯示，家長對於體重管理與體重包容模式的觀點兼而有之。因此，實務上應提供清晰、具體且敏感於個人因素與結構性障礙的體重指引，並說明預期結果。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究透過定性方法深入探討拉丁裔家長在面對兒童體重問題時的具體偏好與考量，填補了對於此特定文化群體在體重管理溝通上細緻觀點的知識空白。其研究結果為提供更具文化敏感性和情境考量的體重指引奠定了基礎。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Hispanic Parent Preferences for Approaching the Weight of Their 8-12-Year-Old Child: A Qualitative Study.
**Abstract:** Characterize parents' views and preferences for managing their child's weight. Semistructured interviews with Hispanic primary caregivers whose health care provider told them their 8-12-year-old child's weight was above the recommended weight for their age. Virtual interviews. The community sample of participants (n = 16) were primarily mothers (81%), immigrants (94%), and aged 37 ± 6 years. Participants were from Mexico (56%), Ecuador (19%), Colombia (13%), El Salvador (6%), and the US (6%). Parent perceptions of and preferences for weight management and weight-inclusive paradigms. Inductive thematic analysis. We identified 3 themes. First, parents consider context to decide how to address their child's weight. Second, parents view weight management as a personal responsibility that is made challenging by environmental barriers. Third, parents follow guidance from sources they feel are reliable and relatable. Parent perspectives aligned with aspects of both weight management and weight-inclusive paradigms. In practice, these themes indicate parents are likely to want clear, specific guidance on weight that is sensitive to personal factors (e.g., child developmental stage, culture), structural barriers (e.g., time, access, affordability), and that clarifies expectations about potential outcomes from pursuing either approach.
