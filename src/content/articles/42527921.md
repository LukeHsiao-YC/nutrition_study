### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討工作-隱私衝突 (WPC) 與父母親在產後兩年的關係滿意度之間的關聯，並揭示人格特質（大五人格）的調節作用。研究從德國東部的一個縱向世代研究中收集了686位母親和702位父親的數據，測量了產後8週的人格特質、14個月的WPC和2年的關係滿意度，並使用階層線性迴歸進行分析。結果顯示，母親的關係滿意度受到WPC與神經質特質的複雜交互作用影響，平均或高神經質分數本身即是關係滿意度的潛在風險因素；而對於父親，WPC單獨顯著負向預測關係滿意度，且不受人格特質調節。本研究強調了WPC對父母親關係滿意度的不同影響機制，並建議發展工作-家庭福利措施與正向心理學干預。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
過去研究已指出工作-隱私衝突 (WPC) 對人際關係有負面影響，但其影響程度因人而異。本研究透過探討大五人格特質在WPC與父母親關係滿意度之間的調節作用，成功填補了理解WPC影響個體差異機制的知識空白。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Associations between work-privacy conflict and parental relationship satisfaction two years after childbirth: unveiling the moderating role of personality.
**Abstract:** Previous studies have shown that work-privacy conflict (WPC) can have detrimental effects on mental and physical health, interpersonal relationships, and family functioning. However, these detrimental effects vary widely between individuals. At the same time, there is evidence that the Big Five personality traits interact with how people cope with stress and conflict, so they may partly explain these differences. Thus, this study aims to examine the moderating role of the Big Five personality traits in the association between WPC and relationship satisfaction in mothers and fathers. Data from 686 mothers and 702 fathers were derived from a longitudinal cohort study from Eastern Germany. Established questionnaires were used to measure personality at eight weeks, WPC at 14 months, and relationship satisfaction at two years after childbirth. Hierarchical linear regression analyses were performed including relevant confounding variables. WPC alone did not predict maternal relationship satisfaction, whereas it significantly interacted with neuroticism. Mothers with average and high neuroticism levels showed consistently low relationship satisfaction, irrespective of their perceived WPC. Mothers with low neuroticism levels had low relationship satisfaction when their WPC was high. For fathers, WPC alone significantly negatively predicted relationship satisfaction, yet there was no significant interaction effect with personality. The present findings indicate a complex interplay between WPC, neuroticism, and relationship satisfaction in mothers, including that average or high neuroticism scores alone are a potential risk factor for relationship satisfaction. Additionally, our results suggest that WPC may have a detrimental effect on relationship satisfaction in fathers, regardless of their personality traits. Based on our results we recommend developing and expanding measures related to work-family benefits to ensure balance between both domains. We also suggest prevention and intervention in the field of positive psychology.
