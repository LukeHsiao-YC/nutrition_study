### 重點摘要
這項回溯性觀察性世代研究旨在評估一款結合呼吸生物回饋的數位健康體重管理程式（Key to Health）對商業用戶的兩年體重減輕效果。研究分析了11,365名超重或肥胖用戶的去識別化數據，結果顯示，用戶在104週後平均體重減輕了9.6%，其中77%的完成者達到至少5%的體重減輕，45%達到至少10%。此外，早期生物回饋參與度較高與更顯著的體重減輕相關。這表明結合呼吸生物回饋的自動化數位健康程式，可幫助用戶實現約10%的兩年體重減輕。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了數位體重管理程式長期有效性數據有限的研究空白，提供了為期兩年的真實世界證據。然而，作為一項回溯性觀察性研究，它無法建立因果關係，且可能存在未測量的混雜因素。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Two-Year Weight Loss With a Breath Biofeedback mHealth Program: A Real-World Evidence Study.
**Abstract:** Long-term effectiveness data for digital weight management programs remain limited. We evaluated 2-year weight loss among commercial users with overweight or obesity using the Key to Health (Keyto, Inc.) program. This retrospective observational cohort study analyzed deidentified data from 11,365 users (48% female; age 47.3 ± 11.5 years; BMI 32.1 ± 5.9 kg/m Total body weight loss was 4.1% (95% CI: 3.3%-4.9%) at 12 weeks, 6.1% (5.3%-6.9%) at 24 weeks, 9.3% (8.5%-10.1%) at 52 weeks, and 9.6% (8.8%-10.4%) at 104 weeks. At 104 weeks, 77% of completers achieved ≥ 5% and 45% achieved ≥ 10% weight loss. Prespecified sensitivity analyses supported these findings. Higher early biofeedback engagement was associated with greater weight loss (highest vs. lowest quartile: 10.8% vs. 7.4%; p < 0.0001). An automated mHealth program incorporating breath biofeedback was associated with approximately 10% 2-year weight loss. ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07430618.
