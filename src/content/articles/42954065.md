### 重點摘要
膽道閉鎖（BA）的早期診斷對改善預後至關重要，其典型症狀為淺色糞便。本研究比較了卷積神經網絡（CNN）與醫療及非醫療專業人員使用糞便顏色卡（SCC）來辨識淺色糞便的準確性與一致性。結果顯示，非醫療專業人員在淺色糞便的敏感度較低且判讀一致性差，而專業人員間也存在較高的變異性。相較之下，所選的CNN模型在淺色和正常糞便的分類準確性分別達到97.5%和98.5%，顯示數位化糞便顏色篩檢具有巨大潛力。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了傳統糞便顏色卡判讀易受人為因素（特別是非醫療專業人員判讀的不一致性及專業人員間的差異）影響的空白，並提出了運用深度學習進行自動化、標準化篩檢的可行性。未來研究的限制可能包括需要進一步在大規模、多樣化的真實世界環境中驗證其普適性，以及探索如何將此技術無縫整合至現有醫療篩檢流程中。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Digital stool color screening for biliary atresia-Comparing a deep learning approach to the analogue stool color card.
**Abstract:** Early diagnosis of Biliary atresia (BA) improves native liver and overall survival. Pale stools are a characteristic symptom and might be detected by deep learning image recognition. We tested convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to classify pictures of pale and healthy stools and compare the results with stool color card (SCC) assessment of health care professionals (HCP) and non-health care professionals (NHCP). One thousand six hundred and sixteen stool photos with diagnostic labels including BA and healthy controls were graded as pale or normal according to the SCC. A subset of photos (33 BA and 241 healthy) was presented to HCP and NHCP for SCC assessment to evaluate accuracy and interobserver reliability. For CNN model selection and training all images were split into training, validation, and test sets. Different visualization strategies and secondary data augmentation were used for explainability and estimating robustness. In SCC assessment, sensitivity by NHCP trended lower than HCP (86.4% vs. 97.3%), while there were no differences in specificity. Intra-cohort agreement on classification of BA stools (pale vs. normal) was also lower for NHCP than for HCP (36.4% vs. 78.8%), while no significant difference was observed in healthy stools. The selected CNN (ResNet18) correctly classified 97.5% of pale and 98.5% of normal labeled stools, also showing robust results in explainability methods. The high accuracy of CNN shows the potential for a digital stool color screening. In contrast, NHCP assessment of pale stools tends to be inconsistent, and there is high interobserver variability among HCP and NHCP.
