### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討韓國社區成人禁食時間長度與飲食品質（營養商數NQ）之間的關聯。研究採用橫斷面設計，招募了320名19-64歲的社區成人，依習慣性禁食時間分為小於14小時組和等於或大於14小時組，並透過線上問卷評估飲食品質。結果顯示，禁食時間小於14小時的組別，其總體NQ分數及平衡、適度和實踐等子分數均顯著較高，且較長的禁食時間與關鍵食物攝取量減少及早餐攝取量顯著降低有關。研究結論指出，現實生活中的長時間禁食與較差的飲食品質相關，而非更健康的飲食，因此營養教育應強調進餐時間、早餐攝取和飲食多樣性，而非單純的禁食時間長度。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了在真實社區環境中，長時間禁食對飲食品質實際影響的空白，挑戰了僅強調禁食時間長度而忽略飲食結構的觀點。它揭示了在非實驗室控制下，延長禁食可能導致飲食多樣性降低和早餐攝取不足的問題。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Associations Between Fasting Duration and Nutrition Quotient Scores in Korean Adults.
**Abstract:** To examine the association between fasting duration and dietary quality (Nutrition Quotient [NQ]) in a real-world community setting. Cross-sectional. Community-based adults recruited via online announcements. Adults aged 19-64 years (n = 320) were classified by habitual fasting duration: < 14 hours (n = 106) and ≥ 14 hours (n = 214). Individuals with shift work-related irregular sleep-wake or meal schedules were excluded. Online questionnaires assessed general characteristics, sleep and smartphone use, and 18 NQ items (Balance, Moderation, and Practice). Fasting duration was calculated from self-reported times of the first and last meals. Body mass index (BMI) was computed from self-reported height and weight. Group differences were examined using χ² and t tests. Analysis of covariance was used to compare total NQ and factor scores between groups, adjusting for sex, age, sleep duration, and smartphone use. The < 14-hour group showed significantly higher total NQ (P < 0.001) and factor scores, including Balance (P < 0.001), Moderation (P = 0.008), and Practice (P = 0.01). Longer fasting was associated with a lower intake of key food groups and markedly reduced breakfast consumption, whereas BMI did not differ. In real-life fasting practices, prolonged fasting is associated with poorer dietary quality rather than healthier eating. These findings support emphasizing meal timing, breakfast consumption, and dietary diversity rather than fasting duration alone in nutrition education.
