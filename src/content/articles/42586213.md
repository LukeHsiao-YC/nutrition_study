### 重點摘要
印度於2022年實施餐廳菜單標示規定後，本研究進行了一項四組隨機對照試驗，旨在評估菜單標示對消費者食物選擇的影響。研究招募了2462名成年用餐者，隨機分為對照組及三種不同標示格式（量化、解釋性指導、情境性指導）的介入組，比較其點餐內容的卡路里、脂肪、添加糖和鹽含量。結果顯示，在其中一家餐廳，所有三種標示格式均顯著降低了卡路里攝取，其中解釋性指導還額外降低了脂肪攝取。本研究結論指出，菜單標示，特別是解釋性指導，有助於減少所點餐點的卡路里和脂肪，儘管效果會因場景而異。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了印度實施菜單標示規定後，其對消費者食物選擇實證影響的空白，並比較了不同標示格式的有效性。然而，研究結果顯示其效果因餐廳環境而異，且對添加糖和鹽並無顯著影響，這表明該介入措施的通用性可能受限。未來的研究應進一步探討這些異質性的原因，並將研究範圍擴展到更多樣的餐廳類型和地理區域，以提高結果的普適性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effect of restaurant menu labelling on consumer food choices: A randomised controlled trial in South India.
**Abstract:** India implemented a menu labelling regulation for restaurants with at least ten outlets or a five-star certification in 2022. We conducted a parallel, four-arm, randomised controlled trial to evaluate the impact of menu labelling on consumers' food choices in two chain restaurants in an Indian metropolitan city. We recruited 2462 adult diners (40% female, mean age 36.6 years) exiting the restaurants. We randomised them to receive a control menu or one of three menus labelled with: quantitative nutrient information, interpretational guidance, or contextual guidance. Participants ordered first from the usual restaurant menu (pre-intervention) and then from the labelled menus (post-intervention). We compared the calories, fat, added sugar, and salt content of the post-intervention meal orders in each intervention arm with the control arm using ANCOVA-based gamma generalized linear models, adjusted for pre-intervention values. Since we observed heterogeneity in effects between restaurants for calories, we present the results stratified by restaurants. In Restaurant 2, all three labelling formats significantly reduced calories compared to control - interpretational guidance (ratio of means - 0.88, 95% CI 0.81-0.95), contextual guidance (0.89, 95% CI 0.82-0.96) and quantitative labels (0.90, 95% CI 0.83-0.97). Interpretational guidance reduced the fat (0.94, 95% CI 0.88-0.99) ordered. There was no observable effect on added salt and sugar. About 87% of participants reported that labels were understandable, and 63% reported that labelling influenced their food choices. Menu labelling, particularly interpretational guidance, reduces calories and fat ordered, though effects vary by settings. TRIAL REGISTRATION CLINICAL TRIALS REGISTRY: India: CTRI/2024/05/067074.
