### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討兒童加護病房 (PICU) 出院後早期基層醫療追蹤是否能降低急性照護使用。研究利用公私立保險兒童的理賠數據，匹配了超過18萬名有或沒有早期 (7天內) 基層醫療追蹤的PICU住院兒童，比較出院後8至90天內的急性照護 (急診或再住院) 使用天數。結果顯示，接受早期基層醫療追蹤的兒童，無論是公保或私保，其急性照護使用率均顯著較低，尤其在年齡較大的兒童及敗血症患者中效果更顯著。研究結論指出，推廣早期基層醫療追蹤對於降低兒童重症出院後的急性照護利用是有效的。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究透過分析大規模的理賠數據，填補了兒童加護病房 (PICU) 出院後早期基層醫療追蹤與後續急性照護使用減少之間關聯的量化證據空白。它明確證實了及時的初級照護介入對於降低兒科重症患者醫療資源利用的重要性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Association of Early Primary Care Follow-Up With Acute Care Use After ICU Discharge.
**Abstract:** To determine if early primary care follow-up after pediatric intensive care unit (PICU) hospitalization is associated with reduced acute care use. Using claims data from publicly and privately insured children, we identified a cohort of PICU hospitalizations from January 2016 to December 2019. We matched children with and without early primary care follow-up (within 7 days of hospital discharge) 1 to 1 on demographic and clinical characteristics. We compared the number of days of acute care use (emergency department or rehospitalization) during days 8 to 90 postdischarge using negative binomial regression and reported incidence rate ratios (IRR). The sample included 180 516 PICU hospitalizations, including 158 544 for publicly insured children and 21 972 for privately insured children. Among PICU hospitalizations for publicly and privately insured children, 56 247 (35.4%) and 5156 (23.5%) were associated with acute care use with 8 to 90 days postdischarge. Hospitalizations of children with early primary care follow-up were less likely to be associated with acute care use than those without follow-up (public insurance: IRR, 0.93; 95% CI, 0.90-0.96; P < .001; and private insurance: IRR, 0.70; 95% CI, 0.63-0.77; P < .001). Subgroup analyses suggested early primary care follow-up was associated with larger decreases in acute care after hospitalizations of older children and children with sepsis. The association between early primary care follow-up and acute care use persisted in a secondary analysis using data from 2022 to 2023. Early primary care follow-up was associated with lower acute care use after discharge from PICU hospitalization. Promotion of early primary care follow-up may be warranted after pediatric critical illness.
