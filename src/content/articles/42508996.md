### 重點摘要
這項研究為一項為期12週、隨機、等熱量餵養試驗的後期分析，旨在比較肥胖和糖尿病前期或飲食控制型第二型糖尿病成人中，10小時時間限制飲食（TRE）與16小時常規飲食模式（UEP）對飲食行為、食慾激素和發炎標誌物的影響。研究結果顯示，無論是10小時TRE還是16小時UEP，兩者均在輕微且相當的體重減輕背景下，促進了飲食行為的改善。然而，研究並未發現兩種飲食模式在食慾和發炎指標上有臨床意義的顯著差異。因此，需要更大規模的前瞻性研究來確認是否存在具臨床意義的獨特效益。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究在等熱量餵養的嚴格控制條件下，探討了時間限制飲食對飲食行為、食慾激素和發炎標誌物的影響，為現有文獻提供初步的生理學洞察。然而，由於此為後期分析且樣本量相對有限，未能確認時間限制飲食是否能比常規飲食模式帶來額外且具臨床意義的效益，這突顯了未來需要進行更大規模前瞻性研究的必要性，以驗證這些發現並評估其臨床轉化價值。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Time-Restricted Eating on Appetite and Inflammation in Adults With Obesity and Prediabetes: A Randomized Feeding Study.
**Abstract:** Time-restricted eating (TRE) has gained attention for weight loss and cardiometabolic health improvement. The Time-Restricted Intake of Meals (TRIM) study was a 12-week randomized isocaloric and eucaloric feeding trial comparing 10-h TRE to a 16-h usual eating pattern (UEP) in adults with obesity and either prediabetes or diet-controlled type 2 diabetes. This post hoc analysis investigates TRE's effects on eating behavior, appetite hormones, and inflammatory markers. Participants completed the Three-Factor Eating Questionnaire (Restraint, Disinhibition, Hunger) and provided fasting blood samples for leptin, adiponectin, CRP, cortisol, and sRAGE at pre- and post-intervention. Ghrelin was measured during a 75 g OGTT. Wilcoxon signed rank and Mann-Whitney U tests were used for within- and between-group differences. Thirty-nine participants (19 TRE; 20 UEP) were analyzed, with a mean (SD) age of 60 (7) years and BMI of 35.2 (6.0) kg/m Both 10-h TRE and 16-h UEP promoted improvements in eating behavior in the context of modest and equivalent weight loss. Larger prospective studies are needed to confirm whether clinically meaningful differences exist. ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03527368.
