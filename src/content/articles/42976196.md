### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討患有第一型糖尿病幼兒的家長在接受遠距認知行為介入（REDCHiP）以減少低血糖恐懼症時，其治療前焦慮程度是否會調節治療效果。此為一項隨機臨床試驗的次級分析，比較REDCHiP與注意力控制組，並評估治療前焦慮對三個月後家長低血糖恐懼症的影響。結果顯示，治療前家長焦慮程度並未顯著調節REDCHiP的治療效果。儘管如此，探索性分析提示在臨床焦慮程度較高的家長中，可能存在有意義的改善模式，建議未來研究可進一步針對參與者特徵進行分析以優化介入。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了REDCHiP遠距介入對於低血糖恐懼症的家長中，哪些人能從中獲益最多的知識空白，特別是檢視了治療前家長焦慮程度的調節作用。研究發現治療前焦慮並非顯著調節因子，但強調未來需進一步分析參與者特徵，以精確引導介入的實施並提升治療效益。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Parent Anxiety and Response to a Telehealth Intervention for Hypoglycemia Fear: Secondary Analysis of a Randomized Trial.
**Abstract:** Parents of young children with type 1 diabetes (T1D) frequently report fear of hypoglycemia (FH), which can impact their diabetes management. REDCHiP (Reducing Emotional Distress for Childhood Hypoglycemia in Parents) is a telehealth-delivered cognitive behavioral intervention shown to reduce FH, but less is known about which parents benefit most. This secondary analysis examined whether pretreatment parent anxiety moderated REDCHiP's effects and explored responder patterns by intervention arm and pretreatment anxiety. This was a secondary analysis of a large, randomized clinical trial comparing REDCHiP to an attention control condition (ATTN). We assessed outcomes before treatment (time 1) and 3 months after treatment (time 3). Our primary outcome was parental FH at time 3, adjusted for time 1 FH. The original trial used validated measures of parent FH and general anxiety. We evaluated parent anxiety at time 1 as both a categorical variable (clinically elevated or not) and a continuous variable (centered). We defined responders as parents achieving a reduction of ≥0.5 SDs in FH from time 1 to time 3. An analysis of covariance tested the treatment arm by anxiety interaction, with sensitivity analyses modeling anxiety continuously. As telehealth-delivered behavioral interventions expand in pediatric type 1 diabetes care, secondary analyses of participant characteristics may help guide targeted implementation and enhance treatment benefit.
