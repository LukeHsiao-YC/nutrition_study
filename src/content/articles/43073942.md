### 重點摘要
照顧視障者的非正式照顧者面臨多重挑戰，嚴重影響其生活品質，而既有研究常忽略各決定因素間的複雜互動與未觀察到的異質性。本研究透過對251名照顧者進行多變量分析，運用「相關隨機參數有序機率模型」整合人口統計、社會經濟、臨床、心理及社會因子，以探討其對生活品質的交互影響。結果顯示，中年女性單身照顧者、面臨財務壓力與交通限制者，以及照顧白內障患者或為「垂直型兒子」的照顧者，其生活品質風險較高；主觀健康滿意度則是改善生活品質的最強預測因子。研究強調需制定客製化且針對特定亞群的政策介入措施，如經濟援助、交通補助、支持網絡及心理健康轉介，以提升照顧者福祉及照護可持續性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了過去研究常將決定因素獨立分析，而忽略非正式照顧者生活品質決定因素之間「複雜互動」與「未觀察到的異質性」的研究空白，並超越了單一決定因素模型，採用綜合性多變量分析方法。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** The interplay of factors shaping quality of life among informal caregivers of people with visual impairment: moving beyond single-determinant models.
**Abstract:** Informal caregivers of people with visual impairment (VI) experience multidimensional challenges that substantially affect their quality of life (QoL). Existing literature often examines determinants in isolation and overlooks complex interactions and unobserved heterogeneity. This paper addresses the QoL of caregivers of visually impaired individuals through a comprehensive multivariate analysis that accounts for complex interactions and unobserved heterogeneity among determinants. Data were collected from 251 caregivers of people with VI. Caregiver QoL was analyzed using a "correlated random parameter ordered probit model" to account for "unobserved heterogeneity" and interdependence among determinants. The analysis integrated demographic, socioeconomic, clinical, psychological, and social factors to capture the multidimensional nature of caregiver well-being. Econometric modeling revealed that caregiver QoL was shaped by cumulative, interacting effects rather than isolated influences. Mid-life (35-50 years old), female, and single caregivers experienced significantly higher risks of poor QoL. Random parameters show 57.1% of single caregivers inclining toward lower QoL. Overall, 54.3% of female caregivers were predisposed to lower QoL outcomes. Financial strain and transportation limitations intensified vulnerability, with financial hardship yielding the highest low-category marginal effects of 0.0690 and 0.1068. Moreover, cataracts drove a 42% combined probability of low QoL, outstripping glaucoma (27%). Vertical son caregivers exhibited a 27% combined probability of low QoL compared to 7% for horizontal sisters. Subjective health satisfaction emerged as the strongest predictor, boosting "good" and "very good" QoL by 16.19% and 33.58%. Finally, correlated random parameters revealed substantial unobserved heterogeneity within the latent system. A strong positive correlation (0.651) between singlehood and social relationships demonstrated that spousal support and social networks are tightly intertwined, while a positive correlation (0.167) between the single and female parameters confirmed that single women face compounded, multi-layered vulnerabilities. The findings indicated the need for tailored policy interventions for informal caregivers, including financial assistance, accessible transportation, and structured support networks for single caregivers, routine health assessments, and mental health referral pathways. Addressing caregiver QoL through integrated, subgroup-specific policies will strengthen caregiving sustainability and improve care quality for visually impaired populations.
