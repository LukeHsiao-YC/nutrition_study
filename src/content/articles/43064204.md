### 重點摘要
運動員的營養諮詢技巧多樣，但動機式訪談法 (MI) 在運動營養領域的應用缺乏統合性證據。本系統性回顧採用 PRISMA 方法，從多個資料庫篩選出 9 篇符合條件的研究，評估其質量和偏誤風險。結果顯示所有研究雖包含 MI，但多數研究品質僅為三顆星，且有兩篇存在高偏誤風險。然而，研究指出 MI 對於改善運動員的營養結果，特別是患有飲食失調或相對能量不足症候群的運動員，展現出良好的潛力。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了動機式訪談法 (MI) 在運動營養諮詢中，對改善營養結果的綜合證據空白，儘管現有研究品質和數量仍有待提升。未來的研究應著重於提高研究品質，特別是針對男性團體運動進行隨機對照試驗設計，並單獨探討 MI 作為主要營養諮詢技巧的效用。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Motivational Interviewing for Nutrition Counseling among Athletes: A Systematic Review.
**Abstract:** Athletes can be placed into four unique categories, based on physical intensity. Athletic status impacts the amount time spent on the sport; everyone should be assessed for dietary intake. Registered dietitian nutritionists utilize a standard clinical practice to evaluate goals for athletes during counseling sessions. There are different types of nutrition counseling techniques which can be applied, but there is lacking evidence for Motivational Interviewing (MI). This systematic review explores how MI can impact sports nutrition and what evidence there is to support positive nutrition outcomes. A Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses method was performed in October 2026. The databases for the study included PubMed, Scopus, Science Direct, Cochrane Reviews and web-based search. Inclusion criteria was MI for nutrition counseling, athletes/recreational individuals, sports, for all ages and genders. There were an original search of 894 in total and then this was narrowed down to 9 studies by two researchers using Rayyan software. The RoB 2.0 was used to assess randomized clinical trial risk of bias and the Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool was used to check quality of all the studies in the final review. While all the studies included MI during the nutrition intervention sessions, only one study used MI as the only counseling technique. The duration of the intervention was 4 weeks to 12 months. The most common quality of the studies were three stars and there were two studies which had a high risk of bias. MI as a counseling technique does illustrate promising results for positive nutrition outcomes, especially among athletes who struggle with an eating disorder and/or Relative Energy Deficiency Syndrome. Future studies with higher quality should focus on male team sports, (ie. contact and court sports) and randomized control trial design for MI as a technique for nutrition counseling.
