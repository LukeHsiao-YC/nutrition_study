### 重點摘要
近年來，肉類飲食選擇因涉及倫理、環境與身份認同，已成為高度兩極化的議題，且傳統研究多從個人層面探討。本評論指出，飲食習慣亦是重要的社會身份和意識形態標誌，因此應從「群體間關係」的角度來審視素食者與肉食者之間日益加劇的對立，這可能是減少肉類消費的潛在障礙。文章探討了社會認同、群體間威脅和道德等群體間框架，並討論了地位、權力關係及多重身份等未被充分探討的群體過程。最終，本文建議採取群體層面的策略來減少這種兩極化，以促進社會整體轉向更可持續的食物選擇。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本篇評論填補了將肉類飲食選擇視為單純個人行為的研究空白，強調其作為社會身份與群體間衝突的面向。它提出將社會認同、群體間威脅、道德、地位與權力關係等跨群體視角應用於飲食行為的研究，並指出未來研究應更深入探討這些群體間動態以有效減少社會對立。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Polarized around meat consumption: Conflict between veg*ns and meat-eaters as a potential barrier to meat reduction.
**Abstract:** As dietary choices have become closely tied to ethical, environmental, and identity-related concerns in recent years, decisions about whether to consume or avoid meat have become an increasingly polarizing topic in public discourse. Meat consumption behavior has been typically examined through individual-level frameworks emphasizing personal preferences and motivations, yet dietary practices also serve as meaningful social identities and markers of broader ideological and moral positions, creating conflictual intergroup dynamics. In this commentary, we argue that the increasing polarization between veg*ns (vegan and vegetarian, inclusively) and meat-eaters could be investigated through an explicit intergroup lens. First, we highlight three dominant intergroup frameworks applied to this context and how they explain the relevant groups' perceptions: social identity, intergroup threat, and morality. We then discuss underexplored intergroup processes such as status and power relations, multiple identities, and collective action dynamics. Finally, we suggest possible group-level strategies for the reduction of polarization, which may ultimately facilitate broader societal shifts towards more sustainable food choices. By conceptualizing meat consumption and avoidance as socially embedded intergroup processes rather than merely individual choices, we seek to advance theory and stimulate future research at the intersection of intergroup relations, dietary behavior, and societal change.
