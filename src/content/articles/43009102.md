### 重點摘要
本橫斷面研究旨在探討健康成人中，膳食纖維攝取量與亞臨床胃腸道發炎之間的關聯是否受FUT2基因分泌者狀態影響。研究分析了297名健康成人的飲食、糞便及宏基因組數據，結果顯示，較高纖維攝取量與較低發炎標誌物鈣衛蛋白及黏蛋白降解酶的關聯僅在FUT2分泌者中觀察到，但在非分泌者中未見此效應。這表明膳食纖維對腸道健康的益處可能因個體的FUT2基因型而異。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究揭示了膳食纖維對腸道發炎的影響取決於FUT2基因分泌者狀態，填補了飲食介入效果可能因基因型而異的知識空白。然而，未來的研究限制在於需要進行營養學臨床試驗，以明確非分泌者是否確實對膳食纖維的反應較不敏感。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Cross-Sectional Association between Fiber Intake and Gastrointestinal Inflammation is Dependent on FUT2 Secretor Status in Healthy Adults.
**Abstract:** Gut microbes consume mucins in the absence of dietary fermentable fiber. People with non-secretor alpha-1-2-L-fucosyltransferase 2 (FUT2) genotypes produce gastrointestinal (GI) mucins that lack terminal fucose and have increased risk for inflammatory bowel disease. This study aimed to determine whether associations between fiber intake and subclinical GI inflammation and/or microbiome function are modified by FUT2 genotype in healthy adults. We assessed interactions between fiber intake, FUT2 genotype, and GI inflammation markers in a cross-sectional observational study of healthy adults. Participants with 24-h dietary recalls and food frequency questionnaires to characterize recent and habitual diet, respectively, and fecal samples, plasma samples and/or shotgun metagenomes (n=297) were included in this analysis. Differences in microbiome composition between secretors and non-secretors were analyzed using PERMANOVA and machine learning. A custom-designed TaqMan Higher energy-adjusted total recent fiber, total habitual fiber, and soluble fiber intake was associated with lower calprotectin levels in secretors, but not in non-secretors. Overall microbiome composition did not differ by secretor status. However, the ratio of mucin-degrading to plant-degrading enzymes, Muc2Plant, in the gut microbiome was positively associated with increased fecal calprotectin in secretors, but not in non-secretors. There was no interaction between dietary fiber intake, the FUT2 genotype, and fecal SCFAs. The association of higher dietary fiber intake with lower subclinical GI inflammation and lower mucin-degrading enzymes was observed in secretors, but not in non-secretors. Clinical trials in nutrition are needed to determine if non-secretors are less responsive to dietary fiber.
