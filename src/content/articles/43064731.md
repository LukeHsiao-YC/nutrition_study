### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討MIND飲食模式與東歐人群認知功能及死亡率的關聯。研究分析了15,427名東歐成年人近20年的數據，發現經年齡和性別調整後，MIND飲食依從性與較低的總體和心血管死亡率以及較好的認知表現相關。然而，在全面調整社會人口學和生活方式因素後，MIND飲食與死亡率的關聯不再顯著，僅與語言流暢度呈正相關。結論指出在東歐人群中，MIND飲食對死亡率沒有明確證據，但對認知功能（特別是語言流暢度）的結果與現有文獻大致一致。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了MIND飲食在東歐人群中對認知和死亡率影響的研究空白。然而，研究的限制在於全面調整多重變數後，MIND飲食與死亡率的關聯不再顯著，這可能暗示其他社會經濟和生活方式因素的強烈混淆作用，或該飲食模式在不同人群中的效果異質性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Association of Mediterranean-DASH intervention for neurodegenerative delay (MIND) diet with cognition and total and cardiovascular mortality: results from the HAPIEE cohort.
**Abstract:** The Mediterranean-DASH Intervention for Neurodegenerative Delay (MIND) dietary pattern, developed to slow cognitive decline, has been linked to cardiometabolic benefits and lower mortality. Its associations with cognition and mortality have not been studied in Eastern European populations. This study aimed to assess the relationship between MIND diet, cognition and mortality in Eastern European adults. Data from 15,427 adults aged 45-69 years at baseline in the Czech and Polish cohorts of the Health, Alcohol and Psychosocial factors in Eastern Europe prospective cohort were analyzed. Baseline MIND diet scores (range 0-15), derived from validated food frequency questionnaires, were classified into three groups: low (<6), moderate (6-7), and high (>7). Trained nurses assessed cognitive domains at baseline. Deaths were ascertained via national registries. Multivariable regressions estimated the MIND diet-mortality and MIND diet-cognition associations, adjusting for sociodemographic and lifestyle characteristics. Over about 20 years of follow-up, 3,792 deaths occurred. After controlling for age and sex, MIND diet adherence was associated with lower total and CVD mortality and better cognitive outcomes. However, after full adjustment, the associations were no longer significant (high vs low adherence for all-cause mortality: HR = 1.01, 95%CI:0.93-1.10; for CVD mortality: HR = 1.01, 95%CI:0.88-1.16), and only the positive association with verbal fluency remained significant (high vs low: coefficient 0.058, SE 0.024, p = 0.015). This study of MIND diet in Central and Eastern European cohort did not find clear evidence on mortality, but the results on cognition, particularly verbal fluency, were generally consistent with the literature.
