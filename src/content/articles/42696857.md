### 重點摘要
Nova分類法將超加工食品(UPF)視為應避免的食物，但口服營養補充品(ONS)等特殊醫學用途食品符合UPF所有標準，卻在臨床上被推薦用於治療疾病相關的營養不良，此造成了矛盾。本文旨在探討這種在加工導向框架下受限制但在結果導向框架下卻被處方的張力，並評估ONS的臨床證據，探討其工業加工特性如何在臨床護理中成為治療工具。證據顯示ONS在攝取量和特定營養不良族群的臨床終點上具有益處，而工業加工是實現這些有益成分和情境特性的手段。文章提出一個「情境感知框架」，建議Nova分類法可作為人群層面的參考，但在個體護理中應以營養適足性和臨床目的為核心，以指導未來的指南、法規和溝通。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了Nova食品分類法（側重於食物加工程度）與臨床營養實踐中口服營養補充品（雖為超加工食品但具治療效果）之間的矛盾。它強調了在個體化臨床護理中，需超越單純的加工分類，轉而關注營養適足性和臨床目的的重要性。未來的研究限制可能涉及對於口服營養補充品在某些「硬性結果」（hard outcomes）上的長期效果仍需更多明確證據。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** When ultra-processed food is clinically indicated: Oral nutritional supplements and the limits of the nova paradigm in clinical nutrition.
**Abstract:** The Nova classification organises foods by the extent and purpose of industrial processing, and its public-health corollary, that ultra-processed foods should be minimised, now shapes dietary guidelines and regulation. One clinically important product class challenges this framework. Oral nutritional supplements (ONS) and other foods for special medical purposes meet every Nova criterion for ultra-processed food, being industrial formulations of protein isolates, maltodextrin, refined oils, emulsifiers, stabilisers, flavourings and added micronutrients. Yet these same products are recommended by the European Society for Clinical Nutrition and Metabolism (ESPEN) and other clinical nutrition bodies for the prevention and treatment of disease-related malnutrition. The same product can therefore be discouraged under a processing-based framework yet prescribed under an outcomes-based one. This review examines that tension. It considers why ONS are unambiguously classified as Nova group 4 products, evaluates the clinical evidence, and explores why several product properties viewed as drawbacks at population level become therapeutic tools in clinical care. The evidence supports intake-level benefits consistently and clinical-endpoint benefits in defined malnourished populations, while remaining mixed for several hard outcomes; legitimate cautions about overreliance, fibre and dietary displacement deserve engagement rather than dismissal. The properties delivering benefit are compositional and contextual, with industrial processing serving as the means by which they are achieved. A context-aware framework is proposed that retains Nova as a population-level signal while making nutritional adequacy and clinical purpose the governing axes for individual care, with implications for guidelines, regulation and communication.
