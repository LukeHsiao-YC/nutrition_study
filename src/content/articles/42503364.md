### 重點摘要
健康素養（HL）作為公共衛生基石，其研究長期分化為強調測量個體特徵的流行病學視角，與側重社區賦權的健康促進視角。這種分野導致研究與政策偏重可量化的功能性健康素養，而忽略對促進健康公平至關重要的批判性健康素養。本文主張這兩種視角實為互補且相互強化，流行病學提供風險人群識別與進度基準數據，而健康促進則提供情境化介入的框架。因此，整合這些視角對於構建更回應性、以人為本且公平的健康系統，並實現全民健康覆蓋等廣泛公共衛生目標至關重要。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了健康素養領域中，流行病學與健康促進兩大視角長期分離且導致研究與政策偏誤的研究空白。它提出一個創新的整合框架，旨在將原被視為相互對立的觀點轉化為互補且相互強化的關係，從而克服實踐中的挑戰，並推動更全面且公平的公共衛生策略。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** A Tale of Two Perspectives on Health Literacy: Epidemiological and Health Promotion Approaches.
**Abstract:** Health literacy (HL) is a foundational concept in public health, yet the field remains divided between two primary perspectives. The epidemiological perspective treats HL as a measurable, individual-level characteristic for population surveillance, while the health promotion perspective frames it as a dynamic, context-dependent measure for community empowerment. This current issue argues that this divide is not merely academic but has significant pragmatic consequences, often leading to a research and policy prioritization of what is easily measurable, namely, functional health literacy over what is fundamentally meaningful for advancing health equity, such as critical health literacy. This viewpoint proposes that these two perspectives are not mutually exclusive but are, in fact, complementary and mutually reinforcing. The epidemiological approach provides the essential surveillance data to identify at-risk populations and benchmark progress, while the health promotion approach offers the methodological framework for developing contextually appropriate, empowering interventions. Synthesizing these views into an integrated framework is therefore essential, enabling the creation of more responsive, person-centered, and equitable health systems that are capable of achieving broader public health objectives, including Universal Health Coverage.
