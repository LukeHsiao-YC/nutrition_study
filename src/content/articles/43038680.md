### 重點摘要
這項國際性真實世界研究長期追蹤269位成人短腸症併發慢性腸道衰竭患者使用teduglutide的療效、安全性與治療模式。結果顯示，約三分之一的患者成功脫離居家靜脈營養，而約一半未脫離的患者其靜脈補充液需求減少至少20%。治療效果與患者的基線營養狀態、靜脈補充液需求和短腸症解剖構造相關，但與治療起始時間無關。約四成的藥物停用歸因於不良事件，導管相關血流感染率雖在治療期間較高，但未發現teduglutide暴露與其有獨立的統計學關聯。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了teduglutide在成人短腸症併發慢性腸道衰竭患者中，長期、國際性真實世界療效、安全性與治療軌跡的數據空白。研究提供了關鍵的療效預測因子，並釐清了治療起始時間對效果的影響。然而，關於結腸連續性的預測作用在敏感性分析中失去統計學意義，這可能是一個需要未來研究進一步確認的限制或方向。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effectiveness, safety and treatment trajectory of teduglutide in adults with short bowel syndrome and intestinal failure: A long-term international real-world survey.
**Abstract:** Several real-world studies evaluated teduglutide in patients with short bowel syndrome-associated chronic intestinal failure (SBS-CIF). To further characterize the drug's real-world effectiveness, safety and use, the cohort of 269 adult patients with SBS-CIF treated with teduglutide enrolled in an international multicenter database between 2015 and 2022 was followed through 2024. Effectiveness outcomes (intention-to-treat-like analysis, ITT): weaning-from-home parenteral nutrition (HPN), intravenous supplementation (IVS) volume reduction ≥20%/week from teduglutide initiation (baseline). Safety outcomes: discontinuation due to adverse events (AEs), HPN/CIF-related major complications. Treatment trajectory and predictors of outcomes were assessed. Sensitivity analyses were performed to assess the robustness of the ITT analysis. As of March 1 2024, the outcome was known for 229 patients: weaning-from-HPN 84 (36.6%), IVS reduction ≥20%/week 52 of 108 patients remaining on HPN with available data (48.1%). Baseline predictors of weaning-from-HPN: higher BMI (p = 0.003), lower IVS volume (p = 0.032), longer remnant small bowel (p < 0.001), colon in continuity (p = 0.050). Predictor of IVS reduction ≥20%/week: higher IVS volume (p = 0.008). Timing of teduglutide initiation after SBS onset did not influence its effectiveness. All findings from the ITT analysis were confirmed by the sensitivity analyses, except for the potential predictive role of colon-in-continuity, which was no longer statistically significant (p = 0.089) when weaning events unrelated to teduglutide were reclassified. Treatment was discontinued in 60 (26.2%); AEs accounted for 38.4% discontinuations. Catheter-related-bloodstream-infection (CRBSI) rate was higher during periods of teduglutide treatment, but patient's calendar year of follow up was the only factor independently associated with CRBSI rate. In SBS-CIF receiving teduglutide, weaning-from-HPN occurred in approximately one-third of patients and IVS reduction ≥20% in one-half of not-weaned. Drug effectiveness was associated with baseline nutritional status, IVS requirement and SBS anatomy, but not with timing of post-resection treatment initiation. AEs accounted for approximately 40% of drug discontinuations. No statistically significant independent association between teduglutide exposure and CRBSI rate was detected.
