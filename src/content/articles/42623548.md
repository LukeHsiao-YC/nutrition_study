### 重點摘要
鑒於嬰兒猝死是美國新生兒死亡的主要原因，而安全睡眠與母乳餵養能降低風險，本研究旨在探討準媽媽與新手媽媽對於使用Facebook私人群組獲取嬰兒照護（特別是睡眠與餵養）教育資訊的接受度。這項描述性定性研究招募了來自兩個州的22名WIC客戶進行深度訪談，並透過歸納法分析其對此平台使用的看法。結果顯示，所有參與者均認可或實際使用Facebook私人群組獲取嬰兒照護指導，並將其主要用於資訊尋求、參與體驗和社會連結。研究結論支持Facebook私人群組作為向母親提供嬰兒照護資訊的有效平台，其洞察可用於設計有效的教育介入措施。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究透過探討準媽媽與新手媽媽對Facebook私人群組作為嬰兒照護教育平台的接受度與使用經驗，填補了社群媒體在健康教育介入中應用的定性研究空白。然而，本研究主要為描述性定性研究，並未評估此類介入的實際效果或行為改變，未來的研究可著重於開發並測試基於此平台的教育介入措施的有效性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Expectant and New Mothers' Use of Facebook Private Groups to Learn About Infant Sleep and Feeding Practices: Descriptive Qualitative Study.
**Abstract:** Sudden and unexpected infant death is a leading cause of postneonatal death in the United States. Following recommended infant care practices such as safe sleep and breastfeeding reduces the risk of sudden and unexpected infant death. Parents use social media to help them make decisions about infant care practices. The objective of our qualitative study was to explore mothers' perspectives on the acceptability of Facebook private groups for providing education on infant care practices. We conducted a descriptive qualitative study that recruited a purposeful sample of pregnant persons and mothers who recently gave birth; all were clients of the Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children in 1 of 2 states. We conducted in-depth interviews that were analyzed inductively through a systematic, iterative process using an interview guide. We asked participants about their perspectives regarding the use of Facebook private groups for learning about infant care practices, including sleep practices and breastfeeding. Data were collected until data sufficiency was reached. Of the 22 participants, 13 (59.1%) were Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children clients from Rhode Island, and 9 (40.9%) were from Virginia. While participants' use of Facebook in general varied, all either used Facebook private groups to obtain infant care practice guidance about safe sleep and breastfeeding or liked the idea of using specifically Facebook private groups to disseminate this information to pregnant persons and new parents. Participants reported using Facebook private groups for three main purposes: (1) information seeking, (2) the experience of engaging with Facebook private groups, and (3) social connectedness. Facebook private groups were endorsed by the participants for providing mothers with information about infant care practices. Participants outlined their reasons for and experience with the use of Facebook private groups, which can inform effective use of the platform for educational interventions.
