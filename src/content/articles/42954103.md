### 重點摘要
本研究評估治療性血漿置換（TPE）對鵝膏蕈中毒引起的兒童急性肝衰竭（AT-PALF）的療效。這是一項回溯性、國際多中心隊列研究，納入2013年至2024年間30名兒童AT-PALF患者，比較TPE組（17例）與標準治療組（13例）的28天免移植存活率。結果顯示，TPE組與標準治療組在免移植存活率上沒有顯著差異（47% vs. 62%, p=0.63），且患者預後主要由肝衰竭嚴重程度和多重器官功能障礙決定。因此，輔助性TPE未能明顯改善兒童AT-PALF的免移植存活率。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了鵝膏蕈毒素引起兒童急性肝衰竭（AT-PALF）中使用治療性血漿置換（TPE）的療效證據空白，顯示輔助性TPE未能顯著改善免移植存活率。然而，由於這是一項回溯性、小規模（30名患者）的觀察性研究，未來需要更大規模、前瞻性的研究，特別是隨機對照試驗，來進一步確認TPE的潛在益處或重新評估其在特定亞組患者中的作用，並深入探討預後指標對治療介入時機的影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Therapeutic plasma exchange in Amatoxin-associated pediatric acute liver failure-Results from the Amanita-PEX study.
**Abstract:** Pediatric acute liver failure (PALF) is a rare but life-threatening condition. Amanita poisoning can lead to PALF with rapid clinical deterioration. Therapeutic plasma exchange (TPE) has been proposed as a potential supportive intervention and is established in adult acute liver failure. Evidence regarding TPE in Amanita toxin-induced PALF (AT-PALF) remains scarce, and clinical practice varies across liver transplant centers. The Amanita-PEX study is a retrospective, international, multicenter cohort study including pediatric patients with AT-PALF treated between 2013 and 2024. The combined primary endpoint was 28-day transplant-free survival. A total of 30 patients from 10 centers were included; 17 received at least one session of TPE, and 13 were treated with standard-of-care (SOC) alone. Baseline characteristics were comparable between groups. The overall 28-day transplant-free survival rate was 53.3% (16/30), with a 28-day mortality of 10% (3/30). All patients received N-acetylcysteine, and 93% were treated with silibinin. Native liver survival did not differ significantly between the TPE and SOC groups (47% vs. 62%, p = 0.63). In contrast, transplant-free survival was primarily associated with established markers of liver failure severity and organ dysfunction, including MELD and LIU scores, the degree of hepatic encephalopathy, acute kidney injury, and the need for vasopressors or mechanical ventilation. After adjusting for these prognostic markers, TPE remained unassociated with improved outcomes. In this real-world cohort of children with AT-PALF, adjunctive TPE showed no clear improvement in transplant-free survival. Outcomes were predominantly determined by the severity of liver injury and multi-organ dysfunction. ClinicalTrials.gov identifier: NCT06187220, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/.
