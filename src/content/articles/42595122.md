### 重點摘要
這項多隊列研究旨在探討童年或成年時期嚴重營養不良是否會導致長期外分泌胰臟功能不全或結構異常。研究追蹤了印度、菲律賓、坦尚尼亞和尚比亞的2,165名曾營養不良和未曾營養不良的參與者，評估其糞便彈性蛋白酶、循環胰蛋白酶原和胰臟形態。結果顯示，曾營養不良者與未曾營養不良者在外分泌胰臟功能不全的發生率上沒有顯著差異，儘管成人胰臟體積較大而兒童較小，且胰臟大小在亞洲和非洲隊列間存在差異。因此，研究結論指出過去嚴重的營養不良並未導致晚年外分泌胰臟功能障礙，但強調了對早期營養不良的長期代謝後果進行進一步研究的必要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了關於嚴重營養不良對長期外分泌胰臟功能和結構影響的知識空白，透過大規模多隊列研究明確指出嚴重的營養不良並不會導致晚年的外分泌胰臟功能不全。然而，研究結果顯示胰臟大小在成人和兒童中呈現不同變化，且在不同地域隊列間存在差異，這突顯了未來需要針對早期營養不良在不同族群中的長期代謝和營養後果進行更深入研究的需求，特別是探索這些差異背後的潛在因素。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Pancreatic exocrine function and morphology following prior malnutrition in Asian and African cohorts: A multi-cohort study.
**Abstract:** Studies of survivors of large twentieth century famines suggest persistent after-effects on metabolism and nutrient handling, including abnormal endocrine and exocrine pancreatic functions, which play key roles in nutrient digestion and absorption. We tested the hypothesis that severe wasting malnutrition in childhood or adulthood leads to long-term exocrine pancreatic insufficiency (EPI) and/or structural abnormalities. We followed up participants from six cohorts of previously malnourished (PM) and not previously malnourished (NPM) adults and children in India, the Philippines, Tanzania, and Zambia. Faecal elastase (FE-1), circulating trypsinogen, amylase and lipase assays were performed. Ultrasound scans (USS) were conducted in all cohorts, and a subset of adults underwent Computed Tomography scan (CT) to estimate pancreatic volume. Statistical analyses included Kruskal-Wallis tests and logistic regression to estimate ORs and 95% CIs. A total of 2,165 participants had assessments of pancreatic structure or exocrine function: 1,799 (83%) had faecal elastase-1 (FE-1) measured, 1,761 (81%) underwent USS, and 329 (15%) had CT. Low FE-1 (below 200μg/g) was found in 252 (32%) of 791 previously malnourished (PM) and 346 (34%) of 1,008 not previously malnourished (NPM participants) (OR 0.89; 95%CI 0.73, 1.10; P=0.29). Radiological measurements of the pancreas were consistently higher in PM than NPM adult participants when adjusted for body weight; this association was reversed in children. Pancreatic volume was apparently higher, and FE-1 lower, in African compared to Asian cohorts, which was not due to inflammation or human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) infection. Circulating trypsinogen was associated with pancreatic size only after adjusting for body weight. In this large multi-cohort study, prior severe malnutrition did not result in exocrine pancreatic dysfunction later in life, though pancreatic size was increased in adults, reduced in children, and varied between cohorts. These findings highlight the need for further research into the long-term metabolic and nutritional consequences of early-life malnutrition across diverse populations.
