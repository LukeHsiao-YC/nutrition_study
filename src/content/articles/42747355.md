### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估加拿大安大略省在單一賽事體育博弈和線上賭博合法化後，因賭博疾患前往急診的變化。這項以人群為基礎的中斷時間序列研究，分析了2012年至2025年間共952例急診就診數據。結果顯示，在線上賭博自由化後，急診就診率整體增加了31.3%，並持續以每季度7%的速度增長，預計至2025年將比預期高出99.0%。此增長主要發生在年輕男性（10-29歲及30-44歲），分別增加154.0%和116.0%，女性則無顯著變化。研究結論指出，線上賭博的擴張與嚴重賭博疾患的急診就診率翻倍有關，主要影響年輕男性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了北美地區線上賭博擴張對賭博相關危害影響理解不足的研究空白，透過具體分析急診就診數據，量化了此類政策變化的後果。然而，急診就診率的增加雖然顯著，但整體案件數仍屬罕見，這可能反映了更廣泛的賭博危害的冰山一角，未能完全捕捉到所有層面的社會影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Emergency Department Visits for Gambling Disorders After the Legalization of Single-Event Sports Betting and Rapid Expansion of Online Gambling.
**Abstract:** Online gambling and single-event sports betting have been legalized and expanded in North America. Although these changes have raised concerns over increased gambling-related harms, the impacts of gambling expansion are not well understood. This study examined changes in gambling-related emergency department visits after the legalization of single-event sports betting and privatized online gambling in Ontario, Canada. This was a population-based study of all individuals in Ontario, Canada, aged ≥10 years, who were eligible for universal health coverage (N=17,353,640) between January 2012 and June 2025. An interrupted time series design estimated changes in the rates of emergency department visits for gambling disorders after online gambling liberalization in April 2022, overall and by age and sex. Over 14 years, there were 952 emergency department visits for gambling disorders from 757 unique individuals (mean [SD] age=41.8 [14.9] years; 72.0% male). The mean quarterly rates of emergency department visits for gambling disorders were 31.3% higher during the period after liberalization than before (1.28-1.68 per 1,000,000 individuals), with increases observed in males (1.81-2.63 per 1,000,000) but not in females (0.77-0.74 per 1,000,000). Visits were stable before liberalization, showed no immediate change on implementation, but began to increase by 7% per quarter (incidence rate ratio=1.07, 95% CI=1.04, 1.10). By the end of the study period (in April-June 2025), the rate of emergency department visits for gambling disorders was estimated to be 99.0% (incidence rate ratio=1.99, 95% CI=1.48, 2.67) higher overall and 154.0% (incidence rate ratio=2.54, 95% CI=1.15, 5.63) and 116.0% (incidence rate ratio=2.16, 95% CI=1.18, 3.95) higher in males aged 10-29 years and 30-44 years, respectively, than the expected rates without gambling market liberalization. Online gambling expansion with legal single-event sports betting was associated with a doubling of the rates of severe cases of gambling disorders, with increases occurring only in young men. Although such visits were rare, they may reflect broader population increases in gambling harms.
