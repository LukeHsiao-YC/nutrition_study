### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討住院期間造血幹細胞移植 (HCT) 病患的營養攝取充足性與臨床結果（住院天數、30天再入院率、急性移植物抗宿主病）之間的獨立關聯。這是一項為期12個月的回溯性世代研究，分析了283名成人HCT患者的電子病歷，並將營養攝取量分為充足 (≥75% 估計需求) 或不足 (<75%)。結果顯示，在自體HCT患者中，充足的營養攝取與較短的住院天數顯著相關，而出院時的營養不良則與較長的住院天數相關；但在異體HCT患者中未發現顯著關聯，且兩組病患的營養攝取均與30天再入院率或急性移植物抗宿主病無關。因此，優化自體HCT患者的營養狀況可能改善預後並縮短住院時間。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究澄清了自體造血幹細胞移植患者中營養攝取充足與住院天數縮短的獨立關聯，填補了該領域部分研究空白。然而，對於異體移植患者的臨床預後，以及所有HCT患者的再入院率和急性移植物抗宿主病，營養攝取充足性與這些結果的獨立關聯仍未被證實，這提示未來研究需進一步探索或考量其他影響因素。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Association of Nutrition Intake Adequacy with Clinical Outcomes in Hospitalized Hematopoietic Cell Transplant Patients: A Retrospective Cohort Study.
**Abstract:** Patients undergoing hematopoietic cell transplantation (HCT) face nutrition-impacting symptoms that compromise oral intake and increase malnutrition risk. The independent relationship between nutrition intake adequacy and clinical outcomes in this population remains incompletely characterized. To determine whether adequate intake (≥75% of estimated needs) during HCT admission is independently associated with hospital length of stay (LOS), 30-day readmission, and acute graft-versus-host disease (GVHD). Twelve-month retrospective cohort study using electronic medical records between June 15, 2022 - June 15, 2023. Nutrition intake was averaged across admission from registered dietitian assessments and categorized as adequate (≥75%) or inadequate (<75%). /Setting: 283 adults (85 autologous, 198 allogeneic) aged ≥18 years admitted to Stanford Health Care for HCT with a length of stay ≥7 days. Hospital LOS, 30-day readmission, and acute GVHD within 100 days. Multivariable linear regression for LOS and logistic regression for readmission and GVHD, each adjusted for age, gender, race/ethnicity, and BMI. In autologous HCT patients, adequate intake was independently associated with shorter LOS (β = -1.91 days, 95% CI -3.33 to -0.48; P = 0.009), and malnutrition at discharge with longer LOS (β = 1.43 days, 95% CI 0.02 to 2.91; P = 0.04). Sensitivity analyses for autologous HCT patients with adequate intake, excluding patients who received enteral or parenteral nutrition support, did not alter these findings (β = -1.51 days, 95% CI -2.99 to -0.03; P = 0.048). In allogeneic patients, no significant associations were found between intake and LOS after adjustment. Intake was not associated with 30-day readmission or acute GVHD in either group. Among autologous HCT patients, adequate intake was associated with shorter LOS and discharge malnutrition with longer LOS. Optimizing nutritional status may help improve outcomes and reduce hospitalization in this population.
