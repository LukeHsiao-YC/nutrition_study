### 重點摘要
這項質性研究旨在探討青少年父親在產前照護服務中對父職的感知，以因應青少年懷孕的公共健康議題及父職對年輕男性身分認同的重要性。研究在巴西一所三級醫院招募了14名19歲以下的準青少年父親，透過深度訪談、社會人口學問卷及田野日記進行數據收集與內容分析。研究歸納出四個主題類別，包括他們對懷孕的反應與父職概念、對嬰兒的想像及未來期望、與伴侶關係的變化，以及社交圈對父職發展的影響。研究強調將青少年父親納入醫療保健措施的重要性，尤其在家庭計畫、親子連結、社會支持網絡及對青少年母親的協助等方面，產前照護有潛力提供全面性支持與指導。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究透過質性方法，填補了對青少年父親在產前照護情境中如何感知父職的深入理解不足。它揭示了該群體在身分認同轉變期的挑戰與需求，為整合青少年父親至醫療保健體系提供了具體方向。未來的研究可考慮擴大地理範圍或採用混合方法以驗證這些發現，並探討不同文化背景下的青少年父親經驗。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** When Adolescents Become Dads: A Clinical-Qualitative Study on the Experience of Male Adolescents Attending a Prenatal Care Service.
**Abstract:** Adolescent pregnancy has garnered public interest due to its implications for health, social dynamics, and economic outcomes. Fatherhood contributes to the formation of the identity of young men, particularly during their transition to adulthood. Prenatal care presents a valuable opportunity to involve adolescent fathers in parenting and supporting their pregnant partners. This study aims to investigate how adolescent fathers perceive fatherhood while attending a specialized prenatal clinic. This qualitative study focused on 14 expectant adolescent fathers aged up to 19 years, recruited from a specialized prenatal care clinic at a tertiary hospital in Brazil. Employing a purposive sampling strategy, we utilized in-depth interviews, sociodemographic questionnaires, and field diaries for data collection. Data analysis involved content analysis, contextualized within the framework of Health Psychology. We raised four categories: (1) "Myself, a dad?!": reactions to a positive pregnancy test and the young men's concepts of fatherhood; (2) The imagined relationship with the baby and expectations for the future; (3) The new dynamics in the relationship with the female adolescent during pregnancy; and (4) The influence of the social circles on the development of fatherhood: the family of origin and the friends. The findings underscore the importance of integrating adolescent fathers into healthcare initiatives, addressing critical life-course topics such as family planning, bonding with the child, social support networks, and assistance for adolescent women. These insights highlight the potential for prenatal care settings to offer comprehensive support and guidance to young men navigating their parental roles.
