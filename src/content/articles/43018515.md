### 重點摘要
為評估SNAP-Ed烹飪課程對符合資格大學生烹飪與食物技能的影響，本研究採用準實驗設計，分析來自加州州立大學系統11個校區、124名參與者於2021至2023年間的課前與課後數據。結果顯示，參與者在規劃食物採購量、了解食物預算、利用營養建議平衡膳食及用少量食材準備健康餐點方面，技能顯著提升。然而，餐點提前規劃的信心在課前與課後皆為最低。整體而言，此類烹飪導向的營養課程有助於改善大學生的食物資源管理技能。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究證實了針對大學生的烹飪導向營養介入，能有效提升其食物資源管理技能，特別是在食物預算與健康餐點準備方面，為此類教育計劃在大學生群體中的成效提供了實證資料。然而，研究也指出參與者在「提前規劃膳食」方面的信心改善不明顯，暗示此方面仍是未來介入需加強或深入研究的方向。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Evaluating the Impact of SNAP-Ed Classes on College Students' Cooking and Food Skills.
**Abstract:** To assess changes in cooking and food skills among college students eligible for the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program in a cooking-focused nutrition intervention. A pretest-posttest design was used to evaluate program outcomes among participants (N = 124) on 11 campuses in the California State University system from 2021 to 2023. The Cooking and Food Skills Assessment measured skills in cooking techniques, meal planning, grocery shopping, and managing food budgets. Paired-samples t tests were used to investigate changes in mean scores for each cooking and food skills item. Participants showed significant improvements in skills in planning how much food to buy, knowing one's food budget, balancing meals using nutrition advice, and preparing healthy meals with few ingredients. Participants reported the lowest confidence in planning meals ahead of time at both pretest and posttest. Cooking-focused nutrition programming for college students may improve food resource management skills.
