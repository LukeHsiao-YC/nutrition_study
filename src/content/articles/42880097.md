### 重點摘要
歐洲臨床營養與代謝學會（ESPEN）於2017年啟動營養醫學教育計畫（NEMS），旨在解決醫學院營養教育不足的問題。2019年，NEMS工作小組發布立場文件，強調營養教育對醫學生的重要性，並定義了基礎、應用與臨床三大領域的課程內容。隨後，NEMS專案小組開發了「NEMS-slide kit」教學工具，該工具於2024年1月完成，包含196張涵蓋三大營養領域的投影片。此實用性方法與標準化教材的開發，有望促進營養學在歐洲醫學課程中的廣泛整合。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了醫學院缺乏標準化且全面的營養教育教材的空白，透過開發「NEMS-slide kit」，為醫學生提供了一套實用且易於整合的教學工具。然而，未來的研究限制可能包括評估此教學工具在不同醫學院校的實際推廣成效與對學生學習成果的影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Nutrition education in medical schools: From the ESPEN NEMS position paper to a practical teaching tool.
**Abstract:** Nutrition education represents a cornerstone in the training of healthcare professionals, particularly physicians, as it contributes to the optimization of therapeutic strategies for nutritional disorders. In 2017, the European Society for Clinical Nutrition and Metabolism (ESPEN) launched the Nutrition Education in Medical Schools (NEMS) Project with the aim of implementing nutrition education in all medical schools. In 2019, the NEMS working group published its first position paper, underscoring the need for mandatory nutrition education for all medical students and outlining the essential content that should form the core of undergraduate medical training in human nutrition. The document identified three key domains (basic, applied/or public health and clinical nutrition) and recommended their integration through flexible curricular models, competency-based teaching, and innovative learning tools. A NEMS task force was established, composed of 20 members including medical doctors, dietitians, and ESPEN representatives, with the objective of preparing the "NEMS-slide kit", a teaching tool designed to enhance and facilitate its integration into the standard medical school curriculum. The group defined learning objectives and standard operating procedures, assigning topics to members to prepare English slide sets with simple language, visuals, and commentary for professors. A unified template was designed, and after iterative revisions the final slide kit was completed in May 2023. In January 2024, the "NEMS-slide kit" was developed, comprising 196 slides that encompass the three major domains of nutrition: basic, applied, and clinical nutrition. The pragmatic approach of the NEMS project and the development of standardized teaching materials could support the widespread integration of nutrition into medical curricula across Europe.
