### 重點摘要
這項隨機對照試驗的次級分析，旨在探討添加益生菌和維生素D3的柳橙汁與未強化柳橙汁對高心血管代謝風險個體血糖代謝的影響。為期八週的雙盲試驗中，50名受試者每日飲用250毫升指定飲品。研究結果顯示，與未強化柳橙汁相比，每日飲用強化柳橙汁並未在血糖代謝方面帶來顯著改善。然而，研究亦指出無論是否強化，柳橙汁的攝取可能對血糖波動具有正面影響。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究評估了特定益生菌和維生素D3強化柳橙汁對高心血管代謝風險個體血糖代謝的潛在益處，填補了該特定配方在血糖控制方面的研究空白。然而，由於強化柳橙汁未能顯示出相較於未強化柳橙汁的顯著優勢，未來的研究可能需要探索不同的益生菌菌株、劑量、維生素D3水平或更長期的介入，以評估其對血糖代謝的具體影響。

**Title:** Effects of orange juice enriched with probiotics and vitamin D3 on glucose metabolism in high-cardiometabolic risk individuals: secondary outcomes of a randomised controlled trial.
**Abstract:** This secondary analysis of a randomised controlled trial (RCT) compared the effects of 100% orange juice (OJ) enriched with probiotics and vitamin D3 on glucose metabolism to unfortified OJ in high-cardiometabolic risk individuals. This 8-week double-blinded RCT included two groups consuming 250 mL daily: unfortified OJ (control) and OJ with vitamin D3 (2000 IU) and probiotics (Lacticaseibacillus rhamnosus GG and Lacticaseibacillus casei Shirota, 10 Fifty individuals (17 males, 34%; 48.7 years; body mass index = 32 kg/m Daily consumption of enriched OJ did not lead to significant changes in glucose metabolism compared to the unfortified OJ. However, the OJ intake, with or without fortification, may have favorable effects on glycemic fluctuations. This study was a part of FunJuice-T2EDK-01922, which was funded by the EU Regional Development Fund and Greek National Resources.
