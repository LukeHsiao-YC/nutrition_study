### 重點摘要
餐後高三酸甘油酯是心血管疾病的風險因子。本研究旨在探討對於15位有阻力訓練經驗的年輕男性，在相同運動量和強度的全身阻力運動中，不同組間休息時間對餐後三酸甘油酯濃度的急性影響。結果顯示，無論是空腹或餐後三酸甘油酯濃度，在各試驗組間均未觀察到顯著差異。因此，研究結論指出，在有阻力訓練經驗的年輕男性中，全身阻力運動的不同組間休息時間並未降低餐後三酸甘油酯濃度。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究明確指出，在維持阻力運動總量與強度一致的前提下，調整組間休息時間並不能有效降低有阻力訓練經驗年輕男性的餐後三酸甘油酯濃度，這填補了該特定條件下運動處方對血脂影響的空白。未來的研究可能需要探索其他運動參數、不同人群或長期效果。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effect of rest interval time during resistance exercise on postprandial triglyceride concentrations in resistance-trained young men.
**Abstract:** Elevated postprandial triglyceride (TG) is a risk factor for cardiovascular disease. This study investigated the acute effect of whole-body resistance exercise with different rest interval durations, but identical volume and intensity, on postprandial TG concentrations. Fifteen healthy men (age: 23.1 ± 3.4 years; BMI: 24 ± 3 kg/m Participants lifted a total volume of 6433 ± 1554 kg on each exercise trial. However, no significant differences were observed in fasting (P = 0.061) or postprandial (main effects of trial, P = 0.147; trial-by-time interaction P = 0.058) TG concentrations among trials. Whole-body resistance exercise with different rest intervals did not reduce postprandial TG concentration in resistance-trained young men.
