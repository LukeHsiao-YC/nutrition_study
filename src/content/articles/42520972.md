### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討不同加工方式的豌豆蛋白與乳清蛋白對健康成人餐後代謝的影響，以填補植物蛋白消化性及代謝效應數據不足的空白。研究採隨機交叉試驗，比較了擠壓豌豆蛋白(PE)、非擠壓豌豆濃縮蛋白(PC)、非擠壓豌豆分離蛋白(PI)與乳清蛋白(WP)的餐後血漿必需胺基酸(EAA)反應。結果顯示，所有豌豆蛋白的餐後EAA濃度均低於乳清蛋白，且擠壓豌豆蛋白的EAA反應有延遲現象，但其他代謝參數和飽足感則與乳清蛋白相似。因此，豌豆蛋白引起的餐後EAA反應低於乳清蛋白，且加工方式會影響EAA吸收的時程。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了市場上日益增加的植物蛋白成分，特別是不同加工方式的豌豆蛋白，在人體內的消化率及代謝效應數據有限的研究空白。它提供了豌豆蛋白與乳清蛋白相比的餐後必需胺基酸反應的詳細資料，並揭示了擠壓加工對胺基酸吸收動力學的影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Delayed plasma amino acid response following ingestion of extruded pea protein compared with non-extruded pea proteins in healthy adults: a randomized crossover trial.
**Abstract:** Despite the growing availability of plant protein ingredients on the market, data on their digestibility and metabolic effects in humans are limited. This study examined the effects of differently processed pea proteins on postprandial metabolism and compared them with those of whey protein. In a randomized crossover trial, healthy men and women (n=20, aged 24 ± 3 y, BMI 21.6 ±1.8 kg/m Plasma essential amino acid (EAA) concentrations increased after ingestion of all test meals. Compared with WP, meals containing PC, PI, and PE resulted in lower plasma EAA concentrations (iAUC In healthy young adults, pea protein elicits a lower postprandial EAA response than WP, independent of processing degree. Comparisons across processing degrees indicate a delayed EAA response following PE consumption. Other metabolic parameters and satiety responses are comparable between pea and WP. This trial was registered at ClinicalTrials.gov (https://clinicaltrials.gov/) as NCT06155656, date of registration: 30 November 2023.
