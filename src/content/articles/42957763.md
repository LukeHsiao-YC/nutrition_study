### 重點摘要
骨關節炎被視為一種全身性疾病，而非局部軟骨問題，其涉及腸道微生物、肌肉骨骼組織與關節微環境間的複雜交互作用。本文提出「腸道-肌肉-關節 (GMJ) 軸」作為一個整合性框架，連結了微生物失調、慢性炎症和代謝失調，進而影響軟骨穩態、肌肉力量和神經肌肉穩定性。GMJ軸的紊亂會加劇氧化壓力、粒線體功能障礙和細胞外基質降解。理解此軸為透過益生菌、後生元、AMPK-SIRT1活化劑和運動等方式，來恢復微生物群、重塑代謝和改善神經肌肉健康，從而預防和治療骨關節炎提供新的精準策略。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本文提出「腸道-肌肉-關節 (GMJ) 軸」作為一個整合性框架，填補了將骨關節炎視為單純局部性疾病的觀念空白，並將腸道、肌肉與關節的複雜交互作用連結起來，提供更全面的系統性視角。未來的研究可著重於驗證此軸的具體機制，並透過臨床試驗評估其建議的介入策略（如益生菌、代謝活化劑和運動）在預防和治療骨關節炎上的實證效果。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** The gut-muscle-joint axis in osteoarthritis: Mechanistic insights and therapeutic perspectives.
**Abstract:** Emerging evidence highlights a complex crosstalk between the gut microbiota, musculoskeletal tissues, and the joint microenvironment, revealing osteoarthritis (OA) as a systemic disorder rather than a localized cartilage disease. The gut-muscle-joint interaction integrates microbial dysbiosis, chronic inflammation, and metabolic dysregulation to influence cartilage homeostasis, muscle strength, and neuromuscular stability. Building upon previously described gut-osteoarthritis and gut-muscle interactions, the Gut-Muscle-Joint (GMJ) axis is presented as an integrative framework linking intestinal permeability, microbial metabolites, systemic inflammatory signaling, neuromuscular health, skeletal muscle function, and joint integrity. Perturbations in this axis amplify oxidative stress, mitochondrial dysfunction, and extracellular matrix degradation across musculoskeletal tissues. Understanding GMJ axis-driven mechanisms offers novel preventive and therapeutic strategies that target microbiome restoration, metabolic reprogramming, and neuromuscular health through probiotics, postbiotics, AMPK-SIRT1 activators, and structured exercise. This integrative perspective positions the GMJ axis as a unifying framework for intervention, enabling precision approaches to preserve joint function, reduce sarcopenia, and attenuate OA progression.
