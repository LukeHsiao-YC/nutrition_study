### 重點摘要
本研究旨在填補多區域縱向研究的空白，探討老年人衰弱狀態及其變化趨勢與認知功能障礙的關聯。研究結合來自美國、英國、歐洲、中國和韓國五個大型世代研究的數據，分析基線衰弱狀態與衰弱軌跡對認知功能障礙風險的影響。結果顯示，無論是基線的衰弱前期或衰弱狀態，以及隨著時間推移呈現較高衰弱軌跡的參與者，其罹患認知功能障礙的風險均顯著升高。這項發現強調了早期衰弱篩查與檢測在老年人群認知功能障礙風險分層和管理中的重要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究填補了先前缺乏跨多區域、多世代的縱向研究，以檢視老年人衰弱狀態及其轉變對認知功能障礙影響的空白。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Frailty, trajectories of frailty, and cognitive impairment: evidence from five longitudinal cohort studies.
**Abstract:** Longitudinal studies examining the impacts of frailty and its transitions on cognitive impairment in older adults across multiple regions remain scarce. This study aimed to explore the associations of baseline frailty status and frailty trajectories with cognitive impairment in five large-scale cohort studies. Data were derived from five prospective cohorts conducted in the United States, England, Europe, China, and Korea. Univariate and multivariate Cox proportional hazards models were used to evaluate the relationship between baseline frailty and cognitive impairment. Meta-analyses were performed to synthesize the overall effect sizes across all cohorts. Trajectories of frailty were identified using latent class growth models. Univariate and multivariate logistic regression models were utilized to investigate the association between frailty trajectories and cognitive impairment. Among the 47,429 participants included in the analyses of baseline frailty status, 8,799 developed cognitive impairment over median follow-up periods of 4.09 to 10.00 years. Compared with robust participants, pre-frail participants exhibited a significantly increased risk of cognitive impairment (adjusted hazard ratio [HR] range: 1.17 [95% confidence interval [CI]: 1.02-1.34] to 1.35 [95% CI: 1.09-1.68], pooled HR: 1.25 [95% CI: 1.18-1.33]). Similarly, frail participants showed an elevated risk of cognitive impairment (adjusted HR range: 1.55 [95% CI: 1.25-1.92] to 2.16 [95% CI: 1.72-2.72], pooled HR: 1.69 [95% CI: 1.50-1.90]). A total of 22,310 participants were included in the analyses of frailty trajectory, among whom 4,717 developed cognitive impairment. Frailty trajectories were significantly associated with cognitive impairment: higher trajectory class was linked to an elevated risk of cognitive impairment (adjusted odds ratio [OR] range: 1.45 [95% CI: 1.09-1.93] to 4.37 [95% CI: 3.43-5.59]), whereas lower trajectory class was connected to a reduced risk (adjusted OR range: 0.46 [95% CI: 0.39-0.54] to 0.72 [95% CI: 0.59-0.88]). Baseline frailty status is associated with cognitive impairment in the broader aging populations, and frailty trajectories are linked to cognitive impairment among the surviving older adults. Early frailty screening and detection may facilitate risk stratification and improve the management of cognitive impairment in older populations.
