### 重點摘要
吡咯喹啉醌 (PQQ) 被視為一種維生素樣輔助因子，廣泛存在於植物性食物、發酵食品和人類母乳中。研究證據顯示，PQQ 依賴性途徑在多種生物中保守，並在動物模型中能改善生長、繁殖、代謝反應、免疫功能及對缺血壓力的抵抗力，其作用涉及乳酸脫氫酶、GPR35 受體以及調控線粒體生成和抗氧化防禦的多個信號通路。儘管小型人體試驗已初步顯示 PQQ 在炎症、線粒體相關功能和認知方面有潛在益處，且毒理學安全性良好，但目前 PQQ 尚未被正式認定為人類必需維生素。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
儘管 PQQ 展現出多重代謝益處，但目前尚未有明確的人體缺乏症候群被描述，其作為人類必需維生素的標準仍未達到。主要限制包括缺乏經驗證的人體生物標誌物、定量營養需求不清、食物成分數據不完整、膳食加合物測量不一致以及腸道微生物的貢獻不明。未來的研究需建立標準化的 PQQ 分析方法、驗證生物標誌物，並進行人體劑量反應、耗盡-補充研究，以及在特定人群（如母嬰、老年人）和代謝、生殖及老化相關疾病中探討其機制與功效。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Pyrroloquinoline Quinone as a Candidate Vitamin-like Accessory Factor: Evidence Gaps and Research Priorities.
**Abstract:** Pyrroloquinoline quinone (PQQ), a bacterially derived ortho-quinone present in plant-based and fermented foods and in human milk, is proposed as a vitamin-like accessory factor. In this Perspective, we evaluate that proposal against the criteria historically used to establish nutritional essentiality. The evidence converges at several levels. PQQ-dependent pathways are conserved from bacteria to mammals. PQQ elicits growth, reproductive, and metabolic responses in invertebrates, fish, birds, rodents, and nonhuman primates. In mice and rats fed chemically defined diets, PQQ deprivation produces a reproducible, repletion-responsive phenotype. This phenotype includes impaired reproduction and neonatal growth, reduced immune responsiveness, lower energy expenditure with altered lipid metabolism, and diminished resilience to ischemic stress, with responses plateauing near 300 ng PQQ/g diet. Mechanistically, PQQ binds lactate dehydrogenase and shifts its kinetics toward pyruvate formation through redox cycling. It also acts as a selective agonist of the metabolite-sensing receptor GPR35 and engages the AMPK/SIRT1/PGC-1α, NRF-1, and KEAP1/NRF2 signaling pathways, which govern mitochondrial biogenesis and antioxidant defense. PQQ further intersects with folate, vitamin B-12, coenzyme Q10, and vitamin E metabolism. Small human trials report changes in inflammatory, mitochondrial-related, and cognitive endpoints, and preclinical toxicology indicates a wide safety margin. Nonetheless, PQQ does not yet meet the standard for an established human vitamin. No human deficiency syndrome has been described, validated status biomarkers and quantitative requirements are lacking, and food-composition data are incomplete. Dietary adducts such as imidazopyrroloquinoline are inconsistently measured, and the contribution of the gut microbiota is unresolved. We propose a research agenda comprising: 1) a standardized LC-MS/MS analysis of PQQ and its adducts, 2) biomarker validation, 3) human dose-response and depletion-repletion studies, 4) maternal-infant and older-adult cohorts, 5) mechanistic trials testing LDH and GPR35 engagement in human tissues, and 6) targeted efficacy trials in metabolic, reproductive, and age-related conditions. Statement of Significance: New and emerging evidence supporting the metabolic essentiality of PQQ is highlighted, including its roles as an enzyme redox accessory factor and as an agonist of receptors regulating mitochondrial functions. Recent studies across multiple species demonstrate the capacity of PQQ to modulate metabolism, with potential for life-stage-relevant health impacts, including early-life development, obesity-related conditions, and age-related functional decline. A framework is provided for future PQQ research.
