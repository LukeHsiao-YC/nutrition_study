### 重點摘要
這篇系統性回顧與統合分析旨在評估維生素D補充劑對非白人族群骨骼健康的影響，因為該族群維生素D缺乏風險較高且相關研究不足。研究涵蓋了8,023名黑人、亞洲人及其他非白人成人，並分析骨密度、骨代謝標誌物及骨折發生率。結果顯示，僅有一項研究發現骨密度有顯著改善，而骨鹼性磷酸酶（BALP）在補充組中略有升高，但整體骨骼健康的益處有限。因此，現有證據尚不足以強力支持維生素D補充劑能改善非白人成人骨骼健康。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻首次針對非白人族群進行維生素D補充劑對骨骼健康影響的系統性回顧與統合分析，填補了此領域的研究空白。然而，研究結果強調支持維生素D補充劑改善非白人成人骨骼健康的證據仍然有限，這表明未來需要更多大規模、長期且針對特定非白人族群的隨機對照試驗來進一步釐清其效果。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effect of vitamin D supplementation on bone health in Black, Asian and other non-White Ethnic adults: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
**Abstract:** Evidence on the effects of vitamin D supplementation on bone health is inconsistent, and no systematic review has focused specifically on non-White ethnic populations, who are at higher risk of vitamin D deficiency. To evaluate the effects of vitamin D supplementation on bone health in non-White adults. CENTRAL, MEDLINE, AMED, EMBASE, EMCARE, CINAHL, and APA PsycInfo were searched from inception to September 2025. Randomised controlled trials involving all ethnic groups other than White adults receiving vitamin D supplementation of any dose or route were included. Outcomes were bone mineral density (BMD), bone turnover markers (BTMs), and fractures. Data were narratively synthesised, with meta-analyses conducted for BTMs using random-effects models. Risk of bias was assessed using the Cochrane Risk-of-Bias 2 tool. Twelve trials (13 articles) involving 8,023 participants aged 18-90 years were included. Study populations comprised Black Africans, South Asians, South Koreans, and Chinese. Intervention duration ranged from 3 months to 5.3 years, with vitamin D doses from 400-6,400 IU/day. Of eight studies assessing BMD, only one study conducted in South Asians, reported significantly higher femoral neck and trochanter BMD after 12 months of vitamin D supplementation (400 IU/day) compared with placebo (p < 0.001).
