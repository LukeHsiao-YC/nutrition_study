### 重點摘要
現行飲食指南多建議限制飽和脂肪酸 (SFA) 攝取以預防心血管疾病。本研究透過敘述性綜合分析，檢視了2001年至2025年間31篇探討SFA攝取與心血管疾病發生率及死亡率關係的系統性回顧與統合分析。結果顯示，大多數研究未發現SFA攝取與心血管疾病結果有顯著一致性關聯，儘管以多元不飽和脂肪酸 (PUFA) 取代SFA可能對心血管有益。總體而言，現有證據未能證明膳食SFA攝取與心血管事件或死亡率間存在一致性關聯，建議飲食方針應著重於整體飲食模式而非單一營養素的替換。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究重新評估了飽和脂肪酸與心血管疾病關聯的證據一致性，挑戰了現行飲食指南中對單一營養素限制的有效性。然而，研究也指出將飽和脂肪酸替換為多元不飽和脂肪酸可能帶來益處，並強調未來研究與飲食建議應更聚焦於整體飲食模式，而非僅限於單一營養素的替代。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Revisiting the Evidence Underlying Dietary Recommendations on Saturated Fats and Cardiovascular Disease: a Narrative Synthesis of Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses.
**Abstract:** The recommendation to limit intake of saturated fatty acids (SFA) remains part of dietary guidelines issued by numerous national and international organizations. We performed a search of previous systematic reviews and meta-analyses on the relationship between SFA intake and cardiovascular disease (CVD) incidence and mortality, including coronary heart disease (CHD) and stroke, and total mortality. We screened 3285 records and included 31 publications (32 systematic reviews/meta-analyses: 23 observational and 9 RCT-based) published between 2001 and 2025. The duration of primary studies ranged from 0.4 to 32 years. The number of assessments included 34 of disease incidence, 39 of mortality, and 2 of composite cardiac endpoints. Most systematic reviews and meta-analyses found no significant relationship between intake of SFA and CVD outcomes. Two found a harmful effect (1 each for CHD and peripheral artery disease), whereas 3 found a beneficial effect (all on stroke in East Asian cohorts). Several others showed mixed results: six observational modeling studies suggested harm with SFA vs. polyunsaturated fatty acids (PUFA) for CVD incidence or mortality, while two RCT-based reviews found increased CVD incidence but no effect on mortality. We conclude that the currently available evidence does not demonstrate a consistent association between dietary SFA intake and cardiovascular events or mortality. Replacing SFA with PUFA appears to be associated with favorable cardiovascular outcomes in observational and well-conducted short-duration clinical research studies. Overall, these findings suggest that recommendations on SFA intake may be more effective when considered within the broader context of dietary patterns rather than replacement of single nutrients.
