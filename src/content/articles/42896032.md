### 重點摘要
乳腺癌的治療抗性是一個重大挑戰，而鐵死亡作為一種鐵依賴性細胞死亡，與鐵代謝、氧化壓力及腫瘤侵襲性密切相關。本綜述旨在總結乳腺癌中調節鐵死亡的機制並探討其作為治療靶點的潛力，透過文獻回顧分析了鐵代謝、氧化壓力與抗氧化系統在乳腺癌鐵死亡中的作用。研究指出細胞內鐵積累會增加癌細胞對鐵死亡的敏感性，且三陰性乳腺癌因氧化活性高而對鐵死亡更敏感，而腔內型和HER2陽性型則表現出抗性。因此，受控誘導鐵死亡有望消除耐藥細胞、提升治療效果，顯示其在連接代謝、氧化壓力與細胞死亡中的關鍵作用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本綜述填補了關於乳腺癌中鐵死亡機制、其與鐵代謝和氧化壓力的關聯，以及不同乳腺癌亞型對鐵死亡敏感性差異的綜合性理解空白，為未來開發基於鐵死亡的治療策略提供了理論基礎。然而，作為一篇敘述性綜述，它本身不提供新的實驗數據或臨床試驗結果，未來的研究限制將在於需要進一步的實驗室和臨床研究來驗證鐵死亡誘導療法的有效性與安全性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Ferroptosis in Breast Cancer: Linking Iron Metabolism, Intertumoral Heterogeneity, and Predictive Biomarkers of Clinical Response.
**Abstract:** Breast cancer (BC) is a prevalent and heterogeneous neoplasm whose therapeutic resistance remains a major challenge. Ferroptosis, an iron-dependent form of regulated cell death that is characterized by lipid peroxidation, has emerged as a key mechanism linking iron metabolism, oxidative stress, and tumor aggressiveness. The objective for this narrative review was to summarize the principal mechanisms regulating ferroptosis in BC and discuss its potential as a therapeutic target. A literature review was conducted to investigate the mechanisms involved in ferroptosis in BC, with emphasis on iron metabolism, oxidative stress, lipid peroxidation, antioxidant defense systems, and differences in ferroptosis sensitivity among BC subtypes. The literature indicates intracellular iron accumulation increases reactive oxygen species generation and enhances the susceptibility of cancer cells to ferroptosis. This process is mainly controlled by the glutathione peroxidase 4-glutathione and ferroptosis suppressor protein 1-coenzyme Q10 antioxidant systems, which neutralize lipid peroxides and preserve membrane integrity. Dysregulation of these pathways contributes to redox imbalance and disease progression. Among BC subtypes, triple-negative tumors show greater sensitivity to ferroptosis due to heightened oxidative activity and reduced expression of protective enzymes, whereas luminal and Human epidermal growth factor receptor 2-positive tumors exhibit increased resistance. Controlled induction of ferroptosis may help eliminate drug-resistant cells and enhance treatment efficacy, reinforcing its relevance as a central axis connecting metabolism, oxidative stress, and programmed cell death.
