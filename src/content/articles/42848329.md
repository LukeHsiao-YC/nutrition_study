### 重點摘要
MyPlate作為美國歷史上運行時間最長的食物基礎指南圖標（2011-2026），在全國層面推廣健康飲食模式方面扮演了關鍵角色。由美國農業部營養政策與推廣中心牽頭，開發了包括MyPlate計畫和Start Simple with MyPlate App在內的大量科學營養教育資源與溝通策略，以服務多元受眾。這些努力使MyPlate的認知度隨時間增加。本研究回顧了MyPlate的創建與推廣過程，旨在為未來制定營養指導與教育策略提供寶貴的經驗和範例。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻透過回顧與分析MyPlate的歷史，填補了對國家級飲食指南在制定、推廣及提升公眾認知度方面實踐經驗的理解空白。它提供了實施大型公共營養教育計畫的寶貴操作範例，但並未探討MyPlate對實際健康結果或特定營養指標的具體影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Reflecting Upon the History of MyPlate to Chart the Future of Nutrition Guidance and Education.
**Abstract:** National food and dietary guidance, and corresponding educational tools, play important roles in promoting healthy eating patterns at the population level. Developed in 2011 and replaced in 2026, MyPlate served as the longest-running food-based icon in US history. Led by the USDA Center for Nutrition Policy and Promotion, a large catalog of science-based nutrition education resources and communication strategies, including MyPlate Plans and the Start Simple with MyPlate App, were created for diverse audiences, professionals, and corporate partners. MyPlate awareness increased over time. Moving forward, the processes for creating and promoting MyPlate can be used as examples for future efforts.
