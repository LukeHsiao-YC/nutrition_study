### 重點摘要
這項探索性研究旨在描述美國小學非午餐課間休息時間的變異性及課間休息的取消做法，因為這些實踐缺乏一致的記錄。研究採用2022-2024學年的橫斷面數據，分析來自51個地區1,416所學校的體育教師問卷報告。結果顯示，平均非午餐課間休息時間為每天16.21分鐘，且課間休息的提供與取消做法在各校間差異顯著。僅有16.2%的學校達到建議的每天至少20分鐘且不取消課間休息，而38.4%的學校課間休息時間不足20分鐘且會取消。這些初步發現提供了全國學校課間休息實踐的現況概覽，並強調未來需採用具代表性的抽樣方法進行後續研究。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了關於美國小學課間休息提供與取消做法現況數據不一致的空白。然而，由於本研究採用探索性設計且非具代表性的抽樣，其主要限制在於無法推論至全國或各司法管轄區的準確估計，因此需要未來研究採用代表性抽樣技術來克服此限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Variations in Scheduled Non-Lunch Recess and Withholding: Preliminary Findings from the U.S. Education and Physical Activity Policy Study Physical.
**Abstract:** Recess provides a critical school-based venue for student physical activity. However, recess provision and withholding practices remain inconsistently documented. This exploratory study described variability in daily scheduled non-lunch recess minutes, and recess withholding practices among U.S. elementary schools participating in a national physical education teacher questionnaire. This cross-sectional exploratory study used schoolyear 2022-2024 data from the U.S. Physical Education and Physical Activity Policy (US-PEPAP) questionnaire. Physical education teachers reported frequency and duration of scheduled non-lunch recess and whether their school withheld recess. After exclusions for ineligible settings, incomplete data, and multiple same-school responses, the analytic sample included 1,416 elementary, K-8, or K-12 schools across 51 jurisdictions (50 states, plus Washington D.C.). An exploratory composite recess practice score (range: 0-3) was constructed based on provision of the recommended at least 20-minutes/day of recess and whether recess was withheld. Unweighted school-level summaries were calculated overall and by jurisdiction. Mean scheduled non-lunch recess was 16.21-minutes/day (SD=8.84). The mean exploratory composite recess practice score was 1.24 (SD=1.13). Among participating schools, 230 (16.2%) reported the recommended 20-minutes/day and did not withhold, while 421 (29.7%) schools provided recommended 20-minutes/day, but practiced withholding. A total of 221 (15.6%) reported less than 20-minutes/day without withholding, and 544 (38.4%) reported less than the recommended 20-minutes/day and practiced withholding. Scheduled non-lunch recess and withholding varied among participating schools. These preliminary findings provide a current overview of school practices across the nation, and indicate a need for follow-up studies using representative sampling techniques to assess jurisdiction estimates.
