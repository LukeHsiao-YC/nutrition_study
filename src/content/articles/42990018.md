### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討飲食模式，如發炎潛力、胰島素潛力及整體飲食品質，如何影響男性體內的循環性類固醇激素。研究方法採橫斷面分析，檢視了1622名65歲以上男性的飲食模式（rEDIP、rEDIH、HEI-2020）與性激素水平，並進行了截至2024年6月的系統性文獻回顧。初步結果顯示，在老年男性中，較低的飲食發炎潛力（rEDIP每增加一個標準差）與較高的總睪固酮水平相關。這項研究為飲食模式與男性性激素之間的關聯提供了進一步的證據。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了關於整體飲食模式對循環性性類固醇激素影響證據有限的空白，特別是在老年男性群體中。然而，由於其採橫斷面研究設計，無法確立飲食模式與性激素之間的因果關係，且研究對象主要為老年男性，結果在外推至其他年齡層或族群時可能存在局限性。

**Title:** Dietary patterns and the testosterone-estrogen axis in men: evidence from osteoporotic fractures in men and a systematic review.
**Abstract:** Diet may influence circulating sex steroid hormones, but evidence for overall dietary patterns remains limited. We examined associations of dietary patterns reflecting lower inflammatory potential, lower insulinaemic potential and higher overall diet quality, with circulating sex steroids in older men and systematically reviewed the literature in apparently healthy men published through June 2024. We conducted cross-sectional analyses of 1622 men aged ≥65 years from the Osteoporotic Fractures in Men Study. Diet was assessed using FFQ and sex hormone-binding globulin (SHBG), total and free testosterone and total and free estradiol were measured. Dietary patterns included the reversed Empirical Dietary Inflammatory Pattern (rEDIP), reversed Empirical Dietary Index for Hyperinsulinaemia (rEDIH) and Healthy Eating Index-2020 (HEI-2020).
