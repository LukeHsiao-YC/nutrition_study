### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討阿拉斯加州安克拉治小學教師對於聯邦免費供餐政策（UFSM）實施前後，學生飢餓、行為、用餐參與度、食物浪費和家庭壓力等方面的看法。研究透過對25位幼稚園至六年級教師進行線上訪談，並採用浸潤/結晶法分析訪談記錄。結果顯示，UFSM期間教師觀察到學生飢餓和家庭壓力減少，但食物浪費增加，且課堂用餐有助於社群建立與行為改善。然而，UFSM取消後，教師注意到用餐參與度下降、飢餓感增加以及對餐費的焦慮，教師因此認為UFSM對於學生健康與公平性至關重要，研究結果支持持續實施UFSM政策並將教師視角納入餐點計畫設計。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了從前線教育工作者（小學教師）視角理解聯邦免費供餐政策實施前後對學生福祉和學校運作影響的質性研究空白。它提供了直接參與學生日常的教師對政策變革所帶來的實際感受和觀察，這對於理解政策的真實影響至關重要，且是量化研究較難捕捉的層面。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** When School Meals Are No Longer Free: A Qualitative Exploration of Elementary School Teachers' Experiences and Perceptions.
**Abstract:** To explore teachers' perceptions of student hunger and behavior, school meal participation, food waste, family stress, supervision, and meal locations during a federal universal free school meals (UFSM) policy, comparing the 2021-2022 school year to the 2022-2023 school year. Twenty-five kindergarten through sixth-grade teachers participated in online interviews in 6 elementary schools in Anchorage, Alaska. Transcripts were analyzed using immersion/crystallization. Researchers coded data (k = 0.82) and identified recurring themes and domains. During UFSM, teachers perceived reduced hunger and family stress but increased food waste. Classroom eating fostered community and improved behavior, despite clean-up challenges and lost planning time. Following UFSM deimplementation, teachers perceived declined participation, increased hunger, and anxiety about meal costs. Teachers view UFSM as essential for student health and equity. Findings support sustained UFSM policies and integrating teacher perspectives into meal program design.
