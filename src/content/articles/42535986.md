### 重點摘要
這項研究描述了一個為期十年的「夏季餐點合作模式」（SMC）的發展與影響，該模式運用集體影響框架，旨在提升夏季食物服務計畫的覆蓋率與效率。透過教育、公共衛生、地方政府和社區組織等跨部門合作，SMC成功擴大了贊助商和兒童參與，並支持了計畫交付的改進研究與評估。此模式證明了多元利害關係人能有效協作，以應對改善兒童糧食安全中的系統性挑戰。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了在提升夏季營養計畫觸及與效率方面，缺乏長期、創新且透過跨部門協作模式來應對兒童糧食安全系統性挑戰的研究空白。它提供了一個具體且經過十年驗證的模型，展示如何透過集體影響框架整合不同領域資源，以改進計畫交付和克服現有挑戰。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Ten-Year Impact of a Summer Meals Collaborative Model: An Innovative Approach to Enhancing the Reach and Efficiency of the Summer Food Service Program.
**Abstract:** This report describes the development and impact of a Summer Meals Collaborative (SMC) using a Collective Impact Framework to enhance the reach and efficiency of the Summer Food Service Program. Over 10 years, the SMC expanded both sponsor and child participation, developed cross-sector partnerships, and supported research and evaluation efforts to improve program delivery. The model shows how diverse stakeholders from education, public health, local government, and community organizations can work together to address systemic challenges in improving child food security. Implications discussed include best practices, research opportunities, and policy recommendations to address persistent gaps to improve challenges in delivering summer nutrition programs.
