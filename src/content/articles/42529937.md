### 重點摘要
魚類攝取與n-3多元不飽和脂肪酸補充可降低心血管疾病風險，而鯨蠟油酸在鮭魚和人類肝癌細胞中能促進n-3 PUFA生成，但人體數據稀缺。本研究為一項為期四週的雙盲、隨機對照試驗，招募健康成年人，隨機分配攝取富含鯨蠟油酸的鯖魚油或對照油，兩者n-3 PUFA濃度相似。結果顯示，補充鯖魚油組的受試者血漿中二十碳四烯酸 (20:4n-3) 和二十碳五烯酸 (20:5n-3) 水平顯著較高，紅血球膜中20:4n-3也增加，且周邊血液單核細胞中ELOVL5蛋白表達降低。這表明富含鯨蠟油酸的鯖魚油補充可能有效提升特定n-3 PUFA水平。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了鯨蠟油酸對人體n-3 PUFA水平影響數據稀缺的空白。然而，由於受試者皆為健康成人，未來的研究應進一步納入患有代謝疾病或心血管疾病風險的患者，以評估其臨床應用潛力。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Cetoleic acid rich fish oil increases plasma levels of eicosapentaenoic and docosapentaenoic acids in healthy volunteers: a double-blinded, randomised, controlled trial.
**Abstract:** Intake of fish and supplementation with n-3 polyunsaturated fatty acids (PUFA) are associated with reduced incidence of cardiovascular disease (CVD). Cetoleic acid promotes n-3 PUFA generation in salmon and human HepG2 cells, but data in humans are scarce. To explore the effect of cetoleic acid on n-3 PUFA levels, we performed a double-blinded randomised controlled trial in healthy adults (37 females and 12 males) for four weeks. The participants were randomised to consume either capsules with mackerel oil, naturally rich in cetoleic acid, or a control oil, both with a similar concentration of n-3 PUFA. Clinicaltrials.gov (NCT04841044). Supplements with mackerel oil resulted in higher plasma levels of the n-3 PUFAs 20:4n-3 (Eicosatetraenoic acid) and 20:5n-3 (Eicosapentaenoic acid) as compared with those receiving control oil. In addition, those who received the cetoleic-rich capsules had higher levels of 20:4n-3 in the RBC membrane and a decrease in expression of elongation of very long chain fatty acids protein 5 in peripheral blood mononuclear cells. We found no differences in lipid, glucose or C-reactive protein between the two treatment groups, consisting of healthy subjects. Our data suggests that supplementation with cetoleic-rich mackerel oil may result in increased levels of certain n-3 PUFAs. Further studies should include patients with or at risk for metabolic and CVD.
