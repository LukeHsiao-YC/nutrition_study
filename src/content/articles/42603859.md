### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估生物電阻抗分析（BIA）衍生的去脂體重指數（FFMI）與雙能量X光吸收儀（DXA）衍生的四肢骨骼肌質量指數（ASMI）在社區成人中的相關性，並依據2019年亞洲肌少症工作組標準開發性別特異性預測圖。研究分析了369名社區居民的數據，發現FFMI與ASMI的相關性在男性中為中等（ρ=0.65），在女性中較弱（ρ=0.36），並確立了性別特異性的篩檢最佳截斷點。結果顯示FFMI作為低肌肉量篩檢工具是實用的，但其準確性受性別和代謝健康狀況影響，需要進一步進行性別及疾病特異性校準。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究為低肌肉量篩檢工具FFMI的應用提供了性別特異性的評估標準，特別針對亞洲族群，填補了BIA在不同性別和代謝狀態下診斷精確度的研究空白。然而，研究結果強調FFMI的準確性會因性別和代謝健康狀況而異，提示未來研究需針對特定疾病進行校準，以進一步提升其在營養不良診斷和肌少症篩檢中的精確性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Sex-specific screening of low appendicular skeletal muscle mass index in community dwelling adults: A descriptive cross-sectional study.
**Abstract:** The Global Leadership Initiative on Malnutrition criteria has recommended using bioelectrical impedance analysis-derived fat-free mass index as a practical screening tool for low muscle mass. In the present study we evaluated the correlation between this index and dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry-derived appendicular skeletal muscle mass index, and developed sex-specific predictive nomograms according to the 2019 Asian Working Group for Sarcopenia criteria. This cross-sectional analysis included 369 community-dwelling adults from the Hallym Aging Study. Body composition was assessed by bioelectrical impedance analysis and dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry. Low appendicular skeletal muscle mass index was defined as <7.0 kg/m This index moderately correlated with appendicular skeletal muscle mass index (ρ = 0.65 in males, ρ = 0.36 in females; P < 0.001). Optimal cutoffs were 17.7 kg/m Fat-free mass index is a practical marker for low muscle mass screening. However, its accuracy varies by sex and metabolic health, requiring sex-and disease-specific calibration to improve malnutrition diagnosis and sarcopenia screening.
