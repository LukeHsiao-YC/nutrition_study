### 重點摘要
這項隨機對照試驗比較了三種不同劑量（2000、3000、4000 IU）口服維生素D3治療印度5歲以下兒童營養性軟骨病的療效。研究發現，在12週後，所有劑量組的放射學軟骨病嚴重程度評分均顯著改善，且各組間的療效沒有統計學差異。然而，高劑量組（3000/4000 IU）觀察到較多高鈣尿症和過高血清25(OH)D的病例。儘管療效相似，研究強調飲食中鈣攝取量與最終軟骨病嚴重程度密切相關。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究直接比較了不同日常維生素D3劑量對治療營養性軟骨病的療效與安全性，填補了此特定兒童群體中精確劑量選擇的知識空白。然而，雖然所有劑量組的療效相似，但高劑量組有較高的不良事件風險，這提示未來研究需進一步探討最佳劑量以平衡療效與安全性。此外，飲食中鈣攝取量的重要性也指出，單獨補充維生素D可能不足以完全解決軟骨病問題。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Comparison of three different daily doses of vitamin D3 in the treatment of nutritional rickets in Indian children under age 5 years: A randomized controlled trial.
**Abstract:** To compare the efficacy of three different doses of oral vitamin D in treatment of nutritional rickets METHODS: This three-group open-labeled randomized controlled trial enrolled children age 6 months to 5 years with nutritional rickets to receive oral vitamin D3 (2000, 3000 or 4000 IU) for 12 weeks. The outcomes were final radiological rickets severity score (RSS) (primary), change in serum 25-hydroxyvitamin D (25(OH)D), calcium, phosphorus, parathyroid hormone (PTH) and alkaline phosphatase (ALP) at 12 weeks. A total of 132 children (68 boys) with a median (IQR) age 24 (18, 39.5) months and mean (SD) RSS 8.86 (1.89) were enrolled (44 in each of the three groups). The median (IQR) RSS improved significantly from baseline to endline RSS 2.0 (1.0, 5.0), 1.0 (0.0, 4.0) and 1.75 (0.5, 4.25); p = 0.280, in the 2000, 3000, and 4000 IU groups, respectively. The median (IQR) serum 25(OH)D levels increased at 12 weeks to 27.3 (17.7, 48.7), 38.95 (19.0, 67.6) and 46.3 (26.7, 64.6) ng/mL in 2000, 3000 and 4000 IU groups, respectively; p = 0.058. One-third children who had RSS > 1.5 had lower serum phosphorus and higher ALP at baseline (p < 0.05). Dietary calcium intake was significantly (p = 0.008) related to final log RSS irrespective of allocation group when adjusted for baseline covariates. Three children had hypercalciuria and six had serum 25(OH)D > 100 ng/mL (all belonged to the 3000/4000 IU groups). Daily oral vitamin D3 doses of 2000, 3000 and 4000 IU were comparable in efficacy in the treatment of nutritional rickets.
