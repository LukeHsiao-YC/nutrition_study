### 重點摘要
膽道閉鎖（BA）是一種罕見且可能致命的嬰兒膽管疾病，主要治療方式為葛西手術，但許多患者最終仍需肝臟移植。本系統性文獻回顧旨在描述美國膽道閉鎖患者的流行病學、自然病史、肝臟存活率以及相關的人文和經濟負擔。研究結果顯示，膽道閉鎖盛行率約為每十萬活產6.5-6.8例，並伴隨多種併發症、高醫療資源利用和疾病成本。儘管現有數據存在異質性且有限，此回顧仍揭示了膽道閉鎖造成的巨大負擔，並凸顯了在疾病管理和研究方面仍有未滿足的需求。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻透過系統性回顧，整合了美國膽道閉鎖在流行病學、自然病史和疾病負擔方面的現有數據，填補了該領域數據分散的空白，並明確指出其對醫療系統造成的實質負擔與未滿足的需求。然而，研究亦指出現有數據的異質性高且數量有限，限制了跨研究的比較性，未來研究應著重於標準化研究設計，以提供更具可比性和全面性的膽道閉鎖成果數據。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Natural history and disease burden of biliary atresia in the United States: A systematic literature review.
**Abstract:** Biliary atresia (BA) is a rare and potentially fatal cholangiopathy affecting infants. The primary treatment approach for BA is Kasai hepatoportoenterostomy (KP); however, even with KP, many patients still require liver transplantation (LT). The objective of this systematic literature review (SLR) was to describe epidemiology, natural history, and native liver survival (NLS), as well as the humanistic and economic burden, in patients with BA in the United States (US). Embase and MEDLINE searches were performed; US-based, English-language publications from 2014 to 2024 were included. Additional searches were conducted for non-peer-reviewed publications, including congress abstracts. The SLR was conducted per Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic reviews and Meta-Analyses guidelines. Across epidemiology, natural history, and NLS, 38/3485 records were included; for the humanistic and economic burden outcomes, 16/1471 records were included. Sample sizes ranged from 10 to 4306 patients, and BA prevalence was 6.5-6.8 per 100,000 live births. Complications of BA included various hepatic, cardiovascular, and renal abnormalities, among others. Post-transplant graft survival rates ranged from 86.0%-96.0% and 79.0%-97.0% at 1 and 5 years post-LT, respectively. BA was also associated with high healthcare resource utilization (HCRU) and disease costs. Overall, sample heterogeneity and study design differences made comparisons across studies difficult. Although there are limited data on BA outcomes in the US, and the published BA studies are heterogeneous, this SLR reveals a substantial burden due to BA-related care and complications. Additionally, data on HCRU and costs highlight the unmet need for additional management and research in BA.
