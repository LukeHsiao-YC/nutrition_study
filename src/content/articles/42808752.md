### 重點摘要
本研究旨在利用兒童飲食行為問卷（CEBQ）開發簡化綜合分數，以評估學齡前兒童的致肥胖飲食風險。研究分析了354名9至12歲兒童CEBQ問卷中的29個項目，透過因子分析建立數據驅動的綜合分數，並將其與身體質量指數（BMI）z分數及肥胖多基因風險分數（PRS）進行相關性分析。結果顯示，三種數據驅動的綜合分數及理論驅動分數彼此高度相關，且比單一領域分數與BMI及PRS有更強的相關性。這表明CEBQ的子集項目可以有效評估由兒童食慾特質帶來的肥胖風險。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究透過數據驅動方法，將複雜的CEBQ問卷簡化為更具預測性的綜合分數，有效填補了在學齡前兒童中，缺乏簡潔且能有效衡量食慾特質相關肥胖風險評估工具的研究空白。未來的研究可進一步驗證這些綜合分數在不同族群中的適用性，並探討其在長期肥胖預防策略中的應用。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Composite Summary Scores Capture Obesogenic Eating Risk Among Preadolescents Using the Child Eating Behaviour Questionnaire.
**Abstract:** The Child Eating Behaviour Questionnaire (CEBQ) measures multiple appetitive traits. We used a data-driven approach to identify simplified composite scores across traits to summarise obesogenic eating risk. From 354 unrelated 9-to-12-year-olds, we examined 29 items from CEBQ domains measuring food approach or avoidance: food responsiveness, enjoyment of food, emotional over-eating, desire to drink, satiety responsiveness, slowness in eating, and emotional under-eating. Factor analysis assuming a single factor was used to reduce the number of items in one composite, obesogenic eating score. Candidate composite scores averaged items with standardized loadings ≥|0.40|, ≥|0.50|, or ≥|0.70|. Those scores, the individual domain scores, and a theory-driven composite score were correlated with BMI z-scores and two obesity polygenic risk scores (PRS, subset n = 247). The three data-driven composite scores (4-18 items) and the theory driven score were highly correlated with each other (r range: 0.77-0.97) and more strongly correlated with BMI (r range: 0.38-0.42; FDR q < 0.001) than any individual domain score. Similarly, these scores were significantly correlated with both PRSs. We identified CEBQ composite scores related to child BMI and genetic obesity risk, suggesting a subset of CEBQ items can measure obesity risk conferred by child appetitive traits.
