### 重點摘要
運動引起的肌肉損傷(EIMD)是高強度體能活動的常見結果，會導致肌肉痠痛、發炎和氧化壓力。白藜蘆醇(RES)作為一種具抗氧化和抗炎潛力的天然多酚化合物，被探討其在緩解EIMD方面的作用。本綜述整合了臨床前與臨床研究，評估白藜蘆醇對肌肉恢復、炎症和氧化壓力標誌物的影響。研究結果顯示白藜蘆醇在減少肌肉損傷和改善恢復方面展現潛力，但其給藥時間、劑量和生物利用度仍存在不一致性，需進一步研究以最佳化其治療效益。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本綜述透過綜合白藜蘆醇對運動引起肌肉損傷的臨床前與臨床研究，填補了該領域知識整合的空白。然而，未來的研究仍需深入探討白藜蘆醇給藥時間、劑量及生物利用度之間的不一致性，以釐清最佳使用策略並最大化其治療潛力。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effects of Resveratrol Supplementation on Exercise-Induced Muscle Damage: A Narrative Review.
**Abstract:** Exercise-induced muscle damage (EIMD) is a common consequence of high-intensity or unfamiliar physical activity and can lead to muscle soreness, inflammation, oxidative stress, and temporary loss of muscle function. Although various interventions have been explored to alleviate EIMD, resveratrol (RES), a natural polyphenolic compound found in grapes, berries, and other plants, has attracted attention due to its potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This review aims to explore the effects of RES on EIMD in terms of its impact on muscle recovery, inflammation, and oxidative stress markers. This review synthesizes the results of preclinical and clinical studies and highlights the timing, dosage, and efficacy of exercise-associated RES. Although the results are promising, with several studies reporting reduced muscle damage and improved recovery, inconsistencies in RES administration timing, dosage, and bioavailability require further investigation. Understanding these factors is critical to optimizing the therapeutic potential of RES in both the exercise and clinical settings. Finally, the review summarizes the potential of RES as a safe and effective intervention for EIMD and implications for future research.
