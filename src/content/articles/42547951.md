### 重點摘要
嬰兒期超重與配方奶餵養及母親超重相關，本研究旨在評估一種校準配方奶餵養指南對高風險嬰兒體重管理的影響。這項針對出生體重≥50th百分位且母親孕前BMI≥25的純配方奶餵養新生兒進行的先導性隨機試驗，旨在測試依據年齡和體重推薦的配方奶量指導。然而，由於僅少數嬰兒符合接受校準指導的體重/身長指數標準，加上高達28.3%的退出率，研究結果顯示干預組與對照組在生長指標上沒有顯著差異。本研究最終指出，校準嬰兒配方奶建議與生長結果無關。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究試圖探討精確控制配方奶量以優化高風險嬰兒生長，但其主要限制在於干預措施的實際應用性不高（僅少數嬰兒符合接受校準指導的條件），且研究對象退出率較高。這些因素可能影響了干預效果的評估，並提示未來研究需重新評估此類干預策略的適用標準或設計。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Pilot Randomised Trial of Calibrated Formula Volumes to Optimise Growth in Infants at Risk for Overweight: The Califormula Randomised Clinical Trial.
**Abstract:** Formula feeding and having a mother with overweight are associated with overweight during infancy. We sought to pilot test calibrated formula feeding guidance using age and weight specific intake recommendations to improve weight outcomes amongst at-risk infants. Exclusively formula fed newborns with birthweight ≥ 50th percentile delivered to mothers with pre-pregnancy body mass index (BMI) ≥ 25 kg/m Sixty infants were enrolled; 36 mothers (66.7%) had pre-pregnancy BMI ≥ 30. Seventeen (28.3%) withdrew before 6 months. At each assessment, a minority had WFL ≥ 75th percentile required to receive calibrated formula guidance. There were no group differences in CWG (intervention mean 0.27, 95% CI -0.20 to 0.75 vs. control mean -0.12, 95% CI -0.62 to 0.37; p = 0.25) or for secondary outcomes. Calibrated infant formula recommendations were not associated with growth outcomes. Clinicaltrials.gov identifier: NCT05104073 (registered October 11, 2021).
