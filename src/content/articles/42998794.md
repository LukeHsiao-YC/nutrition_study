### 重點摘要
本研究探討生成式AI的溝通風格如何影響消費者對替代蛋白的接受度，以促進永續食物系統。透過四項線上實驗，結果顯示在基線情境下，明確溝通能降低消費不適感並提高選擇意願。然而，溝通效果會因推薦框架而異，例如在益處導向框架下，強調環境價值時保留溝通較佳，強調個人健康時明確溝通較佳。此研究基於解釋水平理論，為設計鼓勵永續食物選擇的生成式AI推薦提供了實用指南。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了在替代蛋白接受度研究中，缺乏探討生成式AI溝通風格與推薦框架如何互動影響消費者行為的空白。它為生成式AI如何有效設計推薦訊息以克服消費者不適感、鼓勵永續食物選擇提供了理論基礎與實踐指引。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** How Should GenAI Recommend Alternative Proteins? The Role of Consumption Discomfort and Recommendation Framing.
**Abstract:** Alternative proteins offer a promising pathway toward more sustainable food systems, yet consumer acceptance remains constrained by unfamiliarity and discomfort. Across four online experiments, this research examines how GenAI communication style influences consumers' responses to alternative-protein recommendations. The findings suggest that, in a baseline recommendation context, unhedged communication is associated with greater willingness to choose alternative proteins through lower consumption discomfort. However, this effect depends on the recommendation framing. Under benefit-oriented framing, hedged communication is more effective when environmental value is emphasized, whereas unhedged communication is more effective when personal health value is emphasized. Under barrier-oriented framing, unhedged communication performs better when alternative proteins are assimilated into familiar food schemas, whereas hedged communication is more effective when consumers are invited to accommodate novel protein options. Using construal level theory as a theoretical lens, this research develops a fit account in which communication effectiveness depends on whether linguistic certainty fits the psychological task emphasized by the recommendation frame. The findings advance research on alternative protein acceptance and provide guidance for designing GenAI recommendations that encourage sustainable food choices.
