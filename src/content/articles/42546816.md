### 重點摘要
這項範圍界定回顧旨在探討從懷孕到青春期的關鍵生命階段中，味覺體驗如何影響飲食習慣與健康結果。研究人員透過系統性搜索，篩選出14項符合標準的人體研究。結果顯示，味覺感知和偏好可能會影響食物選擇，甚至降低對營養食物的吸引力，儘管現有證據有限且異質性高。本回顧建議可透過營養教育和食品改良來利用味覺，以促進更健康的飲食，並強調需進一步研究其潛在機制及長期影響。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻透過匯總現有關於味覺體驗與飲食習慣在從懷孕到青春期等關鍵生命階段間關聯的證據，填補了該領域的知識整合空白。然而，研究本身也指出當前證據的局限性，例如研究數量有限且內容異質，並強調未來研究需更深入探討其潛在機制與長期效果，以建立更堅實的證據基礎。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** The Power of Taste: A Scoping Review about How Taste Experiences Shapes Dietary Habits and Health from Pregnancy to Adolescence.
**Abstract:** Taste influences food preferences and consumption, potentially contributing to dietary choices across the lifespan. Hormonal changes during pregnancy and breastfeeding, as well as biological and cognitive development during childhood and adolescence, may alter taste perception, sensitivity, and preferences, with possible implications for diet and health.This scoping review aimed to map evidence on how taste experiences vary across critical life stages (pregnancy, breastfeeding, childhood, and adolescence) and how these changes affect dietary behaviours, food choices, and health outcomes. A comprehensive search was conducted in PubMed, Web of Science, and Scopus from inception to September 2025.Human studies examining taste perception, sensitivity, or preferences, reporting diet- or health-related outcomes, were included. Peer-reviewed articles in English or Portuguese were considered; conference abstracts, reports, and unpublished manuscripts were excluded.Data were extracted on the life stage examined, measures of taste sensitivity or perception, taste preferences or hedonic responses, and reported associations with dietary behaviours and health outcomes. Findings were grouped by life stage and taste-related constructs for descriptive synthesis, and the strength and consistency of evidence were assessed. Of 1206 records, 14 studies met the inclusion criteria. Evidence suggests that taste experiences may influence dietary behaviours and food choices across these life stages, though studies are limited and heterogeneous. Altered taste perception and sensitivity may reduce the appeal of nutritious foods, highlighting the need for targeted strategies. Taste may be leveraged to promote healthier eating through nutrition education that emphasises enjoyment of healthy foods and through industry reformulation that maintains palatability while improving nutritional quality. Further research is needed to clarify underlying mechanisms and long-term effects.
