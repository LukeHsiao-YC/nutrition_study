### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討兒童門脈高壓症（PHT）中T細胞免疫失調的機制，特別根據其病因（慢性肝病或門靜脈血栓）進行區分。研究分析了44名PHT兒童的周邊和門脈血液樣本，評估T細胞亞群、細胞因子和免疫標誌物。結果顯示PHT與循環T細胞減少、免疫表型改變及效應功能受損有關，且慢性肝病相關PHT表現出顯著的促炎和T細胞耗竭特徵。而門靜脈血栓相關PHT則呈現較輕的炎症反應及T細胞遷移模式改變，證實兒童PHT存在病因特異性的T細胞失調。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究初步揭示了兒童門脈高壓症中病因特異性的T細胞免疫失調機制，填補了該領域缺乏深入了解的空白。然而，由於這是一項初步研究，其發現的候選免疫特徵仍需在規模更大、按病因分類的隊列中進行驗證，以確認其臨床應用價值和預後意義。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Altered T cell immune profile and its prognostic implications in children with portal hypertension: A pilot study.
**Abstract:** Immune dysregulation in paediatric portal hypertension (PHT) is poorly understood. We aimed to characterise T cell phenotype and function and circulating immune mediators in children with PHT according to disease aetiology. Forty-four children with PHT (27 chronic liver disease [CLD]-related and 17 portal vein thrombosis [PVT]-related) and 15 healthy controls were prospectively enrolled. Peripheral and portal blood samples were analysed using multiparameter flow cytometry and ELISA to assess T cell subsets, chemokine receptors, cytokines and soluble immune markers. Analyses examined group differences and correlations with liver stiffness and bleeding history. PHT was associated with reduced circulating T cells, altered immune phenotypes and impaired effector function. CLD-related PHT exhibited a pronounced pro-inflammatory profile, with elevated IFN-γ, TNF-α, MMP7 and soluble PD-L1 and increased CD39 and PD-1. PVT-related PHT showed relatively preserved liver function and milder systemic inflammation but altered T cell trafficking, characterised by increased CCR4 and reduced CXCR3 expression. Elevated CXCL9 correlated with CXCR3 expression. T cells in the portal circulation displayed reduced cytokine production and cytotoxicity, suggesting functional adaptation within the hepatic microenvironment. Liver stiffness correlated positively with pro-inflammatory cytokines and immune-regulatory markers. Paediatric PHT is characterised by aetiology-specific T cell dysregulation. CLD-related PHT predominantly features chronic inflammation and T cell exhaustion, whereas PVT-related PHT shows a milder inflammatory phenotype with altered T cell trafficking. These findings provide mechanistic insights and identify candidate immune signatures requiring validation in larger, aetiology-specific cohorts.
