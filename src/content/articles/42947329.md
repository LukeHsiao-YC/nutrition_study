### 重點摘要
這項佇列研究旨在探討美國紐約州雪城兒科難民群體在美國移民後的體重增加與發育遲緩情況。研究透過回溯性圖表審閱收集2012至2017年的數據，發現在抵達時有9.7%的參與者出現發育遲緩，且依原籍地區差異顯著。居住美國五年以上後，24%的難民出現肥胖，19.4%過重。線性混合模型分析顯示，發育遲緩、原籍地區和在美居住時間是體重指數（BMI）隨時間變化的顯著預測因子，研究結果支持對難民人口實施針對性的肥胖預防干預措施，尤其對抵達時有發育遲緩者需額外關注。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了針對美國特定兒科難民群體在移民後，從發育遲緩轉變為超重/肥胖的營養轉變模式及其影響因素的知識空白。它特別識別出發育遲緩、原籍地區和在美居住時間等關鍵預測因子，為該脆弱群體的精準干預提供了重要依據。然而，研究是單一中心的回溯性分析，未來研究可擴展至多中心、前瞻性設計，並深入探討飲食習慣與生活方式改變等更具體的中介因素，以評估不同干預策略的有效性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Stunting and weight gain in paediatric refugees after US immigration.
**Abstract:** This cohort study aims to explore weight gain and stunting in the Syracuse, NY paediatric refugee population. Demographics, height and weight were collected through chart review and electronic abstraction in 2019-2020 for  Pediatric International Health Clinic at an academic medical centre from 2012 to 2017. Paediatric refugees. The cohort was 49 % female with a mean age (sd) upon arrival of 8·9 (4·7) years. Sixty-one percent of refugees were from Africa, with 33 % from the Middle East. Stunting was observed in 9·7 % of participants upon arrival and varied significantly by region of origin. Obesity was observed in 24 % of refugees after living in the US for 5 or more years while the frequency of overweight at 5 plus years was 19·4 %. In linear mixed modelling, stunting, region of origin and duration of residence in the US were found to be significant predictors of BMI change over time. Results support implementing targeted obesity prevention interventions in the Pediatric International Health Clinic refugee population and highlight additional risk for those arriving with stunting.
