### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在評估在肯亞愛滋病影響家庭中，多部門農業與生計介入對6至36個月兒童營養、發育及疾病的影響，以應對飢餓與愛滋病交織帶來的健康風險。該研究是一項群集隨機對照試驗，招募了359名來自糧食不安全且愛滋病毒感染成人家庭的兒童，並每六個月收集人體測量和臨床數據，為期24個月。結果顯示，在招募時年齡介於6至<24個月的兒童中，介入組的身高/長度增長顯著優於對照組。這項介入措施改善了早期兒童的生長與健康，強調了生命早期干預的重要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了在愛滋病影響下的糧食不安全家庭中，缺乏多部門農業與生計介入對幼兒營養、發育和疾病的有效性證據空白，並突顯了生命早期干預在該脆弱群體中的關鍵作用。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effects of a multisectoral household agricultural and livelihood intervention on nutrition, development, and morbidity among HIV-affected children 6 to 36 months of age in Western Kenya: A cluster-randomized trial.
**Abstract:** Food insecurity and HIV are interconnected, potentially increasing nutritional and health-related risks. The Pediatric Shamba Maisha study evaluated the effects of an agricultural and livelihood intervention on growth, development, and common morbidities among children in HIV-affected households in Kenya. The Pediatric Shamba Maisha study was a parallel-arm cluster-randomised controlled trial nested within a multisectoral agriculture and livelihood intervention trial among food-insecure adults living with HIV in rural Kenya. The Pediatric Study enrolled children aged 6 to 36 months from participating households. Anthropometric and clinical data were collected every 6 months for 24 months. Of the 359 children enrolled (intervention arm=173, control arm=186), 334 (90%) completed the 24-month visit.
