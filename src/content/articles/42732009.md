### 重點摘要
本研究探討人體組成在不同人群間的顯著變異性，指出其與地理分佈模式、祖先遺傳及跨生命週期與世代傳遞的生態壓力（如溫度、飲食、病原體及社會經濟因素）有關。這些變異對健康不平等有重大影響，深入理解其潛在機制有助於設計公共衛生干預措施。文章同時強調需重新審視描述人群體組成變異的術語，特別是「種族/族裔」的使用，因「種族」本身缺乏生物學效度。最終目標是透過改進對生物學與族裔關係的理解，以減少健康不平等。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻指出，目前對人群體組成變異的研究在術語使用上存在局限，特別是「種族」雖無生物學效度，卻可能反映種族主義和歧視的暴露。未來的研究應更精確地理解與族裔相關的多重生物學組成，以釐清人群體組成變異的真實來源，並為設計更有效的公共衛生介入以減少健康不平等提供基礎。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Body composition, geography and ethnicity: understanding the associations with health.
**Abstract:** Human body composition varies substantially across populations, for a range of different reasons that remain poorly understood. Similar to body size and limb proportions, both fat-free and fat mass are geographically patterned, which may relate both to ancestry but also to persisting ecological stresses, whose effects may be transmitted through the life-course and across generations. The role of genetics in such variability remains uncertain, moreover relevant ecological stresses include not only physical (e.g. temperature) and biological (e.g. diet, subsistence, pathogen burden) components, but also societal factors including colonisation, conflict, migration and economic inequality. Body composition variability has major implications for health inequalities, and improving our understanding of the different underlying mechanisms may help us understand how to design public health interventions, and optimise the prevention and management of disease. However, the terminology used to address population variability in body composition needs to be reconsidered. Terms such as race/ethnicity, ancestry and geography are widely used to frame investigations and interpret findings, but the variable most often collected in studies is ethnicity, selected by research participants on the basis of their identity. Race has no biological validity, but may be a marker of exposure to racism and discrimination. Improved understanding of the multiple components of biology that relate to ethnicity may help understand the sources of population variability in body composition, and hence improve efforts to reduce health inequalities.
