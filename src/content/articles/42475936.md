### 重點摘要
這項回溯性世代研究旨在評估兒童居家空腸管灌餵養 (JTF) 患者的銅缺乏率，並與胃造口餵養患者進行比較，因為當前JTF患者每年測量銅的建議證據有限。研究分析了76名JTF患者與144名胃造口餵養患者（分為簡單型與複雜型）的銅水平，結果顯示，複雜型胃造口餵養和JTF獨立地與顯著銅缺乏風險相關，其中JTF患者的缺乏程度更深，更容易出現血球減少症並需要補充。這些數據支持對JTF患者進行年度銅測量，並建議將銅篩查擴展到具有相關風險因素的胃造口餵養患者。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
該研究透過大規模回溯性數據，填補了兒童空腸管灌餵養患者年度銅測量建議缺乏強而有力證據的空白，並首次明確指出具有複雜胃腸道疾病的胃造口餵養患者以及具有神經殘疾的兒童，也是銅缺乏的高風險群體。這將有助於更精準地識別需要篩查的患者，優化臨床指引。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** The hierarchy of copper status in paediatric enteral tube feeding: A comparative retrospective cohort study of jejunal and gastric feeding.
**Abstract:** Jejunal tube feeding (JTF) is associated with micronutrient deficiencies, including copper which is an essential trace element. Current paediatric guidelines recommend annual copper measurement in JTF patients based on limited evidence. We aimed to determine the rate of copper depletion and deficiency in a 21-year cohort of paediatric home JTF patients and compare outcomes with paediatric gastrostomy-fed patients. This was a retrospective cohort study in a tertiary paediatric specialist nutrition service. 76 JTF patients (2002-2023) were compared to 144 gastrostomy fed patients (2013-2023) who were categorised into simple (sGF) (n = 124) and complex (cGF) (n = 20) gastric groups based on underlying gastrointestinal comorbidities. All available copper levels were analysed in all groups with significant depletion defined as >10% below the lower limit of normal (LLN). Patients with consequential deficiency and those who received repletion were recorded. Multivariate logistic regression was used to identify independent risk factors for copper depletion. The cGF group were most likely to have ever had copper depletion (25%), followed the JTF group (13%), then the sGF group (2.5%) (p=<0.005). On multivariate analysis, cGF (odds ratio (OR) 17.3, 95% confidence interval (CI) 2.81-116) and JTF (OR 6.27, 95% CI 1.82-28.9) were independently associated with depletion compared to sGF. Neurodisability (OR 5.41, 95% CI 1.29-39.0) was also an independent risk factor. Copper-deplete JTF patients had significantly lower absolute copper levels (median 8.7 μmol/L, interquartile range (IQR) 2.1-11.0) compared to 11.7 μmol/L (IQR 9.5-12.7) for the sGF group and 10.7 μmol/L (IQR 4.5-11.2) for the cGF group. JTF patients were more likely to develop cytopenia and require repletion compared to the gastrostomy fed groups. All deplete patients who received supplementation with gastric copper sulphate subsequently normalised their levels. Both jejunally fed and gastrically fed patients with complex GI pathology are at a significantly increased risk of copper depletion compared to other gastrically fed patients. However, JTF patients experience more profound depletion and are more likely to develop consequential cytopenia and require copper repletion, likely due to bypassing of the usual mechanisms of digestion and absorption in the upper GI tract. These data support yearly copper measurement in JTF patients and we endorse extending screening to gastrostomy fed patients with relevant risk factors.
