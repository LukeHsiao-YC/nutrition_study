### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討高血壓老年人（≥ 60歲）採行健康植物性飲食的心理社會因素和人口統計學特徵。研究透過橫斷面設計，分析了來自馬里蘭州社區高血壓專案的212名高血壓老年人數據，評估其植物性飲食攝取量、感知障礙、準備程度和社會人口學資訊。結果顯示，男性、處於飲食改變行動/維持階段、較少感知障礙、有較佳高血壓自我照護習慣且慢性病較少的老年人，更可能攝取健康植物性飲食，而與家人同住者攝取健康植物性飲食的可能性較低。瞭解這些特徵有助於開發更具針對性的干預措施，以改變飲食模式並改善健康結果。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了高血壓老年人採行植物性飲食的心理社會因素和人口統計學特徵的知識空白，為未來設計更具針對性的飲食干預措施提供了重要基礎。由於研究採用橫斷面設計，無法建立因果關係，未來的研究應考慮縱向追蹤或介入性試驗來進一步驗證這些關聯性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Examining Plant-Based Dietary Adoption Among Hypertensive Older Adults: Characterizing Psychosocial Factors.
**Abstract:** Examine and identify psychosocial factors and demographic characteristics associated with a healthy plant-based diet (PBD) consumption among hypertensive older adults (≥ 60 years). Cross-sectional baseline data from a community-based hypertension project. Senior centers/living across Maryland. Hypertensive, community-dwelling older adults (n = 212). Plant-based diet consumption based on the healthful PBD index (hPDI). Perceived barriers and benefits related to PBDs, readiness to consume a PBD, and sociodemographic information were also collected. Ordinal logistic regression analysis. Older individuals more likely to consume a healthy PBD were men (odds ratio [OR] = 2.60 [95% confidence interval (CI), 1.07-6.32]), lived with family members (OR = 0.17 [95% CI, 0.05-0.60], P = 0.006), had higher levels of readiness, at the action/maintenance stages (OR = 2.76 [95% CI, 1.24-6.02], P = 0.01), had fewer perceived barriers (OR = 0.34 [95% CI, 0.19-0.61], P < 0.001), maintained better hypertensive, self-care practices (medication adherence, low-salt diet, physical activity, and smoking) (OR = 1.25 [95% CI, 1.14-1.37], P < 0.001), and had fewer chronic diseases overall (OR = 0.85 [95% CI, 0.76-0.95], P = 0.004). Understanding older adults' characteristics such as perceptions, readiness, and self-care practices associated with PBD consumptions is an important initial step to more targeted interventions to shift dietary patterns that impact health outcomes.
