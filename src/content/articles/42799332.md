### 重點摘要
這項系統性回顧與統合分析旨在探討休閒足球運動對超重/肥胖兒童與青少年的身體測量、代謝指標、體能和心理健康的影響。研究採用PRISMA 2020指南，檢索了七個資料庫，並使用RevMan 5.4進行隨機效應統合分析。統合分析結果顯示，休閒足球顯著降低了身體質量指數(BMI)，並有強而有力的證據表明能改善腰圍和收縮壓等身體測量和心血管代謝指標。總體而言，定期參與休閒足球能改善超重/肥胖青少年的身體測量和心血管代謝結果。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
儘管研究證實休閒足球對身體測量和心血管代謝有益，但對於其他健康結果（如體能和心理健康）的影響，由於相關研究數量有限，目前僅為初步發現，仍需未來更多研究進一步證實。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effects of Recreational Soccer on Anthropometric Measures, Metabolic Markers, Physical Fitness, and Mental Health in Children and Adolescents with Overweight/Obesity: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
**Abstract:** This systematic review examined the effects of recreational soccer (RS) on anthropometric, metabolic, physical fitness, and mental health outcomes in youth with overweight/obesity. Following PRISMA 2020, this review searched seven databases, assessed quality with Cochrane RoB 2 and PEDro, performed meta-analysis in RevMan 5.4, used random-effects meta-analysis, and graded evidence certainty with GRADE. Meta-analysis showed that RS was associated with significant reductions in BMI  Regular RS participation improves anthropometric and cardiometabolic outcomes in children and adolescents with overweight/obesity, with robust evidence for reductions in WC and SBP. Findings for other outcomes are preliminary due to the limited number of studies and require further confirmation.
