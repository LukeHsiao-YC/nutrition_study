### 重點摘要
這項描述性研究利用遠程食物攝影法，對38對兒童與照顧者進行為期三天的評估，旨在描述提供給兒童的食物、餐盤剩餘量及實際攝取情況。研究結果顯示，所提供食物的鈉、飽和脂肪和添加糖含量顯著超過2020年美國飲食指南（DGA-2020）建議，而纖維和鈣則未達最低標準。此外，所提供食物的健康飲食指數-2020分數高於實際攝取食物的分數，表明兒童可能未充分攝取較健康的選項。研究結論指出，有必要實施旨在改善提供給兒童食物品質的介入措施。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究量化了學齡兒童所獲食物與實際攝取食物在營養成分上與美國飲食指南的差距，特別指出食物中鈉、飽和脂肪和添加糖含量過高，而纖維和鈣則不足。它揭示了改善兒童飲食質量不僅需要關注提供的食物，也需考慮實際的攝取情況。未來的研究應著重於開發並實施旨在提升兒童所獲食物品質的介入措施。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Foods Offered and Plate Waste Among School-Aged Children: A Descriptive Study.
**Abstract:** To (1) describe foods offered to children and their plate waste, (2) assess how foods offered compare to the 2020 Dietary Guidelines for Americans (DGA-2020), and (3) compare foods offered with foods consumed. We conducted a 3-day assessment with the remote food photography method (RFPM) among 38 child-caregiver dyads. Variables measured were energy, nutrients, and Healthy Eating Index-2020 scores for foods offered, consumed, and remaining as plate waste. Linear mixed models examined differences between foods offered, recommended intakes, and foods consumed. Foods offered exceeded the DGA-2020 (difference) for sodium (1,034.5 mg), saturated fat (2%), and added sugars (11%), and did not meet the minimum DGA-2020 threshold for fiber (-5.18 g) and calcium (-228.7 mg). Foods offered had a Healthy Eating Index-2020 score 1.3 units higher than the score of foods consumed. Interventions that target improvements in the quality of foods offered to children are needed.
