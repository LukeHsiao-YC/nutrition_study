### 重點摘要
小兒腸道假性阻塞 (PIPO) 是一種嚴重的胃腸道蠕動障礙，常需腸造口術但最佳位置不明。本研究首次將混合式腸道傳輸閃爍攝影術結合單光子電腦斷層掃描 (ITS-SPECT/CT) 應用於五名 PIPO 患兒，以 3D 影像精確定位功能受損的腸段。所有患者術後無嚴重併發症，12-24 個月追蹤顯示造口排泄量及腸道餵食耐受性均有改善，靜脈營養顯著減少或停用。研究結論指出 ITS-SPECT/CT 是一種安全、客觀的術前工具，可為 PIPO 提供個體化且功能導向的手術規劃。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了在小兒腸道假性阻塞 (PIPO) 患者中，缺乏客觀且能精確定位腸造口部位的術前評估工具之研究空白。透過首次在兒童中應用 ITS-SPECT/CT，它解決了傳統平面閃爍攝影術的組織重疊問題，實現了功能導向的精確手術規劃。未來的研究可能需要更大規模的患者群體來進一步驗證這些發現。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Preoperative intestinal transit scintigraphy for targeted surgery in pediatric intestinal pseudo-obstruction.
**Abstract:** Pediatric intestinal pseudo-obstruction (PIPO) is a severe gastrointestinal motility disorder for which surgical enterostomy is often required to achieve effective bowel decompression and optimize nutrition, although the optimal site of diversion remains uncertain. We report the first pediatric case series utilizing hybrid Intestinal Transit Scintigraphy with Single Photon Emission Computed Tomography/Computed Tomography (ITS-SPECT/CT) to guide enterostomy placement. Five children with confirmed PIPO underwent ITS-SPECT/CT with ^67Ga-citrate to map functionally impaired segments. Unlike conventional planar scintigraphy, this 3D tomographic approach eliminated tissue overlap, precisely localizing stable regions of tracer retention that directly informed surgical planning. All patients underwent targeted interventions without major complications. At 12-24 months of follow-up, all patients showed improved stoma output and enteral feeding tolerance, with discontinuation or substantial reduction of parenteral nutrition. These findings suggest that ITS-SPECT/CT is a safe and objective preoperative tool for individualized surgical planning in PIPO, enabling precise, functionally guided interventions.
