### 重點摘要
這項隨機實驗旨在評估包裝正面標籤（FOPL）對美國家長為子女選擇產品及相關認知上的影響，特別是針對添加糖和非糖甜味劑（NSS）。研究將近三千名家長隨機分為無標籤對照組、僅有添加糖標籤組，以及同時有添加糖和NSS標籤組，並請他們選擇果汁和優格產品。結果顯示，看見同時標示添加糖和NSS的家長，更有可能選擇不含添加糖或NSS的產品，並認為這些產品最健康。這表明結合添加糖和NSS的FOPL可能有效引導家長為孩子做出更健康的飲食選擇。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
該研究填補了關於同時使用添加糖和非糖甜味劑（NSS）包裝正面標籤（FOPL）對家長為子女產品選擇影響的實證空白，特別是揭示了NSS標籤在影響家長對含NSS產品的購買意願和健康認知方面的重要性。未來的研究可以探討這些標籤在真實購物環境中的長期影響，以及對不同產品類別的適用性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effects of front-of-package labels for added sugars and non-sugar sweeteners on parents' selections for their children: a randomized experiment.
**Abstract:** This study aimed to evaluate effects of front-of-package labels (FOPLs) for added sugars and non-sugar sweeteners (NSS) on U.S. parents' product selections and perceptions. Parents of children age 2-12 years (n= 2,896) completed an online survey in November 2024 and were randomized to: no-FOPL control (n = 990), added sugar FOPL (n = 949), or FOPLs for both added sugars and NSS (n = 957). Participants were shown four fruit drinks and four yogurts. For each set, one product did not contain added sugars or NSS, one was sweetened with added sugars, one with NSS, and one had added sugars and NSS. Products were shown in random order, with FOPLs per assigned treatment arm. Participants also viewed six NSS-containing products separately and reported their purchase intentions and product perceptions. Data were analyzed in 2025. Participants exposed to the added sugar and NSS FOPLs were more likely to choose the fruit drink without added sugars or NSS (58% versus 39% for added sugar FOPL (p<0.001) and 32% for control (p<0.001)) and yogurt without added sugars or NSS (60% versus 29% for added sugar FOPL (p<0.001) and 14% for control (p<0.001)). Participants in the added sugar and NSS FOPL arm were also more likely to perceive products without added sugars or NSS as the healthiest (70% for fruit drinks, 76% for yogurts), compared to the added sugar FOPL arm (46% for fruit drinks, 49% for yogurts) and control (37% for fruit drinks, 33% for yogurts) (all p<0.001). Those who viewed NSS FOPLs had lower purchase intentions and perceptions of product healthfulness for NSS-containing products, and higher perceptions of product sweetness compared to the added sugar FOPL or control arms. Viewing products with both added sugar and NSS FOPLs increased the likelihood of choosing products without added sugars or NSS and lowered parents' purchase intentions and healthfulness perceptions of NSS-containing products. FOPLs for NSS and added sugar may support parents in making potentially healthier choices for their children.
