### 重點摘要
高於1.04%的工業生產反式脂肪酸(IP-TFA)紅血球水平與死亡率增加相關，而美國的IP-TFA水平則呈現下降趨勢。儘管歐洲在2014-2015年僅有1.3%的樣本超標，但近期其IP-TFA水平顯著上升，從2024年的2.12%增至2026年上半年的7.21%。此上升主要由反式油酸(18:1t)驅動，而非乳製品攝取，並推測來源於摻假橄欖油。這項趨勢預示著歐洲的死亡率可能隨之增加。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻揭示了歐洲近年來工業生產反式脂肪酸水平的上升趨勢，填補了關於歐洲地區反式脂肪酸攝取量最新變化的數據空白。然而，研究的限制是其推論主要基於反式脂肪酸與死亡率的已知關聯，並未直接測量此一時期內歐洲的死亡率變化，也未直接證明特定食品（如摻假橄欖油）是主要驅動因素。未來的研究需要更直接地連結歐洲的IP-TFA上升與具體健康結果，並進一步探討其膳食來源。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Increasing levels of industrially derived trans fatty acids in European erythrocytes.
**Abstract:** Combined erythrocyte levels of C18:1t, 18:2tc, 18:2tt, and 18:2ct (or industrially-produced trans fatty acids, IP-TFA) > 1.04% are associated with increased mortality, as compared with lower levels. In the United States, mean erythrocyte levels of IP-TFA declined from 2009 to 2016, with a parallel decline in mortality. In Europe, in 2014 and 2015, only 1.3% of erythrocyte samples had levels of IP-TFA > 1.04%. However, recently IP-TFA levels increased, driven only by increases of 18:1t. In 2024, in 2.12% of 20,510 samples, IP-TFA were >1.04%; in 2025, in 4.47% of 27,834 samples; in January - June 2026, in 7.21% of 18,577 samples (p for trend <0.001). Since levels of C16:1n7t, a marker of dairy intake like vaccenic acid (C18:1t-11), remained constant from 2020 to 2026, we assume the increase to be driven mainly by elaidic acid (C18:1t-9), detected, e.g., in adulterated olive oil. This increase is likely to be associated with increased mortality in Europe.
