### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估後生元Akkermansia muciniphila (AKK)補充劑對肥胖相關多內分泌代謝性卵巢症候群 (PMOS) 患者的輔助效果。一項針對100名PMOS女性的隨機對照試驗顯示，在標準口服避孕藥治療的基礎上，AKK後生元補充劑顯著改善了體重、高雄激素表現、葡萄糖-胰島素穩態及血脂異常。此外，AKK干預也促進了腸道菌相的有利變化，包括微生物多樣性增加和特定菌群豐度改變。本研究為AKK後生元輔助治療PMOS的代謝和內分泌異常提供了首個臨床證據，為PMOS治療策略奠定基礎。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
儘管Akkermansia muciniphila在代謝疾病方面具有顯著治療潛力，但由於其在中國食品微生物菌株目錄中的限制，臨床研究一直稀缺。本研究首次提供了AKK後生元在肥胖相關多內分泌代謝性卵巢症候群患者中的臨床證據，填補了該領域的臨床研究空白，並為未來將AKK轉化為臨床應用提供了堅實基礎。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Adjunctive Akkermansia muciniphila postbiotic supplementation modulates metabolic and endocrine profiles in obesity-associated polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome: A randomized controlled trial.
**Abstract:** As a flagship next-generation probiotic, Akkermansia muciniphila (AKK) has been extensively validated for its prominent therapeutic advantages against metabolic diseases. However, clinical research on AKK remains scarce owing to its exclusion from the Official Catalog of Approved Food Microbial Strains in China. In this study, 100 women with obesity-associated polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome (PMOS) receiving standardized oral contraceptive therapy were randomized to receive AKK postbiotic supplementation or placebo, and the adjunctive effects of AKK postbiotics were evaluated on body weight, hyperandrogenic manifestations, glucose-lipid metabolism, and gut microbiota composition. The results showed that AKK postbiotic intervention achieved a significantly greater reduction in weight than placebo (1.92 vs. 0.37 kg, p < 0.001), relieved hyperandrogenic manifestations including hirsutism and acne. Additionally, AKK postbiotics markedly improved glucose-insulin homeostasis and dyslipidemia, with significantly greater reductions from baseline observed in serum LH levels (3.67 vs. 1.98 mIU/mL, p < 0.05), LH/FSH ratio (0.70 vs. 0.37, p < 0.05), and testosterone concentrations (7.74 vs. 4.97 ng/dL, p < 0.05) compared with placebo. Furthermore, AKK postbiotics were associated with favorable post-intervention microbial characteristics, including increased microbial diversity and altered abundance patterns of specific taxa, such as enrichment of Bifidobacterium and Faecalibacterium and decreased abundance of Bacteroides and Fusobacterium. Taken together, this study provides the first clinical evidence that adjunctive AKK postbiotic supplementation combined with standard oral contraceptive therapy may further improve metabolic and endocrine abnormalities in women with obesity-associated PMOS, thus establishing a promising therapeutic strategy for PMOS and laying a robust foundation for the future clinical translation of AKK.
