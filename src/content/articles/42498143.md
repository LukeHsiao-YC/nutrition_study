### 重點摘要
這項混合方法研究調查了中國杭州334個嬰幼兒家庭中，跨代共親職模式如何影響母親的餵養行為。研究識別出低參與衝突、中等和高參與和諧三種共親職模式，並發現中等模式的母親展現出更優質的回應式餵養，表現為較少施壓和工具性餵養。質性訪談進一步揭示，中等模式的優勢源於一種「有限度協作」模型，其特徵是明確的任務界限、低衝突協商以及祖父母支持與母親決策自主權之間的策略平衡。研究結果強調，在城市跨代家庭中，優化回應式餵養更取決於維持母親的決策自主權，而非一味追求絕對的家庭和諧。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了以往研究對於不同跨代共親職模式如何具體影響母親在嬰幼兒輔助性餵養期間實踐的不足，特別是針對中國城市家庭的獨特背景。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Intergenerational Coparenting and Maternal Feeding Practices among Infants and Toddlers in Hangzhou, China: A Mixed-Methods Study.
**Abstract:** Intergenerational coparenting is common in urban Chinese families, yet how distinct coparenting profiles influence maternal feeding practices during complementary feeding remains underexamined. This explanatory sequential mixed-methods study aimed to identify latent intergenerational coparenting profiles among 334 families with infants and toddlers (6-24 months) in Hangzhou, China, and to examine differences in maternal feeding practices across these profiles. Latent Profile Analysis based on the Coparenting Relationship Scale identified three distinct profiles: Low-Involvement Conflict (16.2%), Intermediate (48.2%), and High-Involvement Harmonious (35.6%). MANCOVA results revealed a non-linear association: mothers in the Intermediate profile exhibited significantly higher responsive feeding quality, evidenced by less pressure to eat and less instrumental feeding, than those in both the High-Involvement Harmonious and Low-Involvement Conflict groups (p < .001). Subsequent semi-structured interviews with 18 mothers (six per profile), combined with cross-profile comparisons, revealed that the advantage of Intermediate families stemmed from a "bounded collaboration" model. This model is characterized by clearly demarcated task boundaries, low-confrontation negotiation, and a strategic balance between grandparental support and maternal decision-making autonomy. These findings suggest that in urban intergenerational settings, optimizing responsive feeding depends more on maintaining maternal decision-making autonomy than on pursuing absolute family harmony.
