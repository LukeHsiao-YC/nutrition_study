### 重點摘要
本研究探討了10-12歲中童失調性飲食態度、正念飲食與類成癮性飲食症狀之間的關聯，特別聚焦於無意識飲食的間接作用。這項對1060名兒童進行的橫斷面研究，採用多種問卷收集數據，並利用相關性、多元線性回歸及探索性間接效應模型進行分析。結果顯示，BMI類別和跳餐具有顯著影響，且無意識飲食與類成癮性飲食症狀呈現最強的獨立關聯。這表明無意識飲食是中童類成癮性飲食症狀的重要行為關聯，可能作為失調性飲食態度與類成癮性飲食行為之間的橋樑。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了在10-12歲中童中，無意識飲食如何作為失調性飲食態度與類成癮性飲食症狀之間行為關聯的空白，為理解此關鍵發展階段的飲食模式提供了新視角。然而，作為一項橫斷面研究，它無法確立因果關係，未來的縱向研究將需要進一步驗證這些關聯並探索針對無意識飲食的干預策略。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Mindless eating as a behavioural correlate of addictive-like eating symptoms in 10-12-year-old children: associations with disordered eating attitudes.
**Abstract:** Middle childhood is a critical developmental period for the emergence of eating-related self-regulatory processes and maladaptive eating patterns. This cross-sectional study of 1060 children aged 10-12 years examined associations among disordered eating attitudes, mindful eating dimensions and addictive-like eating symptoms, focusing on the indirect role of mindless eating. Data were collected using a socio-demographic form, the Mindful Eating Questionnaire for Children (MEQ-C), the dimensional Yale Food Addiction Scale for Children 2.0, the Stunkard Pictogram and the Children's Eating Attitudes Test. Correlation analyses were conducted to examine associations among variables. Multiple linear regression was used to examine variables independently associated with addictive-like eating symptoms. An exploratory indirect-effect model tested whether mindless eating was involved in the statistical association between disordered eating attitudes and addictive-like eating symptoms. BMI category and meal skipping showed significant multivariate effects.
