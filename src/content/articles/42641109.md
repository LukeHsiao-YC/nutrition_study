### 重點摘要
這項系統性文獻回顧旨在評估兒童和青少年維生素D缺乏與心血管風險因子之間的關聯，因其潛在的早期心臟代謝功能障礙貢獻及證據的爭議性。研究依循PRISMA指南，廣泛檢索相關文獻，納入評估血清25(OH)D水平與心血管風險因子的兒科研究。結果顯示，觀察性研究中維生素D與肥胖指標呈一致的負相關，但與其他風險因子的關聯性不一且常在調整後減弱；介入性研究則顯示維生素D補充劑的影響微乎其微。總體而言，目前證據不支持維生素D缺乏與兒科心血管風險之間存在明確的因果關係，其關聯性似乎主要受混淆因子和共同生活方式決定因素影響。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻回顧填補了對維生素D缺乏與兒童青少年心血管風險因子之間關聯性、獨立性和臨床相關性方面知識的系統性評估空白。然而，研究結論指出，現有證據尚不足以支持兩者間存在明確的因果關係，且觀察到的關聯性可能受混淆因子和共同生活方式的影響。未來的研究仍需透過設計良好的縱向研究和隨機對照試驗，以釐清維生素D是否在心血管風險的早期發展中扮演獨立角色。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Association Between Vitamin D Deficiency and Cardiovascular Risk Factors in Children and Adolescents: A Systematic Review.
**Abstract:** Vitamin D deficiency is prevalent among children and adolescents and has emerged as a potential contributor to early cardiometabolic dysfunction. Growing evidence suggests links between low serum 25-hydroxyvitamin D (25[OH]D) concentrations and adverse cardiovascular risk profiles; however, the magnitude, independence, and clinical relevance of these associations in pediatric populations remain controversial. This review aimed to critically evaluate evidence regarding the relationship between vitamin D deficiency and cardiovascular risk factors in pediatric populations. This review was conducted in accordance with Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic reviews and Meta-Analyses (PRISMA) guidelines and registered in the International Prospective Register of Systematic Reviews (PROSPERO). A comprehensive electronic search of PubMed, SciELO, and Embase was performed up to September 10, 2025. Studies assessing serum (25[OH]D) levels in relation to established cardiovascular risk factors in pediatric populations were included. Risks of bias and overall quality of evidence were evaluated. Findings were synthesized through a structured qualitative narrative approach. In observational studies, a consistent inverse association was observed with markers of adiposity, with lower circulating 25(OH)D concentrations repeatedly reported in study participants with overweight or obesity. Associations with dyslipidemia, insulin resistance, and elevated blood pressure were also described; however, these relationships were heterogeneous and frequently attenuated after adjustment. Interventional studies showed largely null or clinically modest effects of vitamin D supplementation on pediatric cardiovascular risk. Considerable heterogeneity was observed across studies regarding participant age, developmental stage, vitamin D thresholds, outcome definitions, and study design. Current evidence does not support a clearly established causal relationship between vitamin D deficiency and pediatric cardiovascular risk. The observed associations with certain cardiovascular risk markers, particularly those related to obesity and adiposity, appear substantially influenced by confounding and shared lifestyle determinants, and further research is needed to fully elucidate the nature of the relationship. Vitamin D status should be interpreted within a broader cardiometabolic and behavioural framework. Well-designed longitudinal studies and randomized controlled trials are warranted to clarify whether vitamin D plays an independent role in the early development of cardiovascular risk. PROSPERO registration No. [CRD420251137216].
