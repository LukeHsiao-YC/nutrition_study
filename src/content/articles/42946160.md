### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討午餐時間長度、下課時間安排與運動機會等學校政策，如何影響安克拉治學童的學校餐點攝取。研究採橫斷面設計，分析了2,963份小學生的午餐餐盤浪費情況，比較了實施「健康促進計畫」（午餐與下課時間更長、有更多活動）及下課時間在午餐前後的差異。結果顯示，「健康促進計畫」與主菜、牛奶和水果的攝取量增加顯著相關；而下課時間在午餐前，則與主菜、牛奶、水果和蔬菜的攝取量增加有關。研究結論指出，支持足夠用餐時間、體育活動以及午餐前下課的政策，有潛力改善學生的餐點攝取。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了在阿拉斯加安克拉治地區小學生群體中，學校營養與體育活動政策對飲食影響的知識空白。然而，由於是橫斷面研究設計，它僅能呈現相關性而非因果關係，且其發現可能不完全適用於其他地區或學齡層的學生。未來的研究需要探索這些政策的長期效果及在更廣泛人群中的適用性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Longer Lunch Periods, More Student Movement Opportunities, and Recess Before Lunch Associated with Greater School Meal Consumption in Anchorage, Alaska.
**Abstract:** School nutrition and physical activity policies related to lunch period lengths, recess timing and duration, and movement breaks have the potential to improve children's diets and overall health, but more research is needed to understand their impact on diverse student populations. The aims were to examine the associations between a multi-component initiative related to lunch period lengths, recess timing and duration, and more movement breaks with school meal consumption. This was a cross-sectional post-test only study. /Setting: Elementary schools with differing recess timing (before vs. after lunch) and implementation of a Wellness Initiative (longer lunch periods [30 vs 20 minutes], longer recess [30 vs 20 minutes], and movement breaks) in Anchorage, Alaska were examined during the 2022-23 school year. A total of 2,963 school lunch trays were analyzed. Consumption was measured using plate waste. Mixed-model analysis of variance, accounting for school as a random effect and student demographics were used to examine differences in school meal consumption by Wellness Initiative implementation and recess timing. The Wellness Initiative was associated with greater consumption of entrées (12.1 percentage points [% pts], standard error [SE] 4.9; p=0.03), milk (13.3% pts [SE=4.7]; p=0.005), and fruit (16.2% pts [SE=5.6]; p=0.004). Additionally, recess before lunch was associated with greater consumption of entrées (5.5% pts [SE=2.3]; p=0.02), milk (11.5% pts [SE=2.3]; p<0.0001), fruits (6.9% pts [SE=3.1]; p=0.02), and vegetables (4.4% pts [SE=2.0]; p=0.03). Policies that support sufficient time to eat, physical activity, and recess before lunch have potential to improve students' meal consumption.
