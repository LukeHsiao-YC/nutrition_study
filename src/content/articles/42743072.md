### 重點摘要
本研究是一項系統性回顧與統合分析，旨在評估限時飲食（TRE）結合運動（EX）對過重或肥胖成人體組成與心血管代謝健康的影響，並與僅運動組（CD+EX）進行比較。研究納入了11項隨機對照試驗，結果顯示TRE+EX組在體重、體脂肪、腰圍、身體質量指數（BMI）和胰島素阻抗指數（HOMA-IR）方面有更顯著的改善。然而，對於脂肪不變量、血脂和血壓則無顯著差異。研究結論指出，TRE+EX能改善體組成和HOMA-IR，且較長期的介入可能對血糖標誌物有更大益處。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了過重或肥胖成人中，限時飲食與運動結合介入效果的實證空白。然而，研究發現TRE+EX對血脂和血壓的影響不明顯，且仍需更大規模、更長時間追蹤的隨機對照試驗，以確認這些發現並確立最佳的限時飲食與運動組合方案。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effects of Time-Restricted Eating Combined with Exercise on Body Composition and Cardiometabolic Health in Adults with Overweight or Obesity: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
**Abstract:** The global prevalence of overweight and obesity continues to rise, imposing substantial health and economic burdens. Time-restricted eating (TRE) and exercise each improve body composition and cardiometabolic health, although both carry limitations when applied in isolation. However, evidence specifically examining their combined effects in adults with overweight or obesity remains limited. To synthesize evidence from randomized controlled trials (RCTs) to evaluate the effects of TRE combined with exercise (TRE + EX) compared with a control diet (CD) plus exercise (CD + EX) on body composition and cardiometabolic health in adults with overweight or obesity. The PubMed, Web of Science, Embase, and the Cochrane Library databases were searched from inception through April 2026, with reference lists additionally screened. Two reviewers independently extracted study characteristics, participant demographics, intervention protocols, and outcome data. Eleven RCTs enrolling 602 participants were included. Compared with CD + EX, TRE + EX produced modest reductions in body weight (WMD = -1.64 kg; 95% CI, -2.49 to -0.79), fat mass (WMD = -1.30 kg; 95% CI, -1.72 to -0.89), and waist circumference (WMD = -1.53 cm; 95% CI, -2.61 to -0.44), and significant improvements in body mass index (WMD = -0.83 kg m-2; 95% CI, -1.34 to -0.33) and Homeostasis Model Assessment of Insulin Resistance (HOMA-IR) (WMD = -0.44; 95% CI, -0.82 to -0.06), without significantly affecting fat-free mass. No significant between-group differences were observed for fasting glucose, fasting insulin, hemoglobin A1c, lipid profiles, or blood pressure. Subgroup analyses indicated that interventions longer than 8 weeks produced significantly greater reductions in fasting glucose, fasting insulin, and HOMA-IR values. Compared with CD + EX, TRE + EX improved body composition and HOMA-IR in adults with overweight or obesity, whereas no significant differences were observed in lipid profiles or blood pressure. Furthermore, longer intervention durations may contribute to greater improvements in glycemic markers. Large-scale RCTs with longer follow-up are needed to confirm these findings and identify optimal TRE + EX combinations. PROSPERO registration No. CRD420261298734.
