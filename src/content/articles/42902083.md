### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討參與居家訪視計畫的圍產期母親自殘意念的盛行率和預測因子。研究分析了9638名美國母親在產前及產後六個月內完成愛丁堡產後憂鬱量表的數據，特別關注其第10項自殘意念的報告。結果顯示，6.8%的母親在產前有自殘意念，產後為4.0%，且年輕母親的自殘意念程度高於年長母親。研究強調，鑑於圍產期母親自殘風險未被識別和治療的危險性，居家訪視員在支持並連結母親至心理健康服務方面扮演著獨特而重要的角色。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究提供了居家訪視計畫中圍產期母親自殘意念的具體盛行率和相關預測因子數據，填補了此特定高風險族群相關知識的空白，並突顯了對此類母親進行早期識別與介入的迫切性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Self-Harm Ideation in Perinatal Mothers in Home Visiting Programs.
**Abstract:** To examine the prevalence and predictors of self-harm ideation during the perinatal period among mothers (N = 9638) enrolled in a home visiting program. Subjects were all mothers enrolled in a home visiting program in the United States between 2006 and 2023 who completed the Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS; Cox et al., 1987) prenatally and/or at up to 6 months postpartum. Item 10 of the EPDS, reflecting thoughts of self-harm, was used to operationalize self-harm ideation and severity. Demographic and clinical variables were examined as predictors of self-harm ideation and change over the two time points. 6.8% of the sample reported self-harm ideation prenatally, with 33.8% of these mothers endorsing the two highest levels of severity. 4.0% of the sample reported self-harm ideation postnatally. Of those who reported self-harm ideation prenatally, 77.9% no longer reported it postnatally. Reporting of self-harm ideation was associated with mean EPDS scores above the established clinical cutoff (≥ 13). Younger mothers reported higher levels of ideation compared with older mothers. A large proportion of mothers reported self-harm ideation prenatally, and younger mothers reported higher levels of ideation compared with older mothers. Given the dangers of unrecognized and untreated suicide risk in perinatal mothers, home visitors are in a unique and important position to support and link mothers to mental health services.
