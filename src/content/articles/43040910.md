### 重點摘要
INFANT計畫是一個在澳洲推行的早期生活健康行為計畫，旨在預防兒童過重與肥胖。本研究透過對INFANT協調員和推動者的半結構式訪談，探索了支持該計畫大規模持續推行的實施策略。研究識別出14項實施策略，分屬五個主題，這些策略被認為對維持計畫推動至關重要。然而，多數策略依賴個人與組織努力，並受限於資金不足與人力，未來需有系統層級的策略以優化計畫持續性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了在真實世界中，大規模健康行為計畫如何長期持續推行的實施策略與挑戰的實證研究空白。它揭示了計畫持續推動的關鍵成功因素，同時也指出目前主要依賴個別與組織努力，且受限於資金和人力，強調未來需要更強大的系統層級支援來優化計畫的持續性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Supporting the Sustained Delivery of an Early Life Health Behaviour Program at Scale: A Qualitative Study of INFANT Implementation Strategies.
**Abstract:** Early life is critical for preventing childhood overweight and obesity. INfant Feeding, Active play and NuTrition (INFANT) is an evidence-based health behaviour program supporting parents with infant feeding and active play, delivered at scale in Victoria, Australia. This study explored the perspectives of INFANT-trained coordinators and facilitators, focusing on implementation strategies supporting at-scale sustained delivery. A multi-site qualitative exploratory study was conducted (2022-2024). Semi-structured interviews were undertaken with coordinators and facilitators at 12- and 24-months post-training. Transcripts were analysed thematically using a hybrid deductive-inductive approach, guided by the Implementation Strategies Applied in Communities framework. 19 participants from 15 of 41 local government areas with sustained INFANT delivery (7 metropolitan, 8 regional) provided insights. Fourteen implementation strategies supporting sustained delivery were identified and organised into five themes: (1) equipping and supporting facilitators; (2) leveraging opportunities, partnerships and funding; (3) providing structured guidance and resources; (4) adapting and tailoring the program; and (5) monitoring delivery through pragmatic evaluation. Participants perceived implementation strategies as important for sustaining delivery. Most relied on individual and organisational efforts and were constrained by inconsistent and inadequate funding and limited workforce capacity. System-level strategies including embedded implementation support are needed to optimise INFANT sustainment.
