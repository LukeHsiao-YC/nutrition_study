### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討2001年至2023年間澳洲人口偶發性與慢性孤獨感及社會孤立感的趨勢。研究採用澳洲家庭、收入與勞動動態研究（HILDA）的縱向世代數據，分析了13,120名參與者的變化。結果顯示，孤獨感整體保持相對穩定，但在2010年代後呈現上升趨勢，而社會孤立感則在研究期間大幅增加，慢性社會孤立感約翻倍。研究結論指出，過去二十年來澳洲社會孤立感顯著增加，孤獨感亦適度上升，且不成比例地影響弱勢族群，突顯了公共衛生干預的必要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究透過長達22年的縱向分析，明確揭示了澳洲人口孤獨感與社會孤立感隨時間變化的具體趨勢，特別是區分了偶發性與慢性形式，並識別出受影響最嚴重的脆弱族群，填補了對此問題長期演變理解的空白。未來的研究應著重於開發和評估針對這些結構性與社會決定因素的公共衛生干預措施。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Have Australians become more lonely and socially isolated? A longitudinal analysis of the Household, Income and Labour Dynamics in Australia study.
**Abstract:** This study aimed to examine temporal trends in episodic and chronic loneliness and social isolation in the Australian population from 2001 to 2023. We conducted a population-based longitudinal cohort study (n = 13 120). Data were drawn from the Household, Income and Labour Dynamics in Australia (HILDA) survey. Episodic (at least one wave) and chronic (at least two consecutive waves) loneliness and social isolation were assessed using validated measures. Mixed-effects logistic regression models estimated marginal predicted probabilities over time and across demographic subgroups. Sensitivity analyses were conducted to test the robustness of the findings. Loneliness remained relatively stable overall, with a slight early decline followed by a modest increase and a gradual rise in chronic loneliness from the late 2000s. In contrast, social isolation increased substantially over time, with chronic isolation roughly doubling across the study period. Loneliness trends generally followed a decline during the 2000s and a rise from the 2010s onwards across most demographic groups, although the magnitude and timing varied between subgroups. In contrast, social isolation increased over time across most populations, with particularly marked increases among older adults and individuals in the lowest education and highest area-level disadvantage. Social isolation increased substantially in Australia over the past two decades, while loneliness showed a moderate increase, disproportionately affecting vulnerable populations. Findings underscore the need for ongoing public health interventions that address the structural and social determinants of loneliness and social isolation.
