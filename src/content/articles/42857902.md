### 重點摘要
這項回溯性世代研究旨在探討新生兒期接受全靜脈營養 (TPN) 是否與嬰兒期長期食物過敏風險增加有關。研究利用韓國國家健康保險服務資料庫，追蹤了超過230萬名嬰兒，其中約7600名新生兒曾接受TPN，並觀察其1歲後的食物過敏診斷。結果顯示，未經調整的分析發現TPN與食物過敏有關，但在經過傾向分數重疊加權調整基線臨床嚴重程度後，TPN暴露與長期食物過敏風險之間並無顯著關聯 (HR 1.00)。研究最終結論為新生兒TPN暴露與長期食物過敏風險無顯著相關，先前未調整的關聯可能歸因於基線臨床嚴重程度的混淆。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了新生兒全靜脈營養 (TPN) 是否會增加長期食物過敏風險的知識空白，透過大規模數據分析並調整了基線臨床嚴重程度等混淆因子，證實兩者之間並無顯著關聯，減輕了臨床上的疑慮。然而，研究結果也指出，儘管未發現顯著關聯，但仍無法完全排除存在輕微風險增加的可能性，這可能是未來研究需要進一步探索的限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Total parenteral nutrition exposure in neonates and the risk of long-term food allergy: A retrospective cohort study.
**Abstract:** Total parenteral nutrition (TPN) is essential for critically ill neonates but bypasses enteral stimulation and may induce gut dysbiosis, raising concern about long-term allergic outcomes. We hypothesized that neonatal TPN exposure is associated with an increased risk of food allergy in infants compared with the absence of TPN. Using the South Korean National Health Insurance Service database, we followed 2,325,275 infants born between 2005 and 2010 from their first birthday until December 31, 2023. Our exposure of interest was TPN administered during the first 28 days of life; our primary outcome was the initial clinical diagnosis of food allergy after the first year of life. Propensity score overlap weighting, targeting the average treatment effect in the overlap population, constituted the primary analysis, with robust sandwich variance estimators. In total, 7661 infants (0.33%) received TPN. Food allergy occurred in 156 TPN-exposed infants (1.33 per 1000 person-years) and 45,608 unexposed infants (1.23 per 1000 person-years). The unadjusted association (hazard ratio [HR], 1.42; 95% CI: 1.21-1.66) was fully attenuated after overlap weighting (HR, 1.00; 95% CI: 0.83-1.21; P = 0.99). Results were consistent in multivariable, pre-exposure-covariate-only, and competing-risks (Fine-Gray) analyses, across food allergy subtypes and high-risk subgroups, and no association was observed per additional day of TPN administration. Neonatal TPN exposure was not significantly associated with the long-term risk of food allergy. The unadjusted association appears attributable to confounding by baseline clinical severity, although a modest increase in risk cannot be excluded.
