### 重點摘要
越來越多證據顯示，父親在受孕前的身體質量指數（BMI）和飲食等因素可能影響後代發育。本研究對一項隨機對照試驗的數據進行二次分析，納入162對父親與子女，探討父親在懷孕第15週回報的BMI、飲食（蛋白質攝取、升糖指數）、吸菸及飲酒習慣與子女出生至五歲期間體重發育的關聯。研究發現在子女出生至三歲期間，父親的特徵與子女發展結果並無一致性關聯，但在子女五歲時，父親較高的BMI與子女較高的BMI z分數及脂肪量指數顯著相關。總體而言，父親BMI與子女五歲時的體重結果相關，而父親飲食因素的關聯則較不一致。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究探討了父系受孕前因素對子代體重發育的影響，特別在子女五歲時觀察到父親BMI的影響，這填補了該領域在時間點上的研究空白，因多數研究多聚焦於母系因素。然而，其父系特徵為孕期回報而非嚴格的受孕前數據，且飲食因素的結果不一致，可能限制了結論的普適性與因果推論。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Associations Between Paternal Pre-Conceptional Body Mass Index and Lifestyle Factors and Offspring Weight Development.
**Abstract:** Evidence suggests that pre-conceptional paternal factors, including BMI and diet, may influence offspring development. We examined the associations between paternal BMI, dietary protein intake, glycemic index (GI), smoking and alcohol consumption and offspring development during the first 5 years of life. This secondary analysis of an RCT included 162 father-child pairs from pregnancies among women with pre-pregnancy overweight or obesity. Paternal characteristics were reported at gestational week 15, reflecting the preceding 3 months. Offspring anthropometry was measured at birth, 6 and 18 months, 3 and 5 years. Associations were examined using linear mixed models and linear regression models. No consistent associations were found between paternal characteristics and offspring outcomes from birth to 3 years.
