### 重點摘要
本研究旨在質性探討照護者在使用整合式行為健康照護中的電子病患入口網時所面臨的障礙與促進因素。透過對7位0-7歲小兒科患者照護者的焦點團體訪談，研究發現照護者普遍認為該入口網有益，並歸納出六個相關主題，涵蓋了外部環境、內部環境及個人特徵。研究結論指出，電子病患入口網對於發展遲緩兒童及尋求行為健康資訊的家庭而言，是有效且廣受歡迎的工具。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了在整合式初級照護背景下，照護者使用電子病患入口網之質性經驗研究的不足。未來的研究和改進應著重於提升服務的個人化程度、確保公平可近性，並將入口網的服務範圍擴展至單一機構之外，以克服現有的推行限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Caregiver Perspectives on Electronic Portal Use in Integrated Primary Care: Qualitative Study.
**Abstract:** Pediatric patient care portals through electronic health records (EHRs) provide opportunities to enhance patient care. However, challenges exist for making portals usable, and few studies have examined qualitative experiences of caregivers when using integrated primary care (IPC) services for their children. This study aimed to qualitatively examine barriers to and facilitators of using electronic patient portals in integrated behavioral health care. Data were collected from 7 caregivers of pediatric patients (0-7 years old) during focus groups. Interview data were analyzed qualitatively and coded by an interdisciplinary group. An iterative approach identified overarching themes within the following Consolidated Framework for Implementation Research (CFIR) domains: outer setting, inner setting, and characteristics of individuals. Overall, caregivers found the portal beneficial. Six themes emerged during thematic analysis. Outer setting themes were "lags and barriers to care" and "connections with outside providers." Inner setting themes were "putting the 'human' in 'human-centered design'" and "virtual access to information." Characteristics of individuals themes were "flexibility" and "personalization." The current study sheds light on caregiver perceptions of electronic portal use. Patient portals are an effective and well-received tool for patients with developmental delays and families accessing behavioral health information. Future targets for improvement include personalization, equitable access, and reach beyond single institutions.
