### 重點摘要
這項以族群為基礎的研究旨在描述兒童期發病發炎性腸道疾病 (pIBD) 患者的共病症及其負擔。研究回溯性追蹤了1344名法國EPIMAD登錄資料中在17歲前診斷的克隆氏症或潰瘍性結腸炎患者，追蹤期中位數為8.3年，以評估多種共病症。結果顯示，最常見的共病症包括體重過輕 (18.4%)、腸道外表現 (15.8%)、皮膚病 (12.7%)、免疫介導的發炎性疾病 (IMID, 9.3%) 和肥胖 (8.5%)。研究證實了pIBD患者常見的慢性共病症，並觀察到肥胖、IMID和皮膚病的發生率在研究期間有所增加。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究透過大規模族群數據，首次詳細描繪了兒童期發病發炎性腸道疾病患者的共病症分佈與負擔，填補了該領域在族群層面缺乏全面性數據的空白。然而，其回溯性設計限制了對因果關係的探討，且未評估這些共病症對患者長期預後及生活品質的具體影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Chronic conditions associated with paediatric onset inflammatory bowel diseases: A population-based study.
**Abstract:** Several conditions are associated with inflammatory bowel disease (IBD), including immune-mediated inflammatory diseases (IMIDs), extraintestinal manifestations (EIMs), and psychiatric disorders. This study aimed to describe at population-level the comorbidities and their relative burden in paediatric-onset IBD (pIBD). All patients diagnosed with Crohn's disease (CD) or ulcerative colitis (UC) before age 17 between 1988 and 2011 and recorded in the EPIMAD population-based registry were retrospectively followed until 2013. Comorbidities included chronic diseases, obesity (defined as a body mass index [BMI] z-score >1.645), and underweight (defined as a BMI z-score <-2), conditions requiring hospitalisation, serious infections, pneumopathy, meningitis, pyelonephritis, IMID, and EIM. A total of 1344 patients were included (1007 CD, 337 UC), with a median age at diagnosis of 14.3 years (interquartile range 11.7-16.0). Over a median follow-up of 8.3 years (4.3-13.9), 15.8% developed at least one EIM, 9.3% at least one IMID, 12.7% developed dermatological conditions, and 5.1% serious infections; 18.4% were classified as underweight and 8.5% as obese. Younger age at diagnosis (<14 years) and more extensive disease were associated with an increased risk of EIM and underweight, while CD was independently associated with underweight (hazard ratio, 3.3; 95% confidence interval, 2.0-5.0). Patients with dermatological conditions or IMID were more frequently treated with biologic therapies. The incidence of obesity, IMID and dermatological conditions increased across the study period. In this population-based pIBD cohort, the most common conditions were underweight, IMID, dermatological diseases, EIM and obesity.
