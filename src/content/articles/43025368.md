### 重點摘要
這項研究探討了同理心在醫療照護中的重要性及其表達方式如何受文化和結構背景影響。研究者透過英國、奈及利亞、日本、印度和巴西的案例，提出一個文化適應性框架，該框架將同理心分為核心能力、文化調節因子和結構影響三個層次。此框架的核心概念是「同理心校準」，即臨床醫師調整同理心表達以適應不同情境的能力。此探索性框架旨在為培訓臨床醫師提供具文化回應性且以人為本的照護提供起始點。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這是一個探索性的框架，主要限制在於需要更多的實證研究，以便在更系統性取樣的情境中進行測試與完善，以驗證和精煉其適用性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Empathy across borders.
**Abstract:** Empathy is central to effective healthcare, but its expression is shaped by cultural and structural contexts. We provide vignettes from the United Kingdom, Nigeria, Japan, India, and Brazil to illustrate how the same capacities may be enacted differently depending on cultural and other health-system constraints. We then propose a culturally adaptive framework in which empathy operates across three levels: core empathic capacities (cognitive, affective, behavioural); cultural modulators (including norms governing touch, eye contact, expressiveness, and relational roles); and structural influences (such as time pressure, caseload, hierarchy). Central to the framework is empathic calibration, which is the clinician's capacity to adapt empathic expression across these layers while preserving core intent. This exploratory framework offers a starting point for training clinicians to deliver culturally responsive, person-centred care. Further empirical research is needed to test and refine it across more systematically sampled contexts.
