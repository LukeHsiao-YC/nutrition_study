### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討孤獨感對晚年認知功能的影響，特別是其與其他因素的複雜交互作用。研究採用參數 g-公式（反事實分析）對34,774名50-80歲的健康與退休研究參與者進行模擬分析，評估預防孤獨感對認知衰退的影響，並探討憂鬱症的介導作用。結果顯示，預防孤獨感能有效保護所有年齡層的認知功能，且效益隨年齡增長而提升，部分效果透過降低憂鬱症實現，尤其在男性中更顯著。這項創新研究證實，針對孤獨感的介入策略有望在人群層面維持認知功能並促進心理健康。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究運用創新的反事實分析框架，有效克服傳統方法難以區分孤獨感與其他複雜生命歷程風險因子對認知功能影響的挑戰，並量化了預防孤獨感在人群層面的保護效益，以及憂鬱症作為潛在介導途徑的作用，特別是其在性別上的差異。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Loneliness and cognitive function in later life: a population-based counterfactual analysis.
**Abstract:** Loneliness is increasingly recognized as a major public health challenge. Yet, its impact on cognitive function remains poorly understood. A complex interplay of life-course risk factors makes it challenging to disentangle the effect of loneliness from other factors and to assess underlying mechanisms when using traditional approaches. We applied the parametric g-formula with the Health and Retirement Study (1996-2018; n = 34 774; aged 50-80 years) to simulate an intervention estimating the effect of preventing loneliness on cognitive function. We assessed whether this effect varies by gender. Formal mediation analysis was also performed to assess depression as a potential pathway. Preventing loneliness was protective against cognitive decline at all ages, with the magnitude increasing with age. Averaged across all ages, an intervention to prevent loneliness would slow cognitive decline by 0.075 points at the population level [95% confidence interval (CI): 0.063-0.089] and by 0.157 points among individuals experiencing loneliness (95% CI: 0.138-0.178). Overall, 17.3% of the total effect of preventing loneliness was mediated via depression, with the proportion mediated being 19.4% among men and 16.9% among women. Employing an innovative counterfactual framework, this study shows that preventing loneliness could provide an insulative effect against cognitive decline, even at the population level. Importantly, with advancing age, addressing loneliness yields progressively greater cognitive benefits. The effects partly operate through reductions in depression, especially in men. Interventions against loneliness may help maintain cognitive function, with benefits extending to mental health, highlighting a promising avenue for public health strategies.
