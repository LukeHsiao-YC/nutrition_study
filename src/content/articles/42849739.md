### 重點摘要
韓國營養學會 (KNS) 自 1967 年成立以來，在推進營養科學、促進公共衛生和支持循證營養政策方面發揮了關鍵作用。過去六十年，KNS 建立了包括韓國人膳食參考攝取量等重要的基礎研究設施，並透過其研究和政策活動顯著改善了韓國國民的營養狀況和健康。學會成員藉由營養流行病學和韓國傳統飲食研究等在國際上獲得聲譽，並積極透過國際合作貢獻於全球營養科學。此回顧旨在紀念 KNS 成立 60 週年，強調其歷史發展與主要成就，並展望其在全球營養科學領域的角色以及在數位營養、精準營養等領域的國際合作機會。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇回顧文獻主要概述了韓國營養學會過去六十年的發展與成就，並未填補特定的實證研究空白。然而，它指出未來營養科學發展的方向和挑戰，特別是在數位營養、精準營養以及營養數據標準化和國際合作方面，這些領域仍有待全球學術界進一步深入研究與推動。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** 60 Years of the Korean Nutrition Society: Legacy, Leadership, and Path to Global Collaboration.
**Abstract:** Since its establishment in 1967, the Korean Nutrition Society (KNS) has played a pivotal role in advancing nutrition science, promoting public health, and supporting evidence-based nutrition policies in Korea. The growth of KNS has paralleled the evolution of nutrition science in Korea, from establishing basic nutrition standards and addressing nutritional deficiencies in the 1960s and 1970s, to expanding nutrition research and policy activities in the 1980s and 1990s, and subsequently advancing evidence-based dietary guidelines, nutrition surveillance systems, and research infrastructure since the 2000s. Over the past six decades, KNS has contributed to the development of foundational research infrastructure, including the Dietary Reference Intakes for Koreans and the Computer Aided Nutritional analysis program, which have served as essential tools for nutrition research, dietary assessment, and national nutrition policy. These achievements have supported evidence for improving the nutritional status and health of Koreans. Beyond its domestic impact, individual researchers affiliated with KNS have gained international reputation through advances in nutritional epidemiology, dietary pattern research, and studies of traditional Korean diets and fermented foods. National resources such as the Korea National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey have provided evidence on diet-disease relationships and have contributed to the global nutrition literature. Through collaboration with international professional societies, including the American Society for Nutrition (ASN), International Union of Nutritional Sciences (IUNS) and Federation of Asian Nutrition Societies (FANS), KNS has promoted scholarly exchange and contributed to nutrition science in Asia and beyond. This review commemorates KNS's 60th anniversary by highlighting its historical development, major achievements, and contributions to nutrition research infrastructure. Furthermore, it discusses the global role of Korean nutrition science and explores opportunities for international collaboration in digital nutrition, precision nutrition, and harmonized nutrition data. Building on six decades of experience, KNS is well-positioned to contribute to global nutrition science and public health.
