### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估中低收入國家居家腸道營養（HEN）患者的生存率及相關因素，因該領域研究甚少。這是一項針對巴西1000名患有神經或腫瘤疾病且接受HEN的成年人進行的描述性世代研究，分析其人口學、臨床、營養、功能和共病變數。結果顯示，年齡較大、男性、使用食物基礎飲食、鼻胃管和多重共病與較短的生存期相關；而神經系統診斷、保留的功能狀態和混合飲食則與較長的生存期和恢復經口進食相關。本研究強調臨床、營養和功能因素顯著影響居家腸道營養患者的預後，突顯了個體化監測和居家照護計畫的重要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了中低收入國家在神經或腫瘤疾病患者居家腸道營養生存率評估方面的研究空白，提供了該地區缺乏的實證數據。未來的研究可以探討更具因果關係的干預措施，並將研究範圍擴展至不同國家和醫療系統以驗證結果的普遍性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Survival of individuals with neurological and oncological diseases receiving home enteral nutrition: A descriptive cohort study.
**Abstract:** Home enteral nutrition is mainly indicated for individuals with neurological or oncological diseases. However, few studies have evaluated survival in this population in low- and middle-income countries. To investigate factors influencing survival in individuals receiving home enteral nutrition and to identify variables associated with hospital readmission and return to oral feeding during follow-up in a nutritional care program. Descriptive cohort study including adults (≥18 years) enrolled in the Nutritional Care Program for Individuals with Special Dietary Needs in Curitiba, Brazil (2006-2015). Individuals exclusively receiving home enteral nutrition, with neurological or oncological diseases, were analyzed considering demographic, clinical, nutritional, functional variables, and comorbidities. Weibull regression was used to analyze survival time; logistic regression for return to oral feeding; and quasi-Poisson regression for the frequency of hospital readmission. A total of 1,000 individuals were included. Most were older adults (68.9%), bedridden (60.6%), and had neurological conditions (62.8%). Median duration of home enteral nutrition was 153 days (1-3,378). Shorter survival was associated with older age, male sex, food-based diets, nasogastric tube use, and multiple comorbidities (p < 0.05). Longer survival and return to oral feeding were associated with neurological diagnosis, preserved functional status, and use of a mixed diet (p < 0.05). Hospital readmissions were more frequent among individuals with neurological disease, longer duration of home enteral nutrition, more comorbidities, and mixed diet use (p < 0.05). Clinical, nutritional, and functional factors significantly influence survival and outcomes in individuals receiving home enteral nutrition, highlighting the importance of individualized monitoring and home care planning.
