### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討大腦能量代謝策略在生命週期中如何變化以及其在不同物種間的保守性。研究團隊分析了人與恆河猴大腦皮層的核心能量代謝途徑，發現能量代謝的轉錄組水平策略在產前到產後的轉變具有物種間的相似性，其中合成代謝通路在產前達到高峰，而產能通路則在產後上升。此模式在小鼠、大鼠和雞中也得到驗證，並揭示了粒線體基因表現的兩個一致性模式。總體而言，這項研究為跨生命週期的大腦能量代謝研究提供了比較轉錄組參考。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了此前對於大腦能量代謝策略如何隨生命週期變化以及其在不同物種間保守性的理解空白，提供了一個詳細的比較轉錄組學參考。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Comparative lifespan trajectories of brain energy metabolism in human and macaque.
**Abstract:** The brain's high energy demands are met by a suite of metabolic pathways that selectively appropriate glucose across development, yet how this metabolic strategy changes across the lifespan and whether it is conserved across species remains incompletely understood. We previously mapped five core energy metabolism pathways across the human cortex and lifespan, revealing a fundamental dichotomy between anabolic and energy-producing pathway expression: the pentose phosphate pathway, which contributes to biomass, peaks prenatally, while glycolysis, the tricarboxylic acid cycle, and oxidative phosphorylation rise postnatally. Here, we extend the analysis to the rhesus macaque and show that the prenatal-to-postnatal transition is similar across the two species. Using the MitoCarta3.0 annotation framework, we further identify two consistent mitochondria-specific programs: a progressive decline in mitochondrial genome maintenance pathways, and a postnatal rise in the expression of genes involved in mitochondrial energy production and substrate utilization, replicated across human and macaque. Extending to mouse, rat, and chicken, we find this shifting transcriptome-level metabolic strategy is a conserved feature of vertebrate brain development. Finally, we map the cortical expression of mitochondria-localized pathway genes in the adult human cortex, finding that the anabolic-oxidative dichotomy follows in the mature brain. Together, these findings provide a comparative transcriptomic reference for studying brain energy metabolism across the lifespan.
