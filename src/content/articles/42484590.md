### 重點摘要
該研究旨在評估即將於2025年7月生效的SNAP計畫大幅削減對受助者的潛在影響。為此，作者回顧了2023年2月緊急補助金（EAs）終止後，SNAP參與者的親身經歷。先前的研究已證實EAs成功降低了食物不安全，但其終止隨後導致食物不安全率再度上升。本研究透過分析這些過去的經驗，旨在理解未來SNAP政策變革的潛在衝擊，並為制定可持續的政策提供建議。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇「觀點」文章透過聚焦SNAP緊急補助金終止後參與者的實際經歷，填補了未來聯邦削減SNAP計畫時，對受助者實際生活衝擊理解不足的研究空白，旨在為制定更具人本關懷的可持續政策提供依據。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Informing the Next Chapter of SNAP Policy: Lessons from SNAP Participants' Experiences in the Aftermath of the Emergency Allotment Expiration.
**Abstract:** The upcoming legislative package that was signed into law on July 4, 2025, makes significant cuts to the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), the largest antihunger federal program. To better understand the implications of reducing federal support for SNAP, we draw on SNAP participants' experience with the termination of temporary policy measures during the coronavirus disease 2019 pandemic, a period of disruption and uncertainty. Congress passed the First Coronavirus Response Act in March 2020, which provided emergency relief. This included the emergency allotments (EAs), a temporary increase in monthly SNAP benefits. Studies have shown that EAs were successful in decreasing food insecurity. In February 2023, the EAs were terminated; rising food insecurity rates followed. In this Perspective, we highlight SNAP participants' experiences in the aftermath of the EA expiration to understand the potential impacts of the recent changes to SNAP and to inform sustainable policy recommendations.
