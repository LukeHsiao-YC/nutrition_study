### 重點摘要
本質性元整合研究旨在系統性地探討心血管疾病患者用藥依從性的感知與經驗，並評估相關質性研究的品質。研究採用嚴謹的方法篩選出27項文獻，歸納出以患者為中心的信念、實際與情感挑戰，以及人際與內在支持策略三大核心主題。研究結果建立了一個概念行為模型（CBM），指出用藥依從性是受這些因素相互作用影響的動態複雜過程。此模型為開發以患者為中心的介入措施提供了理論框架，以提升心血管疾病患者的用藥依從性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻透過對大量質性研究的系統性綜合，填補了心血管疾病患者用藥依從性背後深層感知和經驗理解的空白。然而，研究亦指出未來需進一步深入探索文化、情感和心理因素對依從性的具體影響，以期促進心血管藥物的長期依從性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Medication adherence in cardiovascular patients: A qualitative meta-synthesis of perceptions and experiences.
**Abstract:** To identify, synthesize and critically appraise the quality of qualitative studies examining perceptions and experiences of medication adherence among patients with cardiovascular diseases. This meta-synthesis was conducted and reported in accordance with the Enhancing Transparency in Reporting the Synthesis of Qualitative Research (ENTREQ) statement, the Joanna Briggs Institute meta-aggregation method, and the Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses (PRISMA) guidelines. A comprehensive search of PubMed, Embase, CINAHL, Web of Science, and Cochrane databases was conducted from inception to January 13, 2025. The quality of included studies was critically appraised using the Joanna Briggs Institute Qualitative Assessment and Review Instrument. Of 2576 records screened, 27 studies (n = 804 patients) met the inclusion criteria. Three core themes emerged:(1) patient-centered beliefs, including beliefs about the necessity and effectiveness of medications, cultural and spiritual influences, trust and communication with healthcare professionals, and knowledge gaps and misconceptions; (2) practical and emotional challenges such as forgetfulness and routine disruption, perceived or actual side effects, access and cost barriers, emotional burden, identity concerns, and medication regimen complexity; and (3) interpersonal support systems and intrapersonal strategies, whereby family and social support, healthcare professionals, reminder tools, routines, and habit formation facilitated sustained adherence. The Conceptual Behavioral Model (CBM) generated from this synthesis illustrates how medication adherence in cardiovascular diseases is shaped through reciprocal interactions between these three themes. Medication adherence among patients with cardiovascular diseases is a dynamic, behaviourally complex process shaped by the interaction of patient-centered beliefs, practical and emotional challenges, and interpersonal and intrapersonal support mechanisms. The Conceptual Behavioural Model (CBM) provides a theory-integrative framework to guide the development of patient-centered adherence interventions. Future research is needed to explore cultural, emotional, and psychological factors influencing adherence to promote long-term adherence to cardiovascular medications.
