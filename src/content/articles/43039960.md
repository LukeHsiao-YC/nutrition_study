### 重點摘要
腹部肥胖與心血管代謝風險密切相關，但對於最佳飲食策略仍無共識。本為期六個月的隨機臨床試驗，比較了三種結合熱量限制與不同巨量營養素管理（均衡、低碳水化合物、低脂肪）的飲食介入，以及一個對照組，對超重成人腹部脂肪和心血管代謝參數的影響。結果顯示，各介入組在體重和內臟脂肪均有適度但顯著的減少（平均體重減輕約3%），且心血管代謝參數也有顯著改善。然而，各組間的下降趨勢並無顯著差異，表明巨量營養素組成對這些結果沒有觀察到差別影響。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了關於不同巨量營養素比例飲食策略對腹部脂肪和心血管代謝參數影響的共識空白，發現只要有熱量限制，多種飲食策略都能產生相似的適度改善。然而，研究結果顯示巨量營養素組成對這些改善沒有顯著的差別影響，提示未來的研究可能需要探索更精確的飲食介入、更長的追蹤時間，或更深入的個體化反應差異。此外，體重減輕幅度相對適度，也為未來尋求更大減重效果的研究提供了方向。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** A 6-month randomized clinical trial to test the effect of different dietary interventions on abdominal fat components and cardiometabolic profile in overweight adults.
**Abstract:** Abdominal obesity is strongly associated with cardiometabolic risk and can be modified through nutritional interventions, yet there is still no consensus on the dietary strategy capable of producing the best outcomes. This study examined the effects of different dietary interventions, with macronutrient management, on abdominal fat components and cardiometabolic parameters in overweight adults. A randomized clinical trial was conducted with three individualized dietary interventions involving caloric restriction combined with balanced macronutrient management (BALANCED), carbohydrate reduction (LOWCARB), and fat reduction (LOWFAT), in addition to a control group that received qualitative and group-based counseling (CONTROL). Participants were followed for six months, with assessments at baseline, three, and six months. Visceral adipose tissue (VAT) and subcutaneous adipose tissue (SAT) were evaluated by ultrasound, along with body weight, biochemical parameters, and blood pressure. Body weight and VAT decreased significantly over time, with no significant time × group interactions, indicating comparable trajectories across the intervention groups. The average weight reduction was ~3%. Significant changes over time were also observed for total cholesterol, glucose, HbA1c, and blood pressure, with no significant time × group interactions for cardiometabolic parameters. After six months of dietary intervention, without changes in physical activity level, modest but significant reductions in body weight and VAT were observed over time, with no significant differences in their trajectories among the intervention groups. No evidence of a differential effect of macronutrient composition on these outcomes was observed.
