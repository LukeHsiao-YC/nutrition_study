### 重點摘要
埃及老年人口老化及慢性病負擔日益加劇，本研究旨在評估埃及老年患者的健康素養與成功老化及其關聯。此橫斷式比較研究納入246名（慢性病患者與健康對照組各123名）65歲以上老年人，結果顯示慢性病患者的健康素養和成功老化分數顯著較低，且健康素養與成功老化呈顯著正相關，是其最強的獨立預測因子。本研究證實健康素養與埃及老年人的成功老化密切相關，提示提升健康素養可能有利於改善老年健康成果。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了埃及老年族群中健康素養與成功老化之間關聯性證據的空白。然而，其限制在於採用橫斷面設計，未能確立因果關係；未來的研究應考慮採用縱向設計、社區機率抽樣、使用經過充分驗證的阿拉伯語測量工具，並最終進行健康素養干預的隨機評估以確立其效益。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Health literacy and successful aging among elderly patients at Kasr Alainy Hospital: a cross-sectional comparative study.
**Abstract:** Population aging and chronic disease burden are increasing in Egypt. Health literacy (HL) may play a key role in promoting successful aging (SA), but evidence is limited. To assess HL and SA and examine their association among elderly patients at Kasr Alainy Hospital. A cross-sectional comparative study of 246 participants (123 patients with chronic diseases and 123 healthy controls) aged ≥ 65 years was conducted using the Health Literacy Questionnaire and Successful Aging Scale. Patients had significantly lower HL and SA scores than controls (p < 0.001). HL was positively correlated with SA (r = 0.612, p < 0.001) and remained the strongest independent predictor in regression analysis. Higher education and income improved SA, while age, rural residence, and smoking negatively affected it. Future research should employ longitudinal designs, community-based probability sampling, fully validated Arabic versions of the instruments with reported psychometrics in the study population, and, ultimately, randomized evaluations of health-literacy interventions to establish whether improving HL improves successful-aging outcomes. Health literacy is strongly associated with successful aging among older adults in Egypt. Higher health literacy is associated with better aging outcomes; interventions to improve HL may therefore be beneficial.
