### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討專業西班牙語遠端醫療口譯員在面對面醫療會談中提供服務的視角，彌補了以往研究中對口譯員經驗關注不足的空白。研究訪談了19位美國境內的專業口譯員，並進行了內容與主題分析。結果顯示，口譯員不僅是單純的翻譯者，還會主動作為患者的倡導者，並運用技巧與醫護人員建立關係以促進溝通，他們認為這能提升溝通效果、改善就診流程並處理複雜需求。這項研究的發現可為醫護人員培訓、組織政策及支援措施的制定提供依據，最終提升患者護理品質。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了以往研究未能充分納入專業醫療口譯員自身工作經驗與視角的空白，特別是在遠端口譯於面對面醫療會談情境中的獨特角色與挑戰。研究限制在於其僅針對美國的西班牙語口譯員進行定性研究，可能無法完全推廣至其他語言或更廣泛的醫療口譯情境。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Remote interpretation during in-person medical encounters: Perspectives from professional Spanish-language interpreters.
**Abstract:** Professional interpreters are critical for effective communication between providers and non-English-preferring patients, 62% of which are Spanish-preferring in the US. Yet research-to-date has not typically included interpreters' experiences. We examine perspectives of national sample of professional Spanish-language medical interpreters providing remote interpretation during in-person medical encounters. We partnered with a national interpretation services company serving thousands of inpatient and outpatient healthcare organizations across the country. We recruited professional US-based Spanish-language interpreters. We interviewed 19 (of 32 eligible) interpreters (response rate 59%). We conducted content and thematic analysis. Remote interpreters during in-person medical encounters work set shifts of back-to-back calls across care settings with little-to-no information prior to being connected. Interpretation complexity varies by topic handled, patient characteristics, and whether patient is alone, and the provider uses their Spanish skills. Interpreters differentiated their approach based on patient needs, acting often as patient advocates (keeping patients calm, clarifying explanations, conveying needed details). With providers, interpreters engaged in strategies to build rapport and support communication. Interpreters believed they enhanced communication, improved visit flow, and helped address complex patient needs. They raised the need for provider training on effectively using interpreters (pacing, focused questions), consistent organizational supports (procedures concerning breaks, transferring calls), and reassessment of policies (providing interpreters patient information before calls). During in-person medical encounters, professional interpreters connecting via audio/video are critical to meet Spanish-preferring patients' needs. Interpreters' role extends beyond mere interpretation; they employ subtle, unique techniques with the provider and with the patient to enhance mutual understanding and facilitate effective patient-provider communication. Remote professional interpreters' perspectives on their work environment and challenges they face can inform provider training and development of policies, practices and institutional supports that enable effective communication, enhance patient understanding, and ultimately lead to better care experiences and high-quality patient care.
