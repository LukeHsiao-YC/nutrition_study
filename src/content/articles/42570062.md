### 重點摘要
這項美國全國性研究旨在評估2000至2018年間長期居住在核電廠附近與全因死亡率的關聯性。研究採用生態學框架，分析各縣人口中心點與200公里內核電廠的距離，並使用廣義估計方程的Poisson迴歸模型進行調整。結果顯示，核電廠鄰近度與全因死亡率呈正相關，尤其在中老年族群中影響更為顯著，估計約有39萬例死亡與此鄰近度相關。研究結論指出，即使是低劑量暴露，也可能對人群健康產生可測量的影響，值得進一步探討。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了先前關於核電廠慢性低劑量輻射暴露對人群層級死亡率影響證據有限的研究空白。然而，研究本身是以生態學框架進行，並假設了因果關係來估算死亡人數，其結果仍需進一步的因果推論和更詳細的調查來證實。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Proximity to nuclear power plants and total mortality risk in the USA during 2000-2018.
**Abstract:** Nuclear power plants (NPPs) are a major source of electricity in the United States, yet concerns persist regarding potential health effects of chronic low-level radiation exposure among nearby populations. Evidence on population-level mortality impacts remains limited. To evaluate the association between long-term exposure to NPPs and all-cause mortality across U.S. counties from 2000 to 2018. A proximity-based exposure was calculated as the sum of inverse distances from each county's population centroid to all active nuclear power plants within 200 km for each year and averaged over a 20-year moving window to account for latency. Using aggregated county-level data in an ecological framework across the United States, associations between nuclear power plant proximity and age- and sex-specific all-cause mortality were estimated using Poisson regression models within a generalized estimating equations (GEE) framework, adjusted for county-level covariates. Positive associations between NPP proximity and all-cause mortality were concentrated in middle- and older-age groups. The largest effect estimates were observed among females aged 55-64 (β = 0.834, 95% CI 0.508, 1.160) and males aged 65-74 (β = 0.757, 95% CI 0.456, 1.058). Coefficients are reported on the log-relative risk scale, corresponding to the change in mortality per one-unit increase in the NPP proximity metric. Based on these associations, if we assumed a causal framework we would attribute approximately 390,000 deaths (95% CI 190,000-590,000), corresponding to 1.7% (95% CI 0.8%-2.6%) of total U.S. mortality in the included counties during the study period, were associated with NPP proximity. In this national analysis, long-term proximity to NPPs was associated with higher all cause mortality. These findings suggest that even low-level exposures may have measurable population health impacts and warrant further investigation.
