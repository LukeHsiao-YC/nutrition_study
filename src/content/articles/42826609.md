### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討子宮內膜異位症患者與醫護人員對疾病管理的看法，以改善醫病共享決策過程。透過對20位患者和13位醫護人員進行半結構式焦點團體訪談，研究發現醫病雙方在荷爾蒙治療的「有效性」上存在觀點差異，醫護人員優先考量長期閉經，而患者則著重於疼痛緩解。患者普遍反映缺乏傾聽與同理心、治療選項及副作用資訊不足，且家庭成員未能參與照護。研究結論強調改善醫病溝通、提升資訊透明度及促進共享決策，對減輕患者心理負擔及提升照護品質至關重要。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了子宮內膜異位症管理中醫病雙方在治療期望與優先次序上的認知落差，並深入分析了導致溝通障礙和心理負擔的關係問題。這些發現為未來開發共享決策工具提供了重要的基礎資訊。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Experiences towards endometriosis management: A qualitative study among patients and healthcare professionals.
**Abstract:** Endometriosis is a chronic disease for which no curative treatment currently exists. The available palliative treatments are not without side effects and must be tailored to the individual expectations and objectives of each patient. This poses significant challenges for clinician-patient communication. This study explores the perceptions of patients and healthcare professionals regarding endometriosis management to identify ways to improve shared decision-making processes. This cross-sectional qualitative study was performed using a semi-structured focus group methodology. Twenty patients with endometriosis and thirteen healthcare professionals were separately involved in this study between June 2024 and January 2025. Focus group topics investigated the representations of the concept of treatment effectiveness, the emotions associated with endometriosis management, and the perceived impact of care pathways on patient-clinician and patient-environment relationships. The study highlighted contrasting perspectives between patients and clinicians regarding what constitutes effective hormonal therapies. Long-term amenorrhea is a priority for healthcare professionals, whereas pain reduction remains the immediate priority for patients. Patients also expect surgery to be as effective as possible and are enthusiastic about non-medical treatments. Patients who participated in the focus groups reported a lack of listening and empathy, a deficit of shared information as regards treatment options and side effects, and a need to involve partners and family members in care. These factors contribute to communication issues between patients and clinicians and contribute to substantial psychological burden on both sides. The study highlighted the central role of communication and analysed, at different levels, the factors underlying relational issues that affect patient care and quality of life. Several factors should be addressed to improve shared decision-making in the management of endometriosis, which may inform the development of a shared decision-making tool in the near future.
