### 重點摘要
本研究旨在根據《刺胳針糖尿病與內分泌學》委員會超越身體質量指數(BMI)的新肥胖定義，評估非肥胖、臨床前期肥胖和臨床肥胖族群的身體活動模式。透過回溯性分析24,679名成人使用Fitbit裝置和電子健康紀錄的數據，研究人員評估了步數、中等至劇烈運動及久坐時間。結果顯示，從非肥胖到臨床肥胖組，身體活動量呈現顯著下降趨勢，例如每日步數分別為7846、6702和5128步，且久坐時間增加。這項研究結論指出，穿戴式裝置數據能有效區分臨床前期肥胖與臨床肥胖之間獨特的身體活動模式。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究成功地填補了過去對肥胖定義過於依賴BMI的不足，透過利用穿戴式裝置數據，精確地揭示了依據新的肥胖定義劃分的臨床前期肥胖和臨床肥胖個體，其身體活動模式存在顯著差異，為未來更精準的肥胖評估與管理提供了實證基礎。然而，本研究為回溯性觀察，未能探討這些活動模式變化的因果關係或潛在的干預策略。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Wearable-Derived Physical Activity Patterns in Preclinical and Clinical Obesity.
**Abstract:** The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology Commission issued a new obesity definition to move beyond BMI and prioritize excess body fat and dysfunctional adiposity, stratified by preclinical and clinical groups. We evaluated the physical activity patterns across obesity groups with wearable-derived accelerometry and risk for cardiovascular disease. We performed a retrospective cohort analysis of step count, moderate-to-vigorous exercise, and sedentary time in 24,679 adults with Fitbit and electronic health records from the All of Us Research Program, stratified by no, preclinical, and clinical obesity groups. There was a decline in physical activity across obesity groups: median daily steps were 7846, 6702, and 5128, weekly moderate-to-vigorous physical activity exercise 140, 91, and 35 min; and daily sedentary time 12.2, 12.8, and 13.4 h for no, preclinical, and clinical obesity, respectively. Physical activity decreased with BMI and obesity-related multimorbidity. Wearable-derived physical activity patterns show distinct patterns for preclinical and clinical obesity.
