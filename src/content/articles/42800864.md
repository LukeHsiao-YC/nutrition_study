### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討1987-2017年間台灣農藥及巴拉刈中毒自殺的負擔、趨勢、地理分佈與人口學特徵。研究分析台灣死因統計數據，顯示農藥自殺率雖下降74%，但巴拉刈仍是農藥自殺的主要原因，佔已記錄農藥自殺的59.9%，且其在整體自殺中的比例在不同縣市間差異顯著。農藥自殺者多為男性、65歲以上老年人及農村居民，而巴拉刈自殺者則傾向較年輕。作者預期2018-2020年實施的巴拉刈禁令，將對高風險地區及族群的自殺率產生重大影響。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究詳細描繪了台灣農藥及巴拉刈中毒自殺的長期趨勢、地理分佈與高風險人口特徵，填補了該領域在台灣的流行病學數據空白。它為評估未來巴拉刈禁令的實際成效提供了重要的基準資料，但實際影響仍待後續研究追蹤評估。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** The burden of suicide by paraquat poisoning in Taiwan (1987-2017): trends, geographic variations, and demographic characteristics.
**Abstract:** Pesticide self-poisoning accounts for 14%-20% of global suicides. Paraquat, a highly lethal pesticide, is involved in a considerable number of suicides in some countries. This study investigated the burden, trends, geographic variations, and demographic characteristics of pesticide and paraquat suicides in Taiwan. Suicide data (1987, 1992, 1997, and 2002-2017) were obtained from Taiwan's cause-of-death mortality data files. Trends and geographic variations in pesticide and paraquat suicides were examined using graphs and maps. Logistic regression was used to investigate the differences in the characteristics of pesticide versus non-pesticide suicides and paraquat versus non-paraquat pesticide suicides. Age-standardised pesticide suicide rates decreased by 74%, from 7.7 to 2.0 per 100,000, in 1987-2017. Paraquat was the leading agent involved in pesticide suicides, accounting for 59.9% (2,268/3,786) of pesticide suicides where the pesticide agents involved were recorded on the death certificate and at least 4.0% (2,268/56,361) of overall suicides in Taiwan. The percentage of paraquat suicides in overall suicides varied markedly across cities/counties (0.4%-15.6%). Pesticide suicides were more likely to be male, older people aged 65 + years, and from rural areas than non-pesticide suicides. By contrast, paraquat suicides tended to be younger than non-paraquat pesticide suicides, with no difference in sex distributions in the two groups. The ban on paraquat implemented in 2018-2020 would have a significant impact on pesticide suicides and overall suicides, particularly in high-risk areas and demographic groups.
