### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討WhatsApp如何影響母親的健康教育過程，特別是關於嬰幼兒口腔健康資訊的解讀與應用。研究採用質性探索法，分析了特定母嬰隊列中母親與健康專業人員在WhatsApp群組內的36個對話記錄。結果顯示，主要討論主題為乳牙萌出和兒童磨牙，且母親們將WhatsApp群組視為重要的支持、指導和互助來源。研究結論認為，WhatsApp是獲取嬰幼兒口腔健康資訊和指導的有效工具，但母親們口腔健康素養能力的差異，突顯了專業引導教育策略的重要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究闡明了WhatsApp在母嬰口腔健康教育中作為資訊、指導和支持網絡的角色。未來的研究應著重於開發和評估由專業人士引導的教育策略，以提升母親們的口腔健康資訊品質解讀能力並促進嬰幼兒照護的自主性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Use of WhatsApp in Children's Oral Health Education Among Mothers in a Maternal and Child Cohort: Qualitative Content Analysis.
**Abstract:** Research indicates that social media platforms play an important role in disseminating health information. Mothers, in particular, frequently seek guidance on child health through digital resources. Given the growing demand for health-related content, it is necessary to understand how these tools contribute to maternal health education, especially regarding the ability to interpret and apply health information. This study aims to investigate how WhatsApp influences the health education processes of mothers, as well as to identify the interests and competencies related to infant oral health among mothers participating in a maternal and child cohort. This was a qualitative, exploratory study grounded in the Bardin content analysis framework. Text messages exchanged between mothers and health professionals in a WhatsApp group linked to the Curitiba Maternal and Child Health Cohort (COOSMIC) were analyzed, referring to the years 2020 and 2021. The corpus was composed of 36 dialogues: 28 concerning infant oral health and 8 regarding the group's impact. The recording units were categorized by themes and interpreted according to emerging core meanings. We identified 2 main themes: eruption of primary teeth (mentioned 12 times) and bruxism in children (mentioned 5 times). Furthermore, in the topic related to the group's impact, we observed a pattern among participants in recognizing the group as a source of support, guidance, and mutual help, highlighting the role of professionals and peer-to-peer exchanges in overcoming doubts and insecurities. Mothers demonstrated an active search for information and collaborative interaction with health professionals, establishing WhatsApp as a support and education network. Competencies linked to oral health literacy ranged from autonomy, empowerment, and security to insecurity, misinformation, and knowledge limitations regarding infant oral health information. WhatsApp proved to be a relevant tool for accessing information and guidance on infant oral health, favoring mutual support and communication among peers and professionals. The competencies and limitations identified in the mothers' oral health literacy highlight the importance of professionally mediated educational strategies aimed at improving the quality of information and promoting autonomy in infant care.
