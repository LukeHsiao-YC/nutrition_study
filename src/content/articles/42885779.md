### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在評估2023年WHO指南在塞內加爾六個月以下嬰兒早期檢測生長發育不良風險方面的價值，該地區目前的急性營養不良篩查通常從六個月開始。研究人員對789對母嬰進行前瞻性世代的二次分析，應用WHO檢測標準並探討六個月時消瘦的風險因子。結果顯示，六個月時消瘦盛行率為9.5%，低出生體重和三個月時體重不足是顯著的風險因子。研究結論認為，應用2023年WHO指南有助於早期發現有風險的嬰兒，但計劃入組標準仍需進一步精進以識別需要後續追蹤或治療的嬰兒。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究評估了2023年WHO指南在六個月以下嬰兒早期營養不良檢測中的價值，填補了該年齡組別缺乏早期篩查標準的空白，並識別出低出生體重和三個月時體重不足是六個月時消瘦的關鍵風險因子。然而，研究指出目前的計劃入組標準仍需進一步精進，以更精確地識別需要後續追蹤或治療的嬰兒，這是未來研究和政策制定的限制與方向。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Early Detection of Infants Aged Under Six Months at Risk of Poor Growth and Development: Secondary Analysis of a Prospective Cohort in Senegal.
**Abstract:** Child malnutrition is a major global problem, but in many high-burden countries, screening and treatment of acute malnutrition called wasting (weight-for-length z-score < -2SD) only begins at age 6 months. Infants under 6 months 'at risk of poor growth and development' are, however, increasingly recognised. Latest 2023 WHO guidelines describe anthropometric, feeding and maternal/infant clinical criteria for early detection and treatment. We aimed to explore the value of these criteria to early detect infants at risk of wasting at 6 months. We conducted a secondary analysis of a prospective observational cohort of 789 mother-infant pairs in Senegal. Several WHO detection criteria were applied to examine the dyads and risk factors for wasting at 6 months, the current malnutrition programme enrolment criterion. Descriptive statistics, Alluvial plots and logistic regression analysis were employed. At birth, 6.8% of infants had low birth weight (weight < 2500 g) and most (99.7%) were breastfed. At age 3 months, 9.4% were underweight (weight-for-age z-score < -2SD) and 10% were wasted; 66% were exclusively breastfed. Many infants transitioned between being malnourished and having normal weight over time. Wasting prevalence at 6 months was 9.5%; risk factors included low birth weight (adjusted odds ratio [aOR] 3.16, 95% CI:1.39, 7.19) and underweight at 3 months (aOR 6.62, 95% CI 2.96, 14.8). Infant feeding and maternal factors were not associated. Applying the 2023 WHO guidelines could detect infants at risk of poor growth and development early. Programme enrolment criteria need further refinement to identify infants requiring follow-up or treatment.
