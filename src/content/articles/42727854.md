### 重點摘要
本研究旨在利用指示胺基酸氧化法 (IAAO)，確定60歲以上健康成年人的色胺酸需求量，以優化現行膳食建議。研究結果顯示，色胺酸的估計需求量為4.3毫克/公斤/天，且此需求量不受性別或年齡層（十年組）的影響。這些發現是首次使用同位素方法在老年人中建立色胺酸需求量。這將有助於指導未來的膳食建議，並支持健康老齡化的精準營養。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究首次利用同位素方法，在60歲以上健康成年人中建立了色胺酸的需求量，填補了該年齡組別缺乏精確膳食建議的研究空白。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Dietary Tryptophan Requirements in Adults Aged Over 60 Years is not Affected by Sex or Decades of Age.
**Abstract:** Current dietary tryptophan recommendation of 4 mg The objective of this study was to determine the tryptophan requirement in healthy males and females aged ≥ 60 y. Tryptophan requirement was determined using the indicator amino acid oxidation method (IAAO) with L-[1- A total of 144 studies were analyzed, with participants stratified by sex and decade group. The estimated tryptophan requirement was 4.3 mg This is the first study to establish the tryptophan requirement in older adults using isotopic method. These findings will guide dietary recommendations and support precision nutrition for healthy aging.
