### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討大學生永續營養行為與身體活動態度之間的關係。這項橫斷面研究納入303名大學生，評估其永續營養行為、身體活動健康信念模型量表得分，並測量身體質量指數與腰圍。結果顯示，永續營養行為與身體活動態度呈正相關，且與身體質量指數及腰圍呈負相關。研究指出，對身體活動態度越積極的學生越可能採行永續營養行為，建議未來可整合身體活動與永續營養的健康促進策略。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了大學生永續營養行為與身體活動態度之間關聯性的研究空白，並揭示了兩者相互影響的潛力。作為一項橫斷面研究，其主要限制在於無法確立因果關係。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Sustainable Nutrition Behaviors and Physical Activity Attitudes Among College Students: A Cross-Sectional Study.
**Abstract:** This study aimed to examine sustainable nutrition behaviors and attitudes toward physical activity among college students. A cross-sectional study. College setting. The study included 303 college students (171 female, 132 male). Sustainable nutrition behaviors were assessed using the Behaviors Scale Towards Sustainable Nutrition, whereas beliefs and attitudes related to physical activity were measured using the Exercise Health Belief Model Scale. Anthropometric measurements, including body weight and waist circumference, were obtained by researchers. Pearson and Spearman correlation analyses, as appropriate, and multiple linear regression analyses were conducted. Sustainable nutrition behavior scale total scores were positively correlated with exercise Health Belief Model scores (r = 0.444, P < 0.001) and negatively correlated with body mass index (r = -0.142, P = 0.01) and waist circumference (r = -0.169, P = 0.003). After adjusting for sex, academic level, and income level, sustainable nutrition behaviors remained significantly associated with attitudes toward physical activity (β = 0.400, P < 0.001), explaining 22.6% of the variance (R² = 0.226). Students with more positive attitudes toward physical activity are more likely to engage in sustainable nutrition behaviors. Integrated health promotion strategies addressing both physical activity and sustainable nutrition may support healthier and more sustainable lifestyles among college students.
