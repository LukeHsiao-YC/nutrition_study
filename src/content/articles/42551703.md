### 重點摘要
雖然飲食模式對老年人健康至關重要，但心理特徵如何影響飲食模式的長期變化仍不清楚。本研究使用中國老年健康長壽調查的縱向數據，對3181名中國老年人進行潛在轉變分析，以探討焦慮和認知功能是否預測飲食模式的轉變。研究識別出單一飲食、素食、高蛋白飲食和多樣化飲食四種模式，其中多樣化飲食組的焦慮程度最低、認知功能最高。縱向分析顯示，焦慮和認知功能在飲食模式轉變中扮演調節角色，凸顯其在塑造飲食行為方面的關鍵作用，對促進健康老化的介入措施具有啟示。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了關於心理特徵（特別是焦慮和認知功能）如何影響老年人飲食模式長期變化的知識空白。在過去，對心理屬性與飲食模式動態關係的了解相對較少。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Psychological attributes predict dietary pattern transitions in older adults: Evidence from a national longitudinal study on healthy aging.
**Abstract:** Dietary patterns are important determinants of health in older adults, yet little is known about how psychological attributes influence changes in dietary patterns over time. This study aimed to identify dietary pattern classes in Chinese older adults and to examine whether psychological attributes-specifically anxiety and cognitive function-predict transitions in dietary patterns over time. Longitudinal data from the Chinese Longitudinal Healthy Longevity Survey (CLHLS) were used. We conducted latent transition analysis (LTA) to analyze the transition in dietary patterns of 3181 older adults, with a mean age of 81.11 years at T1 (2014) and 84.97 years at T2 (2018). LTA identified four classes of dietary patterns at T1-mono-diet, vegetarian diet, high protein diet, and diversified diet. The class results were consistent at T2. The diversified diet group had the lowest level of anxiety and the highest level of cognitive function among the four dietary groups at both waves. Longitudinally, the LTA results showed that anxiety and cognitive function played a moderating role in the transition of dietary patterns. Anxiety and cognitive function were associated with transitions in dietary patterns in Chinese older adults. These findings highlight the critical role of psychological attributes in shaping dietary behavior over time, with implications for targeted interventions promoting healthy aging.
