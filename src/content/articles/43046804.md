### 重點摘要
這項回溯性病例對照研究旨在評估兒童發炎性腸道疾病 (pIBD) 患者在感染困難梭狀芽孢桿菌 (CDI) 後的中期疾病進程。研究納入45名患有CDI的pIBD住院患者，並與152名按性別、年齡和IBD亞型等匹配的對照組進行比較，追蹤12、24和36個月的疾病活動度、惡化次數和治療升級等指標。結果顯示，與對照組相比，CDI患者的疾病進程更為嚴重，表現為追蹤期間活動度評分更高、住院頻率增加以及需要更頻繁的治療升級。因此，研究結論指出CDI與兒童IBD更嚴重的中期病程顯著相關，強調早期識別和治療的重要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究填補了先前關於困難梭狀芽孢桿菌感染對兒童發炎性腸道疾病中期影響數據稀缺的研究空白。然而，由於這是一項單中心回溯性研究，其結果的普遍性可能受限，且僅評估了中期的疾病進程，未能觸及長期的影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Short- and median-term disease outcome after Clostridioides difficile infection in paediatric inflammatory bowel disease patients.
**Abstract:** Clostridioides difficile infection (CDI) is a recognised complication of inflammatory bowel disease (IBD). In adults, CDI has been linked to increased long-term colectomy risk, but data on its impact in paediatric IBD remain scarce. We aimed to assess the medium-term course of IBD after CDI in children. We conducted a retrospective, single-centre, case-control study in the paediatric Gastroenterology and Nutrition Department at Necker-Enfants Malades Hospital. All paediatric IBD (pIBD) patients hospitalised with CDI between January 2008 and December 2020 were included. Each case was matched 1:3 with IBD controls by sex, age, IBD subtype, disease location, and year of diagnosis. Outcomes at 12, 24 and 36 months included disease activity scores, number of exacerbations, hospitalisations, digestive surgeries, and treatment escalation. Forty-five pIBD patients with CDI were enrolled and matched with 152 pIBD controls. Compared with controls, children with CDI experienced a more severe disease course, characterised by higher mean activity scores each year of follow-up, more flares requiring hospitalisation at 24 and 36 months, and greater need for therapeutic escalation over time (p < 0.05). In paediatric patients, CDI is associated with a significantly more severe medium-term course of IBD. Early recognition and targeted management of CDI are therefore crucial in this population.
