### 重點摘要
本研究旨在透過全基因組關聯分析 (GWAS) 識別與成人對苦味食物偏好相關的基因變異，以克服過往研究在候選基因、食物種類及統計方法上的限制。研究利用英國生物樣本庫 (UK Biobank) 超過12萬人的基因型與12種苦味食物的李克特量表偏好數據，採用有序多項式回歸模型進行分析，並探討基因功能與飲食攝取量的關聯。結果識別出46個單核苷酸多態性 (SNPs) 與11種苦味食物偏好顯著相關。總體而言，遺傳變異確實對成人苦味食物偏好有所貢獻，但影響效果微弱且不一致，反映了飲食攝取行為的複雜多因素本質。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究透過採用全基因組關聯分析和有序多項式回歸模型，解決了過去研究僅限於候選基因、苦味食物種類有限以及將偏好量表視為連續數據的局限性。儘管發現了遺傳關聯，但效果量很小且不一致，突顯了飲食攝取是一個多因素的複雜過程，未來的研究需要更深入地探討遺傳與環境及行為之間複雜的交互作用。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Genome-wide association studies of preferences for bitter-tasting foods and beverages highlight complex links with brain and behaviour.
**Abstract:** Genetic variation is implicated in individual preferences for bitter-tasting foods. However, previous studies have focused on candidate genes and limited varieties of bitter-tasting foods, and have treated food preference scale responses as continuous data. The present investigation aimed to identify genetic variants associated with preferences for bitter-tasting foods utilizing ordinal multinomial regression models in genome-wide association studies (GWAS). In addition, post-GWAS functional annotation and mapping, genetic correlations, and associations with dietary intake were examined. Food preference and genome-wide genotyping data were utilized from the UK Biobank (n=125,578). Preference data from Likert scale rankings (from 1 to 9) for 12 individual foods were analyzed using ordinal multinomial regression GWAS. In addition, one composite continuous variable was created for preference of cruciferous vegetables as a group and analyzed with a linear mixed model GWAS to enable calculation of a polygenic score (PGS) for cruciferous vegetables preference. Convergent validity of GWAS results was assessed with dietary intake data for the same food items in the CARTaGENE cohort (n=8,176). Post-GWAS gene-level and pathway-level association analyses were conducted in MAGMA. Nevertheless, effect sizes are small and inconsistent, reflecting the multi-factorial complexity of food intake.
