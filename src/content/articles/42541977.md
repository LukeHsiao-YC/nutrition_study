### 重點摘要
維持體重下降是一大挑戰，連續血糖監測（CGM）可能提供生物回饋以支持生活方式改變。這項隨機對照可行性試驗旨在評估將CGM整合至個人化營養師主導的體重維持計畫的可行性及其對體重、飲食與活動的影響。研究發現，結合CGM的介入方案在24週後能顯著提升體重下降幅度並改善熱量攝取，證明其可行性與初步效益。結論支持CGM是體重維持計畫中可行的輔助工具，並建議進行更大規模的隨機對照試驗以評估其長期有效性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇研究填補了將連續血糖監測（CGM）作為非糖尿病患者體重維持策略的可行性與初步有效性評估的空白。然而，由於這僅是一項可行性試驗，其主要限制在於需要更大規模、有足夠檢定力且更長期的隨機對照試驗來確定其長期實際效益。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Optimizing Weight Loss Maintenance: Can Continuous Glucose Monitoring Play a Role (OWL-CGM)? A Randomized Controlled Feasibility Trial.
**Abstract:** Maintenance of weight loss is a major challenge. Continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) could provide biofeedback to support lifestyle changes to facilitate weight maintenance. This randomised controlled trial evaluated the feasibility of incorporating CGM into a personalised, dietitian-led, weight loss maintenance programme (PWM), and whether CGM use led to greater improvements in dietary intake, physical activity, body weight, body composition, and cardiometabolic risk biomarkers over 24 weeks. Adults who lost >5 kg bodyweight in the past six months were eligible. Of 131 individuals who expressed interest, 99 met eligibility criteria. Forty-nine participants (17 men, 32 women) consented and were randomised to either PWM (n = 25) or PWM/CGM (n = 24) groups. Average recruitment rate was five participants/month. All participants received five one-to-one personalised dietitian consultations in weeks 1, 2, 4, 8 and 12; the PWM/CGM group also received a CGM for the 24-week intervention period and education on its use to support diet and physical activity behaviours. 19 PWM and 22 PWM/CGM participants completed the study (83.7% of randomised participants). All completers attended all dietitian consultations, and PWM/CGM participants wore their CGMs on 92.6% of intervention days. In completers, weight loss at 24-weeks was greater for PWM/CGM than PWM (-2.7 ± 1.1 kg vs +0.5 ± 0.8 kg, p = 0.032). This difference remained significant in an intention-to-treat sensitivity analysis (-2.5 ± 1.0 kg vs +0.4 ± 0.6 kg, p = 0.012).
