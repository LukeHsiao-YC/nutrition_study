### 重點摘要
本研究旨在提出一種新穎的「相對位移法」，用於分析身體組成與步速間的關聯，並與現有方法進行比較。研究對145位肥胖老年人測量其身體組成（透過DEXA）與400公尺快走步速，並比較絕對質量、等距對數比轉換 (ILR) 和相對位移三種迴歸模型。結果顯示，所有方法均發現步速與身體組成之間存在顯著關聯。然而，相對位移法無需轉換即可提供一致的預測，且可能比ILR法提供更簡潔的估計與解釋。這表明相對位移模型在處理組成型共變數時具有潛在優勢。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻提出了「相對位移法」，填補了現有組成型資料分析方法（如ILR轉換）在預測物理功能時，可能因總體重差異導致預測不一致，以及絕對質量模型忽略組成相對性的問題。它提供了一種無需轉換、估計和解釋更簡潔且預測一致的新方法，改進了在醫學和營養學研究中對身體組成數據的分析方法。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Compositional Data Analysis of Relative Body Mass and Physical Function Using the Relative Shift Approach.
**Abstract:** The purpose of this study was to present the novel relative shift approach for compositional data analysis of body composition as a predictor for gait speed and to compare it to existing methods. Body composition was quantified from dual-energy x-ray absorptiometry in 145 older adults living with obesity. Gait speed was measured from a fast-paced 400-m walk. The associations between speed and body composition used separate least squares regression models with absolute (kg) mass and compositional methods based on isometric log-ratio (ILR) transformations and the relative shift approach. All methods found significant associations between gait speed and body composition. The ILR approach was fit three times, once for each component variable (lean, fat, and bone mass). Predictions differed for the same magnitude of 5% composition change due to total body mass differences (+4.4 [0.8 to 8.0] vs. +5.0 [1.2 to 8.8] cm/s). The absolute mass model ignored the relative nature of the components. The relative shift model required no transformation and provided consistent predictions for 5% change (+4.6 [1.0 to 8.2] cm/s). Our data suggest the relative shift model may provide simpler estimation and interpretation than ILR for models with compositional covariates. ClinicalTrials.gov identifier NCT04076618.
