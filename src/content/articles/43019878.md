### 重點摘要
胃癌患者常伴有營養不良、免疫抑制及腸道菌群失調。本研究對34項隨機對照試驗進行系統性回顧及統合分析，旨在評估益生菌補充對胃癌患者臨床結果的影響。結果顯示，益生菌組在血清蛋白水平、免疫功能參數（如CD3+、CD4+）及降低併發症（如噁心、嘔吐、腹瀉）方面有所改善，並縮短了住院時間。然而，所有研究均來自中國且多數品質不佳，證據確定性普遍較低，故研究結果應謹慎解讀。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究雖發現益生菌補充對胃癌患者的短期臨床結果和替代實驗室指標可能產生正面影響，但其結果受限於所有納入研究均來自中國、多數研究品質不佳以及證據確定性低或非常低等問題。因此，未來的研究需要更多高質量、多中心、設計嚴謹且標準化干預方案的隨機對照試驗，以進一步驗證益生菌的臨床價值。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** The effects of probiotic supplementation for gastric cancer patients: A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
**Abstract:** Patients with gastric cancer often suffer from malnutrition, immunosuppression, and gut microbiota dysbiosis. This study aims to systematically evaluate the effects of probiotic supplementation on clinical outcomes in patients with gastric cancer. A systematic search of 8 electronic databases was conducted to identify RCTs comparing the effects of probiotics versus standard care in gastric cancer patients. The ROB 2 was used to assess quality of RCTs. Stata 18 was employed to perform meta-analysis, subgroup analysis, meta-regression, sensitivity analysis, and publication bias analysis. The quality of evidence was graded using GRADE. A total of 34 studies (35 RCTs: 2792 patients) were included. The probiotic group showed improvements in serum protein levels (total protein: SMD = 1.25; albumin: SMD = 0.89), immune function parameters (CD3 Probiotic supplementation may be associated with positive changes in several short-term clinical outcomes and surrogate laboratory markers in patients with gastric cancer. However, all studies were conducted in China, most were of poor quality, and the certainty of GRADE evidence was generally low or very low. Therefore, these findings should be interpreted with caution. Future research requires more high-quality, multicenter RCTs with rigorous designs and standardized intervention protocols to further validate the clinical value of probiotics in patients with gastric cancer.
