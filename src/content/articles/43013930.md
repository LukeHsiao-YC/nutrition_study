### 重點摘要
生物電相位角(PhA)是細胞完整性和營養狀況的指標，但其在住院老年患者中預測長期死亡率的價值，特別是與肌少症的關係，尚未在大型前瞻性多中心研究中被充分探討。本研究分析了來自義大利12個急性病房的538名住院老年患者數據，旨在確定入院時的PhA是否能獨立預測36個月全因死亡率，並為肌少症提供額外的預後價值。結果顯示，PhA與36個月死亡率呈負相關，PhA每增加一個三分位數，死亡風險降低22%；值得注意的是，即使沒有肌少症，低PhA（<4.80°）患者的死亡風險與單純肌少症患者相近。研究結論PhA能獨立預測住院老年患者的長期死亡率，並對肌少症提供額外的預後價值，有助於識別標準肌少症篩查可能遺漏的高風險亞組，建議常規報告BIA數據中的PhA。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了生物電相位角(PhA)在大型前瞻性多中心研究中，作為住院老年患者長期（36個月）全因死亡率獨立預測因子，以及與肌少症相互作用的預後價值方面的研究空白。儘管研究證實了PhA的預後價值，但未來的研究可以進一步探討基於PhA進行的早期篩查和介入是否能改善患者的長期預後，以及其在不同族群或病況下的適用性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Bioelectrical phase angle as an independent predictor of 36-month all-cause mortality in older hospitalized patients: The interplay with sarcopenia.
**Abstract:** Bioelectrical phase angle (PhA), corresponding to the argument of the complex bioelectrical impedance measured by BIA (φ = arctan (Xc/R) × 180/π), is a marker of cellular integrity and nutritional status. Its long-term prognostic value in hospitalized older adults, particularly in relation to sarcopenia, has not been investigated yet in large prospective multicentre cohorts. We aimed to determine whether PhA on hospital admission independently predicts 36-month all-cause mortality and adds prognostic value to EWGSOP2-defined sarcopenia. We analysed data from 538 older adults with complete follow-up (mean age 81.2 ± 6.9 years, 48.0% male) enrolled in the GLISTEN prospective multicentre study, conducted in 12 Italian acute care wards. PhA was computed from raw resistance and reactance (Akern, 50 kHz, dominant side). PhA tertiles were obtained, with the following boundaries: T1, <4.32°; T2, 4.32°-5.32°; T3, ≥5.33°. A secondary analysis used the ROC-Youden cutoff of 4.80°, combined with EWGSOP2 sarcopenia status (four groups). All-cause mortality in 36 months was assessed from Kaplan-Meier curves, five nested Cox proportional hazard models, and restricted cubic splines. Mortality decreased stepwise across tertiles (T3: 25.1%, T2: 34.6%, T1: 46.1%; p < 0.0001). In the fully adjusted Cox model, each tertile increase in PhA was associated with a 22% reduction in mortality risk (HR 0.778, 95% CI 0.640,0.944, p = 0.011). The restricted cubic splines analysis confirmed a non-linear, monotonically decreasing relationship between PhA and mortality risk, with the highest hazard at very low PhA values. In the secondary four-group analysis, 36-month mortality ranged from 18.1% (PhA ≥4.80°, no sarcopenia) to 52.0% (PhA <4.80°, sarcopenia; HR 2.90, 95% CI 1.86,4.53). Notably, PhA <4.80° without sarcopenia carried mortality (35.4%) comparable to sarcopenia alone (39.5%). PhA independently predicts 36-month mortality in older hospitalized adults, with a dose-response gradient and additive prognostic value to sarcopenia. Low PhA without sarcopenia confers risk comparable to sarcopenia alone, identifying a high-risk subgroup missed by standard sarcopenia screening. Routine PhA reporting from BIA data could improve prognostic stratification at no additional cost.
