### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討「先下課後午餐」（reverse recess）與「先午餐後下課」對維吉尼亞州小學學生的午餐飲食品質、能量攝取及餐盤浪費的影響。研究採用橫斷面設計，分析了2019-2020學年間來自10所小學共2,524份餐盤的數據，利用數位影像評估午餐飲食品質（HEI-2015）、能量攝取和各餐點的浪費百分比。結果顯示，先下課後午餐與整體午餐飲食品質或總能量攝取無關，但顯著增加了水果的攝取量（例如HEI總水果和全水果分數更高，水果能量攝取更多），並減少了水果的浪費。儘管整體效果不明顯，但先下課後午餐確實改善了學生的水果攝取和減少水果浪費。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究雖然使用了客觀評估方法並涵蓋了大量多樣化的樣本，但其橫斷面設計無法確立因果關係。未來的研究需要採用前瞻性設計，並進行重複、全天的餐盤浪費評估，以確認本研究結果並為學校營養政策提供更堅實的依據。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** How does reverse recess relate to lunch diet quality, energy intake, and plate waste among elementary students in Virginia? A cross-sectional plate waste investigation.
**Abstract:** Modifications to the school environment, such as holding recess before lunch ("reverse recess") instead of after lunch, may be a cost-effective method to improve student nutrition and reduce food waste. Yet, investigations using objective assessments within large, diverse samples are needed. The current study examined differences in lunch diet quality, energy intake, and plate waste between students who had reverse recess and recess after lunch among a large, sociodemographically diverse sample. The current study is a secondary analysis of cross-sectional data from a school-based lunch plate waste investigation. Data were collected during the 2019-2020 school year. /setting: Ten Virginia elementary schools (M enrollment=678; 60% Title I; 54% National School Lunch participation rate) participated in a one-day lunch plate waste assessment. A total of 2,524 trays were evaluated (1,656 reverse recess). Validated digital imagery methods were applied to calculate lunch diet quality (Healthy Eating Index [HEI-2015]), lunch energy intake (kilocalories [kcals]), and lunch plate waste (% grams wasted) for each meal component. Hierarchal mixed models evaluated group differences in outcomes. Group-by-grade interactions were evaluated within grade levels. Variables are reported as mean values + standard deviations. Reverse recess was not associated with overall lunch diet quality or lunch total energy intake. Compared with students who had recess after lunch, students with reverse recess had small but significant differences in HEI Total Fruits (2.7±2.2 vs. 2.2±2.1; p< .0001) and Whole Fruits scores (3.0±2.3 vs. 2.7±2.4; p=.0001) at lunch. Students with reverse recess also consumed more energy from fruit (31±32 kcal vs. 22±26 kcal; p<.0001) at lunch. Fruit waste was lower among students with reverse recess (36% vs. 38% of grams wasted; p=.0001). A significant group by grade interaction was found for Total Protein Foods (p<.0001) and Total Vegetables (p=.0009) at lunch. Reverse recess was not associated with overall lunch diet quality or energy intake. Students with reverse recess had greater fruit intake and less fruit waste than students with recess after lunch, with small but significant differences. Prospective research designs, with repeated, full-day plate waste assessments, are necessary to confirm our findings and inform school nutrition policy.
