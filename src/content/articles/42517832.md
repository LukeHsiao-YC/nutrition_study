### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討一項針對降低失智風險的虛擬烹飪營養教育計畫中，參與者的社交體驗。研究採用質性方法，對21位55歲以上居住在加拿大薩斯喀徹溫省的參與者進行數據收集，該計畫透過Zoom提供營養教育與互動式烹飪示範課程。結果歸納出四個主題，顯示社交互動有助於學習和健康策略的應用，但也存在虛擬環境下的優缺點權衡。研究結論指出，雖然社交互動能促進知識提升和健康行為實踐，但虛擬課程仍需仔細規劃以克服社交障礙。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了在虛擬環境下針對老年人失智風險預防的營養教育計畫中，其社交互動體驗的理解空白，揭示了虛擬課程在促進社交和學習方面的潛力與挑戰。未來的研究需要探索更有效的策略來克服線上課程固有的社交障礙，以最大化參與者的投入和成果。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Examination of the Social Experience in a Virtual Culinary Nutrition Program for Dementia Risk Reduction.
**Abstract:** Examine the social experience of participants in a pilot virtual nutrition education program for dementia risk reduction. Observational fieldnotes, digital journal entries, semistructured interviews (n=15), and focus groups (n = 13) were used to collect qualitative data. Two separate but identical Cognitive Kitchen (CK) programs were delivered via Zoom from November to December 2022. Participants (n = 21) were adults aged 55 years or over living in Saskatchewan, Canada. The CK consisted of a preprogram orientation and 6 60-90-minute sessions that incorporated nutrition education and interactive food demonstrations. The functions of social interactions in the program; facilitators and barriers to social engagement within the virtual setting. The interpretive description approach guided inductive coding and the construction of themes. Four themes were developed. Two themes captured the functions of social interactions in supporting learning and encouraging application. Another theme identified inherent trade-offs: advantages and missed connections with virtual delivery. The last theme described strategies to maximize socialization, ingredients for engagement. Social interactions facilitated perceived outcomes of enhanced knowledge and implementation of health-promoting strategies among adults in the CK. Careful planning is required to overcome inherent barriers to socialization with web-based program delivery.
