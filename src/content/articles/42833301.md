### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討2019年至2023年間加拿大青少年對學校供餐計畫的認知與參與趨勢，特別分析了不同社會人口學和糧食不安全群體的差異。透過對16,173名10至17歲加拿大青少年的全國性調查數據進行二次分析，結果顯示青少年對學校早餐和午餐計畫的認知與參與率普遍偏低，且糧食不安全家庭中不到三分之一的青少年有參與。儘管2020年因COVID-19大流行導致參與率短暫下降，但到2023年已恢復至疫情前水平，且糧食不安全青少年的參與率相對最高。研究結論指出，在2019年至2023年間，學校供餐計畫的整體參與率並無顯著變化，突顯了提高其認知和參與度的重要性，尤其是在最需要幫助的群體中。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了加拿大青少年對學校供餐計畫參與度缺乏長期全國性人口數據的空白，並詳細分析了社會人口學和糧食不安全群體間的差異。然而，其研究限制包括未包含西北地區、育空地區和努納武特的數據，且數據為青少年自我報告。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Low self-reported awareness of and participation in school food programs among Canadian youth: Trends from secondary analysis of cross-sectional surveys from 2019 to 2023.
**Abstract:** School food programs (SFPs) provide access to nutritious food, particularly for youth from food-insecure households. There is limited population-level data on participation in Canadian SFPs over time. This study aimed to examine trends in self-reported awareness of and participation in SFPs among Canadian youth, including differences across sociodemographic and food insecurity groups. This study was a secondary analysis of repeat cross-sectional data from national surveys conducted annually from 2019 to 2023. Data were not collected from Northwest Territories, Yukon, and Nunavut. A total of 16173 youth aged 10 to 17 years residing in Canada participated in the study. The main outcome measures observed were youth self-reported school breakfast and lunch program awareness and participation. Weighted logistic regression models examined changes in breakfast or lunch program self-reported awareness and self-reported participation across survey years and by sex, age, province, and household food insecurity. Across all years, less than 30% of youth reported awareness of a breakfast program and approximately 7.5-13.9% of youth reported participation in a breakfast program. In the same period, 16.2% or fewer youth reported awareness of a lunch program and 4.5-9.1% reported participation in a lunch program. Decreases in self-reported participation coinciding with the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic were observed in 2020 for both breakfast (OR=0.59; 95% CI: 0.50, 0.71; p< 0.001) and lunch programs (OR=0.73; 95% CI: 0.58, 0.92; p=0.009), followed by a return to pre-pandemic levels by 2023. Self-reported participation in SFPs was associated with household food insecurity, race, and province of residence (all contrasts p<0.001). Less than one-third of youth from food-insecure households reported participation. Despite decreases in participation in 2020, no statistically significant change in self-reported participation was observed between 2019 and 2023, with self-reported participation highest among youth experiencing food insecurity.
