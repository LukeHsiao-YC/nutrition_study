### 重點摘要
本研究旨在分析政治因素、制度與治理如何影響坦尚尼亞水果與蔬菜政策的制定與執行，因該國F&V消費量低但政策重視不足。研究採用定性政策分析，透過訪談25位關鍵知情者及審查31份政策文件。結果顯示F&V雖有跨部門行動，但仍需強化政策協調、利害關係人參與及對小農的支持，並指出需改善融資、市場准入和推廣安全生產以促進消費。結論強調F&V在坦尚尼亞營養政策中被忽視，應加強政策連貫性、治理及財政與市場支持，以使其成為永續健康飲食的關鍵。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了坦尚尼亞在水果與蔬菜政策領域，對政治因素、制度與治理影響缺乏深入理解的空白。儘管水果與蔬菜對健康飲食至關重要，但其在國家營養政策議程中長期被忽視，並受主食優先、職權分散及協調不力等問題邊緣化。該研究闡明了這些政策執行上的深層原因，為未來強化政策連貫性、治理及財政與市場支持提供了依據。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Fruit and Vegetables for Sustainable Healthy Diets: Policy and Political Economy Analysis in Tanzania.
**Abstract:** This study aimed to analyze how political factors, institutions, and governance influence the prioritization, formulation, and implementation of fruit and vegetables (F&V) related policies in Tanzania, where - like in many Low- and Middle-Income Countries- consumption is low but there is limited priority and action for F&V in food policies across sectors. We undertook a qualitative policy analysis, based on policy process and political economy theories. Data were collected using key-informant interviews (n=25) and review of 31 policy documents between 2022 and 2023. We used a purposive sampling approach to identify respondents who were knowledgeable about F&V policies. Policy information relevant to F&V food systems was extracted to a matrix. Interview data were coded and processed in Quirkos software based on theory and the aims of the study. Tanzania, Sub-Saharan Africa. Key stakeholders who are knowledgeable about policy making and implementation related to F&V. The findings show a cross-sectoral action on F&V and highlight opportunities to strengthen policy coordination, stakeholder engagement, and support for smallholder farmers. The promotion of sustainable, healthy diets necessitates improving access to financing, safe production promotion, market access, and increased consumption through public campaigns. Fruit and vegetables are a critical but neglected in Tanzania's nutrition policy agenda. Despite their roles in healthy diets being increasingly recognized, the F&V subsector is marginalized by priority staple food, fragmented mandates, and poor coordination. Policy coherence, governance, and fiscal and market support need to be enhanced to place F&V as a strategic lever of sustainable healthy diets.
