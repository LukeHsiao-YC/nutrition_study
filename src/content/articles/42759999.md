### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在評估結合數位工具的營養諮詢對希臘高齡（≥55歲）代謝症候群高風險成人肥胖相關指標和代謝結果的有效性。為期三個月的隨機對照試驗將984名參與者分為標準營養諮詢、標準諮詢加網路平台、以及標準諮詢加平台和數位裝置三組。結果顯示所有組別的腰圍均有顯著下降，但單純標準營養諮詢組的下降幅度最大；身體活動在數位工具組有所增加，但所有組別在BMI、身體組成和血糖標誌物方面的改善程度相似。研究結論指出，營養諮詢能改善高風險成人的身體測量和代謝結果，然而數位工具在短期內未能提供額外的代謝益處，僅增強了身體活動。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了數位工具輔助營養諮詢在短期內能否為代謝症候群高風險老年人提供額外代謝益處的知識空白，結果顯示其主要作用在於增強身體活動而非額外的代謝改善。研究限制為其僅為期三個月，未能評估長期效益。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Impact of Dietetic Counseling Combined With Digital Tools on Obesity-Related Indices in Greek Adults: The GATEKEEPER Study.
**Abstract:** This study aimed to evaluate the effectiveness of dietetic counseling with digital tools on obesity-related and metabolic outcomes in adults aged ≥ 55 years at high risk for metabolic syndrome (MetS). In this 3-month randomized controlled trial, 984 adults aged ≥ 55 years with ≥ 1 MetS risk factor were randomized (1:1:1) to: standard care (monthly dietetic counseling), standard care plus web-based platform, or standard care plus platform and digital devices. Analyses included 954 participants and used linear mixed-effect models under a missing-at-random assumption adjusted for age and sex. The primary outcome was change in waist circumference. Secondary outcomes included BMI, body composition indices, physical activity, and glycemic markers. Waist circumference decreased in all groups (-6.29, -4.92, -4.69 cm), with smaller reductions in the platform (MD -1.38 cm; 95% CI -2.37 to -0.38) and platform + devices groups (MD -1.61 cm; 95% CI -2.61 to -0.60). BMI, body composition indices, and glycemic markers improved similarly across groups. Physical activity increased more in the platform + devices group (MD +55.52 MET-min/week; 95% CI 15.32 to 95.72). Dietetic counseling improved anthropometric and metabolic outcomes in adults at risk for MetS. Digital tools enhanced physical activity but did not provide additional short-term metabolic benefits. ClinicalTrials.gov identifier: NCT05031299.
