### 重點摘要
GLP-1藥物已革新肥胖治療，但快速減重常伴隨瘦體組織減少。本研究假設，快速減重可能因能量與蛋白質攝取、機械負荷及身體活動減少，增加骨骼肌的生物能量脆弱性。文章提出肌酸補充劑是一種具生物學合理性的輔助策略，有望支持此情境下的肌肉能量與功能，以助於保留肌肉品質和功能能力。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本文提出肌酸補充用於GLP-1相關肌肉流失為一項具生物學合理性的假設，但目前缺乏隨機臨床試驗直接評估其在GLP-1藥物減重期間的實際效益。未來的研究應透過多模式肌肉保存策略的臨床試驗來驗證肌酸的效果。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Creatine for glucagon-like peptide-1-associated muscle loss.
**Abstract:** Glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1)-based therapies have transformed obesity treatment, yet reductions in lean tissue may accompany pharmacological weight loss. We hypothesize that rapid weight loss and associated reductions in energy and protein intake, mechanical loading, and potentially physical activity may create conditions of increased skeletal-muscle bioenergetic vulnerability. Creatine supplementation represents a biologically plausible adjunct that could support muscle energetics and function in this setting. However, this proposal is hypothesis-generating, and no randomized clinical trial has directly evaluated creatine supplementation during GLP-1-based pharmacological weight loss. Future trials should determine whether creatine, as part of a multimodal muscle-preservation strategy, can help preserve muscle quality and functional capacity during treatment.
