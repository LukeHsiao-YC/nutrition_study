### 重點摘要
本研究旨在解決嬰兒飲食追蹤的挑戰，開發了一種新穎的糞便DNA分析工具FoodSeq。研究人員分析了73名9至12個月大嬰兒的糞便樣本，並將FoodSeq數據與嬰兒飲食頻率問卷(FFQ)數據進行比較。結果顯示，糞便DNA檢測到的食物與FFQ報告的食物之間存在顯著的一致性，包括堅果、馬鈴薯和配方奶。這項研究支持了糞便DNA分析作為一種非侵入性方法，用於評估12個月以下嬰兒飲食的潛力。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了傳統上高度依賴家長回憶錄入嬰兒飲食資訊而可能導致不準確的研究空白，引入了一種非侵入性、基於生物標誌的客觀評估方法。未來的研究限制可能包括擴展可檢測食物種類的範圍、提高工具對不同食物基質的靈敏度，以及在更廣泛的年齡層和不同人群中進行進一步的驗證。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Assessing dietary intake in infants through fecal sample analysis in the LLNEXT Cohort.
**Abstract:** Accurate diet tracing is fundamental to diet-health association studies, yet it remains challenging. This is particularly the case for early life studies, which rely on parental recall. Fecal sample analysis offers a non-invasive method for assessing those dietary components that have sufficient quantities of DNA remaining in stool, and are able to be taxonomically distinguished by marker gene sequence. We aimed to determine whether a fecal DNA-based approach can be used to characterize infant dietary history. We detected food-derived DNA in fecal samples from 73 infants at 9 and 12 months of age (139 samples total) and 25 mothers (25 samples) in the Dutch Lifelines NEXT cohort using FoodSeq, a novel fecal DNA-based tool. We compared FoodSeq data with infant food frequency questionnaire (FFQ) data using logistic mixed-effects models. We also evaluated the coherence of matching FoodSeq/FFQ in relation to the gut microbiome. Infant fecal samples demonstrated a lower diversity in food groups than observed in the mothers. We identified nine significant associations between matching DNA-detected and FFQ-reported food items including nuts (OR = 1.89 [1.24-2.89], q = 0.035), potatoes (OR = 2.16 [1.94-3.91], q = 0.064), dairy (OR = 2.28 [1.20-4.33], q = 0.061), formula feeding (OR = 3.96 [1.58-9.94], q = 0.055), and pork (OR = 2.44 [1.27-4.67], q = 0.063).
