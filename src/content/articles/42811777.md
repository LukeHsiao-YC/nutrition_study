### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在評估腸道超音波 (IUS) 與磁振造影 (MRE) 在兒童克隆氏症 (CD) 小腸疾病活動度診斷上的準確性，並確定最佳腸壁厚度 (BWT) 閾值。研究納入了78名兒童CD患者，將常規MRE評估的疾病活動度與IUS測量的BWT及IUS評分進行比較。結果顯示，IUS能有效且準確地評估末端迴腸和迴腸的疾病活動度。根據研究結果，兒童CD患者在末端迴腸的最佳BWT閾值為2.4毫米，迴腸為2.0毫米，可區分活動性與非活動性疾病。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究成功為兒童克隆氏症患者定義了使用腸道超音波測量末端迴腸和迴腸腸壁厚度的最佳診斷閾值，填補了該領域在非侵入性評估工具上的具體診斷標準空白。然而，由於空腸受影響的節段數量較少，未能確定其精確的腸壁厚度閾值，這為未來研究留下了探索空間。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Intestinal ultrasound versus magnetic resonance enterography for detecting small bowel Crohn's disease in children.
**Abstract:** In Crohn's disease (CD), magnetic resonance enterography (MRE) is used to assess small bowel disease activity. Intestinal ultrasound (IUS) is a less invasive, cheaper and more easily accessible imaging modality in CD. This study assesses the diagnostic accuracy of IUS versus MRE and defines an optimal bowel wall thickness (BWT) cut-off in children. Children with CD undergoing MRE as routine care had IUS within 7 days. MRE disease activity was assessed using the segmental grading system and compared to IUS BWT and IUS scores. The terminal ileum (TI), ileum and jejunum were analysed separately. Seventy-eight paediatric CD patients (median age: 15 years; 49% female) were included. Based on MRE, 44 bowel segments had mild and 37 moderate-severe disease activity. The area under the receiver operating characteristic of IUS BWT for disease activity in the TI was 0.91 (95% confidence interval [CI] 0.84-0.98) with an optimal cut-off of 2.4 mm, 0.80 (95% CI 0.66-0.94) for the ileum with an optimal cut-off of 2.0 mm (sensitivity 76%; specificity 93%) and 0.69 (95% CI 0.52-0.86) for the jejunum. Due to the low number of affected segments in the jejunum, an accurate BWT cut-off value could not be determined. Previously developed IUS scores did not outperform BWT. IUS is an accurate tool to non-invasively assess CD activity in the TI and ileum. Based on our results, a BWT cut-off of 2.4 mm in the TI and 2.0 mm in the ileum in paediatric CD is optimal to distinguish active from inactive disease.
