### 重點摘要
鑑於當前飲食指南爭議、飲食相關慢性病負擔增加及環境壓力，本研究旨在開發一套兼顧人類與地球健康的全球適用飲食指南。國際碳水化合物品質聯盟 (ICQC) 採用四步驟證據審查流程，整合各國指南、證據綜合及專家共識，並參考EAT-Lancet委員會量化數據，建構了「國際食物指南金字塔」。此金字塔推崇以植物為主的雜食模式，強調蔬菜、水果、全穀物、豆類、堅果、種子與不飽和植物油，並適度攝取動物性食品（禽肉、魚類優於紅肉，乳製品適量）。它特別強調碳水化合物品質，優先選擇全穀物及低升糖指數的主食，同時限制低營養價值的糖分，最終提供了一個連貫、彈性且全球適用的飲食框架，以促進人類與地球健康。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了全球範圍內缺乏一套統合人類健康與環境永續性的飲食指南的空白，特別在飲食指南開發具爭議性、飲食相關慢性病負擔日益增加以及環境壓力日漸升高的背景下。它透過整合多個獨立的循證來源，將其轉化為實用的視覺框架，提供了一種可全球適應的飲食指導方法，並明確納入碳水化合物品質的考量。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Toward International Dietary Guidance: A Food Guide Pyramid for Human and Planetary Health.
**Abstract:** Dietary guidelines shape food policy, education, and food systems worldwide. Recent controversies in dietary guideline development, together with rising burdens of diet-related chronic disease and increasing environmental pressures, highlight the need for globally relevant dietary guidance aligned with both human and planetary health. We developed an evidence-informed International Food Guide Pyramid emphasizing optimal carbohydrate-containing staple foods. The International Carbohydrate Quality Consortium (ICQC) developed the proposed framework using a four-step evidence-review process involving comparison of major national and regional dietary guideline systems, integration of evidence syntheses and selected primary studies, and expert consensus. Quantitative intake ranges from the EAT-Lancet Commission reference diet informed the relative visual proportions of food groups, while the guiding principles were derived from convergence across dietary guideline systems and complementary evidence syntheses. The resulting pyramid depicts a plant-forward omnivorous dietary pattern supported by epidemiological and clinical evidence indicating benefits for cardiometabolic health and chronic disease prevention. Vegetables, fruits, whole grains, and legumes constitute foundational components of the pyramid. Nuts, seeds, and unsaturated plant oils are emphasized as primary fat sources, while foods high in saturated fat are generally limited. Animal-source foods are included in modest amounts, with plant protein sources emphasized, poultry and fish preferred over red meat, and dairy incorporated in modest amounts, consistent with dietary patterns associated with lower environmental impacts. Carbohydrate quality is emphasized through prioritization of whole-grain foods alongside traditional low-glycemic index grain staples, favoring carbohydrate sources rich in dietary fiber, intact plant cells, and micronutrients while limiting sugars consumed in low-nutrient forms, particularly sugar-sweetened beverages. The framework also accommodates vegan and other primarily plant-based dietary patterns. This evidence-informed International Food Guide Pyramid integrates quantitative modeling and qualitative evidence synthesis to provide a coherent, flexible, and globally adaptable dietary framework aligned with both human and planetary health. STATEMENT OF SIGNIFICANCE: This work presents an evidence-informed International Food Guide Pyramid developed through a structured synthesis of major national and regional dietary guideline systems, quantitative modeling from the EAT-Lancet Commission, and complementary evidence syntheses. By translating areas of convergence across these independent evidence-based sources into a practical visual framework, it provides a globally adaptable approach to dietary guidance that explicitly incorporates carbohydrate quality and both human and planetary health.
