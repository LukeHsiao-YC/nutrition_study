### 重點摘要
本研究旨在複製並評估健康行為的COM-B模型在男性主導樣本中的適用性，先前該模型主要在女性樣本中驗證。研究對1358名參與者（67%男性）進行結構方程模型分析，結果顯示COM-B模型整體擬合良好，解釋了22%的飲食品質變異。與女性樣本相似，能力和機會透過動機間接影響飲食行為，動機為最強預測因子。然而，在指標層面發現性別差異，這凸顯了針對不同人群設計測量工具和介入措施的重要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了健康行為理論在應用上對性別中立的假設，明確指出COM-B模型在高階路徑上具有跨性別穩定性，但在特定指標層面可能存在差異。未來的研究需要進一步驗證這些指標層面的性別差異假設，並解決樣本中概念上不同構念間的辨別效度問題。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Capability, opportunity, and motivation for healthy eating: Exploring gender-specific pathways.
**Abstract:** Women generally consume a healthier diet than men; however, both fall short of dietary guidelines. Health behaviour theories are often applied in a gender-neutral manner, implicitly assuming that explanatory models operate similarly across groups. This conceptual replication tested the Capability, Opportunity, Motivation and Behaviour (COM-B) model for dietary behaviour, originally evaluated in a female-dominant sample, in a male-dominant sample (N = 1358; 67% male). Using the same measurement battery and a closely aligned analytic approach, structural equation modelling indicated acceptable overall fit, with the COM-B model explaining 22% of the variance in diet quality. As in the original study, capability and opportunity were associated with behaviour indirectly through motivation, which was the strongest predictor of diet quality. At the indicator level, the male-dominant model retained action control and habits (capability), descriptive norms and the home food environment (opportunity), and self-efficacy, intentions, affective attitudes and perceived behavioural control (motivation), whereas identity, decision-making barriers, instrumental attitudes and the shopping environment indicator were not retained during trimming. These differences, relative to the female-dominant model, suggest potential variation in how COM-B constructs are operationalised across gender-dominant samples and should be treated as hypothesis-generating. Discriminant validity concerns (habits-action control; self-efficacy-intention) further indicated overlap between conceptually distinct constructs in the male-dominant sample. Overall, findings support the stability of the higher-order COM-B pathway while highlighting potential group differences in indicator contributions, with implications for measurement and population-sensitive intervention design.
