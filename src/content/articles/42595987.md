### 重點摘要
這項質性研究旨在探討公眾如何回顧性描述其COVID-19疫情經歷，以及這些經驗如何隨時間被記憶和詮釋。研究分析了公眾在公共空間表演性介入中撰寫的短文，並發現疫情不僅是健康危機，更是重大的社會與情感挑戰，而個人能否從中獲得成長則與穩定資源密切相關。研究結論強調疫情的記憶與社會條件緊密連結，呼籲以包容性與反思性方法理解其長期影響，並指出社會不平等在應對未來公衛危機中的關鍵作用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究填補了過去文獻對疫情經驗如何被回顧性詮釋以及社會條件如何影響這些敘事理解的空白，特別是連結了穩定資源與個人適應或成長能力的關係。未來的研究可進一步探討如何針對特定社會不平等設計具體的介入措施，以提升疫情的長期復原力。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Retrospective narratives of COVID-19 experiences: a qualitative study of written accounts by people in public in the context of a performative intervention.
**Abstract:** This qualitative study explored how people retrospectively described their experiences of the COVID-19 pandemic. It aimed how these experiences are remembered and interpreted over time. A qualitative study design was employed, based on short texts written by members of the public during a performative intervention in public space. The texts were thematically analyzed to identify common experiences and emotions. The findings show that participants described the pandemic not only as a health crisis but also as a major social and emotional challenge. Feelings of fear, uncertainty, and loss of control were common, alongside attempts to find meaning and emotional stability. Narratives also suggest that the ability to reflect on the pandemic as a period of adaptation or personal growth depended on access to stable resources such as housing, social support, and digital connectivity. Overall, the study highlights that how the pandemic is remembered is closely linked to social conditions, underlining the importance of inclusive and reflective approaches to understanding the long-term impacts of COVID-19. In addition, it demonstrates that social inequalities in both the interpretation of past crises and the development of inclusive strategies are important for future public health response.
