### 重點摘要
澳洲正面臨嚴重的食物浪費與日益加劇的糧食不安全問題，特別是醫院的大量剩餘食物目前被送往垃圾掩埋場。為應對此困境，本文提出重新利用醫院剩餘食物的策略，以暫時支持糧食不安全的個人與家庭，直到大規模糧食系統改革實現。文中建議了四種潛在用途，包括提供醫學配方餐、出院時提供食物、捐贈以及醫院內部系統再利用。這些策略有望同時減少醫院食物浪費並為急需者提供糧食援助，並呼籲醫院餐飲服務部門改變現狀。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文章提出了解決醫院食物浪費與糧食不安全問題的具體策略，填補了如何實踐性地重新利用醫院剩餘食物的空白。然而，其限制在於這是一篇評論性文章，尚未實際驗證這些策略的實施成效，並且需要進一步研究如何克服實際操作中的潛在挑戰。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Repurposing surplus Australian hospital food to support food insecurity and reduce food waste.
**Abstract:** Australia is experiencing a current juxtaposition between large amounts of food waste and increasing food insecurity. Surplus food produced by Australian hospitals is currently sent to landfill but could be temporarily repurposed to support food-insecure individuals and families until large-scale food system reform is achieved. We comment on potential uses for this surplus 'food waste', including four strategies: medically tailored meals, food provision at discharge, donation and internal hospital system reuse. These strategies could simultaneously reduce hospital food waste while providing food relief for those who need it most. We highlight the potential challenges to adopting this strategy in practice and provide a final call to action for hospital foodservices to challenge the status quo.
