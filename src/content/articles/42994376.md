### 重點摘要
本研究旨在比較15種簡易指標在識別核磁共振成像（MRI）定義的內臟型肥胖（VO）上的效能，因為MRI不適用於大規模篩查。這項橫斷面研究納入600名中國成年人，結果顯示，腰圍（WC）在整體人群中具有最佳的鑑別效能，其截止值為90.25公分，敏感度為75.1%，特異度為85.0%。研究結論指出，腰圍因其實用性和強大的鑑別能力，可作為社區篩查內臟型肥胖的首要測量工具，而TyG複合指數可在有額外代謝數據時提供補充資訊。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了比較多種簡易指標與MRI定義的內臟型肥胖之研究空白，並提供了在特定人群（中國成年人）中的性別差異表現。然而，本研究得出的指標截止值尚需外部驗證。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Magnetic resonance imaging-based assessment of visceral obesity: Sex-specific performance and screening utility of 15 simple indices.
**Abstract:** Visceral obesity (VO) is strongly associated with cardiometabolic risk, but magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) is impractical for large-scale screening. We compared 15 simple indices for identifying MRI-defined VO. This community-based cross-sectional study included 600 Chinese adults aged 40-65 years (296 men and 304 women). Visceral fat area (VFA) was measured by MRI, and VO was defined as VFA >100 cm Of the 600 participants, 173 (28.8%) had VO. Overall, waist circumference (WC) had the highest AUC (0.866; 95% confidence interval, 0.835-0.898); a cutoff of 90.25 cm yielded 75.1% sensitivity and 85.0% specificity. The TyG-WC composite had an AUC of 0.859, which did not differ significantly from WC. In men, WC had the highest AUC (0.821), while TyG combined with body roundness index (TyG-BRI) was the leading TyG-derived composite (AUC 0.810), with no significant difference between them. In women, TyG combined with body mass index (TyG-BMI) had the highest AUC (0.843) but did not significantly outperform WC or BMI. Adjusted analyses supported these findings. WC combined strong discrimination with practical feasibility, supporting its use as the primary measure for community screening. TyG-derived composite indices showed favorable discrimination and may provide additional anthropometric-metabolic information when fasting glucose and triglyceride data are available. The study-derived cutoffs require external validation.
