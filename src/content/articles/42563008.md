### 重點摘要
血液透析患者常有營養不良問題。本研究旨在驗證使用不同肌肉量評估方法（生物電阻抗分析BIA、中臂肌圍MAMC、小腿圍CC）的全球營養不良領導倡議 (GLIM) 標準，並評估其與功能狀態、生活品質及死亡率的關聯。研究發現GLIM標準在血液透析患者中顯示約25-27%的營養不良盛行率，且所有GLIM變體與多項營養和功能指標相關。其中，BIA-GLIM和CC-GLIM能預測較高的死亡率，證實了GLIM標準的有效性。本研究指出MAMC與CC等身體測量型GLIM變體可作為BIA的實用替代方案，具有高特異性和有意義的並行效度。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了在血液透析患者中，使用多種肌肉量評估方法（尤其是更易於執行的中臂肌圍和小腿圍）來驗證全球營養不良領導倡議 (GLIM) 標準與其臨床預後關聯性的空白，並證明了它們作為生物電阻抗分析的實用替代方案。然而，文章明確指出這些身體測量型GLIM變體仍需在更多樣化的血液透析人群中進行進一步驗證。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Global leadership initiative on malnutrition criteria using different muscle mass assessment methods in hemodialysis patients: Links to physical performance, quality of life, and clinical outcomes.
**Abstract:** Malnutrition is frequent in maintenance hemodialysis (MHD). We aimed to validate the Global Leadership Initiative on Malnutrition (GLIM) criteria using different muscle mass assessment methods and evaluate their associations with nutritional biomarkers, functional status, quality of life, and mortality. This prospective study included 218 MHD outpatients (63% male; mean age 70.6 ± 12.2 years). Muscle mass was assessed using bioimpedance analysis (BIA), mid-arm muscle circumference (MAMC), and calf circumference (CC). Serum biomarkers, handgrip strength, gait speed, and nutritional scores (Malnutrition Inflammation Score [MIS], Geriatric Nutritional Risk Index [GNRI]) were obtained. Malnutrition was defined as MIS > 10. Associations with SF-36 scales and survival were analysed. Malnutrition prevalence was 26.1% with BIA-based GLIM, 25.2% with MAMC-based GLIM, and 27.1% with CC. BIA-based GLIM correlated with albumin, MIS, GNRI, phase angle, handgrip strength, and gait speed. MAMC-based GLIM showed similar associations except for gait speed. CC-based GLIM correlated with albumin and GNRI. Agreement with MIS was fair (κ = 0.31-0.35). All GLIM variants showed high specificity but low sensitivity. Only BIA- and MAMC-based GLIM were associated with reduced quality of life. BIA-based GLIM predicted higher mortality (HR 1.70, 95% CI 1.01-2.85), and CC-based GLIM showed a stronger association (HR 2.94, 95% CI 1.41-6.14). MAMC-based GLIM showed a nonsignificant trend. GLIM demonstrated fair agreement with MIS, high specificity, and meaningful concurrent validity. MAMC- and CC-based GLIM provided practical alternatives to BIA. These findings support further validation of anthropometric GLIM variants in diverse MHD populations.
