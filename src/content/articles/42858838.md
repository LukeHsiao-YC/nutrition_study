### 重點摘要
妊娠糖尿病 (GDM) 患者常因早晨胰島素阻抗導致早餐後高血糖和全天血糖波動大，但實證的飲食治療方案仍不足。本隨機交叉對照試驗比較了低升糖負荷早餐 (LGL-B) 與高升糖負荷早餐 (HGL-B) 對 GDM 婦女早餐後 1 小時血糖和 24 小時血糖變異性的影響。結果顯示，LGL-B 顯著降低了早餐後血糖及 24 小時血糖變異性。因此，將早餐調整為低升糖負荷飲食可能是一種簡單且有效的飲食策略，有助於改善 GDM 婦女的血糖控制並減少早晨胰島素需求。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了妊娠糖尿病 (GDM) 缺乏具體實證飲食策略的空白，特別是針對早餐的飲食組成對血糖控制的影響。然而，由於本研究為短期試驗，其長期效果及對 GDM 婦女的持續健康益處仍需未來的研究進一步探討。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** The Impact of a Low Glycemic Load Breakfast on Diurnal Glucose Profiles in Women with Gestational Diabetes Mellitus: The GLIDING Randomized Crossover Trial.
**Abstract:** Medical nutrition therapy is first-line treatment in gestational diabetes (GDM) management, yet evidence-based approaches remain lacking. In women with GDM, increased morning insulin resistance often results in postprandial (breakfast) hyperglycemia, potentially leading to increased 24-h glycemic variability (GV). In a short-term randomized crossover control trial, we compared the effects of a low glycemic load (GL) breakfast (LGL-B) to a high GL breakfast (HGL-B) on 1-h postprandial breakfast glucose (PPG) and 24-h GV (coefficient of variation [%CV]) in women with GDM. Women were provided an LGL-B (245 kcal; 10.9g carbohydrate) and HGL-B (247 kcal; 43.4g carbohydrate) in random order for 7 days, separated by a 7-day washout. Fasting blood was drawn on Days 1, 8, 15 and 22 (pre/post 7-day interventions) and blinded continuous glucose monitoring was undertaken. Breakfast 1-h PPG and %CV were analyzed using mixed effect models to compare between intervention effects with Bonferroni adjustments. Targeting breakfast may be a simple dietary strategy to improve glycemia and reduce morning bolus insulin requirements.
