### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估兒童飲食行為問卷 (CEBQ) 在薩摩亞兒童中的因子效度，並探討其子量表與體重狀況的關聯。研究招募了400名5-11歲兒童的照顧者完成CEBQ，發現其原始八因子結構不適用於薩摩亞兒童。探索性因子分析提出一個包含14個項目、三個因子的新結構（食物享受、情緒性少食、情緒性過食），其中「食物享受」因子與兒童肥胖風險顯著相關。研究強調在不同文化背景下應用飲食行為測量工具前，需先建立其測量效度。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了CEBQ在薩摩亞兒童族群中因子效度評估的空白，指出其原始結構不適用於該文化背景。未來的研究需要進一步確認本文提出的三因子新結構，以確保其穩定性與適用性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Cross-Cultural Factorial Validation of the Children's Eating Behavior Questionnaire (CEBQ) in Samoan Children: A Methodological Note.
**Abstract:** To evaluate the factorial validity of the 35-item Children's Eating Behavior Questionnaire (CEBQ) among Samoan children and assess whether empirically derived subscales were associated with weight status. Caregivers of n = 400 children aged 5-11 years in the ongoing Ola Tuputupua'e cohort study completed the CEBQ. We tested the original eight-factor structure using confirmatory factor analysis. Because model fit was poor, we conducted exploratory factor analysis and used multinomial logistic regression to examine associations between derived subscales and child weight status. The original CEBQ structure fit poorly (chi-square/df = 3.53, CFI = 0.654, TLI = 0.613, RMSEA = 0.080). EFA identified a 14-item, three-factor solution: Enjoyment of Food, Emotional Undereating, and Emotional Overeating. Compared with children in the lowest Enjoyment of Food tertile, those in the second and third tertiles had higher adjusted odds of obesity (aOR = 4.59, 95% CI: 2.56-8.23; aOR = 10.56, 95% CI: 4.79-23.26, respectively). Emotional eating factors were not significantly associated with weight status. The original CEBQ factor structure was not replicated among Samoan children. The alternative three-factor structure requires confirmation, but the findings underscore the need to establish measurement validity before applying eating behavior instruments across cultural settings.
