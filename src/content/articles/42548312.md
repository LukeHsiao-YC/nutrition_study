### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討Omega-3脂肪酸（DHA和EPA）與美國成年人肥胖相關憂鬱症共病症的關聯性及潛在機制。透過對4,423名NHANES參與者的橫斷面分析，評估血清脂肪酸、肥胖與憂鬱症狀，並運用多變量邏輯迴歸及網路藥理學進行分析。結果顯示，較高水平的DHA與女性及老年人中央型肥胖合併憂鬱症的風險較低有關，DHA亦獨立地與較低的中央型肥胖、憂鬱症狀及其共病症風險相關，而EPA則無顯著關聯。機制探索指出DHA可能透過TNF和IL-17訊息傳導路徑發揮作用，提示DHA對肥胖相關憂鬱症具有潛在的保護作用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究填補了Omega-3脂肪酸（尤其是DHA）與肥胖合併憂鬱症共病症之間關聯性及其潛在抗發炎機制的知識空白，並區分了DHA與EPA在其中的不同作用。然而，由於其為橫斷面研究設計，無法確立因果關係，未來的研究需要前瞻性設計來進一步驗證這些發現。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Serum Omega-3 Fatty Acids and Obesity-Comorbid Depression in US Adults: An Integrated Epidemiology and Network Pharmacology Study.
**Abstract:** Omega-3 polyunsaturated fatty acids (PUFAs) docosahexaenoic acid (DHA) and eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA), possess anti-inflammatory properties, yet their association with obesity-depression comorbidity remains unclear. This study investigated the association among US adults and explored underlying mechanisms. We performed a cross-sectional analysis of 4,423 individuals participating in the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) 2003-2004 & 2011-2014. Serum fatty acids were quantified by gas chromatography. Obesity was defined using anthropometric criteria, and depression was assessed using the PHQ-9 or antidepressant use. Multivariable logistic regression estimated odds ratios (ORs) per standard deviation (SD) increase in PUFA levels. Mechanistic explore through network pharmacology identified potential pathways, which were examined using correlation analyses with inflammatory indices. Higher omega-3 PUFA levels were associated with lower odds of central obesity comorbid depression in females (OR: 0.82, 95% CI: 0.69-0.96) and older adults (OR: 0.79, 95% CI: 0.64-0.97). DHA was significantly associated with lower odds of central obesity (OR: 0.83, 95% CI: 0.73-0.95), depressive symptoms (OR: 0.88, 95% CI: 0.77-1.00), and their comorbidity (OR: 0.85, 95% CI: 0.74-0.98), whereas no significant associations were found for EPA.
