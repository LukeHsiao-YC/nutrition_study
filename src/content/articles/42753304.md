### 重點摘要
本研究評估了數位嚴肅遊戲PeroPap對兒童圍手術期適應及症狀（疼痛、焦慮、舒適度、噁心）的有效性。一項隨機對照試驗納入182名7-10歲接受手術的兒童，實驗組使用PeroPap進行術前準備，對照組則使用標準教育手冊。結果顯示，實驗組在圍手術期焦慮、術後疼痛及噁心方面顯著較低，且舒適度顯著較高。研究結論指出，PeroPap數位遊戲能有效降低兒童手術相關焦慮、疼痛和噁心，並提升舒適度，證明遊戲化教育是改善兒科手術患者圍手術期症狀管理的有效策略。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻透過實證研究填補了在兒科手術領域中，數位遊戲作為一種創新且以兒童為中心的介入措施，在有效管理圍手術期焦慮、疼痛和提高舒適度方面的研究空白。它證明了此類可擴展的數位化教育工具，能改善傳統術前準備的不足，提升患者體驗與照護品質。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effect of a perioperative digital serious game (PeroPap) on pain and anxiety in children: A randomized controlled trial.
**Abstract:** This study aimed to evaluate the effectiveness of the Perioperative Pediatric Application (PeroPap) on perioperative adaptation and symptoms, including pain, anxiety, comfort, and nausea in children. This prospective randomized controlled trial was conducted in the pediatric surgery clinic of a tertiary education and research hospital in Türkiye. The study included 182 children aged 7-10 years undergoing surgery and their parents, who were randomly assigned to the experimental (n = 91) or control group (n = 91). Children in the experimental group received perioperative preparation through the PeroPap digital serious game one day before surgery, whereas the control group received standard preparation using an educational booklet. Perioperative anxiety and postoperative outcomes including pain, nausea, and comfort were assessed using validated instruments. Perioperative anxiety scores at post-intervention assessment time points were significantly higher in the control group (p < 0.001). Postoperative pain and nausea scores at 1 and 2 h after surgery were significantly higher in the control group (p < 0.001). Children in the experimental group demonstrated significantly higher comfort levels than those in the control group (p < 0.001). The PeroPap digital serious game effectively reduced perioperative anxiety, pain, and nausea while improving comfort in children undergoing surgery. Digital game-based education may be an effective strategy for improving perioperative symptom management in pediatric surgical patients. Integrating digital serious games such as PeroPap into routine preoperative education can enhance children's engagement and effectively reduce perioperative anxiety and pain. These scalable, child-centered interventions represent a practical strategy for improving perioperative outcomes and patient-centered care in pediatric settings.
