### 重點摘要
南亞地區含糖飲料（SSB）消費量與飲食相關非傳染性疾病負擔日益增加，但現行政策零碎且執行不力。本研究借鑒菸草控制經驗，提出S-POWER（監測、保護、提供、警告、執行、提高）實施框架，以加強南亞SSB的法規管理。S-POWER整合了監測、兒童保護、健康替代品提供、包裝正面營養標示、行銷限制及健康稅收等六大互補政策領域。此框架旨在將現有實證措施整合為連貫的監管方法，為南亞及其他面臨類似營養轉型和商業壓力的地區提供實用基礎，以強化政策實施。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了南亞地區含糖飲料政策碎片化且實施不均的空白，提供了一個將現有實證措施整合為連貫監管方法的實用框架。其主要貢獻在於提出一個系統化的執行框架，而非新的政策工具，旨在彌補政策缺乏統一整合的現狀。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** From MPOWER to S-POWER: adapting tobacco-control frameworks for sugar-sweetened beverage regulation in South Asia.
**Abstract:** Sugar-sweetened beverage (SSB) consumption is increasing across South Asia, alongside a growing burden of diet-related non-communicable diseases. Although countries in the region have introduced measures including taxation, nutrition labelling and restrictions in institutional settings, policies remain fragmented and implementation is uneven. Drawing on lessons from tobacco control, we propose Surveil, Protect, Offer, Warn, Enforce, and Raise (S-POWER) as an implementation framework to strengthen SSB regulation in South Asia. S-POWER brings together six complementary policy domains: surveillance of consumption and policy impact; protection of children from unhealthy food environments; provision of healthier alternatives; front-of-pack nutrition labelling; restrictions on marketing and promotion and health-oriented taxation. Rather than proposing new policy instruments, the framework seeks to connect existing evidence-based measures within a coherent regulatory approach. S-POWER may provide a practical basis for strengthening policy implementation in South Asia and other settings facing similar nutrition transitions and commercial pressures.
