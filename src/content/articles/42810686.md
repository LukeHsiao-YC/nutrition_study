### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討血清維生素B12濃度與癌症發生率及全因死亡率之間的劑量反應關係和時間結構。研究分析了近400萬名成年人的回溯性世代數據，結果顯示血清B12濃度過高與癌症發生率輕微升高有關，但此關聯性在排除早期事件後顯著減弱。全因死亡率則呈現J型模式，在B12濃度過高或過低時均有所升高，且B12缺乏所導致的死亡率關聯性更為持久。儘管有這些觀察性發現，但研究結論指出，這些結果不足以支持停用臨床上已指示的維生素B12治療。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究利用大規模數據，系統性地描繪了血清維生素B12濃度與癌症及全因死亡率之間的複雜關聯，特別是闡明了高濃度B12與癌症關聯的時間依賴性（即早期事件的影響）。然而，作為一項觀察性研究，它無法建立因果關係，且其發現不足以直接改變現行臨床實踐中對維生素B12的治療指引。未來的研究可能需進一步探討其潛在的生物學機制或進行干預性研究來證實這些關聯。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Elevated Serum Vitamin B
**Abstract:** Serum vitamin B We aimed to characterize the dose-response shape and temporal structure of the associations of serum vitamin B We conducted a retrospective propensity score-matched cohort study of 3,950,497 adults from the TriNetX Global Collaborative Network (169 healthcare organizations, 19 countries, 2006-2020). Adults were classified into six baseline serum B Overall cancer incidence increased modestly above the reference (adjusted hazard ratio [aHR] 1.05-1.08), with no association in the deficiency range. All-cause mortality followed a J-shaped pattern, with elevations at both extremes. The elevated-end cancer association attenuated to near-null after excluding the first two years of follow-up (>1,000 pg/mL: aHR 1.07 to 1.02), whereas the deficiency-end mortality association showed the least attenuation (<200 pg/mL: aHR 1.26 at 1 year to 1.18 at 10 years). Cancer incidence was modestly higher at elevated serum vitamin B12 concentrations but attenuated after exclusion of early events, whereas the mortality association at deficiency-range concentrations persisted. These observational findings do not justify discontinuing clinically indicated vitamin B12 therapy.
