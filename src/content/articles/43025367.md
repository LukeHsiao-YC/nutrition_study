### 重點摘要
動機式晤談 (MI) 雖支持行為改變，但其技能與長者跌倒預防回應（改變談話或維持談話）的關聯性研究仍不足。本研究對10位長者共72次MI會談紀錄進行二次分析，利用修正的MI技能編碼工具與廣義序列分析，評估MI技能引發改變談話或維持談話的機率。結果顯示，複雜性反思最可能引發改變談話，而積極措辭的反思、問題和表達希望，則能使改變談話的可能性幾乎倍增。結論指出，特別是積極的複雜性反思和希望語句，能有效增加長者的改變談話，這對支持長者跌倒預防工作至關重要。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了MI技能如何影響長者跌倒預防會談中改變談話與維持談話反應的知識空白。未來的研究應繼續探討不同年齡層的改變談話機制，並評估MISC+MITI工具作為忠實度評估的適用性，同時認識到長者對MI的反應可能與其他年齡組不同。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Mechanism of motivational interviewing (MI): Generalized sequential analysis of fall prevention MI sessions among older adults.
**Abstract:** Motivational interviewing (MI) supports behavior change, but links between MI skills and older adults' fall prevention responses are understudied. This study addressed this gap and examined how MI skills relate to change versus sustain talk. We conducted secondary analysis of 72 transcribed MI sessions from 10 purposively selected older adults to conduct a secondary analysis. Transcripts were qualitatively coded using modified Motivational Interviewing Skills Coding 2.5 and Motivational Interviewing Therapeutic Integrity 2.4.1 (MISC+MITI) and fall prevention behavior codes. Inter-rater reliability was assessed. Generalized Sequential Analysis was used to estimate the odds of MI skills eliciting change or sustain talk. Multiple models of individual and aggregated MI skills were tested. In the analysis of the 72 transcripts, we found practitioners' complex reflections were most likely to result in change talk (OR = 1.49, 95% CI: 1.32 to 1.67). Questions (OR = 1.25, 95% CI: 1.04 to 1.49) and simple reflections (OR = 1.29, 95% CI: 1.07 to 1.55) increased sustain talk. Positively worded reflections and questions almost doubled the likelihood of change talk: positive complex reflections (OR = 2.07, 95% CI: 1.80 to 2.38), positive simple reflections (OR = 2.02, 95% CI: 1.66 to 2.46), and positive questions (OR = 1.91, 95% CI: 1.59 to 2.30). Hope also increased change talk (OR = 1.97, 95% CI: 1.47 to 2.64). Complex reflections (especially positive) and hope-increased the likelihood of change talk. Affirmations and questions had mixed effects. Future work should continue to examine change talk in diverse age groups and assess MISC+MITI as a fidelity tool. Fall prevention is a complex set of behaviors, though preventable. Older adult responses to MI may be different than other age groups. Understanding language that increases change talk supports fall prevention work.
