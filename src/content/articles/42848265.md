### 重點摘要
DigiHero是德國一項全國性、基於人群的數位隊列研究，最初為快速收集COVID-19相關數據而設立，並旨在成為研究慢性疾病、健康行為和健康老齡化的多功能平台。該研究透過郵寄邀請招募了超過12萬戶家庭的參與者，所有互動均採線上方式，每年進行四次定期聯繫，並評估多種暴露因子，包括問卷、可穿戴設備數據及未來可能的生物樣本。DigiHero利用數位化方法，能迅速且經濟高效地應對新興健康議題，生成相關健康數據，並透過完全虛擬的參與模式補充了德國現有的中心型隊列研究，同時反映區域異質性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了德國在快速、成本效益高且具有全國代表性的數位健康數據收集方面的空白。透過其完全虛擬的參與模式，DigiHero補充了傳統上依賴實體研究中心或地區性的小型隊列研究，使其能夠更迅速地應對新興健康議題，並捕捉到更廣泛的地理和人口異質性數據。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Prospective cohort study for digital health research in Germany (DigiHero): objectives, design and baseline characteristics.
**Abstract:** DigiHero is a population based, nationwide digital cohort study with participants from more than 120 000 households from all federal states in Germany. It was set up during the COVID-19 pandemic to contribute to the rapid collection of COVID-19 related data but already at its set-up, it was intended to be a research platform to study (chronic) diseases, health behaviour, and healthy ageing. Later on, it became part of the German Center for Mental Health and contributes to its research agenda. Participants were recruited via postal invitations with address information obtained from administrative registries; the participation is fully online, with four regular contacts per year. A wide array of exposures is being assessed, with particular focus on change over time. In addition to regularly repeated modules, the flexibility of the study design allows to implement further questionnaires responding to newly emerging health topics. Self-collected bio samples or examinations in study centers or even in medical practices can be included in subprojects. Data from wearables, sensors, and linked health insurance data will enrich the responses to questionnaires. With focus on new digital approaches, DigiHero can react directly and quickly to current health issues, generating health-related data promptly and cost-effectively. Given its fully virtual participation, DigiHero supplements local or study-center associated cohorts in Germany. It is intended to reflect regional heterogeneity with inclusion of rural regions across Germany.
