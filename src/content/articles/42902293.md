### 重點摘要
這項研究探討奈洛比非正式住區幼兒中心的水、環境衛生與個人衛生 (WASH) 政策環境。研究採用雙重方法，系統性回顧25份政策文件並訪談40位政策制定者、家長及幼兒照護提供者，以分析政策內容、背景、參與者與流程。結果顯示WASH政策體系存在系統性碎片化，衛生準則代表性不足，且各部會職權重疊導致實施成效不彰，最終造成不理想的WASH結果。研究結論指出，現行政策框架與實際情況脫節，缺乏針對非正式住區和五歲以下兒童的有效策略，亟需建立一個具備凝聚力、政治支持與監測機制的治理模式。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究揭示了奈洛比非正式住區幼兒中心WASH政策體系中普遍存在的碎片化和政策與實踐之間的脫節。它填補了政策層面如何影響這些弱勢群體WASH服務的研究空白，特別指出現有政策缺乏針對非正式住區和幼兒的具體策略。未來的研究和介入應著重於政策流程的「如何」層面，並建立一個更具凝聚力、政治支持和監測機制的治理架構，以實現具體的社區影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** WASH policy landscape: exploring context, process, content, and actors in childcare centres in Nairobi's informal settlements.
**Abstract:** Adequate water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) services are fundamental to health and development. However, many underserved areas such as informal settlements lack these services, creating significant infrastructural and public health challenges. Inadequate WASH infrastructure in Nairobi's informal settlements reveal significant gaps in childcare centre guidelines. Integrating robust guidelines and quality WASH services into childcare centres optimize operations and directly improves children's wellbeing. This study explores the policy environment governing WASH service delivery in these settings. We analysed the policy landscape's content, context, actors, and processes. This study employed a dual-methodological approach within Nairobi's informal settlements. First, we systematically reviewed 25 policy documents to analyse the content, context, actors, and processes governing WASH guidelines, utilizing Walt and Gilson's Policy Triangle Framework. Second, we conducted 32 in-depth interviews (IDIs) and 8 key informant interviews (KIIs) with policymakers, parents, and childcare providers. These interviews used the same framework to identify policy gaps and practical implementation barriers. Results revealed a systemic fragmentation within the WASH policy landscape. While water (88%) and sanitation (80%) guidelines appeared frequently, hygiene guidelines were significantly less represented (68%). Overlapping mandates among the Ministries of Health, Education, and Water and Sanitation hindered effective implementation of policy guidelines. Gaps in the policy process and fragmented inter-sectoral coordination weakened the overall governance of WASH services and practices. This lack of institutional synergy ultimately resulted in suboptimal and precarious WASH outcomes within informal settlements. Current WASH policy frameworks are misaligned with the realities of informal urban settlements. Existing documents have inadequate strategies for these environments and for children under five. This contextual disconnect, coupled with the barriers of persistent poverty, means that service delivery often remains stalled or unsustainable. Bridging the gap between policy and practice requires moving beyond the policy content to the "how" of the policy process. Relevant actors in the sector should move towards a governance model that prioritizes a cohesive, politically supported, and monitored architecture for tangible community impact.
