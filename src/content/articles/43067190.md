### 重點摘要
本研究旨在開發並評估一種整合健康識能最佳實務的實驗室結果清晰溝通 (CCLR) 模板的可行性、可接受性與使用性，以提升患者知識、理解後續照護需求信心並降低焦慮。研究採用準實驗設計，招募了58名老年初級照護患者，比較標準結果通知與CCLR模板。結果顯示，CCLR模板在患者和提供者之間具有可行性和可接受性，收到CCLR通知的患者對溝通均感到滿意，但提供者偶爾會忘記使用。儘管患者對模板內容持開放態度並認為有價值，提供者也欣賞其用意和易用性，但模板需進一步精煉以適用於實際應用，且建議進行更大規模的研究來評估其效力。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項可行性研究證實了將健康識能最佳實務應用於實驗室結果溝通模板的潛力，為提升患者理解與滿意度提供了初步證據。然而，研究的限制在於樣本量較小、非隨機設計，且未能充分評估CCLR模板在實際臨床情境中提升患者對結果意義、後續追蹤需求理解及焦慮程度的「效力」，同時模板的通用性和客製化能力仍需改進才能廣泛實施。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Embedding health literacy best practices: A feasibility study using Clear Communication of Laboratory Test Results templates.
**Abstract:** To create and test the feasibility, acceptability, and use of a Clear Communication of Lab Results (CCLR) note template to increase participant knowledge, confidence in understanding need for follow-up, and decrease anxiety regarding the results. Template design integrated health literacy best practices and used cognitive interviewing with providers and patients within a geriatric primary care clinic for refinement before use. A non-randomized quasi-experimental feasibility design with two arms; one arm received standard result note and one arm received CCLR templated note. Demographics and health literacy were assessed at baseline. Participants were contacted by phone 7-14 days after blood tests results were sent to assess anxiety, satisfaction, understanding, and confidence in regard to the content of the result note. Mean age of the 58 participants was 79.6 years, and they were highly educated with 59% (n = 33) reporting a college degree and 50% demonstrating adequate health literacy. Providers did not always remember to use the CCLR result note template. All participants who received the CCLR note were satisfied with the communication. EHR-based templates to communicate laboratory results is feasible and acceptable to patients and providers. A larger, powered study is warranted to assess the efficacy of the CCLR note in facilitating patient understanding of meaning of results, need for follow-up, satisfaction with communication from provider and decreased anxiety over results. Patient participants were receptive to the notes and valued the content provided. Provider participants appreciated the intent and ease of use but would like consideration of a more generic template that can easily be customized for multiple test results and recurrent tests results provided to the same patient. Further refinement is necessary before the CCLR templates are ready for use in practice. ClinicalTrials.gov Identifier: NCT06971887 retrospectively registered 5/13/2025.
