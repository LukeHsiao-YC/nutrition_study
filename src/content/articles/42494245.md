### 重點摘要
美國的國際研究生面臨嚴重的糧食不安全問題，盛行率高達22%至46%，遠超全國平均，此為一被忽視的營養公平挑戰。主要原因包括財務困難、就業限制及難以取得文化適宜食物，導致他們轉向攝取高能量、低營養的食物，進而增加胰島素阻抗、高血壓及心血管代謝疾病的風險。儘管實證基礎日益增長，但針對此族群飲食品質與健康結果的長期追蹤數據仍極為缺乏，且大學的營養支持系統亦未能充分適應多元文化需求。文章呼籲營養研究人員、營養師及大學應正視此公共衛生議題，並採取有針對性的研究及文化回應的機構行動。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇評論指出，儘管國際研究生糧食不安全問題日益受到關注，但目前缺乏關於此族群飲食品質及健康結果的縱向研究證據。同時，大學現行的營養支持系統也未能有效滿足國際學生多樣的文化飲食需求，這顯示了未來在學術研究與校園支援策略上的顯著不足。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Invisible and Undernourished: Food Insecurity Among International Graduate Students in the United States as an Overlooked Nutrition Equity Challenge.
**Abstract:** Food insecurity among international graduate students in the United States is a nutrition equity challenge that remains largely unaddressed by the nutrition science community. Prevalence estimates range from 22% to 46% across institutions, substantially exceeding the national household average, driven by converging financial constraints, restricted employment opportunities, and limited access to culturally appropriate foods. These conditions promote rapid dietary acculturation toward energy-dense, low-nutrient food patterns with well-established associations with insulin resistance, hypertension, and cardiometabolic risk. Despite a growing empirical base, longitudinal evidence on dietary quality and health outcomes in this population is virtually absent, and university nutrition support systems are poorly adapted to the cultural dietary needs of an internationally diverse student body. This commentary calls on nutrition researchers, dietitians, and universities to recognize food insecurity among international graduate students as a substantive public health nutrition concern warranting targeted research and culturally responsive institutional action.
