### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估非侵入性標記APRi、iBALF以及組織學Ishak纖維化分數，對於膽道閉鎖兒童在葛西手術後之原肝存活率（NLS）和門脈高壓（PH）的預測價值。這是一項單中心、回溯性觀察研究，分析了接受葛西手術的膽道閉鎖兒童，並使用出院時的實驗室數據計算APRi和iBALF，同時評估術中肝活檢樣本的Ishak纖維化分數。結果顯示，較高的APRi、iBALF和Ishak分數與較低的原肝存活率顯著相關，且APRi和iBALF也是門脈高壓的良好預測因子。這些發現證實了這些生物標記在預測膽道閉鎖兒童術後原肝存活率的潛在價值，並支持將其應用於臨床追蹤和風險分層。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究證實了APRi和iBALF等非侵入性生物標記在膽道閉鎖兒童葛西手術後，預測原肝存活率和門脈高壓的潛在價值，填補了臨床上對可靠且低侵入性預後工具的需求，有助於早期風險分層與臨床管理。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Biomarkers of native liver survival and portal hypertension in biliary atresia.
**Abstract:** To evaluate the prognostic value of noninvasive markers Aspartate Aminotransferase to Platelet Ratio Index (APRi) and Infantile Biliary Atresia Liver Fibrosis score (iBALF), as well as the histological Ishak fibrosis score, as predictors of native liver survival (NLS) and portal hypertension (PH) in children with biliary atresia BA undergoing Kasai portoenterostomy (KPE). A single-center, retrospective observational study was conducted on children diagnosed with BA who underwent KPE. APRi and iBALF were calculated using laboratory data collected at hospital discharge after KPE. The degree of hepatic fibrosis was assessed and reviewed using the Ishak fibrosis score in liver biopsy specimens collected at KPE. NLS was analyzed using Kaplan-Meier survival curves and Cox proportional hazards regression. A p-value < 0.05 was considered statistically significant. Higher APRi, iBALF, and Ishak fibrosis score values were associated with lower NLS (p < 0.006, p < 0.025, and p < 0.048, respectively). APRi and iBALF emerged as good PH predictors (p < 0.005 and p < 0.032, respectively). APRi, iBALF, and the Ishak fibrosis score showed prognostic value in predicting NLS in children with BA after KPE. These findings reinforce the potential use of biomarkers in the clinical follow-up of these patients, supporting risk stratification strategies.
