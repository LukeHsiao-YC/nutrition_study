### 重點摘要
本研究旨在開發並評估一個衡量克服在家準備健康餐點障礙的自我效能的量表。研究團隊利用橫斷面數據，針對169名超重或一級/二級肥胖的成年人，進行驗證性因素分析，比較單一和雙因子模型。結果顯示雙因子模型（包括情境障礙和資源限制）的擬合度優於單一因子模型，但其心理計量特性介於邊緣至可接受之間。儘管情境障礙部分的信度尚可，資源限制部分較低，此量表在此初步分析中顯示出邊緣至足夠的心理計量學特性，但仍需在更大、更多樣化的樣本中進行驗證。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究開發了一個衡量克服在家準備健康餐點障礙自我效能的量表，填補了該領域缺乏專用評估工具的空白。然而，由於研究樣本主要為女性、受過良好教育且收入較高，其心理計量學特性僅介於邊緣至足夠，未來需在更大、更多樣化的樣本中進行驗證。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Development and Psychometric Evaluation of the Self-Efficacy for Cooking Healthy Meals Scale in the Teaching Kitchen Multisite Trial (TK-MT).
**Abstract:** To develop and evaluate a scale measuring self-efficacy for overcoming barriers to preparing healthy meals at home. Cross-sectional data were analyzed using confirmatory factor analysis comparing 1 and 2-factor structures. Teaching Kitchen Multisite Trial. Adults with overweight or class I-II obesity (n = 169; mean age, 46 years; 79% female; 38% Hispanic, 10% Black, 23% Asian). Self-Efficacy for Cooking Healthy Meals, addressing contextual barriers and resource constraints. Confirmatory factor analysis was performed using maximum likelihood estimation with robust errors; fit was evaluated with multiple indexes; internal consistency was assessed with Cronbach α. The 2-factor model fit better than the 1-factor model; the indexes were borderline (SBχ²[43] = 82.9, root mean squared error of approximation = 0.074, Comparative Fit Index = 0.91, Tucker-Lewis Index = 0.88). Reliability was acceptable for contextual barriers (α = 0.79) but lower for resource constraints (α = 0.68). In this preliminary analysis with a predominantly female, educated, higher-income sample, the scale showed borderline-to-adequate psychometric properties; validation in larger, more diverse samples is needed.
