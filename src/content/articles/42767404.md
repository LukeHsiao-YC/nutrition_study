### 重點摘要
本研究旨在建立一套將血基質鐵與非血基質鐵數值納入食物營養資料庫的方法，因其在生物利用度和健康影響上有所不同。該多步驟方法包括文獻審查、依據來源及插補法為核心食物賦值、程式計算非核心食物，並進行品質審查。結果顯示，2025年版資料庫已為所有食物賦予完整的鐵質數值，其中蛋、乳製品和植物性食物的鐵質全為非血基質鐵，而肉類和海鮮的血基質鐵則根據比例計算。此方法成功填補了資料庫的完整性，儘管部分肉類和海鮮數據需透過插補法處理。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了食物營養資料庫中缺乏血基質鐵與非血基質鐵完整數據的空白，為更精確的飲食鐵質評估及相關健康影響研究奠定基礎。然而，由於部分肉類和海鮮的核心食物數據仍需透過插補法估算，這表示在這些特定食物類別中，直接分析數據的不足是未來的研究限制，可能影響數據的精確性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Methodology for Adding Heme Iron and Non-Heme Iron to a Food and Nutrient Database and Dietary Analysis Software.
**Abstract:** Including both heme and non-heme forms of iron in food and nutrient databases is important because they differ in bioavailability and potentially have differential health impacts. The study describes the methodology used for assigning heme and non-heme iron values to foods in the University of Minnesota Nutrition Coordinating Center (NCC) Food and Nutrient Database. /Methods: The NCC Database includes about 19,500 foods, and heme and non-heme iron values (mg/100g) were assigned to foods using a multi-step process: (1) Reviewed scientific literature and international food composition databases to gather analytical data; (2) Assigned values to the core foods based on the above sources and imputation rules; (3) For non-core foods, values were assigned using the NCC Food Calculation program; and (4) As a quality assurance measure, all assigned values were systematically reviewed for consistency across NCC food groups. In the 2025 version of the NCC Database, heme and non-heme iron values were assigned to all foods, with no missing values. Core foods with zero total iron were assigned 0 mg for both forms of iron (14% of core foods). For eggs, dairy, and plant foods with iron content, 100% of the iron was assigned as non-heme (68% of core foods). For meats and seafoods, heme iron was calculated based on literature heme-to-total iron ratios, and non-heme iron was determined by subtraction of heme iron from total iron (18% of core foods). Imputation was required for 24% of the meat and seafood core foods. The methodology used to add heme and non-heme iron to the NCC Database resulted in complete data, though imputation was required for some meats and seafoods.
