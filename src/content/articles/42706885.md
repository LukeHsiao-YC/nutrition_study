### 重點摘要
該研究旨在探討美國慢性病成人中，以K-means聚類分析所識別的進食時間模式是否與死亡風險相關，以填補此領域的知識空白。研究分析了來自NHANES 2005-2016的26,712名成人數據，基於首次進食時間和進食時窗持續時間，識別出早開始、中間和晚開始三種進食模式。在平均224,837.9人年的追蹤期間，共發生3279例死亡。然而，研究結果顯示，這三種進食時間模式與六種慢性病領域的全因死亡率之間，並未達到預設的統計顯著性閾值。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了關於不同進食時間配置與慢性病成人死亡率關聯不確定性的研究空白，指出在此研究方法下，並未觀察到顯著的總體關聯。這意味著在目前的定義下，進食時間模式對慢性病患者的全因死亡率影響可能不明顯。未來的研究可能需要探索更細緻的進食模式定義、特定疾病群體的潛在關聯或特定的死亡原因，以進一步釐清此潛在關係。

**Title:** Eating-timing patterns derived from K-means clustering and mortality risk in US adults with chronic diseases: a cohort study.
**Abstract:** Whether joint eating-timing configurations show comparable mortality associations across chronic diseases is uncertain. We analysed 26 712 adults aged 20 years or older from the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) 2005-2016 who had two reliable 24-h dietary recalls and mortality linkage. Participant-level mean first eating time and eating-window duration were standardised and classified by K-means. Survey-weighted Cox models incorporated strata and primary sampling units within six chronic disease domains; missing covariates were multiply imputed in thirty datasets and prespecified Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery-rate families addressed multiplicity. Three patterns represented 30·2 % (early-start), 57·2 % (intermediate) and 12·6 % (late-start) of the weighted population. During 224 837·9 person-years, 3279 deaths occurred.
