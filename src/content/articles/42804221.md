### 重點摘要
腦性麻痺 (CP) 的遺傳病因日益受到認可，但其診斷率因臨床亞組和表型而異，缺乏實證依據來優先排序基因檢測。本研究為一項系統性回顧與統合分析，旨在評估基因檢測在腦性麻痺中的總體診斷率，並找出影響診斷率的臨床和表型預測因子。結果顯示，非選擇性腦性麻痺患者的整體診斷率為19%，但在隱源性腦性麻痺患者中高達44%，若結合次世代定序與染色體微陣列分析更高達53%；具有正常腦部MRI、面部畸形、先天性異常和神經系統非典型特徵的患者，診斷率也顯著較高。研究結論指出，基因檢測在腦性麻痺，尤其是在隱源性病例及特定臨床表型患者中，具有高診斷率，建議將基因組評估整合至腦性麻痺的標準診斷流程，以實現及時診斷和個人化管理。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了過去對於腦性麻痺 (CP) 患者進行基因檢測時，其診斷率在不同臨床亞組和表型之間不一致，以及缺乏具實證基礎的優先順序指引的研究空白。研究明確了基因檢測的總體診斷率，並識別出高診斷率的特定臨床和表型預測因子，從而為基因組評估整合至標準診斷流程提供了實證依據。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Diagnostic Yield of Genetic Testing in Cerebral Palsy: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
**Abstract:** Genetic etiologies are increasingly recognized in cerebral palsy (CP), yet diagnostic yields across different clinical subgroups and phenotypes remain inconsistent, limiting evidence-based prioritization of genetic testing. To determine the pooled diagnostic yield of genetic testing in CP and identify clinical and phenotypic predictors that influence diagnostic yields. A systematic search of PubMed, Embase, and the Cochrane Library was conducted for studies published between January 2010 and August 25, 2025. Peer-reviewed observational studies reporting genetic testing results in cohorts of at least 10 patients with CP. Data were extracted independently by 2 reviewers following the Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses (PRISMA) 2020 guidelines. A random-effects model with logit transformation was used to calculate pooled diagnostic yields with 95% CIs. Heterogeneity was evaluated using the I2 statistic and Cochran Q test. The primary outcome was the pooled diagnostic yield of genetic testing stratified by CP etiology (cryptogenic, noncryptogenic, and mixed/unselected cohorts). Secondary outcomes included diagnostic yields across phenotype-based subgroups defined by brain magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) findings, motor types, comorbidities, facial dysmorphism, congenital anomalies, and clinical trajectory. A total of 31 studies met inclusion criteria, of which 20 were included in the quantitative meta-analysis. The overall pooled diagnostic yield in unselected CP cohorts was 0.19 (95% CI, 0.11-0.32; I2 = 95.1%). In patients with cryptogenic CP, the yield was 0.44 (95% CI, 0.37-0.50; I2 = 72.2%), compared with 0.13 (95% CI, 0.07-0.21; I2 = 85.1%) in noncryptogenic cases. Higher yields were observed in patients with normal brain MRI findings (25%-80%), facial dysmorphism (66%-71%), congenital anomalies (85%), and neurological atypical features (50%). Combined next-generation sequencing and chromosomal microarray analysis provided the highest pooled yield of 0.53 (95% CI, 0.43-0.62) in the cryptogenic subgroup. In this systematic review and meta-analysis, genetic testing demonstrated a high diagnostic yield in CP, particularly in cryptogenic cases and in patients with specific clinical phenotypes. These findings suggest the integration of genomic evaluation into the standard diagnostic workup for CP, guided by clinical indicators, to facilitate timely diagnosis and personalized management.
