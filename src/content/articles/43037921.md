### 重點摘要
這項研究澄清了分類值（敏感度、特異度）與預測值（陽性、陰性預測值）之間的關係，並探討了其在定量偏差分析中處理錯誤分類的影響。研究發現，即使存在無差別錯誤分類（各暴露組的敏感度和特異度相同），預測值仍可能因結果的真實盛行率差異而在不同暴露分層之間有所不同。使用單一、未分層的預測值會產生偏差的調整估計，甚至錯誤地暗示差別錯誤分類。因此，為獲得無偏差的估計，研究建議在進行定量偏差分析時，應至少在各暴露組內估計並應用分層的預測值。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了定量偏差分析中，因忽略預測值在不同暴露組間可能存在差異（即使錯誤分類為無差別）而導致估計偏差的知識空白。然而，獲取和使用預測值的實際挑戰、常見驗證研究設計的局限性、預測值對結果盛行率的依賴性，以及跨設定傳輸估計值的困難，仍是未來研究和應用需要克服的限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Non differential misclassification does not mean equal predictive values.
**Abstract:** Quantitative bias analysis requires specification of bias parameters, yet important nuances in how predictive values are used are often overlooked. We clarify the relationship between classification values (sensitivity and specificity) and predictive values (positive and negative predictive values) and highlight implications for addressing misclassification. Although non-differential misclassification is defined by equality of sensitivity and specificity across exposure groups, predictive values depend not only on these parameters but also on the underlying true prevalence of the outcome. As a result, predictive values can differ across exposure strata even when misclassification is non-differential. We demonstrate that this has important consequences for quantitative bias analysis. Using a worked example, we show that applying a single, unstratified predictive value generates biased adjusted estimates and even imply differential misclassification when none exists. In contrast, correctly stratifying predictive values by exposure leads to bias analysis that produces unbiased estimates. We also discuss practical challenges in obtaining and using predictive values, including limitations of common validation study designs, dependence on outcome prevalence, and difficulties in transporting estimates across settings. Our findings show that predictive values should, at a minimum, be estimated and applied within exposure strata when conducting quantitative bias analysis.
