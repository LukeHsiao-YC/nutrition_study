### 重點摘要
現有失智症模擬模型未能準確評估類澱粉蛋白標靶療法 (ATTs) 對認知與功能衰退的綜合影響，因其未考慮兩者獨立但相關的軌跡。本研究開發了一款阿茲海默症微觀模擬模型，利用類澱粉蛋白陽性記憶門診患者數據，將認知衰退速度轉化為對功能獨立階段的影響。該模型成功預測了不同階段的病程時間，並顯示當ATTs使MMSE下降速度減緩30%時，輕度認知障礙與輕度失智症的持續時間分別延長0.4和1.0年，機構化時間延遲1.1年。此模型能將ATTs對認知的影響轉化為功能獨立性的影響，可用於評估新療法的成本效益。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了現有失智症模擬模型未能準確評估類澱粉蛋白標靶療法對認知與功能衰退的綜合影響的空白，特別是其無法同時考量認知與功能衰退之間獨立但相關的軌跡，限制了評估藥物對這兩領域效益的準確性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Developing a microsimulation model of Alzheimer's disease for the evaluation of disease modifying therapies.
**Abstract:** Clinical trials of amyloid-targeting therapies (ATTs) for Alzheimer's Disease (AD) showed slowing of decline in continuous cognitive and functional outcomes over eighteen months of follow-up. Existing simulation models used to extrapolate these results do not account for the distinct but correlated trajectories of cognitive and functional decline, limiting their accuracy in evaluating ATT effects on both domains. We developed an AD microsimulation model using an amyloid-positive memory clinic population that can translate slowed cognitive decline into effects on clinical functional independence stages. The model contained stages of mild cognitive impairment (MCI), community dementia, institutionalisation, and death. The transitions from MCI to dementia and from dementia to institutionalisation or death were related to Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) measurements through statistical joint models. Model validation showed that observed and predicted cumulative incidence curves overlapped for all stage transitions, indicating an accurate description of time in each stage. An untreated cohort starting with MCI had a mean duration of MCI, mild, moderate and severe community dementia, and institutionalisation of 3.3, 2.3, 1.6, 1.1, and 1.7 years, respectively, totalling 10.0 years. When ATTs slowed MMSE decline by 30% during MCI and mild dementia (community dementia with an MMSE above 20), the duration of MCI and mild dementia was prolonged by 0.4 and 1.0 years, respectively, and institutionalisation was delayed by 1.1 year. The time institutionalised remained similar. Our AD microsimulation model can translate ATT effects on cognition into effects on functional independence and can be used to assess the cost-effectiveness of new therapies.
