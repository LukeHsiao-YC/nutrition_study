### 重點摘要
這項事後分析探討工作場所心理治療諮詢 (PT-A) 是否能改善患有常見精神疾病 (CMD) 員工所感知的工作條件，包括降低工作要求和提升工作資源。研究從一項德國多中心隨機對照試驗中擷取數據，比較接受PT-A的介入組與接受常規照護的對照組，並於24個月後評估感知工作條件。結果顯示，PT-A與較低的感知情緒需求和工作與生活界線模糊度相關，但經多重比較校正後，其統計顯著性並未維持，且在感知量化需求和工作資源方面未見組間差異。因此，本研究初步表明PT-A可能與長期降低感知情緒需求和界線模糊度有關，但需進一步的確認性研究來驗證這些探索性發現。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究為探索性的事後分析，所評估的工作條件並非預設的主要試驗結果，且經多重比較校正後，主要發現未能維持統計顯著性。因此，需要進一步的確認性研究，以證實PT-A在降低感知情緒需求和工作與生活界線模糊度方面的長期潛在效益，並釐清其對工作資源的影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Changes in perceived psychosocial working conditions after psychotherapeutic consultation at work - exploratory post-hoc analyses from a randomized controlled trial.
**Abstract:** Psychotherapeutic consultation at work (PT-A) is a low-threshold intervention providing early work-focused treatment to employees with symptoms of common mental disorders (CMD). This post-hoc analysis explores whether PT-A, compared with routine care, is associated with perceived reductions in job demands (i.e., quantitative and emotional demands, dissolution between work and private life) and improvements in job resources (i.e., decision authority, development opportunities, social support). The exploratory post-hoc analysis used data from a multicenter randomized controlled trial in Germany. Five-hundred-fifty employees with a contract of ≥ 15 h/week and symptoms or a diagnosis of CMD were randomized 1:1 to the intervention or control group. After one participant withdrew consent, 279 participants remained in the intervention and 270 in the control group. Both groups received basic clinical assessment. Up to 16 work-focused PT-A sessions were offered to the intervention group over nine months, including work-related assessments, work-focused cognitive behavioral therapy and, if needed, support during return-to-work. Perceived job demands and resources were measured before randomization and 24 months later and were not pre-specified as trial outcomes in the study protocol and trial registration. Multiple linear regression analyses examined group differences in perceived working conditions at follow-up, controlling for covariates at baseline (i.e., age, gender, education, occupation, study centre, depressive symptoms, sickness absence days). Data from 231 participants were available for analysis (intervention: n = 137; control: n = 94; mean age: 46 years; 55% female). Participation in PT-A was associated with lower perceived emotional demands (b = 6.61, SE = 2.52, p = 0.009, adjusted p = .072) and dissolution (b = 5.05, SE = 2.52, p = 0.047, adjusted p = .164) at follow-up, but results did not remain statistically significant after Benjamini-Hochberg correction for multiple testing. No group differences were observed in perceived quantitative demands and job resources. Results may indicate that participation in PT-A is associated with long-term reductions in perceived emotional demands and dissolution, but not with improvement in perceived job resources. Confirmative research is needed given the exploratory study design and because findings did not remain statistically significant after adjustment for multiple testing. The study was registered on 1 March 2021 in the German Clinical Trials Register under the project-ID DRKS00023049 ( https://drks.de/search/de/trial/DRKS00023049 ).
