### 重點摘要
缺鐵性貧血是育齡婦女的公共衛生負擔，食物強化是潛在的解決方案。本研究透過建模模擬在印度對育齡婦女實施鐵強化米、麵粉和鹽的三種情境，並利用飲食調查和健康調查數據估算其對鐵攝取量、血紅素濃度及貧血盛行率的影響。結果預測，中度和最大強化情境可分別使每日鐵攝取量增加35%-53%和70%-106%，並將貧血盛行率從52.6%降至45-48%和36-43%。這表明綜合強化這些主食，能有效改善育齡婦女的鐵攝取量、血紅素濃度，進而降低貧血盛行率。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究透過建模量化了不同鐵強化情境對印度育齡婦女貧血狀況的潛在影響，為政策制定提供了具體數據依據。然而，這是一項基於模擬的研究，未來的研究應透過實際的介入試驗來驗證這些預測，並評估現實世界中執行率與依從性的影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Quantifying the Potential Impact of Food Fortification on Iron Intake, Hemoglobin Concentration and Anemia Prevalence Among Women of Reproductive Age in India.
**Abstract:** Anemia is a public health burden, particularly in women of reproductive age (WRA). Food fortification, by adding micronutrients to foods, may alleviate anemia caused by nutritional deficiencies. To model the impact of iron-fortified rice, wheat flour, and salt in India, we simulated three scenarios: no fortification, intermediate fortification (50% coverage for rice and wheat flour and 26.6% coverage for salt, with 100% industry compliance), and maximum fortification (100% coverage and compliance) in WRA. We first used a single-day, 24-h dietary recall survey from the National Nutrition Monitoring Bureau to simulate mean iron intake under these scenarios (n = 11,625). We then used data from the National Family Health Survey 4 (n = 667,258) to simulate hemoglobin changes due to increased iron intake, based on established parameters from a meta-regression analysis. Subsequently, we calculated anemia prevalence. Compared to the no-fortification scenario, the intermediate and maximum fortification scenarios were predicted to increase daily iron intake by 4.3-6.5 mg and 8.6-13.1 mg (35%-53% and 70%-106% increases), respectively, and decrease anemia prevalence from 52.6% to 45-48% and 36-43%, respectively. Our results suggest that combined fortification of rice, wheat flour, and salt with iron at varying levels of coverage and 100% compliance can improve iron intakes and hemoglobin concentration and thus reduce anemia prevalence among WRA in India.
