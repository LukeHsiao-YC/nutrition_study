### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估兒童肝臟移植(pLT)病患轉銜至成人照護後的長期結果，涵蓋病患及移植器官存活率、醫療遵從性與理想結果。這項前瞻性觀察研究納入60名病患，追蹤轉銜後長達15年。結果顯示，轉銜後15年病患存活率達93%，但移植器官存活率降至75%，且達成理想結果的比例從轉銜時的55%顯著降至10年後的25%，同時有20%的病患存在藥物不遵從性。研究結論指出，儘管pLT病患長期存活良好，但轉銜至成人照護後，他們面臨日益增加的挑戰，尤其是在醫療遵從性方面，強調持續且個人化的追蹤照護至關重要。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了兒童肝臟移植病患轉銜至成人照護後，其長期存活、移植器官功能、醫療遵從性及整體照護成效的描述性數據空白。它揭示了隨著時間推移，特別是藥物遵從性下降對長期預後的影響，並指出即便存活率高，轉銜後的照護挑戰仍需關注。然而，本研究為觀察性描述，並未深入探討導致遵從性下降的具體社會心理或系統性原因，也未提出或評估任何旨在改善遵從性或轉銜成效的介入措施，這些均可作為未來研究的方向與限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Long-term follow-up after transfer of care to adult healthcare in paediatric liver transplant recipients.
**Abstract:** Paediatric liver transplantation (pLT) is associated with excellent long-term survival and quality of life. As a growing proportion of recipients survive into adulthood, long-term follow-up after transfer from paediatric to adult care is increasingly important. We aimed to describe patient and graft survival, retention in care, adherence, medical complications and achievement of an ideal outcome after transfer of care. This prospective observational study included all pLT recipients who entered our centre's transition programme between January 2010 and December 2024. Prespecified outcomes included patient and graft survival, retention in adult care, attendance and medication adherence, a composite non-adherence outcome, post-transfer medical complications and the proportion achieving an ideal outcome at predefined timepoints. Sixty patients were included (53.3% male; median age at pLT 5 [2.1-10.4] years; median age at transfer of care 19.8 [interquartile range (IQR) 18.9-24] years). Patient survival at 1, 5, 10 and 15 years after transfer of care was 98%, 96%, 93% and 93%, while graft survival was 98%, 94%, 91% and 75%, respectively. At the time of transfer of care, 55% of patients fulfilled criteria for an ideal outcome; this decreased to 41.6% at 5 years and 25% at 10 years. Medication non-adherence was documented in 20%, and composite non-adherence in 25%. The tacrolimus Medication Level Variability Index was significantly associated with composite non-adherence (p = 0.007) and was higher in patients who developed chronic rejection than in those who did not (p = 0.05). Despite excellent long-term survival, pLT recipients reaching adulthood face increasing challenges over time, particularly regarding adherence. These descriptive findings support continued, individualised follow-up after transfer to adult healthcare.
