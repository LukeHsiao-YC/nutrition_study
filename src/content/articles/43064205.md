### 重點摘要
美國關於超加工食品 (UPF) 的法律與政策定義正迅速增加，但其與學術研究中透過專家判定的Nova分類存在顯著差異。本研究提出「建構保留」、「維度一致性」和「可評估性」三項測試，以評估法律定義能否有效轉化研究分類。將這些測試應用於現有的健康飲食研究專家小組建議與加州法律定義，發現健康飲食研究的定義未通過維度一致性測試，而加州的定義則界定了一個比研究更窄的類別。這項工作揭示了法律定義在反映與健康結果相關的飲食暴露方面所面臨的挑戰，並為評估未來政策提供了實用工具。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了將超加工食品的學術研究定義（依賴專家判斷的Nova分類）轉譯為法律或政策定義（基於成分列表且無專家判斷）之間的鴻溝。它提供了一套客觀的評估框架，以確保法律定義在實務應用時能忠實反映研究證據所關聯的健康影響，但未來的研究限制在於其目前尚未能評估聯邦級別的提案，且這些測試本身並未解決UPF定義本身的科學爭議。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** US legal definitions of ultra-processed food depart from the research construct in different ways.
**Abstract:** Definitions of ultra-processed food (UPF) are multiplying in US law and policy. California enacted a statutory definition in 2025, two Senate bills introduced in 2026 propose federal labeling triggers, and in August 2026 a white paper proposing a federal definition was submitted for interagency review. The white paper is a non-binding notice, and its text has not been released. It is nonetheless likely to become a reference point for states, retailers and researchers. Most evidence on UPF and health comes from studies in which trained coders assigned foods to Nova group 4 and exposure was expressed as the dietary share of those foods. A legal definition must instead classify individual products from ingredient lists, without case-by-case expert judgment. This Perspective proposes three tests for that translation. Construct preservation asks whether the legal rule selects the same products as the research classification. Dimensional consistency asks whether products leave the regulated category on the same basis on which they enter it. Evaluability asks whether changes in regulatory status can be read as changes in the exposure that the evidence links to health outcomes. The tests are applied to the two definitions that are fully specified in the public record. The operational definition recommended by the Healthy Eating Research expert panel appears to track Nova at the level of the food supply but lacks product-level validation, and its nutrient-based exemption fails the dimensional consistency test. California's definition satisfies dimensional consistency by design but defines a narrower category than the one examined in most UPF research. The tests do not presuppose any position on Nova and can be applied to the federal text once it is released.
