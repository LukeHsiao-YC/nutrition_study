### 重點摘要
本研究旨在驗證一個針對美國拉丁裔/西班牙裔成人設計的西班牙語飲食篩檢工具SABE（¿SABE Lo Que Come?）的內容效度，以填補心血管疾病預防工具的空白。研究採用橫斷面、混合方法，對25名拉丁裔/西班牙裔成人進行線上調查，評估35個飲食題項的清晰度和相關性。結果顯示，經過文化適應和修訂後，所有題項的內容效度指數均達標，SABE最終量表的清晰度與相關性內容效度指數分別為0.865和0.969。因此，SABE被證實是一個簡短、具文化相關性且適合臨床與研究使用的飲食篩檢工具，能有效捕捉文化特定飲食模式。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了現有飲食篩檢工具未能充分捕捉美國多元化拉丁裔/西班牙裔社群文化特定食物模式的空白，解決了心血管疾病預防中缺乏經驗證之西班牙語飲食評估工具的關鍵問題。文章未提及未來的研究限制，但此研究主要著重於內容效度。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Validation of a Novel Dietary Screener for Clinical Practice: "¿SABE Lo Que Come?" A Screener Assessment for Better Eating (SABE) in Hispanic/Latino Adults.
**Abstract:** The development and use of brief dietary screeners is recommended for cardiovascular disease (CVD) prevention, yet validated Spanish-language tools for diverse U.S. Hispanic/Latino communities are lacking. Existing tools inadequately capture cultural foods, potentially introducing measurement bias. This study evaluated the content validity of ¿SABE Lo Que Come? (SABE), a culturally adapted Spanish-language dietary screener derived from the Penn Healthy Diet screening instrument. A cross-sectional, mixed-methods study was employed to examine content validity of the SABE screener. Bilingual Hispanic/Latino adults living within the contiguous United States were recruited through convenience and snowball sampling between October and December 2025. Of 40 adults who responded to initial study invitation and met eligibility criteria, 25 (mean age 32 years, SD = 15.0, 56% women) completed the online survey. Participant ratings of 35 screener items on 4-point Likert scales for clarity and relevance. Item-level content validity indices (I-CVI) and modified kappa (κ*) scores were calculated. Items with a I-CVI or κ* less than 0.70 underwent revision and retesting. Four items required revision based on quantitative thresholds; 182 participant comments informed culturally appropriate modifications. Major revisions addressed cultural relevance, including replacing unfamiliar terms and incorporating culturally relevant examples. After revisions, all retested items achieved I-CVI and κ* > 0.70. The final SABE questionnaire demonstrated Scale-CVI of 0.865 for clarity and 0.969 for relevance. SABE is a brief and culturally relevant dietary screening tool with content validity for diverse US Hispanic/Latino adults. This instrument addresses a critical gap in CVD prevention by providing a brief Spanish-language dietary assessment tool that captures culturally specific dietary patterns that is feasible for use in clinical and research settings.
