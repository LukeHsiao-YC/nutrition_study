### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討亞利桑那州美國原住民中與BMI相關的遺傳變異與脂肪細胞代謝之間的機制聯繫。研究發現，三個與高BMI風險相關的遺傳變異位點位於CYP26B1基因，其編碼參與視黃酸代謝的酶。體外實驗證實，這些風險變異會降低CYP26B1 TV3啟動子活性，影響脂肪細胞中視黃酸穩態，並導致相關基因表現改變。這項研究為CYP26B1變異如何影響視黃酸訊號傳導與脂肪細胞代謝，進而導致BMI升高提供了潛在的機制解釋。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了先前在美國原住民中，CYP26B1基因變異與BMI關聯性的分子機制空白，揭示了其可能透過影響視黃酸代謝及脂肪細胞功能來促進肥胖。然而，此研究主要基於體外及小鼠模型，未來需進一步的人體內研究以驗證這些機制在真實人體中的作用。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Functional Characterization of CYP26B1 Short Isoform as a Novel Locus for Obesity in Indigenous Americans From Arizona.
**Abstract:** To identify heritable factors that affect BMI among Indigenous Americans, we previously conducted a genome-wide association study using 7.9 million imputed genetic variants from 6789 participants. This study sought to identify a mechanistic link between variants with the strongest association with BMI and adipocyte metabolism. Variants with the strongest association with BMI (rs114857151, rs187498396, and rs189879565; r These variants map to CYP26B1, which encodes an enzyme involved in retinoic acid (RA) metabolism. In vitro studies showed that the haplotype with the three risk (higher BMI) variants had a 23% decrease in CYP26B1 transcript variant 3 (TV3) promoter activity. RA response element reporter assays showed that the Cyp26b1 TV3 isoform might be important for RA homeostasis in adipocytes. Cyp26b1 TV3 knockdown in mouse OP9 adipocytes resulted in increased Aldh1a1 expression. Alleles carrying these three variants may have reduced expression of CYP26B1 TV3 which in turn influences RA signaling and adipocyte metabolism, providing a possible mechanistic link between CYP26B1 variation and BMI.
