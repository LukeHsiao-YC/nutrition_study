### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討韓國青少年營養標示使用與父母營養標示使用之間的關聯性，並分析其是否因父母使用模式、年齡組別和資料收集期間而異。研究利用韓國國民健康與營養調查2016-2024年的橫斷面次級數據，分析了2,879名12-18歲青少年的資料，採用加權邏輯迴歸進行統計分析。結果顯示，父母使用營養標示與青少年使用營養標示顯著相關（aOR: 1.31），其中父母雙方皆使用時關聯最強（aOR: 1.84），且在12-15歲青少年中關聯性較為顯著。研究證實父母的營養標示使用習慣會影響青少年，並建議未來營養教育應納入家庭參與模式以提升青少年營養標示的使用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了韓國青少年營養標示使用受父母影響的全國代表性數據空白，並首次探討了不同父母使用模式和青少年年齡組別的差異。然而，其橫斷面設計無法建立因果關係，未來的研究可進一步探討家庭介入型營養教育的成效。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Parental Nutrition Label Use and Adolescent Nutrition Label Use in the Republic of Korea.
**Abstract:** To examine the association between parental and adolescent nutrition label use and differences by parental label-use pattern, age group, and data-collection period. Cross-sectional secondary analysis of pooled Korea National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey data from 2016-2018, 2019-2021, and 2022-2024. South Korea. Adolescents aged 12-18 years with linked parent data (N = 2,879). Data were weighted to provide nationally representative estimates. Adolescent nutrition label use; parental nutrition label use was the primary exposure. Survey-weighted logistic regression estimated associations overall and by parental label-use pattern, age group, and survey cycle, adjusting for age, sex, and household characteristics. Parental nutrition label use was associated with adolescent label use (adjusted odds ratio: 1.31; 95% CI: 1.09-1.58). Associations were observed for father-only, mother-only, and both-parent use, with the strongest association being for both-parent use (adjusted odds ratio: 1.84; 95% CI: 1.29-2.64). The association was significant at ages 12-15 years but not at 16-18 years; age-group interaction was not significant. Cycle-specific adjusted estimates were positive but not always significant. Parental nutrition label use was associated with adolescent label use. Family inclusive nutrition education may support adolescent label use.
