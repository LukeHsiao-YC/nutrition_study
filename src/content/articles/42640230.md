### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在識別和比較美國高等教育健康相關課程教師在體重議題上的教學方法與觀點。透過對112位健康專業教師進行的線上問卷調查，收集了他們的教學策略、體重偏見得分及情緒傾向等數據，並區分為體重中心取向、混合取向和體重包容取向。研究結果顯示，體重包容取向的教學更注重行為和社會決定因素，且採用此取向的教師體重偏見得分較低。研究結論指出，教學情緒能決定體重議題的框架，並強調未來需進一步研究體重包容性課程對學生體重態度和行為的具體影響。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇研究辨識並比較了健康教育教師對體重相關內容的教學方法及其與偏見的關聯，但研究並未探討這些不同的教學取向如何實際影響學生的體重態度和行為。未來的研究應著重於評估體重包容性課程對學生學習成果及行為改變的長期成效。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Exploring Health Education Instructors' Perspectives and Approaches to Weight-Related Content.
**Abstract:** To identify and compare pedagogical approaches to weight-related topics among US higher education instructors who teach health-related courses. Survey, including an open-ended question, Universal Measure of Bias-FAT (UMB-FAT) and Beliefs About Obese People (BAOP) scores, collected responses from April to August 2023. Online, 53-item virtual platform survey. One hundred and twelve health profession instructors (aged ≥ 18 years; including nursing, dietetics, medicine, allied health, exercise science) participated and were selected by cluster sampling from 3 randomly selected colleges and universities per state. Instructors' pedagogical strategies and biases toward weight. Sentiment and content analysis conducted by 2 researchers. Sentiments were weight-centric, mixed/hybrid (MH), or weight-inclusive (WI) with categories (weight metrics, topics and concepts, teaching strategies, and theories and constructs) and mapped for comparison. Scale scores (UMBFAT; BAOP) were analyzed for descriptive statistics. Weight-centric approaches emphasized body mass index and dieting, WI emphasized behavior, social determinants, and Health at Every Size. Discussion-based teaching and bias-awareness content appeared more in MH and WI. Weight-inclusive instructors had lower UMB-FAT (1.58 ± 0.52) and BAOP (3.20 ± 0.97) scores. Teaching sentiment can determine how weight is framed. Findings highlight opportunities to investigate how WI curricula affect student weight attitudes and behaviors.
