### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討美國州級政策（種族平等、性傾向包容性、性別認同包容性）的交織性組合，與性別少數群體（SGM）成人延遲就醫、心理健康服務使用及醫療歧視之間的關聯。研究分析了21,701名SGM成人的數據，並結合州級政策指標，運用交織性多層次分析方法。結果顯示，特定州級政策組合（如高種族平等、中等性傾向但低性別認同包容性）預測有最高的就醫延遲與醫療歧視，而另一種組合（低種族平等、低性傾向及負面性別認同包容性）則預測心理健康服務使用最低。總體而言，州級政策在這些層面與SGM成人的就醫延遲、心理健康服務使用及醫療歧視分數的微小變異相關。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究透過檢視州級政策在種族平等、性傾向與性別認同三方面的交織性影響，填補了過去可能較少同時探討多重政策層面對SGM群體健康不平等影響的研究空白。然而，研究結果指出州級政策對這些健康結果的變異解釋程度相對較小，暗示未來研究需更深入探討其他個體層面或更精細的政策機制，以全面理解其影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Health Care Delays, Mental Health Care, and Discrimination among US Sexual and Gender Minority Adults: Intersectional Analysis of State-level Policies.
**Abstract:** Sexual and gender minority (SGM) individuals experience profound health inequities exacerbated by delays in health care and underutilization of mental health services. This study examined the association of intersectional state-level policy inclusiveness (racial equity, sexual orientation inclusiveness, and gender inclusiveness) with delays in health care, mental health service use, and discrimination in medical settings (DMS) among a sample of SGM adults in the United States. Data were analyzed from 21,701 SGM adults participating in the All of Us Research Program 2021-2023, combined with state-level policy measures: National Equity Atlas' Racial Equity Index, and Movement Advancement Project's sexual orientation inclusiveness and gender identity inclusiveness policy tallies. Using intersectional multilevel analysis of individual heterogeneity and discriminatory accuracy (I-MAIHDA), mixed effects logistic and gamma models were built to examine the additive effects and predicted probability of key outcomes by intersectional strata consisting of combinations of state-level policy measures. The null model showed among SGM adults, intersectional strata explained somewhat more variation in DMS score (2.42%), compared to delay in care (2.13%) and mental health care use (0.58%). The intersectional stratum characterized by a high racial equity index, medium sexual orientation inclusiveness, and low gender identity inclusiveness had the highest predicted delays in health care and DMS scores; the stratum with a low racial equity index, low sexual orientation inclusiveness, and negative gender identity inclusiveness had the lowest predicted mental health service use. The models that included both state-level policy variables and individual characteristics improved precision of estimates, with 53.67% of stratum-level variance explained for delay in health care, 24.06% for mental health service use, and 60.41% for DMS score. The results demonstrate that state-level policies across race/ethnicity, sexual orientation, and gender identity are associated with small variations in delays in health care, mental health service use, and DMS scores.
