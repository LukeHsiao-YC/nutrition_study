### 重點摘要
老年性黃斑部病變 (AMD) 是視力喪失的主因，其發病機制可能與白血球亞型有關，但因果關係尚不明確。本研究利用三個大型前瞻性世代研究，透過觀察性分析和孟德爾隨機化 (MR) 遺傳分析，探討白血球計數與臨床診斷之AMD的因果關聯。研究結果顯示，白血球及其多種亞型（特別是嗜酸性球）計數升高與AMD風險相關，且遺傳分析進一步證實，遺傳決定的嗜酸性球計數升高與AMD風險存在因果關係。這項大規模研究明確指出，全身性嗜酸性球驅動的免疫途徑可能是AMD發病機制的重要貢獻者。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究透過結合大規模觀察性與遺傳分析，首次明確證實了嗜酸性球計數升高與老年性黃斑部病變 (AMD) 之間存在因果關聯，成功填補了過去僅知相關性但因果關係不明確的研究空白。未來的研究可進一步探討嗜酸性球在AMD中的具體分子機制與致病途徑，並驗證此因果關係在不同人種群體中的普遍性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Leukocytes and age-related macular degeneration.
**Abstract:** Age-related macular degeneration (AMD) is a leading cause of vision loss. Leukocyte subtypes have been implicated in the pathogenesis, but causality remains uncertain. We investigated whether leukocyte counts are causally associated with clinically diagnosed AMD using observational and genetic analyses in three prospective cohorts. We included 117,714 individuals from the Copenhagen General Population Study (CGPS), 388,758 from the UK Biobank (UKB), and 285,612 from the Million Veteran Program (MVP), all of European ancestry and without clinically diagnosed AMD at baseline. In total, 27,130 incident AMD cases were identified. Baseline leukocyte counts were log2-transformed and standardized. Observational associations with incident AMD were estimated using Cox regression in CGPS and UKB, and then meta-analysed. Genetic instruments derived from genome-wide association studies were used for one-sample mendelian randomization (MR) and multivariable MR (MVMR) analyses in UKB and MVP, and estimates then meta-analysed. Observationally, a 1-standard deviation (SD) increase in leukocyte (Hazard ratio (HR) [95% confidence interval (CI)], 1.07 [1.05-1.09]), neutrophil (1.07 [1.05-1.09]), monocyte (1.05 [1.03-1.07]), basophil (1.06 [1.04-1.08]), and eosinophil (1.05 [1.03-1.06]) counts was associated with increased risk of clinically diagnosed AMD (all p < 0.0001). In the genetic analyses, a 1-SD increase in genetically determined eosinophil counts was causally associated with increased risk of clinically diagnosed AMD in the MR (OR [95% CI], 1.07 [1.00-1.15]; p = 0.038) and MVMR (OR [95% CI], 1.12 [1.03-1.21]; p = 0.006) analyses. This large-scale study provides observational and genetic evidence that elevated eosinophil counts are causally linked to risk of clinically diagnosed AMD. These findings highlight systemic eosinophil-driven immune pathways as potential contributors to AMD pathogenesis.
