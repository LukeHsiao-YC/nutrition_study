### 重點摘要
儘管多項研究指出第二型糖尿病與乳癌發生率相關，但結果存在異質性且與糖尿病病程的關係不明。本研究納入72,295名中國女性進行長達19年的世代追蹤，旨在探討第二型糖尿病、其病程與乳癌風險的關聯。研究結果顯示，整體第二型糖尿病與乳癌風險增加相關，其中在糖尿病診斷後五年內風險最高。這項研究證實了中國女性第二型糖尿病會增加乳癌風險，且風險在診斷後約五年達到高峰。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了過去對於第二型糖尿病病程與乳癌風險關聯性不明的空白，明確指出風險在糖尿病診斷後約五年內達到高峰。然而，研究本身也指出未來研究應收集更詳細的治療與生活方式改變資訊，以進行更深入的分析。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Association between type 2 diabetes mellitus and breast cancer risk: results from a population-based cohort study in Chinese women.
**Abstract:** Although numerous epidemiological studies have reported significant associations between type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) and breast cancer incidence, considerable heterogeneity exists across studies. Moreover, the breast cancer risk in relation to T2DM duration remains unclear. This study aimed to investigate the associations between T2DM, T2DM duration, and breast cancer risk in Chinese women. A total of 72,295 women aged 40-70 years from the Shanghai Women's Health Study were included. T2DM was defined as diagnosis after age 30, and categorized as prevalent at enrollment or incident during follow-up. Incident T2DM was treated as a time-dependent exposure in Cox proportional hazards regression models. The risk trajectory of breast cancer following T2DM diagnosis was characterized using categorical modelling analysis. Over a median follow-up of 19.08 years, 1,759 incident cases of breast cancer were identified. Compared to individuals without diabetes, overall T2DM was associated with an increased risk of breast cancer (HR = 1.22, 95%CI: 1.05-1.43). This association was significant in women diagnosed with incident T2DM during follow-up (HR = 1.28, 95% CI: 1.05-1.55) but not in those with prevalent T2DM at enrollment (HR = 1.15, 95% CI: 0.90-1.46). A clear trajectory of trend was observed, with the highest risk occurring within the first 5 years after T2DM diagnosis (HR = 4.22, 95% CI: 3.27-5.44). T2DM was associated with an elevated breast cancer risk in Chinese women, which peaked at approximately 5 years post-diagnosis. Future studies should collect detailed information on treatment and lifestyle changes in study populations, to enable more in-depth analysis.
