### 重點摘要
這項在衣索比亞農村進行的群體隨機對照試驗，旨在評估整合式雞隻生產（ACGG）以及結合營養行為改變與家庭園藝（ACGG+ATONU）的介入措施，對改善當地女性及家庭飲食多樣性的效果。研究發現，所有介入組別的飲食多樣性均呈現上升趨勢，但僅有整合性的ACGG+ATONU方案顯著改善了家庭飲食多樣性，對女性個人的飲食多樣性則無顯著影響。純粹的雞隻生產介入（ACGG）沒有顯著效果。研究強調，在低收入環境中，營養敏感型農業介入需要整合行為改變的組件以提升成效。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究填補了關於營養敏感型農業介入措施，特別是雞隻生產與營養行為改變在改善女性營養成果方面效果有限的證據空白。其研究結果強調，僅提供生產支持的農業介入不足以改善飲食多樣性，必須整合行為改變組件；然而，儘管家庭飲食多樣性有所改善，女性個體的飲食多樣性並未顯著提升，這指出未來研究需要更深入探討如何將家庭層面的改善轉化為個體層面的營養效益。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effectiveness of Integrated Chicken Production and Nutrition-Sensitive Interventions on Women's Dietary Diversity Outcomes: A Cluster-Randomized Controlled Trial in Rural Ethiopia.
**Abstract:** Rural Ethiopian women have poor dietary diversity. While nutrition-sensitive agriculture offers an opportunity for improving dietary diversity, evidence on effectiveness for women's nutrition outcomes remains limited. To evaluate the effectiveness of a chicken production intervention, with and without a nutrition behavior change component, on women's dietary diversity in rural Ethiopia. We conducted a three-arm, cluster-randomized controlled trial among 2,117 households in 60 villages across four regions of Ethiopia. Villages were randomly allocated to: (1) control receiving standard agricultural extension services; (2) African Chicken Genetic Gains (ACGG), providing 25 improved chickens and production support; or (3) ACGG plus Agriculture to Nutrition (ACGG+ATONU), combining chicken production with nutrition behavior change communication and home gardening. The primary outcome was 1-day Minimum Dietary Diversity for Women (MDD-W) and Household Dietary Diversity Score (HDDS), assessed at baseline, midline (9 months), and endline (18 months). We used generalized linear mixed models accounting for cluster randomization to estimate intervention effects. Among all cases, all three treatment arms showed upward trends in both women's and household dietary diversity. The ACGG+ATONU intervention showed no significant effects on women's dietary diversity outcomes (all P>0.05), but improved household dietary diversity compared with ACGG alone (adjusted mean difference in HDDS: 0.37 food groups; 95% confidence interval (CI): 0.02 to 0.71; P=0.037) and control (adjusted mean difference in HDDS: 0.39 food groups; 95% CI: 0.01 to 0.78; P=0.045). ACGG+ATONU also increased household dietary diversity adequacy versus control (adjusted risk difference: 0.06; 95% CI: 0.01 to 0.11; P=0.047). Mediation analysis revealed three key pathways: increased egg production, enhanced women's empowerment, and improved chicken management practices. The integrated ACGG+ATONU intervention significantly improved household dietary diversity but not women's individual dietary diversity, whereas chicken production alone (ACGG) showed no significant effects. These findings emphasize that nutrition-sensitive agricultural interventions in low-income settings require integrated behavior change components. ClinicalTrials.gov identifier NCT03152227; registered at https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03152227.
