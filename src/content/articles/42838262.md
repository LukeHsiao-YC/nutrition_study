### 重點摘要
既存研究已將文化適應過程及其相關壓力與美國西班牙裔/拉丁裔移民的健康下降連結，但其對照護者餵養方式和家庭食物環境的影響仍不明。本研究利用HCHS/SOL FLOR的母子配對數據，探討母親的文化適應及文化適應壓力與照護者餵養風格及家庭食物環境之間的關聯。結果顯示，較高的母親文化適應壓力與權威型及專制型餵養風格、以及較高的餵養要求度有關；同時，較高的語言文化適應和在美居住時間較長，與家庭中含糖飲料增多和蔬果減少相關。這些發現強調了支持西班牙裔移民適應美國生活的重要性，並需開發具文化適應性的介入措施，以改善移民父母的兒童餵養及食物採購行為。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了以往研究對於文化適應過程及文化適應壓力如何影響西班牙裔/拉丁裔移民照護者的餵養方式和家庭食物環境的空白。研究結果也間接指出未來研究可進一步探討如何開發和實施符合文化背景的介入措施，以解決移民父母在兒童餵養和食物採購行為上的需求。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** The association between maternal acculturation, acculturative stress and factors related to child feeding in the Hispanic Community Health Study/Study of Latinos Family Lifestyle Outcomes Research (HCHS/SOL FLOR) Study.
**Abstract:** The acculturation process and its related stress have been associated with health declines among Hispanic/Latino immigrants in the US. However, the impact on caregiver feeding style and the home food environment are unknown. This study aimed to assess the associations between maternal acculturation and acculturation stress with both caregiver feeding style (n=248) and the home food environment (n=178) using data from mother-child dyads from the Family Lifestyle Outcomes Research ancillary study of the Hispanic Community Health Study/Study of Latinos. Children were aged 3-9 years at the time of the study. Results suggest an association between greater maternal acculturative stress and greater odds of authoritative and authoritarian feeding styles. An association between greater acculturative stress and greater maternal demandingness in feeding was also observed. Additionally, greater language acculturation and greater length of time in the US were associated with more sweetened beverages and fewer fruits and vegetables reported in the home. These results highlight the importance of supporting Hispanic immigrants as they adapt to life in the US and developing culturally appropriate interventions that can address child feeding and food purchasing behaviors among immigrant parents.
