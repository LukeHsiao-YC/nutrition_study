### 重點摘要
這篇政策聲明指出，尋求人道保護的無人陪伴移民兒童及其家庭在遷徙過程中面臨健康與福祉風險，而美國的移民政策及執法措施（如拘留與分離）更加劇了這些風險。文章以「兒童最佳利益 (BIOTC)」框架為基礎，重新審視了對這些兒童與家庭的接收方式。其目的在於提出具體建議，以確保尋求人道保護的兒童與家庭的安全與福祉。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇政策聲明旨在填補美國移民政策在處理尋求人道保護的兒童與家庭時，未能充分考量「兒童最佳利益」的政策空白。它提供了具體的指導原則與建議，以改善現行的接收與評估方式，確保兒童的安全與福祉，而非揭示傳統意義上的研究限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Reception and Assessment of Immigrant Children and Families Seeking US Humanitarian Protection: Upholding the Best Interest of the Child: Policy Statement.
**Abstract:** Unaccompanied migrant children and families seeking humanitarian protection face health and well-being risks throughout the migration journey. US immigration policies and enforcement practices that subject children and families to detention and separation compound these risks. Grounded in the Best Interest of the Child (BIOTC) framework, this policy statement recontextualizes reception and proposes recommendations to ensure the safety and well-being of children and families seeking humanitarian protection.
