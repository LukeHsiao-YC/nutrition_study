### 重點摘要
這篇系統性回顧旨在探討不同類型虛擬母乳哺育介入措施對母乳哺育持續時間和獨家母乳哺育的影響。研究系統性檢索了多個資料庫，納入由醫療照護提供者同步進行的虛擬介入措施隨機對照試驗。結果顯示，在納入的58項研究、62項介入措施中，有51.6%有效延長了母乳哺育時間，特別是結合實體與虛擬的介入效果更顯著。總體而言，虛擬母乳哺育介入能有效延長任何形式的母乳哺育和獨家母乳哺育。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了關於不同類型虛擬母乳哺育介入措施有效性的知識空白，特別是確認了結合實體與虛擬介入能產生更顯著的效果。然而，由於多數回顧的研究是在高收入國家進行，研究結果的普適性受限。未來研究需更深入了解家長對虛擬母乳哺育支援的需求，並將其整合到臨床實踐中，同時也應擴展研究地域範圍。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Virtual Health Interventions to Improve Breastfeeding: A Systematic Review.
**Abstract:** Use of virtual health interventions has increased rapidly, with growing evidence evaluating virtual breastfeeding support. However, the effectiveness of different types of virtual breastfeeding interventions remains unclear. To examine the effects of various types of virtual breastfeeding interventions on breastfeeding duration and exclusivity. We systematically searched PubMed, CINAHL, EMBASE, PsycINFO, Cochrane Library, and clinical trial registries (ClinicalTrials.gov and WHO International Clinical Trials Registry Platform) up to January 2025. Randomized controlled trials evaluating synchronous virtual breastfeeding interventions delivered by health care providers were included. Data on study design, place of origin, participant characteristics, sample size, outcomes of interest, and study outcomes were extracted. A total of 58 studies were included, and 62 interventions were examined. Among them, 32 (51.6%) interventions effectively prolonged breastfeeding duration. Although virtual breastfeeding interventions were effective in extending any and exclusive breastfeeding durations, the interventions that combined in-person and virtual breastfeeding interventions showed more substantial effects in enhancing breastfeeding duration and exclusivity. Several types of interventions improved breastfeeding outcomes, including proactive and reactive telephone support, videoconferencing, and multiple forms of virtual support. Generalizability is limited as the majority of the reviewed studies were conducted in high-income countries. Virtual breastfeeding interventions effectively prolong any breastfeeding and exclusive breastfeeding. As digital health technologies continue to evolve, it is important to understand parents' need for virtual breastfeeding support and enhance the integration of virtual support provision in clinical settings to meet the diverse needs of parents.
