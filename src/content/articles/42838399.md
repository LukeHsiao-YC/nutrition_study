### 重點摘要
研究背景指出，禽類生產中高效利用營養素對永續發展至關重要，而腸道健康已被確立為影響營養素消化、吸收和保留的核心生物學決定因素。本文綜述了腸道健康（包括腸道形態、消化生理、屏障完整性、腸道菌群及免疫穩態）與營養素利用效率之間的生物學機制。文章探討了營養失衡、病原體和環境壓力等因素如何損害腸道功能，並提出了多種營養策略，如精準配方、菌群調節劑、飼料酵素和功能性胺基酸等，以改善腸道功能和營養利用。最終結論強調，維持腸道健康是最大化營養素利用效率的基礎，對於提升生產效率、減少營養流失及促進永續禽類生產具有關鍵意義。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本文填補了傳統上僅關注飼料配方和添加劑的研究空白，明確指出腸道健康才是決定禽類營養素利用效率的核心生物學因素，並為改善生產效率和永續發展提供了全面的生物學基礎和營養優化策略。作為一篇綜述，其主要限制在於其基於現有知識的整合與分析，而非提出新的實驗數據。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Gut health as a determinant of nutrient use efficiency in poultry: biological basis and nutritional optimization.
**Abstract:** Efficient nutrient use is fundamental to sustainable poultry production because it influences productive performance, feed costs, animal welfare, and environmental nutrient losses. Although improvements in nutrient utilization have traditionally focused on diet formulation and feed additives, increasing evidence demonstrates that gut health is a central biological determinant governing digestion, absorption, and retention of nutrients. Gut health encompasses the coordinated interaction among intestinal morphology, digestive physiology, epithelial barrier integrity, gut microbiota, and mucosal immune homeostasis, all of which collectively regulate nutrient use efficiency. Disruption of these components by nutritional imbalances, enteric pathogens, heat stress, mycotoxins, and management-related stressors impairs digestive and absorptive function, increases intestinal permeability, alters microbial ecology, and redirects nutrients from productive processes toward immune activation and tissue repair. Consequently, maintaining intestinal homeostasis has become a major objective of modern poultry nutrition. This review synthesizes current knowledge on the biological mechanisms linking gut health with nutrient use efficiency and examines how intestinal development, digestive function, nutrient transport, barrier integrity, microbial metabolism, and immune regulation interact to determine productive performance. Major nutritional, environmental, and pathological factors compromising gut function are discussed, together with current nutritional strategies including precision diet formulation, microbiota modulation, feed enzymes, functional amino acids, phytogenic compounds, antioxidants, and trace minerals that improve intestinal function and nutrient utilization. Collectively, the evidence synthesized in this review demonstrates that maintaining gut health is fundamental to maximizing nutrient use efficiency and provides a biological foundation for improving productive efficiency while reducing nutrient losses and supporting more sustainable poultry production.
