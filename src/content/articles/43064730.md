### 重點摘要
患有克隆氏症並接受末端迴腸切除術的病人容易缺乏維生素 B12，但目前對於 B12 的監測與補充尚無標準建議。本研究回溯性分析 197 名此類病患的 B12 補充策略，旨在描述臨床實踐模式並找出相關風險因子。結果顯示，60% 的患者在追蹤期間接受了 B12 補充，主要為肌肉注射，且迴腸切除長度超過 20 公分是術後發生 B12 缺乏的唯一相關因子。臨床上的 B12 補充策略多樣且異質，建議對於切除長度超過 20 公分的患者考慮預防性補充，並強調制定標準建議的重要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究揭示了克隆氏症末端迴腸切除患者在維生素 B12 補充策略上的異質性現況，並發現迴腸切除長度超過 20 公分是術後 B12 缺乏的潛在風險因子，彌補了此族群缺乏標準化建議的空白。然而，由於其回溯性、單中心的限制，未來的研究仍需建立最佳的 B12 監測與補充指南以優化臨床實踐。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Cyanocobalamin supplementation strategies in patients with Crohn's disease and terminal ileal resection in clinical practice.
**Abstract:** Vitamin B12 (B12) is absorbed at the terminal ileum. Patients with Crohn's disease (CD) resection of the terminal ileum (RTI) are at risk of B12 deficiency. No standard recommendations on the monitoring and supplementation of B12 after non-extensive RTI are available. Our aims were to describe B12 supplementation modalities in clinical practice in patients with CD and RTI and to identify potential factors associated with B12 deficiency in this population. Retrospective, single-centre study that included patients with CD who underwent RTI between January 2008 and October 2022. B12 levels, supplementation, indication and route of administration after RTI were recorded. One hundred and ninety-seven patients were included. Thirteen (7%) patients were on B12 supplementation prior to RTI. Seventy-nine patients (40%) were not supplemented and did not develop B12 deficiency. B12 supplementation was prescribed for 60% of patients during follow-up (6% of whom were already supplemented before surgery), 53% preventively after surgery and 47% after B12 deficiency. Intramuscular administration was the most common (97%, mainly monthly), and oral administration was limited (11%, mainly weekly). During follow-up, changes to the B12 supplementation occurred in 36% (mainly prolonging the interval of intramuscular administration). RTI length over 20 cm (OR 1.944, 95% CI 1.019-3.709; P = 0.042) was the only factor associated with developing B12 deficiency following surgery. B12 supplementation in patients with CD and RTI is heterogeneous. Preventive supplementation might be driven by ileal resection length, being recommended in those greater than 20 cm. Recommendations on supplementation and monitoring are needed to optimize clinical practice.
