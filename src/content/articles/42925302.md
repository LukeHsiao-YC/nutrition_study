### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討低收入家庭中，父母基於計畫行為理論的健康促進行為相關構念與食物資源管理，如何與家庭食物安全狀況相關聯。透過對1,622位家長進行的橫斷面次級分析，並使用邏輯迴歸模型評估其關聯性。研究發現，知覺行為控制較高以及食物資源管理自信心較強的父母，其家庭食物安全的機率顯著較高。這項結果強調，提升父母的健康促進技能與食物資源管理自信，對於預防食物不安全及維護家庭健康具有重要意義。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了將父母的行為心理構念（如知覺行為控制）與實際的食物資源管理技能自信，和家庭食物安全狀況連結起來的知識空白，特別是在低收入家庭中。它為未來針對改善家庭食物安全狀況的介入措施，提供了具體且可操作的方向，例如增強父母的相關技能與自信心。然而，由於是橫斷面研究，本研究無法確立因果關係，且數據來源於預防兒童肥胖的試驗，可能在結果推論上存在一定限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Parent Attitudes and Behaviors Associated With Household Food Security Among Families With Low Income.
**Abstract:** To examine associations between Theory of Planned Behavior constructs related to parent engagement in health-promoting behaviors for obesity prevention in young children and parent food resource management with household food security status. This is a cross-sectional secondary analysis of baseline data from 1,622 parents in the ENCIRCLE (patiEnt-cliNic-Community Integration to prevent obesity in Rural preschool ChiLdrEn) study, a pragmatic cluster-randomized controlled trial to prevent obesity among preschool-aged children from families with low income. Logistic regression models examined the associations of Theory of Planned Behavior constructs (attitude, subjective norm, and perceived behavioral control) and food resource management (self-confidence, behaviors) with household food security. Parents with greater perceived behavioral control (odds ratio = 1.44, confidence intervals: 1.14-1.83) and food resource management self-confidence (odds ratio = 2.11, confidence intervals: 1.82-2.44) had significantly higher odds of experiencing household food security in adjusted models. Among parents of young children, perceived behavioral control over promoting a healthy lifestyle and self-confidence in managing food resources may help protect against food insecurity. These findings suggest that improving parents' health promotion skills and improving food resource management confidence is important for healthy families.
