### 重點摘要
本研究旨在釐清在當代囊性纖維化肝膽疾病(CFHBI)診斷框架下，多參數超音波參數的生物學解釋。研究對119名囊性纖維化兒童進行了剪切波彈性成像(SWE)、剪切波彌散(SWD)和衰減成像(ATI)等多參數超音波與組織病理學評估。結果顯示，衰減成像(ATI)值與組織病理學定義的脂肪變性顯著相關，但SWE未與纖維化相關，SWD則無特定表型關聯。研究建議，應在ESPGHAN/NASPGHAN框架下對個別超音波參數進行表型導向的解釋，並強調整體CFHBI鑑別需前瞻性多中心驗證。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了當代ESPGHAN/NASPGHAN囊性纖維化肝膽疾病框架下，多參數超音波個別參數生物學解釋的空白，特別是確立了衰減成像(ATI)與脂肪變性的關聯性。此外，研究也揭示了不同CFHBI分類標準間的顯著差異。然而，超音波參數在整體CFHBI鑑別的發現為探索性結果，未來研究仍需進行前瞻性多中心驗證以確認其臨床應用價值。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Multiparametric ultrasound characterizes pediatric cystic fibrosis hepatobiliary involvement.
**Abstract:** The contemporary European Society for Pediatric Gastroenterology, Hepatology and Nutrition (ESPGHAN)/North American Society for Pediatric Gastroenterology, Hepatology and Nutrition (NASPGHAN) framework broadens the definition of cystic fibrosis hepatobiliary involvement (CFHBI), but the biological interpretation of individual multiparametric ultrasound parameters within this framework remains unclear. We evaluated the biological associations of shear wave elastography (SWE), shear wave dispersion (SWD), and attenuation imaging (ATI) with histopathological phenotypes in children with cystic fibrosis (CF). In this ambispective cohort study, 119 children with CF underwent hepatobiliary assessment, including SWE, SWD, ATI, Doppler ultrasonography, clinical and laboratory evaluation. Histopathological assessment was performed when clinically indicated. CFHBI was classified according to the ESPGHAN/NASPGHAN framework, with the Debray criteria used as a secondary exploratory comparison. Exploratory phenotype-oriented analyses evaluated ultrasound parameters according to corresponding histopathological phenotypes. Exploratory phenotype-oriented analyses demonstrated significantly higher ATI values in histopathologically defined steatosis (p < 0.001), with good discriminatory performance (AUC 0.813) and an optimal threshold of 0.655 dB/cm/MHz. In contrast, SWE was not associated with histopathologically defined fibrosis (p = 0.151), whereas SWD showed no significant phenotype-specific associations. Secondary exploratory analyses demonstrated significant overall CFHBI discrimination by all three ultrasound parameters, with the highest AUC observed for SWD. The ESPGHAN/NASPGHAN and Debray classifications demonstrated significant disagreement (McNemar p < 0.001), with 22.7% of patients classified as Debray-negative but ESPGHAN/NASPGHAN-positive. Multiparametric ultrasound should be interpreted within the contemporary ESPGHAN/NASPGHAN CFHBI framework rather than as a single diagnostic tool for hepatobiliary involvement. The distinct biological associations of SWE, ATI, and SWD support a phenotype-oriented interpretation of individual ultrasound parameters, whereas overall CFHBI discrimination should be interpreted as a secondary exploratory finding requiring prospective multicenter validation.
