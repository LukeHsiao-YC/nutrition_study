### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估新診斷發炎性腸道疾病 (IBD) 兒童胰臟受累的盛行率（高血清脂肪酶和影像學表現）及預測因子。這項回溯性研究分析了68名兒童的數據，發現高達39.7%的患者有脂肪酶升高，而20.59%的患者經磁振腸道造影術 (MRE) 證實有胰臟受累。MRE顯示胰臟受累的兒童，其總免疫球蛋白G (IgG) 和IgG1水準顯著較高，且IgG1結合IgG2的模型在預測胰臟MRE變化方面表現最佳。結論指出，新診斷IBD兒童中約五分之一存在結構性胰臟受累，且免疫球蛋白G亞型可作為重要的預測指標。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究量化了新診斷IBD兒童胰臟受累的盛行率，並首次證明免疫球蛋白G亞型（特別是IgG1和IgG2的組合）可作為磁振腸道造影術上胰臟變化的有效預測因子，有助於早期識別高風險兒童。然而，本研究為回溯性設計，且樣本數有限，未來需要更大規模的前瞻性研究來確認這些發現並探討其臨床預後意義。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Pancreatic involvement in children with newly diagnosed inflammatory bowel disease.
**Abstract:** Pancreatic involvement in inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) represents a heterogeneous group of conditions. Our study assessed the prevalence of hyperlipasemia and pancreatic imaging involvement in children with newly diagnosed IBD. We also evaluated predictors of pancreatic changes on magnetic resonance imaging in this population. This retrospective study analysed the standard magnetic resonance enterography (MRE) protocol with additional sequences for pancreatic assessment. The serum activity of lipase, aminotransferases, and total immunoglobulin G (IgG) and its subclasses concentrations were measured at diagnosis, as well as body mass index percentile (pBMI) and IBD activity. Data from 68 children were analysed. Increased IgG concentrations were observed in 25 patients (36.6%) for total IgG and in 25 patients (36.6%) for IgG1. Among 27 patients (39.7%) with increased lipase, 8 (29.6%) had increased IgG concentration and 10 (37.1%) had increased IgG1 concentration; both IgG and IgG1 concentration were elevated in 7 (87.5%) patients. MRE revealed pancreatic involvement in 20.59%. Patients with pancreatic involvement on MRE had higher total IgG (p = 0.012) and IgG1 (p = 0.024) levels and lipase activity 3x higher than the normal limit (p = 0.04). Total IgG and IgG1 concentrations were significantly associated with MRE findings and their combination yielded the highest discriminatory performance. IgG subclasses proved to be useful in a multivariate model incorporating IgG1 and IgG2 concentrations (0.75; 95% confidence interval: 0.59-0.92). Structural pancreatic involvement was observed in one-fifth of our patients. They had significantly higher total IgG and IgG1 levels. The IgG1 + IgG2 model performed best in prediction pancreatic MRE changes.
