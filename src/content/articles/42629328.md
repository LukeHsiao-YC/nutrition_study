### 重點摘要
該研究旨在探討注意力不足過動症（ADHD）對接受多學科治療的肥胖青少年體重管理結果的影響。這項回溯性縱向研究納入269名肥胖青少年，其中約20%患有ADHD，並追蹤其BMI z分數變化及行為數據。結果顯示，無論是否有ADHD，整體減重成功率相似，但成功的預測因子不同：有ADHD的青少年，精神病理學負面影響減重，而身體活動量增加則有幫助；無ADHD的青少年，衝動性飲食會降低減重可能性，而改善飲食行為則會提高成功率。研究結論強調應根據青少年的行為和臨床特徵客製化肥胖治療方案。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇研究填補了在多學科治療肥胖青少年中，注意力不足過動症（ADHD）的存在是否會導致減重成功預測因子差異的研究空白。它指出儘管整體減重成效相似，但成功的關鍵在於針對不同行為和臨床特徵的客製化方案，而非一概而論的治療模式。未來的研究可進一步探討如何設計並實施這些客製化方案，並評估其成效。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Impact of ADHD on Obesity Treatment Outcomes in Adolescents Receiving Multidisciplinary Care: A Retrospective Longitudinal Study.
**Abstract:** Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) is frequent among adolescents with obesity and may influence treatment outcomes. This study aimed to explore differences in treatment outcomes and predictors of weight-loss success between adolescents with and without ADHD, followed at a tertiary Adolescent Obesity Clinic. This retrospective longitudinal study included 269 adolescents (12-17 years) followed for 16.8 (IQR: 5.3) months, of whom 53 had ADHD (19.7%). Obesity-related and behavioural data were collected and analysed using generalised estimating equations (for longitudinal changes) and logistic regression models (to identify predictors of weight-loss success, i.e., a decrease in BMI z-score ≥ 0.25). Weight loss success was achieved in 34.9% of the sample, with no significant differences between those with and without ADHD. Among adolescents with ADHD, psychopathology was negatively associated with weight loss success (OR = 0.25), while increases in total physical activity were positively associated (OR = 1.01). In those without ADHD, impulsive eating reduced the likelihood of weight loss (OR = 0.33), whereas improved eating behaviour increased it (OR = 2.92). Although adolescents with and without ADHD may exhibit similar treatment outcomes, predictors of weight loss success differed, highlighting the importance of tailoring obesity programs according to behavioural and clinical profiles in order to improve obesity treatment among adolescents.
