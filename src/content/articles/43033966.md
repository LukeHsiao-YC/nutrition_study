### 重點摘要
VITAL研究是一項大型隨機試驗，旨在評估海洋omega-3脂肪酸補充劑對普通人群心血管疾病和癌症初級預防的效果。研究發現，每天補充1克omega-3脂肪酸並未顯著降低主要心血管事件或總癌症發生率，但顯著減少了總心肌梗塞、致死性心肌梗塞、經皮冠狀動脈介入治療和心臟衰竭再住院風險，並降低了自體免疫疾病風險。研究結果不支持健康成人常規補充omega-3以預防慢性病，但建議對魚類攝取量低或有心血管風險因子等特定族群進行針對性補充。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了過去缺乏在「未經篩選的一般人群」中，大規模隨機評估海洋omega-3脂肪酸用於心血管疾病和癌症初級預防的空白。未來的研究應更深入探討不同人口統計學、飲食和臨床因素對omega-3補充效果的影響，特別是針對魚類攝取量較低或有特定風險因子的族群進行更精確的效益評估。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Omega-3 Fatty Acid Supplementation Has Coronary and Other Selective Health Benefits: Comprehensive Results from the VITamin D and OmegA-3 TriaL (VITAL).
**Abstract:** The VITamin D and OmegA-3 TriaL (VITAL) is the only large-scale randomized trial of marine omega-3 fatty acid (n-3 FA) supplementation for the primary prevention of cardiovascular disease (CVD) and cancer in a general population unselected for elevated risk. This article provides an integrated overview of the results of the n-3 FA component and its ancillary studies. VITAL is a completed nationwide, randomized, placebo-controlled, 2x2 factorial trial of n-3 FAs (1 g/day) and vitamin D Compared with placebo, the n-3 FA supplement did not significantly reduce the primary endpoint of major CVD events (hazard ratio [HR]=0.92 [95% confidence interval 0.80-1.06]) but did significantly reduce total myocardial infarction (MI; HR=0.72 [0.59-0.90]), fatal MI (HR=0.50 [0.26-0.97]), percutaneous coronary intervention (HR=0.78 [0.63-0.95]), and recurrent hospitalization for heart failure (HR=0.86 [0.74-0.998]). The intervention was not associated with AF risk. Supplemental n-3 FAs did not reduce total cancer incidence, other cancer endpoints, or all-cause mortality but was associated with significantly decreased risk of autoimmune disease (confirmed plus probable cases; HR=0.82 [0.68-0.99]). Effect modification by demographic, dietary, and clinical factors was observed for several endpoints, with greater benefits in Black participants and those with lower fish intake or cardiovascular risk factors, but with variable effects in women vs. men. Supplemental n-3 FAs did not significantly reduce major CVD events or cancer but decreased multiple cardiac endpoints and autoimmune disease. Several factors modified treatment effects. Although the findings do not support routine n-3 FA supplementation among healthy adults for prevention of chronic disease, targeted supplementation of those with low fish intake and other select groups warrants consideration.
