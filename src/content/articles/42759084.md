### 重點摘要
這項混合方法系統性文獻回顧旨在綜合心房顫動患者對醫療環境中衛教的經驗與看法。研究依據Joanna Briggs Institute的方法，將量化與質化證據進行整合分析，生成六項綜合發現。結果顯示，患者強調衛教需清晰、個人化、具持續性、支持自我管理，並能減輕情感負擔。總結而言，心房顫動的衛教應以患者為中心，結合個人化資訊、關係性連續照護、實用自我管理支持、適當時機、對話與重複學習。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻透過闡明心房顫動患者如何體驗和評估衛教支持，填補了該領域在患者中心衛教發展上的理解空白。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Atrial fibrillation patients' experiences and perceptions of patient education in health care settings: aA mixed methods systematic review.
**Abstract:** To synthesise qualitative and quantitative evidence on patients' experiences and perceptions of patient education in atrial fibrillation care. Mixed-methods systematic review using a convergent integrated approach. A systematic mixed-methods review was conducted following the Joanna Briggs Institute convergent integrated methodology. Quantitative findings were transformed into textual descriptions and integrated with qualitative findings to generate synthesised findings reflecting patients' experiences and perceptions of patient education. PubMed, CINAHL, Scopus, and ProQuest (Eric) databases were searched in January 2025 and updated in November 2025. Six synthesised findings comprising 14 categories were generated. Patients emphasised the need for clear, understandable, and personalised information and described emotional burden alongside a need for continuity and reassurance. Education was valued when it supported self-management and when mediated and social modes of learning complemented professional support. Educational experiences were shaped by mismatches between patients' capacities and educational approaches, structural barriers, and the timing and continuity of education. Overall, patient education was experienced as an ongoing, relational, and context-dependent process in which personal relevance, dialogue, continuity, and opportunities to revisit information influenced patients' confidence, reassurance, and engagement in self-management. rom patients' perspectives, patient education in atrial fibrillation care should be individually relevant, practically applicable, relationally supportive, and responsive to changing needs across the care trajectory. The findings support patient-centred development of education in atrial fibrillation care by clarifying how patients experience and evaluate educational support in clinical settings. Patient education in atrial fibrillation care may be strengthened by combining clear and personalised information with relational continuity, practical self-management support, appropriate timing, dialogue and repeated learning. This review followed the PRISMA 2020 statement and the Joanna Briggs Institute methodology for convergent integrated mixed-methods systematic reviews.
