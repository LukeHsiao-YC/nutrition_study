### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討在真實世界情境下，非糖尿病成人零食攝取後的血糖反應及其生活型態決定因子，因為此類大型研究相對缺乏。研究透過連續血糖監測等方式，追蹤了785名新加坡成年人共3,325次零食攝取事件。結果顯示，攝取麵包、油炸食物、甜點及含糖飲料，以及下午或傍晚的零食，會導致較高的餐後血糖增量曲線下面積；而零食後進行中等至劇烈身體活動則與較低的血糖反應相關。研究結論指出，零食的組成、攝取時間和餐後身體活動顯著影響非糖尿病成人的餐後血糖反應，提示生活型態建議有助於改善日常血糖控制。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究填補了在真實世界情境下，針對非糖尿病成人零食攝取後血糖反應及其生活型態決定因子的大型實證研究空白。儘管採用了密集追蹤的觀察性設計，它揭示了重要的關聯性，但作為一項觀察性研究，它無法確立因果關係。此外，研究對象限於新加坡成人，未來研究可考慮擴大地理範圍或進行介入性試驗以進一步驗證這些發現。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Lifestyle determinants of glycemic responses to snacking in free-living adults without diabetes: an intensive longitudinal observational study.
**Abstract:** Snacking is common and can contribute to blood glucose fluctuations. However, large studies investigating lifestyle determinants of glucose responses to snacking under real-world conditions are scarce. In this intensive longitudinal observational study, we analyzed data from 3,325 snacking episodes captured by ecological momentary assessment surveys (six daily), continuous glucose monitoring, and accelerometry over nine free-living days among 785 male and female adults aged 21-69 years without diabetes in Singapore. Postprandial glucose responses were assessed using 2-hour postprandial glucose incremental area under the curve (iAUC) following snacking episodes. Lifestyle exposures included consumption of snack foods and beverages, snack timing, postprandial physical activity, and daily physical activity and sleep prior to snacking episodes. Linear mixed-effects models were used to estimate within-person and between-person associations between these exposures and 2-hour postprandial glucose iAUC. The average 2-hour postprandial glucose iAUC was 63.8 (SD: 61.8) mmol/l*minute after snacking episodes. Consumption of bread or buns (within-person β: 30.7; 95% CI: 20.4, 41.1), deep-fried foods (β: 19.8; CI: 9.2, 30.4), sweets (β: 14.6; CI: 6.6, 22.6), and sugary beverages (β: 12.2; CI: 4.3, 20.0) was associated with higher glucose iAUC. In contrast, greater postprandial moderate-to-vigorous physical activity was associated with a lower glucose iAUC (β: -28.4 per hour; CI: -49.2, -7.5). In addition, glucose iAUC was higher following afternoon snacking (β: 9.3; CI: 2.0, 16.6) and early evening (β: 10.7; CI: 2.5, 18.9) than morning snacking. Snack composition, snack timing, and postprandial physical activity were associated with postprandial glucose responses to snacking in adults without diabetes. Recommendations on snacking-related lifestyle behaviors may improve glycemic control in daily life.
