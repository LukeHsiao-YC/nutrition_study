### 重點摘要
研究背景指出食物水解蛋白中常含羥脯胺酸(Hyp)與支鏈胺基酸(BCAAs)，但其組合的生物學效應不明。本研究旨在探討Hyp是否會改變BCAAs對秀麗隱桿線蟲壽命與健康壽命的影響，並評估其是否涉及微生物與代謝重塑。結果顯示，Hyp與BCAAs的組合能以劑量與BCAA種類依賴的方式改善線蟲的壽命與健康相關表型，包括提升抗壓性、維持運動能力與肌肉結構、減少氧化損傷並增加ATP。多體學分析進一步揭示了這些效應與線蟲腸道微生物組成、NAD前驅物代謝（如NaR）以及宿主能量穩態的改變有關。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了羥脯胺酸與支鏈胺基酸組合對生物體壽命與健康壽命影響不明的空白。儘管研究在秀麗隱桿線蟲中確定了微生物組成、NAD前驅物代謝及宿主能量穩態為潛在介質，但這些機制仍需在未來研究中進一步驗證，並探討其在更複雜生物體中的普適性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Hydroxyproline Enhances Branched-Chain Amino Acid Effects on Lifespan and Healthspan in Caenorhabditis elegans.
**Abstract:** Food-derived protein hydrolysates frequently contain both collagen-associated hydroxyproline (Hyp) and branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs), but the biological consequences of their combination remain unclear. This study tested whether Hyp changes the effects of leucine (Leu), isoleucine (Ile), and valine (Val) on lifespan and healthspan-related phenotypes in Caenorhabditis elegans and whether these treatment effects are accompanied by coordinated microbial and metabolic remodeling. Amino acid concentrations that did not strongly disrupt bacterial food clearance were selected for lifespan, stress-resistance, motility, muscle-integrity, biochemical, microbiome, metabolomic, and transcriptomic analyses. Hyp+BCAA combinations improved lifespan- and healthspan-related phenotypes in a BCAA- and dose-dependent manner. The strongest combined treatments enhanced resistance to thermal and oxidative stress, preserved age-associated motility and muscle structure, reduced oxidative and lipofuscin-associated markers, and increased ATP-related readouts. Multi-omics profiling showed that these direct treatment effects were accompanied by distinct C. elegans-associated microbiota signatures, altered NAD precursor-related metabolites including nicotinic acid ribonucleoside (NaR), and a Leu+Hyp transcriptional response enriched for energy metabolism, proteostasis, and extracellular-matrix remodeling. Integrated microbiome-metabolome analysis identified a positive association between Rhodococcus abundance and NaR levels. These findings demonstrate that, under the tested culture conditions, Hyp+BCAA exposure improves longevity-related phenotypes and identifies microbial composition, NAD precursor metabolism, and host energy homeostasis as candidate, testable mediators.
