### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討學齡前兒童的工作記憶與身體質量指數z分數（BMIz）之間的關聯，以及不健康飲食攝取在其中的潛在作用。這項針對中國武漢1,557名學齡前兒童的橫斷面研究發現，工作記憶綜合分數與BMIz呈負相關，而健康食物攝取不足與BMIz呈正相關。進一步分析顯示，健康食物攝取不足解釋了工作記憶與BMIz總關聯的4.94%。這些結果表明學齡前兒童的工作記憶與BMIz之間存在間接關聯，其中健康食物攝取不足扮演了重要角色。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了學齡前兒童執行功能、飲食行為與肥胖之間關聯的空白，特別指出健康食物攝取不足是工作記憶與BMIz之間潛在的間接連結。然而，由於採用橫斷面設計，無法確立這些關聯的時間順序和因果關係，未來的世代研究對於釐清這些動態關係至關重要。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Working memory and BMIz in preschool children: an indirect association involving insufficient healthy food intake.
**Abstract:** Executive function and health behaviors may be relevant to obesity in preschool children, a critical period for obesity prevention, but their associations remain unclear. Our cross-sectional study investigated these associations in 1,557 preschool children from Wuhan, China. The primary adiposity outcome was body mass index z-score (BMIz). Executive function was assessed using seven tasks, from which standardized task scores were averaged to derive working memory and inhibitory control composite scores. Dietary intake was assessed using a Food Frequency Questionnaire. The insufficient healthy food intake and excessive unhealthy food intake scores were summed to derive an overall dietary risk score. Linear mixed-effects models with a random intercept for kindergarten showed that working memory composite score was inversely associated with BMIz. This association was not observed in inhibitory control composite score. Both insufficient healthy food intake score and overall dietary risk score were positively associated with BMIz. The indirect association analysis indicated that insufficient healthy food intake accounted for 4.94% of the total association between working memory composite score and BMIz. These findings indicate an inverse association between working memory and BMIz in preschool children and suggest a potential indirect association through insufficient healthy food intake. Given the cross-sectional design, prospective studies are needed to clarify the temporal ordering and directionality of these associations.
