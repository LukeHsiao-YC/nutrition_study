### 重點摘要
這項研究探討了診斷測試結果的溝通方式，特別是機率框架和數字格式，如何影響病人對正常結果的理解、情緒反應和後續醫療使用意圖。透過一項包含564名參與者的實驗模擬研究，參與者在觀看腦部腫瘤症狀情境和正常CT結果溝通後，研究發現正面框架（強調「沒有疾病的高度可能性」）相較於負面框架（強調「疾病的低可能性」），能顯著降低病人對患病可能性的感知、情緒擔憂和後續就診意圖。數字格式（頻率或百分比）則未顯示顯著影響。研究結論指出，對低風險資訊的細緻溝通，能有效支持病人安心感並減少不必要的醫療追蹤。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了在傳達正常診斷測試結果時，溝通策略（如機率框架）如何影響病人心理反應和醫療行為的知識空白。其研究結果強調，透過優化醫病溝通方式，可作為一種簡單、低成本且可規模化的介入措施，以提升病人對低風險資訊的理解與安心感，進而降低不必要的醫療服務利用。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** When 'unlikely' is not the same as 'likely not': Effects of likelihood framing and numerical format on patients' responses to normal diagnostic test results.
**Abstract:** Communicating the results of diagnostic testing is a central task in clinical practice. Even when results are normal, patients may remain concerned, particularly depending on how risk information is presented. The present study investigated how likelihood framing and numerical format influence patients' understanding, emotional responses, and intended health care use following normal diagnostic test results. In an experimental analogue study (N = 564), participants were presented with a symptom vignette suggestive of a possible brain tumour. They then viewed a videotaped consultation in which a physician communicated a normal CT result. Likelihood presentation was manipulated in a 2 × 2 factorial design, varying framing (low likelihood of disease vs. high likelihood of no disease) and numerical format (frequency vs percentage), with an additional control condition not mentioning any likelihoods. Thoughtful communication of low-risk information may represent a simple, cost-free, and scalable approach to support patient reassurance and reduce unnecessary follow-up.
