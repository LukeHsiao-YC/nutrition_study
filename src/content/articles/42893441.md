### 重點摘要
這項混合方法研究旨在評估難民和移民親子對美國國家學校午餐計畫 (NSLP) 的看法。研究發現，兒童普遍喜歡 NSLP 餐點，但難以將其健康信仰、語言或宗教飲食習慣融入其中。父母認同 NSLP 符合營養指南，但認為其缺乏文化多元性。結論指出，NSLP 可透過增加國際菜單選項和在地化政策，以幫助移民兒童更順利地適應。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了關於移民和難民家庭對國家學校午餐計畫具體看法的研究空白，揭示了文化適應的挑戰。未來的研究應進一步量化這些擔憂的廣泛程度，並探索有效的解決方案，而不僅限於初步的定性觀察。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Migrant Parent-Child Dyads Perceive the National School Lunch Program as Enjoyable and Beneficial, but Difficult to Navigate Culturally.
**Abstract:** To evaluate refugee and immigrant parent-child dyads perceptions of the National School Lunch Program (NSLP) using mixed methods. Researchers interviewed 11 refugee or immigrant children regarding food acculturation and the NSLP, including a drawing portion. Researchers surveyed parents' perceptions of the NSLP. Thematic analysis and SPSS software were used for analysis. Children's NSLP drawings depicted pizza, milk, burgers, and fruit. Some children preferred NSLP lunch, whereas others preferred their home country school lunch. Half of the parents preferred NSLP lunch for their children. Children noted the challenge of integrating their health beliefs, languages, or religious food practices within the NSLP. Parents disagreed that the NSLP offers cultural foods but agreed that it follows nutritional guidelines. These children enjoyed NSLP meals but could benefit from more international menu options and locally appropriate policies to make the transition smoother. Further research can identify the extent of these concerns and effective solutions.
