### 重點摘要
這項質性研究旨在探討患有高血壓的非裔美國成年人參與數位營養介入計畫（Nourish）以促進DASH飲食依從性的招募偏好。研究透過對30位患有高血壓的非裔美國成年人進行個別訪談，分析其招募需求。結果發現，非裔美國成年人渴望參與針對高血壓和整體健康的數位營養介入，且文化、歷史與情境能力比營養師的種族更重要；招募材料應突顯科技、家庭、自我與健康，並利用傳統（如教堂）及數位管道進行招募。研究建議，透過反映非裔美國成年人生活經驗、在傳統或數位場域進行宣傳，並納入具文化能力的營養師，可有效提高非裔美國成年人在數位營養介入中的參與度，進而降低心血管風險。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了在數位營養介入中，針對患有高血壓的非裔美國成年人招募策略的研究空白，明確指出了能提高他們參與度的具體偏好和關鍵要素。這對於設計更具包容性和文化適應性的健康介入措施至關重要。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Paths to Participation With Black Adults: Identifying Recruitment Preferences and Interest in a Digital Nutrition Intervention to Reduce Hypertension.
**Abstract:** To examine the recruitment preferences of Black adults with hypertension who were recruited to participate in Nourish, a multicomponent intervention to promote adherence to the Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension. A qualitative study ancillary to the Nourish randomized controlled trial, conducted using individual interviews. A public university in the Northeastern US. Black adults with hypertension (male: n = 16, female: n = 14). Recruitment needs for diverse participation in a digital nutrition trial to address hypertension. Reflexive thematic analysis was used to generate themes by sex. Four themes were generated: (1) Black adults want to participate in digital nutrition interventions that target hypertension and overall health; (2) cultural, historical, and contextual competence is more important than dietitian race; (3) technology, family, self, and health must be evident in recruitment materials; and (4) use traditional (e.g., church) and digital recruitment placement. Participation in a digital nutrition trial may be supported through recruitment materials that reflect Black adults and their lived experiences, placement in traditional or digital sites, and the inclusion of culturally competent dietitians. This may support greater representation of Black adults in nutrition interventions to reduce cardiovascular risk.
