### 重點摘要
該研究旨在評估巴拿馬血液透析成人患者的營養攝取充足性與肌肉健康指標。結果顯示，患者普遍存在能量與蛋白質攝取不足（分別有 89.5% 和 79.0% 未達建議攝取量的 80%），且肌少症盛行率高達 22.9%。研究指出肌少症與小腿圍顯著相關，且患者的相位角普遍偏低，尤其肌少症患者的相位角更低。這強調了血液透析患者營養狀況與肌肉健康的惡化，並建議在常規護理中納入肌肉健康評估以指導營養介入，改善患者預後。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了在資源有限的醫療系統中，血液透析患者營養攝取與肌肉健康評估數據不足的空白。研究指出該族群能量與蛋白質攝取普遍不足，且肌少症盛行率高，為未來制定針對性的營養介入策略提供了基礎。然而，本研究採用橫斷面設計且僅以單次 24 小時飲食回憶評估，可能無法反映長期營養狀況，亦無法建立因果關係，未來需進行更長期且多維度的研究。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Nutritional intake adequacy, muscle mass, strength, and sarcopenia in adults on hemodialysis in Panama: A descriptive cross-sectional study.
**Abstract:** Malnutrition and sarcopenia are highly prevalent among patients on hemodialysis, leading to reduced functional capacity, impaired quality of life, and increased mortality. Despite its relevance, nutritional and muscle health assessments remain limited in routine dialysis care, particularly in resource-limited health systems. Our study aimed to evaluate nutritional intake adequacy and muscle health indicators in adults receiving maintenance hemodialysis. We performed a descriptive cross-sectional study in 105 adults receiving maintenance hemodialysis (January-July 2025). Dietary intake was assessed with a single 24-h dietary recall. Muscle health was evaluated using skeletal muscle mass index, phase angle, calf circumference, and handgrip strength. Median energy and protein intakes were 17.0 kcal/kg/day and 0.8 g/kg/day, with 89.5% and 79.0% of participants consuming <80% of requirements. Sarcopenia was present in 22.9% of participants (31.6% women vs. 17.9% men, p = 0.17) and was significantly associated with severely reduced body-mass-index-adjusted calf circumference (OR 7.1, 95% CI 2.3, 22.3). Calf circumference correlated positively with the skeletal muscle mass index (r = 0.37, p < 0.001). The standardized phase angle was low on 62.9% of participants, and significantly lower in those with sarcopenia [-3.3 (1.5) vs. -1.8 (2.5); p = 0.003], correlating with handgrip strength (r = 0.26, p = 0.009). Dialysis vintage was not associated with sarcopenia or dietary adequacy. Hemodialysis patients in a Latin American public health setting showed markedly inadequate energy and protein intake, high prevalence of sarcopenia, and reduced phase angle. Routine assessment of muscle health may provide a feasible and comprehensive approach to guide nutritional interventions and improve outcomes.
