### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在評估初產婦早期懷孕的身體圓度指數 (BRI) 與不良妊娠結局的關聯。透過對9671名個體的二次分析，發現較高的第一孕期BRI與妊娠糖尿病、高血壓疾病、早產、剖腹產及胎兒過大等多種不良妊娠結局顯著相關。研究特別指出，當BRI與BMI的分類不一致時，會顯著影響妊娠糖尿病和剖腹產的風險。結論認為，早期懷孕的BRI，作為中心性肥胖的指標，可改善妊娠糖尿病和剖腹產的風險分層，超越單純使用BMI的評估。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究填補了早期懷孕時期利用身體圓度指數（BRI）作為中心性肥胖指標，來更精確地評估不良妊娠結局風險的空白，顯示其在妊娠糖尿病和剖腹產風險分層方面優於傳統BMI。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Early Pregnancy Body Roundness Index and Development of Adverse Pregnancy Outcomes: A Secondary Analysis of the nuMoM2b Study.
**Abstract:** This study assessed the association between early pregnancy body roundness index (BRI) and adverse pregnancy outcomes in nulliparas. In this secondary analysis of a multicenter prospective cohort study, waist circumference, height, and weight were measured between 6 and 13 6/7 weeks' gestation and used to calculate BMI and BRI (364.2-365.5 × {1 - [(waist circumference/2π)/(0.5 × height)] Among 9671 individuals, greater BRI was associated with gestational diabetes (GDM), hypertensive disorders, preterm birth, cesarean delivery, and large-for-gestational age infants. Discordant BRI and BMI assignments affected GDM and cesarean odds. Those with Q2 BMI but Q3-Q4 BRI had increased odds of GDM (aOR 2.28, 1.49-3.47) and cesarean (aOR 1.39, 1.15-1.68). Those with Q3 BMI and Q1-Q2 BRI demonstrated reduced odds for GDM (aOR 0.31, 0.15-0.63) and cesarean (aOR 0.75, 0.60-0.94). Participants with Q4 BMI and Q1-Q3 BRI experienced decreased odds for cesarean (aOR 0.55, 0.43-0.70) but similar GDM odds. Greater first trimester BRI, a reflection of central adiposity, is associated with adverse pregnancy outcomes. Early pregnancy BRI may improve risk stratification for GDM and cesarean delivery beyond BMI.
