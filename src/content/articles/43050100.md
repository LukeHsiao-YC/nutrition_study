### 重點摘要
這項研究探討超加工食品（UPF）飲食對新興成年人大腦反應的影響，特別關注此群體大腦發育的關鍵期。研究採隨機對照交叉試驗，讓18-25歲參與者在兩週內分別攝取高UPF與無UPF飲食，並透過功能性磁振造影（fMRI）測量其對奶昔的大腦反應。結果顯示，總體而言飲食條件對大腦反應無顯著差異，但探索性分析發現18-21歲的晚期青少年在UPF飲食後，眼眶額葉皮層對獎勵的反應下降，非UPF飲食後則上升。這表明急性UPF飲食干預可能僅改變晚期青少年的大腦反應。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究初步揭示了急性超加工食品飲食干預可能對18-21歲晚期青少年大腦獎勵反應造成的短期影響，填補了此特定年齡層在短期UPF飲食干預下大腦反應的初步研究空白。然而，研究結果亦指出，需要進一步探討晚期青少年對飲食中超加工食品引起的大腦反應變化的潛在脆弱性，且本研究為急性（短期）干預，長期影響仍待釐清。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effects of Controlled Diets High in and Free of Ultraprocessed Food on the Brain of Emerging Adults.
**Abstract:** The average American consumes 55% of daily energy from ultraprocessed foods (UPF), created through industrial processes and additives not used at home. We investigated if a high-UPF diet alters brain response to milkshake compared with a diet free from UPF in emerging adults, who are in a critical brain development period and typically consume high amounts of UPF. In a randomized controlled crossover trial participants aged 18-25 years completed two 2-week controlled feeding periods including a UPF (81% UPF) and non-UPF (0% UPF) diet. Before and after each diet intervention, participants consumed milkshake concomitant with functional magnetic resonance imaging. In the entire cohort, there were no differences between diet conditions in brain response. An exploratory analysis revealed orbitofrontal cortex response to milkshake decreased after the UPF diet and increased following the non-UPF diet in late adolescents (18-21 years) but not young adults (22-25 years). In all participants, habitual UPF intake (g) was positively associated with orbitofrontal cortex response to milkshake, independent of diet condition. An acute UPF dietary intervention may only alter brain response in late adolescents. Further work is needed to determine potential vulnerability of late adolescents to changes in dietary UPF on brain response to rewards. ClinicalTrials.gov identifier NCT05550818.
