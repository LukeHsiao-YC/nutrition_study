### 重點摘要
兒童胃輕癱是一種嚴重疾病，目前缺乏多樣且有效的治療選項，而胃幽門肉毒桿菌毒素注射 (IPBT) 作為一種潛在療法，其在兒童中的應用證據有限。本回顧旨在匯總 IPBT 治療兒童胃輕癱的現有文獻，主要關注症狀改善和治療反應預測因子。研究發現，大多數文獻雖報告IPBT注射後症狀有所改善，但這些結論來自小型、回顧性且缺乏對照組的研究，且客觀預測因子未能可靠預測治療反應。因此，未來需要更多前瞻性、對照性研究來強化 IPBT 治療兒童胃輕癱的有效性結論，並明確其在臨床實踐中的作用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了針對兒童胃輕癱使用胃幽門肉毒桿菌毒素注射的現有證據的總結性空白，但同時也揭示了當前研究設計的局限性，例如缺乏高品質的前瞻性、對照組研究，以及客觀治療反應預測因子的不足。未來的研究需要克服這些限制，以提供更堅實的臨床證據並確立 IPBT 的臨床應用地位。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Efficacy and safety of intrapyloric botulinum toxin injection in pediatrics: Current evidence and future directions.
**Abstract:** Pediatric gastroparesis is a debilitating disease requiring more diverse and effective treatment options. Intrapyloric botulinum toxin (IPBT) injection has emerged as a therapeutic option, although evidence supporting its use in children remains limited. The aim of this review is to summarize the current literature on IPBT injection for pediatric gastroparesis, focusing on symptomatic outcomes and potential predictors of response. Overall, most studies report some degree of symptomatic improvement following the injection; however, these findings are derived from small, retrospective cohorts without control groups. Objective predictors of response evaluated include gastric emptying scintigraphy, which did not show correlation with treatment response. Similarly, antroduodenal manometry general impressions and endoscopic functional luminal probe parameters did not reliably predict symptomatic response to the injection. Several studies describe repeated injections, although protocols for repeat are unclear, and the response is varied. Body surface gastric mapping represents a novel modality that may provide gastric physiological data allowing its use as a biomarker guiding IPBT injection, although pediatric data is still limited. Other emerging modalities, including gastric emptying breath test and gastric magnetic resonance imaging, may further enhance assessment of gastroparesis and treatment response. The current pediatric evidence on IPBT injection for gastroparesis is limited by suboptimal study designs and lack of objective assessments of treatment response. Future prospective, controlled studies are needed to strengthen conclusions on IPBT injection efficacy for pediatric gastroparesis and to clarify its role in clinical practice.
