### 重點摘要
這項隨機對照研究旨在評估罹癌營養不良兒童的微量營養素缺乏盛行率、其臨床影響以及即食治療食品 (RUTF) 補充的效果。研究發現，罹癌兒童普遍存在微量營養素（特別是維生素 D3、鋅、磷和鐵）缺乏，且這些缺乏與治療延遲、毒性死亡等不良臨床結果相關。儘管 RUTF 補充對鋅和維生素 B12 濃度顯示出不一致的改善，但未能全面解決所有微量營養素問題。本研究強調未來需要更多介入性試驗，以開發針對罹癌兒童的實證微量營養素補充策略。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究雖然確認了罹癌營養不良兒童微量營養素缺乏的普遍性及其與不良臨床結果的關聯，並初步探討了 RUTF 的效果，但其結果顯示 RUTF 對微量營養素水平的改善「不一致」。這突顯出目前仍缺乏針對罹癌兒童的全面且具實證基礎的微量營養素補充策略，未來研究應聚焦於開發更有效、更一致的介入性試驗來填補此研究空白。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** The impact of ready-to-use-therapeutic-food (RUTF) on micronutrient levels in undernourished children with cancer: a randomized open-labelled controlled study.
**Abstract:** Emerging evidence suggests that micronutrient deficiencies are predictive of poorer outcomes in children with cancer. We assessed the prevalence and clinical impact of circulating micronutrient concentrations, as well as the change in micronutrient status after RUTF supplementation, in undernourished children with cancer. Undernourished children with cancer were randomized 1:1 to receive either standard nutritional therapy(SNT) or SNT + RUTF for 6 weeks. The primary outcome was weight gain, and secondary outcomes included changes in micronutrient status from study entry to 6 weeks, including serum copper, phosphorus, zinc, iron, B12 and 25-OH vitamin D3. Predefined laboratory reference cut-offs for low circulating micronutrient concentrations were applied. Clinical outcomes assessed were significant infection, grade 3-4 mucositis and treatment delay >1 week. Between July-2015 and March-2018, 260 subjects were enrolled; 191 (76.7%) had low circulating concentrations of at least one micronutrient and 37 (14.9%) low circulating concentrations of 3 or more micronutrients. Low circulating concentrations were noted with vitamin D3-111 (42.7%), serum zinc in 97 (37.3%), phosphorus-62 (23.8%) and iron-45 (18%). Fewer children who received RUTF-34 (27.6%) had low zinc concentrations compared to SNT-45 (35.7%, p = 0.045) and 35 (25.5%) had improvement in vitamin B12 concentrations compared to SNT-23 (18.3%, p = 0.045). Irrespective of treatment arm, low circulating zinc and copper at study entry were associated with treatment delay (OR 1.8; 95% CI 1.1-3.2 and OR 4.7; 95% CI 0.4-53.2, respectively). Low circulating vitamin B12 was associated with toxic death (OR 9.7; 95% CI 2.4-38.8). There is a high prevalence of low circulating micronutrient concentrations, specifically vitamin D3, zinc, phosphorus and iron in children with cancer, often associated with adverse clinical outcomes. Supplementation with RUTF showed an inconsistent improvement in micronutrient levels. The clinical impact of micronutrient deficiencies in children with cancer should be the focus of future interventional trials. There is a need for evidence-based micronutrient supplementation in children with cancer.
