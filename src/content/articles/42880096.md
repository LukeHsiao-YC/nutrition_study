### 重點摘要
過去研究雖指出肌少症與粒線體能量、脂質重塑及肉鹼相關途徑有關，但更廣泛的代謝途徑仍不明。本研究整合代謝體全基因組孟德爾隨機化(MR)分析與患者血清及骨骼肌多體學分析，旨在識別肌少症相關代謝途徑。結果顯示，離胺酸降解是唯一與負向肢體瘦肉量(ALM)相關的代謝途徑，且多體學分析揭示了其中三甲基離胺酸衍生的肉鹼代謝具有最一致的跨體學模式。這些發現支持離胺酸相關代謝作為肌少症的候選代謝框架，但仍需進一步的機制和縱向驗證。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究透過整合孟德爾隨機化與多體學分析，首次將離胺酸降解途徑確定為與肌少症（特別是肢體瘦肉量）相關的代謝框架，填補了過去對於肌少症更廣泛代謝途徑理解不足的空白。然而，文章也指出所發現的是異質且區室特異性的代謝重塑，而非整個途徑的統一改變，且研究本身呼籲需要進一步的機制性和縱向驗證，這代表對其因果關係和長期影響仍存在限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Lysine degradation-related metabolic remodeling in sarcopenia: Mendelian randomization prioritization and multi-omics-based characterization.
**Abstract:** Previous metabolomic studies have implicated mitochondrial energy metabolism, lipid remodeling, and carnitine-related pathways in sarcopenia, but broader metabolic pathways linking these abnormalities remain unclear. We aimed to identify sarcopenia-related metabolic pathways by integrating metabolome-wide Mendelian Randomization (MR) with patient-derived serum and skeletal muscle multi-omics analysis. Two-sample MR analyses were performed using genome-wide association study summary statistics for 1400 circulating metabolite-related traits and three sarcopenia-related traits, appendicular lean mass (ALM), handgrip strength, and walking speed. Candidate metabolites retained after MR sensitivity filtering were subjected to Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes (KEGG)-based pathway enrichment. The MR-prioritized pathway was then examined in an independent cohort of 40 postmenopausal women with or without sarcopenia using untargeted serum and skeletal muscle metabolomics. Patient-derived and the external public skeletal muscle transcriptomic datasets were analyzed to evaluate pathway-related transcriptomic patterns. Lysine degradation was the only pathway meeting the predefined pathway-level FDR criterion in the ALM-negative direction. Lysine, carnitine, and N6,N6,N6-trimethyllysine contributed nominal negative MR associations to this pathway. Colocalization identified strong evidence of a shared causal signal for one carnitine locus (rs1171617; PP.H4 = 0.995). Multi-omics characterization indicated heterogeneous and compartment-specific remodeling rather than uniform alteration of the entire pathway, with the most coherent cross-omics pattern involving trimethyllysine-derived carnitine metabolism. These findings support lysine-related metabolism as a candidate metabolic framework for sarcopenia while warranting further mechanistic and longitudinal validation.
