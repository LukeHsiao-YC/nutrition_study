### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估Upadacitinib (UPA) 在兒童發炎性腸道疾病(IBD) 患者中從起始劑量降階至維持劑量的耐受性、持久性及復發預測因子。這項回溯性研究顯示，35% 的降階患者在降階後中位數 37.5 天內復發，但75%的復發患者在重新升階後恢復反應。研究發現，高治療前PUCAI分數和低血紅素是預測復發的因子。總體而言，UPA降階對大多數兒童IBD患者是安全的，且重新升階治療能有效恢復臨床反應。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了Upadacitinib在兒童發炎性腸道疾病中從起始劑量降階至維持劑量的臨床安全性、有效性及其復發預測因子（如治療前PUCAI分數和降階時血紅素水平）的研究空白，並指出重新升階治療能有效恢復反應。然而，本研究為回溯性且樣本量相對較小，未來需要更大規模的前瞻性研究來進一步驗證這些發現並探索長期結果。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Upadacitinib durability and clinical relapse following step-down from induction to maintenance dosing in pediatric inflammatory bowel disease.
**Abstract:** To evaluate upadacitinib (UPA) durability, tolerance of step-down from induction to maintenance dosing, and predictors of relapse in pediatric inflammatory bowel disease (IBD). This retrospective study included children with Crohn's disease (CD) or ulcerative colitis (UC) who initiated UPA. The primary outcome was clinical relapse following step-down. Predictors were evaluated using univariate Firth's penalized logistic regression and receiver operating characteristic curves. Overall, 49 of 53 patients completed induction, after which 15 patients remained on induction dosing and 34 patients underwent step-down to maintenance dosing at a median of 12.1 weeks. For patients who completed induction, 84% remained on UPA at 12 months. Maintenance dosing was continued in 65% (22/34), while 35% (12/34) relapsed at a median of 37.5 days following step-down. Re-escalation to induction dosing recaptured response in 75% (9/12) of patients. Higher pre-treatment pediatric ulcerative colitis activity index (PUCAI) was predictive of UC relapse after step-down (odds ratio [OR] 1.05; p = 0.027). At step-down, lower hemoglobin also predicted relapse (OR 0.45; p = 0.042). At 12 months, endoscopic remission was achieved in 87% (13/15) of the step-down group. Adverse events occurred in 21% of patients (11/53), primarily consisting of acne or rosacea (9 patients, 18%). Among 13 patients who initiated UPA while hospitalized, 11 remained surgery-free at 12 months. UPA step-down from induction to maintenance dosing is safe in most pediatric patients with IBD. Pre-treatment PUCAI and hemoglobin at step-down can help stratify risk prior to step-down. Re-escalation to induction dosing reliably recaptures clinical response in most patients.
