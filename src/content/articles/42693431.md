### 重點摘要
儘管DSM-5-TR將圍產期憂鬱症與重度憂鬱症視為症狀上無區別，但有證據表明孕婦、產後婦女和非圍產期婦女的憂鬱症狀表現可能有所不同。本研究利用2005-2021年NHANES數據，對632名孕婦、385名產後婦女和6,503名非圍產期婦女進行了PHQ-9量表的測量不變性(MI)和差異項目功能(DIF)分析。結果支持PHQ-9在這些群體中具有測量不變性，且雖然發現了統計學上顯著的差異項目功能，但其臨床相關性極小。這表明PHQ-9在這些群體中能等效評估憂鬱症狀，允許有意義的比較，且症狀偏差的缺乏不支持將圍產期憂鬱症與DSM-5-TR重度憂鬱症區分開來。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究透過嚴謹的統計分析，填補了關於孕婦、產後婦女和非圍產期婦女憂鬱症狀評估工具（PHQ-9）的測量等效性是否一致的空白，並挑戰了圍產期憂鬱症具有獨特症狀表現的觀點。它證明了在同等憂鬱嚴重程度下，不同群體間PHQ-9項目反應的差異微乎其微，並不支持將圍產期憂鬱症與重度憂鬱症在症狀層面上加以區分。

**Title:** Depressive symptom profiles across pregnant, postpartum, and nonperipartum women: measurement invariance and differential item functioning analyses of 2005-2021 NHANES data.
**Abstract:** The DSM-5-TR classifies peripartum depression as symptomatically indistinguishable from major depressive disorder (MDD). However, evidence suggests that depressive symptom profiles may differ among pregnant, postpartum, and nonperipartum (NPP) women. This study used 2005-2021 National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey data to evaluate Patient Health Questionnaire-9 (PHQ-9) depressive symptoms and assessed measurement equivalence across these groups. Measurement invariance (MI) and differential item functioning (DIF) analyses included 632 pregnant, 385 postpartum, and 6,503 NPP women (aged 15-49 years). After confirming the PHQ-9 factor structure, we evaluated MI using multigroup confirmatory factor analysis (MGCFA) and assessed DIF using ordinal logistic regression and item response theory. The cognitive-affective factor included anhedonia, depressed mood, guilt/worthlessness, concentration difficulty, psychomotor changes, and suicidal ideation.
