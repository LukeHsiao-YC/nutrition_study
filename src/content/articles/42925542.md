### 重點摘要
危害比（Hazard Ratio, HR）是使用 Cox 比例危害模型估計，廣泛用於描述共變數對時間至事件結果的關聯或效應。然而，近期有學者質疑即使在隨機試驗中且符合比例危害假設，危害比仍缺乏因果解釋，這引發了廣泛關注。本文旨在回顧這些批判，並提供如何正確解釋危害比的指導。鑑於其解釋上的挑戰，文章建議未來可考慮使用其他替代測量方法來描述暴露或治療對時間至事件結果的影響。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了危害比（Hazard Ratio, HR）在因果解釋上的爭議與困惑，為研究人員提供了解釋危害比的明確指導。未來的研究應考量其在因果推論上的潛在局限性，並探索更合適的時間至事件結果測量方法。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Hazard ratios and their causal interpretation: challenges and guidance.
**Abstract:** The hazard ratio, typically estimated using Cox's famous proportional hazards model, is the most common effect measure used to describe the association or effect of a covariate on a time-to-event outcome. In recent years, the hazard ratio has been argued by some to lack a causal interpretation, even in randomized trials, and even if the proportional hazards assumption holds. This is concerning, not least due to the ubiquity of hazard ratios in analyses of time-to-event data. We review these critiques and describe how we think hazard ratios should be interpreted. In light of the issues involved, alternative measures may be preferable to describe effects of exposures or treatments on time-to-event outcomes.
