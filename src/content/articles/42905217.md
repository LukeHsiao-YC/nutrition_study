### 重點摘要
鑑於圍產期心理健康篩查對母嬰健康的重要性，本研究旨在土耳其驗證世界衛生組織五項幸福感量表（WHO-5）在334名孕婦中的心理計量特性，並探討相關因素。研究通過信度、效度及迴歸分析，證實WHO-5具有高內部一致性與單一因子結構，並確定截止分數為18。結果顯示，幸福感隨年齡增長而下降，未定期產檢及有健康問題的孕婦幸福感較低。這些發現支持WHO-5在評估孕期幸福感方面的有效性和可靠性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了在孕婦群體中缺乏經實證驗證的心理幸福感評估工具的研究空白，證明了WHO-5對於孕期心理健康篩查的適用性。然而，研究地點僅限於土耳其，未來的研究可能需要在不同文化和地理背景下進一步驗證其普適性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Validation of the World Health Organization 5 (WHO-5) Well-Being Index in Pregnant Women: Psychometric Properties and Associated Factors.
**Abstract:** Conducting mental health screenings during the perinatal period is crucial for maternal and child health and requires valid and reliable instruments appropriate for the characteristics of pregnant women. The aim of this study is to validate the World Health Organization-Five Well-Being Index (WHO-5) among pregnant women and to identify related factors. The study included 334 pregnant women in Türkiye and used the WHO-5, Psychological Well-Being and Edinburgh Postpartum Depression scales to collect data. Reliability and validity were assessed through Cronbach's alpha, McDonald's Omega, confirmatory factor analysis, and receiver operating characteristic (ROC) analysis. Robust regression analysis was used to identify factors associated with well-being, with multicollinearity assessed via Variance Inflation Factors (VIFs). The WHO-5 demonstrated high internal consistency (Cronbach's α = 0.844; McDonald's ω = 0.868), and its single-factor structure was confirmed. ROC analysis identified a cutoff score of 18, with a sensitivity of 69.68% and specificity of 49.32%. Regression analysis showed that well-being decreased with increasing age and was lower among women who did not attend regular prenatal check-ups, whereas pregnant women without health problems had higher well-being levels. These findings support the validity and reliability of the WHO-5 for assessing well-being during pregnancy.
