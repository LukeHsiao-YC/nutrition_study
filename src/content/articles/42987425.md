### 重點摘要
這篇系統性回顧旨在探討減重飲食如何影響超重與肥胖成年人的循環微RNA（miRNA）表現，以理解其在代謝適應中的作用。研究透過廣泛的資料庫搜尋，最終納入9項隨機臨床試驗，分析其飲食介入與循環miRNA的數據。結果顯示，共發現32種不同的循環miRNA表達差異，且這些miRNA的改變與體重減輕、脂肪量、血糖、血脂及發炎反應相關，生物資訊分析也指出它們參與了發炎、胰島素訊號和脂質代謝相關途徑。本研究結論指出減重飲食能調節循環miRNA譜，可能影響糖脂代謝和發炎反應，並支持這些miRNA作為代謝健康潛在生物標誌物的潛力。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇系統性回顧填補了減重飲食如何系統性地影響超重和肥胖成年人循環微RNA譜的知識空白，綜合了零散的研究證據，並確認了miRNA在代謝調節中的潛在作用。然而，研究中的飲食方案（特別是巨量營養素組成）存在顯著異質性，這限制了對特定飲食模式與miRNA變化之間精確關係的歸納，也為未來更精確的生物標誌物研究帶來挑戰。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effects of Weight-Loss Diets on Circulating MicroRNA Profiles in Overweight and Obese Adults: Systematic Review of Randomized Clinical Trials.
**Abstract:** Obesity, particularly visceral adiposity, is associated with metabolic inflammation and increased cardiometabolic risk. Dietary interventions are essential for weight loss and improved metabolic health; however, individual responses vary widely. Circulating microRNAs are key epigenetic mediators of metabolic adaptation, although their variable expression patterns often preclude a unified biological interpretation. This systematic review aims to synthesize evidence on how weight-loss diets affect the circulating microRNA profile in adults with overweight or obesity. PubMed Central, LILACS, Scopus, Embase, Web of Science, ClinicalTrials.gov, and Google Scholar were searched without language or publication date restrictions through May 2026. Of 4383 records identified, 12 articles representing 9 independent randomized clinical trials met the eligibility criteria. Data on study characteristics, dietary interventions, and circulating microRNA expression profiles were extracted. Risk of bias was assessed using the Cochrane Risk of Bias 2.0 tool. The included studies were conducted in 6 countries and evaluated dietary interventions lasting 2-18 months. A total of 32 distinct circulating microRNAs were differentially expressed, with miR-221-3p, miR-223, miR-99b, miR-122, and miR-128 reported in more than one study. Considerable heterogeneity was observed among the dietary protocols, particularly regarding macronutrient composition. Alterations in microRNA expression were associated with weight loss, adiposity, glycemic parameters, lipid profile, inflammation, and cardiovascular risk. Bioinformatic analyses indicated enrichment of pathways related to inflammation, insulin signaling, and lipid metabolism, including the PI3K-Akt and MAPK pathways, suggesting an integrated role for these microRNAs in metabolic regulation. Weight-loss diets modulate the circulating miRNA profile, potentially influencing glycolipid metabolism and inflammation, supporting their role as candidate biomarkers. PROSPERO registration No. CRD42024580048.
