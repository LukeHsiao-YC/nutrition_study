### 重點摘要
育兒壓力與學齡前兒童挑食行為相關，但其透過父母自我效能（PSE）不同維度的潛在路徑仍未被充分探索。本橫斷面研究納入中國烏魯木齊的459對親子，分析育兒壓力、一般PSE（效能與滿意度）及挑食嚴重程度之間的關係。結果顯示，育兒壓力透過一般PSE的效能和滿意度雙重路徑部分中介了與挑食的關聯，其中父母情感上的滿意度是更強的路徑。本研究支持社會認知理論中情感路徑在餵養中的作用，並建議未來干預應同時提升父母能力與照護滿足感。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了育兒壓力如何透過父母自我效能的「不同維度」（效能與滿意度）影響兒童挑食的知識空白。然而，本研究為橫斷面設計，未來需要縱向研究來驗證這些探索性路徑，且觀察到的效應量較小，實踐意義需謹慎解釋。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Parenting Stress and Picky Eating Severity in Preschoolers: An Exploratory Cross-Sectional Analysis of Potential Pathways via Parental Self-Efficacy.
**Abstract:** Parenting stress links to preschoolers' picky eating, yet underlying pathways via parental self-efficacy (PSE) dimensions remain underexplored. This study examined whether general parenting self-efficacy, comprising efficacy and satisfaction dimensions, statistically mediates the association between parenting stress and picky eating severity, acknowledging that the measure assesses overall parenting competence rather than domain-specific feeding self-efficacy. This cross-sectional study involved 459 parent-child dyads from Urumqi, China. Using two-phase data collection, we assessed parenting stress, general PSE (efficacy and satisfaction), and picky eating. Hayes' PROCESS macro (Model 4) explored parallel mediation, adjusting for child age, caregiver education, and family type. Sensitivity analyses confirmed robustness across bootstrap specifications. Parenting stress correlated negatively with efficacy and satisfaction, which inversely associated with picky eating severity. Total association between stress and picky eating was significant (b=0.047). Direct association remained significant but attenuated after mediation (b=0.023). Both efficacy (b=0.008) and satisfaction (b=0.016) showed significant indirect associations, jointly explaining 51.24% of the total relationship. Notably, satisfaction's indirect pathway was approximately twice as strong as efficacy's (contrast analysis confirmed this difference was statistically significant). Parenting stress associates with picky eating partially through dual general PSE pathways, with emotional satisfaction being the stronger link. Effect sizes were small and may be partly attributable to the large sample size (N = 459), warranting cautious interpretation of practical significance. Findings support social cognitive theory, highlighting affective pathways in feeding. Interventions should enhance both parental competence and caregiving fulfillment. Longitudinal research is needed to validate these exploratory pathways.
