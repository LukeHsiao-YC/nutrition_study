### 重點摘要
兒童慢性腎臟病 (CKD) 會對父母造成較高壓力和焦慮。本研究旨在評估CKD患兒父母是否認為其子女更脆弱並採取更多保護行為，同時探討子女脆弱感知與父母壓力、焦慮及其他變數的關聯。研究比較了45位CKD患兒父母與45位健康兒童父母，發現CKD患兒父母確實認為其子女更脆弱，但並未表現出更高程度的過度保護行為。此外，在CKD組中，子女脆弱感知與育兒壓力及焦慮呈正相關，且兒童合併其他疾病與更高的子女脆弱感知有關。這些發現強調了在兒科腎臟病科實施心理社會干預措施以支持父母情緒調適的重要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究釐清了兒童慢性腎臟病患兒父母對子女脆弱感的認知與實際過度保護行為之間的關係，發現父母雖感知到高脆弱性但並未過度保護，這挑戰了直接因果關係的假設。此外，它揭示了兒童脆弱感知與育兒壓力及焦慮（特別是當兒童合併其他疾病時）之間的關聯性，為兒科腎臟病領域的心理社會干預提供了實證基礎和具體介入方向。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Perceived Child Vulnerability and Protective Behavior by Parents of Children with Chronic Kidney Disease.
**Abstract:** Chronic kidney disease (CKD) in childhood not only impacts the child itself, but may also result in higher stress and anxiety in parents. This study aims to assess whether these parents perceive their child as more vulnerable and use more protective parenting behavior, compared to parents of healthy children. Additionally, it explores the associations of perceived child vulnerability (PCV) and overprotection (OP) with parenting stress and anxiety, and various clinical and sociodemographic variables. Both parent groups completed a sociodemographic questionnaire, the Child Vulnerability Scale, and the Parental Overprotection Scale. The Parenting Stress Index-Short Form and the Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale were simultaneous collected but analyzed and reported before. The study included 45 parents (32 mothers) of 45 CKD patients (ages 0-18; median age 8; 16 girls). Compared with the control group (N = 45; 35 mothers), parents of CKD patients perceived their children as more vulnerable but did not exhibit higher levels of overprotective behavior. In the CKD group, parenting stress and anxiety were positively correlated with PCV, and medical comorbidities in the child were associated with higher PCV. Parents of CKD patients perceive their children as more vulnerable than parents of healthy children, yet they do not exhibit higher levels of overprotectiveness. Higher PCV was associated with increased parenting stress and anxiety, especially in parents of children with medical comorbidities. These findings underscore the importance of implementing psychosocial interventions targeting these parental adjustment outcomes in pediatric nephrology departments.
