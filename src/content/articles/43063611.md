### 重點摘要
這項隨機、雙盲、安慰劑對照交叉試驗旨在評估丁酸鹽與棕櫚醯乙醇胺複合配方（BP 3:1）對腸躁症（IBS）患者症狀及腸道微生物組的影響。研究結果顯示，BP 3:1顯著改善了腹脹、腸鳴和噁心等特定IBS症狀，儘管整體IBS嚴重度評分僅呈現具臨床意義但未達統計顯著的下降。值得注意的是，該配方在不改變腸道微生物分類組成的情況下，調節了微生物的代謝潛力，特別是增加了甲硫胺酸生物合成相關途徑。這些發現支持BP 3:1透過功能性微生物組介導的機制改善IBS症狀，為IBS的輔助治療提供了新見解。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了腸道菌群介入在腸躁症治療中，可能不需改變其物種組成，而是透過調節其功能性代謝潛力來產生臨床效益的知識空白。它證明了丁酸鹽與棕櫚醯乙醇胺的複合配方能夠改善特定腸躁症症狀，並透過功能性而非分類學上的微生物組調節機制發揮作用，挑戰了傳統上單純關注微生物組成變化的觀點。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Butyrate and palmitoylethanolamide: Exploring their metabolic potential in explaining clinical improvement in irritable bowel syndrome patients, data from a randomised, placebo-controlled trial.
**Abstract:** Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) is a disorder of gut-brain interaction in which gut microbiome plays a major role. Butyrate and palmitoylethanolamide (PEA) exert anti-inflammatory and neuromodulator effects and may represent promising adjunctive treatments. We evaluated the clinical and microbiome-related effects of a combined formulation of sodium butyrate and PEA (BP 3:1) in patients with IBS. In a randomized, double-blnd, placebo-controlled, 2 × 2 crossover trial, adults with diarrhoea-predominant or mixed IBS received BP 3:1 or placebo for two 6-week periods separated by washout. Gastrointestinal symptoms, IBS Severity Scoring System (IBS-SSS), intestinal permeability, and safety were assessed. Fecal microbiota composition and predicted functional potential were analyzed using 16S-rRNA sequencing and PICRUSt2. Forty-four patients completed the study. Alpha and beta diversity and taxonomic composition remained stable. In contrast, functional profiling revealed treatment-specific enrichment of microbial pathways involved in methionine and S-adenosyl-L-methionine biosynthesis, while placebo was associated with pathways related to complex carbohydrate degradation. BP 3:1 significantly improved abdominal bloating (p = 0.020), stomach gurgling (p = 0.014) and nausea (p = 0.030) compared with placebo, with consistent estimates in mixed-effects models adjusting for period and sequence effects, while IBS-SSS showed a clinically relevant but non-significant numerical reduction (p = 0.070). No effects were observed on intestinal permeability. Among clinically improving patients, BP 3:1 was associated with modulation of polyphenol-degrading, biosynthetic and proteolytic microbial pathways. BP 3:1 was well tolerated, with a safety profile comparable to placebo. BP 3:1 significantly improves IBS symptoms and modulates microbial metabolic potential without inducing a quantitative and/or qualitative alteration in the taxonomic composition of the microbiota. These findings support a functional microbiome-mediated effect in IBS. gov NCT06432569 (https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06432569).
