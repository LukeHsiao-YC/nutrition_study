### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討墨西哥尤卡坦地區泌尿道結石患者的飲食酸負荷(DAL)及其決定因素。研究團隊對53名患者進行橫斷面研究，評估了淨內源性酸產生(NEAP)、潛在腎臟酸負荷(PRAL)和淨腎臟酸排泄(NAE)等指標。結果顯示，男性患者的NEAP、PRAL和NAE均顯著高於女性。此外，這些飲食酸負荷指標與多種膳食攝取（如鉀、鎂、脂肪、蛋白質、鈣、磷、液體）、身體組成（體重、腰圍、BMI、體脂肪、肌肉量）及血壓等健康指標呈現顯著關聯。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究為墨西哥尤卡坦地區泌尿道結石患者的飲食酸負荷及其決定因素提供了具體數據，填補了該特定人群及地區的相關研究空白。然而，由於是橫斷面研究且樣本量相對較小(n=53)，無法確立因果關係，其結果的推廣性也可能受限。未來的研究可進一步探討飲食介入對泌尿道結石患者酸負荷的影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Dietary determinants of acid load in a population with urolithiasis from Yucatán, Mexico.
**Abstract:** Dietary acid load (DAL) is a factor that predisposes individuals to the development of urolithiasis (UL). A cross-sectional study was conducted including individuals with UL (n = 53). We estimated DAL using the net endogenous acid production (NEAP) (90.6 [68.8-110] mEq/d), potential renal acid load (PRAL) (13 [0.07-20] mEq/d), and net renal acid excretion (NAE) (45 [30-55.3] mEq/d) indices in individuals with UL. Compared with females, male participants had higher NEAP, PRAL and NAE (p < 0.05). NEAP was associated with potassium, magnesium and lipid intake, body weight (BW), waist circumference (WC), hip circumference (HC), body mass index (BMI), fat mass, muscle mass, systolic blood pressure (SBP), and diastolic blood pressure (DBP). NAE was correlated with total energy, protein, lipid, phosphorus and fluid intake, BW, WC, HC, BMI, waist-to-height ratio, fat mass, muscle mass, SBP and DBP, and PRAL with total energy, protein, lipid, calcium, and phosphorus intake, as well as BW, WC, HC, muscle mass, and SBP.
