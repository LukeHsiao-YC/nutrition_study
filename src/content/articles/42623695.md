### 重點摘要
本研究旨在利用健康行政資料庫中預先驗證的機率性病例確認模型，估計阻塞性睡眠呼吸中止症 (OSA) 的人口水平盛行率及相關因素。研究首先以1.8萬名PSG確診的臨床隊列評估誤分類，然後將機率模型應用於安大略省83萬名接受PSG檢查的成年人。結果顯示，引導式估計法 (BIA) 在盛行率估計中偏差最小，推估省級PSG隊列中度至重度OSA盛行率為44.3%，並透過情境分析推估一般人群盛行率為3.4%-17.1%。這項研究證實，儘管缺乏臨床診斷數據，使用健康行政數據的機率性病例確認方法，仍能穩健地估計OSA的人口水平流行病學。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了在缺乏黃金標準PSG數據的情況下，利用健康行政資料庫進行阻塞性睡眠呼吸中止症 (OSA) 人口水平盛行率估計的空白。然而，其對一般人群的盛行率預測仍需依賴於假定的OSA診斷不足率，這限制了對實際全面人口盛行率的精確判斷。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** A population-based study of obstructive sleep apnea: using probabilistic case ascertainment through health administrative databases.
**Abstract:** Although polysomnography (PSG) is the diagnostic gold standard, PSG data on obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) presence and severity are rarely available at the population level. Health administrative databases offer broad coverage for OSA case ascertainment but require methods that address inherent misclassification. We utilized a previously validated probabilistic case ascertainment model of OSA to estimate population-level prevalence and correlates of OSA. We conducted a population-based retrospective cohort study in Ontario, Canada. First, using a clinical cohort with PSG-confirmed OSA severity (measurement cohort; n = 18 581), we quantified misclassification in probabilistic OSA case definitions. We then applied the externally validated probability models to all adults 18 years and older in Ontario who underwent in-laboratory overnight PSG between 2010 and 2018 (provincial PSG cohort; n = 834 361). OSA prevalence and correlates were estimated using multiple probability thresholds and a threshold-free bootstrap imputation approach (BIA). Compared to the PSG-based gold standard, the BIA surrogate definition had the lowest average relative bias in OSA prevalence estimation (BIA: 5% versus >38% probability threshold: 11.4%). Using BIA, we estimate a 44.3% prevalence of moderate-severe OSA in the provincial PSG cohort, which in scenario-based projections yields a 3.4%-17.1% general population prevalence dependent on an assumed OSA underdiagnosis rate. OSA prevalence was higher in men, older ages, and previous comorbidity and was stable annually. Probabilistic case ascertainment using health administrative data enables robust population-level estimation of OSA epidemiology despite the absence of clinical diagnostic data and provides a framework for studying OSA prevalence, correlates, and outcomes over time.
