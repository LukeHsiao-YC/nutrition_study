### 重點摘要
背景方面，ω-3脂肪酸被認為具有抗發炎和改善血流動力學的作用，但其在高血壓和血脂異常患者中的綜合影響尚未完全釐清。本研究是一項探索性隨機雙盲安慰劑對照試驗，為期12週，對100名正在接受常規藥物治療的成人，每日補充2克ω-3脂肪酸或安慰劑。結果顯示，與安慰劑組相比，ω-3補充與嗜中性粒細胞淋巴細胞比值(NLR)的顯著降低相關。儘管研究識別出NLR、鐵蛋白及部分血壓測量等探索性信號，但由於樣本量有限及結果完整性不足，這些發現尚需在更大規模的試驗中進行獨立驗證。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻初步探討了ω-3在高血壓和血脂異常患者中，對發炎生物標誌物和血壓的綜合影響，為此領域提供了初步的探索性數據。然而，由於本研究的試驗性質為探索性、樣本量有限、多重比較問題以及結果可用性不完整，其結果不能作為確證性解釋。未來的研究需要更大規模、預先註冊且終點明確的試驗，以獨立驗證這些初步發現並提供更堅實的證據。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effects of Omega-3 Supplementation on Inflammatory Biomarkers and Ambulatory and Central Blood Pressure in Patients Treated for Hypertension and Dyslipidemia: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial.
**Abstract:** Omega-3 fatty acids exert anti-inflammatory and hemodynamic effects; however, their concurrent effects in patients receiving treatment for hypertension and dyslipidemia remain incompletely characterized. To evaluate the effects of 12 weeks of supplementation with pharmaceutical-grade omega-3 fatty acids on inflammatory biomarkers, central and brachial blood pressure including office and 24 h ambulatory measurements, and vascular function in this population. In this exploratory, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled pilot trial, 100 adults received omega-3 ethyl esters at a capsule dose of 2 g/day or a matching placebo for 12 weeks, in addition to stable usual pharmacological treatment. Treatment effects were estimated using linear mixed-effects models.  Compared with placebo, omega-3 supplementation was associated with greater reductions in the neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio (NLR; -0.43; 95% CI, -0.73 to -0.13;  The trial identified exploratory signals involving NLR, ferritin, selected ambulatory blood-pressure measures, central systolic blood pressure, and algorithm-derived ePWV. Multiplicity, limited sample size, and incomplete outcome availability preclude confirmatory interpretation. Independent replication in larger, prospectively registered trials with clearly specified endpoints and reproducible sample-size calculations is required.
