### 重點摘要
該研究旨在探討中國西南部地區成人教育程度與骨骼健康（經QUS定義）之間的關聯，並探究其地域異質性及潛在的間接影響因素。這項橫斷面研究納入了來自中國多民族隊列的73,600名成年人，採用定量超音波（QUS）評估骨骼狀況，並使用累積對數序迴歸和省份特異性路徑分析。結果顯示，教育程度與骨骼狀況的關聯存在顯著地域差異，例如在四川、貴州和西藏，教育程度較高與骨骼狀況較差相關，而在重慶則與較佳骨骼狀況相關。研究證實了中國西南部教育程度與骨骼狀況之間存在省份特異性關聯，但間接影響因素的確認仍需長期研究。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
該研究填補了教育程度與骨骼健康關聯在不同地理和社會經濟背景下存在顯著異質性的研究空白，挑戰了教育普遍有利於骨骼健康的傳統觀念。然而，作為一項橫斷面研究，其所揭示的社會經濟、行為和飲食因素的間接關聯仍需透過縱向研究進一步確認，以建立因果關係。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Geographic heterogeneity in the association between educational attainment and QUS-defined bone status among adults in Southwest China: a cross-sectional study.
**Abstract:** Although education is commonly regarded as a favorable factor for bone health, its association with bone status may vary across different socioeconomic and ecological contexts. We examined the association between educational attainment and QUS (quantitative ultrasound)-defined bone status across five provinces in Southwest China and explored province-specific indirect statistical associations. This cross-sectional study included 73,600 adults aged ≥ 30 years from Sichuan, Chongqing, Guizhou, Yunnan, and Tibet, recruited through a multistage, stratified cluster sampling design within the China Multi-Ethnic Cohort. QUS-defined bone status was classified as normal, reduced, and markedly reduced using calcaneal quantitative ultrasound-derived T-scores. Geographic effect modification was evaluated using cumulative logit ordinal regression. Province-specific path analysis was further conducted to explore potential indirect associations involving health behaviors, nutritional factors, and socioeconomic status. Partial proportional-odds models were applied in sensitivity analyses. Participants had a mean age of 51.54 years (SD, 11.43), and 62.1% were women. Overall, 60.6%, 35.0%, and 4.4% of participants were classified into the normal, reduced, and markedly reduced QUS-defined bone-status categories. Education showed marked regional heterogeneity in its association with bone status (P < 0.001): higher educational attainment was associated with poorer bone status in Sichuan (β=-0.088, P < 0.05), Guizhou (β=-0.047, P < 0.05), and Tibet (β=-0.067, P < 0.05), better bone status in Chongqing (β = 0.045, P < 0.05), and no association in Yunnan (β = 0.015, P = 0.155). Path analyses showed distinct provincial patterns: higher alcohol consumption and higher dairy intake in Sichuan; higher income, higher dairy intake, and reduced smoking in Chongqing; lower physical activity in Guizhou and Yunnan; and no statistically significant indirect association was identified in Tibet. In PPOM sensitivity analyses, high (vs. low) educational attainment was associated with lower odds of worse bone status in Chongqing at both cumulative thresholds (OR = 0.738, 95% CI, 0.652-0.837; OR = 0.688, 95% CI, 0.541-0.875), higher odds of worse bone status in Guizhou (OR = 1.174, 95% CI, 1.018-1.352), and no clear association in Yunnan (OR = 0.873, 95% CI, 0.696-1.094). Educational attainment showed province-specific associations with QUS-defined bone status in Southwest China. Indirect statistical associations involving socioeconomic, behavioral, and dietary factors require confirmation in longitudinal studies.
