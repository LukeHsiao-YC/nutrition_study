### 重點摘要
澳洲自2016年起推行提早引入過敏原食物的嬰兒餵食指南，以預防食物過敏。本研究比較指南實施前後，在墨爾本對6歲兒童食物過敏盛行率進行了兩項族群研究，使用問卷、皮膚點刺測試及口服激發試驗進行評估。結果顯示，調整相關風險因子後，食物過敏的盛行率顯著下降2.0%（相對減少31%），主要體現在腰果和花生過敏的減少。這項研究提供證據，支持提早引入過敏原的嬰兒餵食指南能在族群層面有效降低兒童食物過敏。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了提早引入過敏原的嬰兒餵食指南在真實世界族群層面實施後，對兒童食物過敏盛行率影響的實證空白，證明了其預防效果。然而，研究並未深入探討牛奶和雞蛋過敏盛行率未顯著改變的原因，以及此政策的長期效益和對其他過敏原的影響仍需進一步研究。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Changes in Childhood Food Allergy Following Implementation of Infant Feeding Guidelines.
**Abstract:** Since 2016, Australian infant feeding guidelines recommend introducing allergenic foods into the diet in the first year of life to prevent food allergy. Here, we evaluated whether the prevalence of food allergy in 6-year-old children changed following implementation of these guidelines. We conducted 2 population-based studies using the same methodology in Melbourne, Australia, before and after the guideline changes, recruiting 5276 12-month-old infants in 2007 to 2011 and 1933 infants in 2018 to 2019. Both studies followed up with children at age 6 years (2012-2016 and 2024-2025 respectively) with questionnaires, optional skin prick tests, and oral food challenges. Direct regression standardization compared prevalence estimates between cohorts, adjusting for demographic differences between the samples. Multiple imputation was used to handle missing data. Participation at age 6 years in 2012 to 2016 was 84% and in 2024 to 2025 was 83%. After adjusting for temporal changes in food allergy risk factors, and applying multiple imputation, the prevalence of food allergy decreased: absolute reduction -2.0% (95% CI -3.4% to -0.6%, P = .005; n = 7209) and relative reduction -31% (95% CI -50% to -11%, P = .002). Findings were similar using complete cases (absolute reduction -2.9%, 95% CI -4.5% to -1.2%, P < .001; relative reduction -37%, 95% CI -57% to -18%, P < 0.001; n = 4173). This reduction was primarily driven by changes in cashew and peanut allergy; prevalence of milk and egg allergy did not change substantially in this age group. These findings provide evidence that infant feeding guidelines recommending earlier allergen introduction may translate to measurable reductions in childhood food allergy at the population level.
