### 重點摘要
關於青少年吸食電子煙是否會增加未來過早死亡的風險及其程度尚不明確。本研究利用微觀模擬模型和蒙特卡洛分析，評估了2016年美國410萬12歲青少年終身吸食電子煙和吸菸相關的死亡結果，比較了電子煙存在與不存在的情境。研究發現，電子煙的存在可能導致平均每位隊列成員的過早死亡風險增加0.44個百分點（減少壽命≤31.3天）或減少0.06個百分點（增加壽命≤2.7天）。總體而言，如果青少年吸食電子煙影響終身過早死亡率，其預計的增減幅度將非常小。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究利用模型分析填補了青少年時期開始吸食電子煙對終身過早死亡率影響的量化空白。然而，其結果基於對多個關鍵參數（如電子煙死亡風險、戒菸率等）的假設範圍，而非長期實際追蹤數據，因此其預測的精確性仍受限於這些假設的準確性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** The Lifetime Mortality Implications of Vaping and Smoking Initiated During Adolescence.
**Abstract:** Whether, and if so how much, adolescent vaping increases the risk of future premature mortality is unknown. This microsimulation model and Monte Carlo analysis evaluated plausible lifetime vaping- and smoking-associated mortality outcomes with vaping and smoking initiated during adolescence for the 4.1 million U.S. 12-year-olds in 2016. Outcomes with e-cigarettes available throughout life were compared with a counterfactual in which e-cigarettes never existed. The analysis considered three ways vaping could impact mortality: (i) vaping during adolescence followed by smoking continuing into adulthood; (ii) vaping continuing into adulthood without smoking; and (iii) vaping increasing adult smoking cessation for cohort members who began smoking in adolescence. Parameters included vaping's mortality risk (5%-30% that of smoking), the smoking cessation increase attributable to vaping (0%-30%), the vaping cessation rate (1-4 times the smoking cessation rate), and years of vaping after vaping-induced smoking cessation (5-20). Data were collected in 2024 and analyzed in 2024-2025. E-cigarette availability was associated with the average cohort member's vaping-associated lifetime probability of premature mortality ranging from an increase of 0.44 percentage points to a decrease of 0.06 percentage points, corresponding, respectively, to a loss of ≤31.3 days of life expectancy to a gain of ≤2.7 days. In two illustrative model setups, individuals who smoked at age 20 years had a lifetime probability of premature mortality of 7-8%. Those who vaped but did not smoke had a probability of 0.15-0.84%. If youth vaping affects lifetime premature mortality, the increase or decrease is projected to be very small.
