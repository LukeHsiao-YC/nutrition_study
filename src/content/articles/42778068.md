### 重點摘要
本研究旨在提供芬蘭北部三個世代隊列共8,959名參與者（58%女性）的Three-Factor Eating Questionnaire -R18 (TFEQ-R18) 性別、年齡與BMI特定常模值。研究發現，女性在認知限制和情緒化飲食方面得分普遍高於男性；年輕成年期衝動性飲食和情緒化飲食最高，老年期最低，而認知限制在老年期更高。TFEQ-R18分數在不同BMI類別間有差異，且與BMI呈正相關或輕微曲線關係。這些常模有助於醫療專業人員識別異常飲食行為，並強調TFEQ-R18分數應根據性別、年齡和體重狀況進行解釋，而非採用單一通用切點值。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究提供了TFEQ-R18的性別、年齡和體重狀況特異性常模值，填補了缺乏全面性解釋指南的空白，並指出不應使用通用切點值。未來的研究應探討飲食行為的縱向變化，旨在找出用於篩查飲食失調的切點分數，並應認識到一般人群和尋求肥胖治療者中飲食行為各維度的共存現象。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Normative values of the Three-Factor Eating Questionnaire -R18 in three Northern Finland cohort populations by sex and BMI classes.
**Abstract:** The revised Three-Factor Eating Questionnaire -18 (TFEQ-R18) is used to assess eating behaviour in weight management, obesity, and epidemiological studies. We provided sex-specific normative values as percentiles for the TFEQ-R18 from three population-based samples (Northern Finland cohorts) across different age groups (i.e., 33-35, 45-46, and 68-70 years) among 8,959 participants (58% female). Mean values with SD were compared across six BMI classes, stratified by sex and age group. Percentile and mean values for cognitive restraint and emotional eating were higher in females than in males across all age groups. Additionally, the mean values for uncontrolled eating and emotional eating were highest in young adulthood and lowest in old adulthood, whereas cognitive restraint was higher in old adulthood than among younger adults. TFEQ-R18 domain scores differed between BMI classes; visual inspection suggested positive linear or slightly curvilinear associations in all age groups. These population-based norms may help healthcare professionals identify deviant eating behaviour and target support. Normative data also enhance the clinical and research utility of the TFEQ-R18. According to our findings, universal cut-off values cannot be defined for TFEQ-R18 scores; instead, the scores should be interpreted in relation to sex, age, and weight status. Future research should examine the longitudinal changes of eating behaviour in the general population and aim to find out cut-off scores for the screening of disordered eating. Also, the co-occurrence of eating behaviour dimensions in the general population and among obesity-treatment-seeking individuals needs to be recognised for the multidimensional manifestations of eating behaviour.
