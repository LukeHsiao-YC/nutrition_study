### 重點摘要
鑑於兒童生長監測中準確人體測量的重要性，以及親自測量可能面臨的限制，本研究旨在評估由家長在家中測量幼兒身高體重的可靠性和重複性。研究招募了30名2-5歲的兒童，比較了單一父母在家中依標準化指示測量的數據與受過訓練的研究團隊在臨床環境中測量的數據。結果顯示，家長報告的測量結果具有高度的可靠性和重複性，尤其在體重測量方面表現突出，身高測量雖有輕微差異，仍在可接受範圍內。這些發現支持在家中由家長進行人體測量，這有助於在無法進行親自測量時，提高研究和臨床護理的可及性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
該研究填補了在無法進行現場或臨床測量時，如何獲取兒童準確人體測量數據的研究空白。它提供了證據，證明了家長在家中測量兒童身高體重在特定情況下是可靠且可重複的替代方案，從而提升了兒童生長監測在研究和臨床照護上的可近性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Reliability and Repeatability of At-Home Parent-Reported Height and Weight Measurement of Young Children.
**Abstract:** Accurate anthropometric measurement is essential for monitoring child growth, yet access to in-person measurement collection can be limited. This study examines the reliability and repeatability of height and weight measurements taken at home by a single parent compared to those taken by a trained researcher team in a clinical setting. Thirty children aged 2-5 years enrolled in the TARGet Kids! cohort were measured by two trained researchers working in a pair and by their parent at home using standardized instructions. Technical error of measurement (TEM), relative TEM (%TEM), and the coefficient of reliability (R) were used to assess measurement reliability. Results demonstrated high reliability and repeatability of parent-reported measurements, particularly for weight. Bland-Altman plots showed slightly greater variation in height measurements collected by the parent at home, though still within acceptable limits. These findings support the use of parent-collected anthropometry at home, which may enhance accessibility for research and clinical care when in-person measurement is not feasible.
