### 重點摘要
研究背景指出，儘管指引建議為嚴重精神疾病患者的照護者提供早期且系統性的家庭心理教育，但這類方案在臨床實踐中仍未被充分利用。本研究採用單組前後測設計，評估了593名家庭照護者參與BREF計畫後，其負擔與憂鬱症狀的變化，以及對方案的滿意度。結果顯示，參與BREF計畫後，照護者的憂鬱與負擔分數顯著降低，且在三個月追蹤時仍維持此改善，同時對家庭協會的參與度提高，並對方案表現出高度滿意。BREF計畫作為一個標準化、可擴展且易於推廣的介入措施，為精神醫學領域照護者的支持途徑提供了一個有前景的基礎與切入點。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻成功地評估並展示了一個可擴展且易於推廣的心理教育方案 (BREF)，填補了現有指引建議的家庭心理教育方案在精神健康照護機構中實踐不足的空白。它提供了一個實證有效且接受度高的介入措施，以促進早期、系統性及永續的照護者支持。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Bridging the gap between guidelines and practice: Outcomes of the BREF psychoeducational program for caregivers in psychiatry.
**Abstract:** Despite guidelines recommending early and systematic provision of family psychoeducation to caregivers of individuals with a severe mental disorder, such programs remain underutilized and infrequently implemented in mental health care settings. The BREF program was developed to enable early, systematic and sustainable support for caregivers in psychiatry. This study evaluated caregiver outcomes associated with participation in the BREF program using a single-group pre-post design. Data from 593 family caregivers who participated in the BREF program from November 2020 to May 2024 were analysed. Caregivers' burden (Zarit Burden Interview; ZBI) and depression (Center for Epidemiological Studies-Depression; CES-D) were assessed before and immediately after the program. A subset of 250 participants completed assessments at three-month follow-up where changes in engagement with family associations, satisfaction and perceived usefulness of the program were also evaluated. Lower depression and burden scores were observed immediately after the program (Cohen's d=0.24 and 0.44; p < 0.001) and at three-month follow-up among caregivers who completed follow-up assessments (d=0.29 and 0.69; p < 0.001). Consistently, the proportion of caregivers exceeding established thresholds for depressive symptoms (CES-D ≥16) or moderate-to-severe burden (ZBI ≥41) was lower immediately after the program (p < 0.001) and at follow-up (p < 0.003). Greater engagement with family associations was observed at follow-up (p < 0.001). Satisfaction and perceived usefulness of the program were high, 98% reported being satisfied or very satisfied, and 96% found the program highly useful. As a standardized, scalable, and easily disseminated intervention, the BREF program shows promise as a foundational step in a structured and graduated support pathway for caregivers in psychiatry. BREF may represent a scalable and accessible entry point into broader caregiver support pathways in psychiatry. Its high acceptability supports early and systematic implementation within routine mental health services, while fostering engagement with family associations and ongoing support resources.
