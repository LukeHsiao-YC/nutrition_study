### 重點摘要
冠心病患者常合併多種慢性病，這增加了照護複雜性與醫療成本，但過去研究較少探討不同多重共病模式與住院費用水平及結構的關聯。本研究回溯性分析中國某三級醫院33,044名中老年冠心病住院患者數據，運用潛在類別分析識別共病模式，並評估其與住院費用的關聯。研究識別出五種共病模式，結果顯示所有共病模式的住院費用均顯著高於低共病組，其中以心臟功能障礙及糖尿病-腎臟模式的費用增幅最大且費用結構亦有差異。這些發現有助於制定更精準的照護計劃和支付調整，以優化三級醫院的住院服務。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
過去研究多依賴單一疾病或簡單的共病數量統計，較少深入探討不同多重共病模式與住院費用水平及結構之間的具體關聯。本研究透過識別具體的共病模式，並分析其對住院費用水平及構成的影響，填補了此一研究空白。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Comorbidity patterns and inpatient expenditure among patients with coronary heart disease in China.
**Abstract:** Coronary heart disease (CHD) often co-occurs with other chronic conditions. This increases care complexity and hospital costs. Most previous studies have relied on single diseases or simple comorbidity counts. Less is known about whether different multimorbidity patterns are associated with different levels and structures of inpatient expenditure. We aimed to identify comorbidity patterns among middle-aged and older inpatients with CHD and to examine their associations with hospital costs. We conducted a retrospective study using electronic health record data from a tertiary hospital in Shanxi Province, China, from 2020 to 2024. Patients aged 45 years or older with a principal discharge diagnosis of CHD (ICD-10: I20-I25) were included. The outcome was total inpatient expenditure per admission. Latent class analysis was applied to 14 comorbidities with prevalence of at least 1%. We then used generalised linear models with a gamma family and log link to examine the association between latent classes and inpatient expenditure, adjusting for sociodemographic and clinical covariates. Among 33,044 patients used for pattern identification, 85.16% (28,140) had at least one included comorbidity. Five classes were identified: low comorbidity (39.89%), vascular disease (5.20%), hypertension-dominant (42.27%), diabetes-renal (3.16%), and cardiac dysfunction (9.48%). Compared with the low-comorbidity class, all other classes were associated with higher inpatient expenditure, with the largest increases observed in the cardiac dysfunction class (ER = 1.33, 95% CI: 1.30-1.36) and the diabetes-renal class (ER = 1.27, 95% CI: 1.23-1.32). Predicted mean expenditure was highest in the cardiac dysfunction class ($2,615.77; 95% CI: 2,562.30-2,669.24) and the diabetes-renal class ($2,502.99; 95% CI: 2,421.49-2,584.49), compared with $1,964.85 (95% CI: 1,943.54-1,986.16) in the low-comorbidity class. Medical consumables accounted for the largest share of expenditure in all groups, while the cardiac dysfunction class had the highest proportions of diagnostic and medication costs. Multimorbidity was highly prevalent among middle-aged and older inpatients with CHD. Distinct comorbidity patterns were associated with different levels and structures of inpatient expenditure. These findings may help inform more tailored care planning and more refined payment adjustment in tertiary inpatient settings.
