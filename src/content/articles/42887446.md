### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在評估為期八週的能量限制生酮飲食（KD）與標準飲食對超重和肥胖女性氧化還原平衡生物標誌物的影響，以釐清酮症與體重減輕在氧化還原適應中的相對貢獻。研究將80名女性隨機分為生酮組或標準飲食組，並測量了氧化壓力、抗氧化防禦和代謝壓力生物標誌物。結果顯示，兩種飲食均顯著改變了多個氧化還原相關生物標誌物，抗氧化能力和8-OHdG升高，綜合氧化壓力指數（OSI）淨下降，但生酮飲食組與標準飲食組之間未發現額外的顯著差異。研究結論指出，對於超重和肥胖女性，能量限制和體重減輕引起的變化，可能比營養性酮症本身，更能決定觀察到的氧化還原適應。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了生酮飲食對氧化還原平衡影響中，營養性酮症與體重減輕何者貢獻較大的研究空白。研究結果表明，在超重和肥胖女性中，體重減輕和能量限制可能是氧化還原適應的主要決定因素，而非生酮飲食特有的效應。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Circulating biomarkers of oxidative stress and antioxidant status in women with overweight and obesity following an 8-week energy-restricted ketogenic diet: Results from the randomized KETO-MINOX study.
**Abstract:** Ketogenic diet (KD) has been proposed to improve oxidative stress through mechanisms related to nutritional ketosis; however, the relative contribution of ketosis and weight loss to redox adaptation remains unclear. This study aimed to evaluate the effects of an eight-week energy-restricted KD on circulating biomarkers of redox homeostasis in women with overweight and class I obesity. Eighty women were randomised to either an energy-restricted Mediterranean-style KD (KETO; n = 40) or an energy-restricted standard diet (STD; n = 40). Oxidative-stress, antioxidant-defence, and metabolic-stress biomarkers (FGF21 and GDF15) were assessed at baseline (T0), week 4 (T1), and week 8 (T2). A composite oxidative stress index (OSI) was calculated, and associations with inflammatory markers, ketone bodies, amino acids, and body composition were explored. Sixty-six participants were included in the final analyses (KETO, n = 32; STD, n = 34). Both interventions induced significant changes in several redox-related biomarkers, with a marked rise in antioxidant capacity accompanied by a rise in 8-OHdG, and a net reduction in the composite OSI. No between-group difference survived correction for multiple testing at either time point; the early differences in ACW, PCL and total protein at week 4 were nominal only. Within-arm trajectories of FGF21 and GDF15 differed, but the randomised between-diet contrasts for both markers were non-significant. Principal component analysis revealed no distinct ketogenic redox phenotype. An eight-week energy-restricted KD did not provide detectable additional benefits for systemic redox homeostasis compared with a standard energy-restricted diet. The increase in antioxidant capacity was proportional to the degree of weight loss, whereas the OSI was not, indicating an absence of a KD-specific effect rather than establishing weight loss as the determinant of redox adaptation overall. The findings suggest that changes related to energy restriction and weight loss may be more important determinants of the observed redox adaptations than nutritional ketosis per se in women with overweight and obesity.
