### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討新生兒加護病房中早產兒維生素E和A的攝取量與血清濃度之間的關聯。這項回溯性世代研究發現，長期靜脈營養與較高的血清維生素E濃度顯著相關，但與維生素A無關；腸道攝取則與血清濃度無關。此外，壞死性小腸結腸炎與長期靜脈營養相關，且高血清維生素E可能是胃腸道出血的風險因子。研究結論指出，血清維生素A和E濃度不一定能持續反映攝取量，且長期靜脈營養會增加高血清維生素E的風險。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇研究揭示了早產兒在長期靜脈營養下，血清維生素E濃度可能過高，且血清濃度與維生素攝取量不完全一致的現象，並指出高維生素E可能與壞死性小腸結腸炎及胃腸道出血有關。然而，這是一項回溯性研究，其限制在於需要未來的獨立前瞻性研究來進一步釐清這些潛在風險，並為早產兒更精確地制定靜脈維生素產品配方。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Associations between intake of vitamins E and A and serum concentrations in preterm infants: A retrospective cohort study.
**Abstract:** Infants in neonatal intensive care are at risk of both deficiency and excess of fat-soluble vitamins, particularly E and A, due to limited reserves and the need for prolonged intravenous nutrition. We aimed to determine the effects of increased intravenous vitamin intake and to characterise the relationships between vitamin intake and serum concentrations. Retrospective cohort of infants admitted to neonatal intensive care who had serum vitamin E or A concentrations quantified in the hospital laboratory using high-performance liquid chromatography. Associations between vitamin intakes, serum concentrations, neonatal outcomes, and maternal ethnicity were explored. Longer duration of intravenous nutrition was associated with higher serum concentrations of vitamin E (Spearman's ρ = 0.45, p < 0.001), but not vitamin A (ρ = 0.01, p = 0.93). Enteral vitamin intake from supplements was not associated with serum concentrations. Necrotising enterocolitis was associated with prolonged intravenous nutrition and may be a risk factor for high serum vitamin E. High vitamin E may be a risk factor for gastrointestinal bleeding. No differences in morbidity, mortality, or vitamin intake were observed between ethnicity groups. Serum vitamin A and E concentrations in neonatal intensive care infants did not consistently reflect intakes. Prolonged intravenous nutrition increases the risk of high serum vitamin E concentrations. Prospective studies are needed to clarify these risks and to inform targeted formulation of intravenous vitamin products for preterm infants.
