### 重點摘要
該研究旨在探討早期生命階段攝取乳製品蛋白（酪蛋白、乳清蛋白、全乳）與植物性大豆蛋白，對幼鼠腸道菌群組成、肥胖易感性及胰島素敏感性的影響。幼鼠在斷奶後被隨機分配到四種等熱量飲食中持續四週，隨後再給予高脂肪、高蔗糖飲食（HFHS）五週進行代謝挑戰。結果顯示，早期攝取全乳可改善體脂肪百分比並調節腸道菌群，但未改善葡萄糖耐受性或胰島素敏感性。儘管早期飲食能正向調控腸道菌群和改善身體組成，但這些益處不足以抵消後續高脂肪、高蔗糖飲食所帶來的負面代謝影響。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究闡明了早期膳食蛋白質來源對腸道菌群和代謝健康影響的知識空白，特別是指出早期飲食干預在面對高脂肪、高蔗糖飲食的強烈代謝挑戰時，其保護作用的局限性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Early-Life Dietary Protein Source Reshapes Gut Microbiota but Fails to Curtail Diet-Induced Metabolic Syndrome in Young Rats.
**Abstract:** Dairy consumption plays a protective role in obesity that may be mediated through the gut microbiota. This study examined the role of dairy proteins, casein, whey, and complete dairy (skim milk powder), and a plant-based alternative, soy, on gut microbiota composition, obesity susceptibility, and insulin sensitivity in early development. Following weaning, rat pups were randomized into four isocaloric diets (n = 8-11/sex/diet) for 4 weeks: (1) complete dairy; (2) casein; (3) whey; and (4) soy. Primary outcomes included food intake, growth, body composition, glucose tolerance, and fecal microbiota composition. Animals were then metabolically challenged with a high-fat, high-sucrose diet (HFHS, 40% kilocalories from fat) for 5 weeks. The impacts on gut microbiota and metabolic outcomes were sex and protein dependent. Compared to casein, complete dairy consumption reduced diversity, while early exposure to complete dairy improved body fat percentage but not glucose tolerance or insulin sensitivity. Soy elicited differential effects in males and females. Despite preserved microbial patterns, diets were unable to overcome the detrimental metabolic impacts of HFHS diet exposure. Results show that complete dairy positively modulates gut microbial profiles and benefits body composition early in life, but it could not curtail the negative metabolic impacts of HFHS feeding.
