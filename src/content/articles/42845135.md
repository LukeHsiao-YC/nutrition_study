### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討在社會經濟弱勢社區中，課後活動計畫的實施策略如何影響學童的體能活動(PA)參與率，以應對普遍存在的兒童肥胖問題。研究採前瞻性觀察設計，收集了紐約布朗克斯區三所中學共491名學生的去識別化人口統計和為期36週的體能活動出席數據，並使用ANOVA進行比較分析。結果顯示，不同學校提供的體能活動次數存在顯著差異（例如C校每天4.0次，A校1.6次）。研究推斷，計畫的課程結構、體能與非體能活動的排程以及學生個人偏好可能影響體能活動的參與率，並建議制定標準化的課後體能活動提供與出席要求，以期提高潛在參與度。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了在社會經濟弱勢地區，課後體能活動計畫的實施策略如何影響學童參與率的知識空白，為改善兒童肥胖問題提供了實證見解。然而，由於研究是觀察性的且在特定地理位置（布朗克斯區）進行，其結果可能無法廣泛推論至其他地區或更廣泛的群體，且對於哪種計畫結構是「最佳」的，仍需進一步的介入性研究來證實。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Identifying Optimal Physical Activity Programming Structure in Three Bronx Middle Schools.
**Abstract:** Childhood obesity disproportionately affects socioeconomically disadvantaged communities, where access to physical activity (PA) is limited. This study compares overall PA offerings and attendance across three afterschool programs located in a resource-poor setting to identify how program implementation strategies influence student attendance at PA opportunities. Prospective observational study using deidentified demographic and repeated-measures attendance data for comparative analysis of overall PA attendance among three conveniently selected Bronx middle schools (A, B, C) serving low-socioeconomic-status student populations during the 2023-2024 school year (36 weeks). Routinely collected administrative daily data were aggregated weekly per student to calculate average daily PA time and proportion of PA sessions attended. Outcomes included average daily PA sessions offered, PA minutes, and proportion of PA sessions (out of all PA and non-PA sessions) attended, compared across schools and demographics using ANOVA. Four hundred and ninety-one students attended three afterschool programs (A = 120, B = 216, C = 155; 50% female). School C offered the most PA sessions (4.0/day vs. A: 1.6, B: 3.2;  Program structure and scheduling of PA and non-PA activity type, as well as individual student preferences, may influence PA attendance. Standardized minimum afterschool program PA offering and attendance requirements may improve potential PA participation.
