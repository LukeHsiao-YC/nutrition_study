### 重點摘要
這項橫斷面研究旨在探討超重/肥胖兒童和青少年動作能力與代謝症候群各組成部分之間的關聯。研究招募了54名6-14歲的超重/肥胖青少年，測量其動作能力、腰圍、血壓、空腹血糖、三酸甘油酯和高密度脂蛋白膽固醇等指標。結果顯示，動作能力與高密度脂蛋白膽固醇呈正相關，與中心性肥胖及三酸甘油酯呈負相關。本研究結論指出，較低的動作能力與較差的代謝狀況有關，提示提升兒童及青少年的動作能力可能促進其代謝健康發展。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
在該研究進行之前，超重/肥胖的兒童和青少年中，動作能力是否與代謝症候群的組成部分相關尚不清楚，本研究填補了此一知識空白。然而，由於本研究為橫斷面設計，無法建立因果關係，未來的研究應考慮縱向設計以探討動作能力對代謝健康的長期影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Motor Competence and Components of Metabolic Syndrome in Children and Adolescents with Overweight and Obesity.
**Abstract:** Motor competence is associated with a number of health benefits in children and adolescents. However, it is unknown whether motor competence is linked to components of the metabolic syndrome (MetS) in individuals with excess body weight. The aim of this study was to investigate the relationship between motor competence and components of MetS in young people with overweight/obesity. A cross-sectional study was conducted recruiting 54 young individuals (55.6% girls) with overweight/obesity aged 6-14 years ( Mean values of motor competence, waist circumference, systolic and diastolic blood pressure, fasting glucose, triglycerides, and high-density lipoprotein cholesterol (HDL-c) were, respectively, 75.1 (±9.9), 86.9 cm (±13.7), 101.4 mmHg (±11.7) and 64.2 mmHg (±9.2), 89.1 mg/dL (±8.1), 97.9 mg/dL (±52.5), and 45.1 mg/dL (±11.6); 87% of participants had low motor competence, 11.1% elevated fasting glucose, 20.4% elevated triglycerides, 31.5% low HDL-c, and 87.8% central obesity. Three or more risk factors associated with MetS were observed in 11.1% of the sample. Motor competence was positively associated with HDL-c and negatively associated with central adiposity and triglycerides. Motor competence was associated with components of MetS in young children and adolescents with overweight/obesity, suggesting that lower motor competence is linked to a worse metabolic profile. The development of adequate motor competence throughout childhood and adolescence may promote healthier trajectories, including metabolic outcomes.
