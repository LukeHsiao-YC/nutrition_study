### 重點摘要
重症病患常面臨肌肉流失，而增加腸道蛋白質攝取對其肌肉量、力量、身體功能及生活品質的影響尚不明確。本子研究比較了72位重症病患高蛋白（100克/升）與常規蛋白（63克/升）等熱量腸道配方對股四頭肌厚度變化的影響。研究結果顯示，兩組在7天內股四頭肌厚度流失無顯著差異。儘管高蛋白組在出加護病房時右側握力較佳，但其身體功能下降幅度也較大，而左側握力與90天生活品質則無顯著差異。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究旨在釐清重症病患高蛋白腸道營養對肌肉量流失、肌肉力量、身體功能和生活品質的影響，填補了此領域的不確定性。然而，研究的肌肉量評估時間僅為7天，未來的研究可探討更長期高蛋白補充的效果及不同功能指標的綜合影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Association between augmented enteral protein and muscle mass attenuation in critically ill patients: A sub-study of a cluster randomized crossover trial.
**Abstract:** Critically ill patients experience muscle wasting, impacting recovery. The associations between augmented enteral protein and muscle mass, strength, physical function, and quality of life are uncertain. We performed a sub-study in adult TARGET Protein participants from two sites between 23/11/2022-23/05/2023. Our exposure of interest was enteral formula with augmented protein (100 g protein/L) and comparator usual protein formula (63 g protein/L), which were isocaloric. Our primary outcome was the change in quadriceps muscle layer thickness from Days 1 to 7, determined by ultrasonography and compared between groups. Our secondary outcomes were group differences in handgrip strength, change in physical function, and quality of life. Data are numbers (%), mean (standard deviation), and differences with 95% confidence intervals (CI). Seventy-two patients were included (augmented protein n = 46, 61 (16)y, 65% male; usual protein n = 26, 56 (16)y, 46% male), 56 had primary outcome data (augmented protein n = 35; usual protein n = 21). Delivered protein was higher with augmented protein (1.2 (0.4) vs 0.8 (0.3) g/kg/day). Quadriceps muscle layer thickness decreased in both groups, with change from Days 1 to 7 similar (0.01 cm; 95% CI, -0.27, 0.28; P = 0.970). Left handgrip strength at ICU discharge and quality of life at Day 90 were similar. Augmented protein was associated with greater right handgrip strength at ICU discharge (6.23; 95% CI, 0.67, 11.79) and was associated with greater decline in physical function at ICU discharge (-15.00; 95% CI, -19.75, -10.25), but not at Day 90. No clear difference was detected in quadriceps muscle layer attenuation over 7 days between augmented enteral protein and usual protein. Australian New Zealand Clinical Trials Registry identifier: ACTRN12622001465741.
