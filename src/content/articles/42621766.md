### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估COVID-19非藥物介入措施（NPIs）是否影響台灣膽道閉鎖（BA）的發生率及晚期葛西手術比例。研究採全國性族群世代分析，回溯台灣2004年1月至2023年12月所有確診的膽道閉鎖案例，並依COVID-19政策劃分四個階段進行發生率及晚期葛西手術的比較分析。結果顯示，NPI期間膽道閉鎖的調整發生率與非NPI時期無顯著差異（IRR 1.08），而晚期葛西手術的比例在後NPI時期有下降趨勢。總體而言，台灣膽道閉鎖的發生率並未在COVID-19 NPI期間下降，暗示減少感染暴露對其發生影響有限；後NPI時期晚期葛西手術減少則可能反映了早期診斷或照護有所改善。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項全國性研究直接評估了COVID-19非藥物介入措施對膽道閉鎖發生率的影響，填補了關於環境感染暴露與膽道閉鎖病因之間關係的實證數據空白。它也揭示了疫情期間及後疫情時代台灣膽道閉鎖的醫療照護趨勢，例如晚期葛西手術比例的變化，但並未深入探討這些變化的具體原因或影響因素，這可能需要未來研究進一步探討。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Biliary atresia incidence in Taiwan before, during, and after the COVID-19 pandemic: A nationwide population-based study.
**Abstract:** To evaluate whether COVID-19 nonpharmaceutical interventions (NPIs) were associated with changes in biliary atresia (BA) incidence and in late Kasai portoenterostomy in Taiwan. We conducted a nationwide population-based cohort study of all confirmed BA cases in Taiwan from January 2004 to December 2023. Monthly BA incidence was calculated per 10,000 live births. COVID-19 policy phases were defined as No NPI (Jan 2004-Dec 2019), NPI (Jan 2020-Nov 2022), NPI-relaxation (Dec 2022-Feb 2023), and Post-NPI (Mar-Dec 2023). Phase-specific incidence rate ratios (IRRs) were estimated using Poisson regression with live births as an offset and calendar-month fixed effects. Late Kasai was defined as portoenterostomy performed at age > 60 days; phase differences were assessed using logistic regression. A total of 586 infants with BA were identified (overall incidence 1.55/10,000). Crude incidence was 1.52/10,000 in No NPI (485 cases), 1.64/10,000 in NPI (73), 0.90/10,000 in NPI-relaxation (3) and 2.18/10,000 in Post-NPI (25) Compared with No NPI, adjusted IRRs were 1.08 (95% confidence interval [CI] 0.84-1.38) for NPI, 0.58 (0.19-1.82) for NPI-relaxation, and 1.46 (0.98-2.19) for Post-NPI. The proportion of late Kasai decreased from 35.3% in No NPI to 16.0% (4/25) in Post-NPI (odds ratio 0.38, 95% CI 0.14-1.08). The incidence of BA in Taiwan did not decline during the COVID-19 NPI period, suggesting limited impact of reduced infectious exposure on BA occurrence. A trend toward fewer late Kasai procedures in the post-NPI phase may reflect improved early detection or care delivery.
