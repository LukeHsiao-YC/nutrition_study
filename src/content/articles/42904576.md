### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討接受胃腸道手術的極低出生體重嬰兒（VLBW）靜脈營養相關膽汁淤積（PNAC）的發生率及危險因子，透過回溯性分析61名VLBW病患的醫療紀錄。結果顯示，近一半（49%）的VLBW嬰兒發展出PNAC，且發病時間多在開始靜脈營養後的八週內。腸道衰竭及術後六週內無法達成完全腸道餵養是PNAC的獨立危險因子，即使常規使用SMOFlipid仍無法顯著降低其發生率。本研究強調早期餵養進展可能反映疾病嚴重性，並作為PNAC風險的重要臨床指標。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了在接受胃腸道手術的極低出生體重嬰兒中，靜脈營養相關膽汁淤積（PNAC）的實際發生率及關鍵危險因子（如腸道衰竭與無法達成完全腸道餵養）的知識空白。同時，它也突顯了即使常規使用SMOFlipid，PNAC問題依然普遍且嚴峻，對於嚴重膽汁淤積，魚油單一療法效果不佳，提示未來需要更有效的預防或治療策略。其回溯性設計是其研究限制之一。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Incidence and risk factors of parenteral nutrition-associated cholestasis in surgical very low birth weight infants.
**Abstract:** Surgical intervention for necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC), spontaneous intestinal perforation (SIP), and meconium-related ileus (MRI) is frequently complicated by parenteral nutrition-associated cholestasis (PNAC). This study aimed to determine the incidence and identify risk factors of PNAC in very low birth weight (VLBW) infants following gastrointestinal surgery. We conducted a retrospective review of medical records for VLBW infants who underwent gastrointestinal surgery between January 2012 and December 2020. PNAC was defined as direct bilirubin ≥2.0 mg/dL persisting for two postoperative weeks while on PN. Among 61 VLBW patients who underwent surgery for NEC, SIP, or MRI, 30 (49%) developed PNAC. The median duration of PN until PNAC onset was 6 weeks, and 70% developed cholestasis within 8 weeks of PN initiation. Intestinal failure was present in 79% of the cohort and was significantly more frequent in infants with PNAC. Inability to achieve full enteral feeding within 6 weeks was independently associated with PNAC. Despite routine use of SMOFlipid, PNAC incidence remained substantial. Among infants with severe cholestasis (direct bilirubin ≥6 mg/dL), switching to fish oil monotherapy rarely resulted in reversal. PNAC developed early and frequently in surgical VLBW infants despite SMOFlipid use and was strongly associated with intestinal failure and inability to achieve full enteral feeding. Early feeding progression appears to reflect overall disease severity and may represent an important clinical marker for PNAC risk.
