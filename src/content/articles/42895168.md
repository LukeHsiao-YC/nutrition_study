### 重點摘要
這項範圍界定回顧旨在探討並綜合澳大利亞卡比卡比原住民族的傳統飲食與食物習俗。研究系統性檢索學術及灰色文獻，篩選出43份資料並辨識出270個食物詞彙，隨後進行營養分析、文化繪製與主題式分析。結果顯示其食物系統複雜多元，受季節性、環境知識和文化等因素影響，且傳統飲食營養豐富。本研究揭示了一個與環境協調、具韌性且精密的食物系統，支持文化復興，並強調傳統飲食對當代健康的維持作用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了文獻中關於卡比卡比原住民族傳統飲食與食物習俗的系統性綜合性知識空白，透過挑戰殖民描述並支持文化復興，提供了一個全面且精確的描繪。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Mapping the traditional diet and foodways of the Kabi Kabi Peoples (a first nations tribe of Australia): a scoping review.
**Abstract:** This scoping review aimed to explore and synthesise the literature on the traditional foods and foodways of the Kabi Kabi Peoples and highlight current gaps in the literature. This review adhered to the Joanna Briggs Institute Methodology and the Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-analysis for Scoping Reviews guidelines. Academic and grey literature were systematically searched. Data were compiled in Excel, food terms were standardised and frequency of mention was assessed to guide selection for nutritional analysis and cultural mapping. Nutritional analysis and cultural mapping were undertaken for frequently mentioned foods, and a deductive thematic analysis was carried out using predefined themes. The traditional lands of the Kabi Kabi Peoples of South East Queensland, Australia. Eligible sources included academic and grey literature discussing the traditional diet or foodways of the Kabi Kabi Peoples. Of 810 initial sources identified, forty-three met inclusion criteria. In total, 270 standardised food terms were identified. Thematic analysis revealed a complex food system, shaped by seasonality, environmental knowledge, cultural significance, taste and gender roles. Analysis of the frequently mentioned foods illustrates a diverse, nutrient-rich diet and cultural maps highlight the widespread distribution of foods still present across Kabi Kabi Country. This review illustrates an environmentally attuned, resilient, sustainable and sophisticated food systems that challenges colonial portrayals and supports cultural revitalisation. Moreover, it highlights the contemporary relevance of traditional foods in maintaining health and well-being.
