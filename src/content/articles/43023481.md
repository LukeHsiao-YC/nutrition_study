### 重點摘要
這項兩階段研究旨在建立和驗證日本成人全球領導營養不良倡議（GLIM）標準中嚴重肌肉量減少的臨界值。研究首先在老年門診患者中，使用雙能量X光吸收儀和生物電阻抗分析，推導出附肢骨骼肌質量指數和無脂肪質量指數的臨界值，並以一年內住院作為結果進行接收者操作特徵分析。隨後，利用全國索賠數據驗證了這些臨界值與住院成人死亡率的關聯，結果顯示嚴重肌肉量減少的臨界值與較高的死亡風險顯著相關。這些建立的臨界值有望促進GLIM標準的嚴重程度分級，並改善預後風險分層。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了全球領導營養不良倡議（GLIM）標準中，缺乏明確的附肢骨骼肌質量指數和無脂肪質量指數嚴重肌肉量減少臨界值的空白。它首次為日本成人提供了這些基於儀器評估的嚴重度分級臨界值，有助於標準化GLIM診斷和預後評估。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Development and validation of severe muscle mass reduction cutoffs for GLIM Criteria in Japanese adults: A two-phase study.
**Abstract:** The Global Leadership Initiative on Malnutrition prioritizes instrument-based assessment of reduced muscle mass, but severity-grading cutoffs for appendicular skeletal muscle mass index and fat-free mass index are not established. We derived cutoffs for severe muscle mass reduction in older Japanese outpatients and examined their associations with mortality in hospitalized adults. A frailty clinic registry identified thresholds for appendicular skeletal muscle mass index by dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry or bioelectrical impedance analysis and fat-free mass index by bioelectrical impedance analysis. Receiver operating characteristic analyses used 1-year hospitalization as the outcome, with thresholds refined according to hospitalization risk. Nationwide claims data were used for validation. Mortality was assessed by Kaplan-Meier and Cox proportional hazards analyses adjusted for age, sex, comorbidities, and frailty. The development cohort included 1035 participants (mean age, 76.5 years). Severe muscle mass reduction cutoffs for appendicular skeletal muscle mass index were <6.4 kg/m These cutoffs may facilitate severity grading according to the Global Leadership Initiative on Malnutrition criteria and improve prognostic risk stratification.
