### 重點摘要
巴基斯坦兒童肥胖與體能活動不足是主要的公共衛生問題。本研究為期12週，在巴基斯坦八所公立學校對1200名9-17歲過重或肥胖的兒童青少年進行準實驗性介入，方案包含體育、健康教育、家長參與等多面向策略。結果顯示，介入組在體能活動、久坐行為、睡眠、BMI、體脂肪、飲食習慣及家長支持方面均有顯著改善，且在男孩和女孩中均有效。本研究證實此多面向的校園介入能有效改善兒童青少年的運動行為、飲食習慣及肥胖相關指標，支持整合學校與家庭策略以預防兒童肥胖。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
目前關於針對24小時動態行為的多面向校園介入措施，特別是考量性別差異的方法，其研究證據仍然有限。本研究在巴基斯坦的背景下，透過一項性別分層的準實驗性研究，填補了此類多成分校園介入研究的空白。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effectiveness of a Socioecological School-Based 24-h Movement Behaviour Intervention on Lifestyle Behaviours Among Children and Adolescents With Overweight or Obesity: A Gender-Stratified Quasi-Experimental Study.
**Abstract:** Childhood obesity and inadequate physical activity are major public health concerns in Pakistan. Evidence on multicomponent school-based interventions targeting 24-h movement behaviours, particularly with gender-specific approaches, remains limited. A 12-week quasi-experimental cluster-controlled study was conducted in eight public schools in Lahore, Pakistan. A total of 1200 children and adolescents (577 boys, 623 girls; aged 9-17 years) were assigned to an intervention group (n = 570) or a control group (n = 630). The intervention, guided by the socioecological model, included enhanced physical education, extracurricular physical activity, health education, parental engagement and supportive school environments. Outcomes included anthropometric measures and 24-h movement behaviours. These findings support integrated school- and family-based strategies for preventing childhood obesity across both boys and girls.
