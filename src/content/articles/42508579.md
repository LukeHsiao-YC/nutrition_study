### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在闡明個人味覺偏好如何轉化為食物氣味的動機價值，進而影響對食物的「想要」程度。研究招募43名參與者，首先評估他們對甜味和鹹味刺激的喜好度以建立味覺偏好檔案，隨後評估九種食物氣味的感知甜度、鹹度及其引起的「想要」程度。結果顯示，味覺喜好度與食物氣味的甜鹹感知相互作用，對食慾產生獨立的正交影響，且將氣味定位於甜鹹空間的加法模型表現更佳。本研究揭示了氣味透過味覺聯想引發食物渴望的機制，為發展改善飲食模式的行為干預措施提供了潛在依據。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了關於個人味覺偏好（甜味和鹹味喜好）如何轉化為食物氣味的動機價值，以及這種轉化機制如何影響對食物的「想要」程度的理解空白，明確指出氣味是透過味覺聯想引發食物渴望的。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Predicting appetitive response to food odours from expected gustatory properties and taste-liking phenotypes in a cross-sectional sample.
**Abstract:** Food intake is influenced by hedonic mechanisms involving the pleasure from consumption (liking) and the appetitive drive to eat a food (wanting). Taste is a key driver of liking, while orthonasal olfaction elicits wanting. However, how hedonic values from individual taste preferences are transferred onto food odours to influence their motivational value remains unclear. This study examined to what extent wanting for orthonasally presented odours is influenced by their perceived sweetness and savouriness and the individual liking for sweet or savoury tastes. Forty-three participants completed a taste identification and rating task involving six sweet and six savoury stimuli of increasing concentration. Pleasantness ratings were used to characterise individual sweet- and savoury-liking phenotypes using bounded negative quadratic functions. Subsequently, participants rated the perceived sweetness, savouriness and wanting of nine food odours. Linear mixed-effects models predicted odour wanting from taste liking. Model performance was compared using leave-one-subject-out cross-validation. Most participants exhibited an inverted U-shaped hedonic response to both taste qualities, with sweet taste generally being preferred over savoury taste. Taste liking profiles interacted with the perceived sweetness and savouriness of food odours to exert separate, orthogonal effects on wanting for foods associated with them. An additive model, where odours were located on a sweet-savoury space, outperformed a subtractive model, where odours were located on a sweet-savoury spectrum, with a higher correlation between predicted and observed values (Δr = -0.24; t(42) = -2.79, p = .008; 95% CI [-0.41, -0.07]; Cohen's d = -0.43). Taken together, our findings characterise a mechanism by which odours elicit food craving through taste associations. Our results have the potential to inform behavioural interventions and encourage healthier and more sustainable dietary patterns.
