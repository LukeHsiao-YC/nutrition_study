### 重點摘要
藥物難治性癲癇在成人中負擔沉重且治療選擇有限，而生酮飲食療法（KDT）對此具有潛在益處。本研究透過哥倫比亞專家共識，利用改良名義群體技術、PICO框架及文獻回顧，制定了KDT在成人藥物難治性癲癇中的實用、循證應用建議。共發布十二項建議，涵蓋KDT的適應症、實施、隨訪及安全性，並強調跨學科團隊和結構化教育的重要性。結論指出，KDT可作為藥物難治性癲癇成人的安全輔助療法，有助於減少癲癇發作頻率、改善生活品質並降低藥物負擔。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這份共識填補了將生酮飲食療法的現有證據轉化為臨床實踐的空白，特別是在拉丁美洲等發展中國家。未來的研究限制在於需要更多多中心前瞻性研究來進一步評估其療效、安全性及資源利用情況。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** How to Implement a Ketogenic Diet Therapy in Developing Countries: Colombian Consensus on Ketogenic Diet Therapy Use in the Management of Adults with Drug-Resistant Epilepsy.
**Abstract:** Drug-resistant epilepsy affects up to one-third of patients with epilepsy and is associated with higher morbidity, mortality, trauma events, psychosocial challenges, and decline in quality of life. In Latin America, disease burden is high and access to advanced therapies remains limited. Ketogenic Diet Therapy (KDT) has been shown to be beneficial in adults, but its implementation requires standardized protocols and experienced multidisciplinary teams. This study aimed to develop practical, evidence-based recommendations to guide the indication, implementation, and follow-up of KDT in adults with drug-resistant epilepsy through expert consensus in Colombia. A modified version of the Nominal Group Technique was used to develop, discuss, and vote on management concepts. Clinical questions were structured using the PICO framework. Literature searches were conducted in PubMed and EMBASE for articles published since 2015, and the evidence was integrated with the experience of a multidisciplinary expert panel. Twelve recommendations were issued, covering commercial formulas, induction and follow-up protocols, individualized dietary interventions, safety, diagnostic support, nutritional supplementation, and drug-diet interactions. The consensus highlights the need for interdisciplinary teams, structured education, early adherence strategies, and close follow-up, and the role of enteral emergency formulas in intensive care settings. Evidence around KDT shows it can be implemented safely as an adjunctive treatment for adults with drug-resistant epilepsy and is reasonable to consider following the failure of two adequate antiseizure medications. Structured implementation may reduce seizure frequency, improve quality of life, and reduce the pharmacological burden. Multicenter prospective studies are needed to further assess efficacy, safety, and resource utilization. These recommendations contribute to closing the gap between evidence and clinical practice.
