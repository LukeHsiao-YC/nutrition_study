### 重點摘要
鑑於熱量限制(CR)可能降低骨密度而限時飲食(TRE)會調節生理時鐘與腸道菌群，但兩者結合對骨骼的影響不明，本研究隨機對49名過重或肥胖的停經後女性進行16週的TRE+CR或單純CR干預。結果顯示，CR組在非負重部位（橈骨遠端）出現顯著骨質流失，而TRE+CR組則無，且髖部骨密度兩組皆未流失。這表明TRE可能減輕了體重減輕引起的非負重部位骨質流失，且這些益處可能與腸道菌群的變化有關。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了熱量限制(CR)結合限時飲食(TRE)對停經後女性骨骼健康影響的初步空白，並探討了腸道菌群的潛在關聯。然而，未來需要更大規模的研究來明確其作用機制及長期健康效益。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Time-Restricted Eating and Caloric Restriction Impact Bone and the Gut Microbiota in Postmenopausal Women: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
**Abstract:** Caloric restriction (CR) can reduce bone mineral density (BMD). Time-restricted eating (TRE) modulates circadian rhythms and gut microbiota, but its effects on bone when combined with CR are unknown. This study examined the effects of TRE+CR on bone in postmenopausal females with overweight or obesity and whether it is associated with changes in the gut microbiota. Forty-nine postmenopausal females (body mass index ≥25 kg/m Participants (61±5 years, 33±4 kg/m TRE mitigated weight-loss-induced bone loss at a clinically relevant non-weight-bearing site, but not at the hip, and these effects may be associated with changes in the gut microbiota. Larger studies are needed to establish mechanisms and sustained health effects.
