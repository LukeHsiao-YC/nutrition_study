### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討運動產生的乳酸是否透過GPR81，在肥胖小鼠中介導白色脂肪組織褐變，並闡明其分子機制。研究採用肥胖小鼠與細胞模型，透過外源性乳酸給予及不同時程的高乳酸運動干預，分析白色脂肪組織褐變現象及相關分子路徑。結果顯示，單次乳酸給予或運動皆能促進白色脂肪褐變，長期運動也能誘導適應性變化。這項研究證實乳酸是運動誘發代謝適應的關鍵介質，透過活化乳酸/GPR81-p38-UCP1訊號通路，能有效改善肥胖引起的脂肪組織褐變障礙及代謝缺陷。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了運動誘發的乳酸如何在肥胖情境下，透過GPR81及p38激酶具體調控白色脂肪組織褐變的分子機制空白，明確指出乳酸/GPR81-p38-UCP1訊號通路是改善肥胖相關代謝缺陷的關鍵。它將運動、乳酸、GPR81及脂肪組織褐變之間長期存在的關聯性，透過詳細的細胞與動物模型實驗，建立起明確的因果及分子路徑。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** The Role of the Lactate/GPR81-p38 Pathway in Exercise-Induced Regulation of White Adipose Tissue Browning.
**Abstract:** This study aimed to investigate whether lactate, as a key metabolic by-product of exercise, mediates exercise-induced white adipose tissue (WAT) browning in obese mice via the G protein-coupled receptor 81 (GPR81) and to elucidate the underlying molecular mechanisms. Obese mice, wild-type 3T3-L1 adipocytes, and GPR81-overexpressing 3T3-L1 adipocytes were utilized. An incremental-load exercise protocol designed to induce high lactate production (termed "high-lactate exercise") was established. Interventions included single-dose exogenous sodium lactate administration, single-session high-lactate exercise, and a 4-week high-lactate exercise regimen. Mechanistic pathways were analyzed using both in vitro cell models and in vivo mouse validation. Both single-dose exogenous sodium lactate and a single session of high-lactate exercise elicited comparable WAT browning effects. Furthermore, 4 weeks of high-lactate exercise induced adaptive changes promoting WAT browning. Mechanistic investigations identified p38 as the critical downstream target of the lactate/GPR81 axis in regulating WAT browning, a finding that was subsequently validated in mouse models. Exercise activates the lactate/GPR81-p38-UCP1 signaling axis to promote WAT browning. This pathway effectively ameliorates obesity-impaired adipose tissue browning and reverses metabolic deficits caused by obesity, highlighting lactate as a key mediator in exercise-induced metabolic adaptation.
