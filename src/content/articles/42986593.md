### 重點摘要
這項研究調查了美國愛荷華州鄉村生育單位關閉（自2000年來共41個）對產前護理與母嬰健康的影響。研究採用混合方法，分析了1999年至2017年間超過68萬份出生記錄，並對17名家庭訪視人員進行質性訪談。結果顯示，生育單位關閉顯著延遲了學歷較低母親的產前護理開始時間、減少了訪視次數，並增加了護理不足的風險，儘管WIC和Medicaid能部分緩衝這些影響。研究結論強調，這種夥伴合作與整合數據的方法揭示了總體分析中難以察覺的影響，為鄉村母嬰健康政策提供了及時可行的見解。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究透過整合個人層級的出生記錄與縣級關閉數據，並採用跨機構夥伴合作模式，成功揭示了在總體分析中可能被掩蓋的鄉村生育單位關閉對弱勢母親產前護理的具體負面影響。它填補了缺乏針對特定脆弱群體和細緻數據整合分析的空白，為制定更具針對性的鄉村母嬰健康政策提供了關鍵的實證基礎。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** A Partnership and Data Integration Approach for Investigating Rural Birthing Unit Closures.
**Abstract:** Rural birthing unit closures have raised concerns about access to prenatal care and maternal and infant outcomes. Iowa has experienced 41 closures since 2000, leaving a majority of counties without obstetric services. This study examines the impacts of these closures through a four-phase mixed-methods study conducted in partnership with Iowa State University, Iowa Department of Public Health, and Maternal, Infant, and Early Childhood Home Visiting. We replicated aggregate analyses used by state partners, then analyzed 681,187 individual birth records from 1999 to 2017 linked to county-level closure data. Hierarchical linear and logistic regression models estimated effects of closures on prenatal care and birth outcomes, stratified by maternal education, WIC receipt, and Medicaid coverage. We conducted qualitative interviews with 17 home visiting staff across eight counties impacted by closures and engaged partners in deliberating findings. Closures were associated with significant differences in prenatal care for mothers without a high school degree, including later initiation (0.30 months later when giving birth pre-closure, p < 0.05); fewer visits (1.53 fewer pre-closure, p < 0.05), and higher odds of inadequate prenatal care (OR = 5.7 pre-closure, p < 0.05). However, WIC and Medicaid buffered against these disruptions. Qualitative data highlighted increased travel burden, reduced choice, and weakened provider relationships. Closing rural birthing units disrupts prenatal care access, disproportionately affecting mothers without a high school degree. A cyclical, partnership-engaged approach using integrated data revealed impacts masked in aggregate analyses and offers a model for generating timely, actionable insights to guide rural maternal health policy and practice.
