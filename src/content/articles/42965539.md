### 重點摘要
本研究描述了PlantVillage Food應用程式的開發與驗證協議，這是一款利用人工智慧（Gemini大型語言模型）來改進美國大學生飲食評估的行動應用程式。該應用程式透過建立食物資料庫並整合大型語言模型，實現食物辨識與份量估計。研究將包含一項驗證研究（66名學生）以評估應用程式報告的能量與營養素攝取量與實際稱重紀錄的準確性，以及一項可行性隨機對照試驗（114名學生）來評估介入組（提供目標設定與遊戲化提示）與對照組（僅提供飲食評估）之間的依從性、可接受度與可用性。此研究旨在驗證AI飲食評估的準確性，並評估其在大學生族群中的可行性與使用者體驗。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了目前飲食評估方法耗時且準確性不足的研究空白，特別是透過整合大型語言模型於行動應用程式中，以自動化並提升食物辨識與份量估計的準確性。它為開發更有效率且易於使用的飲食追蹤工具提供了創新的途徑。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Leveraging Artificial Intelligence to Improve Dietary Assessment in US College Students: Development of Large Language Model Technology and Protocol for Testing the PlantVillage Food Application.
**Abstract:** To describe the development, validation, and feasibility of the randomized controlled trial (RCT) protocol of the PlantVillage Food application in the United States, a mobile app that leverages artificial intelligence for dietary assessment. Development included photographing foods with corresponding portion weights, creating a food database, and integrating a Gemini-based large language model for food recognition and portion estimation. Data collection will be conducted at the University of California, Davis. College students (aged 18-24 years) will be enrolled in the validation study (n = 66) and the feasibility RCT (n = 114). Participants randomized to the intervention group will receive goal setting and gamified nudging cues in addition to dietary assessment delivered through the app over 6 weeks. The control group will receive dietary assessment only. Accuracy will be assessed by comparing mean energy and nutrient intakes reported via the app to weighed food records in the validation study. Adherence, acceptability, and usability of the app will be compared by treatment arm in the feasibility RCT. Equivalence will be tested via mixed-effects linear regression (20% margin) in the validation study. In the feasibility RCT, ANCOVA and modified Poisson regression will be used to estimate mean differences and prevalence ratios, respectively.
