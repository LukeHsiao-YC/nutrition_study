### 重點摘要
本研究評估了一項基於手機的多組件嬰兒睡眠干預措施，對嬰兒和母親睡眠及母親心理健康的影響。此單盲隨機對照試驗招募了32對母嬰，干預組使用了為期四週的手機應用程式，提供嬰兒睡眠教育、監測和個性化支持。結果顯示，與對照組相比，干預組的嬰兒在睡眠效率和清醒時間等多個睡眠指標上有所改善，母親在睡眠效率、覺醒次數和憂鬱分數上也有顯著改善。研究結論指出，這種基於手機的家庭中心多組件睡眠干預能有效改善嬰兒和母親的睡眠，並減輕母親的憂鬱。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究證明了基於手機的嬰兒睡眠干預在短期內的有效性，但仍需未來的研究來評估這些干預措施的長期效果和穩定性，以確認其持久的影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effects of Mobile-Based Multicomponent Infant Sleep Intervention on Infants and Mothers: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
**Abstract:** This study evaluated the effects of a mobile-based multicomponent infant sleep intervention on infant and maternal sleep and maternal mental health. In this single-blind randomized controlled trial, mother-infant dyads with infants aged 3-12 months were recruited from an online parenting community in South Korea; 32 dyads were randomized and 29 were analyzed. From February to May 2021, the intervention group used a mobile application for 4 weeks providing infant sleep education, sleep monitoring, and personalized support. Sleep was assessed by actigraphy for 5 consecutive days at baseline, post-intervention (6 weeks), and follow-up (10 weeks). Maternal depression, fatigue, and sleep disturbance were assessed using the Patient-Reported Outcomes Measurement Information System. Linear mixed-effects models examined group differences over time. Compared with the control group, infants in the intervention group showed greater improvements from baseline in sleep efficiency and wake after sleep onset at 6 and 10 weeks and in average awake time at 10 weeks. Mothers in the intervention group showed greater improvements in sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset, and number of awakenings at 10 weeks. They also had a greater decrease in depression scores at 10 weeks and a difference in sleep disturbance scores favoring the intervention at 6 weeks, whereas fatigue did not differ between groups. A mobile-based, family-centered multicomponent sleep intervention was associated with greater improvements in selected infant and maternal sleep outcomes and maternal depression scores. Future studies should evaluate the long-term effectiveness and stability of these effects. This study was registered with the Clinical Research Information Service, a member of the World Health Organization International Clinical Trials Registry Platform (KCT0005283), registered on August 4, 2020 ( https://cris.nih.go.kr/cris/search/detailSearch.do?seq=22693&search_page=L ).
