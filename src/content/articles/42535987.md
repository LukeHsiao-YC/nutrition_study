### 重點摘要
這項混合方法研究評估了「兒童和成人照護食品計畫」(CACFP) 重新實施分級報銷制度對加州家庭式托兒所(FCCH) 提供者、贊助商和家庭的影響。研究發現，高達92.4%的提供者認為分級報銷金額不足，導致食物種類和品質下降，並顯著增加提供者和家長的經濟負擔。研究建議，為了支持FCCH兒童的健康飲食，應將每日每童的理想報銷金額提高至7.58美元，並涵蓋4餐。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究透過綜合性的混合方法調查與訪談，填補了對美國特定兒童食品計畫（CACFP）中重新實施的分級報銷政策，對家庭式托兒所服務品質與財務影響的實證評估空白。它具體量化了當前報銷不足所帶來的負面後果，並提出了未來政策調整的具體建議，有助於政策制定者改進計畫以更好地支持兒童健康與照護提供者。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** "It's just not worth it anymore": A Mixed Methods Study Examining the Impact of Reinstating Reimbursement Tiers in the Child and Adult Care Food Program.
**Abstract:** Assess impacts of the Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP) reinstatement of tiered reimbursements among family child care home (FCCH) providers, sponsors, and families. Mixed methods (survey and interviews). California. Randomly selected FCCH providers surveyed (n = 277); purposively selected FCCH providers (n = 10) and sponsors (n = 5), and conveniently selected families (n=5) interviewed. Perceptions of adequacy and impacts of tier reinstatement, and optimal amount and number of meals/snacks covered by CACFP. Linear and logistic regression for survey responses by tier; qualitative analysis using the framework method. Irrespective of tier, surveyed providers reported that the reinstated, tiered reimbursements were insufficient (92.4%), resulting in reduced food variety (39.1%) and quality (14.8%), and increased financial burden on providers (96.9%) and parents (24.0%). The ideal number of meals/snacks reimbursed was 4, and the ideal reimbursement amount for 2 meals/1 snack daily per child was $7.58 (currently $5.70 tier 1, $2.72 tier 2). Qualitative analysis identified 4 themes providing context on the challenges resulting from tier reinstatement: economic landscape, CACFP participation, food and care provided, and policy solutions. Increases to CACFP reimbursements and meals/snacks covered is recommended to support healthy eating by FCCH children.
