### 重點摘要
這項研究利用2021-2023年全國健康與營養檢查調查（NHANES）數據，探討美國成人（無論有無身心障礙）遵守24小時動態指引與自評健康狀況的關聯性。研究發現，無論有無身心障礙，完全遵守所有指引的成年人，其自評健康狀況為良好至優秀的機率顯著較高。總體而言，遵守的指引數量越多，自評健康狀況越佳，特別是包含身體活動的指引組合，對自評健康有正向關聯。對於身心障礙成人，遵守至少兩項指引即與較佳的自評健康狀況相關。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項橫斷面研究詳細分析了美國成人（特別是區分身心障礙與非身心障礙族群）遵守24小時動態指引與自評健康狀況之間的關聯性，填補了對不同族群中全面性健康行為效益理解的空白。然而，作為橫斷面研究，其無法建立因果關係。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Adherence to 24-hour Movement Guidelines and Self-rated Health among U.S. Adults with and without Disabilities.
**Abstract:** Self-rated health (SRH) reflects individuals' perceptions of their overall health status and is a strong predictor of morbidity and mortality. This study examined the associations between adherence to 24-hour movement guidelines and SRH among U.S adults with and without disabilities. This cross-sectional study utilized data from the 2021-2023 National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES), a nationally representative sample of U.S. adults (n = 8,153). Survey-weighted logistic regression with multiple imputation assessed associations between meeting individual and combined 24-hour movement guidelines and SRH by disability status. Sensitivity analysis was conducted using complete-case analysis with (1) survey-weighted complex-sample logistic regression and (2) unweighted logistic regression to assess the robustness of the findings. Meeting full 24-hour movement guidelines was associated with higher odds of good-to-excellent SRH compared with meeting none, among adults without disabilities (AOR = 4.63, 95% CI: 3.00-7.16), and adults with disabilities (AOR = 2.69, 95% CI: 1.73-4.17). Among adults without disabilities, meeting all three guidelines showed the greatest associations with good-to-excellent SRH (AOR = 4.63), while among adults with disabilities, the highest associations were observed for meeting physical activity (PA) + sedentary behavior (SB) (AOR = 3.47, 95% CI: 2.11-5.68) and PA + sleep (AOR = 3.10, 95% CI: 1.18-8.14) guidelines. The odds ratios of good-to-excellent SRH increased progressively with the number of guidelines met across all adults (met 1: AOR = 1.38; met 2: AOR = 2.24; met 3: AOR = 4.18; all P < 0.05). Adherence to multiple compositions of the 24-hour movement guidelines, especially those including PA, was positively associated with SRH. For adults with disabilities, meeting at least two guidelines was associated with better SRH than meeting none.
