### 重點摘要
本研究旨在分析區域性住房成本、財務壓力與家庭食物採購行為之間的關係，並探討SNAP參與及購買力的調節作用。研究利用2012-2013年全國家戶食物採購調查的橫斷面數據進行分析。結果顯示，在SNAP參與者中，住房成本與在SNAP購買力較低地區使用優惠券的機率增加有關；而在符合資格的非參與者中，較高的非自由支配支出與每週食物採購事件次數增加有關。總體而言，住房成本和財務壓力可能促使低收入家庭採取節省成本的策略來減少每月食物開支。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻探討了住房成本和財務壓力對低收入家庭食物採購策略的影響，但指出未來研究應蒐集更近期且縱向的數據，並採用準實驗分析方法。其目的在於更深入理解財務壓力與食物採購行為之間的關聯，以及SNAP參與或購買力是否能緩衝這些影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Housing Costs and Financial Strain May Prompt Families to Use Cost-Saving Strategies When Shopping for Food.
**Abstract:** To analyze the relationships between area-level housing costs, financial strain, and food shopping behaviors, as moderated by Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) participation and SNAP purchasing power. Cross-sectional, using nationally representative data from the Household Food Acquisition and Purchase Survey, 2012-2013. Among SNAP participants, housing costs were associated with 14% higher odds of using coupons in areas with low SNAP purchasing power (odds ratio [OR] = 1.14; 95% confidence interval [CI], 1.01-1.29). Among eligible non-participants, high non-discretionary spending was associated with a higher number of weekly food shopping events in areas with high and low purchasing power, respectively (OR = 1.23; 95% CI, 1.05-1.45; OR = 1.28, 95% CI, 1.06-1.55). Housing costs and financial strain may encourage low-income households to adopt cost-saving strategies to reduce their monthly food bills. Future research should collect more recent longitudinal Food Acquisition and Purchase Survey-style variables and use quasi-experimental analyses to better understand the associations between financial strain and food shopping and whether SNAP participation or purchasing power can buffer these relationships.
