### 重點摘要
美國先天性梅毒案例過去十年顯著增加，本研究旨在探討各州產前梅毒篩檢政策的應對及對CDC指南的遵循度。研究員分析2013-2023年間各州梅毒比率變化與2021年CDC指南遵循度，發現儘管整體比率上升，遵循或超越指南的司法管轄區先天性梅毒增幅最小，而無篩檢政策者增幅最大。然而，這些差異未達統計顯著性。結論指出，符合CDC指南的產前篩檢政策可能有助於預防先天性梅毒，但應搭配改善照護連續性與治療可近性等綜合措施。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了過去對於美國各州在先天性梅毒增加趨勢下，其產前梅毒篩檢要求、執行政策變化以及對CDC指南遵循程度的了解空白。然而，由於研究結果中政策遵循度與梅毒比率增幅的差異未達統計學上的顯著性，這暗示了未來需要更深入的研究來明確量化政策遵循度的直接影響，或者探討將政策與其他介入措施（如照護連續性與治療可近性）結合時的協同效應。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Prenatal Syphilis Screening Requirements: A Missed Opportunity to Prevent Congenital Syphilis, United States 2013-2023.
**Abstract:** Congenital syphilis cases in the U.S. have increased significantly over the past decade. The extent to which U.S. jurisdictions responded to the increases with changes in prenatal syphilis screening and enforcement policies and followed CDC sexually transmitted infection (STI) syphilis screening guidelines is unknown. CS and PS syphilis rates for U.S. jurisdictions (states and the District of Columbia) were obtained from the CDC. Prenatal syphilis screening requirements and enforcement policies were obtained from Nexis Uni or Westlaw. We used the Kruskal-Wallis rank-sum test to evaluate the relationship between the percent change in CS and PS syphilis rates (2013-2023) and 2021 CDC guideline compliance. CS and PS syphilis rates rose from 2013 to 2023. From 2016 to 2023, 15.7% (8) of jurisdictions added prenatal screening requirements, and none reduced their requirements. Jurisdictions with enforcement policies decreased by 28.6% (14 to 10) during this time. Jurisdictions without a screening policy (4) experienced the largest percent increase in CS rates from 2013 to 2023, while states that met or exceeded the 2021 CDC STI guidelines (14) had the lowest percent increase in CS and PS syphilis rates; however, the differences were not statistically significant. The smallest percent increase in CS and PS syphilis was observed among jurisdictions with prenatal syphilis screening policies that at least met the 2021 CDC STI screening guidelines. Prenatal syphilis policies that comply with the guidelines may help prevent CS cases but should be used in combination with other measures such as improved continuity of care and treatment accessibility.
