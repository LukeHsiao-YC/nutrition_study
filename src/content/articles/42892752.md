### 重點摘要
住院病人普遍存在營養不良及營養風險，儘管營養師主導的個別化營養介入被推薦，但在非重症住院病人常規實踐中的實證有限。本研究採用回溯性世代研究，分析中國一所三級醫院的電子病歷，將入院72小時內接受早期營養師介入的營養風險成年住院患者與對照組進行配對比較。結果顯示，早期營養師介入顯著降低了非預期性加護病房轉移和主要腸胃道事件的發生率，並增加了C反應蛋白的臨床顯著下降。這項研究結論指出，對於有營養風險的住院成年患者，早期營養師主導的營養介入與較低的病情惡化相關結果有關。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了日常實踐中，關於早期營養師介入對非重症住院病人病情惡化相關結果影響的實證空白。然而，研究也指出儘管常規進行營養風險篩查，早期營養師介入的比例仍偏低，這凸顯了實施上的落差，並建議未來應進行進一步的前瞻性評估以驗證這些發現。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Early dietitian-led nutrition intervention and unplanned intensive care unit transfer in hospitalized adults with nutritional risk: A retrospective cohort study.
**Abstract:** Malnutrition and nutritional risk are common among inpatients and are associated with adverse outcomes. Although dietitian-led individualized nutrition intervention is recommended, evidence from routine practice among non-critically ill inpatients remains limited. We aimed to evaluate the association between early dietitian-led nutrition intervention and deterioration-related outcomes among adults at nutritional risk. We conducted a retrospective cohort study using electronic medical records from a Chinese tertiary hospital (2016-2023). Adults aged 18-90 years with Nutritional Risk Screening 2002 scores ≥3 were included; patients directly admitted to intensive care unit were excluded. Patients receiving early dietitian-led nutrition intervention within 72 h after hospital admission were propensity score matched 1:1 to comparators. Outcomes were unplanned intensive care unit transfer, major gastrointestinal events after admission, and predefined clinically meaningful changes in laboratory indicators. Associations were estimated using logistic regression and reported as odds ratios (ORs) with 95% confidence intervals (CIs). Among 96,532 eligible patients, 16,661 (17.3%) received early dietitian-led nutrition intervention. After matching, 16,419 pairs were analyzed. Early dietitian-led nutrition intervention was associated with lower odds of intensive care unit transfer (3.0% vs. 3.4%; OR 0.88, 95% CI 0.77-0.99), major gastrointestinal events (4.0% vs. 4.4%; OR 0.89, 95% CI 0.80-0.99), and higher odds of clinically meaningful reduction in C-reactive protein (12.6% vs. 10.9%; OR 1.18, 95% CI 1.11-1.27). Early dietitian-led nutrition intervention was associated with lower odds of deterioration-related outcomes among hospitalized adults at nutritional risk. Limited early dietitian involvement despite routine nutritional risk screening suggests an implementation gap and warrants further prospective evaluation.
