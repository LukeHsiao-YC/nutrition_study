### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討時間營養學行為與糖尿病前期成人身體測量及身體組成之間的關聯。研究對120名糖尿病前期成人進行為期六個月的前瞻性縱向研究，評估其社會人口學資料、身體測量、身體組成及時間營養學行為。結果顯示，較晚的最後一餐時間與較高的體重和較大的腰圍相關，而飲食時間超出早上7點至晚上7點窗口的每小時偏差，也與體重增加有關，夜間零食頻率增加則與較高的身體質量指數相關。這表明時間營養學行為對糖尿病前期成人的體重和身體組成有顯著影響。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了時間營養學行為與糖尿病前期成人身體測量及身體組成關聯性的研究空白，此前這些關聯性在一般人群中有所發現，但在糖尿病前期族群中仍不明確。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Association between chrononutrition behaviours, anthropometric measurements, and body composition in adults with prediabetes.
**Abstract:** Evidence showed delayed eating timing is associated with higher risk of overweight and/or obesity in the general population. However, its associations with anthropometric measurements and body composition in individuals with prediabetes, remains ambiguous. This study investigated the association between chrononutrition behaviours, anthropometric measurements, and body composition in adults with prediabetes. This prospective longitudinal study enrolled 120 participants aged 54±15 years, who received standard care at healthcare clinics. Socio-demographic data were collected via an interviewer-administered questionnaire. Anthropometric measurements, body composition metrics and chrononutrition behaviours (assessed with the Malay-translated Chrononutrition Profile Questionnaire), were assessed at baseline and six months. Multiple linear regression analyses with adjustment for confounders were conducted. Participants had their first meal at 8.27am(±1.38hours) and their last meal at 7.35pm(±1.44 hours), with an average gap of 3.3 hours (±1.9hours) between the last meal and bedtime. A later last meal was associated with higher body weight(β: 0.68kg, 95%CI: 0.31,1.04) and larger waist circumference (β:1.38cm, 95%CI: 0.57,2.19). Each hour deviation from eating beyond the 7.00am to 7.00pm time window corresponded to an increase in body weight of 1.41kg(95%CI: 0.05,2.77).
