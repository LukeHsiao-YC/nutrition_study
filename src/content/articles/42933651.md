### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討母體甘露寡糖 (MOS) 補充對母豬生理、乳汁品質及仔豬斷奶期腸道復原力的影響。母豬從妊娠後期至哺乳期接受 MOS 補充，並評估其對仔豬生長、乳汁成分及斷奶期急性發炎挑戰後腸道反應的影響。結果顯示，母體 MOS 顯著增加了仔豬的生長、斷奶體重，並提高了乳汁中的脂肪和免疫球蛋白 IgA 含量。此外，母體 MOS 增強了仔豬在發炎挑戰下的腸道屏障功能並降低了腸道發炎反應，證明其能透過乳汁傳遞營養與免疫，提升仔豬腸道復原力。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究填補了母體甘露寡糖 (MOS) 補充是否能增強仔豬斷奶期復原力的研究空白。它證明了母體 MOS 透過改善乳汁的營養和免疫轉移，確實提升了仔豬在斷奶相關發炎壓力下的腸道健康與復原力。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Maternal Mannan-Oligosaccharide improves intestinal resilience of weanling pigs via enhancing milk-mediated immune transfer.
**Abstract:** Maternal nutrition during late gestation and lactation influences milk composition and early-life intestinal development. Whether maternal mannan-oligosaccharide (MOS) supplementation enhances offspring resilience during weaning remains unclear. The effects of maternal MOS supplementation on sow physiology, milk quality, offspring growth, and intestinal responses to an acute inflammatory challenge were evaluated. Sixty sows received a control diet or 800 mg MOS/kg diet from gestation day 85 to lactation day 21 (n = 30/group). Reproductive outcomes (litter size, birth weight, subsequent estrus, insemination, conception, and farrowing) and litter outcomes (survival, daily gain, and weaning weight at 21 d) were recorded. Sow feces were collected on gestation day 109 for microbiota analysis, plasma and colostrum at farrowing and milk on lactation day 14 for antioxidant, inflammatory, nutrient, and immunoglobulin analyses, as applicable. At weaning, 24 piglets from 12 litters received saline or lipopolysaccharide (LPS; 100 μg/kg). Plasma and intestinal tissues were collected 6 h later for antioxidant, morphology, barrier, and inflammation measurements. Mixed models assessed maternal diet and, in piglets, maternal diet × LPS, with litter accounted for. Compared with controls, MOS increased sow lactation feed intake (5.68 vs 5.21 kg/d; P = 0.036), piglet daily gain (224.22 vs 195.35 g/d; P < 0.001), and weaning weight (6.67 vs 5.91 kg; P < 0.001). Colostral fat and IgA were 5.26% vs 3.80% and 5.37 vs 3.00 mg/mL, respectively; day-14 milk fat and IgA were 7.72% vs 5.19% and 0.43 vs 0.19 mg/mL (all P < 0.05). Relative to LPS-challenged controls, maternal MOS increased jejunal occludin protein (0.78 vs 0.44, normalized to β-actin) and decreased jejunal IL-1β (146.31 vs 169.77 pg/mL; both P < 0.05). Maternal MOS supplementation improved milk-mediated nutritional and immune transfer and enhanced intestinal resilience of offspring during weaning-associated inflammatory stress in piglets.
