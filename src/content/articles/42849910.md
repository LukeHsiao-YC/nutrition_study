### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討馬匹輔助服務（包括治療性馬術和無馬匹接觸的馬廄活動）對患有自閉症並合併精神共病症狀的青少年之效益。一項包含236名6-16歲青少年的多中心隨機對照試驗顯示，與等待組相比，治療性馬術和馬廄活動均能顯著降低青少年的易怒和過動症狀，並改善情緒調節和社交行為。研究指出，治療性馬術在減少過動症方面比單純的馬廄活動有更顯著的額外效益。因此，馬匹輔助活動是改善自閉症合併精神共病青少年行為挑戰的有效介入方法，其中治療性馬術可能對過動症提供額外助益。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究專注於患有自閉症且合併精神共病症狀的青少年群體，這是一個在心理健康介入方面風險較高的群體，成功驗證了馬匹輔助活動對其行為挑戰的有效性。它不僅填補了針對此高風險亞群體實證介入效益的空白，更進一步區分了治療性馬術在降低過動症方面的獨特附加效益。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Therapeutic Horseback Riding for Youth With Autism and Psychiatric Comorbidities: A Randomized Trial.
**Abstract:** Autism spectrum disorder care is often complicated by co-occurring psychiatric symptoms. Equine-assisted services show promise for improving behavioral outcomes in this population. We aimed to examine benefits of equine-assisted services for youth with autism at higher risk for mental health interventions. A total of 236 youth with autism and psychiatric comorbidity (aged 6-16 years.) were enrolled in a randomized controlled multisite clinical trial, stratified by nonverbal IQ, and assigned to 1 of 3 intervention arms: therapeutic horseback riding (THR), no horse contact barn activity (BA), or waiting period followed by a BA/THR hybrid. Caregivers completed outcome measures. Speech outcomes were evaluated by blinded raters. Intervention methods expand those from a previous efficacy trial. Intent-to-treat analyses with 169 participants completing at least 1 intervention lesson revealed that compared with the waitlist control (n = 28), both THR (n = 74) and BA (n = 67) interventions showed clinically significant reductions in co-primary outcomes of irritability (P < .0001; THR effect size d = 1.15; BA effect size d = 1.04) and hyperactivity (THR P < .0001, effect size d = 1.06; BA P = .005, effect size d = 0.68). The THR group showed significantly greater reductions in hyperactivity compared with the BA group (P = .03; effect size d = 0.39) beginning at intervention week 4. Additional significant improvements in THR and BA groups vs the waitlist control were seen in emotional regulation and social behaviors. Equine-assisted activities are an effective intervention to address behavior challenges in youth with autism and psychiatric comorbidity; THR may offer additive reductions in hyperactivity.
