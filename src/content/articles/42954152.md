### 重點摘要
馬拉威的腦性麻痺兒童面臨高度營養不良風險，但缺乏相關的群體數據。本研究採用病例對照設計，比較了260名腦性麻痺兒童與259名對照組兒童的營養狀況。結果顯示，腦性麻痺組的發育遲緩、體重不足和消瘦盛行率均顯著高於對照組，餵食依賴性在腦性麻痺兒童中也更為普遍。研究指出嚴重的運動障礙、痙攣型腦性麻痺和餵食困難與較差的營養狀況相關。結論強調馬拉威腦性麻痺兒童的營養不良問題極為嚴峻，急需制定包容殘疾的營養指南、提供照護者餵食支持並將腦性麻痺照護整合至現有營養計畫。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇研究填補了馬拉威腦性麻痺兒童營養不良盛行率及相關影響因素的群體水平數據空白。未來的研究可以專注於評估所建議的干預措施（如殘疾包容性營養指南和照護者餵食支持）的有效性及實施挑戰。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Prevalence of Malnutrition and Contributing Factors in Children With Cerebral Palsy in Malawi: A Case-Control Study.
**Abstract:** Children with cerebral palsy (CP) are at increased risk of malnutrition, yet no population-level data exist for this group in Malawi. This study aimed to estimate the prevalence of undernutrition among children with CP in Malawi compared to peers without CP, and to explore contributing factors. This cross-sectional case-control study was conducted in Mangochi District, Malawi. A total of 260 children aged 2-18 years with clinician-confirmed CP were enroled alongside 259 age- and sex-matched controls. Nutritional status was assessed using standard anthropometric measures. Feeding difficulties and household food security were captured using existing tools. Linear regression examined factors associated with weight-for-age (WAZ) and BMI-for-age z-scores (BMIZ). Children with CP had significantly worse nutritional status across all anthropometric indicators compared to controls. Stunting (90.9% vs. 45.6%), underweight (81.5% vs. 33.2%), and wasting (42.2% vs. 12.3%) were substantially higher in the CP group (all p < 0.05). Feeding dependency was prevalent in 64.7% of children with CP versus 14.5% of controls. Severe motor impairment (GMFCS IV-V), spastic CP subtype, and greater feeding difficulties, were independently associated with lower WAZ and BMIZ scores. In conclusion, malnutrition is highly prevalent among children with CP in rural Malawi, far exceeding national and regional estimates. Disability-inclusive nutrition guidelines, caregiver feeding support, and integration of CP-specific care into existing nutrition programmes are urgently needed.
