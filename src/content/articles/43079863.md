### 重點摘要
菲律賓為應對營養不良問題，於八個市鎮推行為期增強型孕產婦營養與產前照護（ANC）介入計畫。本研究透過混合方法評估其採納度、可接受度與服務品質，發現整體接受度高，常規服務如身體測量和營養諮詢的採納率超過80%，但實驗室檢查利用率較低。質性分析顯示孕產婦對新服務高度讚賞且健康意識提升，然服務輸送存在地區差異、人力短缺、實驗室可近性及多種微量營養素補充劑供應不足等障礙。為提升計畫成效，需解決地理限制、擴展服務、確保物資供應並改善產後照護。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究評估了菲律賓衛生部新頒布的孕產婦營養與產前照護介入計畫在實際實施中的採納度、可接受度及服務品質，填補了此項國家級政策在地方層面實施效果的實證資料空白。然而，研究結果也揭示了服務輸送中的地理差異、人力資源限制、物資供應不穩及實驗室可近性等挑戰，這些仍是未來研究或政策制定需重點關注的限制與改進方向。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Uptake and Acceptability of Antenatal Care and Nutrition Services in the Philippines.
**Abstract:** In response to persistent micronutrient deficiencies and the double burden of malnutrition, the Philippines Department of Health (DOH) issued new guidelines to improve maternal health, leading to the implementation of an enhanced maternal nutrition and antenatal care (ANC) intervention package across eight municipalities of Samar and Zamboanga del Norte (ZDN). The aim is to strengthen routine ANC and introduce novel nutrition interventions. This mixed-methods study assessed the uptake, acceptability, and quality of the intervention package using quantitative surveys and qualitative focus group discussions (FGDs) with pregnant and postpartum women conducted before and after implementation. Overall acceptability was high, with uptake exceeding 80% for services such as anthropometric measurements, blood pressure checks, physical examinations, and nutrition counseling. However, utilization was lower for ultrasound examinations, blood tests, urinalysis, oral health checks, and thyroid screening. Comparative analysis of pre- and post-implementation FGDs indicated improved perceived service quality, strong appreciation for new components (e.g., mid-upper arm circumference measurement, gestational weight gain monitoring, multiple micronutrient supplements (MMS), dietary assessment, and nutrition counseling), and increased maternal awareness of prenatal nutrition, supplementation, weight monitoring, and regular ANC attendance. However, disparities in service delivery were observed between Samar and ZDN, particularly for dietary assessment, attributed to workforce shortages, time constraints, and heavy workloads in ZDN Persistent barriers included limited access to laboratories, transportation costs, MMS stock-outs, and suboptimal postnatal care. Addressing geographic constraints, expanding services in underserved areas, ensuring consistent MMS supply, and improving postpartum care are essential to enhancing program effectiveness.
