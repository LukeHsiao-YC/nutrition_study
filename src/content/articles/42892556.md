### 重點摘要
本研究透過貝氏網路和組件網路統合分析，比較了78項隨機對照試驗中，單純運動、單純飲食及兩者結合的介入方式，對代謝症候群各組成成分的影響。結果顯示，所有四種結構性運動介入皆能降低收縮壓和舒張壓，阻力訓練亦能降低空腹血糖；而部分運動結合飲食的介入（如地中海飲食加運動）則能降低腰圍、三酸甘油酯和空腹血糖。研究發現，腰圍和三酸甘油酯的聯合效果與運動和飲食的單獨效果總和一致，但其他四項結果則否。鑑於大多數比較的證據確定性偏低，本研究雖未明確指出偏好的單一介入方式，但提供了哪些運動、飲食及組合策略值得在未來進行具足夠檢定力的直接比較試驗。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了既有研究未能釐清單純運動、單純飲食及複合式介入方式，對代謝症候群各別組成成分的相對效果，以及探討複合式介入效果是否為各單獨介入效果簡單總和的研究空白。未來的研究限制在於目前證據的確定性普遍偏低，且尚未能明確識別出最佳的介入策略，因此需要更多具備足夠檢定力的直接比較試驗來進一步驗證這些發現。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Exercise, Dietary, and Combined Exercise-Dietary Interventions for Metabolic Syndrome: A Bayesian Network and Component Network Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials.
**Abstract:** Existing reviews have not clarified how exercise only, dietary only, and combined exercise dietary interventions compare across individual metabolic syndrome (MetS) components. We aimed to compare structured exercise-only, dietary-only, and combined exercise-dietary interventions in adults with MetS and overweight or obesity with an inactive or minimal-intervention control or other active interventions for waist circumference (WC), systolic blood pressure (SBP), diastolic blood pressure (DBP), fasting blood glucose (FBG), triglycerides (TG), and high-density lipoprotein cholesterol. We also assessed whether the combined effects were compatible with the sum of their component effects. We searched PubMed, Embase, Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials (CENTRAL), Web of Science, and Scopus from inception to December 2025 for randomized controlled trials. Comparative effects were estimated using Bayesian random-effects network meta-analysis; a frequentist component network meta-analysis was used to assess additivity, and certainty was assessed using the Confidence in Network Meta-Analysis framework. Seventy-eight trials with 8,452 participants were included. Relative to the control node, all four structured exercise-only interventions were associated with lower SBP and DBP, and resistance training was associated with lower FBG. Among nodes representing combined exercise-dietary interventions, combined exercise plus Mediterranean diet, moderate-intensity continuous training (MICT) plus caloric restriction, and MICT plus a low-carbohydrate diet were associated with lower WC, TG, and FBG, respectively, relative to the control node. For WC and TG, the results were consistent with a simple model that adds the estimated effects of exercise and diet, this model did not fit the other four outcomes. Certainty was low to very low for most comparisons. As a Bayesian synthesis, the findings do not identify a preferred intervention, but indicate which exercise, dietary and combined approaches warrant testing in adequately powered direct comparative trials. REGISTRY: This review was registered in the International Prospective Register of Systematic Reviews (PROSPERO) (CRD420251134142).
