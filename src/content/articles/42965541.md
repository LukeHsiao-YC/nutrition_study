### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估市售即時餐包是否能滿足懷孕婦女與妊娠糖尿病患者的營養需求。研究人員利用飲食模式建模，分析了來自10家美國供應商的12道即時餐包食譜，建構了64種理論日常飲食模式，並評估其營養充足性與飲食品質。結果顯示，這些餐包的平均飲食品質分數為55.4/100，屬於不佳；纖維、維生素D、鐵和鈣普遍不足，碳水化合物常超出妊娠糖尿病目標，且缺乏全穀物。因此，本研究結論市售即時餐包未能達到懷孕或妊娠糖尿病的特定營養目標。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻透過飲食模式建模，首次系統性地揭示了市售即時餐包在滿足懷孕和妊娠糖尿病特定營養需求方面的不足。未來的研究或應用可利用此建模方法來優化即時餐包的配方，使其更能符合特定人群的營養目標，並作為未來介入措施的指導。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Dietary Pattern Modeling Reveals Gaps in Meal Kits in Meeting Nutritional Goals of Gestational Diabetes.
**Abstract:** To test whether a selection of commercial meal kits meets pregnancy and gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) nutrient goals using a dietary pattern modeling approach. Cross-sectional modeling of 64 theoretical daily patterns was performed using 12 randomly selected recipes (4 breakfasts, 4 lunches, and 4 dinners) drawn from a database of 1,220 recipes across 10 US commercial meal kit providers. Nutritional adequacy for pregnancy and GDM was assessed via Dietary Reference Intakes, and overall diet quality was assessed via the Healthy Eating Index 2020. Modeled intakes produced an average Healthy Eating Index score of 55.4 out of 100, indicating suboptimal diet quality. Fiber, vitamin D, iron, and calcium were consistently below the recommended levels. Carbohydrate content often exceeded GDM targets, and no recipes included whole grains. Commercial meal kits did not meet pregnancy or GDM-specific nutrient goals. Dietary pattern modeling can identify gaps and guide the optimization of meal kits for future interventions.
