### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在評估紐約市增加社區健康工作者（CHW）計畫投資，對具有心血管疾病風險（肥胖、高血壓、糖尿病）的居民預期壽命、成本效益及健康不平等狀況的影響。研究運用國家轉變模型，預測額外投資可使全市平均預期壽命增加0.13年，每投資10萬美元可帶來14.72年的預期壽命回報，效益顯著。值得注意的是，收入較低、預期壽命較短及少數族裔的社區獲得的改善幅度更大，顯示此策略有助於減少社區間的健康差距。研究結論指出，額外投資CHW計畫能有效且具成本效益地提升全市預期壽命，同時縮小社區間的健康不平等。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究具體量化了在紐約市針對心血管疾病高風險族群，增加社區健康工作者（CHW）投資所帶來的預期壽命改善、成本效益以及對健康不平等的影響。這填補了在大型都市環境中，此類社區健康介入策略在經濟效益和促進健康公平性方面的具體實證數據空白，尤其在量化其對不同社會經濟背景社區的差異性影響方面提供了重要證據。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Quantifying benefit and cost-effectiveness of additional investment in community health workers for people with cardiovascular risks in New York City.
**Abstract:** To estimate the life expectancy gains, cost effectiveness, and impact on life expectancy inequality achieved by additional investment in community health worker (CHW) programs in New York City Methods: Using a state-transition model of mortality-driving conditions adapted to New York City anchored to 2021, we examined the impact of additional investment in CHWs for people with obesity, hypertension, and diabetes on (1) change in life expectancy, (2) health-inclusive (lifespan) return on investment (LROI), and (3) change in inequality of life expectancy distribution between neighborhoods. Inequality analyses included neighborhood-level baseline life expectancy, median income, and plurality race or ethnicity. Analyses were conducted in 2026. Scaling up CHWs results in a 0.13-year life expectancy gain on the citywide level, with favorable LROI of 14.72 years per $100,000 invested. Neighborhood-level gains range from 0.05 years to 0.33 years, while LROI ranges from 11.76 to 29.62 life-years/$100,000. In general, life expectancy gains and LROI were higher for lower-income, lower life expectancy, and minoritized race neighborhoods. Additional investment in CHW programs in New York City has the potential to cost-effectively raise the citywide life expectancy while reducing inequalities between neighborhoods.
