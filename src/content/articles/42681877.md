### 重點摘要
這項回溯性研究評估了抗腫瘤壞死因子alpha (anti-TNF-α) 合併維多珠單抗 (VDZ) 雙重生物製劑療法 (DBT) 對於單一生物製劑治療無效的兒童發炎性腸道疾病 (PIBD) 患者的療效。研究分析了21名兒童的22個治療案例，發現臨床緩解率從基線的9%顯著提升至3個月的68%，並在6個月和12個月分別維持在55%和50%。此外，內視鏡及組織學緩解率亦有顯著改善，且糞便鈣衛蛋白有下降趨勢，同時副作用事件較少。研究結論指出，對於單一生物製劑治療反應不佳的兒童PIBD患者，anti-TNF-α-VDZ雙重生物製劑療法能有效改善臨床、內視鏡和組織學結果。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了針對對單一生物製劑（特別是anti-TNF-α）治療無效的兒童發炎性腸道疾病患者，使用抗腫瘤壞死因子alpha與維多珠單抗雙重生物製劑療法的療效證據不足的研究空白。然而，由於本研究為回溯性且樣本數較小，未來需要更大規模、前瞻性的研究來進一步驗證這些初步發現並評估長期療效及安全性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Dual biologic therapy in inflammatory bowel disease, a retrospective cohort study of vedolizumab and anti-tumor necrosis factor alpha therapy in children.
**Abstract:** Despite advances in pediatric inflammatory bowel disease (PIBD) with biologics, remission rates remain at 47%-60% with loss-of-response rates at 60%-68%. Evidence for efficacy of dual biologic therapy (DBT) with anti-tumor necrosis factor alpha therapy (anti-TNF-α)- vedolizumab (VDZ) for children refractory to anti-TNF-α therapy is limited. This study aimed to describe rates of steroid-free clinical remission (CR), endoscopic and histological remission, trends in biochemistry, predictors of response, and adverse events in this population. We performed a retrospective study of children with PIBD treated with anti-TNF-α-VDZ DBT for 6-12 months from 2020 to 2023. Twenty-two treatment episodes were analyzed in 21 patients (17 Crohn's disease, 5 ulcerative colitis) with 13 continuing to 12 months. CR increased from 9% at baseline to 68% at 3 months (95% confidence interval [CI] 47%-83%, p < 0.01), 55% at 6 months (95% CI 35%-73%, p < 0.05) and 50% at 12 months (95% CI 31%-69%, p < 0.05). Seventy-two percent in total achieved CR during follow-up. Endoscopic remission increased from 5% to 38% (p = 0.02), histological remission from 5% to 29% (p = 0.046). Fecal calprotectin decreased from 930 to 500 μg/g at 3 months (p = 0.04), 680 μg/g at 6 months (p = 0.03), and 255 μg/g at 12 months (p = 0.12). Trends toward remission were observed in patients with colonic disease, incomplete response rather than loss of response to anti-TNF-α, and ulcerative colitis. Two significant adverse events occurred: pulmonary mucormycosis and herpes-simplex virus keratitis. Anti-TNF-α-VDZ DBT improved clinical, endoscopic, and histological outcomes in children refractory to biologic monotherapy. This study looked at children with inflammatory bowel disease who did not get better with one biologic. Using two biologic medicines together helped many children feel better, heal the gut lining, although some still needed surgery. Few had side effects.
