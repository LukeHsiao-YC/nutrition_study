### 重點摘要
高流量造口和腸道皮膚瘻管常導致脫水、電解質失衡和營養不良。本描述性隊列研究評估了一種自動化糜乳再灌注裝置在住院成人高流量造口或腸道皮膚瘻管患者中的技術可行性與安全性。在12名患者中，10名（83.3%）達到了技術可行性，且部分患者的腸道外營養需求減少，體重和血清白蛋白有所改善。然而，9名患者出現再灌注相關困難，包括2例導管移位，其中1例導致小腸穿孔。研究結論指出，自動化糜乳再灌注對選定患者而言技術上可行，但實際困難和裝置相關併發症仍普遍存在。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
該研究初步驗證了自動化糜乳再灌注裝置在處理高流量造口及腸道皮膚瘻管上的技術可行性，填補了此類自動化設備初步應用數據的空白。然而，由於其為描述性隊列研究且樣本量較小，它無法提供明確的患者選擇標準、全面的安全效益評估或確切的臨床益處。未來的研究仍需透過前瞻性研究，以更詳細地定義患者選擇、裝置安全性以及明確的臨床效益。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Feasibility and safety of an automated chyme reinfusion device for high-output stomas and enterocutaneous fistulas in hospitalized adults: A descriptive cohort study.
**Abstract:** High-output stomas and enterocutaneous fistulas cause dehydration, electrolyte disturbances, malnutrition, and prolonged dependence on parenteral nutrition. Chyme reinfusion may restore distal enteral continuity but remains technically demanding. Our descriptive cohort study evaluated the technical feasibility of chyme reinfusion using an automated chyme reinfusion device in adults with high-output stomas or enterocutaneous fistulas. We included consecutive hospitalized postoperative adults who initiated an automated chyme reinfusion device for a high-output stoma or enterocutaneous fistula at Geneva University Hospitals between 2022 and 2025. The primary outcome was technical feasibility, defined as successful use of the device without premature discontinuation due to device-related failure. Secondary outcomes were tolerance, device-related complications, changes in parenteral nutrition requirements, and body weight and serum albumin at baseline and Week 4. Twelve patients were included. Technical feasibility was achieved in 10 (83.3%). Chyme reinfusion was initiated a median of 37 days after surgery and maintained for a median of 42 days. Among 10 patients receiving parenteral nutrition at baseline, it was discontinued in three and reduced in three. Median body weight was 61.7 kg at baseline and 64.6 kg at Week 4. Median serum albumin was 25 and 29.5 g/L, respectively. Reinfusion-related difficulties occurred in nine patients. Two catheter migrations occurred, one causing small-bowel perforation requiring resection. Automated chyme reinfusion appears technically feasible in selected patients with high-output stomas or enterocutaneous fistulas, but practical difficulties and device-related complications are common. Prospective studies are needed to define patient selection, safety, and clinical benefit.
