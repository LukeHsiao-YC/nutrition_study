### 重點摘要
這篇敘述性綜述強調，生命最初1000天是腸道微生物組建立的關鍵窗口，對兒童成長、免疫發展和疾病風險具有終身影響。文章綜合了母體、圍產期特別是營養因素如何影響早期腸道菌群定植的證據。研究指出，營養，包括母乳餵養、配方奶使用以及早期輔食，對微生物組的組成和功能有著主導且可修改的影響。微生物組失調，常與營養不良和不當飲食相關，會導致多種急性和慢性疾病。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究指出，目前缺乏對於「健康」兒科微生物組的通用定義，這限制了針對性干預措施的開發。未來的研究需要進一步驗證恢復微生物平衡的策略，尤其是在營養脆弱的族群中。文章同時提及新興的指標，如微生物組年齡Z分數和功能性微生物組分析，可能有助於定義微生物組的成熟度和評估治療效果，暗示這仍是一個待探索的領域。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Nutritional and environmental determinants of maturation and disruption of the early life gut microbiome: A narrative review.
**Abstract:** The first 1000 days of life represent a critical window for gut microbiome assembly, with lifelong implications for child growth, immune development, and disease risk. This review synthesizes evidence on maternal, perinatal, and especially nutritional factors that influence early-life intestinal colonization and highlights the consequences of microbial disruptions during this period. In utero exposures and birth-associated factors can profoundly shape microbial development, reducing diversity and beneficial taxa. Nutrition exerts a particularly dominant and modifiable influence: breastfeeding supports Bifidobacterium-rich communities, while formula use and early complementary feeding shape microbiota diversity and metabolic function. Dietary quality, fiber intake, and food diversity are key determinants of microbial maturation and resilience. Undernutrition and inadequate diets contribute to microbial dysbiosis, creating a self-reinforcing cycle of malabsorption, growth failure, and long-term metabolic consequences. Early microbial perturbations are associated with a range of acute and chronic diseases, including necrotizing enterocolitis, obesity, type 1 diabetes, inflammatory bowel disease, and atopic disorders. Strategies to restore microbial balance require further validation, particularly in nutritionally vulnerable populations. The absence of a universal definition for a "healthy" pediatric microbiome limits the development of targeted interventions. Emerging metrics, such as microbiota-for-age z-scores and functional microbiome profiling, may help define microbiota maturity and therapeutic efficacy.
