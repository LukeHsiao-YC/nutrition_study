### 重點摘要
這項單中心回溯性研究旨在描述澳洲兒童極早發炎性腸道疾病（VEO-IBD，診斷年齡小於6歲）和嬰兒期發炎性腸道疾病（IO-IBD，小於等於2歲）的臨床特徵、表型和早期治療。研究分析了52名兒童的數據，發現潰瘍性結腸炎是最常見的診斷，且IO-IBD與VEO-IBD在臨床和腸道表型上存在顯著重疊。研究亦發現IO-IBD組有單基因疾病的案例，並確認了早期原發性硬化性膽管炎是一個重要的次要發現。這些數據支持根據表型進行基因評估，並進一步研究VEO-IBD的早期肝膽疾病。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究提供澳洲單一中心在極早發炎性腸道疾病（VEO-IBD）兒童的詳細臨床表型和早期管理數據，填補了此特殊族群本地化數據的空白。它強調了單基因疾病在嬰兒期發炎性腸道疾病（IO-IBD）中的潛在角色，並突出了早期原發性硬化性膽管炎的重要性。未來的研究應著重於更廣泛的基因評估以辨識更多單基因疾病，並深入探討VEO-IBD中早期肝膽疾病的機制與治療。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Clinical phenotypes of very early onset inflammatory bowel disease in Australian children: A single-centre retrospective study.
**Abstract:** Very early onset inflammatory bowel disease (VEO-IBD), diagnosed at <6 years old, includes a subset of infantile onset IBD (IO-IBD, ≤2 years). We describe presenting clinical features, phenotypes, and early management of children with VEO-IBD at a single tertiary centre. Retrospective cohort study of children with VEO-IBD diagnosed at Queensland Children's Hospital from January 2010 to March 2025. In 52 children, (24 IO-IBD; 28 VEO-IBD), median Paediatric Ulcerative Colitis Activity Index on presentation was 35 (interquartile range [IQR] 15), similar in both groups (35 IO-IBD; 39.5 VEO-IBD). Ulcerative colitis (UC) was the most frequent diagnosis (50%; 38% Crohn's disease; 12% IBD-Unclassified [IBD-U]). Among children with UC/IBD-U, pancolitis was common in both groups (57.1% IO-IBD; 66.7% VEO-IBD). Paris-classified anatomic phenotype did not differ between IO-IBD and VEO-IBD. Atypical colitis, upper gastrointestinal involvement, and granulomas occurred in 23%, 38% and 35% of cases, respectively. Prior allergic/atopic diagnoses were documented in 44%. Primary sclerosing cholangitis (PSC) was identified in 8% within 12 months of IBD diagnosis. Two monogenic disorders were identified, among 38 genotyped children, with severe perianal disease and successful bone marrow transplantation. Corticosteroids were used in 67% (35/52); 86% (30/35) responded without biologic rescue; five required anti-tumour necrosis factor therapy. One child required early colectomy. IO-IBD and VEO-IBD demonstrated substantial overlap in presenting clinical and intestinal phenotypes. Monogenic disease was confined to IO-IBD, while early PSC was a notable secondary finding. These data support phenotype-guided genetic evaluation and further study of early hepatobiliary disease in VEO-IBD.
