### 重點摘要
地中海飲食的依從性顯著下降，尤其在年輕世代，部分歸因於對其健康益處的認識不足，凸顯了營養教育的重要性。本研究開發並驗證了ICARO-食物知識問卷（I-FKQ），旨在評估兒童、青少年和成人對地中海飲食的相關知識。該問卷顯示出滿意的心理測量學特性，包括良好的內容效度、表面效度、重測信度與整體內部一致性。然而，其建構效度僅在成人群體中得到驗證，因此目前主要適用於成人族群的教育與公共衛生計畫。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究開發並驗證了I-FKQ問卷，填補了評估不同年齡層對地中海飲食知識的工具空缺。然而，該問卷的建構效度僅在成人群體中得到確認，這限制了其在兒童和青少年族群中的全面應用，需待未來進一步的心理測量學評估。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Development and validation of a questionnaire on food knowledge and perception of the Mediterranean diet: the ICARO food knowledge questionnaire.
**Abstract:** Adherence to the Mediterranean diet has significantly declined over the last decades, particularly among younger generations, due to socioeconomic factors, increased consumption of ultra-processed foods and limited awareness of its positive effects on health. Addressing these barriers necessitates the enhancement of nutritional education and improved access to healthy food options. This study aims to develop and validate a comprehensive questionnaire, the ICARO-Food Knowledge Questionnaire (I-FKQ), to assess food knowledge related to the Mediterranean diet among children, adolescents, and adults. In the context of the ICARO Study, we recruited a sample of 46 parents (for children), 111 adolescents, and 164 adults, from November 2023 to July 2024. Content validity was established through a review by an expert panel, which refined the questionnaire. Assessments of face validity indicated that the I-FKQ is manageable and user-friendly for online administration. Item analysis demonstrated acceptable difficulty and discrimination indices. High test-retest reliability was observed, with strong correlations in scores between the two testing occasions, indicating robust temporal stability. All constructs of the questionnaire demonstrated adequate Cronbach's α and Pearson's correlation coefficients. The overall internal consistency of the questionnaire was acceptable (Cronbach's α = 0.848), although Section 2 showed slightly lower reliability (Cronbach's α = 0.662). Construct validity, evaluated only in adults, was supported by expected differences in scores according to educational level. The I-FKQ demonstrated satisfactory overall psychometric properties and reliable temporal stability. However, while fundamental reliability and face validity parameters were confirmed across multiple age groups, construct validity was established exclusively within the adult population. This specific lack of structural validation currently restricts the full applicability of the instrument in pediatric and adolescent groups. Consequently, pending further psychometric evaluation in younger demographics, the I-FKQ is best suited to support educational and public health initiatives targeting adult populations.
