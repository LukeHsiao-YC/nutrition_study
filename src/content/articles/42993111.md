### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在評估美國「婦女、嬰兒和兒童」(WIC) 計劃參與者在COVID-19大流行期間（2021年）和之後（2023年）的糧食安全、現金價值福利（CVB）充足性感知以及WIC電子福利轉帳卡和應用程式的滿意度。研究透過橫斷面調查收集了來自11個WIC機構的大量參與者數據。結果顯示，2023年糧食不安全的盛行率顯著高於2021年，這與疫情相關援助計畫的結束有關，儘管參與者調整了其糧食援助來源模式。然而，對WIC卡片和應用程式的滿意度在2023年有所提升，這可能反映了WIC現代化工作的成效。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了COVID-19大流行時期及其後，WIC參與者糧食安全狀況、福利充足性感知與服務滿意度變化方面的實證數據空白，特別是疫情相關緊急援助計畫結束後的具體影響。然而，由於研究採用橫斷面調查而非縱向研究設計，其限制在於無法追蹤相同個體的長期變化，這意味著它可能無法完全捕捉個人層面的適應或影響機制。未來的研究可以考慮採用縱向設計來更深入地探討這些變化。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Changes in WIC Participants' Experiences During and After the COVID-19 Pandemic.
**Abstract:** To evaluate Women, Infants, and Children (WIC) participants' food security, adequacy of cash value benefits (CVB), and WIC Electronic Benefit Transfer card and app satisfaction during (2021) and after (2023) the COVID-19 pandemic. Cross-sectional surveys. 11 US WIC agencies. WIC participants, 2021 (n = 25,808; 4.5% response rate), 2023 (n = 20,542; 5.5% response rate). Food security, food assistance, CVB adequacy, WIC card and app satisfaction. Generalized linear mixed models, with time as a fixed effect and state as a random effect; significance was set at P < 0.05; models were adjusted for age, race, education, and work status. In 2023, food insecurity prevalence was higher than in 2021 (56.8% vs 72.8%, P < 0.001). Pandemic-Electronic Benefit Transfer (33.5% vs 30.6%, P < 0.001) and school meals (29.0% vs 19.6%, P < 0.001) were lower in 2023 than in 2021; food from family/friends (24.0% vs 32.7%, P < 0.001), food pantries (25.4% vs 33.0%, P < 0.001), and Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program participation (42.0% vs 44.5%, P < 0.001) were higher; and perception of CVB adequacy (15.0% vs 31.6%, P < 0.001), satisfaction with the WIC card (3.43 vs 3.69, P < 0.001), and the app (3.35 vs 3.60, P < 0.001) were higher in 2023 than in 2021. Although participants altered their food assistance patterns from 2021 to 2023, food insecurity was higher in 2023 as pandemic-related programs ended. Higher WIC card and app satisfaction may reflect WIC's modernization efforts.
