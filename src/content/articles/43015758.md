### 重點摘要
非傳染性疾病（NCDs）預計將於2030年成為非洲主要的發病和死亡原因，其中高血壓的診斷與治療存在顯著缺口。本研究在盧安達農村地區對192名無高血壓病史的成年人進行了橫斷面社區篩檢，評估未診斷高血壓與中心型肥胖的盛行率，並探討篩檢方法的適用性。結果顯示，近三分之一的受試者（29.69%）患有未診斷的高血壓，中心型肥胖盛行率高達45.3%至57.3%，其中年齡大於40歲是中心型肥胖的重要風險因子。本研究證明開放空間的社區篩檢被證明是適當且可接受的，對於識別這些未被發現的健康問題具有重要意義。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究揭示了盧安達農村地區未診斷高血壓和中心型肥胖的實際盛行率遠高於全國已診斷的數據，填補了NCD照護鏈中早期篩檢和診斷的知識空白。它也證明了開放空間的社區篩檢模式在資源有限地區的可行性與接受度，為未來擴大NCD篩檢策略提供了實證依據。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Undiagnosed elevated blood pressure and central obesity among adults in rural Rwanda: risk factors and scalability of a pilot community outreach screening to close care cascade gaps.
**Abstract:** Non-communicable diseases (NCDs), driven by a rising burden of hypertension, are projected to become the leading cause of morbidity and mortality in Africa by 2030. This increasing burden is attributed to substantial gaps in the NCD and hypertension care cascade. For instance, in Rwanda, 22% of hypertensive people have been diagnosed and are receiving treatment. We aimed to assess the prevalence of undiagnosed elevated blood pressure and central obesity among adults during an outreach screening and to evaluate the approach's scalability. We conducted a cross-sectional screening during the International Nursing Day commemoration at Rusumo football pitch in Butaro, Rwanda. Up to 192 consenting adults with no prior diagnosis of hypertension were screened for elevated blood pressure, Body Mass Index (BMI), Waist-to-Hip Ratio (WHR), and Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR), and data on risk factors were collected. The mean age was 36 years (SD = 14.2 years), with 114 (59.4%) males, all from wealth categories 1, 2, and 3. Dietary sodium intake was low, with 169 (88.0%) consuming below 2 g per day. Tobacco smoking prevalence was 10.4% (95%CI = 6.8; 15.6%), while 63.5% (95%CI = 56.5; 70.1%) consumed alcohol. Central obesity, defined by waist-to-hip ratio (WHR), was prevalent in 45.3% (95% CI = 38.4%; 52.5%); using waist-to-height ratio (WHtR ≥ 0.5), obesity prevalence was higher, at 57.3% (95% CI = 50.2%; 64.1%). Meanwhile, 29.69% (95% CI: 23.62%- 36.57%) screened positive for elevated blood pressure. Being 40 years of age or older was associated with higher odds of central obesity than being younger than 40 years (aOR = 4.52; 95% CI = 2.01, 10.17). Additionally, being male compared to being female (aOR = 0.20, 95%CI = 0.08; 0.50) and fasting at least once monthly, compared to never fasting at all (aOR = 0.43, 95%CI = 0.19; 0.96), was associated with lower odds of obesity as measured through WHtR. Outdoor screening was seen as acceptable by 94.3% of participants and appropriate by 98.95% participants. Nearly a third of adults in Burera had undiagnosed elevated blood pressure, a proportion almost double the national estimated prevalence of diagnosed hypertension. Additionally, almost half of the adults have central obesity, with being of age 40 and above being a significant risk. Open-space screening proved appropriate and acceptable.
