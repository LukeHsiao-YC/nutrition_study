### 重點摘要
產後體重滯留(PPWR)是婦女長期過重/肥胖及心血管代謝疾病的重要危險因子，而哺乳被視為一個可改變的因素。本研究追蹤779名中國產婦，透過群組基礎軌跡模型分析PPWR軌跡，並評估不同哺乳模式及累積哺乳指數(CBI)與PPWR/顯著PPWR(SPPWR)的動態關聯。研究識別出三種PPWR軌跡，結果顯示早期純母乳哺育及較高的CBI與更佳的體重恢復相關，且其保護效果隨哺乳時間增加。早期低哺乳意願/狀態的群體被指出是干預SPPWR的重要窗口。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了過去對於哺乳對產後體重滯留的動態影響與時間變化關係不清楚的研究空白，特別是透過軌跡分析來描繪PPWR的變化模式。它提出累積哺乳指數(CBI)作為初步的累積暴露指標，這暗示該指標可能需要進一步的驗證或在不同人群中的適用性研究。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Dynamic association between breastfeeding and weight retention within the first year postpartum: A trajectory analysis study based on the China multicentre mother-infant cohort.
**Abstract:** Postpartum Weight Retention (PPWR) is a major risk factor for long-term maternal overweight/obesity and cardiometabolic diseases. Breastfeeding is a key modifiable factor for PPWR, but its dynamic impact and time-varying relationship with PPWR remain unclear. To characterize PPWR trajectories, examine time-varying associations of breastfeeding with PPWR/substantial PPWR (SPPWR, ≥5.0 kg), and test whether early breastfeeding predisposition predicts SPPWR. This prospective multicentre cohort study was conducted among 779 Chinese mothers enrolled in the Phoenix Study, with follow-ups at 1, 4, 6, and 12 months postpartum. Group-based trajectory modeling (GBTM) identified PPWR trajectories. Breastfeeding was characterized by mode (EBF, Exclusive Breastfeeding; MBF, Mixed Breastfeeding; NBF, No Breastfeeding) and the Cumulative Breastfeeding Index (CBI) integrating intensity and duration at 1, 6, and 12 months. Linear mixed-effects models and logistic regression examined associations with PPWR/SPPWR; multinomial logistic regression evaluated CBI with trajectory membership. Three PPWR trajectories emerged from 714 participants: low retention (26.6%), moderate retention (59.4%), and high retention (14.0%). EBF and a higher CBI correlated with more favorable weight recovery, with its protective effect after childbirth increasing as the breastfeeding period gets longer. Each 1-point increase in CBI was associated with lower odds of being classified into the high retention trajectory (OR= 0.86, 95% CI: 0.76-0.98, P= 0.020) and the moderate retention trajectory (OR= 0.78, 95% CI: 0.65-0.94, P= 0.007) versus the low retention trajectory. Separately, 'Low Breastfeeding Predisposition/State Class' from Latent Class Analysis (LCA) was associated with a higher risk of SPPWR at 6 months postpartum (OR= 0.60, 95% CI: 0.40-0.90, P= 0.015). Early exclusive breastfeeding and greater cumulative breastfeeding exposure were strongly associated with favorable maternal weight trajectories. Low early breastfeeding predisposition/state highlighted an early intervention window for SPPWR. The CBI may serve as a preliminary cumulative exposure indicator in this Chinese cohort.
