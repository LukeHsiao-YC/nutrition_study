### 重點摘要
儘管全球倡議純母乳哺育，其普及率仍低，因此本研究旨在評估行動健康（mHealth）應用程式介入對促進孕婦及產後母親母乳哺育的成效。透過系統性文獻回顧和統合分析13項隨機對照試驗，結果顯示mHealth介入能顯著提升母乳哺育的自我效能、知識與信心。然而，對於母乳哺育的起始率、純母乳哺育率和持續時間的影響，證據則較不一致且證據力較弱，存在高度異質性。整體而言，mHealth在增強心理層面有潛力，但對實際哺育行為的直接效益仍需進一步驗證。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了mHealth介入對母乳哺育成效缺乏全面性評估的空白，明確指出其在提升自我效能、知識與信心方面的潛力。然而，研究也揭示mHealth對於純母乳哺育率和持續時間的影響缺乏一致且強大的證據，這反映了未來研究在評估直接效益上的限制。此外，結果中存在的高度異質性也暗示了需要更標準化或更具針對性的mHealth介入策略。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Evaluating the Effectiveness of mHealth Application Interventions to Promote Breastfeeding Amongst Pregnant Women and Postpartum Mothers: A Global Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
**Abstract:** Despite global recommendations, less than 40% of infants ≤ 6 months are exclusively breastfed (EBF) reflecting socio-economic, cultural, and systemic barriers. Mobile health (mHealth) interventions (delivered through mobile phone applications to support medical/public health practices) have been developed to support breastfeeding, but their effectiveness remains unclear. In this systematic review we searched PubMed, Embase, CINAHL, Web of Science, and Cochrane Library from 1 January 2010 to 1 May 2025 for randomised controlled trials in English involving pregnant or postpartum women up to 1 year, assessing the effectiveness of any form of mHealth intervention aimed at the mother, excluding qualitative studies (PROSPERO: CRD420251032811). The main outcome was EBF ≤ 6 months; Secondary outcomes were EBF at later time points, overall breastfeeding duration and breastfeeding self-efficacy up to 12 months. Quantitative synthesis used Harvest plots, binomial calculations, and meta-analysis. Of 3102 retrieved records, 13 studies (3269 women) were included. Interventions typically combined mobile apps with text messaging or telelactation. Binomial calculations/harvest plots suggested strong evidence for improved breastfeeding self-efficacy, knowledge, and confidence, with less evidence for effects on initiation, exclusivity, and duration.
