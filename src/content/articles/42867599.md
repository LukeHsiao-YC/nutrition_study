### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討台灣孕婦的知覺壓力、產前哺乳自我效能與哺乳意圖之間的關聯。這項橫斷面研究納入200名台灣中部孕婦，結果顯示知覺壓力與產前哺乳自我效能及哺乳意圖呈負相關，而產前哺乳自我效能則與哺乳意圖呈正相關。多重迴歸分析指出，較高的產前哺乳自我效能、產前哺乳計畫及年齡與較強的哺乳意圖獨立相關。研究結論建議產前照護應評估孕婦的哺乳信心並討論餵養計畫。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究釐清了台灣孕婦知覺壓力、產前哺乳自我效能與哺乳意圖之間的關聯，並識別出產前哺乳自我效能及哺乳計畫是哺乳意圖的重要獨立預測因子。然而，本研究為橫斷面設計，無法確立因果關係，且僅探討哺乳意圖而非實際哺乳結果；因此，未來需要進行前瞻性研究，以確定針對這些因子提供支持是否能改善產後實際哺乳結果。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Associations Among Perceived Stress, Prenatal Breastfeeding Self-Efficacy, and Breastfeeding Intention Among Pregnant Women in Taiwan.
**Abstract:** To examine associations among perceived stress, prenatal breastfeeding self-efficacy, and breastfeeding intention among pregnant women in Taiwan. This cross-sectional study included 200 pregnant women from a prenatal outpatient clinic in central Taiwan. Participants completed the Perceived Stress Scale, Prenatal Breastfeeding Self-Efficacy Scale, and Infant Feeding Intentions Scale. Descriptive, bivariate, and multiple regression analyses were performed. Mean scores were 16.37 (SD = 5.15) for perceived stress, 73.25 (SD = 13.08) for prenatal breastfeeding self-efficacy, and 9.87 (SD = 3.71) for breastfeeding intention. Perceived stress was negatively correlated with prenatal breastfeeding self-efficacy (r = - .36, p < .001) and breastfeeding intention (r = - .18, p = .013), whereas prenatal breastfeeding self-efficacy was positively correlated with breastfeeding intention (r = .29, p < .001). In multiple regression, prenatal breastfeeding self-efficacy (β = 0.17, p = .018), a prenatal plan to breastfeed (β = 0.49, p < .001), and age (β = 0.12, p = .049) were significantly associated with breastfeeding intention, explaining 35% of the variance. In sensitivity analysis excluding the breastfeeding-plan variable, prenatal breastfeeding self-efficacy remained significantly associated with breastfeeding intention (β = 0.29, p < .001). Higher prenatal breastfeeding self-efficacy and a prenatal plan to breastfeed were independently associated with stronger breastfeeding intention, whereas perceived stress was associated with weaker intention only in bivariate analyses. Prenatal care may provide opportunities to assess breastfeeding confidence and discuss feeding plans. Prospective studies are needed to determine whether support targeting these factors improves postpartum breastfeeding outcomes.
