### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估DASH飲食對血清陽性類風濕性關節炎患者疾病活動度和治療結果的影響。這項為期12週的隨機對照試驗納入82名患者，分為DASH飲食組和常規飲食對照組。結果顯示，DASH飲食組在多個疾病活動度指標（如DAS-28 ESR）和患者報告結果（如疼痛、整體健康和生活品質）方面均有顯著改善。因此，DASH飲食介入可能為血清陽性類風濕性關節炎患者提供臨床意義上的益處，支持其作為輔助治療的潛在作用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究證明DASH飲食模式可能作為輔助管理血清陽性類風濕性關節炎的有效飲食方法，填補了該領域非藥物飲食介入的潛在研究空白。然而，研究存在相對較高的患者流失率，這限制了結果的解釋和普遍性，未來研究應關注如何提高患者依從性並減少流失。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** A 12-week randomized controlled trial of the dietary approaches to stop hypertension (DASH) diet in seropositive rheumatoid arthritis: Effects on disease activity and outcomes.
**Abstract:** Dietary interventions may improve rheumatoid arthritis (RA) outcomes through anti-inflammatory and metabolic pathways. This study aimed to evaluate the effects of the Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension (DASH) diet on disease activity and treatment outcomes in patients with seropositive RA. In this 12-week parallel-group randomized controlled trial, 82 patients with seropositive RA were randomly assigned to a DASH diet intervention (n = 40) or a control group maintaining their usual diet (n = 42). The primary outcome was change in disease activity assessed using the Disease Activity Score in 28 joints based on erythrocyte sedimentation rate (DAS-28 E SR). Secondary outcomes included change in DAS-28 based on C-reactive protein (DAS-28 CRP), as well as tender and swollen joint counts, ESR, CRP, visual analogue scale (VAS) scores for pain and general health, the Health Assessment Questionnaire (HAQ), and the Short Form-36 (SF-36). Adherence to the intervention was evaluated using the DASH diet score. Assessments were performed at baseline and at weeks 4, 8, and 12. Data were analyzed according to the intention-to-treat principle. Repeated clinical outcomes were analyzed using robust mixed ANOVA or generalized estimating equations (GEE), as appropriate. Of the 82 randomized patients, 49 completed the 12-week study. Adherence to the DASH diet increased significantly in the intervention group compared with the control group (p < 0.001). From baseline to Week 12, DAS-28 ESR decreased by 0.92 points in the DASH group versus 0.17 points in the control group, corresponding to a 0.75-point greater reduction (group × time interaction, p = 0.001). Significant between-group differences in change were also observed for DAS-28 CRP (-0.62), ESR (-3.90 mm/h), VAS-pain (-2.19), VAS-general health (-2.12), and HAQ (-0.38) (all group × time p˂0.05). For SF-36, significant between-group differences in change were observed for physical functioning (+10.15), role-physical (+47.30), bodily pain (+22.17), vitality (+18.52), role-emotional (+38.61), and mental health (+12.06) (all group × time p < 0.05). CRP, tender and swollen joint counts did not show significant group × time interactions (p > 0.05). Furthermore, in the intervention group, improvements in DASH diet adherence were negatively correlated with changes in DAS-28 ESR and DAS-28 CRP scores (both p < 0.05). A 12-week DASH dietary intervention may provide clinically meaningful improvements in disease activity and patient-reported outcomes in patients with seropositive RA. These findings support the potential role of the DASH dietary pattern as a promising dietary approach to complement the management of RA. However, the relatively high attrition rate should be considered when interpreting the findings. This trial is registered with Clinicaltrials.gov (ID: NCT07170033). https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07170033.
