### 重點摘要
本研究旨在解決青少年在數位健康溝通中難以參與的挑戰，並特別關注訊息「如何」表達而非「傳達什麼」的不足之處。研究採用了一種創新的AI語言客製化方法，利用大型語言模型將健康訊息調整為符合青少年個人的溝通風格。透過一項為期兩週、包含587名青少年的預先註冊隨機對照試驗，本研究旨在探討此語言客製化策略是否能有效提升說服反應並影響植物性午餐的選擇。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了數位健康傳播中，現有訊息客製化策略多聚焦於「溝通內容」而非「訊息表達方式」的研究空白。它透過探討AI自動化的語言客製化，為青少年健康溝通提供了創新且具潛力的新視角。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** The effect of AI-based linguistically tailored health messages on plant-based lunch choices of adolescents: A randomised controlled trial.
**Abstract:** Adolescents are a notoriously difficult audience to engage in digital health communication. While many tailoring strategies focus on tailoring what is communicated, far less attention has been paid to how messages are expressed. Addressing this gap, we examined automated AI-based linguistic tailoring as an innovative approach to personalised health communication. Using large language model-based text style transfer, we tailored health messages to adolescents' individual communication styles to examine whether linguistic tailoring enhances persuasive responses and dietary behaviour.
