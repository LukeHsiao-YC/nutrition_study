### 重點摘要
混合管灌飲食(BTF)作為兒童長期管灌餵食的替代方案日益普及，本研究旨在探討荷蘭兒科醫療專業人員對BTF的經驗與態度。透過對252名荷蘭兒科專業人員（主要是營養師和兒科醫師）進行橫斷面問卷調查，結果顯示他們對BTF的熟悉度高且態度普遍積極，並認同其改善餵食耐受度等益處，但主要挑戰為照顧者負擔。然而，研究揭示了實踐上的顯著差異，且對現有國家指南的認識不足，未來需要更多實證研究與專業培訓以支持一致的家庭導向實施。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究揭示了荷蘭兒科醫療專業人員對混合管灌飲食的經驗、態度和實踐現狀，填補了對此特定群體看法的知識空白。然而，研究發現專業人員對現有國家指南的認知度低，且實踐上存在顯著差異，未來仍需要更多干預性研究、制定實證建議以及加強專業培訓，以支持一致且以家庭為中心的實施。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Experience and attitudes toward blended tube feeding in Dutch pediatric healthcare professionals: A cross-sectional survey.
**Abstract:** Blended tube feeding (BTF)-enteral nutrition based on whole foods-is increasingly adopted as an alternative to commercial formulas in children requiring long-term tube feeding. While parents often initiate this practice, healthcare professionals are crucial for its safe and effective implementation. This study explored the perspectives of Dutch pediatric healthcare professionals regarding BTF to identify knowledge gaps and support future clinical guidance. We performed a cross-sectional survey in 252 participants based in the Netherlands from February to May 2025. The self-developed 30-item questionnaire addressed BTF knowledge, attitudes, and experiences. Responses were analyzed descriptively. A total of 252 respondents completed the survey, primarily pediatric dietitians (n = 89) and pediatricians (n = 64). Familiarity with BTF was high (82%), and 56% had cared for children receiving it, most commonly via gastrostomy, although practices varied. Attitudes were generally positive (65%), particularly among dietitians (81%). Reported benefits included improved feeding tolerance (79%), reduced reflux/vomiting (72%), and improved stool patterns (67%); while caregiver burden (61%) and perceived parental insecurity regarding nutrition (28%) were the main challenges. Awareness of the 2022 national BTF position paper was low (54%), especially among physicians (20%), although 77% of healthcare professionals expressed interest in further education. This nationwide survey reveals high familiarity and positive attitudes toward BTF among pediatric healthcare professionals but considerable variation in practice and limited awareness of existing guidelines. Future interventional studies, evidence-based recommendations and professional training are needed to support consistent, family-centered implementation.
