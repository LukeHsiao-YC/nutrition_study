### 重點摘要
這項回溯性世代研究旨在比較經皮內視鏡胃造瘻術中推入式（push）與拉出式（pull）置放技術的臨床結果，特別關注意外導管脫落的風險。研究分析了305名成人患者的數據，其中205名採用推入式置放。結果顯示，推入式置放的患者在一年的追蹤期內，意外導管脫落的風險顯著高於拉出式置放。然而，兩種技術在造瘻口感染、計畫性導管移除以及全因死亡率方面並無顯著差異。因此，儘管推入式置放會增加導管脫落的風險，但在解剖或疾病因素導致拉出式置放無法執行或禁忌時，推入式仍是一個適當的替代方案。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究透過比較推入式與拉出式經皮內視鏡胃造瘻術的臨床結果，特別是意外導管脫落的風險，為這兩種技術的相對安全性與併發症提供了重要數據，填補了此領域的實證空白。然而，作為單一中心的回溯性研究，其結果的普適性可能受限，且推入組患者通常因解剖或疾病因素而無法使用拉出式，這可能意味著兩組患者在基線狀況上存在潛在差異，可能影響結果解釋。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Push percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy and the risk of accidental tube dislodgement relative to pull placement in adults receiving enteral nutrition: A retrospective cohort study.
**Abstract:** Enteral feeding is the first choice to treat or avoid malnutrition in patients with impaired oral intake. Percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy tubes can be placed using a push or pull technique, the former of which is the preference in cases when pull method is contraindicated. We compared outcomes of push versus pull percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy. We performed a retrospective single-center cohort study combining data from patients undergoing pull placement and push placement. The primary outcome was 1-year accidental tube dislodgement, and secondary outcomes were planned removal, peristomal infection requiring antibiotics, and all-cause mortality. A parametric multistate nonlinear time-to-event model was developed using stepwise covariate modeling to identify baseline predictors of outcomes. We included 305 patients in our analysis, of which 205 underwent the push placement. In multistate analyses, push placement had a higher risk of accidental tube dislodgement than pull placement, whereas peristomal infection, planned tube removal for adequate oral intake, and all-cause mortality did not differ between techniques CONCLUSION: Push placement was associated with a higher risk of accidental tube dislodgement, whereas other outcomes did not differ between techniques. Therefore, when anatomical or disease-related considerations preclude or contraindicate pull placement, push placement remains an appropriate alternative despite the increased risk of tube dislodgement.
