### 重點摘要
這項隨機對照試驗旨在評估線上抑制性控制訓練（ICT）對不健康食物選擇的影響，此前實驗室研究顯示其有潛力，但線上研究結果不一。研究招募了512名參與者進行線上ICT或控制組訓練，隨後要求他們從模擬披薩外送網站訂購虛擬食物。結果顯示，接受ICT訓練的參與者所訂購的平均卡路里（1270大卡）與控制組（1287大卡）之間沒有顯著差異。本研究結論指出，此種單次線上ICT介入未能有效影響參與者的虛擬食物選擇行為。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了線上抑制性控制訓練（ICT）在影響大規模人群實際食物選擇意向方面的有效性空白，特別是在其應用於線上且結果不一致的背景下。研究結果表明，單次、線上執行的ICT可能不足以改變不健康食物的虛擬訂購行為，這對未來ICT的介入設計和實施提出了限制，並強調了需進一步探討線上情境與現實行為間的轉化機制。

**Title:** A failure of food-specific inhibitory control training to affect food choices via a hypothetical food delivery platform.
**Abstract:** Inhibitory control training (ICT) is a computerised intervention which requires individuals to repeatedly inhibit a pre-potent motor response to unhealthy food-related cues. In doing so the cues are devalued, which may in turn reduce the choice and/or consumption behaviour towards unhealthy foods. Whilst single-session laboratory evidence suggests ICT may be a promising intervention, online studies show equivocal results. In this pre-registered randomised controlled trial, we administered ICT online (vs a control group) to 512 individuals (47.6% female, mean age = 41.68). Immediately following training participants were asked to make a hypothetical food order from a mock pizza delivery website based on a popular UK chain. Devaluation of pizza, salad, and unhealthy but untrained images was also measured pre- and post-training.
