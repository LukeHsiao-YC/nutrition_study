### 重點摘要
這項縱向觀察性研究旨在描述2009年至2024年英國10-15歲青少年心理健康和行為結果的最新趨勢，並探討社會人口學差異。研究分析了來自家庭調查的16,356名參與者共46,218份自我報告數據，評估了情緒問題、過動、行為問題、健康行為、親子關係及風險行為等。結果顯示，情緒問題在該期間增加超過一倍，尤其在女孩和英國白人青少年中增幅最大；過動症狀也顯著上升，但行為問題、酒精與菸草使用則呈下降趨勢。總體而言，早期青少年的情緒問題已達流行規模，而許多外化和風險行為卻有所下降，凸顯了急需投入資源預防青少年情緒問題驅動因素的必要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了關於英國10-15歲青少年心理健康和行為趨勢的最新、全面的數據空白，特別是揭示了情緒問題與外化和風險行為之間日益擴大的趨勢差異。它強調了特定群體（女孩和英國白人青少年）中不良結果的集中性，暗示了現有預防策略可能未能有效應對這些新興的內部化問題，也為未來研究探索特定的風險和復原力機制提供了方向。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Trends in mental health and behavioural outcomes in UK 10-15 year olds from 2009 to 2024.
**Abstract:** This is a longitudinal observational study aiming to describe recent trends in mental health and behavioural outcomes among UK adolescents 10-15 years-old and examine differences by socio-demographic characteristics. We used self-reported data from a household-based UK survey; 2009-2024to examine trends the prevalence of emotional problems, hyperactivity, and conduct problems (Strengths and Difficulties Questionnaire); health behaviours (diet, physical activity, social media use); parent-child relationships (conflict, communication); risk behaviours (alcohol, tobacco, vaping, staying out late); and antisocial behaviours (violence, vandalism, shoplifting). Overall, 16,356 participants provided 46,218 observations. Between 2009 and 2024, emotional problems more than doubled (from 11.4% to 27.1%), with acceleration after 2014 and again after 2019. Increases were greatest among girls (38.9% in 2024 vs. 15.8% in boys) and white British adolescents. Hyperactivity symptoms were stable up to 2017 (~ 16.1%) but rose to 27.4% by 2024, mainly in girls. Conduct problems declined steadily from 13.6% in 2009 to 11.4% in 2024, driven by reductions among boys. Peer problems rose (particularly among girls), and vaping increased sharply after 2020. In contrast, alcohol and tobacco use, staying out late, and conflict with parents all declined. CONCLUSION: These findings align with previous reports indicating that emotional problems in early adolescence have risen at an epidemic scale while many externalising and risk behaviours have declined. The divergence of trends, and concentration of poor outcomes among girls and white British adolescents, points to specific mechanisms of risk and resilience. These findings, alongside current service demand, support urgent investment in prevention of the drivers of the increases in adolescent emotional problems.
