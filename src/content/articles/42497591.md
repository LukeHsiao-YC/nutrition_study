### 重點摘要
胸主動脈瘤(TAA)患者面臨顯著的醫療與心理社會挑戰，但其支持性照護需求與優先順序知之甚少。本研究調查了106名TAA患者，評估他們對醫療、心理、行為和運動等領域支持服務的感知幫助程度、參與意願及障礙。結果顯示，患者對體育活動、睡眠管理和壓力/焦慮管理計畫表現出強烈興趣，但儘管感知有用性高，實際參與意願卻較低，主要障礙是距離和時間。研究結論指出，TAA患者對心理社會與行為介入有需求，但參與受到結構性和動機性障礙限制，建議將可近性高的支持計畫嵌入常規照護中。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了胸主動脈瘤(TAA)患者心理社會和行為支持性照護需求知識的空白，明確指出患者對壓力、睡眠和身體活動介入的高度興趣，並揭示了距離和時間等限制參與的結構性障礙。未來的研究應著重於開發並評估如何將簡短、易於參與的教育、心理社會及行為計畫有效地融入TAA的常規照護中，以克服這些障礙並提升患者的生活品質。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Identifying patient-centered support needs in aortic aneurysm care.
**Abstract:** Thoracic aortic aneurysm (TAA) presents significant medical and psychosocial challenges. Despite advances in medical care and outcomes, little is known about the psychosocial and behavioral supportive care needs and priorities of individuals living with TAA. Patients (N = 106) with a current or previous TAA completed a structured Needs Evaluation survey assessing perceived helpfulness, likelihood of attendance, and barriers across medical, psychological, behavioral, and exercise domains. Participants were categorized by treatment pathway (surgery vs. surveillance) and distress severity (high vs. low), using scores from the Depression Anxiety Stress Scales (DASS-21). Across groups, patients endorsed strong interest in physical activity education, sleep management, and stress/anxiety management programs (≥80%). Despite high perceived helpfulness, fewer patients indicated willingness to attend (e.g., 78% vs. 23% for depression programs). The most common barriers were distance (43%) and time (36%), while disinterest and health limitations were infrequent. Patients with elevated distress more frequently endorsed psychological and symptom-management services as helpful and were more likely to attend. Endorsement patterns were largely similar across surgical and surveillance groups. Patients with TAA express consistent interest in psychosocial and behavioral interventions, particularly those addressing stress, sleep, and activity. However, structural and motivational barriers limit participation. Embedding brief, accessible educational, psychosocial, and behavioral programs within routine TAA care may enhance patient engagement and quality of life.
