### 重點摘要
本研究旨在建立一個指導學齡前兒童（0-5歲）全人發展促進與衡量的框架，因應現有此類框架的缺乏。研究透過系統性文獻回顧，識別了6個相關框架及9項衡量指標，這些指標常涵蓋健康依戀、韌性、參與度、好奇心及情緒健康。研究將「好奇心」納入一個既有的社區共識框架中，並提出一個統一且可操作的框架，以期指導未來評估工具的開發。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了學齡前兒童全人發展領域缺乏統一操作框架的空白，透過整合現有文獻並新增「好奇心」領域，為其促進與衡量提供了基礎。然而，研究主要引用的文獻來自西方高收入國家，這限制了其發現的多元性及在不同文化背景下的普遍適用性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Flourishing Childhood Frameworks: A Scoping Review.
**Abstract:** Experiences in early life impact childhood, lifelong flourishing, and overall well-being. Yet a framework to guide promoting and measuring flourishing in early childhood has not emerged. The objective of this study was to identify frameworks and measures related to promoting and assessing early-childhood flourishing (0-5 years) to build on a preexisting, community-informed framework. A systematic search was conducted across electronic databases and the gray literature for articles published between 2014-2026. Of 13 902 total references, 8592 were eligible for screening. A machine learning-assisted tool (Research Screener) was used to streamline screening of the abstracts against the inclusion criteria. A deductive approach guided by a preexisting community-informed framework was used to interpret the data. From 32 studies, we identified 6 frameworks and 9 measures that related to early-childhood flourishing. Frameworks typically identified preconditions of flourishing and indicators of child flourishing. Child flourishing measures commonly included items assessing healthy attachment, resilience, engagement and curiosity, and emotional openness and well-being. The National Survey of Children's Health Child Flourishing Index was the most cited measure. A new "curiosity" domain was added to the preexisting framework. Findings will guide the development of a new assessment tool. Studies from Western, high-income countries were largely represented. Optimizing early-childhood flourishing requires a positive health promotion approach that both fosters preconditions for and monitors flourishing as an outcome. This paper presents a unified, operational framework for measuring flourishing across key domains.
