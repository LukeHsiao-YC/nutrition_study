### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討美國癌症倖存者總鈣攝取量與憂鬱症狀之間的關聯性，以彌補此特定族群流行病學證據的不足。研究分析了2007-2018年NHANES數據中2204名癌症倖存者的資料，將PHQ-9分數≥5定義為有憂鬱症狀，並依據24小時飲食回顧估計的總鈣攝取量分為四個四分位數。調整混淆變數後，結果顯示，與鈣攝取量最低的Q1組相比，攝取量較高的Q3和Q4組的憂鬱症狀發生風險分別降低了40%和44%。這表明較高的總鈣攝取量與癌症倖存者較低的憂鬱症狀機率相關。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
過去研究雖已探討鈣攝取與憂鬱症狀的關係，但針對癌症倖存者的流行病學證據仍然有限，本研究彌補了此特定族群的知識空白。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Association between total calcium intake and depressive symptoms in cancer survivors based on the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) 2007-2018: a cross-sectional study.
**Abstract:** Though previous studies have explored the relationship between Ca intake and depressive symptoms, epidemiological evidence on cancer survivors remains limited. This study aimed to investigate the association between estimated total Ca intake and depressive symptoms among cancer survivors in the USA. This study analysed data from 2204 cancer survivors in the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey 2007-2018. Participants with the Patient Health Questionnaire (PHQ-9) scores ≥5 were defined as having depressive symptoms. Total Ca intake was defined as the sum of dietary Ca and Ca supplements, estimated through the first 24-h dietary recall, and was grouped into four ranges [Q1 (≤606·5 mg/day), Q2 (606·5-962·5 mg/day), Q3 (962·5-1476·0 mg/day) and Q4 (>1476·0 mg/day)] according to quartiles. Logistic regression and restricted cubic spline (RCS) were used to assess the relationship.
