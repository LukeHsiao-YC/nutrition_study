### 重點摘要
神經性梅毒在抗生素時代已極為罕見，尤其是在兒童和HIV陰性者中，但梅毒病例近年來有所上升。本文報告了一名17歲青少年出現肌陣攣和癲癇發作的病例，透過HEADSS評估發現其性活躍史，進而進行性傳染病篩檢。最終，該患者經血清和腦脊髓液檢測確認診斷為神經性梅毒。研究結論指出，對於所有性活躍且有不明原因神經系統症狀的青少年，應將神經性梅毒納入鑑別診斷，並強調全面且保密的社會史詢問及定期性傳染病篩檢的重要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了對神經性梅毒在性活躍青少年中鑑別診斷考量的不足，特別是在梅毒發病率上升的背景下，提醒臨床醫生儘管此病罕見，仍需保持警惕。此外，它強調了在青少年病史詢問中，全面且保密的社會心理評估對於早期診斷的重要性，這可能是在臨床實踐中被忽視的一環。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** An Adolescent With Neurosyphilis.
**Abstract:** Neurosyphilis is a central nervous system (CNS) infection that has become exceedingly rare in the postantibiotic era, especially in children and HIV-negative persons. It can develop during any stage of an untreated syphilis infection and is known as "the great imitator" for its highly variable clinical presentation with inconsistent lab and imaging findings. We describe the case of a previously healthy 17-year-old boy who presented with 2 weeks of right thumb and middle finger myoclonus followed by right upper extremity focal seizures with secondary generalization. Routine and confidential psychosocial history using the HEADSS (Home; Education, Eating, and Exercise; Activities; Drugs; Sexuality; Suicide and Sleep) assessment revealed that our patient was sexually active with one female partner, prompting sexually transmitted infection (STI) screening. The final diagnosis of neurosyphilis was made following a reactive rapid plasma reagin screen and confirmatory treponemal testing of the cerebrospinal fluid. In light of the recent rise in syphilis case incidence and the devastating consequences of CNS involvement, neurosyphilis should be considered in the differential diagnoses for all sexually active adolescents with unexplained neurologic symptoms. Additionally, this case highlights the importance of comprehensive and confidential social history taking in all adolescents, as well as regular STI screening for those at risk.
