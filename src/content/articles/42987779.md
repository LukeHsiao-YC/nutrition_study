### 重點摘要
本研究旨在開發並評估首個專為有家族癌症風險個體設計的「家族癌症賦權量表」(FCES)。研究團隊透過專家小組轉譯與改編現有量表，並進行認知訪談及對 194 名參與者的線上調查，以評估 FCES 的可理解性、相關性及心理計量特性。結果顯示，包含十個項目的 FCES 具有良好的可接受性與滿意的整體內部一致性 (ω = 0.82)，初步支持其作為評估工具的潛力。FCES 可用於臨床諮詢與研究，以系統性地評估患者賦權並指導以患者為中心的照護。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
過往缺乏專門針對有家族癌症風險個體所設計的賦權評估工具，本研究開發的 FCES 填補了此一空白。然而，本研究的限制在於其因素結構尚需獨立驗證，且 FCES 的心理計量特性仍需進一步的評估。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Patient empowerment in the context of familial cancer burden: Development and psychometric evaluation of the Familial Cancer Empowerment Scale (FCES).
**Abstract:** Individuals with familial cancer risk often experience emotional strain and face complex decisions. Empowerment enables patients to engage actively and make informed choices. Although the Genetic Counselling Outcome Scale (GCOS-24) measures empowerment in genetic counseling, no tailored instrument exists for individuals with familial cancer risk. The aim of this study was to develop and evaluate a concise scale for this purpose based on the GCOS. The Familial Cancer Empowerment Scale (FCES) was developed using the TRAPD method. The GCOS-24 was translated, adapted and shortened by an expert panel including patient representatives. Comprehensibility and relevance were assessed in cognitive interviews and were analysed using structured content analysis. The final FCES was evaluated in a cross-sectional online survey. Acceptability was assessed alongside a psychometric evaluation comprising item characteristics, exploratory factor analysis, and internal consistency. The FCES comprised ten items. Seven cognitive interviews indicated high comprehensibility and relevance. Evaluation in the cross-sectional survey (n = 194) showed good acceptability and generally satisfactory item performance. Overall internal consistency was satisfactory (ω = 0.82). Exploratory factor analysis supported a three-factor solution, although the factorial allocation of Items 7 and 10 remained ambiguous. Internal consistency was satisfactory for factors 2 and 3 (ω = 0.80 and ω = 0.74, respectively), while the two items defining factor 1 showed a moderate correlation (ρ = 0.53). The FCES is the first brief German-language instrument specifically developed to assess empowerment in individuals with familial cancer risk. Initial psychometric findings support its potential utility, while independent validation of the factorial structure and further psychometric evaluation are warranted. The FCES can support systematic assessment of patient empowerment in clinical counselling and research, helping identify areas where individuals may need additional information or support. It may also support evaluation of genetic counselling outcomes and inform patient-centred care.
