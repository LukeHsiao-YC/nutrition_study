### 重點摘要
癌症患者因惡性腫瘤而面臨嚴重感染的高風險，疫苗接種是重要的預防策略，但其接種率仍有改善空間。本研究在德國一所大學醫院進行了為期1.5年的前瞻性與回顧性觀察研究，透過患者教育、員工培訓、現場接種及推廣活動，旨在評估並提升癌症患者的疫苗接種率。結果顯示，整體疫苗接種率從43%上升至49%，其中流感、肺炎球菌和帶狀皰疹疫苗的接種率顯著提升。儘管依公共衛生建議完全接種的患者比例從6%增至16%但仍偏低，研究證實重點式的疫苗接種活動能有效提高癌症患者的疫苗接種率。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻成功證明了現場疫苗接種活動在提升癌症患者疫苗接種率方面的有效性。然而，研究的限制在於未能深入探討癌症患者未能依建議時程完全接種疫苗的具體障礙和拒絕原因。未來的研究應著重於識別這些障礙，以制定更精準和個人化的介入策略。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Effectiveness of an on-site vaccination campaign for patients with cancer.
**Abstract:** Patients with haematological or oncological malignancies are at increased risk of severe infections. Vaccination is a key preventive strategy and is recommended by National Immunization Technical Advisory Groups (NITAGs). Yet, vaccination rates in this population remain improvable. This project aimed to evaluate and enhance vaccine uptake in cancer patients. We conducted a pre- and post-observational study at the University Hospital Cologne in Germany to assess vaccination status over a 1.5-year intervention. Relevant infections included COVID-19, influenza, herpes zoster, pneumococci and RSV. The campaign included patient education, staff training, on-site vaccination, and targeted promotional efforts. A total of 749 patients were included (n = 398 in 2023, n = 351 in 2025). Overall vaccination rates increased from 43 to 49%, while COVID-19 vaccine coverage remained above 90%. Notable increases were observed for influenza (37% to 48%), pneumococcal (16% to 36%), and herpes zoster (22% to 33%) vaccines. The proportion of patients fully vaccinated according to public health recommendations rose from 6 to 16%, whereas those completely unvaccinated remained low (4% to 6%). Focused vaccination campaigns can effectively increase vaccine uptake in patients with cancer; however, the proportion of individuals vaccinated per recommended schedule remains low. Future research should investigate barriers and reasons for vaccine refusal to guide more targeted interventions.
