### 重點摘要
這項全國性世代研究旨在探討丹麥患有危及生命疾病 (LLC) 的兒童和青少年之存活率及其預後不良的潛在因素，以填補此領域的研究空白。研究納入 102,793 名 0-21 歲的患者，採用 Kaplan-Meier 存活曲線和回歸模型分析。結果顯示，整體 1 年和 10 年存活率分別為 95.6% 和 94.5%，但嬰幼兒期、多重診斷、父母教育程度較低及 2010 年前診斷的患者存活時間較短。結論指出，大多數患有 LLC 的兒童和青少年能存活多年，強調需明確其在疾病全程中對支持性及安寧照護的需求。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了過去關於患有危及生命疾病的兒童和青少年存活率及其影響因素未被全國性系統性調查的空白，首次提供了大規模的存活數據。未來的研究限制或方向可以進一步探討支持性與安寧照護的具體需求和實施效果，以優化對這些患者的照護。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Survival in children and adolescents with life-limiting conditions-a nationwide cohort study.
**Abstract:** There is an increasing prevalence of children and adolescents with life-limiting conditions (LLC) with potential need for supportive and palliative care, but their survival has never been investigated. We aimed to investigate the survival of children and adolescents with LLC and potential factors for poor prognosis through a registry-based nationwide cohort study. We included all patients aged 0-21 years, born in Denmark and diagnosed with an LLC between January 1, 1995 and December 31, 2021. Kaplan-Meier survival curves were plotted across disease groups. Regression models with 1-year and 10-year restricted mean survival time as outcomes were used to investigate factors for poor prognosis including child sex, age, parental education and diagnostic period. We identified 102,793 children and adolescents with a total of 116,599 LLC (10.8% had >1 diagnosis). Median follow-up time was 9.85 years (interquartile range 5.15, 16.05) with 5728 deaths. Most deaths occurred within one year of diagnosis. Overall 1-year and 10-year survival rates were 95.6% and 94.5% respectively. In children <1 year, the poorest overall survival was seen in children with metabolic diseases before 2010 and neurologic diseases after 2010. In children ≥1 year, the poorest overall survival was seen in those with malignancy diseases regardless of diagnostic period. Children <1 year, with multiple concurrent diagnoses, parents with shorter educations, and diagnosed before 2010 had the shortest survival times. Most children and adolescents with LLC live for many years with illness. Thus, it is important to clarify their need for supportive and palliative care, along the illness trajectory.
