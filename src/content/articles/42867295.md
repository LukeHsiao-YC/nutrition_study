### 重點摘要
懷孕期間的肥胖與鐵缺乏皆極為普遍且嚴重影響產科結果，但肥胖對懷孕、產後及新生兒鐵狀態的影響尚不明確。本系統性回顧分析了76項研究，探討孕婦肥胖與其鐵狀態指標之間的關聯，特別著重於懷孕期、產後和新生兒時期。結果顯示，肥胖影響了懷孕期間及新生兒的鐵狀態，例如第一孕期sTfR較高，第二孕期血紅素和sTfR有增加趨勢但血清鐵降低，以及新生兒臍帶血鐵蛋白較低。研究結論指出，肥胖對鐵狀態的影響在第一、二孕期及新生兒時期最為顯著。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇系統性回顧填補了以往對孕婦肥胖如何影響其自身及其新生兒鐵狀態不明確的知識空白，透過綜合現有證據揭示了不同孕期及新生兒的具體影響。然而，未來研究仍需加強對產後婦女鐵狀態與肥胖關聯的探討，因為本回顧中僅有一項研究涵蓋此階段。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Influence of Maternal Adiposity on Iron Status in Pregnancy, Early Postpartum, and the Neonatal Period: A Systematic Review.
**Abstract:** Obesity and iron deficiency are highly prevalent in pregnancy, and both can severely affect obstetric outcomes. However, the influence of obesity on iron status during the critical stages of pregnancy, postpartum, and the neonatal period remains unclear. This systematic review seeks to examine the influence of adiposity on markers of iron status during pregnancy, postpartum, and the neonatal period. MEDLINE, EMBASE, Web of Science, CINAHL, and Scopus databases were searched with no date restrictions. Observational or intervention human studies were included, assessing the association between adiposity and iron status in pregnancy, postpartum fewer than 6 weeks after birth, and newborns less than 28 days of age. Screening of titles/abstracts/full texts, data extraction, and quality assessment were performed by 2 independent reviewers. Seventy-six studies met the selection criteria of which 61 were classified as having a low risk of bias. Fourteen assessed iron status in the first trimester, 24 in the second trimester, 24 in the third trimester, 16 in fetuses/newborns, and one in the postpartum period. Adiposity was primarily assessed by calculating body mass index (n = 58, 95%). The following was found in women with obesity compared with those without obesity: in the first trimester, most studies reported similar or lower hemoglobin concentrations, similar ferritin concentrations, and higher soluble transferrin receptor (sTfR); in the second trimester, there was a trend toward increased hemoglobin and sTfR, as well as decreased serum/plasma iron, with no differences in ferritin, transferrin saturation, or hepcidin; in the third trimester, most studies showed similar concentrations of iron markers but lower ferritin concentrations in umbilical cord blood/newborns. Obesity impacts iron status during pregnancy and in newborns, with the most pronounced effects occurring in the first and second trimesters and in the neonatal period. PROSPERO registration no. CRD 42024552184.
