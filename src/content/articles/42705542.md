### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在解決小野黍（Panicum miliaceum）中抗營養因子限制營養生物利用度的問題，並評估不同加工技術（碾磨、浸泡、預煮、爆裂）對其理化、功能和結構特性的影響。研究發現，加工顯著改善了小野黍的功能特性，例如預煮提高了吸水能力和酚類含量，而浸泡則顯著增強了抗氧化活性。此外，微觀結構分析顯示加工引起澱粉糊化和蛋白質變性，in silico分析亦揭示了生物活性化合物與消化酶的強結合親和力。總體而言，本研究證明適當的加工方法能有效提升小野黍的營養和功能品質，支持其在功能性食品開發中的應用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了關於不同加工方法如何整合性地（從理化、結構到in silico）改善小野黍抗營養因子限制，並提升其功能與營養潛力的研究空白。然而，未來研究可能需要進一步評估這些加工後小野黍的實際生物利用度、在人體或動物中的健康益處，以及其在實際功能性食品產品開發中的應用性和感官特性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Integrated physicochemical, structural, and in silico analyses revealed the enhanced functional potential of processed proso millet (Panicum miliaceum).
**Abstract:** Proso millet (Panicum miliaceum) contains anti-nutritional factors such as phytates, tannins, and enzyme inhibitors that limit nutrient bioavailability; however, appropriate processing techniques effectively reduce these constraints. This study evaluates the effects of milling, soaking, parboiling, and popping on the physicochemical, functional, and structural properties of proso millet, along with in silico analysis of its bioactive compounds. Processing significantly influenced functional properties, with water absorption capacity observed highest in parboiling (41.31±0.03%), followed by soaking (23.79±0.01%), popping (22.02±0.08%), and milling (18.05±0.01%). In contrast, oil absorption capacity was highest in milling (18.21±0.08%), followed by popping (17.9±0.05%), parboiling (14.96±0.07%), and soaking (14.01±0.07%). Phenolic content ranged from 0.51±0.004 to 0.56±0.0047, with the highest value in parboiling, while tannin content varied slightly (0.07-0.09 mg/g). Antioxidant activity (DPPH) was highest in soaking (9.97±0%), followed by popping (9.0±0.006%), milling (7.54±0%), and parboiling (6.81±0.006%). FTIR and SEM analysis revealed structural modifications, including starch gelatinization and protein denaturation, particularly in parboiled and popped samples. In silico studies showed strong binding affinity of palmitic acid with human pancreatic α-amylase (-7.2 kcal/mol) and yamogenin with pancreatic lipase (-6.8 kcal/mol), supported by stable RMSF and NMA profiles. Statistical analysis confirmed significant differences among treatments (p<0.05). Overall, processing enhanced the functional and nutritional quality of proso millet, supporting its potential in functional food development.
