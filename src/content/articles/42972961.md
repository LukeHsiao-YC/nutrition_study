### 重點摘要
針對年幼兒童肥胖的密集生活型態治療與同步線上模式證據有限，本研究旨在評估一項為4至8歲兒童設計的線上診所-社區模式在可行性、可接受性與初步效果。這項隨機對照試驗納入40名兒童，將其分為線上家庭介入組或常規照護組，為期6個月。研究發現該線上模式具備可行性和可接受性，並顯示在身體生活品質、動作技能和BMI指標上有所改善，儘管BMI相關的組間差異未達統計顯著。研究建議需進行更大規模的試驗以確認其長期有效性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了年幼兒童（4至8歲）肥胖症透過同步線上模式進行密集生活型態治療的證據空白。其限制在於樣本量較小，導致BMI相關的初步改善未達統計顯著性，未來的研究需要進行更大規模、具足夠統計功效的試驗，以確認其長期有效性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Pilot Implementation of an Adapted Online Clinic-Community Model for Childhood Obesity.
**Abstract:** Intensive health-behaviour lifestyle treatment is recommended for paediatric obesity, but evidence for young children and synchronous online delivery remains limited. To evaluate the feasibility, acceptability, and preliminary effectiveness of an adapted online clinic-community model for children aged 4 to < 8 years with overweight or obesity. In this two-arm randomized controlled trial, children were randomized to an online family-based intervention or usual care. The primary outcome was treatment dose at 6 months, defined as intervention contact hours. Secondary outcomes included BMI z-score, percentage of the 95th BMI percentile, health-related quality of life, eating behaviours, and perceived movement skills at 3 and 6 months. Forty children were enrolled; mean age was 6.6 ± 1.0 years and 58% were male. At 6 months, 70% of intervention participants achieved at least 26 treatment hours. Although the between-group differences were not statistically significant, the intervention group demonstrated numerically greater reductions in BMI z-score (-0.2 ± 0.3 vs. -0.1 ± 0.1; p = 0.091) and percentage of the 95th BMI percentile (-3.5 ± 6.4 vs. -0.2 ± 4.0; p = 0.075). Physical quality of life improved at 3 and 6 months, object control skills at 3 months, and perceived locomotor skills at 6 months. The adapted online model was feasible and acceptable, with preliminary improvements in physical quality of life, movement skills, and BMI metrics. Larger adequately powered trials are needed to confirm effectiveness and longer-term outcomes.
