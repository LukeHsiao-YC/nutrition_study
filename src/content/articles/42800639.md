### 重點摘要
全球正面臨糧食不安全與慢性病盛行等營養危機，主要由於飲食過度依賴少數作物且健康飲食難以負擔。本文提出被忽視及未充分利用的物種（NUS）作為永續且具韌性的替代方案，因其富含營養且能適應惡劣氣候。未來研究應著重於將NUS融入日常飲食、探索其對抗全球糧食挑戰的潛力，並評估其藥用特性。最終目標是透過公共衛生框架推廣NUS，以改善社區、區域及全球人口的福祉。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文章指出了目前飲食結構過於單一，未能有效應對全球糧食不安全及慢性病問題。它填補了在主流作物之外，提出被忽視及未充分利用的物種（NUS）作為解決未來營養挑戰潛在方案的空白。未來的研究限制在於需要更深入地探索如何將這些作物實際整合到人群日常飲食中，並科學地評估其營養價值與藥用特性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Neglected and Underutilized Species (NUS) of Crops: A Solution for Future Nutritional Challenges?
**Abstract:** This article addresses the current state of global food insecurity and the rising burden of chronic diseases-both of which are nutrition-related crises-highlighting the urgent need to diversify the global dietary matrix. Currently, a mere four crops supply 60% of human energy intake, while macroeconomic barriers prevent over a third of the global population from affording a healthy diet. In this context, neglected and underutilized species (NUS) emerge as a highly sustainable and resilient alternative, given that certain species possess exceptional nutritional profiles and high adaptability to adverse climatic conditions. Consequently, future lines of research are proposed to integrate these crops into the population daily lives, aiming to explore their capacity to combat global food challenges and evaluate their medicinal properties. Ultimately, this approach seeks to promote the collective utilization of NUS within public health frameworks to improve population well-being at community, regional, and global levels.
