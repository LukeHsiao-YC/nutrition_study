### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討內臟脂肪組織（VAT）與多種心臟代謝特徵以及蛋白質組學炎症標誌之間的橫斷面關係。研究人員在兩個大型世代隊列中測量了身體脂肪分佈、心臟代謝指標和血漿蛋白質組學，並通過線性迴歸分析識別了與VAT及其他心臟代謝特徵相關的炎症蛋白質標誌物。結果顯示，VAT具有最強的炎症蛋白質組學特徵，且在校正VAT後，大多數心臟代謝特徵與蛋白質的關聯顯著減弱，表明VAT是這些關聯的關鍵驅動因素。本研究識別出TGFB1等具高中心性的蛋白質，這些蛋白質可能反映了連結VAT與心臟代謝特徵的共享炎症途徑，為未來的機制和治療研究提供了假說。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻透過蛋白質組學分析，系統性地揭示了內臟脂肪組織（VAT）在多種心臟代謝特徵的炎症途徑中扮演核心角色，並識別出一些潛在的關鍵炎症蛋白，填補了對VAT如何驅動全身性炎症及其與心臟代謝疾病之間分子連結的理解空白。未來的研究限制可能包括其橫斷面研究設計無法確立因果關係，以及需要進一步的機制和治療干預研究來驗證這些「中心蛋白」的功能和臨床應用。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Inflammatory Proteomic Signatures of Cardiometabolic Traits and the Contribution of Visceral Adipose Tissue: A Cross-Sectional and Network Proteomic Analysis.
**Abstract:** Visceral adipose tissue (VAT), fat located within the abdominal cavity, is strongly associated with systemic inflammation and poor cardiometabolic health. This study investigates the cross-sectional relationships between multiple cardiometabolic traits, including VAT, and proteomic-based inflammatory signatures. Body adiposity distribution quantified using dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA), cardiometabolic traits, and plasma proteomics inflammation panel (Olink Explore 384) were measured in a discovery cohort from the Vitamin D and Omega-3 Trial (VITAL; n = 525) and a replication cohort from the Cocoa Supplement and Multivitamin Outcomes Study (COSMOS; n = 371). We derived inflammatory proteomic markers of VAT, systolic blood pressure (SBP), high-density lipoprotein cholesterol (HDL-C), triglycerides (TG), fasting glucose, and insulin resistance (Homeostasis Model Assessment of Insulin Resistance; HOMA-IR). Inflammatory proteomic markers were identified via linear regression at a false discovery rate (FDR) < 0.05. VAT showed the strongest inflammatory proteomic profile, with 221 associated proteins (62% of those tested) and effect estimates of up to β = 0.59 (SE = 0.03). Proteins associated with the cardiometabolic traits showed directions of association largely consistent with those observed for VAT, except for HDL-C, which showed inverse associations. After adjusting the regression models for VAT, most protein associations with glucose and SBP (>97%), HOMA-IR (70%), TG (62%), and HDL-C (56%) were no longer statistically significant suggesting that VAT accounts for a substantial portion of the shared variance in these associations. To further characterize these shared molecular associations, we examined the Bayesian network architecture of 86 proteomic markers common to all cardiometabolic traits. Some of the VAT-attenuated protein signatures with high centrality were TGFB1, PDLIM7, COLEC12, and LAIR1. These hub-like proteins may reflect shared inflammatory pathways linking VAT with cardiometabolic traits and provide hypotheses for future mechanistic and therapeutic investigation.
