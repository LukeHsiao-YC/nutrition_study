### 重點摘要
無家可歸的青少年 (YEH) 面臨比一般同齡人更高的懷孕風險，本研究旨在深入探討其懷孕矛盾心理、懷孕史、保險套與避孕措施使用情況。研究分析了 446 名 16-25 歲性活躍 YEH 的基線數據，發現 43% 存在懷孕矛盾心理，而 39% 有懷孕史、41% 最近使用保險套、15% 使用避孕措施。然而，研究結果顯示懷孕矛盾心理與懷孕史、近期保險套使用和避孕措施使用之間沒有顯著關聯。本研究強調，了解 YEH 的懷孕矛盾心理、避孕行為及懷孕史，有助於醫療提供者制定符合其性決策需求的客製化政策與方案。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了對無家可歸青少年（YEH）懷孕意願中「懷孕矛盾心理」這一細緻面向的理解空白。然而，研究結果指出懷孕矛盾心理與避孕行為或懷孕史並無顯著關聯，這提示未來的研究或介入措施應超越單純的懷孕意願分類，更全面地探討影響 YEH 性決策和避孕使用的深層多重因素，以更有效地預防非預期懷孕。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Pregnancy Ambivalence, History of Pregnancy, Condom Use, and Contraceptive Use Among U.S. Youth Experiencing Homelessness.
**Abstract:** Youth experiencing homelessness (YEH) are 5-8 times more likely than housed peers to become pregnant. A more nuanced examination of YEH pregnancy desire may better inform pregnancy and pregnancy prevention guidance. This study examined pregnancy ambivalence, pregnancy history, condom use, and contraceptive use among sexually active YEH. We utilized baseline data of YEH aged 16-25 from a randomized controlled trial published elsewhere (Santa Maria et al., 2021). Pregnancy desire was categorized as ambivalence (43%) and non-ambivalence: desiring pregnancy (10%) and not desiring pregnancy (47%). We calculated adjusted prevalence ratios (aPRs) and 95% confidence intervals (95% CI) between pregnancy ambivalence with three outcomes (pregnancy history, condom, and any contraceptive use), stratified by male/female participants, controlling for demographics (e.g., gender, sexual orientation). Among YEH (n = 446, 44% female, 50% male, 6% another gender; mean age 21.1 years), 39% reported pregnancy history, 41% reported recent condom use, and 15% reported contraceptive use. Adjusted prevalence ratios stratified by male/female participants indicated that pregnancy ambivalence was notsignificantly associated with pregnancy history, recent condom use, and reported contraceptive use. By characterizing YEH pregnancy ambivalence, condom and contraceptive use, and the pregnancy history, providers may develop tailored policies and programs that respond to the preferences and sexual decision-making needs of YEH who are more vulnerable to undesired pregnancy, regardless of pregnancy desire.
