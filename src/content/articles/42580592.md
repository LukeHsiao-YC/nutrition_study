### 重點摘要
本縱向研究旨在評估2017-2019年間尼泊爾青春期少女的身體質量指數（BMI）和身高成長變化及相關因素。研究分析了三波追蹤數據，對象為未婚且未懷孕的10-19歲少女，並使用BMI-for-age difference、height-for-age difference及條件性生長指標。初步結果顯示，這些少女的BMI持續偏低，偏離WHO中位數。文章標題指出，這些持續存在的BMI和身高缺陷反映了結構性和地理上的不平等。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了對尼泊爾青春期少女長期生長軌跡及其深層社會經濟和地理決定因素理解的空白，特別是將持續的BMI和身高缺陷與結構性及地理上的不平等聯繫起來。然而，由於摘要內容不完整，具體的研究限制或未來的研究方向未能充分闡述。

**Title:** Persistent BMI and height deficits reflect structural and geographic inequities among adolescent girls in Nepal.
**Abstract:** Adolescence is a period of rapid growth; however, many adolescent girls experience suboptimal growth trajectories. This longitudinal study assessed changes in body mass index (BMI) and linear growth and associated factors among Nepali adolescent girls from 2017-2019. We analysed panel data from three waves (2017 [n=770], 2018 [n=682] and 2019 [n=618]) of never-married and non-pregnant girls aged 10-19 years in 2017. BMI-for-age difference (BMI-AD) and height-for-age difference (HAD), indicating deviation from WHO age- and sex-specific medians, were used to assess change over time. BMI-AD and conditional growth (calculated as residuals from regressions of 2018/2019 HAD on prior HAD and age) were used in the regression analyses to identify associated factors. Conditional growth reflects linear growth above or below what would be expected, based on prior height and age.
