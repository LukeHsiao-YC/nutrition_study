### 重點摘要
早期產前照護對於降低母嬰發病率和死亡率至關重要，但在資源匱乏地區，延遲啟動仍很普遍。本研究旨在確定剛果民主共和國卡米納大學診所女性延遲啟動產前照護的盛行率及其相關因素，共納入203名孕婦，延遲定義為懷孕16週後才首次產檢。研究結果顯示，延遲產前照護的盛行率高達63.5%，相關因素包括極端孕婦年齡、伴侶教育程度低、最年幼子女年齡小以及意外懷孕。研究指出，延遲產前照護的普遍性可能對母嬰健康產生不利影響，建議介入措施應優先關注青少年和高齡產婦，並加強伴侶參與及避孕策略。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究揭示了剛果民主共和國卡米納地區延遲啟動產前照護的盛行率及其相關因素，填補了該特定低資源環境下的數據空白。然而，研究設計為橫斷面且僅限於單一醫療機構，因此無法確立因果關係，且研究結果的普遍性受限，未來需要進行更廣泛、縱向的研究來驗證這些發現並探討其因果機制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Prevalence and Factors Associated With Late Initiation of Antenatal Care Among Women Attending the University Clinics of Kamina: Cross-Sectional Study.
**Abstract:** Early initiation of antenatal care (ANC) is a key strategy for reducing maternal and perinatal morbidity and mortality. It enables timely detection and management of pregnancy-related complications, identification of high-risk pregnancies, and appropriate referral. Despite its proven benefits, late initiation of ANC remains common in low-resource settings. This study aimed to determine the prevalence of late initiation of ANC and identify its associated factors among women attending their first ANC visit at the University Clinics of Kamina, Democratic Republic of the Congo. We conducted an analytical cross-sectional study from December 1, 2024, to August 30, 2025, at the University Clinics of Kamina. Consecutive sampling of all eligible women attending their first ANC visit during the study period was performed. The study included 203 women who were pregnant. Data were collected through face-to-face interviews using a structured questionnaire administered electronically through KoBoCollect and were complemented by information extracted from ANC cards. Late initiation of ANC was defined as the first antenatal consultation occurring after 16 weeks of amenorrhea, in accordance with national standards. Data were analyzed using IBM SPSS Statistics (version 23). Logistic regression was used to identify factors associated with late initiation. Variables with  The prevalence of late ANC initiation (after 16 weeks of amenorrhea) was 63.5% (95% CI 56.9%-70.1%). After multivariable adjustment, the following factors were independently associated with late ANC initiation: extreme maternal age (<20 years or >35 years; adjusted odds ratio [aOR] 3.74, 95% CI 1.07-13.06), low partner educational level (aOR 3.16, 95% CI 1.13-8.80), age of the youngest child (≤2 years; aOR 4.47, 95% CI 1.94-10.29), and unintended pregnancy (aOR 2.46, 95% CI 1.05-5.76). The model demonstrated good discriminatory ability, with an area under the receiver operating characteristic curve of 79.4% (95% CI 0.716-0.873). Late initiation of ANC was highly prevalent among women attending the University Clinics of Kamina and may adversely affect maternal and perinatal outcomes. The observed associations suggest that interventions should prioritize adolescents and older women, strengthen the involvement of male partners, and improve access to family planning and early pregnancy detection strategies. These findings should be interpreted cautiously given the cross-sectional, single-facility design.
