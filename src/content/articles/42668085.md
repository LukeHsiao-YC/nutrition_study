### 重點摘要
餵養實踐和行為在囊性纖維化兒童 (cwCF) 中被認為是異常的，但缺乏系統性證據。本回顧對六個電子資料庫進行了廣泛檢索，篩選出 38 篇研究，旨在全面梳理cwCF嬰幼兒的餵養實踐、行為和障礙文獻。結果顯示，cwCF的純母乳餵養率顯著較低（3個月時14-25%，6個月時10-16%），且問題進食行為發生率是健康兒童的兩倍（40-43% vs 21-22%），並與父母壓力及專制教養風格相關。研究指出，未來的研究應探討母乳餵養、親子關係以及CFTR調節劑療法對cwCF餵養模式的影響。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本回顧透過系統性方法綜合了囊性纖維化兒童餵養相關的文獻，填補了該領域缺乏全面證據整理的空白。然而，研究發現目前尚無文獻探討囊性纖維化跨膜傳導調節劑（CFTRm）療法對cwCF餵養實踐、行為或障礙的影響，這是一個重要的未來研究限制及方向。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Feeding practices, behaviours and disorders in infants and children with cystic fibrosis: A scoping review.
**Abstract:** Feeding practices and behaviours in children with cystic fibrosis (cwCF) are thought to be atypical, with evidence pertaining to lower breastfeeding rates and higher problematic mealtime behaviours compared to the general population. To date, there has been no systematic approach to synthesising this evidence. This review aims to map the available literature on feeding practices, feeding behaviours and feeding disorders in infants and cwCF. Six electronic databases (MEDLINE, Embase, Emcare, PsycINFO, Cochrane and CINAHL) were searched on September 13 The search yielded 6116 articles; 279 full-text studies were screened and 38 were included in the final synthesis. Twenty-two studies described breastfeeding practices, 15 studies described feeding behaviours and 1 study explored feeding disorders in cwCF. Eighty-two percent (31 of 38) were observational studies, 71% (27 of 38) were conducted in the United States. Exclusive breastfeeding rates at 3 months were 14-25%, reducing to 10-16% by 6 months. Problematic mealtime behaviours were twice as common in cwCF, compared to healthy counterparts; 40-43% compared to 21-22%, respectively. Problematic mealtime behaviours were associated with parental stress and authoritarian parenting styles. There were no studies exploring the impact of CF transmembrane conductance regulator modulator (CFTRm) therapy on feeding practices, behaviours or disorders in cwCF. Future research should include breastfeeding exclusivity, duration, support and associated child and maternal health outcomes; strategies for improving the bidirectional relationship between parent and child during feeding and mealtimes; and the impact of early life CFTRm therapy on feeding practices and behaviours in cwCF.
