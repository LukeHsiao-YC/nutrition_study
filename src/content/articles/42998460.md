### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討多發性硬化症 (MS) 患者在線上支持團體中，如何討論藥物治療及其副作用，以促進以患者為中心的照護並解決資訊誤導問題。研究利用 Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) 分析了約 112,000 字的線上討論，並輔以主題分析和卡方檢定來識別主要主題及主題分佈差異。結果揭示了三個相關主題，其中「疾病經驗、治療評估和比較」最為普遍，且主題分佈與治療類別顯著相關。研究強調線上討論超越生物醫學層面，整合了患者的經驗、情感和症狀管理，因此建議臨床溝通應納入患者視角，並強化數位健康素養及藥物警戒。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了對慢性病患者如何在線上社群中討論藥物治療與副作用的理解空白，特別是揭示了這些討論如何超越單純的生物醫學資訊，深入到患者的親身經驗、情感表達和對治療的綜合評估。研究指出未來需更深入探討如何有效將這些線上患者視角整合到臨床實踐中，以及如何提升線上健康資訊的品質與患者的數位健康素養，這也是研究未來的潛在限制或發展方向。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Understanding discourses on multiple sclerosis medications and side effects in an online support group: a Latent Dirichlet Allocation analysis.
**Abstract:** Understanding how individuals discuss medication side effects in online support communities is critical for informing patient-centred care and addressing misinformation in chronic illness contexts. This study examines discourse surrounding multiple sclerosis (MS) treatments and side effects in an online support group for people living with MS. A corpus of approximately 112,000 words comprising discussions of medications and their associated side effects was analysed using Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA), supplemented by thematic analysis, to identify dominant themes. A subsequent chi-square analysis examined differences in the distribution of themes across four treatment categories. Three interrelated themes were identified in the corpus: (1) treatment use, administration, and risk considerations, (2) illness experiences, treatment evaluations, and comparisons, and (3) symptom burden and clinical monitoring. Among these, illness experiences, treatment evaluations, and comparisons emerged as the most prevalent theme, highlighting the centrality of lived experience and emotional expression in online discourse. Statistical analysis revealed a significant association between theme distribution and treatment category, χ²(6) = 142.45, p < .001, with a moderate effect size (Cramér's V = 0.29). The findings demonstrate that discussions of MS treatments and side effects in online support communities extend beyond biomedical concerns and are closely tied to experiential, emotional, and symptom-related dimensions of illness management. Online communities therefore function as important spaces for information exchange, experiential sharing, and treatment evaluation. The study shows the need to integrate patient perspectives and experiential concerns into clinical communication, particularly regarding treatment expectations and side effects. The findings also underline the importance of strengthening digital health literacy and pharmacovigilance efforts to improve the quality of treatment-related information available in online support communities.
