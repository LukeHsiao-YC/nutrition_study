### 重點摘要
本研究基於挑戰與威脅的生物心理社會模型，探討職業教育教師對工作要求的評估方式及其對心理健康的影響。透過對德國1470名職業教育教師的線上問卷調查，發現「教學和與學生互動」最受挑戰，且挑戰評估能提升工作滿意度並降低倦怠，而威脅評估則降低身心健康。研究亦顯示，韌性對工作要求評估有顯著影響，高韌性與更積極的挑戰評估相關。這些結果表明工作要求評估與教師心理健康密切相關，建議透過建立資源和提升韌性來改善教師的身心健康。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究彌補了過往研究中，工作要求與壓力之間非直接關聯的空白，透過引入生物心理社會模型中的挑戰與威脅評估機制，解釋了工作要求如何影響職業教育教師的心理健康。它深入探討了評估在工作要求與身心健康結果之間的調節作用，並揭示了韌性在此評估過程中的關鍵影響，為提升教師心理健康提供了具體的介入方向。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Challenge and threat appraisal of job demands and their impact on mental health among vocational education teachers.
**Abstract:** Teachers in vocational schools are confronted with many daily job demands. However, these demands are not directly associated with stress. According to the biopsychosocial model of challenge and threat, such demands can be judged as either challenging or threatening. Based on this model, the present study addressed the following research questions: (1) To what extent are different job demands appraised as challenges and threats by teachers in vocational education? (2) How does the appraisal of job demands moderate the relationship between perceived job demands and well-being, job satisfaction, risk of burnout, and experience of stress among teachers in vocational schools? (3) How do the personality traits and resilience of these teachers influence the appraisal of job demands? To answer these questions, we drew on data from an online survey conducted in Baden-Württemberg, Germany. With the use of closed items, 1470 teachers in vocational education were asked about their perception of six categories of job demands as well as their personality traits, resilience, tendency to burnout, well-being, job satisfaction, and experience of stress. Descriptive analyses, moderation analyses using structural equation modelling, and path analyses were conducted. The descriptive analyses showed that the category of teaching and interacting with learners was perceived as the most challenging category. Furthermore, challenge appraisal as a moderator was found to increase job satisfaction and decrease levels of burnout, while threat appraisal decreased well-being. Finally, resilience had a significant influence on the appraisal of job demands: higher levels of resilience led to higher values for challenge appraisal and lower values for threat appraisal. These results imply that the appraisal of job demands is related to the mental health of teachers in vocational education. Building a repertoire of resources, e.g., cognitive appraisal training or cooperation between colleagues, may support a more optimistic challenge appraisal of job demands and improve teachers' mental health. Furthermore, as resilience has been found to influence this appraisal process in favor of teachers' mental health, teachers are encouraged to increase their resilience, for example, by using interventions.
