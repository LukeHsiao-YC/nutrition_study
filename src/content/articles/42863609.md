### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討月桂酸 (C12:0) 是否能導致肝臟受體同源物-1 (LRH-1) 的配體二月桂醯磷脂醯膽鹼 (DLPC) 的內源性生成並增強LRH-1的轉錄活性，此前DLPC的內源性產生未被報導。研究透過在細胞、小鼠和人體中提供C12:0，發現DLPC會迅速累積，且LRH-1靶基因表達顯著增加。這些效應依賴於LRH-1，並顯示DLPC的生成涉及CDP-膽鹼途徑。研究結論膳食月桂酸可導致LRH-1配體DLPC的內源性產生及LRH-1的轉錄活化，揭示月桂酸可能透過LRH-1激動作用產生潛在的多效性作用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了DLPC作為LRH-1配體，其在體內的內源性生成機制及其與膳食脂肪酸的關聯性研究空白。過去DLPC的內源性存在並未被報導，本研究明確指出膳食月桂酸（C12:0）可直接導致DLPC在體內生成，並進一步活化LRH-1。未來的研究限制可能包含需要進一步探索LRH-1活化後在人體內長期和全面的生理學效應，尤其是在月桂酸已知具有高膽固醇血症作用的背景下。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Provision of Lauric Acid Results in Endogenous Production of the Liver Receptor Homolog-1 Ligand, Dilauroylphosphatidylcholine, and Evidence of LRH-1 Transactivation.
**Abstract:** The unusual phosphatidylcholine species dilauroylphosphatidylcholine (DLPC) has been reported to bind and activate the orphan nuclear receptor liver receptor homolog-1 (LRH-1). To date, DLPC has not been reported endogenously. Herein, we test the hypothesis that the provision of the acyl constituent of DLPC, lauric acid (C12:0), a saturated fatty acid rich in coconut oil, will 1) result in endogenous DLPC production and 2) enhance LRH-1 transcriptional activity. We measured DLPC following provision of C12:0 to HepG2 cells, C57/BL6J mice, and to healthy human participants in an acute, randomized, controlled crossover trial. LRH-1 DLPC was not observed in any C12:0-free system. Provision of C12:0 in the culture media or to C57/BL6J mice resulted in the rapid accumulation of DLPC, including DLPC's presence in multiple LRH-1 expressing tissues. Coconut oil-fed human participants exhibited DLPC in postprandial serum samples. Ex vivo and in vivo C12:0 provision resulted in increased mRNA expression of LRH-1 target gene(s), an effect that was not observed in hepatic knockout mice. Methyl-d9-choline administration revealed a complex reliance on CDP-choline-derived DLPC. C12:0 provision results in endogenous production of the LRH-1 ligand DLPC and LRH-1 transcriptional activation phenotypes. Our findings highlight potential pleiotropic effects of lauric acid, a common hypercholesterolemic dietary saturated fatty acid, secondary to LRH-1 agonism. NCT03481608.
