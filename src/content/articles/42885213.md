### 重點摘要
兒童克隆氏症（CD）的早期治療預測能力有限，但及時的生物製劑治療至關重要。本研究開發並外部驗證了一個多模態模型，結合大腸鏡影像和臨床數據，以預測兒童CD患者在常規治療後六個月內是否需要開始使用英夫利西單抗。該模型在發現隊列中達到0.839的AUROC和0.732的平衡準確度，外部驗證的AUROC分別為0.857和0.688，並透過可解釋性AI識別出關鍵預測因子。該模型展現了良好的預測性能，有望支援早期風險分層並幫助識別適合提早介入生物製劑治療的兒童CD患者。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇研究填補了兒童克隆氏症治療路徑早期預測能力有限的空白，提供了一個結合多模態數據的預測模型，以識別需要及早生物製劑介入的患者。然而，研究的外部驗證隊列規模相對較小（n=10和n=20），未來仍需在更大、更多元的數據集上進行驗證，以進一步提升模型的泛化能力和臨床實用性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Multimodal prediction of infliximab initiation within 6 months after initiation of conventional therapy in pediatric Crohn's disease: A multicenter algorithm development and validation study.
**Abstract:** Early prediction of treatment course in pediatric Crohn's disease (CD) remains limited despite the importance of timely biologic therapy. We developed and externally validated a multimodal model integrating colonoscopy images and clinical data to predict infliximab initiation within 6 months after conventional therapy. We collected data from children newly diagnosed with CD at three hospitals: Seoul National University Bundang Hospital for internal validation, Kyung Hee University Medical Center, and Soonchunhyang University Bucheon Hospital for external validation. Each patient contributed 7 endoscopic images and 20 clinical variables. An attention-based multiple-instance learning framework aggregated endoscopic images into a patient-level representation and integrated them with clinical features. The imaging encoder used a ResNet-50 backbone pretrained with momentum contrastive learning on GastroNet-5M, and clinical features were encoded using a multilayer perceptron. Performance was assessed using the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUROC), balanced accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 score. Explainable AI was evaluated using attention maps (image data) and SHAP values (tabular data). Seventy-seven children were included for model development and internal validation, with external validation performed in two independent cohorts (n = 10 and n = 20). The multimodal model achieved an AUROC of 0.839 and balanced accuracy of 0.732 in the discovery cohort. External-validation AUROCs were 0.857 and 0.688. SHapley Additive exPlanation analysis identified immunoglobulin G, body mass index, prealbumin, hematocrit, and age as key predictors. Attention maps highlighted inflamed mucosa and ulcerative lesions. A multimodal model integrating colonoscopy images with clinical data showed promising performance for predicting infliximab initiation within 6 months after conventional therapy in pediatric CD. Multimodal information may support early risk stratification and identify patients who could benefit from earlier biologic intervention.
