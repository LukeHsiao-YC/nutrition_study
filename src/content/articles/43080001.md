### 重點摘要
本總統演說強調專業網絡與積極參與如ASPEN等組織對臨床創新和挽救生命的重要性，並指出人際連結和同儕知識共享能彌補醫學文獻的不足，加速基礎研究轉化為臨床應用。文章透過ASPEN主席Kathleen Gura在營養支持藥學領域的經驗，分享三個關鍵臨床案例，包括推動魚油脂肪乳劑的應用與FDA核准、解決越南兒科維生素短缺並建立當地營養學會，以及在乙醇短缺時尋找替代導管鎖定液。這些經驗證明解決複雜臨床營養挑戰需依賴協作社群，並鼓勵新一代臨床醫師積極參與專業網絡以優化專業病患照護。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文章主要透過實例說明專業網絡與知識共享如何彌補醫學文獻在臨床應用上的不足，並加速研究成果轉化為實際病患照護方案。它強調了協作社群在解決複雜臨床挑戰中的關鍵作用，而非填補特定科學知識的空白。未來的研究可進一步量化此類網絡對臨床結果和創新效率的影響。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Your network is your net worth: Membership in professional organizations helps innovate and saves lives.
**Abstract:** Professional networking and active participation within organizations such as the ASPEN are critical drivers of clinical innovation and lifesaving patient care. This presidential address describes how human connection and peer-to-peer knowledge sharing bridge critical gaps in medical literature, directly accelerating the translation of bench research into bedside solutions. Drawing on a multi-decade career in nutrition support pharmacy at Boston Children's Hospital, ASPEN president Kathleen Gura shares three pivotal clinical scenarios that illustrate the power of the ASPEN network. Advancement of Lipid Emulsions: An international connection led to the first compassionate use of an intravenous fish oil lipid emulsion to treat essential fatty acid deficiency. This spark ignited 20 years of collaborative animal and clinical research, ultimately securing FDA approval for treating intestinal failure-associated liver disease. Global Health: Using ASPEN's drug shortage guidelines allowed an interdisciplinary team to resolve a critical pediatric multivitamin shortage in Vietnam as part of an educational effort leading to the advancement of nutritional care nationwide and ultimately the establishment of the Vietnamese Society for Parenteral and Enteral Nutrition (VietSPAN). Catheter Lock Solutions: Peer collaboration helped identify alternative catheter lock solutions to reduce central line-associated bloodstream infections (CLABSIs). When domestic regulatory shifts caused severe ethanol shortages, efforts were made to import 4% tetrasodium EDTA lock solutions under FDA compassionate use guidelines. These experiences demonstrate that solving complex clinical nutrition challenges requires a collaborative community. This address challenges the next generation of clinicians to actively engage, share unpublished insights, and utilize the collective expertise of the ASPEN infrastructure to optimize specialized patient care.
