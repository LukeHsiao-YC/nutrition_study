### 重點摘要
加薩地區面臨嚴重的兒童營養不良危機，起因於食物供應不足、飲食質量差、微量營養素缺乏及人道援助受限等複雜因素。本文綜合已發表研究與公開報告，指出衝突期間五歲以下兒童的急性營養不良情況惡化，且流離失所和不安全因素嚴重阻礙了治療的連續性。研究強調緊急應對措施必須立即處理兒童消瘦問題，同時建立長期支持兒童健康發展的條件。為此，建議實施整合性監測、協調多樣化服務，並確保人道援助能持續且去中心化地提供。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本文整合現有證據，填補了對加薩兒童營養不良危機全面理解與有效應對策略規劃的空白。然而，由於這是一篇綜合性論述，並未提供新的實證數據，未來的研究應著重於實地監測膳食多樣性、微量營養素狀況及治療保留率等具體數據，以更精確評估介入措施的成效。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Child Malnutrition in Gaza Requires Adequate Diets and Continuity of Care.
**Abstract:** Gaza's child malnutrition crisis reflects insufficient food availability, poor dietary quality, micronutrient deficiencies, disrupted treatment, displacement, insecurity, and constrained humanitarian access. Evidence before and during the 2023-2024 escalation documents household food insecurity and inadequate dietary diversity, alongside childhood anaemia. Conflict-period studies report acute malnutrition among children younger than five, with geographic disparities and higher severe acute malnutrition in northern Gaza. Programme evidence indicates that outpatient nutrition care can improve anthropometric measures among children retained in treatment, while attrition linked to displacement and insecurity undermines continuity of care. This letter synthesises published studies and public reporting to argue that emergency responses must address immediate wasting and the conditions required for child health and development. We recommend integrated surveillance of dietary diversity, micronutrient status, geographic inequities, and treatment retention; coordinated delivery of therapeutic nutrition, infant and young child feeding support, immunisation, psychosocial stimulation, and water, sanitation, and hygiene services; and sustained humanitarian access with fuel and decentralised care. Recovery should be assessed not only by food deliveries or short-term survival, but also by access to nutritious diets and essential child health services.
