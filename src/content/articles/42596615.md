### 重點摘要
兒童門脈高壓症 (PHT) 的管理在不同醫療中心之間存在顯著差異，本研究旨在進一步探討其未達成共識的領域。透過對門脈高壓特別興趣小組 (PHT-SIG) 進行問卷調查，結果發現儘管在關鍵定義和食道胃十二指腸鏡檢查上存在共識，但在非選擇性β受體阻滯劑應用、肝脾硬度測量標準、門靜脈血栓處理、MesoRex分流及介入性放射學的適應症，以及術後抗凝血方案等方面仍存在顯著分歧。這些發現凸顯了建立標準化照護的迫切性，呼籲未來應進行前瞻性多中心臨床試驗。最終目標是制定統一且基於證據的指南，以協調兒童 PHT 的臨床照護。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻明確指出當前兒童門脈高壓症照護中，在非選擇性β受體阻滯劑的應用、非侵入性標誌物的標準化、門靜脈血栓治療、介入性治療的適應症以及術後抗凝血方案等方面，缺乏統一的標準與證據。這項研究為未來的多中心臨床試驗提供了明確的方向，目的在於建立標準化的照護指南，但本身並未提供這些最終的指南或解決方案。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Beyond consensus: Identifying divergences in the care of children with portal hypertension.
**Abstract:** Portal hypertension (PHT) poses a significant clinical challenge in children, with management approaches varying widely across different medical centres, as previously reported by the European Society for Paediatric Gastroenterology, Hepatology and Nutrition Portal Hypertension Special Interest Group (PHT-SIG). This brief communication reviews the results of a follow-up questionnaire-based study carried out by the PHT-SIG to explore further management issues not covered in the initial survey. While most clinicians concur on key definitions and regularly use oesophagogastroduodenoscopy for both primary and secondary prophylaxis, notable differences remain in the application of non-selective beta-blockers. Liver and spleen stiffness measurements are increasingly employed as non-invasive markers of PHT, although no standardised paediatric thresholds currently exist. Practices concerning portal vein thrombosis and the indications for MesoRex shunt and interventional radiology also differ. Anticoagulation protocols following intervention are applied inconsistently. The findings highlight the necessity for prospective multicentre clinical trials to establish standardised, evidence-based guidelines, thereby harmonising the care provided to children with PHT.
