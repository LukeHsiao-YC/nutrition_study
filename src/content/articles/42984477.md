### 重點摘要
鑑於超重與肥胖是重大公共衛生挑戰，傳統研究常將健康行為孤立看待，忽略日常波動與個體內差異。本研究旨在評估在130名55-75歲高齡者中，使用四個為期七天的「測量爆發設計」來密集評估多重健康行為（飲食、身體活動、久坐行為、睡眠）的可行性。研究結果顯示，高參與者留存率（89.2%）和良好的數據依從率（83.5-91.8%）證實了此研究方案的可行性。這項研究成功地展示了整合多種評估方法於測量爆發設計中，以在更精細的時間與個體層次上分析高齡者健康行為的價值。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
過去研究在探討多重健康行為時，常將其視為獨立因子，且忽略行為在一天內及日復一日的波動與個體內差異。本研究透過採用「測量爆發設計」與密集縱向評估，成功地填補了此一研究空白，提供了一種可行的方法來同時研究飲食、身體活動、久坐行為和睡眠等複合式健康行為的暫時共變性與個體內變異性，為未來精細化分析提供了基礎。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Studying multiple health behaviours in older adults using a measurement burst design: protocol, feasibility, compliance, behavioural patterns and lessons learned.
**Abstract:** Overweight and obesity are major public health challenges, requiring preventive efforts targeting diet, physical activity (PA), sedentary behaviour (SB) and sleep. Current research often treats these behaviours in isolation, ignores daily fluctuations and focuses on between-person differences. Studies examining the temporal covariance of these behaviours, assessing within-day and day-to-day variations in determinants and exploring how these time-varying determinants predict within-person variability in behaviours are needed. This paper aims to evaluate the feasibility of a measurement burst design with repeated intensive longitudinal assessments and to present patterns of behaviours and determinants. A 12-month observational study with a measurement burst design was conducted (January-December 2025). In total, 130 participants aged 55-75 years enrolled in the study, which consisted of four 7-day measurement bursts. Data collection included monitoring of diet, PA, SB, sleep and time-based ecological momentary assessment (EMA). Compliance rates, descriptive statistics per burst and across bursts were computed. 116 participants completed all bursts (retention rate = 89·2 %). Compliance rates ranged from 83·5 % for time-based EMA to 91·8 % for SB, supporting feasibility of the protocol. Mean values of 1692 kcal/day (sd = 565) for energy intake, 9·4 h/day (sd = 2·4) for sedentary time and 10 610 steps/day (sd = 6302) were observed. Mean daily light, moderate and vigorous PA-levels were 102·9 (sd = 37·4), 65·5 (sd = 43·4) and 11·5 (sd = 17·0) minutes, respectively. Mean sleep duration was 6·4 h/night (sd = 1·3), with a mean sleep efficiency of 79·2 % (sd = 10·2). This paper demonstrated the feasibility of integrating multiple assessment methods within a measurement burst design for studying health behaviours in adults aged 55-75 years and highlighted the value of analyses at more fine-grained temporal and individual levels.
