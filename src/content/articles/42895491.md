### 重點摘要
這篇論文詳細闡述了「德州MyPlate大使計畫」的設計與方法學，旨在評估兩種多面向營養介入課程（MyPlate影響者和MyPlate教育者）及其相關輔助措施，以提升高中生的營養知識與飲食品質。研究採用非隨機雙組設計來比較兩種課程，並嵌入隨機試驗以評估MyPlate簡訊教育（客製化vs一般）及執行支援（同儕與教師vs教師通知）的效益。計畫將招募100名高中生大使，分別執行營養環境改造或同儕教育，並透過前後測問卷、皮膚類胡蘿蔔素等指標來評估飲食品質與正向青少年發展等主要結果。其分析方法包括配對樣本t檢定和一般線性模型，旨在深入了解不同介入策略的效果。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
該研究透過創新的多面向設計，特別是嵌入式隨機試驗（SMART），旨在填補複雜營養介入措施中，不同組成部分（如簡訊教育類型、執行支援模式）對高中生效果影響的知識空白。此研究設計有助於系統性地比較不同介入策略（如同儕引導與環境改變），並優化未來的青少年營養干預策略，儘管主課程比較採用非隨機設計，可能在因果推論上存在潛在限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Design and Methodology of the Texas MyPlate Ambassador Program: Two Multicomponent Nutrition Interventions for High School Students.
**Abstract:** Evaluate 2 new curricula-MyPlate Influencer and MyPlate Educator-and related intervention components (e.g., text messages, implementation support). A non-randomized two-arm design is used to evaluate (single-arm pretest to posttest changes) and compare (Influencer vs Educator) curricula. An embedded sequential multiple assignment randomized trial is used to evaluate MyPlate text message education and implementation support. Texas, USA PARTICIPANTS: Ambassadors (n = 100) at public high schools or home schools will be recruited online and in person. MyPlate Influencers implement a nutrition-focused environmental change project in their schools (e.g., taste-testing recipes, enhancing local wellness policies, and hosting agriculture events). MyPlate Educators provide peer-to-peer education to younger students. All ambassadors receive MyPlate goal-setting education. MyPlate text message education (tailored vs general) and implementation support (peer and educator vs educator notification) will be randomized (1:1 ratio). Primary outcomes-diet quality and positive youth development-will be assessed using pretest and posttest surveys. Other outcomes include dermal carotenoids and MyPlate knowledge (posttest only), monthly goal reports, and process evaluation measures (e.g., event tracking and interviews). Single-group pre- to post-intervention changes (paired sample t tests), and between-group differences and embedded randomized components (general linear models) will be evaluated.
