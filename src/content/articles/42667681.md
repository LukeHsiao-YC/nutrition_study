### 重點摘要
負向對照在因果研究中是處理混淆因子與偏差的有效工具，然而，目前缺乏關於如何選擇、應用、報告及解釋其發現的通用指南。本文旨在提供一套完整的指南，涵蓋進行負向對照分析的關鍵步驟、背景資訊、可行性與有效性考量。內容包括所有方法的概覽、其理論依據、重要考量因素與實例，並提供實用的檢查清單，以促進研究人員與審稿者對負向對照分析進行完整且嚴謹的報告。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本文填補了因果研究領域中，缺乏關於負向對照分析的選用、實施、報告與解釋的全面性通用指南的空白。它提供了一套系統性的框架和實用工具，旨在提升這類研究的嚴謹性、透明度和可重複性，進而加強研究發現的可信度。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Negative controls and how to use them: a guidance paper.
**Abstract:** Negative controls have gained traction as a useful tool for causal research to address confounding and bias. However, general guidance on how to select and apply negative controls and how to report and interpret their findings is lacking. This paper lays out the key steps of conducting a negative-control analysis and provides all relevant background information, important feasibility and validity considerations, and practical suggestions for the implementation and interpretation of each step. This includes a convenient overview of all methods, complete with their rationale, key considerations, and illustrative examples, and practical checklists that both researchers and reviewers can use to facilitate the complete and rigorous reporting of negative-control analyses.
