### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在評估致肥環境與兒童過重/肥胖率之間的關聯，並開發一個修訂版兒童致肥環境指數（COEI）。研究分析了3140個美國縣的環境數據，使用迴歸模型在全國及不同城鄉連續體層級進行分析。結果顯示，修訂後的COEI與全國及所有農村城鎮分類層級的兒童過重/肥胖率均呈正相關，且在農村縣份的關聯性更強。這強調了致肥環境因素在不同地理背景下作用各異，凸顯地方性預防兒童肥胖策略的重要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
過去的研究在探討致肥環境特徵的組合如何影響不同城鄉連續體下的兒童過重/肥胖率方面存在不足。這項研究透過開發一個加權修訂版兒童致肥環境指數（COEI），並在全國和不同城鄉層級進行分析，填補了此研究空白，並指出致肥環境因素在不同地理背景下的作用機制差異，強調地方性策略的重要性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Obesogenic Environment Features and Childhood Overweight/Obesity Across the Urban-Rural Continuum: Development of a Revised Childhood Obesogenic Environment Index.
**Abstract:** Environmental factors such as food access, walkability, and recreation opportunities are associated with childhood obesity risk and urban-rural obesity disparities. Although the Childhood Obesogenic Environment Index (COEI) was previously developed to characterize obesogenic environments across US counties, limited research has examined how combinations of obesogenic environment features relate to childhood overweight/obesity rates across the urban-rural continuum. The purpose of this study was to assess associations between obesogenic environments and childhood overweight/obesity rates and to develop a revised COEI composed of variables associated with childhood overweight/obesity rates. Data from 3140 US counties were analyzed using 10 environment variables associated with childhood obesity estimates. Counties were classified by Rural-Urban Continuum Codes (RUCC) and Census divisions. Regressions identified best-fit models nationally and by RUCC level, and beta coefficients from the national model were used to develop a weighted COEI.
