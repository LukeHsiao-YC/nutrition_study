### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估在護士家訪計畫中加入關係教育，對低收入初產婦的產婦抑鬱、物質濫用和自傷意念的效益。這項隨機對照試驗招募了238名母親，隨機分派至標準或結合關係教育的增強型計畫，並追蹤至產後兩年。結果顯示，增強型計畫改善了與孩子父親的穩定伴侶關係，並在產後一年時降低了抑鬱症狀，但在兩年追蹤期間未能顯著降低抑鬱症狀、自傷意念或物質濫用。本研究結論指出未來應探索變革機制，並整合伴侶參與、共同育兒及更具針對性的干預策略於家訪服務中。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究評估了在護士家訪計畫中加入關係教育對低收入初產婦心理健康與物質濫用問題的效益，發現其能改善伴侶關係穩定性及短期抑鬱症狀，但長期效果有限。這揭示了單純加入關係教育可能不足以長期顯著改善複雜的心理健康和物質濫用問題。未來的研究應著重於探索更有效的變革機制，並整合伴侶參與、共同育兒及更具針對性的干預策略，以解決這些未被充分解決的長期挑戰。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** A Randomized Controlled Trial of Relationship Education Within a Nurse Home Visiting Program to Improve Mental Health and Prevent Substance Use Among First-Time, Low-Income Mothers.
**Abstract:** To study home visiting programs augmented with relationship education addressing maternal depression (MD), substance use (SU), and self-harm ideation (SHI). We performed a secondary data analysis of a three-wave longitudinal randomized controlled trial of the Nurse-Family Partnership (NFP) program. In Oregon, 238 low-income, first-time pregnant mothers were randomized to a standard or augmented program where trained nurses integrated the Within My Reach curriculum and intimate partner violence screening and referral with the NFP workflow one-on-one at home visits. Nurses maintained good fidelity in the augmented program, tailored training to clients' immediate needs, and were advised not to share with the staff in the standard program. At pregnancy, 1-year, and 2-year follow-ups, research assistants interviewed mothers with the Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (including an SHI item), Alcohol Use Disorder Identification Test, and Drug Abuse Screening Test. Multilevel models and generalized estimating equations with the wave-intervention interactions were performed for MD, SHI, and SU, adjusting for race/ethnicity, age, education level, and nativity status. Depressive symptoms (25%), SU (36%), and SHI (6%, 7%, 9% across 3 waves) were common. Compared with the standard program, the augmented program indicated a stable romantic relationship with the child's father (66% vs. 50%, p = 0.01) and reduced depressive symptoms at the 1-year follow-up (p = 0.02) but did not reduce depressive symptoms, SHI, or SU over the two years. The findings can guide pragmatic research identifying mechanisms of change and integrating partner engagement, co-parenting, and targeted approaches to depression, SHI and SU into real-world home visiting services. Trial Registration ClinicalTrials.gov ID NCT01811719 https//clinicaltrials.gov/ct2/show/NCT01811719.
