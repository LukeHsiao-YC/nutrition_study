### 重點摘要
背景指出食物浪費導致氣候變遷，而弱勢族群卻面臨健康食物獲取不均的問題，食物再分配計畫旨在同時解決這兩大挑戰。本研究透過在瑞士沃州對食物再分配系統的組織利益相關者進行半結構式訪談和實地觀察，以質性描述性研究方法探討其觀點與經驗。結果顯示該系統是一個協作努力，並歸納出確保食物品質、同時解決食物需求與浪費、高度彈性以及各方之間善意合作等四個主題。研究結論指出，該多方利害關係人計畫的成功歸因於協作、適應性以及共同致力於減少食物浪費並改善食物獲取。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究透過質性方法，詳細闡述了瑞士沃州食物再分配計畫中利益相關者的視角與成功要素，填補了對此類計畫運作細節與協作機制的理解空白。未來的研究可進一步探討此類計畫對弱勢族群的實際營養健康影響，或擴展至不同地理區域進行比較分析。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Stakeholder perspectives of a food redistribution supply chain program to reduce food waste and improve food access for disadvantaged populations in Western Switzerland.
**Abstract:** Food waste is a major problem that contributes to climate change, while inequities in access to healthy food persist among socially vulnerable populations. Surplus food redistribution initiatives operate at the intersection of waste reduction and efforts to support food access. Our objective was to explore and better understand the perspectives and experiences of organizational stakeholders within the food redistribution system in the Canton of Vaud, Switzerland. We conducted a qualitative descriptive study using semi-structured qualitative interviews, as well as field observations. Data were analysed using conventional qualitative content analysis. This study took place in the Canton of Vaud in Western Switzerland, with most participants being in the capital city of Lausanne. A variety of participants were interviewed, including managers of the food delivery organisations, social service organisations, and grocery stores. A total of 14 interviews and 8 field observations were conducted. Participants described the food redistribution system as a collaborative effort at reducing food waste and increasing access to nutritious foods for socially disadvantaged populations. Four major themes emerged: (1) Processes are in place to ensure the food that reaches individuals is reasonably fresh and of high nutritional quality; (2) Food redistribution contributes to addressing food needs, while simultaneously reducing food waste; (3) A high degree of creativity and flexibility is required to optimally use the redistributed foods; (4) All parties involved in the food redistribution participate with goodwill towards one another. This study demonstrates that the success of the multi-stakeholder program currently in place to redistribute food products in the Canton of Vaud is due to collaboration, adaptability, and a shared goal of reducing food waste while improving food access.
