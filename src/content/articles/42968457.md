### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討歷史性居住區劃（redlining）這一結構性種族主義如何透過食物獲取途徑，影響心臟代謝疾病的盛行率。研究人員分析了來自38個州的11,457個普查區數據，使用結構方程模型揭示紅線區劃與食物獲取不足均直接與糖尿病、高血壓、冠心病及肥胖的較高盛行率相關。此外，紅線區劃還透過降低食物可近性間接促使這些疾病的發生。研究結果強調結構性種族主義對健康的負面影響，並建議需採取社區層面的介入措施來改善健康食物的可近性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究透過明確指出食物獲取作為結構性種族主義（紅線區劃）與心臟代謝疾病盛行率之間的中介途徑，填補了關於種族主義如何影響健康結果的機制性研究空白。它不僅證明了關聯性，更深入解釋了其作用路徑。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Direct and Indirect Effects of Historic Residential Redlining on Prevalence of Cardiometabolic Diseases via Food Access.
**Abstract:** This study investigated food access as a potential pathway between structural racism and the prevalence of cardiometabolic disease. A total of 11,457 census tracts across 201 counties, within 38 states, were combined for a final analytic sample. Direct and indirect relationships between redlining and each cardiometabolic disease via food access were investigated using structural equation modeling run in Stata v17, controlling for the population of each census tract. Mean prevalence of diabetes was 11.8%, high blood pressure 31.9%, coronary heart disease 6.0%, and obesity 31.8%. Redlining (0.22, p < 0.001) and lower food access (-0.11, p < 0.001) were directly associated with higher prevalence of diabetes. Redlining (0.10, p < 0.001) and lower food access (-0.10, p < 0.001) were directly associated with higher prevalence of high blood pressure. Redlining (0.12, p < 0.001) and lower food access (-0.04, p < 0.001) were directly associated with higher prevalence of coronary heart disease. Redlining (0.18, p < 0.001) and lower food access (-0.12, p < 0.001) were directly associated with higher prevalence of obesity. Redlining was also significantly indirectly associated with each condition via food access. Structural racism was associated with higher cardiometabolic disease prevalence via decreased food access. Interventions to improve access to healthy food at the community level may be needed to address the impact of structural racism on health.
