### 重點摘要
這項研究評估了針對兒童肥胖的「Bright Bodies」密集行為與生活方式治療，其成效是否會因參與者的社區特徵而異。研究分析了391名兒童的BMI變化，並依其居住地的兒童機會指數（COI）和健康環境指數（COI-HE）進行分組。結果顯示，所有社區組的BMI均有所下降，但居住在高COI和高COI-HE社區的兒童，其BMI下降幅度最大。研究結論指出，儘管治療普遍有效，但社區環境會影響治療成效，提示未來的干預措施應解決健康生活環境資源不均的問題。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了關於社區環境（特別是兒童機會指數和健康環境指數）如何影響兒童肥胖密集治療效果的研究空白，強調了社會經濟和建成環境在健康干預中的重要性。未來的研究應著重探討如何修正現有治療方案，以解決建構環境中促進健康活躍生活資源不平等的問題。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Neighborhood-Level Variation in the Effectiveness of Intensive Health Behavior and Lifestyle Treatment for Pediatric Obesity.
**Abstract:** This study assessed variation in BMI change by neighborhood characteristics of participants in Bright Bodies, a family-based intensive health behavior and lifestyle treatment for pediatric obesity. For first-time participants (2008-2018) with initial BMI ≥ 85th percentile, we geocoded addresses and assigned Child Opportunity Index (COI) and COI-Healthy Environment (COI-HE) scores (Low/Moderate/High). Using multivariable mixed effect linear regression, we assessed monthly change in BMI percent of the 95th percentile (%BMIp95) and extended BMI z score by COI and COI-HE. Of 391 youth (mean ± SD age 11.7 ± 2.8 years, 60.1% female), 80.8% resided in Low COI and 66.5% in Low COI-HE neighborhoods. Monthly %BMIp95 change differed by COI (p = 0.039 for interaction) and COI-HE (p = 0.004). Mean %BMIp95 decreased in all subgroups; greatest reductions were in High COI (-1.86 [95% CI: -2.40, -1.33]) and High COI-HE groups (-2.32 [-2.78, -1.85]) and lowest in Moderate COI (-1.17 [-1.59, -0.75]) and Low COI-HE groups (-1.53 [-1.75, -1.32]). Change in extended BMI z score differed significantly between COI-HE subgroups only (p < 0.001). Bright Bodies participation was associated with BMI reduction among all COI/COI-HE subgroups, but the highest COI/COI-HE groups had the greatest reductions. Modifications to intensive health behavior and lifestyle treatments should address inequities in access to built environments that facilitate healthy, active living.
