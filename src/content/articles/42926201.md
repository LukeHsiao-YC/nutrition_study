### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討年齡與族裔對血清全轉鈷胺素 (HoloTC) 和甲基丙二酸 (MMA) 這兩種維生素B12指標的影響。透過分析倫敦一家醫院的61,193份多族裔樣本，研究發現HoloTC濃度存在顯著的族裔差異，在兒童時期差異不明顯，但成年後黑人患者HoloTC濃度最高，亞洲人居中，白人最低。在HoloTC介於25-70 pmol/L的患者中，使用年齡特定參考值，白人、黑人及亞洲人分別有32.7%、14.8%和34.1%呈現MMA升高。研究結論指出，維生素B12的HoloTC和MMA濃度隨年齡和族裔而顯著變化，其中黑人成年人表現出較高的HoloTC和較低的MMA濃度，暗示其維生素B12狀態可能較佳。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了在大型多族裔人群中，維生素B12生物標記濃度受年齡和族裔影響的流行病學數據空白，強調了在臨床評估中考慮這些人口學因素的重要性。然而，本研究主要為觀察性數據，未能深入探討導致這些族裔差異的潛在生物學、飲食或社會經濟因素，這將是未來研究的限制與方向。

**Title:** Age and ethnicity influence serum holotranscobalamin ('Active' B12) and methylmalonic acid concentrations, two markers of vitamin B
**Abstract:** Serum vitamin B HoloTC was measured in 61,193 samples from an ethnically-diverse hospital population in London (Jan 2022-Oct 2023). HoloTC <25, 25-70 and >70 pmol/L were classified as deficient, indeterminate and replete, respectively. MMA was measured in 12,233 patients with holoTC 25-70 pmol/L. HoloTC concentrations differed significantly by ethnicity. Ethnic differences were minimal in children but became apparent in adulthood, with Black patients showing the highest holoTC concentrations, Asians intermediate, and White the lowest (p < 0.001). Overall, 1.7% of patients were holoTC deficient and 25.3% indeterminate. Among patients with holoTC 25-70 pmol/L, elevated MMA occurred in 32.7% of White, 14.8% of Black and 34.1% of Asian patients (p < 0.001) using age-specific MMA cut-offs ( > 280 nmol/L for ≤65 years; >360 nmol/L for >65 years). Using a higher MMA cut-off ( > 750 nmol/L), prevalence fell to 4.0%, 1.2%, and 4.7%, respectively.
