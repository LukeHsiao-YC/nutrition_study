### 重點摘要
這項回溯性研究旨在比較 tirzepatide 與注射型 semaglutide 在預防體重過重或肥胖且患有第二型糖尿病成人主要不良肝臟事件（MALO）的有效性。研究分析了 TriNetX 全球網路的新使用者數據，並以首次 MALO 事件（包括肝硬化、失代償事件和肝細胞癌）為主要觀察終點。結果顯示，在中位數約17個月的追蹤期間，tirzepatide 和 semaglutide 在 MALO 風險方面沒有顯著差異，且此結果在敏感性分析中仍保持一致。因此，對於患有第二型糖尿病的高風險族群，兩種藥物在短期至中期追蹤中，於減輕肝病進展和 MALO 方面具有可比擬的有效性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇研究填補了 tirzepatide 與注射型 semaglutide 在預防第二型糖尿病、超重或肥胖成人主要不良肝臟事件方面的直接比較數據空白。然而，由於研究是回溯性設計且中位追蹤時間僅約17個月，未能評估這兩種藥物在更長期對肝臟預後和疾病進展的影響，這將是未來研究需要探討的限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Major Adverse Liver Outcomes With Tirzepatide and Injectable Semaglutide in Adults With Overweight or Obesity and Type 2 Diabetes.
**Abstract:** This study aimed to compare the effectiveness of tirzepatide versus injectable semaglutide in preventing major adverse liver outcomes (MALO) among adults with overweight or obesity and type 2 diabetes. This retrospective target trial emulation utilized the TriNetX global network to analyze new users of tirzepatide and semaglutide. The primary outcome was time-to-first MALO (a composite of cirrhosis, decompensated events, and hepatocellular carcinoma). Propensity score matching balanced baseline covariates. Over a median follow-up of ~17 months, no significant difference in MALO risk was observed between tirzepatide and semaglutide initiators (estimated incidence rate [IR] 4.05 vs. 4.04 per 1000 person-years [PY]; hazard ratio [HR] 1.04, 95% CI 0.88-1.23). This comparable risk profile was maintained across sensitivity analyses, including people with MASLD (IR 9.97 vs. 10.03 per 1000 PY; HR 1.03, 95% CI: 0.75-1.42) and "as treated" analysis (HR 0.98, 95% CI: 0.80-1.21). Tirzepatide achieved greater BMI reduction (mean difference ~1.1 kg/m In this high-risk population with type 2 diabetes, tirzepatide and injectable semaglutide offered comparable effectiveness in mitigating liver disease progression and MALO in the short- to medium-term follow-up.
