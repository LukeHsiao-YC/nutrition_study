### 重點摘要
這項研究評估了兒童膽管畸形患者術中與術後的肝纖維化程度及其臨床結果，綜合運用組織病理學、基因表達分析和非侵入性評估。研究發現顯著肝纖維化與手術年齡較輕、膽汁淤積及生化指標異常相關，並伴有特定基因的上調。儘管術中肝纖維化未預示術後併發症，但術後肝臟硬度升高卻與長期肝膽發病率密切相關。本研究強調術後肝臟硬度在兒童膽管畸形患者追蹤中的重要性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻結合了組織病理學、基因表達和非侵入性評估方法，深入探討兒童膽管畸形相關的肝纖維化病理機制，並發現術後肝臟硬度而非術中纖維化程度更能預測長期肝膽併發症。這填補了對術後長期預後評估工具的空白。然而，作為單中心回顧性研究，其結果推廣性可能受限，且基因表達分析的樣本量相對較小。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Liver fibrosis and clinical outcomes in pediatric choledochal malformations.
**Abstract:** To assess fibrotic liver injury associated with choledochal malformations (CMs) at the time of operative treatment and during postoperative follow-up using histopathology, gene expression analysis, and non-invasive fibrosis assessment. This single-center retrospective study included all pediatric patients (<18 years) operated for a CM at Helsinki University Hospital between 2003 and 2024 with available intraoperative liver biopsies and/or postoperative liver stiffness measurements. Liver histology was graded using the Metavir staging. Liver biochemistry, non-invasive fibrosis markers (AST-to-platelet ratio index [APRi], Fibrosis-4 score [FIB-4], Gamma-glutamyl transpeptidase-to-platelet ratio [GPR]), and liver stiffness were analyzed. In 15 patients, liver micro RNA (mRNA) expression of selected fibrosis-, inflammation-, and cholangiocyte-related genes were quantified by quantitative polymerase chain reaction (qPCR) and compared with 16 controls. Among 40 patients with intraoperative biopsies, 7 showed significant fibrosis (Metavir ≥2), which was associated with younger age at surgery (median 0.49 vs. 4.7 years, p = 0.007), cholestasis and elevated alanine aminotransferase (ALT), aspartate aminotransferase (AST), gamma-glutamyl transferase (GGT), bilirubin, APRi, and GPR. Collagen (COL10A1, COL15A1, and COL22A1), pro-inflammatory (TLR4 and CD14), and cholangiocyte-associated genes (KRT7, KRT19, SCTR, and MMP7) were upregulated among CM patients, while ACTA2 expression remained unchanged. Postoperative liver stiffness was elevated (>6 kPa) in 7/30 patients, correlating with higher ALT, GGT, GPR and intraoperative fibrosis stage. Long-term hepatobiliary morbidity was associated with higher postoperative liver stiffness, but not with intraoperative liver fibrosis. Significant liver fibrosis in pediatric CM is associated with early operative age, histopathological cholestasis and biochemical injury, supported by transcriptomic changes. While significant liver fibrosis was not associated with postoperative morbidity, patients with early and long-term complications showed elevated liver stiffness emphasizing its value in postoperative patient follow-up.
