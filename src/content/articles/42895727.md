### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在比較不同動態心電圖監測模式在兒童心律不整診斷與臨床應用上的效益，此前相關模式的診斷效能尚不明確。研究納入435名兒童，比較了24小時霍特、7天連續霍特及7天事件紀錄器三種模式。結果顯示，7天連續霍特心電圖在偵測主要心律不整方面（6.2% vs 2.3%）和敏感性（73% vs 27%）均顯著優於其他兩種模式。特別是對於影響臨床處置的心跳過速性心律不整，7天連續監測能帶來更高的管理變更率，因此建議在無結構性心臟病的兒童中，優先選擇7天連續動態心電圖監測以提高診斷效益。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
此研究填補了兒童病患中不同動態心電圖監測模式診斷效益不明確的研究空白，明確指出7天連續霍特心電圖在偵測心律不整上的優越性。然而，本研究為單中心研究，且主要針對無結構性心臟病的兒童，未來可考慮進行多中心研究以提升結果的普適性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Ambulatory Electrocardiography Modalities in Pediatric Arrhythmia Detection and Management.
**Abstract:** The diagnostic efficacy of different ambulatory electrocardiographic monitoring modalities in this patient population remains unclear. To compare the diagnostic yield and clinical utility of 3 ambulatory electrocardiographic modalities in pediatric patients. This single-center study took place at an outpatient clinic and an inpatient ward of National Taiwan University Children's Hospital. Patients younger than 18 years who underwent ambulatory electrocardiographic monitoring between December 2022 and January 2025 were included. These data were analyzed February 2025 to March 2026. A continuous 7-day Holter device that functioned as an event recorder was used. Recordings from the first 24 hours were defined as the 24-hour Holter results. Arrhythmias were categorized as major (tachyarrhythmia or bradyarrhythmia) or minor (extrasystolic arrhythmia). All patients were followed up for 1 year; the final arrhythmia diagnosis was used as the reference standard. The primary and secondary outcomes were detection of arrhythmia and a change in clinical management attributable to monitoring findings, respectively. In total, 435 children (235 female [54%] and 200 male [46%]; mean [SD] age, 12.3 [3.9] years) were included. The mean (SD) duration of analyzable ambulatory electrocardiographic data was 4.9 (1.7) days. Of 411 children who activated the event recorder, 10.7% had symptom-correlated arrhythmias. The 7-day Holter monitoring demonstrated a significantly higher rate of major arrhythmias (6.2%) than the 24-hour Holter or 7-day event recordings (2.3%; rate difference 95% CI, 1.9%-6.0%; P < .001). Seven-day Holter monitoring showed superior sensitivity (7-day Holter monitoring: 73%; rate difference 95% CI, 54.1%-84.6%; 24-hour Holter monitoring: 27%; rate difference 95% CI, 13.4%-43.1%; and 7-day event recorder: 27%, rate difference 95% CI, 13.4%-43.1%; P < .001) and a high negative predictive value (97.5%, 95% CI, 95.2%-98.6%). Tachyarrhythmias were associated with higher rates of management changes; management changed in 7.1% of patients with 7-day Holter monitoring, 1.6% with 24-hour Holter (rate difference 95% CI, 3.0%-7.6%; P < .001) and 2.8% with a 7-day event recorder (rate difference 95% CI, 2.0%-6.2%; P < .001). In this study of pediatric patients without structural heart disease who underwent electrocardiographic monitoring, 7-day continuous ambulatory electrocardiographic monitoring was associated with a significantly higher diagnostic yield than 24-hour Holter and 7-day event recording. Continuous monitoring may be preferred to detect tachyarrhythmias likely to influence clinical management.
