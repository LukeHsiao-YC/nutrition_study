### 重點摘要
石榴汁富含多酚，被認為具有代謝益處，但其急性代謝影響尚不明確。這項隨機交叉試驗招募了42名成人，比較等熱量的石榴汁、糖水和水對餐後血糖與腸泌素反應的影響。研究結果顯示，石榴汁引起的血糖反應與糖水相似，但腸泌素反應卻明顯不同。此外，研究發現個體間的血糖反應模式存在顯著異質性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了關於石榴汁與糖水在急性血糖與腸泌素反應上的直接比較空白。然而，由於觀察到個體血糖反應模式的異質性，未來的重複測量研究需要進一步探討這些現象的可重現性及其生物學基礎。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Pomegranate Juice Induces Similar Glycemic but Distinct Incretin Responses Compared with Sugar-Water: A Randomized Crossover Trial.
**Abstract:** Pomegranate juice (PomJ) is a rich source of polyphenols, particularly ellagitannins and anthocyanins, that have been linked to metabolic benefits. However, evidence regarding its acute metabolic effects remains inconclusive. To compare acute glycemic and incretin responses following consumption of isocaloric pomegranate juice (PomJ) and sugar-water, and to explore individual variability in glycemic response patterns. In this randomized, controlled, crossover trial, 42 adults completed three interventions following a standardized low-polyphenol diet, which was maintained for the entire study. Participants consumed isocaloric PomJ, sugar-water, and water, in random order, each separated by a 1-week washout period. Postprandial glucose, insulin, glucagon, GLP-1, GIP, C-peptide, and triglycerides were measured over 2 hours. Glycemic and incretin responses were analyzed using mixed-effects models. Exploratory analyses, including dynamic time warping (DTW) and response stratification, were performed to characterize individual glycemic response patterns. Participants at baseline had a mean age of 33.6±10.4years, a mean BMI of 27.1±4.6kg/m Pomegranate juice produced similar glycemic but distinct incretin responses compared with sugar-water. Exploratory analyses highlighted heterogeneity in glycemic response patterns under standardized study conditions. Further repeated-measures studies are needed to determine the reproducibility and biological basis of these observations. This study was registered in clinicaltrials.gov NCT04591782.
