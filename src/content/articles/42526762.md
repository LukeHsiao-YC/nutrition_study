### 重點摘要
本研究旨在開發以乳酸菌蜂膠萃取物（LPE）強化的機能性小麥麵條，以提升抗氧化潛力，並評估其生物活性保留、體外生物可利用性及感官接受度。結果顯示，LPE劑量依賴性地增加麵條的抗氧化活性，且在模擬消化後，酚類化合物與抗氧化能力顯著提升，關鍵類黃酮的穩定釋放也獲證實。儘管高濃度（0.9%）LPE影響了麵條的感官品質，但0.6%的LPE配方能在提供顯著健康效益與高生物可利用性的同時，維持良好的適口性。這項研究證實LPE是將小麥麵條轉化為具備生物活性的機能性食品基質的有效天然策略，並適合工業化應用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了將蜂膠整合到如麵條等主食中，以及其在複雜澱粉-麩質基質中的生物可利用性研究不足的空白，首次系統性地探討了乳酸菌蜂膠萃取物在麵條中的添加效果、生物活性保留與消化釋放機制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Enhancing the Functional Potential of Wheat Noodles via Lactic Acid-Based Propolis Fortification: A Pioneer Study on Bioactive Retention and Bioaccessibility.
**Abstract:** The development of functional foods using natural bioactive resources is essential for mitigating oxidative stress-related diseases. Propolis is a potent antioxidant source, but its integration into staple foods like noodles and its subsequent bioaccessibility within a complex starch-gluten matrix remain under-researched. This study aimed to develop functional wheat noodles enriched with a lactic acid-based propolis extract (LPE) and to investigate the impact of enrichment on the phytochemical profile, bioactive retention, in vitro bioaccessibility, and sensory acceptability. Noodles were formulated with varying concentrations (0.0%, 0.3%, 0.6%, and 0.9%) of LPE. Phytochemical profiling was conducted using LC-MS/MS, and the release kinetics of phenolics were monitored through the standardized INFOGEST in vitro digestion protocol (oral, gastric, and intestinal phases). Total phenolic content (TPC) and antioxidant capacity (DPPH, CUPRAC) were measured at each phase. Sensory evaluation was performed by a trained panel using a 5-point hedonic scale. LPE enrichment significantly increased the baseline antioxidant activity in a dose-dependent manner. Simulated digestion revealed a significant "matrix effect," where TPC and antioxidant capacity increased progressively through the intestinal phase, reaching 81.31 mg GAE/100 mL for the 0.9% LPE-added sample. LC-MS/MS confirmed the stability and enhanced release of key flavonoids, including chrysin, galangin, and pinocembrin, following enzymatic hydrolysis. While color and texture remained unaffected, sensory scores for taste and odor declined significantly at the 0.9% concentration. Lactic acid-based propolis extract (LPE) is an effective natural agent for transforming wheat noodles into a bioactive food matrix. The 0.6% LPE formulation provides an optimal balance, delivering substantial health-promoting bioactivity and high bioaccessibility while maintaining consumer palatability, making it suitable for industrial-scale functional food applications.
