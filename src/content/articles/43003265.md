### 重點摘要
這項前瞻性世代研究旨在利用新穎的軌跡分析框架，描繪孕期腸道菌群的動態變化，並探討其與母體生理及生活型態因子間的關聯。研究分析了239位孕婦在各孕期提供的糞便、血液樣本和問卷資料，透過主座標分析結合k-means聚類識別出三種獨特的腸道菌群軌跡模式。這些模式包括最佳適應、漸進性失調和持續性失調，並與母體的代謝、肝臟、腎臟及免疫功能以及可調整的生活型態因子呈現顯著且差異化的關聯。此研究證實軌跡分析框架能提供可解讀的腸道菌群模式，有助於理解母體生理適應及相關風險因子。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究成功填補了在孕期中動態捕捉腸道菌群的縱向模式，並將其與母體生理學精確關聯起來的方法學挑戰。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Pregnancy gut microbiota follows three distinct trajectories linked to maternal physiology and lifestyle.
**Abstract:** The gut microbiota undergoes dynamic changes during pregnancy, yet capturing its longitudinal patterns and linking them to maternal physiology remains methodologically challenging. We aimed to characterize gut microbiota dynamics across pregnancy using a novel trajectory-based framework and to investigate its associations with maternal host physiology and lifestyle factors. In this prospective cohort, 239 pregnant women provided fecal, blood samples, and completed questionnaires during each trimester. Gut microbiota trajectory patterns were identified through a two-step analytical framework: principal coordinate analysis (PCoA) of Bray-Curtis distances was first performed on all longitudinal samples, followed by k-means clustering applied to the resulting per-trimester PCo coordinates (PCo1 and PCo2). Associations of the derived patterns with maternal blood parameters (non-parametric tests and mixed-effects models), and risk factors (multinomial logistic regression) were assessed. We identified three distinct gut microbiota trajectory patterns: Pattern 1 (n=108, 45.2%), an optimal adaptive trajectory with increasing microbial favorable gradient and high abundance of beneficial taxa; Pattern 2 (n=88, 36.8%), a progressive dysbiotic trajectory with a declining microbial favorable gradient; and Pattern 3 (n=43, 18.0%), a persistently dysbiotic trajectory with the highest pathogen load despite quantitative improvement.
