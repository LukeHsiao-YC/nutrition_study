### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在識別韓國成年人罹患糖尿病、高血壓和高膽固醇血症的共同及特定年齡層危險因子。研究透過對韓國國民健康營養調查（2022-2023年）的橫斷面數據進行二次分析，並按年齡分層進行多變量邏輯迴歸。結果顯示，年齡增長和肥胖是這些慢性病的共同危險因子，但肥胖與年輕人糖尿病的關聯最強，低蔬果攝取與65-74歲者高血壓相關，而運動不足則與中老年人糖尿病相關。研究結論強調，需根據不同年齡和行為特徵，制定個人化的預防策略。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究明確了特定年齡層的行為與臨床危險因子，為制定更具針對性的慢性病預防策略提供了實證依據，填補了對年齡差異化風險理解的空白。然而，作為一項橫斷面研究，它無法建立因果關係，這將是未來縱向研究可以進一步探討的限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Age-Specific Behavioral and Clinical Risk Factors for Diabetes, Hypertension, and Hypercholesterolemia.
**Abstract:** To identify common and age-specific risk factors for diabetes, hypertension, and hypercholesterolemia among adults. Secondary analysis of cross-sectional, nationally representative data from the ninth Korea National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (2022-2023). South Korea. Adults aged > 19 years with complete data on all study variables. Diabetes, hypertension, and hypercholesterolemia; independent variables included obesity, smoking, alcohol use, physical activity, dietary behaviors, sleep duration, perceived stress, and sociodemographic factors. Multivariable logistic regression assessed associations between sociodemographic, behavioral, and dietary factors and each chronic condition, stratified by age groups (19-44, 45-64, 65-74, and ≥ 75 years). In the full analytic sample, older age and obesity were consistently associated with higher odds of diabetes, hypertension, and hypercholesterolemia (all P < 0.001). In age-stratified analyses, obesity showed the strongest association with diabetes among younger adults (P < 0.001). Low fruit and vegetable intake was associated with hypertension among adults aged 65-74 years (P = 0.02), and insufficient physical activity was associated with diabetes in middle-aged and older adults (P = 0.01). Age and behavior-specific differences highlight the need for tailored prevention strategies integrating demographic and lifestyle factors.
