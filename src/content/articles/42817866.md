### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討人類皮下（S-ASC）和內臟（V-ASC）脂肪幹細胞在脂肪分化過程中，胰島素受體（IR）及其亞型（IRa、IRb）和類胰島素生長因子-I受體（IGF-IR）的表達動態。研究發現，在脂肪分化過程中，IR在兩種類型的幹細胞中均增加，但內臟脂肪幹細胞的IRb增幅顯著高於IRa，而皮下脂肪幹細胞則表現出更高的IRa表達和胰島素誘導的Akt活化。此外，皮下脂肪幹細胞的脂肪生成效率遠高於內臟脂肪幹細胞。這些結果表明，IR亞型的脂肪庫特異性動態可能影響脂肪生成能力和胰島素反應性，並暗示脂肪細胞分化可能存在部分不依賴受體的途徑。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究藉由比較皮下和內臟脂肪幹細胞在脂肪分化過程中胰島素受體亞型的動態變化，填補了對不同脂肪庫在分子層面上影響脂肪生成能力和胰島素敏感性的理解空白。然而，研究結論中「部分不依賴受體途徑的脂肪細胞分化」的發現指出，未來仍需進一步探討除了受體作用之外，影響脂肪生成和胰島素反應性的其他分子機制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Depot-Specific Insulin Receptor Isoform Dynamics During Human Adipose-Derived Stem Cell Adipogenesis.
**Abstract:** The insulin receptor (IR) and the insulin-like growth factor I receptor (IGF-IR) regulate adipose tissue (AT) differentiation. This study investigated the expression of IGF-IR, IR, and the IR isoforms (IRa and IRb) in human subcutaneous (S-ASC) and visceral (V-ASC) adipose-derived stem cells. S-AT and V-AT biopsies were obtained from human donors and ASC were differentiated into adipocytes. mRNA levels of all receptors were quantified by qPCR at baseline and after differentiation. IR protein expression and adipogenic efficiency were assessed by flow cytometry. In undifferentiated ASC, IGF-IR transcripts exceeded IR and were higher in S-ASC than V-ASC. Upon differentiation, IGF-IR decreased in S-ASC but remained unchanged in V-ASC, whereas IR increased in both depots. S-ASC displayed ~twofold higher IRa expression and greater insulin-induced Akt activation. During adipogenesis, IR expression rose in both depots, with IRb exceeding IRa particularly in V-ASC (+50%). In whole AT, IR exceeded IGF-IR, and no depot-specific differences were observed between IR isoforms. Adipogenic conversion was higher in S-ASC (60%) than V-ASC (20%). Only ~77% of S-ASC- and ~61% of V-ASC-derived adipocytes expressed surface IR. Depot-specific dynamics of IR isoforms may influence adipogenic capacity and insulin responsiveness and suggest partially receptor-independent pathways of adipocyte differentiation.
