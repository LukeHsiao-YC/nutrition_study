### 重點摘要
這份政策聲明探討了離開寄養體系青少年所面臨的健康與福祉方面的差距及結構性障礙。文章評估了現有政策，如延長寄養服務和擴大醫療補助，這些政策雖增加了照護的可近性，但仍受行政複雜性、人力短缺和碎片化照護系統的限制。為改善此族群的結果，聲明建議將其生活經驗納入政策制定、加強兒科醫師與兒童福利系統的合作、擴大延長寄養服務的範圍，並強化醫療照護的轉銜規劃。兒科醫師在此過程中扮演著關鍵角色，需透過倡議和創傷知情照護來支持青少年成功轉銜至成年。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇政策聲明旨在填補現有政策在支持離開寄養體系青少年健康與福祉方面的不足，並提出具體且以生活經驗為中心的政策建議。然而，文獻中未詳細說明如何具體克服行政複雜性、人力短缺和碎片化系統的實施挑戰，這可能是未來研究或政策實踐需要進一步探討的限制。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Supporting the Health and Well-Being of Youth Aging Out of Foster Care: Policy Statement.
**Abstract:** This policy statement examines disparities and structural barriers affecting youth aging out of foster care and evaluates existing policy efforts, such as extended foster care and Medicaid expansion, that have expanded access to care but remain constrained by administrative complexity, workforce shortages, and fragmented systems of care. This statement provides policy recommendations to improve outcomes for this population, including centering lived experience in policy development, strengthening collaboration between pediatricians and child welfare systems, expanding access to extended foster care, and enhancing transition planning for health care. Pediatricians play a key role in advocacy and trauma-informed care to support successful transitions to adulthood.
