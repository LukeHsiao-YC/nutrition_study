### 重點摘要
本文回應了Renson和Westreich對「差中差設計是否應被視為準實驗」的質疑，指出準實驗研究的定義應基於「明確的設計選擇」，而非因果推論的強度。作者認為差中差設計結合了選擇合適對照組的設計元素與平行趨勢的強假設，處於隨機分配和純統計調整方法之間。最終結論是，任何流行病學設計，包括隨機對照試驗，都不應僅因其標籤而獲得特殊地位，因果推論的可信度取決於具體設計和分析選擇的品質。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻澄清了準實驗設計的定義，特別是將差中差設計納入其中，並強調了評估因果推論可信度時，應著重於具體設計和分析選擇的嚴謹性，而非研究類型的標籤，從而填補了流行病學研究方法論中關於設計分類和因果推論有效性評估的空白。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Quasi-experiments rely on design choices: a reflection on Renson and Westreich's 'What makes a study design quasi-experimental?'.
**Abstract:** Renson and Westreich's recent article questions whether difference-in-differences designs should be considered quasi-experimental. While we agree that difference-in-differences requires careful interpretation and should not be uncritically privileged, we argue that this is not sufficient grounds for excluding it from the quasi-experimental category. We argue that quasi-experiments are defined not by the strength and transparency of their justification for causality, but by the presence of explicit design choices. Design choices vary in strength. At one end of the spectrum are designs that guarantee exogeneity, such as focusing only randomized allocation to an exposure; at the other are approaches that rely primarily on statistical adjustment. Difference-in-differences occupies an intermediate position because it combines an explicit design element: the selection of a suitable comparator, with the strong functional-form assumption of parallel trends. We further argue that no epidemiological design, including difference-in-differences, Mendelian randomization, or randomized trials, merits special status solely because of its label. Instead, the credibility of causal inferences depends on the quality of the specific design and analytical choices used to justify identifying assumptions.
