### 重點摘要
傳統飲食評估工具在非西方地區因缺乏本地食物代表性，導致準確性受限。本研究旨在評估使用美國農業部（USDA）標準資料庫與客製化地方食物資料庫（DbF）輔助照片的24小時飲食回顧法，對20名哥倫比亞成年人飲食評估的準確性。結果顯示，兩種方法的誤報率相似，但DbF在熱量和巨量營養素方面與實際秤重記錄呈現更強的相關性。這項研究指出，建構包含地方食物的客製化資料庫能有效提升非西方人口飲食評估的準確性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
傳統飲食評估工具因缺乏非西方地區的本地食物資訊，導致評估準確性受限。本研究透過建立客製化地方食物資料庫並結合照片輔助24小時飲食回顧法，有效彌補了現有國際參考資料庫（如USDA FNDDS）在地域與文化偏見上的不足，提升了對哥倫比亞等非西方飲食模式的捕捉能力。

**Title:** Overcoming geographical and cultural bias in dietary assessment: a local food database and photograph-assisted 24-h recalls improve accuracy in Colombian adults.
**Abstract:** Accurate dietary assessment in non-Western populations is hindered not only by recall bias but also by the limited representation of local foods in international reference databases. When standardised tools fail to capture regional dietary practices, the quality of dietary assessments is potentially compromised. This cross-sectional study evaluated the accuracy of photograph-assisted 24-h recalls (24hr) analysed using the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) Automated Self-Administered 24-Hour Dietary Assessment Tool (ASA24) system (based on Food and Nutrient Database for Dietary Studies (FNDDS) 2017-2018) or a customised database enriched with local foods and international measurement units (DbF). Twenty Colombian adults photographed and weighed foods consumed over two non-consecutive days. A dietitian conducted 24hr interviews using the multiple-pass method based on the photographs. Intake estimated with ASA24 and DbF was compared against weighed food records as reference. Blood and urine samples were collected to assess nutrient biomarkers. Accuracy was evaluated using correlation analyses and Bland-Altman plots, while usual intake was estimated using PC-SIDE. Misreporting was similar between ASA24 and DbF (15 %).
