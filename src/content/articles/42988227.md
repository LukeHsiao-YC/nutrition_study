### 重點摘要
連續式血糖監測儀 (CGM) 在非糖尿病健康人群中用於優化代謝健康及個人化營養的應用日益受到關注。食品與營養科學促進研究所為此召集專家研討會，探討CGM在健康人群中的科學、方法學及臨床應用考量。會議強調餐後血糖反應存在顯著的個體內外變異性，CGM雖能捕捉這些動態，但目前面臨分析變異性、單次評估精確度不足、缺乏標準化協議及長期臨床結果證據等限制。因此，在建立高品質隨機對照試驗和共識性指導之前，CGM在非糖尿病護理中的應用應謹慎，並最好在有專業監督的研究或臨床環境中進行。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻透過評估CGM在非糖尿病人群中使用的科學考量、方法學限制和臨床意義，填補了營養與健康研究中的重要空白。然而，目前最大的研究限制在於缺乏高品質的隨機對照試驗、標準化研究協議、共識性指南，以及將CGM衍生的指標與長期臨床結果連結的足夠證據。未來的研究需要彌補這些空白，以確定CGM在健康人群中的臨床相關性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Research and Clinical Considerations for Continuous Glucose Monitoring in Healthy Populations.
**Abstract:** The expanding availability of continuous glucose monitors (CGMs) has generated growing interest in their application beyond diabetes management, particularly in healthy individuals seeking to optimize metabolic health through personalized nutrition. In May 2025, the Institute for the Advancement of Food and Nutrition Sciences (IAFNS) convened a multidisciplinary expert workshop to examine the scientific, methodological, and clinical considerations surrounding continuous glucose monitor (CGM) application in healthy populations. Discussions addressed normal glycemic physiology, determinants of postprandial glycemic responses (PPGRs), CGM use and functional health outcomes, research applications, and implications for clinical practice. The purpose of this initiative was to develop a perspective paper that synthesizes current evidence and provides guidance on these key considerations to inform responsible research, clinical interpretation, and future policy related to CGM use in populations who do not have diabetes mellitus. Evidence discussed during the workshop underscored substantial inter- and intra-individual variability in PPGRs driven by complex interactions among dietary composition, lifestyle behaviors, and physiological characteristics. CGMs offer the ability to capture these dynamics under real-world conditions and may help identify meaningful individual differences, particularly as PPGRs are increasingly recognized as sensitive markers of early disturbances in glucose regulation. However, important limitations constrain interpretation in healthy populations, including analytical variability, limited precision for single-meal or acute assessments, lack of standardized research protocols and consensus guidance, and insufficient evidence linking CGM-derived metrics to long-term clinical outcomes. High-quality randomized controlled trials, standardized protocols, consensus guidance and integrated metabolic frameworks are needed to determine the clinical relevance of CGM use in healthy populations. Until such evidence is established, CGM use outside of diabetes care should be approached cautiously and ideally within structured research or in clinical contexts that provide appropriate education and professional oversight. STATEMENT OF SIGNIFICANCE: This perspective addresses an important and timely gap in nutrition and health research by evaluating the scientific considerations, methodological limitations, and clinical implications of CGM use in individuals living without diabetes. This paper provides important insights and considerations for the potential integration of CGMs into personalized nutrition research and practice.
