### 重點摘要
該研究旨在探討美國兒科嗜酸性粒細胞性胃腸道疾病（EGID）的照護模式和未滿足的需求。透過對參加北美兒科胃腸病學、肝臟病學和營養學研討會的胃腸科醫師進行問卷調查，結果顯示多數醫師每月診治較多嗜酸性粒細胞性食道炎（EoE）患者，但非EoE的EGID患者量則較少。改善治療選項是EoE照護的首要未滿足需求，且在藥物使用、飲食療法及先進監測技術上存在顯著的實踐差異。總體而言，兒科EGID的照護存在實踐模式差異和未滿足的需求，特別是學術機構在先進監測技術的應用率較高。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本文獻填補了美國兒科嗜酸性粒細胞性胃腸道疾病照護實踐模式和未滿足需求的現況研究空白，特別是區分了嗜酸性粒細胞性食道炎（EoE）與非EoE的EGID患者量、治療方法及監測技術的應用差異。儘管研究揭示了實踐上的差異和未滿足的需求，但並未深入探討造成這些差異的具體障礙，例如成本、培訓或設備可及性，這將是未來研究的重點。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Practice patterns and unmet needs in pediatric eosinophilic gastrointestinal diseases in the United States.
**Abstract:** To characterize pediatric eosinophilic gastrointestinal disease (EGID) care practices and unmet needs in the United States. A Research Electronic Data Capture (REDCap) survey was administered to attendees of the 2024 North American Society for Pediatric Gastroenterology, Hepatology, and Nutrition single-topic symposium. The response rate was 60% (203/339). Among US attending gastroenterologists (AGs), 72% reported seeing >5 eosinophilic esophagitis (EoE) patients per month, whereas 68% saw ≤5 non-EoE EGID patients per year. Improved treatment options were the leading unmet need for EoE care. AGs' rates of dupilumab use for current EoE patients: <10% (29%), 10%-25% (41%), 26%-50% (18%), and 51%-100% (6%). One-food elimination diets were used in >25% of current patients by 53% of AGs. The proportions of AGs reporting swallowed topical corticosteroid use in >25% of current EoE patients were 61%, 44%, and 62% in academic, community, and private practices, respectively; corresponding proportions for proton pump inhibitors were 39%, 56%, and 31%. Reported use of unsedated transnasal esophagoscopy and endoluminal functional lumen imaging probe was higher in academic than in community or private practices (48% vs. 0% vs. 0%, p < 0.0001; 52% vs. 6% vs. 15%, p < 0.001). Esophageal string and sponge tests were rarely used by AGs' (string = 7%; sponge = 2%). Practice variation and unmet needs persist in pediatric EGID care. Use of advanced monitoring technologies was greatest among academic AGs. Further research should identify barriers to implementation.
