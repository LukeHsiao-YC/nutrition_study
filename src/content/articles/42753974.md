### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討父母及嬰兒特徵如何影響嬰兒的食慾特徵，進而影響未來肥胖風險。研究追蹤432名塞浦路斯嬰兒，透過母親填寫問卷評估嬰兒在不同時期的食慾行為。結果顯示，妊娠糖尿病與食物反應性呈正相關，而配方奶餵養則呈負相關；飽足反應性與嬰兒體重身高Z分數呈正相關，與父親教育程度呈負相關；進食緩慢則與配方奶餵養及嬰兒體重身高Z分數呈負相關。這些發現證實了與肥胖相關的父母及嬰兒因素，與嬰兒食慾特徵存在多變的關聯，支持進一步探討它們在肥胖發展起源中的作用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了過去對於父母及嬰兒特徵如何透過影響嬰兒食慾特徵，進而關聯到未來肥胖風險的知識空白。未來的研究應進一步深入探討這些關聯的因果機制，以及它們對肥胖發展起源的具體貢獻，並可能需要擴大研究對象以增加結果的普適性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Associations of parental, pregnancy and infant characteristics with appetite traits in infancy: Results from a prospective study of infants in Cyprus.
**Abstract:** Parental and infant factors (eg pre-pregnancy maternal BMI, gestational body weight, breast or formula feeding) are known to influence the risk of developing obesity in later life, but whether this is influenced by their effects on appetite traits is unknown. We investigated the influence of developmental factors associated with obesity on appetite traits in infancy. Appetite traits were assessed by maternal completion of the Baby Eating Behaviour Questionnaire at 2-4 weeks and 6 months and the Child Eating Behaviour Questionnaire at 12 months in a cohort of 432 infants born in Cyprus and followed prospectively. Postulated parental and infant variables associated with infant obesity risk were analysed for their association with appetite traits. Multivariable linear regression models were built for the nominally statistically significant variables from minimally adjusted models for each appetite trait, adjusting for confounders. Associations between maternal and infant characteristics are based on appetite traits at 2-4 weeks which were the main determinants of later traits. Gestational diabetes (β=0.581, 95% CI: 0.209, 0.952) was positively while formula feeding (β=-0.320, 95%CI: -0.603, -0.038) was negatively associated with Food Responsiveness. WFA z-score (β=0.134, 95%CI: 0.005, 0.262) at 4 weeks was positively, while father's educational attainment (β=-0.319, 95%CI:-0.499, -0.138) was negatively associated with Satiety Responsiveness. Both formula-feeding at 4 weeks (β=-0.422, 95% CI: -0.707, -0.139) and WFA z-score at 4 weeks (β=-0.195 95% CI: -0.366, 0.025) were associated with lower Slowness in Eating. Our findings demonstrate that parental/infant factors linked with obesity show variable associations with appetite traits in infancy, supporting further investigation of their contribution to the developmental origins of obesity.
