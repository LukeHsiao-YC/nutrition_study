### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討8至12歲兒童對於環境永續飲食的動機，以及他們如何溝通並影響父母的永續飲食行為，以應對氣候變遷。研究透過對20名兒童及其一位家長進行深入質性訪談。結果顯示，除了口味偏好外，動物福利、環境及重要他人的偏好也是兒童選擇永續食物的重要驅動力；兒童會透過提問、告知及要求來影響家庭飲食決策。然而，家長的支持是兒童影響力的關鍵，因此未來的介入措施應同時顧及兒童的動機與家長的考量，以促進長期永續飲食習慣。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項質性研究填補了先前對於兒童（8-12歲）在環境永續飲食方面的動機，以及他們在家庭飲食決策中扮演的角色與影響方式的理解空白。雖然研究提供了深入的洞察，但其質性設計限制了結果的普遍性，未來研究可考慮量化驗證或針對家長支持進行更深入的探討。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Eating Green Together: A qualitative study to explore children's motivations and role in household decision making for environmentally sustainable diets.
**Abstract:** A transition towards more environmentally sustainable dietary patterns is crucial to mitigate the impact of current dietary habits on global climate change. Children represent a key target group in this transition, as they are in the process of developing their dietary habits, and they are future consumers who will bear the long-term consequences of today's dietary choices. Therefore, this study aims to explore the motivations of children aged 8 to 12 for environmentally sustainable dietary patterns, as well as how they communicate about and attempt to influence their parent's sustainable dietary behaviours. To do so, in-depth individual qualitative interviews were conducted with 20 children and one of their parents. Results show that, while taste preferences remain an important driver for children's (sustainable) food choices, also animal welfare, the environment, and significant others' preferences are important. Furthermore, children tend to ask parents questions, inform them about sustainable foods, and sometimes request specific (alternative) foods. However, for children to be able to influence the household's food decisions, parental support was a crucial factor. To encourage long-term sustainable dietary patterns, interventions should use these insights and address children's motivations while simultaneously addressing parental concerns.
