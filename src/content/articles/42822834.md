### 重點摘要
關於在健康飲食模式中適量攝取草飼紅肉的健康影響尚不確定。這項為期10週的隨機飲食干預研究比較了健康成人攝取適量草飼紅肉的彈性素食模式與包含植物性肉類替代品的素食模式對多不飽和脂肪酸狀態和心臟代謝健康的影響。研究結果顯示，與彈性素食組相比，素食組的血漿Omega-3 DPA在第5週時顯著下降，隨後在第10週恢復。然而，兩組在心臟代謝生物標誌物或大多數脂質譜方面沒有觀察到顯著差異，僅有單一脂質物種PE-O 39:6有顯著變化。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究探討了在健康飲食模式中，適量草飼紅肉與植物性肉類替代品對多不飽和脂肪酸狀態和心臟代謝健康的影響，此前這方面的健康效益尚不明確。儘管素食組的血漿DPA經歷了下降後恢復的模式，其潛在機制及長期影響需要進一步研究。此外，本研究未發現兩組在主要心臟代謝生物標誌物上的顯著差異，這可能需要更長時間的干預或更大的樣本量來觀察。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Pasture-Raised Red Meat Compared with Plant-Based Meat Alternatives Within Healthy Dietary Patterns: Effects on Polyunsaturated Fatty Acid Status and Cardiometabolic Health in a Randomized Dietary Intervention.
**Abstract:** The health effects of moderate consumption of pasture-raised red meat within healthy dietary patterns remain uncertain. Determine whether inclusion of moderate amounts of pasture-raised red meat altered polyunsaturated fatty acid (PUFA) status compared with a vegetarian dietary pattern incorporating plant-based meat alternatives (PBMAs) and examine effects on cardiometabolic biomarkers and circulating lipidomic profiles METHODS: In a 10-week free-living randomized dietary intervention, healthy adults consumed either a flexitarian dietary pattern including moderate amounts of pasture-raised red meat (3.3 serves/week at week 10) or a vegetarian dietary pattern incorporating PBMAs. Plasma fatty acids, cardiometabolic biomarkers, and lipidomic profiles were assessed at baseline, 5 weeks, and 10 weeks. Longitudinal changes were analysed using linear mixed-effects models accounting for repeated measures and household clustering. Benjamini-Hochberg correction was applied to prespecified plasma fatty acid outcomes and lipidomic analyses RESULTS: Seventy-eight participants completed the intervention. Plasma omega-3 PUFA docosapentaenoic acid (DPA) exhibited a significant intervention × time interaction, reflecting a decline in the vegetarian group between baseline and week 5 compared with the flexitarian group (estimate = -6.47 μmol/L, 95% CI: -10.39 to -2.56 μmol/L; p = 0.001), followed by recovery between week 5 and week 10 (estimate = -4.15 μmol/L, 95% CI: -8.07 to -0.24 μmol/L; p = 0.038). Following Benjamini-Hochberg correction, DPA remained significant (FDR-adjusted p = 0.030). No intervention × time effects were observed for cardiometabolic biomarkers.
