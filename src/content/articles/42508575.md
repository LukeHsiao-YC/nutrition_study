### 重點摘要
本研究旨在探討II-III期結直腸癌患者在接受卡培他濱化療時，一碳代謝生物標誌物濃度與化療引起毒性之間的關聯。研究納入了297名患者，測量其治療前血漿中葉酸、維生素B群及其他一碳代謝相關生物標誌物濃度，並分析其與卡培他濱治療毒性相關修飾的關聯，同時考慮MTHFR C677T基因型。結果顯示，總體人群中葉酸與毒性無關，但在MTHFR C677T CT/TT基因型患者中，較高的葉酸濃度與較低的治療毒性風險相關。這表明針對特定基因型患者，優化葉酸水平可能改善卡培他濱的耐受性。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項前瞻性研究首次詳細探討了一碳代謝生物標誌物與卡培他濱化療毒性的關係，並發現了葉酸與MTHFR基因型之間的交互作用，為精準營養干預提供了潛在基礎。然而，未來需要進一步的研究來證實透過營養指導優化一碳代謝生物標誌物是否能真正提高患者的治療耐受性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** ONE-CARBON METABOLISM AND CHEMOTHERAPY-INDUCED TOXICITIES IN PATIENTS WITH STAGE II-III COLORECTAL CANCER: A PROSPECTIVE COHORT STUDY.
**Abstract:** One-carbon metabolism (OCM) is a target of the chemotherapeutic agent capecitabine. We investigated OCM biomarker concentrations in relation to capecitabine-induced toxicities in patients with stage II-III colorectal cancer (CRC). Within a prospective cohort, 297 patients receiving adjuvant capecitabine-based chemotherapy were included. Pretreatment plasma concentrations of the OCM biomarkers folate, vitamin B2, vitamin B6, vitamin B12, total homocysteine, methionine, serine, and glycine were determined. We also investigated whether associations differed according to MTHFR C677T genotypes. Chemotherapy-induced toxicities were defined as toxicity-induced modifications of capecitabine treatment. To allow for inspection of shapes of the associations, restricted cubic splines (RCS) and Cox proportional hazards models were used to calculate hazard ratios (HR) and 95% confidence intervals (CI) adjusted for age and sex. In total, 156 (53%) patients experienced toxicity-induced modifications of capecitabine treatment. Folate was not associated with toxicities in the overall population.
