### 重點摘要
本研究重新檢視國家兒童健康調查（NSCH）在方法學變革後，其家長自報身高體重數據與國家健康營養調查（NHANES）的實際測量數據在估算6-17歲兒童及青少年肥胖盛行率上的一致性。研究方法是比較來自兩項調查的肥胖盛行率、身高、體重和身體質量指數。結果顯示，整體而言，家長自報數據估算的肥胖盛行率（16.9%）低於實際測量數據（20.1%）。雖然家長自報數據對6-11歲兒童的估計相似，但明顯低估了青少年的肥胖盛行率，因此使用家長自報數據時，尤其在細分亞組中，應謹慎解釋。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了國家兒童健康調查（NSCH）在重大方法學變革後，其家長自報數據與黃金標準的實際測量數據（NHANES）之間，兒童及青少年肥胖盛行率估計差異的重新評估空白。研究揭示了家長自報數據在評估青少年肥胖時存在低估的限制，這意味著未來使用此類數據進行肥胖監測或研究時，特別是針對青少年群體或詳細亞組分析，其結果準確性需格外審慎，可能需要額外的校正或輔助數據。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Obesity Prevalence in the United States among 6 - 17-Year-Old Children and Adolescents: Measured vs. Parent-Reported Height and Weight from Two National Surveys, 2015-2018.
**Abstract:** Previous comparison of obesity prevalence from the National Survey of Children's Health (NSCH) and the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) found differences between measured and parent-reported estimates for children and adolescents. The NSCH has undergone major methodological changes prompting re-examination of the correspondence between NSCH and NHANES obesity prevalence among children/adolescents ages 6-17 years. To compare obesity prevalence estimates among children/adolescents ages 6-17 years derived from parent-reported height/weight in NSCH (2016-2018) and measured height/weight in NHANES (2015-2018). Obesity prevalence, height, weight, and body-mass-index for age among children/adolescents ages 6-17 years from both surveys were compared by age, sex, and race/ethnicity using absolute and relative measures of difference. Overall obesity prevalence was lower using parent-reported data than measured data (16.9% vs. 20.1%,  Parent-reported data produced similar obesity estimates for children ages 6-11 years but underestimated adolescent obesity and should be interpreted cautiously, especially for detailed subgroups.
