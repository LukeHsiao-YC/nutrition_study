### 重點摘要
生育意願是生殖健康的核心，但如何最佳衡量卻缺乏共識。本研究比較了三個單一問題（關於時機、重要性和想要程度）與一個經過驗證的14項「避免懷孕意願」（DAP）量表在衡量美國女性生育意願上的表現。結果顯示，雖然單一問題的極端答案與DAP量表得分大致一致，但它們在分類上存在顯著差異，特別是單一問題的高意願類別包含了大量中等程度避免懷孕意願的個體。研究指出，儘管單一問題具效率，但其測量精準度不足且無法準確分類受訪者，是其應用上的重大缺點。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了關於不同生育意願測量工具在精準度與分類能力方面比較的空白，特別是揭示了單一問題在區分非明確想懷孕個體生育偏好上的局限性。未來研究應著重開發或採用更具精準度且能細緻區分生育意願的測量方法。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Comparing Approaches to Measuring Prospective Fertility Desires in the United States: A Population-based Study.
**Abstract:** Fertility desires are central to reproductive health, yet researchers lack consensus on how to best measure them. We compared participants' responses to three individual questions - Timing ("How do you feel about having a child now or sometime in the future?"), Importance ("How important is it to you to avoid becoming pregnant now?"), and Wantedness ("Do you want to become pregnant in the next year?") - to responses to the 14-item Desire to Avoid Pregnancy (DAP) scale, a psychometrically valid measure of pregnancy preferences. We analyzed data from the Surveys of Women, population-based longitudinal studies of reproductive-aged females in nine US states. We used multinomial logistic regression to generate predicted responses to each individual question by DAP score, and logistic regression to estimate annual incident pregnancy for each approach. While responses to individual fertility desire questions aligned with DAP scores, the approaches yielded substantially different categorizations. The lowest and highest categories of Timing, Importance, and Wantedness largely captured individuals with low and high DAP scores, respectively. However, the highest categories also included large proportions of individuals with a moderate preference to avoid pregnancy (Timing: 45% of those with mid-range DAP scores, Importance: 43%, Wantedness: 70%). Similarly, the non-polar categories of the individual fertility desire questions included individuals across wide ranges of DAP scores. Asking about wanting pregnancy can identify those most open to pregnancy but does not differentiate fertility preferences among individuals not explicitly desiring pregnancy, which is most people. While single questions are efficient, lack of measurement precision or evidence that they accurately categorize respondents is a compelling drawback to their use.
