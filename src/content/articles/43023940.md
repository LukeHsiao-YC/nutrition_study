### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估2022年美國嬰兒配方奶粉短缺對照護者的生活經驗、應對行為及感知影響。研究採用混合方法設計，結合243份線上問卷與14份半結構式訪談。結果顯示，照護者面臨資訊需求未被滿足及社會支持不一，其中55.1%因短缺改變了原定的嬰兒餵養方式，這與家庭和照護者心理健康受負面影響顯著相關。研究強調需連結支援、資源與資訊給照護者，以保護嬰兒餵養、促進照護者心理健康並防範未來短缺。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了關於2022年美國嬰兒配方奶粉短缺對照護者生活經驗、應對行為及身心影響的實證資料空白，提供了對此特定危機深度的質性與量化分析。未來的研究應考慮更廣泛的地理範圍或縱向研究設計，以探討長期影響或不同國家文化背景下的差異。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Lived Experiences, Coping Behaviors, and Impacts of the 2022 Infant Formula Shortage on Caregivers in the United States.
**Abstract:** Assess and explore associations between lived experiences, coping behaviors, and perceived impacts on caregivers affected by the 2022 infant formula shortage in the United States (US), referred to as the shortage. Convergent mixed methods study design with cross-sectional online survey (n = 243) and semi-structured interviews (n = 14). National. US caregivers responsible for an infant during the shortage and affected by the shortage. Lived experiences, coping behaviors, perceived impacts of shortage. Descriptive statistics conducted. Associations tested using Pearson's Chi-Squared tests with a post hoc analysis of adjusted call residuals. Qualitative content analysis of semi-structured interviews performed. Caregivers reported unmet information needs and both positive and negative social support experiences as they navigated the shortage. The most frequently reported coping behavior (55.1%) was changing planned infant feeding practices (e.g., breastfed longer than intended) because of the shortage, which were correlated with perceived impacts of the household being negatively effected (p = 0.003) and that caregiver mental health was negatively effected (p = 0.002) by the shortage. Caregiver interviews revealed detrimental physical effects on infants due to switching infant formulas, negative mental health impacts on caregivers, financial strains, and concerns about future shortages. Efforts that link support, resources, and information to caregivers are needed to protect infant feeding, promote caregiver mental health, and safeguard against future infant formula shortages.
