### 重點摘要
本研究強調兒童健康素養的重要性，其為終身健康行為和知情決策的基礎。研究旨在翻譯、跨文化調適並對GeKo工具進行心理計量學驗證，對象為葡萄牙一所學校的10-12歲兒童便利樣本。研究採用標準程序進行跨文化調適，並對83名參與者評估了內部一致性、因子結構、重測信度和聚合效度。最終成功地完成了該健康素養評估工具的跨文化調適與心理計量學驗證。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了在葡萄牙10-12歲兒童群體中，缺乏經過跨文化調適和心理計量學驗證的健康素養評估工具GeKo的空白。然而，其便利抽樣和單一學校的環境可能限制了研究結果的普適性。

**Title:** Cross-cultural adaptation and psychometric validation of the GeKo
**Abstract:** Promoting health literacy in childhood is essential, as it lays the foundation for lifelong healthy behaviors and supports informed decision-making. This study aimed to translate, cross-culturally adapt, and psychometrically validate the GeKo The study was conducted at a school in Leiria, Portugal (October-December 2024), with a convenience sample of children aged 10-12 years. The cross-cultural adaptation followed standard procedures including translation-back translation, cognitive debriefing, and content validation by an expert panel. Psychometric evaluation included 83 participants, assessing internal consistency via Cronbach's alpha and corrected item-total correlations. Factorial structure was examined using confirmatory factor analysis (CFA), with χ²/df, CFI, TLI, SRMR and RMSEA as fit indices. Test-retest reliability was assessed using the intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC (3,1)).
