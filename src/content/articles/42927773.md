### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討美國青少年使用尼古丁袋（NP）的原因盛行率及其與使用模式的關聯。根據2025年「監測未來」的全國代表性調查數據顯示，實驗、尼古丁快感和放鬆是青少年使用NP最常見的原因。研究發現，口味、便利性和感覺成癮與過去30天內NP使用及頻率呈正相關，而實驗則與使用頻率呈負相關。這些結果強調了制定針對口味和便利性的預防與監管策略的重要性，並指出現有針對青少年尼古丁電子煙的有效策略可能適用於NP使用。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了關於美國青少年使用尼古丁袋（NP）的具體原因及其與使用模式之間關聯的現有知識空白。它提供了對青少年NP使用動機的實證數據，並將其與電子煙使用原因進行了比較，為制定預防和監管策略提供了重要依據。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Reasons for Nicotine Pouch Use Among US Youth: 2025.
**Abstract:** To document prevalence of reasons for nicotine pouch (NP) use and associations with use patterns among US youth. Data were from US youth reporting ever NP use in the nationally representative 2025 Monitoring the Future survey. We estimated (1) prevalence of 10 reasons for NP use (yes/no) and (2) association of each reason with past 30-day NP use (yes/no) and frequency (1-30 days). For comparison, we contrasted reasons for NP and e-cigarette use among youth reporting NP use (n = 57) or e-cigarette use (n = 397) on at least 10 of the past 30 days. Among 484 respondents reporting ever NP use (6.1% of youth surveyed on NP use and reasons), the most common reasons were experimentation (32.3%), nicotine buzz (30.5%), relaxation (22.7%), appealing flavors (17.4%), convenience (16.5%), socializing (16.0%), boredom (11.7%), and ability to conceal use (11.0%). To quit smoking (6.2%) and feeling addicted (2.1%) were less common. Flavors, convenience, and feeling addicted were positively associated with both past 30-day NP use (prevalence ratios [PRs] range = 1.50-2.35) and frequency (incidence rate ratios [IRRs] range = 1.60-2.55). Nicotine buzz, relaxation, socializing, concealment, and to quit smoking were only associated with past 30-day use (PRs range = 1.57-1.85). Experimentation was inversely associated with frequency (IRR = 0.42). The prevalence of reasons overlapped between NPs and e-cigarettes, with relaxation, convenience, nicotine buzz, and flavors most prevalent among youth regularly using each product. Prevention and regulatory strategies addressing NP use reasons, particularly flavors and convenience, are warranted. Existing effective strategies targeting youth nicotine vaping may be applicable to NP use.
