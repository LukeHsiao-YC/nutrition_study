### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估一項名為 APA-FIT 的數位混合生活方式介入計畫，對於居住在農村地區且具中高度心血管風險的成人，在身體活動、靜態行為、地中海飲食依從性和正向心理健康方面的影響。這項多中心隨機對照試驗招募了436名參與者，將其隨機分為介入組和對照組，介入組接受為期9個月的跨學科介入（包括運動處方、地中海飲食推廣和心理支持）並結合數位平台進行監測。結果顯示，介入組在總身體活動量、地中海飲食依從性顯著增加，同時久坐時間和體重顯著降低，但正向心理健康未見組間差異。結論指出 APA-FIT 是一種有效的跨學科數位支援策略，有助於改善農村地區心血管風險成人之生活方式。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了將跨學科和數位健康介入整合到醫療系統的不足，特別是針對農村地區具心血管代謝風險的成人，證實了此類混合式生活方式介入的有效性。然而，該研究未能改善參與者的正向心理健康，未來的研究應探索如何強化此一面向。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** APA-FIT: A Digital Hybrid Lifestyle Intervention for Adults at Cardiometabolic Risk in Rural Primary Care: A Randomized Trial.
**Abstract:** Non-communicable diseases are a major global health burden, strongly associated with lifestyle. Interdisciplinary and digital health interventions may improve lifestyle behaviors and cardiometabolic risk, although their integration into healthcare systems remains limited. This study evaluated the effects of APA-FIT on physical activity, sedentary behavior, Mediterranean diet adherence, and positive mental health among adults with moderate-to-high cardiovascular risk living in rural areas. Multicenter randomized controlled trial in rural primary care in Catalonia (Spain). Data were collected in September 2025-April 2026 and analyzed in May-July 2026. 436 participants aged 18-80 years with moderate-to-high cardiovascular risk were randomized 1:1 to the intervention group (IG) or control group (CG; 218 per group). The IG received a 9-month interdisciplinary intervention including physical activity prescription, Mediterranean diet promotion and psychoemotional support, combined with an app-web platform for monitoring and follow-up. The CG received usual care. Outcomes included physical activity (IPAQ), sedentary behavior, Mediterranean diet adherence (PREDIMED), positive mental health (PMHQ-SF18) and body composition. Mixed-effects models included group, time, and their interaction as fixed effects and a participant random intercept. Group-by-time analyses showed increases in total physical activity (difference, 986.63 MET-min/week; 95% CI, 559.75 to 1,413.52; p<0.001) and Mediterranean diet adherence (PREDIMED score difference, 1.00 point; 95% CI, 0.62 to 1.38; p<0.001), and reductions in sedentary time (difference, -0.76 h/day; 95% CI, -1.26 to -0.26; p=0.003) and body weight (difference, -1.07 kg; 95% CI, -2.05 to -0.09; p=0.032) in the IG relative to the CG. No between-group change was observed in positive mental health. APA-FIT may represent a useful interdisciplinary and digitally supported strategy for promoting healthier lifestyles among adults with cardiovascular risk living in rural areas. Further research should explore how its positive mental health component could be strengthened. ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07172490, 25 August 2025.
