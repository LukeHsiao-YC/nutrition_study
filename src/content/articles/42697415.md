### 重點摘要
這項為期20年的SUN計畫追蹤研究，旨在評估飲食模式在自體免疫疾病初級預防中的作用，特別是地中海飲食（MedDiet）依從性與類風濕性關節炎及乾癬發生風險的關聯。研究分析了15,874名西班牙大學畢業生的數據，發現高度依從地中海飲食與類風濕性關節炎的發生風險呈顯著負相關（HR=0.53）。然而，對於乾癬的關聯則不顯著（HR=0.86）。研究結論指出，長期依從地中海飲食可能有助於降低類風濕性關節炎的風險，但對乾癬的影響尚無定論。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了飲食模式在特定自體免疫疾病（尤其是類風濕性關節炎）初級預防方面知識不足的空白。然而，對於地中海飲食與乾癬的關聯性仍未有定論，且研究對象主要為西班牙大學畢業生，其結果的普遍性可能存在限制，未來研究可擴展至更多元的人群。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Long-term adherence to the Mediterranean Diet and risk of rheumatoid arthritis and psoriasis: 20-year follow-up of the SUN project.
**Abstract:** The role of dietary patterns in the primary prevention of specific autoimmune conditions remains poorly understood. To evaluate the association between Mediterranean Diet (MedDiet) adherence and the risk of incident rheumatoid arthritis and psoriasis. We conducted a prospective analysis of 15,874 IMID-free participants from the SUN Project cohort (median follow-up: 15.1 years). The outcomes were the incidence of rheumatoid arthritis and psoriasis. Adherence to the MedDiet was repeatedly assessed using validated food-frequency questionnaires with the Mediterranean Diet Score (MDS) and classified into energy-adjusted tertiles. Incident cases were identified through biennial questionnaires. Primary analyses used Cox proportional hazards models with cumulative average dietary exposure. Hazard ratios (HRs) and 95% confidence intervals (CIs) were estimated from multivariable models, stratified by age and follow-up period and adjusted for sociodemographic, lifestyle, and clinical confounders. During follow-up, we identified 165 incident cases of psoriasis (0.73 per 1,000 person-years) and 119 incident cases of rheumatoid arthritis (0.52 per 1,000 person-years). High MedDiet adherence (T3 compared with T1) was inversely associated with rheumatoid arthritis risk (HR=0.53; 95% CI: 0.32, 0.89; p-trend=0.020). For psoriasis, higher adherence showed a non-significant inverse association (HR=0.86; 95% CI: 0.57, 1.32; p-trend=0.491). In exploratory item-by-item analyses for rheumatoid arthritis, lower meat intake and higher fruit/nut intake were associated with lower disease risk (HR=0.41; 95% CI: 0.27, 0.61; HR=0.64; 95% CI: 0.43, 0.95, respectively). High MedDiet adherence was inversely associated with the risk of incident rheumatoid arthritis among Spanish university graduates, whereas the association for psoriasis remained inconclusive. Clinical Trials Registry: NCT02669602; Website: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02669602.
