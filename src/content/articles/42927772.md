### 重點摘要
本臨床報告探討受親代物質使用影響的兒童與家庭所面臨的短期與長期風險，這些風險涵蓋醫療、心理社會、安全與行為層面。報告旨在為小兒科醫師提供全面指南，內容涵蓋美國親代物質使用的流行病學、偏見與結構性不平等對家庭的影響、產前與產後物質使用的已知風險。此外，報告也討論毒物學檢測、篩檢方法、預期性衛教重點，並提出針對兒虐或疏忽的處置建議。其宗旨在於支持受親代物質使用影響的兒童與家庭的福祉。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇臨床報告填補了小兒科臨床實踐在處理受親代物質使用影響之家庭方面的指導空白，整合了流行病學、風險評估、篩檢方法與介入建議，並強調結構性不平等的影響。然而，其限制在於不提供新的原創研究數據，而是以現有文獻與專家共識為基礎。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Children and Families Affected by Parental Substance Use: Clinical Report.
**Abstract:** Children and adolescents whose parents have an alcohol or substance use disorder are at increased risk of short- and long-term sequelae, ranging from medical conditions to psychosocial, safety, and behavioral challenges. In the course of providing health care services to children, pediatricians and other pediatric clinicians are likely to encounter families affected by parental substance use and are in a unique position to support the well-being of both the child and family. The purpose of this clinical report is to briefly discuss the epidemiology of substance use during pregnancy and while parenting in the United States; highlight the influence of bias, racism, and other structural inequities on families affected by substance use; describe the known risks associated with prenatal and postnatal parental substance use; discuss use and interpretation of toxicology testing as part of a substance exposure evaluation; review approaches to screening for prenatal and parental substance use; propose key topics for anticipatory guidance in the pediatric clinic; identify and address concerning parental and child presentations related to parental substance use leading to concern for child abuse or neglect; and provide recommendations for pediatric clinicians involved in the care of children from families affected by substance use.
