### 重點摘要
這項研究旨在探討義大利北部孕婦飲食行為的相關因素，以填補此領域證據有限的空白。研究採用橫斷面分析，納入1254名孕婦，透過問卷評估飲食行為分數，並使用多變量迴歸模型分析與參與者特徵的關聯。結果顯示，社經地位是影響孕期飲食行為的重要因素，社經地位越高，飲食習慣越佳。研究結論指出，孕期飲食行為受社經、行為和健康相關因素的複雜交互作用影響，而非僅僅是營養知識，強調需針對社會和背景因素設計客製化介入措施。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文獻填補了關於孕婦健康飲食行為相關因素的有限證據空白，特別指出社經、行為和健康相關因素的複雜交互作用對於飲食行為的影響，超越了單純的營養知識。

**Abstract:** Diet during pregnancy plays a crucial role in promoting healthy dietary behaviours and positive maternal and child health outcomes. However, evidence regarding the factors associated with healthy dietary behaviours among pregnant women is limited. The  This cross-sectional analysis included 1254 pregnant women recruited from two Northern Italian centres (Turin and Cuneo). Dietary behaviour was assessed using a questionnaire, which generated a diet-related scores. Associations between these scores and participants' characteristics were investigated using multivariable regression models. Overall, participants reported relatively favourable dietary habits and a low prevalence of risky behaviours. These findings highlight the need for tailored interventions that address the social and contextual features of dietary behaviour to encourage healthier habits during pregnancy.
