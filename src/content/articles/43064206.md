### 重點摘要
本研究旨在評估手持式超音波測量設備在五歲以下兒童身高測量上的可靠性與準確性，並與標準測量板進行比較。透過測量57名0-5歲兒童，結果顯示超音波設備與標準測量板展現相似的可靠性，技術測量誤差均符合現行標準。然而，超音波設備測得的身高略高於測量板（平均高0.20公分），且在年齡較大的兒童中偏差更明顯。研究結論指出，該超音波設備在五歲以下兒童身高測量上可靠性高，但準確性存在變異，特別是年齡較大的兒童。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這項研究填補了對手持式超音波身高測量設備在兒童生長監測應用上初步評估的空白。然而，未來的研究需要進一步探討不準確性的具體來源，並評估該設備在不同情境下的可靠性。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** A Digital Ultrasonic Height Measurement Device for Children Aged Under Five Years show high reliability yet variable accuracy.
**Abstract:** Accurate height measurement is essential for monitoring child growth. Our aim was to assess the reliability and accuracy of a handheld ultrasonic measurement device compared with a standard measurement board METHODS: We used a cross-sectional methods-comparison study design with experts measuring n=57 children aged 0-5 years in metropolitan Melbourne, Australia. The ultrasonic device was our index test and the measurement board, our reference test. The ultrasonic device demonstrated a similar reliability to the board with a technical error of measurement (TEM) of 0.25cm (95%CI: 0.22, 0.28cm) and 0.19cm (95%CI: 0.17, 0.22) respectively, both within current standards. F-test ratios (ultrasonic vs. board) for intra-measurer TEMs were higher for children ≤2 years at 1.39 (95% CI: 1.03, 1.87; p=0.03) than children >2 years at 1.20 (95%CI: 0.94, 1.51; p=0.14). The ultrasonic device measured children slightly taller than the board (mean difference 0.20 cm; 95% CI: 0.02, 0.38; SD=0.67; p<0.01), with a larger difference in older children who were measured standing (0.35 cm; p=0.01). The Bland Altman 95% limits of agreement ranged from -1.13cm to 1.53cm, demonstrating variance in the measurements between the different tools. We found that the ultrasonic device showed high reliability for children under 5 years when compared to the board, though accuracy was variable, with greater bias found in older children. Further testing is required to identify the source of inaccuracies and also evaluate reliability in other contexts.
