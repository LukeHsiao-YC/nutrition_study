### 重點摘要
鑒於先前對健康預設飲品（HDB）政策的評估顯示實施和執行不一致，本研究旨在全面盤點並比較美國各地已實施和擬議的HDB政策。研究團隊透過系統性搜索政策資料庫、網站和文獻，識別了2015年至2024年間的86項政策，並分析其執行、實施、公平性條款，以及與「健康飲品健康兒童」（HDHK）建議的符合程度。結果顯示，儘管HDB政策在美國各地日益普及，但執行、評估和實施條款並不常見，公平性條款僅出現在地方政策中，且沒有政策完全符合HDHK建議。研究結論強調，強化政策的實施、執行和公平性條款，對於提升其減少兒童餐廳含糖飲料攝取的有效性至關重要。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了先前研究中，缺乏對美國各地已實施和擬議的健康預設飲品政策的實施、執行和公平性條款進行全面盤點與比較分析的空白，藉此識別影響政策成效的潛在不足之處。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Healthy default beverage policies: A comprehensive inventory and comparative analysis of enacted and proposed policies across U.S. state and local jurisdictions.
**Abstract:** Previous evaluations of healthy default beverage (HDB) policies showed inconsistent implementation and enforcement. No study has described and compared implementation and enforcement provisions in enacted and proposed policies to identify gaps impacting policy success. To identify and examine trends in enacted and proposed U.S. local and state HDB policies; compare implementation, enforcement, and equity provisions across policy status and jurisdictional levels; evaluate alignment with Healthy Drinks Healthy Kids (HDHK) recommendations; and illustrate how enacted policies changed during committee review. This study identified enacted and proposed local and state policies through systematic searches of policy databases (American Legal Publishing Code Library, Municode, NexisUni, Westlaw), websites, and literature in September 2024. Policies were reviewed for inclusion, and a jurisdiction's final policy version coded for key characteristics. Enacted policy's legislative websites were separately searched for accompanying documents (e.g., committee reports). The outcomes were number of enacted and proposed policies; frequency of implementation, enforcement, and equity provisions; HDHK recommendations alignment; and committee-required changes to enacted policies. Intercoder agreement was calculated with Cohen's kappa, and descriptive statistics summarized policy contents. As a case-study, accompanying documents from state-level enacted Hawaii and Illinois policies were qualitatively reviewed. This inventory included 86 policies (28 local, 58 state) from 2015-2024. After excluding duplicate state-level bicameral policies and counting each re-introduction of a policy across legislative sessions as an additional policy, there were 65 unique policies. Policies were enacted in 28 local jurisdictions and 4 states, and proposed (not enacted) in 14 states. Implementation, evaluation, and enforcement provisions were uncommon across jurisdictions and statuses. Equity provisions appeared only in local policies. No policies completely met HDHK recommendations. Accompanying documents illustrated how the legislative process strengthened or weakened policy provisions in Hawaii and Illinois. HDB policies are increasingly enacted across U.S. jurisdictions. Strengthening implementation, enforcement, and equity provisions may enhance policy effectiveness in reducing children's restaurant sweetened beverage intake.
