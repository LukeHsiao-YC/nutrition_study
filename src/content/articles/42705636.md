### 重點摘要
本研究指出生成式人工智慧（Gen AI）平台在醫療領域中日益普及，能有效協助臨床醫師簡化任務並輔助病患照護，對受訓者評估複雜醫學資訊亦有助益。然而，病患及家屬對Gen AI的接受度仍不明朗，且缺乏明確的知情同意標準；AI工具可能省略兒科臨床互動中重要的「閒聊」並有捏造內容的傾向。此外，醫學生及住院醫師在批判性技能尚未成熟前，可能輕信AI生成的資訊。儘管面臨挑戰，Gen AI在臨床空間中將持續存在，因此需在全面考量下，安全有效地將其整合至病患照護與醫學教育中。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
這篇文章指出了目前生成式人工智慧在臨床應用上，病患與家屬接受度、知情同意標準、以及其內容可靠性（如省略「閒聊」、捏造內容）等方面的現有挑戰與尚未解決的議題。同時，它也凸顯了醫學生和住院醫師在批判性評估能力不足前，可能盲目接受AI生成資訊的風險，並強調未來在醫療教育和臨床實踐中，安全有效整合AI的必要性，作為進一步研究與政策制定的基礎。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** AI at the Bedside: An Argument for a Training Wheels Approach.
**Abstract:** Bedside use of artificial intelligence (AI) platforms is increasingly common. Busy clinicians may welcome these generative AI (Gen AI) tools, which have the potential to streamline many time-consuming tasks and aid in patient care. Trainees may find them useful to quickly evaluate complex medical information. Acceptance of bedside Gen AI tools by patients and their families, however, is less clear, and a definitive standard surrounding informed consent has yet to be established. Omission of certain types of information by Gen AI tools, including "small talk," which comprises an essential element of many pediatric clinical interactions, demands attention alongside the tendency of Gen AI tools to fabricate content. Medical students and resident physicians may be early adopters of Gen AI tools and may accept AI-generated statements at face value before having fully developed adequate knowledge and critical skills to independently evaluate their veracity. Despite these challenges, Gen AI tools in the clinical space are here to stay. Safe and effective incorporation of these tools in patient care and medical education must balance a wide range of considerations. In the following Ethics Rounds, stemming from a 2024 Pediatric Academic Society Bioethics Club Meeting session on the use of AI, the commentators draw on their diverse background as pediatric clinicians, bioethicists, and educators to explore these issues.
