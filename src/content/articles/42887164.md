### 重點摘要
這篇討論文章旨在解決加護病房 (ICU) 預後不確定性溝通教育不足的問題。研究透過敘事綜合分析，整合不確定性理論與多方文獻，提出 ICU 護理師協助病患與家屬面對預後不確定性的五個候選能力領域。這些領域包括協調角色與訊息、描繪病患參與與偏好、說明已知與未知、回應情緒並確認理解，以及隨時間重新探討不確定性。本研究的成果為教育與實踐提供了初步的概念性框架，但仍需進一步的病患與家屬共同設計和驗證才能實施。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
本研究填補了當前醫護溝通教育多專注於準確資訊傳達，而非如何處理預後不確定性的空白。然而，本研究為作者主導的敘事綜合分析，過程具解釋性而非系統性，且未包含獨立編碼或病患與家屬的共同參與。文章中提出的能力領域僅為概念性提案，未來實施前仍需透過病患與家屬共同設計、內容驗證、情境評估以及成果測試。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** Beyond information delivery: Candidate competencies for ICU nurses navigating prognostic uncertainty with patients and families.
**Abstract:** Prognostic uncertainty is intrinsic to critical illness, yet communication education often treats the clinician's task as accurate information transfer. This Discussion Paper presents and critically examines author-derived candidate competencies for intensive care unit (ICU) nurses supporting adult patients and families when prognosis remains uncertain. A four-step, author-led narrative synthesis integrated uncertainty theory, adult patient and family evidence, ICU communication literature, and nursing role constraints. Recurrent nursing functions were compared, grouped, and cross-checked against reported needs, harms, and role boundaries. The resulting domains are: align roles and messages; map patient participation, uncertainty, and preferences; name what is known, unknown, and next; respond to emotion and verify understanding; and revisit uncertainty across time and transitions. Their enactment depends on trust, interdisciplinary coherence, staffing, continuity, interpreter access, family-centred policies, and a suitable communication environment. The process was interpretive rather than systematic and did not include independent coding or patient and family partners. The domains are a transparent conceptual proposal for education and debate, not a disclosure protocol or validated standard. Patient and family co-design, content validation, context-sensitive behavioral assessment, and testing of learner, relational, and system outcomes are required before implementation.
