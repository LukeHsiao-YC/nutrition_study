### 重點摘要
極端天氣與人口老化使慢性病預防複雜化，亟需在地化氣候測量與可比較的研究成果。此研究連結了來自中國157個城市的23,725名45歲以上成人的健康調查數據與氣象資料，利用多尺度地理與時間加權迴歸及動態雙重機器學習方法進行分析。結果顯示，滯後性相對寒冷是城市層面最明確的氣候指標，與多重慢性病呈正相關，並在多數城市年份中具顯著正向係數。研究支持針對寒冷季節進行在地化監測，並提供具目標性的慢性病照護追蹤。

### 研究縫隙 (Research Gap)
該研究填補了過去缺乏在地化、城市特異性氣候指標來評估極端天氣對多重慢性病影響的研究空白，並首次提供了城市與個體層面可比較的成果，為精準公共衛生規劃提供了實證基礎。

### 原文資訊 (Original English)
**Title:** City-Specific Climate Extremes and Multimorbidity Inequities Among Middle-Aged and Older Adults in 157 Chinese Cities.
**Abstract:** Extreme weather and population aging complicate chronic disease prevention. Planning requires locally relevant climate measures and comparable city- and individual-level outcomes. Repeated China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study and China Longitudinal Aging Social Survey data from 23,725 adults aged 45 years or older in 157 Chinese cities were linked to meteorological and contextual data. Survey waves were 2016, 2018, and 2020; analyses were conducted in 2026. The individual outcome was chronic-condition count (0-13); city-year prevalence of chronic disease multimorbidity (at least 2 of 13 conditions) was used for spatial analysis. City-specific 2011-2015 percentiles defined heat and cold within 3-year pre-survey windows. Multiscale geographically and temporally weighted regression estimated spatially varying city associations; dynamic double machine learning estimated standardized individual adjusted contrasts. Lagged relative cold was the clearest city-level climate measure. Relative cold had a positive local coefficient in 92.3% of city-years and exceeded |t|>1.96 in 67.2% (mean coefficient=1.372; R City-specific relative cold identified a consistent city-level multimorbidity pattern, while fixed-threshold cold provided a positive individual-level companion contrast. The findings support locally calibrated cold-season surveillance and targeted chronic-care follow-up.
